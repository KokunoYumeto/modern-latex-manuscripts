## How To Use A Record

Reader PDFs are the front-facing material. They should be opened first and are meant to be browsable in Zenodo preview.

Artifact ZIPs are the working material. They usually contain TeX, source witnesses, component PDFs, OCR text, page images, render checks, provenance notes, or repair material.

Manifest and summary files explain the state of the record. They are useful when deciding whether a PDF is a finished reading draft, a translation fragment, or a source reference.
