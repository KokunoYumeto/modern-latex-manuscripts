## Exact Scope

Both independent readers cover Exposés 149, 182, 190, 195, 212, 221, 232,
and 236, the 1962 *Commentaires*, and all eight linked errata. This completes
the declared bounded source-text scope and its shared pre-Stacks
semantic/reference scaffold. It does not assign or invent official Stacks
Project tags.

The direct scan for the Exposé 182 erratum exposes printed pages 298–299 while
the intake catalog says 298–300. That authority-metadata discrepancy remains
explicit in `resid.csv`; it has not been silently repaired.
