# Gauss — current I/II readers and preserved editions

This collection makes works of Carl Friedrich Gauss available as original-language faithful transcriptions, faithful English translations, editable sources, historical source images and separate AI editorial commentary. Its retained materials cover Bands I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, IX and XI Part I, individual papers and selected passages across number theory, analysis, geometry, geodesy and astronomy. Coverage and review status differ by component.

## Authorship and scope

Gauss and the identified historical editors remain the historical authors. **Original-language faithful transcription** and **Faithful English translation** retain the author's words, mathematical objects, formulas, conditions, arguments, structure and order. Source errors and unresolved marks remain identifiable; later editorial findings do not silently replace historical statements.

**AI editorial commentary — ChatGPT, GPT-6 Astra, Ultra**

Commentary author: **ChatGPT**. Recorded model: **GPT-6 Astra**. Reasoning effort: **Ultra**. These separate readers contain modern explanations, conceptual errata, supplementary arguments and extensions. They are not Gauss's historical text. Band I commentary covers physical 88–483 only. Unknown model or effort identities of older reconstructions remain unidentified. Curation and packaging preparation used ChatGPT, GPT-6.1 Sol, Ultra; this is separate from the recorded Astra commentary authorship. Compression does not establish a new author or model identity for the historical text or commentary.

Band I assembles historical physical leaves 2–483, including the recovered physical 80–87. Physical1 of its authority is a modern library metadata and terms sheet. The recovery records retain their original-source qualifications; no missing historical source is inferred to have been found. Band II extends through final physical 544 of the witness printed in 1876; the older filename containing 1870 is retained as provenance. Printed folios, physical leaves and generated PDF pages are distinct locators.

Historical coverage assembly is not final native/font clearance. Full-volume final reviews and disclosed source-mark, typeface and comparison holds remain open, including the retained I 85.U01 and II 43 readings. The current Band II source/English files include the later selected presentation corrections. Historical source formulations remain identifiable; no mathematical correction is inferred merely from expected values.
