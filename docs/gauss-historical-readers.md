# Gauss historical readers and English translations

This collection brings together modern LaTeX working reconstructions of Carl Friedrich Gauss's Werke and individual papers, with historical source transcriptions, faithful English translations, editable sources, source images and separately identified editorial commentary. It makes the historical texts available for reading and further correction while retaining their mathematical objects, statements, formulas, arguments and order. Coverage and review status vary by component; this is not a claim that all collected works are transcribed or translated completely.

The retained collection includes work on Bands I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, IX and XI Part I, individual papers and selected passages, earlier source snapshots and repair packages. Its subjects include number theory, analysis, geometry and geodesy, astronomy and related work represented in those volumes. The Band IV German/Latin geodesy transcription and its TeX sources, earlier Band II reading materials through printed page 303, and individual-paper source/English pairs remain available. The legacy reader once mislabeled as a Band I alternate remains identified as Band IX. All 88 files of the preceding edition are retained unchanged.

Gauss and the identified historical editors are the historical authors. The source transcription and English translation are the principal deliverables. Existing modern apparatus, source corrections, supplementary proofs and extensions by ChatGPT, GPT-6 Astra at Ultra, are supplied separately as editorial commentary. They are not silently substituted into the historical text or attributed to Gauss.

The historical source transcription and faithful English translation are the principal reading layers. Gauss's original statements, formulas, arguments and order remain identifiable. Existing ChatGPT, GPT-6 Astra at Ultra, commentary and supplementary mathematics are supplied in separate readers and editable files.

Band I now includes the newly recovered leaves 80–87 between the retained 2–79 and 88–483 components. Its historical source reader has 821 PDF pages and its English reader 606. The physical first page of the 483-page GDZ authority is a modern library metadata/terms sheet; historical coverage therefore runs from physical 2 through 483. The full authority remains retained. Earlier component footers and the initial assembly cover preserve their dated status wording; this note records the subsequent first-leaf inspection and recovery. No historical original 83 or 87 is claimed to have been found.

Band II remains assembled through final physical 544 of the witness printed in 1876. Its source, English and editorial readers have 1214, 819 and 1027 PDF pages. The reviewed derivatives correct two confirmed Latin copying errors (112: consuletur; 118: esse deberet) and the contents destination on 538 from 357 to 387. Initial public PDFs and exact correction preimages remain available. The historical filename containing 1870 is retained for provenance.

The separate Band I editorial reader covers 88–483 and has 552 PDF pages; it does not claim editorial coverage of the recovered gap. Earlier editorial components remain in the source collection. The Band II commentary reader has 1027 pages. These modern contributions are not attributed to Gauss or substituted into the historical translation.

Complete coverage assembly is not final review clearance. Full-volume FinalP43/P45 and cold audits are ongoing on the two existing web accounts. The retained reading records identify actual comparisons, unavailable renders and unresolved readings. In particular, I 85.U01 and the small II 43 exponent remain visibly uncertain. The latest captured II review through 161 records two remaining local detail holds. The title and blank verso were confirmed locally, and the proposed punctuation correction on 130 was rejected because the native source itself has the comma. No correction is inferred from mathematical expectation.

The new independently extractable recovery archive supplies the original captured source/English fragments, reviewed derivatives, exact native PNGs, independent TeX inputs, correction preimages and review records. Its additive manifest distinguishes original and public hashes; private machine prefixes and conversation/account references are removed only in identified publication derivatives. Combine this archive with all the earlier retained source archives and manifests. The earlier 56,382-path source manifest, restoration script, assembly recipe and complete reading notes remain unchanged. Exact component-to-reader maps accompany the new material.

The wider Gauss collection retains partial material from Bands I–IX and XI Part I, individual papers, earlier reconstructions and repair drafts. Inclusion does not certify complete coverage or uniform review for those other volumes. The legacy item formerly labeled as a Band I alternate remains identified as Band IX. All 88 files of the preceding edition are preserved unchanged. The new default preview is the Band I historical source reader.

The recovery archive also includes RECOVERY_ASSEMBLY.json, its cover pages and recovery_assembly.py. Extract the older content-addressed object archives and the new recovery archive into one directory; run the script there to assemble the three Band I readers. All component identities were checked against the retained public manifest. Band II correction inputs and integration bindings remain separately identified.
