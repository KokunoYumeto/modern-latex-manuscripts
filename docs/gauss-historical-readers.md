# Gauss historical readers and English translations

This collection brings together modern LaTeX working reconstructions of Carl Friedrich Gauss's Werke and individual papers, with historical source transcriptions, faithful English translations, editable sources, source images and separately identified editorial commentary. It makes the historical texts available for reading and further correction while retaining their mathematical objects, statements, formulas, arguments and order. Coverage and review status vary by component; this is not a claim that all collected works are transcribed or translated completely.

The retained collection includes work on Bands I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, IX and XI Part I, individual papers and selected passages, earlier source snapshots and repair packages. Its subjects include number theory, analysis, geometry and geodesy, astronomy and related work represented in those volumes. The Band IV German/Latin geodesy transcription and its TeX sources, earlier Band II reading materials through printed page 303, and individual-paper source/English pairs remain available. The legacy reader once mislabeled as a Band I alternate remains identified as Band IX. All 71 files of the preceding edition are retained unchanged.

Gauss and the identified historical editors are the historical authors. The source transcription and English translation are the principal deliverables. Existing modern apparatus, source corrections, supplementary proofs and extensions by ChatGPT, GPT-6 Astra at Ultra, are supplied separately as editorial commentary. They are not silently substituted into the historical text or attributed to Gauss.

Band I source and English components cover physical leaves 2–79 and 88–483. Recovery of the missing 80–87 leaves remains in progress; no new complete Band I reader is claimed. The current source archives retain the available components, and the earlier public readers remain unchanged. The three newly assembled PDFs in this edition are for Band II, through its final witness leaf 544.


Physical source leaves, printed page numbers and generated PDF pages are different locators. Bookmarks and the source manifest identify the exact component order. Internal covers retain their dated checkpoint statements; the new outer covers and reading guide state the scope of this assembly. Native formula images and the original editable reading layers are retained. A formula missing from PDF text extraction is not automatically missing from the visible reader.

The source manifest contains 56,382 logical paths and 27,480 content-addressed objects. It preserves every logical path of the prior dated supplement and binds the new readers to their component PDFs, exact selected pages, covers and editable materials. Ordinary independently extractable archives from 3 and 5 October together supply the complete manifest. The restoration script verifies hashes and safe paths; the portable assembly recipe reconstructs the readers from their retained components. Earlier TeX, source images, review records and build recipes remain retained. Historical build evidence does not claim that every older recipe was freshly rebuilt or made portable.

Full-volume P43/P45 review and the complete final cold audits remain pending. The supplied review records distinguish actual source comparison, assembly checks, uncertain readings and unresolved editorial questions. Publication makes the retained historical readers available without certifying every glyph or mathematical claim. The main preview is the Band II historical source PDF.
