# Open Logic na medžuslovjanskom jezyku

To jest praktično medžuslovjansko izdanje projekta Open Logic za čitateljev slovjanskyh jezykov, vključajuči ljudij bez znanja anglijskogo. Latinična redakcijska svědočba jest kanonična; kirilica jest deterministično izvedena iz nej i ne jest druga nezavisna translacija.

> To rabotno izdanje jest zapisano v [centralnom katalogu prěvodov projekta Open Logic](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/OpenLogic-translations), ktory oddělno ukazuje točny prijety prefiks i polne izdanja.

## Complete English counterpart

This is a practical Open Logic Interslavic edition for readers of Slavic languages, including readers who may not know English. Latin Interslavic is the editable canonical witness; Cyrillic is generated deterministically and is not an independent translation.

Checkpoint OLP-0198 advances the accepted prefix to 198 of 722 units (524 pending), covering 110,194 of 367,220 source alphabetic tokens (30.01%). The canon contains 400 active concepts across 453 physical revisions. Accepted means installed after structural, formula, terminology, canon and visual checks; pending does not imply that a candidate already exists.

The newly accepted block is OLP-0184 through OLP-0198. Its 55 new canon entries (OLISV-T0346–OLISV-T0400) and two revisions of earlier entries expose exact senses, grammar, cross-Slavic comparanda, evidence, alternatives, uncertainty and supersession. The rationale-bearing dictionary and 400-row expert log are complete for the active canon.

The serial QA reader has 777 pages and stops before OLP-0199. Pages 727–777 were rendered and visually inspected. Seven inherited `??` references remain from the known partial-reader boundary; the new block adds no unresolved reference.

This is a reversible working edition, not a claim of completion, official standardization, native-speaker certification, peer review, or infallibility.
