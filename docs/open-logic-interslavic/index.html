OLP-0182 — Teorija modelov dodavaje model-teoretičny okvir k Open Logic čitanki. Editorialny tekst jasno kazuje, že material jest nepolny i eksperimentalny: nyně on jest adaptacijeju zapisok Aldo Antonelli, bez tem teorije, polnoty i kompaktnosti, ktore už sųt pokryte v logikě prvogo reda. Tekst takože označaje potrěbu obširnějšego vvedenja, motivacije, objasnjenja i zadanj, aby čest byla koristna za učebnik.

Complete English counterpart

OLP-0182 — Model Theory adds the model-theory frame to the Open Logic reader. Its editorial text explicitly says that the material is incomplete and experimental: it is currently an adaptation of notes by Aldo Antonelli, excluding theory, completeness, and compactness already covered in first-order logic. It also records the need for broader introduction, motivation, explanation, and exercises before the part can serve as a textbook.

182 of 722 units are translated and reviewed; 540 remain. The accepted prefix covers 101,712 of 367,220 source alphabetic tokens. The canon contains 344 unique concepts across 395 physical records. Continuation 0037 adds nine entries, OLISV-T0336–OLISV-T0344, with sense, grammar, comparisons, alternatives, evidence, confidence, and revision lineage.

The serial QA reader has 731 pages and includes OLP-0182 on pages 727–728; it deliberately stops before untranslated OLP-0183. Pages 726–729, including both adjacent controls, were rendered and inspected. The complete 731-page PDF is QA evidence, not a claim that the whole book is finished.

Teorija modelov · OLP-0182

Materialy o teoriji modelov sųt nepolne i eksperimentalne. Nyně one sųt prosto adaptacijeju zapisok, ktore napisal Aldo Antonelli, s izključenjem tem, pokrytyh v česti o logikě prvogo reda (teorije, polnota, kompaktnost). Potrěbujut mnogo obširnějšego vvedenja, motivacije i objasnjenja, a takože zadanj, aby byli koristne za učebnik. Andy Arana planuje osoblivo rabotati nad tutoju čestju (issue #65).

The source's caveat is retained rather than hidden: this accepted unit is a faithful, readable Interslavic rendering of an explicitly incomplete editorial wrapper. The pending basics chapter is not silently represented as translated.

Model Theory · English source counterpart

The material on model theory is incomplete and experimental. It currently consists simply of adaptations of notes written by Aldo Antonelli, excluding topics covered in the first-order logic section (theories, completeness, compactness). It needs a much broader introduction, motivation, and explanation, as well as exercises, to be useful as a textbook. Andy Arana plans to work specifically on this part (issue #65).

The source caveat is preserved rather than concealed: this accepted unit is a readable rendering of an explicitly incomplete editorial wrapper. The pending basics chapter is not represented as translated.
