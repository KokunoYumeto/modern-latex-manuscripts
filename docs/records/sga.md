## How To Read This Record

Open the eight English reader PDFs first. Their numeric prefixes place SGA1
through SGA6 in sequence; SGA2 R8 remains the default preview. Primary
editable TeX files are direct downloads. Recursive source, diagrams, ledgers,
QA, bounded checkpoints, and predecessor maps are grouped into thirteen ZIPs.

SGA1 and SGA4 proper are complete in translation scope as working readers but
are not yet exhaustively convention-v2 certified. SGA3 remains incomplete:
the cumulative reader reaches complete Expose III, and Exposes IV and V are
separate complete working readers. Exposes VI--XXVI remain untranslated.
Expose V has 66 temporary source-PDF-derived diagram PNGs whose native or
faithful Loop-2 replacement remains open. SGA4half remains rights-held.
Historical versions remain immutable.

Corrections, source comparisons, LaTeX fixes, and translation improvements can
be suggested through GitHub:
<https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/modern-latex-manuscripts>.
