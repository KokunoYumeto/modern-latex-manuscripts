## How To Read This Record

Open the four English reader PDFs first. SGA2 R8 is the default preview. The
primary editable TeX files are direct downloads; source closure, ledgers,
visual QA, bounded checkpoints, and predecessor maps are grouped into eight
ZIPs.

SGA2 R8, SGA5 R9, and SGA6 use their current reference-linked successors.
SGA1 is explicitly a convenience reader: its translation remains in progress
and it has not received the exhaustive internal-reference retrofit. SGA3 is
incomplete and absent, SGA4 proper is fail-closed, and SGA4half remains
rights-held. Historical versions remain immutable.

Corrections, source comparisons, LaTeX fixes, and translation improvements can
be suggested through GitHub:
<https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/modern-latex-manuscripts>.
