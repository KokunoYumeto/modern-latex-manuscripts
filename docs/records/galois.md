## Source Authority

- `galois_1897_oeuvres_ia_600ppi_jp2.zip`: printed `Oeuvres`, 96 JP2 page images. Internet Archive metadata reports 600 PPI; sampled JP2 dimensions are high resolution, but individual JP2 files do not embed DPI.
- `galois_manuscripts_umich_600ppi_tif.zip`: University of Michigan manuscript collection, 78 TIFF page images; sample TIFF embeds 600 x 600 pixels per inch.

Derivative PDFs and OCR text are convenience/locator files only. They are not source authority for formula transcription, tables, marginalia, cancellations, or manuscript notation.

## Workflow Rule

Use the 600-PPI image masters for source-critical readings. OCR text and derivative PDFs may be used to locate prose, headings, or approximate page regions, but important mathematical and manuscript details must be checked against the image masters before promotion.
