# P. G. Lejeune Dirichlet

Zenodo record: [22873449](https://zenodo.org/records/22873449); [stable concept DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20520679).

Public title: P. G. Lejeune Dirichlet: Werke Band II Source-Witnessed Working Drafts and English Translations

The collection retains Band II Papers I–XLI working readers, source files, scans, correspondence, notices and the R23 repair queue. The 2026-09-21 edition adds complete Band I Paper IV French and English pp.65–98, copy63–64 and separate apparatus. Dirichlet is the author of the mathematics; transcription and translation are AI-assisted. Read the [whole-author map](../dirichlet-map.md) for distinctions between current readers and historical drafts.

**Current reading text for Paper IV:** the new 34-page French and English PDFs below supersede the partial R27/R28 readers. All seven prior files remain unchanged as collection contents and provenance. Their Public Summary describes the older edition. Band II XXV still requires complete formula-level repair and XXVII a typed German source track; this release does not certify either collected volume or constitute independent human peer review.

## Readers, editable sources and audit

The default Zenodo preview is the complete Paper IV English reader. [Editable LaTeX and every public edition file](../../sources/dirichlet/Band_I_Paper_IV_20260921/edition/) are also available directly on GitHub. The complete archive contains current and historical source bytes, full and scoped source scans, correction snapshots, page-by-page audit evidence, manifests and build scripts. All 73 final reader pages were compiled and inspected locally; the final source pass records 21 further corrections, 93 cumulative.
