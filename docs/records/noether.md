## Current Head

The current fronted German/source-control PDF is [`00_Noether_German_Cumulative_Working_SourceControl_R813_v12_20260710.pdf`](https://zenodo.org/records/21300773/files/00_Noether_German_Cumulative_Working_SourceControl_R813_v12_20260710.pdf). It compiles to 466 pages.

The current grouped source-control package is [`07_Noether_Current_SourceControl_Integrated_v12_R805_R813_20260710.zip`](https://zenodo.org/records/21300773/files/07_Noether_Current_SourceControl_Integrated_v12_R805_R813_20260710.zip). It integrates and independently checks R805-R813 on top of v11. The final semantic delta is confined to Paper 19: R805 resolves the printed p57 terminal family; R809 replaces the temporary R807 early-band reading; R810 resolves the p65-p66 class-decomposition family; R812 was reconstructed from the R811/R813 delta and checked against p61-p64 witnesses; and R813 restores three source-backed emphases on pp.65-66.

The superseded R796, R804/v11, and narrow R805 current-control files were removed from the latest surface to avoid presenting several competing heads. They remain preserved in earlier immutable Zenodo versions. The current v12 package is source-control evidence, not Noether closure, multilingual synchronization, or a critical edition.
