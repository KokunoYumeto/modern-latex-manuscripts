# Pierre Deligne

GitHub is the primary public reader/source repository. Zenodo record [22208089](https://zenodo.org/records/22208089) preserves this version; concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.20410853](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20410853). Predecessor: [22178773](https://zenodo.org/records/22178773).

**Quality warning:** These are maintained mathematical editions, not a claim of critical-edition certification. Authority scans, source witnesses, apparatus and audit ledgers remain available for checking disputed readings.

## Current coverage

The independently audited sparse corpus includes D001-D016, D018, D021-D023, D025-D030, D034-D036, D038-D040, D043. This version adds complete D013, *Les intersections complètes de niveau de Hodge un*, after D012 and before D014. Its separate English and French editions each have 14 pages. Every later work page retains exact raster and extracted-text identity.

The public gaps are D017, D019-D020, D024, D031-D033, D037, D041-D042. They identify works not included in this version, not missing downloads. Later completed gaps will be inserted numerically without removing later complete works. Other complete returned works not included in this version include D017, D019, D031, D033, D042, D045, D047, D049 and D050; returned completeness is distinct from independent corpus acceptance and publication.

Partial accepted coverage: D020 30/36 physical pages (next P06, physical 31-36 / printed 302-307); D032 66/117 (next P12, physical 67-72 / printed 202-207); D037 12/30 (next P03, physical 13-18 / printed 101-106); D044 54/174 dispositions (next P10, physical 55-60 / printed 54-59 after companion binding). Unaccepted later drafts do not advance these cursors.
