# Pierre Deligne

GitHub is the primary public reader/source repository. Zenodo record [22211312](https://zenodo.org/records/22211312) preserves this version; concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.20410853](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20410853). Predecessor: [22208089](https://zenodo.org/records/22208089).

**Quality warning:** These are maintained mathematical editions, not a claim of critical-edition or mathematical-proof certification. Authority scans, witnesses, apparatus and audit ledgers support checking disputed readings.

## Current coverage

The independently audited sparse corpus includes D001-D016, D018, D021-D023, D025-D031, D034-D036, D038-D040, D043. This version adds complete D031, *Variétés de Shimura: interprétation modulaire, et techniques de construction de modèles canoniques*, after D030 and before D034. Its source-French and standalone English readers each have 43 pages, printed247-289. No page290 is invented. Retained work pages preserve exact raster and extracted-text identity.

The public gaps are D017, D019-D020, D024, D032-D033, D037, D041-D042. They identify works not included in this version, not missing downloads. Later completed gaps are inserted numerically without removing later complete works. Other complete returned works not included in this version: D017, D019, D033, D042, D045, D047, D049, D050. Returned completeness is distinct from independent corpus acceptance and publication.

Local partial-work cursors (not complete papers included in this version): D020: 30/36 physical; next P06 physical 31-36 / printed 302-307; D032: 72/117 physical; nextP13 physical73-78 / printed208-213; D037: 12/30; next P03 physical 13-18 / printed 101-106; D044: 54/174 physical dispositions; next P10 physical 55-60 / printed 54-59 after companion-trio binding. Unaccepted later drafts do not advance these cursors.
