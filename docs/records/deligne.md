## Current Coverage

The independently audited sparse corpus includes D001-D012, D014, D015,
D021, D025-D029, D036, and D040. Public gaps do not block later complete
works: D013, D017-D020, D022-D024, D030-D035, and D037-D039 remain absent,
while D016 remains an unpublished partial lane at 66 of 97 authority pages. A
later audited gap work will be inserted into its numerical position without
removing later complete works.

The English cumulative reader has 364 pages and the source-language cumulative
reader has 374 pages. D028, D029, D036, and D040 were independently normalized,
rendered, visually checked, deterministically rebuilt, and cold-audited before
inclusion. D040 is the complete eight-page joint paper *Duality for
Representations of a Reductive Group over a Finite Field*.
