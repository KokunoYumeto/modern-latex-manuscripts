Mathematics 2: Sinugbuanong Binisaya Unit 1 Learner’s Material
View Download

Learning Material, Learning Guide, Learning Module  |  PDF

Published on 2013 September 10th

Description
This material consists of lessons and activity sheets for understanding key concepts and skills in Grade 2 Mathematics.
Objective
1. Understand relationships between numbers and place value of whole numbers up to 1,000 and of ordinal numbers up to 20th.
2. Compute using the four operations of whole numbers and the identity and zero properties of multiplication.
3. Understand concepts of unit fractions and similar fractions and identify money value up to 100.
4. Visualize and draw geometric shapes.
5. Apply learned strategies in completing patterns.
6. Apply concepts of time and use standard units in measuring length, mass, area and capacity.
7. Make diagrams and present data using scales.

Education Type K to 12
Grade Level Grade 2
Learning Area Mathematics
Content/Topic Numbers and Number Sense
Intended Users Learners, Students
