Mathematics 1: Sinugbuanong Binisaya Unit 1 Learner’s Material
View Download

Learning Material  |  Textbook  |  PDF

Published on 2014 June 19th

Description
This material consists of lessons and activity sheets for understanding key concepts and skills in Grade 1 Mathematics.
Objective
1. Apply the concept of cardinal and ordinal numbers up to 100 in various contexts.
2. Solve addition and subtraction problems.

Curriculum Information

Education Type K to 12
Grade Level Grade 1
Learning Area Mathematics
Content/Topic Numbers and Number Sense
Intended Users Learners
Competencies Creates situations involving addition of whole numbers including money Illustrates subtraction as taking away or comparing elements of sets Counts groups of equal quantity using concrete objects up to 50 and writes an equivalent expression. e.g groups of 5
