# Source classes and official anchors

Accessed 2026-08-14.

Shared encoding and markup:

- Unicode, [UTR #25: Unicode Support for Mathematics](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr25/).
- W3C, [MathML 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/mathml4/) (Working Draft; work-in-progress status retained).

Chinese:

- W3C, [Requirements for Chinese Text Layout](https://www.w3.org/TR/clreq/).
- PRC National Standard, [GB/T 3102.11-1993](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=3DE79450D562E62D41CB6E79FF411054).
- PRC National Standard, [GB/T 15834-2011](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=22EA6D162E4110E752259661E1A0D0A8).
- National Committee for Terms in Sciences and Technologies, [《数学名词》（1993）](https://www.cnterm.cn/sd/xxjs/zwh/sx/sdml/202311/t20231128_761286.html).
- Taiwan NAER, [數學名詞（第四版）](https://teric.naer.edu.tw/wSite/ct?ctNode=645&mp=teric&xItem=1849186).
- Hong Kong Education Bureau, [數學科常用英漢辭彙](https://www.edb.gov.hk/tc/curriculum-development/kla/ma/res/glossary.html).
- Macao DSEDJ, historical [高中數學大綱](https://www.dsedj.gov.mo/crdc/course/Senior/SMath.htm) and the [2017 senior-secondary mathematics curriculum guide](https://www.dsedj.gov.mo/crdc/guide/data/senior/Math_guide_c.pdf).

Japanese:

- W3C, [Requirements for Japanese Text Layout](https://www.w3.org/TR/jlreq/).
- Mathematical Society of Japan, [岩波「数学辞典」の歴史](https://www.mathsoc.jp/overview/history/dictionary/dictionary.html).
- Mathematical Society of Japan, [「数学」publication guidance](https://www.mathsoc.jp/publications/sugaku/).

Korean:

- Korean Agency for Technology and Standards, [KS A ISO80000-2](https://standard.go.kr/KSCI/standardIntro/getStandardSearchView.do?ksNo=KSAISO80000-2&reformNo=02&tmprKsNo=KSAISO80000-2).
- Korean Mathematical Society, [수학용어](https://www.kms.or.kr/mathdict/list.html).
- W3C, [Korean Script Resources](https://www.w3.org/TR/kore-lreq/) and [Requirements for Hangul Text Layout and Typography](https://www.w3.org/TR/klreq/).

Project artifacts are identified by bytes and SHA-256 in `evidence.jsonl`. Their short artifact labels intentionally omit machine-local absolute paths from the public package.
