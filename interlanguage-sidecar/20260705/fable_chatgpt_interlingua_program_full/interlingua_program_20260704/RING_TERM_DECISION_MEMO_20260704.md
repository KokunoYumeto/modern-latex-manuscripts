## Options with access-ledger assessment

**A. Keep `kolco` (status quo, East continuity).**
main_register_retention: high for East readers; marginal_inter_intelligibility_gain over Russian-only: near zero for W/S readers (form is East-specific); dominance_penalty: HIGH — this is the "rough Russian" failure mode instantiated in the corpus's most frequent term; cross_standard_recognition: East only.

**B. Switch to `prsten` (South + everyday-word transparency).**
Gains hr/sr/bg (+possibly the community's general-language lexeme); loses East transparency less than expected (uk перстень / ru перстень exist as 'ring (jewellery)' — recognizable but wrong register cue: jewellery, not algebra); pl pierścień is the SAME lexeme family → covers Polish too. Coverage: South + Polish strong, East partial-with-register-caveat, cs/sk weak (okruh unrelated).
**Note: prsten/pierścień is the largest single-lexeme coalition in the family (pl + hr + sr + bg + everyday recognizability elsewhere).**

**C. Switch to `okruh`.**
cs/sk only; collides with uk округ / ru округ 'district' — a real false friend across East; reject on collision grounds (adverse evidence, not just weak support).

**D. Keep `kolco` in text + mandatory first-use doublet and glossary line: `kolco (prsten, okruh)`.**
Preserves 1182pp of rendered continuity; makes the branch variants visible at every entry point; cheapest; but keeps the East form as the running surface — dominance penalty reduced, not removed.

**E. Adopt `prsten` as running surface + first-use doublet `prsten (kolco)`; okruh in the glossary crosswalk.**
Maximizes lexeme-coalition coverage (B) while keeping East readers anchored via doublet; requires a corpus-wide term migration (mechanical: the 41-row family is enumerated; rebuild + rerender is the established pipeline); register caveat: prsten's everyday sense is 'finger-ring' in South too — exactly like German *Ring* and English *ring*, i.e. the metaphor is the SAME one the source tradition uses, which is an argument FOR it, not against.

## Recommendation (for review, not enacted)
**E as target, D as interim.** Rationale: the source tradition itself (Ring/anneau/anello/ring) uses the everyday-ring metaphor; prsten reproduces that metaphor with the largest cross-branch lexeme coalition (pl+hr+sr+bg) and passive recognizability elsewhere; kolco survives as doublet for East readers and full continuity with the published renders. C is rejected on typed adverse evidence (okrug collision). A is the measured dominance failure mode. If the Interslavic community dictionary already fixes the algebra sense to a specific lexeme, that ruling wins — authority separation stands.
