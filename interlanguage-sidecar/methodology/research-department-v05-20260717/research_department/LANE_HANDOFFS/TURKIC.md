# Turkic research handoff

## Manager scope

The manager translates every work into the selected Turkic target standards and coordinates cross-Turkic terminology and script research. It must preserve real Oghuz, Kipchak, Karluk, Siberian, and other sublane boundaries rather than treating “Turkic” as one language.

## Current research state

- The programme’s earlier Pan-Turkic construction lane was source-blocked under its original machine-readable-source requirement.
- The correct response is format relaxation and native-source intake, including PDFs and institutional archives, not model-generated filling.
- Unified v6.2 contains a small `turkic_source_canon` set and a frontier set with explicit gaps. Its values are routing data, not evidence of lane readiness.
- Alphabet harmonization may be a useful script-bridge layer, but it is not a substitute for separate living-language terminology and translation.

## Required controls

- Declare target languages and subbranches for every tranche.
- Distinguish Turkish volume from cross-Turkic breadth; repeated Turkish sources may not dominate the family model.
- Maintain Arabic-, Cyrillic-, Latin-, and other script histories and current standards explicitly where relevant.
- Do not transliterate a form and count it as a new language witness.
- Separate inherited cognates, recent internationalisms, Russian-mediated loans, Persian/Arabic loans, and local terminology in the evidence graph.
- Apply source-floor and sense-review gates before any pan-Turkic recommendation.
- Use a symmetric or explicitly justified cohort tree if branch weighting is attempted.

## Manager output

The default production unit is an ordinary translation into a named standard plus a cross-Turkic concept crosswalk. A zonal or controlled bridge candidate remains exploratory until native sources, subgroup reception, script policy, and human comprehension are demonstrated.
