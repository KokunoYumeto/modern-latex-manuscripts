# Language-manager research handoffs

These eight files correspond to the persistent language-group managers. They are not separate work-specific translation tasks.

Every manager is responsible for translating **any work** into its target languages, including Noether, SGA, and source material not yet in English. The manager maintains the relevant source corpus, native examples, concept web, terminology and adverse-evidence ledgers, script/register invariants, worker return criteria, and decision history.

Shared method:

- `..\INTERLINGUISTIC_METHOD_SYNTHESIS.md`
- `..\MATHEMATICAL_OBJECTS_AND_AUDIT.md`
- `..\CLAIM_STATUS_REGISTER.json`
- `..\OPERATIONAL_DECISION_INTERFACE.schema.json`

Administrative grouping does not imply linguistic unity. CJK, Africa, Pacific, Arabic/Persianate, and similar managers must maintain real sublane boundaries and must not manufacture pan-language claims from a shared manager label.
