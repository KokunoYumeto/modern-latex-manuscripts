# Spanish / French / Romance research handoff

## Manager scope

The manager translates every work into the relevant Romance targets and maintains the Pan-Romance comparison/bridge research. French and Spanish are first-class target and source languages; the lane is not “Spanish plus variants.”

French is a triple interlock:

- source language for SGA;
- target language in the Noether corpus;
- major Romance witness and proof-register source.

Preserve those roles separately in provenance.

## Current research state

- The Pan-Romance C2 pass contains 27 internally witnessed concepts after row/context review, with specialist gaps left open rather than guessed.
- `covariant` and `contravariant` were demoted because observed contexts were tensor/differential-geometry or category-theory senses, not the intended invariant-theory sense.
- `binary form`, `complete system`, and `ground form` produced false-sense hits and were rejected for those contexts.
- `modulus` was sense-resolved to the module concept in the relevant Noether material; modulo-arithmetic hits are excluded.
- Spanish `kernel` hits from kernel-density statistics are excluded; `núcleo` and French `noyau` are the relevant witnesses in the audited contexts.
- Six specialist Noether gaps remain source-intake questions, including transvection and specialized form-system terminology.
- Unified v6.2’s 98.5 Pan-Romance score is not readiness: it combines 139 support rows with 401 candidates and ignores hard gates.

## Required controls

- Maintain Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Catalan, Galician, Romanian, Romansh, and other included standards as explicit sublanes.
- Test and report dominance against both Spanish volume and French source prestige.
- Treat Latin ancestry and internationalism as candidate evidence, not proof of current living usage.
- Keep Latin/source-history, native-language witness, and constructed Pan-Romance proposal on separate axes.
- Run sense checks on every F14 trap and context checks on every phrase-level hit.
- Do not sum diacritic-folded forms or spelling variants as independent evidence.
- Use the global-register column only as a comparator: a Pan-Romance form earns promotion when it provides marginal Romance intelligibility beyond the global/international candidate.

## Production handoff rule

Each work-specific worker receives the current target-language terminology decisions plus source-language sense anchors. Returns must identify deviations, new native examples, and cross-target inconsistencies. The manager adjudicates the web across all works rather than allowing SGA, Noether, or another project to grow isolated terminologies.
