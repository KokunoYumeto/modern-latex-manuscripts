# Arabic / Persianate / RTL research handoff

## Manager scope

One manager coordinates every-work translation into Arabic and selected Persianate/RTL targets, but the linguistic sublanes remain separate. Arabic, Persian, Dari, Tajik, and other targets do not witness one another merely because of historical contact or shared script.

## Required controls

- Maintain Arabic and Persianate concept, source, and decision records separately.
- Distinguish Modern Standard Arabic, historical Arabic, regional standards, and pedagogical registers where relevant.
- Distinguish Persian, Dari, Tajik, and any additional target by current usage and script.
- Treat canonical German/French/English mathematical sources as source normalization only.
- Preserve RTL direction, bidi isolation, formula direction, punctuation, embedded Latin identifiers, citations, numerals, line breaks, and font support as tested invariants.
- Never infer a shared living-language term from a common Arabic-script spelling without language-specific context.
- Apply the F14 sense traps and retain false-sense forms as adverse evidence.

## Manager output

For every work, return named-target translations and a crosswalk of related or borrowed terminology. A shared manager may reuse tooling and source indexes, but each language’s witness and review status remains independent.
