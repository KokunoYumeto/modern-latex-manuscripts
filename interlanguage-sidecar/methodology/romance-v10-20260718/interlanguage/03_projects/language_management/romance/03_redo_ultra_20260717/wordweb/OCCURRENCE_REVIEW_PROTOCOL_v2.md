## Disposition and sense-link rules

Each occurrence has one primary row disposition: accepted, rejected/adverse,
or held. The one preserved 2021 T57 occurrence is intentionally nonexclusive
at the sense level: it supports ordinary-direction T57-S2 and is adverse to
algebraic right-action T57-S1. Multi-sense links do not create additional
occurrences. In particular, the two accepted T59 rows that each support both
left- and right-zero-divisor senses remain two occurrence records, not four.

An accepted row establishes only that the cited internal source context
realizes the named modeled sense. It does not establish a canonical lemma,
independent-source count, bridge-form choice, branch-wide coverage, or human
comprehension. Accepted navigation, title, macro, or template rows remain
lexical evidence and contribute zero running-body attestations.

Rejected rows remain searchable adverse evidence. A form-admission adverse
target is distinct from a claim that the form can never realize the modeled
sense. Cross-sense adverse links are retained separately from the primary row
disposition so an accepted directional or identity-element occurrence cannot
be reused under an algebraic action, identity-map, algebraic-identity, or
invertible-element sense.

Held rows support neither acceptance nor rejection. Code comments, unused
macros, table-of-contents entries, unresolved historical field names, and
unsided zero-divisor contexts remain typed holds. Evidence-family and
cross-occurrence identifiers prevent template, parallel-article, shared-line,
and near-parallel contexts from being summed as independent witnesses.
