\section*{What this release adds}

Romance v10 binds the existing Romance corpus and WordWeb infrastructure to the
current Spanish and French Noether R823 terminology ledgers. It corrects six
semantic links, retains every prior attestation and adverse result, and adds no
unearned language evidence. The package is designed to be rerunnable: its
machine gate, manifest, builders, ledgers, source bodies, and bounded production
outputs are included together.

\section*{Four-stage state}

\textbf{A. Provenance and branch acquisition: not complete.}
The route ledger records 61 explicit routes. Eight have active bodies; 53 remain
zero-body acquisition gaps. Rumantsch Grischun has three verified general school-
mathematics sources but no specialist-algebra body. Five regional Romansh idioms
remain separate zero-body routes and are not represented by a dominant standard.

\textbf{B. Consolidated corpus: current tranche passes.}
Corpus v3 contains 148 representations, 142 primary unique records, six
representation aliases, 66 counting-eligible records, and five excluded records.
Passing this structural tranche does not close the documented branch-depth gaps.

\textbf{C. Semantic alignment and access infrastructure: structurally passes,
not human validated.}
The effective-link contract covers 104 controlled terminology rows and corrects
six links. The Spanish/French production crosswalk contains 194 rows. It maps only
pinned or unique exact identities and creates no new attestation. Thirty-three
senses still lack accepted corpus support.

\textbf{D. Controlled creation: six bounded tranches pass technical checks.}
T001--T006 cover German authority lines 21047--21254. Their TeX builds, final PDF
copies, and 15 pinned render pages reproduce. They have not been validated by
native speakers. The next authority cursor is line 21256.

\section*{The empirical boundary}

The 954 access-grid rows are 106 senses crossed with nine declared cohorts. All
human-result fields remain empty, every row is pilot-ineligible, and there are no
form promotions. Orthographic proxy values are design diagnostics only. They do
not measure intelligibility and do not authorize vocabulary or grammar choices.

A future human protocol must collect participant-level language or variety,
mathematical literacy, cross-Romance exposure, randomized item order, responses
and abstentions, latency, confidence, uncertainty, consent, and review metadata.
Until such observations exist, no empirical marginal-intelligibility index,
scalar-readiness claim, or controlled-form promotion is warranted.

\section*{How the archive is organized}

The reader-facing PDF is accompanied by one structured evidence ZIP. Inside that
ZIP, the original workspace-relative relationship among the Romance lane, its
lane-control and report directories, and the canonical output PDFs is preserved
so that the relative paths in the v10 manifest continue to resolve. The ZIP
contains the exact manifest targets rather than an indiscriminate workspace dump.

The broader Zenodo record also retains earlier source-body baselines, the weighted
Interslavic automaton and term ledgers, v0.4/v0.5 methodology material, and the
v0.6 Interslavic normalization evidence. Version labels distinguish those earlier
components from this Romance v10 update.
