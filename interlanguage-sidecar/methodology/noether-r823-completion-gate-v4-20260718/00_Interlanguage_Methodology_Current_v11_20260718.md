# Interlanguage and Mathematical Translation Methodology

## Current public map

This concept record is the methodology and reproducibility sidecar for the
AI-run mathematical translation program. It preserves the interlanguage source
baselines, weighted-automaton and terminology work, manager handoffs,
Interslavic normalization evidence, Romance-language infrastructure, reusable
scripts, and bounded translation experiments. Actual author and series readers
remain on their author or series records.

The current record has two principal active method layers:

1. **Romance v10:** 61 explicit language or variety routes, 60 WordWeb
   concepts, 106 senses, 682 reviewed occurrence records, a 194-row
   Spanish/French terminology crosswalk, and six bounded controlled-Romance
   production tranches. This is research infrastructure, not a completed
   Romance translation or an empirical intelligibility result.
2. **Noether R823 completion gate v4:** an executable 35-check release gate for
   Spanish and French full-corpus translation candidates. The Spanish replay
   passes against the exact current 473-page candidate; French remains a moving
   production lane and is not certified by that result.

## Spanish R823 gate result

The v4 gate binds all 81 declared units: Papers 1-43, the long-form book title
and Sections 1-31, both post-45 units, bibliography, notices, reviews, and books
with Noether. It also verifies:

- exact German authority and expanded-target hashes;
- byte-exact reconstruction of the source-parity ledger;
- all 81 direct reconciliation records and their support files;
- 101 terminology decisions, including 68 independent hash-pinned native
  Spanish TeX line witnesses and 33 excluded target-only citations;
- a current 473-page build and recorded TeX dependency graph;
- fresh Poppler derivation of every page at 120 dpi;
- exact normalized-pixel equality between the candidate PDF and all stored
  render evidence;
- a structured all-page visual-review decision and readable final audit;
- end-of-run snapshot stability.

The adversarial replay rejects target-only evidence, stale manifests, arbitrary
rehashed pixels, stale review records, weak audit labels, and hand-edited parity
output.

## Evidence boundary

The gate is intentionally narrower than scholarly certification. A pass means
that the declared scope, evidence graph, build, and render artifacts are
internally bound and reproducible. It does not prove native-language quality,
semantic perfection, mathematical correctness, peer review, or critical-edition
status. The Noether reader remains a source-reconciled working translation and
important passages should be checked against the German authority and scans.
