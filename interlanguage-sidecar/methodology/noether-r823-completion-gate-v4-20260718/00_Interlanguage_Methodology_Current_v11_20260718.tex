\section*{Romance v10 remains infrastructure}

Romance v10 records 61 explicit routes, 60 concepts, 106 senses, 682 reviewed
occurrences, a 194-row Spanish/French terminology crosswalk, and six bounded
controlled-Romance tranches. It is not a completed Romance translation or an
empirical intelligibility result. Fifty-three routes still have no source body,
33 senses have no accepted support, and the access grid contains no human
observations or form promotions.

\section*{Noether R823 completion gate v4}

The executable gate covers Papers 1--43, the long-form book title and Sections
1--31, both post-45 units, bibliography, notices, reviews, and books with
Noether. The Spanish candidate passes all 35 checks against the exact verified
German authority and current 473-page reader.

The gate verifies:

\begin{itemize}
  \item authority, expanded-target, unit, and whole-document hashes;
  \item byte-exact parity-ledger reconstruction from 81 direct reviews;
  \item every evidence record and every declared support artifact;
  \item 101 terminology decisions, of which 68 have independent native Spanish
        TeX line witnesses with matching files, hashes, and line ranges;
  \item structural omission tripwires, a successful current build, and the
        recorded TeX dependency graph;
  \item a readable final audit bound into every unit evidence record;
  \item fresh 120-dpi Poppler derivation of all 473 candidate pages;
  \item normalized-pixel equality between that fresh render and all stored
        render evidence;
  \item a structured all-page review decision and final snapshot stability.
\end{itemize}

The adversarial replay additionally proves that target-only terminology
citations, unpinned TeX lines, stale manifests, arbitrary rehashed images, stale
review records, weak audit labels, and hand-edited parity output do not pass.

\section*{What the gate does not prove}

A passing gate is evidence-integrity and completeness control, not a linguistic
or mathematical oracle. It does not certify native Spanish quality, semantic
perfection, peer review, or critical-edition status. The Spanish artifact is a
source-reconciled working translation. Important passages should still be
checked against the German authority and source scans. The French R823 lane is
moving and is not certified by this Spanish result.
