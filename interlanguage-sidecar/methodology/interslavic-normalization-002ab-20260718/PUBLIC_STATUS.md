# Public status: Interslavic normalization methodology through Tranche 007

This record publishes research method, evidence-graph and automata audits,
language-lane handoffs, source-body baselines, and reproducible normalization
materials for the mathematical-translation programme.

This addition records controlled Interslavic normalization across the complete
extant Noether production corpus: 221 Latin and 221 Cyrillic canonical TeX
units. Tranches 002A-007 cover reviewed orthography, exact lexical surfaces,
the correspondence family, simultaneity policy, four small contextual queues,
an algebraic-ring competitor, and one final diacritic correction. Tranches
003-007 record 1,111 replacements across 247 distinct units. Each pass retains
exact ledgers, diffs, build/render reports, QA adjudication, continuation state,
and scripts.

The final streaming audit covers 221/221 canonical Latin units and closes the
internally resolvable declared queue. The sole explicit external-authority
blocker is the abstract mathematical sense of 353 `važi*|važe*` probes in 101
files.

The actual translation bodies and cumulative readers are published under the
Noether concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.20412587`.

Status is conservative: internally normalized working translations with
verified artifact/build integrity. This is not community certification,
native-speaker approval, a critical edition, peer review, or mathematical
source-fidelity certification. Held and context-sensitive terminology remains
open and is documented in the package.
