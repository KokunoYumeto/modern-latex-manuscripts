# Public status: Interlanguage methodology sidecar v0.6

This record publishes research method, evidence-graph and automata audits,
language-lane handoffs, source-body baselines, and reproducible normalization
materials for the mathematical-translation programme.

The v0.6 addition records controlled Interslavic normalization across the
complete extant Noether production corpus: 221 Latin and 221 Cyrillic canonical
TeX units. Tranche 002A changed 146 files under reviewed orthographic rules;
Tranche 002B changed 92 files under exact lexical rules. Both passes were
idempotent and retained ledgers, diffs, build reports, render reports, visual QA
sheets, continuation state, and scripts.

The actual translation bodies and cumulative readers are published on the
Noether record `10.5281/zenodo.21421049` under concept DOI
`10.5281/zenodo.20412587`.

Status is conservative: internally normalized working translations with
verified artifact/build integrity. This is not community certification,
native-speaker approval, a critical edition, peer review, or mathematical
source-fidelity certification. Held and context-sensitive terminology remains
open and is documented in the package.
