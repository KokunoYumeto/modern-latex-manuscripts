# Final large-family review

Date: 2026-07-18

## Internally resolvable item

The two Latin `prsten` tokens (and two paired Cyrillic `прстен` tokens) in Paper 25 both denote an algebraic ring, not a physical ring. The same corpus uses the `kolco` family roughly 792 times for this technical concept. These four tokens can therefore be normalized to `kolco` / `колцо` without external authority.

## Families not changed

- `ręd*` is a productive, polysemous and corpus-entrenched family covering row, order, sequence/series, and technical “series of forms” senses. A root-wide replacement would collapse distinct senses. The forms are not shown to be invalid; no normalization target is internally justified.
- `jednako*` includes inflected “equal/same” meanings as well as possible connective/adverbial uses. The old substring probe conflated them. No root-wide edit is justified.
- `slučaj*` is the corpus-primary spelling after tranche 005 and is a valid case/instance family. The former “wording” concern does not itself establish a defect or a single better target.
- `kolc*` is the corpus-primary mathematical-ring terminology and is retained. Only the isolated same-sense competitor is normalized.

## External-authority blocker

`važi*` / `važe*` is consistently used for mathematical “holds/applies/is valid”, but the available dictionary evidence did not settle that abstract sense extension across the intended Slavic breadth. Replacing hundreds of occurrences with a guessed verb would be a semantic rewrite, not spelling normalization. This family remains the only identified normalization question requiring additional language authority; it is not called erroneous or incomplete translation.

This classification is typed editorial evidence, not community certification.
