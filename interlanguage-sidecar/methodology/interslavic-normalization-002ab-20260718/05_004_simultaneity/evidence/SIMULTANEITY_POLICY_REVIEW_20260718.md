# Simultaneity-family policy review

Date: 2026-07-18

## Decision

Use `jednočasno` as the canonical adverb and `jednočasnom` as the corresponding adjectival form in the normalized Noether corpus. Apply paired Cyrillic `једночасно` / `једночасном`.

Reviewed source families:

- `jednovrěmenno`, `Jednovrěmenno`, `jednovrěmennom`;
- unaccented `jednovremenno` / `Jednovremenno`;
- `istovrěmenno`;
- `samočasno`;
- their extant Cyrillic counterparts.

## Why this does not contradict the dictionary

Both `jednočasno` and `jednovrěmenno` are sanctioned dictionary headwords. The latter is therefore not labelled wrong. The corpus nevertheless needs one editorial default.

The recorded cohort profiles are complementary:

- `jednočasno`: `bg+ hr~ pl+ ru- sk+ sr+ uk+`;
- `jednovrěmenno`: `bg+ hr+ pl- ru+ sk- sr+ uk+`.

For a pan-Slavic intelligibility normalization, `jednočasno` has the broader declared family coverage: it reaches Polish, Slovak, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, and Serbian, with partial Croatian support, while avoiding the Russian-shaped default that caused the original Russian/Ukrainian-heavy calibration concern. The choice is therefore a declared family/cohort breadth policy, not a scalar auto-decision and not community certification.

## Context review

The 148 planned paired-script occurrences all express simultaneity (“at the same time”, “simultaneously”, or an adjectival “simultaneous consideration”). The six `samočasno` and three `istovrěmenno` contexts do not express “only temporally”; they function as simultaneity adverbs. The two `-m` forms modify `razsmotrenje` and require the adjectival target `jednočasnom`, not the adverb.

Preflight coverage: 75 Latin-script and 73 Cyrillic-script replacements across 71 files. The two-script count difference is inherited corpus variation, not a claim of sentence-by-sentence alignment.

## Limit

This is a bounded corpus-normalization policy. It does not invalidate the alternative dictionary headword, certify community usage, or establish independent source-faithfulness of the translations.
