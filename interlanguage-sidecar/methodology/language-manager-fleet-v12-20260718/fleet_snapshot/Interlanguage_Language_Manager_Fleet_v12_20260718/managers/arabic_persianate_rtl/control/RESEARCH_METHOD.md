## Operational interpretation

1. Arabic and Persianate records never witness one another merely because of
   contact history or Arabic-script spelling.
2. The evidence graph records provenance and routing. Its edges are typed and
   its weights, when present, are counts or permitted-use values, never truth
   probabilities.
3. The family/cohort object records dependence and source breadth. The current
   tree has no edge lengths and produces no numeric branch statistic.
4. Translation drafts may generate or stabilize candidates, but they cannot be
   native-language witnesses.
5. `support`, `candidate`, `competitor`, `adverse`, `absence`, and `veto` remain
   distinct. Adverse evidence cannot be averaged away.
6. No scalar auto-promotes a term. W0 remains a projection and unified v6.2
   readiness is rejected.
7. No lane item is described as externally or community certified. There is no
   accepted external review return in the audited programme state.
