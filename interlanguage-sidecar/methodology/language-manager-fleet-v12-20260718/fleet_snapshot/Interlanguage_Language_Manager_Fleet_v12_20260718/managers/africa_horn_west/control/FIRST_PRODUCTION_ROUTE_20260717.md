# First production route — 2026-07-17

## Selection

The first buildable translation tranche is a selected 113-word extract from
OpenStax *Prealgebra 2e*, section 2.1, “Use the Language of Algebra,” translated
independently into Somali (`so-Latn`) and Oromo (`om-Latn-ET`).

This follows the recovered R9 candidate register, which placed the Prealgebra
ladder ahead of advanced Noether sampling for Somali, Oromo, and Tigrinya. The
later source returns supplied independent Somali and Oromo school-mathematics
ledgers with directly relevant variables, constants, expressions, equations,
and equality language.

## Why this route is supportable

- The selected extract stays inside the foundational algebra domain actually
  represented in the recovered Grade 6/8 target-language sources.
- It uses a fixed official source URL and a verified current license page.
- It is short enough to expose terminology and grammar decisions without
  pretending that the entire section or book is complete.
- It produces independent target TeX/PDF outputs and therefore exercises the
  mandatory build/render gate.

## What this route does not mean

- It is not a pilot-readiness claim.
- It is not a promoted term ledger.
- It is not native or community approval.
- It is not a pan-Horn bridge translation.
- It does not remove responsibility for Noether, SGA, or any other work in
  `WORK_REGISTRY_20260717.csv`.
- It does not establish that either target has an adequate higher-algebra or
  proof-prose floor for advanced works.
