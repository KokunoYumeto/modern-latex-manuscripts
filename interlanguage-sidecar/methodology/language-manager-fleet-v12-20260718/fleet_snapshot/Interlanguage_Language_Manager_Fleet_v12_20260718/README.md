# Interlanguage and Mathematical Translation Methodology

## What is public now

This record is the methodology, evidence, and programme-state sidecar for the
AI-run mathematical translation project. Actual author and seminar readers
remain on their author or series records. This version adds a frozen,
human-readable snapshot of the eight language-manager lanes that were active
on 17-18 July 2026. It publishes the manager controls and small genuine work
products without presenting a source corpus, a compiled draft, or a completed
build as scholarly certification.

The principal public payloads are:

- **Noether:** current German R823 source control; English working reader;
  source-reconciled Spanish and French working readers; paired-script
  Interslavic readers; bounded current CJK, Arabic, Persian, and Indonesian
  components. Latest record: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21423112>.
- **SGA 5 and SGA 6:** French workpasses, a 309-page SGA 5 English
  source-checked working translation, a corrected 381-page layered SGA 6
  English reader, and a bounded Spanish SGA 6 Expose X tranche. Latest record:
  <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21422245>.
- **Interlanguage methodology:** source-body baselines, weighted-automaton and
  term ledgers, research handoffs, Interslavic normalization evidence,
  Romance v10 infrastructure, the Noether R823 completion gate, and this
  manager snapshot. Concept DOI: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21124403>.

## What the v0.12 ZIP contains

The file 16_Interlanguage_Language_Manager_Fleet_Snapshot_v12_20260718.zip
contains the stable control ledgers for all eight lanes, their current status
and continuation records, and four small classes of genuine working output:

1. Somali and Oromo OpenStax microtranches with TeX, PDFs, source note, and QA;
2. Arabic and Iranian-Persian Noether Paper 6 opening tranches with TeX, PDFs,
   decision records, bidi checks, and QA;
3. Indonesian Noether Paper 36 TeX and complete source/terminology evidence;
4. Kyrgyz, Uyghur, and Uzbek Hefferon review drafts with deterministic builds
   and render checks.

Large source-body packages and full readers are not duplicated in this new
ZIP: they are already preserved in the earlier numbered packages and on the
Noether and SGA records. The included payload map points to those authorities.

## Evidence boundary

The words *complete*, *pass*, and *current* apply only to the scope declared by
each artifact. A complete one-page tranche is not a complete language edition;
a successful build is not a translation review; an OCR witness is not a source
transcription; and a source-reconciled working reader is not a critical edition.
No file in this release is presented as peer reviewed, universally
mathematically correct, or critically complete.

Corrections and improved evidence can be proposed through issues or pull
requests at <https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/modern-latex-manuscripts>.
