## Arms and passages

Required roles are current v018, official Interslavic, a prospectively fixed dominant-East baseline, a branch-balanced candidate, and a native-language control. Byte-identical roles count once. An unavailable arm receives an explicit absence reason; it is not model-filled.

Every whole-passage arm must share one source passage and exactly the same frozen information units, mathematical propositions, definitions, formulae, names, citations, examples, question semantics, and answer key. Only declared lexical, morphology/morphosyntax, syntax/connective/register, or script contrasts may differ. Each passage must be coherent. Tokenwise mosaics and unilateral glosses, definitions, or doublets are forbidden. Failure excludes the complete matched passage set or stops the affected batch.

Native-language controls calibrate the instrument and participant comprehension. They must be independently authored or sourced and independently reviewed by competent humans. They are not bridge candidates.
