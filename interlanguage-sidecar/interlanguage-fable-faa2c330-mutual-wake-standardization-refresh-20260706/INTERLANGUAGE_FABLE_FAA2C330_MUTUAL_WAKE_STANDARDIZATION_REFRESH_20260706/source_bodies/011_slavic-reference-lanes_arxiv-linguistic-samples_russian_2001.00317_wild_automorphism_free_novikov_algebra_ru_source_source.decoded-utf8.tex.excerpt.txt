L79: A wild automorphism of a free Novikov algebra}
L87: \noindent{\sc Abstract.} An example of a non-triangulable locally nilpotent derivation and an example of a wild exponential automorphism of the free Novikov algebra $N\left\langle x,y,z\right\rangle$ in three variables $x,y,z$ over a field of characteristic zero are constructed.\medskip
