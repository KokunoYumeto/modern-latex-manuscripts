# Tajik algebra source anchor

## Purpose

The interlanguage source map retained an open row for a Tajik abstract-algebra
or native-TeX source. A fresh public-library search found two substantial
Tajik-language university algebra sources. This packet preserves their exact
downloaded PDF bytes and closes the Tajik **algebra-source** row.

It does not claim that native Tajik TeX was found.

## Public source

- Collection: <https://zarowadk.ru/skachat-besplatno-2/kitobho-matematika.html>
- Site: `zarowadk.ru`
- Retrieval: 2026-08-01

The preserved works are:

1. R. D. Davlatov and U. Choriev, *Algebra va nazariyai adadho. Qismi 1*,
   Dushanbe, 1976. The public catalogue reports 76 pages; the downloaded PDF
   has 81 physical pages. Its Tajik body covers complex numbers, sets and
   binary relations, algebraic structures, groups, rings, fields,
   isomorphisms, Gaussian elimination, exercises, and bibliography.
2. U. Choriev and M. I. Olimov, *Kompleksi ta'limiyu metodi az fanni
   "Algebra va nazariyai adadho"*, Dushanbe, 2017. The imprint reports 164
   pages; the downloaded PDF has 165 physical pages. It contains a university
   course plan, lecture notes, worked material, exercises, tests, and
   bibliography spanning foundational set theory, relations and mappings,
   complex numbers, linear algebra, algebraic structures, and number theory.

Exact titles, authors, landing/download URLs, physical page counts, bytes, and
SHA-256 identities are in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.csv`. The inspected mathematical
coordinates are in `TAJIK_ALGEBRA_SCOPE_LEDGER_20260801.csv`.

## Verification

Both PDFs were parsed independently with Poppler and pypdf. They are readable,
unencrypted, contain no JavaScript, and have complete physical page sequences.
Representative cover, contents, lecture, formula, algebraic-structure, group,
and terminal pages were rendered and directly inspected. The 1976 item is an
ABBYY-backed scan; the 2017 item is a generated A4 PDF. Their text layers are
useful for location but contain historical-font/OCR substitutions, so the
page image remains the control for exact reading.
