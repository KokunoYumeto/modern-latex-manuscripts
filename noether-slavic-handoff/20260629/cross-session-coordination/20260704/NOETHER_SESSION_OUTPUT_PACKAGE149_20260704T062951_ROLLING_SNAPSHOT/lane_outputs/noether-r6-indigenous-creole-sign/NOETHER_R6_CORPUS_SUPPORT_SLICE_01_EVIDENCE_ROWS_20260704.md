## Slice Rule

A row appears here only if it has local captured evidence or validated route metadata sufficient to support a reviewer/source-owner question. Inclusion does not mean source authority is accepted. It means the row can be used to prepare a corpus-access support brief.

Allowed in these slices:

- Row IDs and source titles.
- Local evidence status and threshold.
- Reviewer/source-owner questions.
- Non-canonical access-use framing.
- Blocked gates and next required return.

Not allowed:

- Quoting source prose.
- Copying media or screenshots.
- Extracting or promoting local terms/signs.
- Translating Noether or source text.
- Treating route evidence as authority.
