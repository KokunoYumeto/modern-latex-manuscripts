Session G receives Malay-Indonesian and SEA/Pacific rows. Comparator-only regional sources must remain comparator-only until exact content and reviewer routes exist.

Session D keeps NOVEL/OWNERLESS streams only as research-only construction/source-evidence packets and method templates.
