## Routing Rule

OCR and accessibility work may support source discovery, legibility checks, internal reviewer questions, and access-needs planning. It may not convert a captured source, route, video pointer, API row, label, or OCR string into authority, reuse permission, accepted terminology, accepted signs, excerpts, translations, or pilot material.

For signed languages, accessibility routing is visual/temporal and reviewer-led. Generated captions, written glosses, transcripts, labels, SWU/API fields, thumbnails, and still descriptions are metadata or reviewer questions only. They are not sign-language access products and are not accepted signs.

## Reviewer-Route Addendum

Future return ledgers should add these fields before any OCR/accessibility output is used beyond internal planning:

| Field | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| `access_preprocessing_allowed` | Whether OCR/text extraction/caption/transcript/alt-text preparation is allowed for internal review, public support, both, or neither. |
| `access_preprocessing_scope` | Exact rows, pages, videos, fields, or links covered. |
| `correction_reviewer_profile` | Who may correct OCR, captions, transcript notes, alt text, or sign/variant descriptions. |
| `reuse_mode_for_access_output` | Whether outputs are link-only, private-review-only, short-quote, excerpt, caption, transcript, alt-text, or no-reuse. |
| `media_mode` | For signed-language rows: link, embed, copied clip, still, thumbnail, transcript, caption, or none. |
| `variant_and_rejection_notes` | Required preservation of variant, disagreement, local scope, rejected rows, and do-not-use notes. |
| `accessibility_not_authority_statement` | Explicit note that accessibility preprocessing does not itself accept source authority, terms, signs, or translations. |

## Sign / Visual-Access Specific Boundaries

| Boundary | Rule |
|---|---|
| Captions | Source-provided captions may be recorded as source metadata. Generated captions are not allowed as sign-language evidence without qualified review and media permission. |
| Transcripts | Transcripts of sign videos are not sign equivalents. A transcript route may only be a reviewer question unless explicitly authorized and reviewed. |
| Stills and thumbnails | No stills, thumbnails, screenshots, or derived images may be copied or generated for use without media clearance. |
| SWU/signwriting | SWU/API fields are metadata until DGS or other qualified signed-language review accepts their scope. |
| Alt text | Alt-text planning may describe accessibility needs, but public alt text for source media requires rights and qualified signed-language review. |
| Cross-lane transfer | No ASL to LSQ/DGS transfer, no DGS to International Sign transfer, and no International Sign replacement of local signed-language routes. |
