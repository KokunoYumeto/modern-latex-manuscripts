# EGA English readers

Open `00_GLOBAL_EGA_0_IV_English_Complete_Linked_Reader_20260801.pdf`
for one continuous, internally linked EGA 0-IV reader. The five
standalone volume readers remain directly available. Download
`00 Current_EGA_English_Readers_and_Buildable_TeX_20260801.zip` for
all six readers and their complete buildable TeX.

Coverage: EGA 0 through Section 13; EGA I and II through EOF; the
published EGA III text through 7.9.14; and EGA IV Sections 1-21
through EOF. These are working English readers, not critical editions
or new rights grants.
