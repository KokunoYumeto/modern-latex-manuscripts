# al-Battani, Opus Astronomicum: Table Coverage Status

This update improves the al-Battani table layer without declaring the whole numerical-table stream complete.

## Complete Or Promoted

- Text stream: all 100 trilingual text segments are present in the current v083 reader.
- Fixed-star catalogue: refreshed complete critical table edition with 485 stars, authoritative ecliptic longitude/latitude coordinates, magnitudes, north/south signs, modern identifications, and expanded description data.
- Geography gazetteer: 93 regional midpoint rows plus 176 city rows are now transcribed, for 269 localities with coordinates.
- Chronology and Canon of Kings: a partial working edition is now included for the principal eras and the regnal list from Nabonassar through the Byzantine emperors and early caliphs.
- Earlier Western Europe leaf material is superseded by the current geography-gazetteer packet.

## Still In Progress

- Chronology/regnal tables still need full collation of ancient regnal-year figures against the printed table.
- Zodiac, terms, houses, and auxiliary numerical tables.

## Publication Wording

Use "complete fixed-star catalogue", "complete geography gazetteer", and "partial chronology/Canon-of-Kings working edition" for this update. Do not describe the full numerical table stream as complete yet; chronology collation, zodiac, and auxiliary tables remain open.
