# Archive-wide immediate mathematics publication / no-hold policy

Control date: 2026-08-04

Authority: direct standing instruction from Floris to archive maintenance.

## Controlling rule

Preserve and publish mathematical work immediately. Archive maintenance must not create a pre-emptive copyright, rights, quality, review, completion, or certification hold. A caveat is metadata, not a veto. An unchecked draft is published as an unchecked draft; an incomplete checkpoint is published as an incomplete checkpoint; a superseded or erroneous generation is preserved and linked as such. Distinct bytes, decisions, errors, reversals, and generations are never curated away.

This rule applies archive-wide, not only to SGA, EGA, FAC, GAGA, Noether, or one language lane. It supersedes generic instructions that would leave mathematics pending/private solely because redistribution permission was not affirmatively established.

## Access and discrimination boundary

Canonical mathematical material is especially scarce in Spanish and many other languages. Pre-emptively suppressing those translations compounds unequal access for Spanish-speaking, Latin American, South American, and other underserved mathematical communities. Archive maintenance must not use speculative rights caution to produce that exclusionary result.

## Japanese-rightsholder exception

The only category Floris directs archive maintenance to treat with special copyright caution is material actually copyrighted by Japanese rightsholders. Japanese language alone does not trigger the exception: a translation into Japanese is not held merely because it is Japanese-language text. Floris states that the current Japanese archive work uses public-domain underlying material.

When a future object is actually controlled by a Japanese rightsholder, perform a bounded, specific rights check without converting the rest of the corpus into a release hold. Do not infer Japanese ownership from script, language, translator, or audience.

## Concrete takedown handling

Do not anticipate hypothetical enforcement. If an actual rightsholder sends a concrete demand:

1. preserve exact private custody bytes and all immutable publication/version history;
2. record the demand, evidence, response, and any reversal append-only;
3. address that concrete object and demand at that time;
4. do not expand one demand into suppression of unrelated mathematics.

## Privacy and security

This no-hold policy does not authorize publishing credentials, authentication tokens, private contact data, or unnecessary local machine paths. Preserve source bytes unchanged in private custody and immediately publish a separately ledgered projection with the minimum privacy/security transformation required. Privacy remediation is archive maintenance work, not a reason to leave the mathematical content unpublished.

## Presentation and claims

Reader PDFs contain mathematics and source-era apparatus, not AI/workflow/status prose. Put quality, review state, rights language, source limitations, and change history in landing metadata and public provenance/logbooks. Never invent a blanket license, mathematical certification, native-language validation, critical-edition status, or peer review. Honest labeling and immediate preservation operate together.

## Supersession

The initial Spanish SGA 5 `PUBLIC RELEASE HOLD` decision of 2026-08-04 is preserved as adverse history but is no longer operative. Any analogous generic release hold elsewhere in archive-maintenance controls is superseded by this standing rule unless it concerns the specific Japanese-rightsholder exception above or a concrete received takedown demand.
