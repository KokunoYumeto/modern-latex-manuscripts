Required fields include alternatives, choice, motivation, evidence, uncertainty/adverse evidence, consequences, review status, revisit conditions, related artifacts, and a public-claim boundary. Delivery, archive review, incorporation, publication, readback, rights clearance, and human validation remain separate states.
