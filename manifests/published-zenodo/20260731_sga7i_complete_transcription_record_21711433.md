## Scope

The reader contains all six written exposés in SGA7 I: I, II, VI, VII, VIII,
and IX. Exposés III-V are not separate written exposés in this volume. Seven
editable TeX files carry 528 continuous source-page markers, 162 native
diagrams, and no raster diagram inputs. The source language is preserved as
printed, including English Exposé VI.

This is a complete working source transcription of SGA7 I, not a complete
English translation, critical edition, rights clearance, accessibility
certification, or mathematical certification. The authority scan is not
redistributed.
