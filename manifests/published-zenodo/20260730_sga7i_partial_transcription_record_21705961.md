## Scope

This is a fresh scan-based source-transcription checkpoint for complete SGA7
Exposes I, II, VI, and VII: 217 of 529 known body pages, compiled as a clean
115-page working reader with 116 native diagrams and no raster diagram inputs.
It preserves the source language as printed, including English Expose VI.
Expose VIII remains unfinished and excluded, and Expose IX has not started.
It is not a complete SGA7 transcription, complete English translation,
critical edition, rights determination, accessibility certification, or
mathematical certification.
