This same-concept successor replaces the direct SGA3 and SGA4 readers with
reader-clean builds. Direct reader PDFs contain mathematics and genuine
source-era scholarly apparatus only. AI, project, workflow, source-status,
correction-history, and comparison-lineage notes remain available in the
grouped editable-source/history archives instead of appearing in the books.

SGA1, SGA2, SGA5, and SGA6 required no reader replacement in this pass.
SGA2's retained editor notes are genuine apparatus from the controlling
edition, not project-production notes.
