## Claim limits

SGA 3 remains incomplete. The current public scopes are the cumulative
Editorial Notice, Introduction, and Exposes I--III plus separate complete
Expose IV and Expose V working readers. Exposes VI--XXVI remain untranslated.
Expose V still has 66 temporary source-PDF-derived diagram PNGs that require
native or faithful Loop-2 replacement.

The SGA 1 reader is substantially linked: 1,212 named destinations and 1,569
valid internal GoTo annotations across 233/262 pages. It is not exhaustively
convention-v2 certified, and this release does not claim that every theorem,
equation, or bare internal reference is fixed.

These are working translations and editions, not critical editions,
mathematical certifications, independent human peer review, blanket rights
determinations, or accessibility certifications.
