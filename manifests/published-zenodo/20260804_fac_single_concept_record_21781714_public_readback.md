## Scope and claim limits

The project coordinator did not know that the Achinger-Krupa translation
existed when the Codex FAC translation was produced from the French working
transcription. The external translation was discovered afterward. FAC nos.
1-79 therefore form the bounded blind-comparison scope; nos. 80-81 postdate
discovery and are outside that claim. The record preserves 79 unit reviews,
138 findings, and a 219-entry self-correction ledger.

This is inspectable translation and provenance evidence, not a scalar score,
general benchmark, mathematical peer review, critical edition, certification,
or blanket rights clearance. The French authority scan and Achinger-Krupa
source are identified but not redistributed.
