## Release boundary

The release preserves the current Weber Volume I source-image derivatives that
carry real audit value:

- 248 tight crops with exact printed-page and source-PDF-page locators;
- 846 recovered formula-, glyph-, word-, and detail-level audit images whose
  page locator is unresolved;
- exact per-image dimensions, byte counts, SHA-256 values, parent-scan
  identity, locator state, and QA disposition.

It deliberately excludes 1,972 routine whole-page, top/middle/bottom strip,
offset-check, and enhancement renders. Those are cheap derivatives of the
already available zoomable source PDF, rather than the hard-won crop corpus.

## Claim limits

The images are visual/provenance evidence. They do not certify the German
transcription, English translation, mathematics, completeness, or
critical-edition status. Unresolved page locators remain unresolved rather than
being replaced with invented coordinates. The 1895 source work is public
domain; no new copyright is asserted in the historical text or mechanical
crop pixels.
