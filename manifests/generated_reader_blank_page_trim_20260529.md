# Generated Reader Blank Page Trim

Date: 2026-05-29

A current reader audit found a small number of fully blank generated pages in public-facing PDFs. These were removed from generated LaTeX reader PDFs only. Explicit reference scans were left untouched, because blank or low-content pages can be part of the facsimile evidence.

Affected surfaces:

- Non-European combined English reader.
- Non-European combined modern Chinese reader.
- Non-European combined Arabic reader.
- Non-European Indian original-language reader.
- Non-European Islamic/Arabic original-language reader.
- Selected non-European work-level generated readers.
- Weber `Lehrbuch der Algebra`, Volume III modern LaTeX draft.
- Gauss Werke Band III, Band VI, and Band VII generated reader PDFs.
- Riemann complete-draft generated reader PDF.

This is a presentation repair only: source/provenance ZIPs and superseded local copies retain the prior generated PDFs for comparison.
