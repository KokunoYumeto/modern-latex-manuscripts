# FAC accidental blind-comparator methodology evidence

This package preserves an accidental methodological control created during the Codex source-first reconstruction and English translation of Jean-Pierre Serre's *Faisceaux Algébriques Cohérents* (FAC).

## Backstory

Codex translated, compiled, and source-reviewed FAC nos. 1--79 from the controlling French authority before Floris discovered the already-existing English translation by Piotr Achinger and Łukasz Krupa. The public comparator PDF was found first; its editable source archive was acquired afterward. Because the Codex no. 1--79 text already existed, the overlap became a chronology-defined blind comparison rather than a drafting dependency.

Nos. 80--81 were completed after the comparator was known. They are excluded from blind-performance claims and treated only as comparator-aware process controls.

## Result

- 79/79 chronology-bounded units were personally adjudicated against corrected French;
- 20 units supplied substantive independent agreement with no material finding;
- 59 units produced one or more material external-lineage findings;
- 138 exact findings are recorded with source, Codex, and external locators, selected forms, rejected alternatives, and rationales;
- no comparator finding required changing the admitted Codex English or corrected French;
- 95/95 frozen input identities replayed without missing files, byte mismatches, or SHA-256 mismatches.

These counts are not a score or ranking. Mechanical difference classes routed review but never decided correctness. The French authority adjudicated every disagreement.

## Package contents

- final methodology report and chronology control;
- exact 79-unit inventory and 95-row input-identity ledger;
- 79 unit-review rows and 138 finding rows;
- generation and aggregate validation JSON;
- complete privacy-clean project, editorial-decision, and self-correction logbook snapshots;
- source/output/comparator identity table;
- rights/provenance and readiness records;
- exact self-excluding SHA-256 manifest and package validation.

The full Achinger--Krupa PDF and source archive are not redistributed here. Their authorship, public URLs, sizes, and hashes are recorded so the analytical evidence remains reproducible without assuming a redistribution license.
