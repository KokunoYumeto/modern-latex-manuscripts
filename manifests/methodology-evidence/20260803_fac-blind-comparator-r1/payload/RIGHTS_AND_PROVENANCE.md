# Rights and provenance

## Source and project layers

Jean-Pierre Serre's 1955 French article *Faisceaux Algébriques Cohérents* is the mathematical and textual authority for the FAC project. The package records the authority scan by hash and bibliographic locator but does not redistribute that scan.

The Codex English and the diplomatic/corrected French TeX layers are project-produced source-critical artifacts. This methodology package does not redistribute the complete readers or source trees; it binds their exact identities and includes only analytical evidence, locators, and project logbooks.

## External comparison lineage

The independently existing English translation is credited to Piotr Achinger and Łukasz Krupa:

- PDF: `https://achinger.impan.pl/fac/fac.pdf`;
- editable source archive: `https://achinger.impan.pl/fac/fac.tar.gz`.

No explicit redistribution license was located in the acquired source tree. Accordingly, this package does not contain the external PDF, source archive, or full external TeX. It contains authorship, public URLs, exact hashes, minimal locator-bound analytical descriptions, and project-authored comparison ledgers. Any future redistribution of the external files requires a separate rights determination.

## Evidentiary limits

The Achinger--Krupa English is target-language comparison material, not French authority and not an independent source witness. Agreement between English lineages does not certify a reading. Disagreement is adjudicated from French.

The accidental overlap is evidence about this bounded no. 1--79 cohort and the source-first comparator method. It is not a general benchmark, scalar quality score, leaderboard, or proof of one translator's universal superiority.
