# Rights and provenance

The mathematical source authority and translation lineage differ by volume.
The enclosed per-volume source histories and manifests identify the exact
editable inputs used for each reader.  French source TeX, editor re-editions,
and print/PDF witnesses remain authority or control material according to those
volume records; English comparison translations are never promoted to French
source authority.

No package-level license grant is invented here.  Rights in the underlying
French works, editions, typography, and third-party support files remain with
their respective rightsholders.  Where an English comparison lineage declares
a license, such as Jacob C. Reinhold's declared CC BY 4.0 comparison material,
that license applies only to the material covered by that declaration.  It does
not license the underlying French works.

This presentation package contains English reader PDFs, editable English
TeX/source closures, reproducibility tools, and provenance controls.  It omits
publisher scans, French authority PDFs, OCR witness images, and private source
crops.  Archive maintenance must retain the rights caveat and must not infer a
broader grant from the presence of editable files.
