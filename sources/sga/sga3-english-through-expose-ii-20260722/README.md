# SGA 3 English checkpoint through Expose II

This bounded checkpoint contains the Editorial Notice, Introduction, complete
Expose I, and complete Expose II through its bibliography. The continuation
cursor is Expose III, component-local page 1 / combined-reader page 111. It is
not a complete SGA 3 translation.

The controlling source is the 1,366-page Polo--Gille current complete reader,
SHA-256
`B0984B5BF322A88AB455709578E4E41EB43D6E1C91931282CFEE64F2C1F69BE2`.
OCR was used only as locator and drafting material.

Jacob C. Reinhold's public `jcreinhold/sga` lineage at
<https://github.com/jcreinhold/sga>, snapshot
`e7a259f3f8608ad3edf9bf6eead3fd504dd2d23e`, was used as credited English
comparison and drafting material. Reinhold describes that contribution as
LLM-generated and licenses his translation contribution under CC BY 4.0. It
is not the source authority or independent mathematical corroboration, and
its license does not determine rights in the underlying French work.

The reader is a 117-page A4 PDF. Its editable wrapper depends on the included
macros, fifteen numbered component TeX units, and 66 included source-derived
diagram images. A two-pass build completed without errors or overfull boxes;
two harmless underfull diagnostics remain.

The PDF was visually reviewed and is usable as a reading copy, but it is not
an accessibility-certified or reliably extractable text surface. It is
untagged, has no XMP metadata stream, and uses legacy custom math fonts without
complete Unicode mappings. A full `pdftotext` audit found 1,112 forbidden
control bytes in the extracted stream. Use the included TeX sources for
searchable text, reuse, or accessibility remediation.

The producer's original 102-row closure is preserved as
`ORIGINAL_HANDOFF_SHA256SUMS.csv`. Archive curation made two public-surface
changes without altering the reader, wrapper, components, macros, or diagrams:

- the build log was path-sanitized by replacing the private home prefix with
  `<USER_HOME>`;
- sixteen recomputable page renders of the already published reader PDF were
  omitted while their exact identities and disposition remain recorded in
  `VISUAL_EVIDENCE_DISPOSITION.csv`.

The package-local `.gitattributes` disables text normalization for this tree
so GitHub blob and raw-download bytes retain the hashes recorded by the public
manifest.

This is a machine-assisted, source-controlled working translation checkpoint,
not a complete SGA 3 edition, critical edition, mathematical certification,
independent human peer review, rights determination, or accessibility claim.
