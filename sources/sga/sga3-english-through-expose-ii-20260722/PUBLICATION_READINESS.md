# Publication readiness

Status: `READY_FOR_BOUNDED_ARCHIVE_PUBLICATION_WITH_CAVEATS`.

The frozen 102-row producer closure replayed with zero missing, byte, or hash
differences when anchored at the producer project root. The public projection
preserves every textual source and required diagram dependency, the frozen
117-page reader, and the frozen wrapper. The only transformed member is the
build log, whose private user-home prefix was replaced mechanically with
`<USER_HOME>`.

Sixteen target-PDF QA renders are not redistributed because they duplicate a
directly published, zoomable PDF and are fully recomputable. Their producer
hashes remain in the original closure and the visual-evidence disposition
table. The 66 source-derived diagrams are retained because they are actual
reader dependencies and carry the same source and rights caveat as the reader.

The 117-page PDF passed the recorded visual review, but it is not tagged and
has no XMP metadata stream. Its legacy custom math fonts do not provide
complete Unicode mappings: full-document `pdftotext` extraction contained
1,112 forbidden control bytes. Accordingly, this release makes no PDF
accessibility or reliable-text-extraction claim. The included editable TeX is
the durable searchable and remediable text surface.

Underlying French rights and attribution remain with their respective
rightsholders. Jacob C. Reinhold must remain credited for the identified
comparison/drafting lineage and its author-declared CC BY 4.0 translation
contribution. No blanket license for the underlying French work is asserted.

Publish only through the existing SGA GitHub and Zenodo lineage. Do not create
a duplicate Zenodo concept or describe this bounded checkpoint as complete
SGA 3. The direct PDF should be labeled as a visually reviewed reading copy,
not as an accessible or source-certified edition.
