# Source-review evidence scope

This lean public support bundle contains the cumulative formula/correction
ledgers, additional-repair ledger, terminology/adverse ledger, reopened-locus
adjudication, exact TeX delta review, and final structural-parity tables.

Some reports name more granular receipts retained in the private working tree.
In the 432-row final-resolution ledger, 178 evidence locators resolve to the
bundled exact-comparison CSV and 254 point to omitted granular private-tree
reports. Those omitted materials are not silently represented as bundled. They include
source-scan crops/renders, intermediate repair maps, private machine paths, and
pre-reopen evidence archives. The source scan, all scan-derived images, French
controls, inherited English, and external English candidates are excluded.

The public files establish the packaged decision trail and summary checks. The
strongest permitted claim remains a machine-assisted, source-synchronized
working edition of ten curated exposés, not a certified or critical edition.
