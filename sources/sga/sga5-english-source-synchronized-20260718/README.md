# SGA 5 English source-synchronized working edition

Public version: [10.5281/zenodo.21431041](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21431041), under concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.20410947](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20410947).

This directory mirrors the replacement 309-page English working reader and
its complete 54-file editable-source, ledger, build, and render-QA package.
Its declared scope is ten curated exposes (I, III, III B, V, VI, VII, VIII,
X, XII, and XV) through printed p. 480 plus the index through printed p. 484.

Printed p. 14 includes an explicit note for a defective inherited chain.
Printed p. 43 retains an unresolved source-glyph/subscript ambiguity; no
unsupported emendation was made. The package excludes the source scan,
scan-derived source pages, French source body, and inherited English witness
body.

This is a machine-assisted, source-synchronized working edition at the stated
scope. It is not complete SGA 5, a critical edition, independent human review,
rights clearance, proof checking, or certification of every locus.

`PUBLIC_RELEASE/` retains the exact files uploaded to Zenodo, the comprehensive
record status, and the verified publication receipt. The unpacked directories
are the byte-identical contents of the public support ZIP.
