# SGA public status

This record contains modern LaTeX working editions, source workpasses,
translations, and audit material for SGA 1, SGA 5, and SGA 6. The files have
different authority levels; broad page coverage does not imply uniform source
certification.

## Reader order

1. `SGA5_English_WorkingEdition_NotCritical_20260718.pdf` is the current
   309-page SGA 5 English working reader. Its exact scope is ten curated
   exposes (I, III, III B, V, VI, VII, VIII, X, XII, and XV) through printed
   p. 480 plus the index through printed p. 484.
2. The four-page SGA 1 English checkpoint covers the opening of Expose I and
   section I.1 only. It continues at French authority line 605, section I.2.
3. The 381-page corrected SGA 6 English reader covers the extant scan with
   explicitly layered authority.
4. The 374-page French SGA 6 source-rescribe workpass is frozen at source
   index 684; later material remains inherited scaffold.
5. The Spanish SGA 6 file is a bounded four-page Expose X checkpoint.

## Important boundaries

- The current SGA 5 English reader is a machine-assisted,
  source-synchronized working edition at the scope above. Printed p. 14 has an
  explicit editorial note for a defective inherited chain; printed p. 43
  retains a disclosed unresolved source-glyph/subscript ambiguity rather than
  a guessed correction. It is not complete SGA 5, a critical edition,
  independent human review, or a rights determination.
- The SGA 1 checkpoint is a machine-assisted, source-audited working unit, not
  a complete SGA 1 translation, critical edition, peer review, or independent
  scholarly certification.
- The SGA 6 English reader is full-range but not uniformly certified: its
  prefix is inherited and partially synchronized, its middle layer was checked
  against the available French workpass, and its later layer is scan-checked
  draft material pending further French reconciliation.
- Historical Spanish SGA 5 checkpoints remain withdrawn and are not included
  as current reader material.
- The earlier 149,702,010-byte SGA 5 support archive is absent from this
  version. Its replacement contains editable TeX, ledgers, build evidence, and
  complete render-QA coverage without the source scan or scan-derived pages.
- None of these files is presented as a proof check, diagram-by-diagram
  certification, whole-SGA completion, or critical edition.

Editable sources, public ledgers, build evidence, render checks, attribution
caveats, and exact hashes are grouped in the corresponding support ZIPs.
