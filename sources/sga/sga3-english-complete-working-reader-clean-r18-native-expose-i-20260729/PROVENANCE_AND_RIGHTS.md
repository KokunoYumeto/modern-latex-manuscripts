# Provenance and rights

The mathematical authority is the current Polo-Gille SGA 3 re-edition.
Authority PDFs are not included as general source-reader redistributions.
The pre-existing user-supplied OCR was consulted read-only as a locator and
drafting witness; it was not regenerated and is not included.

Jacob C. Reinhold's public `jcreinhold/sga` English Markdown at revision
`e7a259f3f8608ad3edf9bf6eead3fd504dd2d23e` was a declared comparison and
drafting lineage. Reinhold describes that translation contribution as
CC BY 4.0. That attribution does not make the comparison text an authority
and does not grant rights in the underlying French edition.

R18 retains the R17 native-TeX successors for Exposes X, XVI, XVIII, XXII,
and XXIV and adds all twenty-five Expose-I native diagram inputs. The exact
recursive closure still contains 103 PNG dependencies used
elsewhere in the current reader. Those files are retained as build
dependencies, not as a claim of final native reconstruction or independent
rights clearance.

No new blanket license is asserted for the French source, the editorial
re-edition, source-derived diagram material, or the package as a whole.
Rights remain with their respective holders.
