`FA679DEBFC8ADA3232D7E752A1837FC6CE474488E20A44D7641CF296876E1297`.

transcription witnesses; the source image controlled consequential readings.

This package contains a project English working translation and editable
source. It does not assert a blanket license, transfer ownership, or decide
the rights status of the underlying source. Rights and attribution remain
with their respective holders. The release is a complete SGA 7 II working
reader, not a new critical edition or a complete SGA 1-7.2 edition.
