# Provenance and rights

The current Polo--Gille re-edition PDFs control the French text,
mathematics, numbering, notes, page locators, and diagram appearance. Those
authority PDFs and authority crops are not redistributed in this package.
Pre-existing user-supplied OCR was consulted read-only as a locator and
drafting witness, never as authority.

Jacob C. Reinhold's public English Markdown at
https://github.com/jcreinhold/sga, commit
`e7a259f3f8608ad3edf9bf6eead3fd504dd2d23e`, was used as a comparison and
drafting lineage, not as source authority or independent corroboration.
Reinhold states CC BY 4.0 for his translation contribution; that statement
does not grant rights in the underlying French edition or this package as a
whole.

No new license grant or redistribution right is asserted. Rights remain
with their respective holders.
