# Public original-print adjudication - SGA 1 I.9.3

The original print and re-typeset PDF witnesses were bounded local audit
controls only. They and every scan-derived image are excluded from this
scan-free package.

The original print breaks between physical PDF pages 33 and 34, printed pages
16 and 17: page 16 ends after the French word corresponding to "isomorphic",
and page 17 begins with the words corresponding to "to a localization".
Corrected TeX places its page marker later, after "isomorphe à une". The
English margin marker follows the original-print break and the offset is
disclosed rather than silently conflated.

The original branch uses roman p_i in the minimal-prime formula; corrected
orig=false TeX uses gothic prime ideals and is the target's sole text
authority. No PDF witness is treated as a translation substrate.
