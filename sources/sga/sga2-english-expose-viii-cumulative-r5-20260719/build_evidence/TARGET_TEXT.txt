3. Applications
From these results one deduces a coherence condition for the higher direct images of a coherent sheaf under a
morphism that is not proper.
Theorem 3.1.—Let f : X → Y be a morphism of preschemes. Suppose that Y is locally Noetherian and that f
is proper. Suppose that X is locally embeddable in a regular prescheme. Let n ∈ Z. Let U be an open subset of X,
and let F be a coherent OU -module. Suppose that, for every x ∈ U such that

                                           codim({x} ∩ (X \ U ), {x}) = 1,

one has prof Fx ≥ n. Then the OY -modules
                                                     Rp (f ◦ g)∗ (F)
are coherent for p < n, where g is the canonical immersion of U into X.
Indeed, there is a Leray spectral sequence whose abutment is R∗ (f ◦ g)∗ (F) and whose initial term is given by

                                                E2p,q = Rp f∗ (Rq g∗ (F)).

Moreover, there exists a coherent OX -module G such that G|U ≃ F (EGA I, 9.4.3). It then follows from the
preceding section that condition (iv) on p. 74 is satisfied [French printed p. 97], i.e. that Rq g∗ (G|U ) is coherent for
q < n. Applying the finiteness theorem of EGA III, 3.2.1 to f and the sheaves Rq g∗ (F), one finds that E2p,q is
coherent for q < n, whence the conclusion.

 (a) Rp i∗ (F) is coherent for p < n;
 (b) for every irreducible component S ′ of the closure S of the support S of F, one has

                                                codim(S ′ ∩ (X \ U ), S ′ ) > n.


Proof, opening.—Let G be a coherent OX -module such that G|U ≃ F (EGA I, 9.4.3). Applying Corollary 2.3 to G,
we find that condition (a) is equivalent to (c):
 (c) for every x ∈ S, one has

                                   prof Fx > n − c(x),          c(x) = codim({x} ∩ Y, {x}).


Proof, (a)⇒(b).—Indeed, let S ′ be an irreducible component of S, and let s be its generic point. Since F is
Cohen–Macaulay, one has prof Fs = dim OS,s = 0. Moreover, {s} = S ′ , and the conclusion follows.
Proof, (b)⇒(a), opening.—Let x ∈ S, and let S ′ be an irreducible component of S such that x ∈ S ′ . Let s be the
generic point of S ′ . Since F is Cohen–Macaulay, we know that

                                                 prof Fx = dim O{s},x .


Proof, (b)⇒(a), case split.—If c(x) = +∞, there is nothing to prove. Otherwise, there exists y ∈ Y ∩ {x} such
that
                                             c(x) = dim O{x},y .

Proof, (b)⇒(a), conclusion.—Now OX,y is a quotient of a regular local ring by hypothesis; hence

                                     dim O{s},y = dim O{s},x + dim O{x},y > n.
