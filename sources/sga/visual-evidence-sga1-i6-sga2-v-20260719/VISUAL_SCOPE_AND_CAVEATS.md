# Visual evidence scope and caveats

This package contains 26 project-generated images: all ten pages of the current
SGA 1 English opening-through-I.6 reader, all nine pages of bounded SGA 2 Expose V,
and seven machine-readable ledger previews for Expose V. Every image is classified
`open_payload` and linked to its parent artifact, page or structural unit, hash,
geometry, DPI, and QA disposition.

No French source page, scan crop, source raster, or third-party image is included.
The image evidence verifies rendering and data-preview legibility only; it is not
human peer review, proof certification, source certification, or rights clearance.
