\chapter*{Reconstruction and attribution status}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Reconstruction status}
This is a cumulative current-progress English reconstruction containing
complete current working scopes for Expos\'es~I--XVI, XVIII, and XX,
plus frozen partial scopes for Expos\'es~XVII, XXI, and XXII.
Expos\'es~XIX and XXIII--XXVI are not included. Expos\'e~X is the complete
44-authority-page Loop-1 body supplied on 28 July 2026, not a gap or an
older fragment. The published Polo--Gille PDFs control the French text,
mathematics, numbering, notes, and diagram appearance. The pre-existing
user-supplied OCR was used only as a read-only locator and drafting
witness. Expos\'es~IV--IX and XI--XIII use native \LaTeX{} diagrams.
Source-page image witnesses still present in Expos\'es~I--III, the four
raster diagram placeholders in Expos\'e~X, the appendix raster in
Expos\'e~XIV, the three raster diagram placeholders in Expos\'e~XVI,
the one raster diagram in Expos\'e~XVIII, nine source-derived diagram
placeholders in Expos\'e~XX, and three direct diagram crops in the
partial Expos\'e~XXII snapshot remain explicitly provisional until their
own native-diagram passes are complete.

Jacob Reinhold's CC~BY~4.0 English Markdown is credited and was used as
a comparison and drafting lineage. It is not the source authority; the
Polo--Gille PDFs control every admitted passage, formula, and diagram.
The CC~BY~4.0 statement belongs to that comparison lineage and does not
by itself license the underlying French edition or this reconstruction.

No editable Polo--Gille source was publicly available when this
reconstruction began. Formulas and document structure were therefore
reconstructed in \LaTeX{}. This checkpoint does not claim completion of
SGA~3 as a whole. It binds the latest archive-admitted English scopes into
one reading surface while preserving every omission and partial cursor
visibly. The frozen partial boundaries are: Expos\'e~XVII through
Section~5.9 on authority local page~34; Expos\'e~XXI through
Application~2.1.2 on authority local page~5; and Expos\'e~XXII through
Proposition~4.1.2 on authority local page~10.
