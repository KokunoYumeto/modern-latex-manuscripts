- SGA 3 is incomplete: Expose XIX and Exposes XXIII-XXVI are absent.
- Exposes XXI and XXII are explicitly partial.
- Some source-derived raster diagrams remain pending native reconstruction.
- Expose XVII is textually complete, but its whole-expose high-zoom diagram
  pass remains open.
- Expose V figures 007-011 have known strict label-placement defects found
  after the earlier release; their mathematics is intact and a corrected
  diagram successor remains pending.
- Earlier component certifications vary. This cumulative snapshot does not
  upgrade every included component to a common terminal quality tier.
- The visual check supports a readable working release. It is not a new
  volume-wide Claude-style high-zoom diagram certification.
- The PDF is not claimed tagged or accessibility-remediated.
- No blanket license or transfer of underlying French rights is asserted.

The package is suitable for public survival, reading, correction, and
continuation. Later no-overwrite successors should replace it as more
translation and native-diagram work becomes available.
