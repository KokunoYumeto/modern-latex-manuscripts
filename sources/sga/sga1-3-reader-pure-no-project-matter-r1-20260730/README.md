# SGA 1-3 reader presentation cleanup

This no-overwrite successor keeps the direct English readers focused on
the books themselves. Project dates, workflow status, source-locator
banners, and source-version narration belong in the grouped source and
history archives, not in the reading PDFs.

Changes:

- SGA 1 removes the `Complete volume` status line, project date, and
  status-like PDF subject. Page 2 through page 259 preserves the verified
  predecessor content streams exactly.
- SGA 2 removes the project build date from the title page. Pages 2
  through 178, all destinations, and all internal links are unchanged.
- SGA 3 removes two project page-locator banners, suppresses one visible
  source-version footnote, and shortens the title subtitle to `English
  Translation`.

Genuine historical editorial apparatus intrinsic to the source editions
remains. This cleanup does not rewrite the mathematics, erase provenance,
or claim a critical edition, rights clearance, peer review, or tagged-PDF
accessibility.

The direct PDFs and masters are intended for the compact public shelf.
Annotated source, production history, and exact member manifests are
grouped in the three ZIP files.
