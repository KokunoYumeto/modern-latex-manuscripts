# Visual evidence scope and caveats

This package contains 25 project-generated output images: nine SGA 1 target
reader pages, nine SGA 2 Expose V target reader pages, and seven previews of
machine-readable SGA 2 evidence ledgers. Every image is indexed with its exact
hash, geometry, parent artifact, linked structural unit, review output, and QA
disposition.

No French source page, original-print scan, scan crop, or source-derived pixel is
included. Source witnesses remain represented by the authority hashes and page
coordinates in the evidence packages. Their redistribution rights are not
inferred from local availability.

The images document output and QA only. They do not establish human review,
mathematical certification, critical-edition status, accessibility conformance,
or rights clearance.
