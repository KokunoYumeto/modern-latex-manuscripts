# Rights and provenance

The underlying French work, typography, and scan remain the property of their
respective rightsholders. This package grants no blanket license, transfers no
rights, and makes no legal determination about the parent reader.

The parent PDF and the obsolete lower-resolution PDF are excluded. Public
pixels are limited to 12 small, task-specific excerpts that were actually
opened to inspect mathematical notation, formula rows, or diagrams. Routine
page renders, full-page views, comparison composites, unresolved derivatives,
and generated-but-unread crops are withheld as pixels. Their hashes and
provenance metadata are retained so that the visual-evidence gap is explicit.

The public crop manifest binds each excerpt to:

- the parent PDF SHA-256;
- zero-based parent index, one-based physical page, and book folio;
- fractional page bounding box;
- crop dimensions and SHA-256;
- recovered generator-definition and execution identities;
- computational render scale;
- linked working TeX file and SHA-256;
- exact read-event and QA disposition.

Generator commands and session logs are not bundled because they contain
private operational paths and unrelated history. Their complete byte
identities are recorded in the validation file, and the twelve sanitized
provenance overrides were checked against those exact logs.

These excerpts are evidence of the production process. They do not certify
the transcription, translation, mathematics, completeness, or source rights.
Users must make their own rights determination for any further redistribution.
