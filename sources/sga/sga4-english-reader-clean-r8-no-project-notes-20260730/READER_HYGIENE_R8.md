# SGA 4 reader hygiene successor R8

This no-overwrite successor keeps the mathematics and source-era editorial
apparatus in the direct PDF while moving project-production apparatus to the
source archive.

The reader suppresses:

- the project edition/status line;
- the reader-facing source and rights notice;
- eight project-added notes beginning `The frozen source`.

The complete suppressed text remains in the editable TeX under
`\SGAArchiveOnly{...}`. Genuine `N.D.E.` notes from the frozen Orgogozo
edition remain visible. In particular, the Exposé XVIII note beginning
`This translation merits some explanation` is source-era apparatus: its
French authority begins `Cette traduction mérite quelque explication`.

Rights and provenance remain documented in `RIGHTS_AND_PROVENANCE.md`.
