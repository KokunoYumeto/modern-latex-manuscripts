# SGA 7 I English working reader through Expose VI, Corollary 3.6

This bounded current-progress reader contains complete English Exposes I and
II and Expose VI from its opening through Corollary 3.6. Expose VI was
printed in English in the source volume and has been checked against that
source; Exposes I and II are source-first English translations.

The exact continuation cursor is Proposition 3.7 at the French working-source
line 1787, zero-based scan index 89, source folio 78. Exposes VII-IX and the
remainder of Expose VI are not included. This package is therefore not a
complete English SGA 7 I, critical edition, peer review, mathematical
certification, accessibility certification, or rights-clearance decision.

## Direct reading and source

- `reader/SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Through_3_6_20260731.pdf`
  is the 53-page A4 mathematical reader.
- `source/SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Through_3_6_20260731.tex`
  is the build wrapper.
- `source/components/` contains the exact 29 editable components used by the
  wrapper.
- `SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Through_3_6_Reader_and_TeX_20260731.zip`
  groups the reader, editable source, and package notices for one-click use.

The PDF starts directly with the mathematical text. Package documentation is
kept outside the reader. The public wrapper also sets the small comparison
diagram in component 29 at the normal mathematical size, removing a harmless
producer-time `\scriptsize`-in-math warning without changing mathematical text.
