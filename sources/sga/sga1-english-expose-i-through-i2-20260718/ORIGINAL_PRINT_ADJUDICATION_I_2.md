# Original-print adjudication — SGA 1 Exposé I §I.2

French authority line 636 prints `f^{-1}(x)` after introducing `f: X -> Y`
and `x in X`.

The original-print Wstein scan, SHA-256
`B97308E4DA3542CDCA400FD965B59033F8E236D76D0B7102F8D76FEFB8ADB7E0`,
was inspected at physical PDF p.19 / printed Exposé I p.2. It clearly has the
same `f^{-1}(x)` expression. Earlier and current arXiv renderings also retain
it. The form is therefore a printed-source defect, not a modern-transcription
error.

Because `x` is a point of `X`, the fiber of `f` through `x` is
`f^{-1}(f(x))`; literal `f^{-1}(x)` is generally ill-typed. The English body
uses the type-correct formula and immediately discloses the source-original
form in a footnote. Silent correction and unqualified reproduction were both
rejected.

The scan establishes the printed reading but not the earlier seminar-stage
origin of the error. The scan and all scan-derived images are audit-only and
are excluded from this public checkpoint.
