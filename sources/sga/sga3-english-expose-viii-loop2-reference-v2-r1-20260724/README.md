# SGA3 Expose VIII English checkpoint

This package preserves the complete bounded English Expose VIII checkpoint
prepared on 2026-07-24. It contains:

- a direct 31-page A4 reader; and
- one ZIP containing the complete 65-file editable-source and QA checkpoint.

The scope is Expose VIII only: Sections 1-7, the terminal bibliography, and
the hard stop before the first nonblank page of Expose IX. It is not a
cumulative SGA3 reader and does not claim that SGA3 is complete.

## Authority and provenance

The controlling witness is the Polo-Gille Expose VIII PDF
`Exp8-8nov09.pdf`, 30 pages, SHA-256
`06E43E0571D411CC5579975778FCC03C8ECAA67189248D1A053E61DC653AF510`.
That authority PDF is not redistributed here. OCR and external English
material were locator or comparison controls, not textual authority.

The checkpoint contains 10 editable TeX files, four native diagrams, and
the reference-v2 machine data. Its reader has 270 named destinations and
248 valid internal GoTo links. All 31 rendered pages were reviewed.
