# SGA 3 English current-progress cumulative reader

This package is the newest preservation-oriented reading surface for the
English SGA 3 work available on 28 July 2026. It is deliberately published
before the whole volume is finished.

## Included scope

- Complete current working bodies: Editorial Notice, Introduction, and
  Exposes I-XVI and XVIII.
- Expose XVII: authority local pages 1-8, with page 8 partial, through
  Definition 3.6. The next cursor is Corollary 3.7.
- Expose XX: authority local pages 1-7 through the proof of 1.10. The next
  cursor is the proof of Lemma 1.9 at paragraph 1.12.
- Expose XXII: authority local pages 1-10 through Proposition 4.1.2. The
  next cursor is Remark 4.1.3 on local page 11.
- Explicit gaps: Exposes XIX, XXI, and XXIII-XXVI.

Expose X is the complete 44-authority-page Loop-1 body, not an older gap or
fragment. Its producer master has SHA-256
`1639A07F89969EE835158F1C471FA9D91EB63B07EB9F7B1EA0F44248AC10C2F2`;
its standalone 44-page reader has SHA-256
`809FCB5873ABEA9DFBB47B99FFABF37254A9DD9E8B946D5DC54ECBEE2716EB40`.

complete SGA 3 translation, a critical edition, a blanket rights clearance,
or an exhaustive final reference/diagram certification.

## Authority and comparison lineage

The listed Polo-Gille SGA 3 PDFs control the French wording, formulas,
numbering, notes, page order, and diagram appearance. Floris's pre-existing
GPU OCR was consulted read-only as locator and drafting material; no OCR was
generated or rerun for this integration.

Jacob Reinhold's English Markdown at
`https://github.com/jcreinhold/sga`, revision
`e7a259f3f8608ad3edf9bf6eead3fd504dd2d23e`, is credited comparison and
drafting lineage, not source authority or independent corroboration. His
stated CC BY 4.0 applies to his translation contribution only.

## Diagram state

Native TeX diagrams are present across much of the included range. Remaining
source-derived Loop-1 witnesses are disclosed in the reader, including four
Expose X diagrams, the Expose XIV appendix raster, three Expose XVI diagrams,
one Expose XVIII diagram, and three diagrams in the partial Expose XXII
snapshot. These are readable preservation witnesses pending later native
reconstruction.

## Package shape

The cumulative PDF and its master TeX are direct files for readers. Editable
dependencies, frozen producer controls, current standalone checkpoints,
machine manifests, and the predecessor cumulative reader are grouped in one
source/history ZIP so the public landing page remains compact.

The earlier cumulative-through-XVIII release remains immutable public history.
This snapshot supersedes it only as the preferred current reading surface.
