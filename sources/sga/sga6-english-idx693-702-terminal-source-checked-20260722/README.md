# SGA6 Expose XIV idx693-idx702 terminal English checkpoint

This package preserves the source-checked terminal English tranche for
current-rescribe indices 693-702, printed pages 680-689, and source-PDF pages
683-692. It continues section 4.7 after the words `if and`, then covers
sections 4.8 through 8, the bibliography, and the terminal notes. The exact
continuation is the end of Expose XIV and the end of the available SGA6
source; there is no idx703.

The editable TeX and seven-page reader are publication-facing successors to
the producer target. The English mathematical and prose body is unchanged.
Two `pdfglyphtounicode` declarations map Latin Modern's large parenthesis
glyphs to Unicode parentheses, removing two U+0001 bytes from layout-aware
text extraction. All seven 160-dpi successor renders are byte-identical to the
producer renders.

The admitted French authority remains unchanged through idx684, with SHA-256
`7F32C2080A78A2746CBE52DCC1EC43A8505269F25518FA7B9A86E4E89AF858AC`.
For idx693-idx702, the ultimate source control is the 702-page SGA6 scan with
SHA-256
`5194436E290B8FCA54BACD5FF672588335408F1AAD3AE07D62BBA68DF35E3D76`.
The scan is identified but not redistributed. Later French and inherited
English material served only as locator or comparison witnesses.

Two scan-backed printed oddities are corrected transparently in the English
under stable IDs `SGA6-XIV-IDX696-D-VS-F-SRCDEF-001` and
`SGA6-XIV-IDX702-XREF96-VS-XREF46-SRCDEF-001`; the French witness is
unchanged and excluded.

certification of earlier SGA6 layers, a critical edition, mathematical

determination. Underlying French rights and scholarly attribution remain with
