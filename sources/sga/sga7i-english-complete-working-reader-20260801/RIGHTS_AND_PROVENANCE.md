# Rights and provenance

The controlling source witness is the public SGA 7 I scan identified by
SHA-256
`9CD40FF06EB1E488AF385A56899D4F492492A06A1E2E3C0ED6876B82E3E3603F`.
The project French working transcription has complete 528/528 page-marker
coverage; its frozen master has SHA-256
`7B7394BEAF970AC724EFDE80C841B2DAACC28D64E3145538A39AA2FA915BF355`.
Neither the scan nor the French transcription is redistributed here.

The English reader was rebuilt source-first against that transcription, with
the scan deciding consequential ambiguities. An older inherited English file
was treated only as comparison material and is not included.

This package asserts no new license over the underlying French work. Rights in
the source remain with their respective holders. The English text is presented
as an Interlanguage Mathematical Translation Project working translation, not
as a critical edition or a legal rights determination.
