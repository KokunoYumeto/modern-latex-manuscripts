\chapter*{Reconstruction and attribution status}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Reconstruction status}
This is a cumulative current-progress English reconstruction containing
complete Loop-1 expos\'e bodies I--XXIII and XXV--XXVI, together with
the translated terminal Tome-III index. The two visible production gaps
are the four-page Tome-III mathematical guide and Expos\'e~XXIV, both
still being completed by their disjoint producing session. The published
Polo--Gille PDFs control the French text, mathematics, numbering, notes,
and diagram appearance. The pre-existing user-supplied GPU OCR was used
only as a read-only locator and drafting witness; no OCR was generated or
rerun for this integration. Native-diagram state remains heterogeneous.
Session C's Expos\'es~XII, XIV, XX, XXI, and XXIII are native and
high-zoom reviewed; disclosed raster witnesses in other expos\'es remain
Loop-2 debt owned by their producing sessions.

Jacob Reinhold's CC~BY~4.0 English Markdown is credited and was used as
a comparison and drafting lineage. It is not the source authority; the
Polo--Gille PDFs control every admitted passage, formula, and diagram.
The CC~BY~4.0 statement belongs to that comparison lineage and does not
by itself license the underlying French edition or this reconstruction.

No editable Polo--Gille source was publicly available when this
reconstruction began. Formulas and document structure were therefore
reconstructed in \LaTeX{}. This internal checkpoint does not yet claim
whole-volume completion or release readiness. It exists to prove that
the completed Loop-1 source trees can coexist in one reader while
preserving the two remaining gaps explicitly. Exhaustive native-diagram,
reference-v2, privacy, rights, packaging, and publication gates remain
separate.
