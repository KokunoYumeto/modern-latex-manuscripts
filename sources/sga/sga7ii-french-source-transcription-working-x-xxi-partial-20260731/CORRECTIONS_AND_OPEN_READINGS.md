# Corrections and open readings

corrections and the minimum TeX integration repairs needed to compile Expose
XXI.

## Source-backed corrections

1. At scan index 280, diagram (4.2.5), the `j^*` vertical connector has
   arrowheads in both directions. The working TeX had only the downward arrow;
   the projection restores the bidirectional connector.
2. At scan index 384, both occurrences of the Galois group are
   `Gal(overline(F)_q/F_q)`. The working TeX omitted the numerator twice; both
   occurrences are restored.
3. At scan index 382, formula (1.4.6) contains the factor `(-1)^(n-1)`. The
   working TeX had lost the minus sign and the closing trace structure; the
   source reading and delimiters are restored.
4. At scan indices 386 and 398, displayed mathematics had been left outside a
   math environment in the unintegrated Expose-XXI body. The projection adds
   display boundaries without changing the formula content.
5. Displays (1.3.7) and (1.4.6) are line-broken to prevent clipping in the A4
   reader. Their words and mathematics follow the source.
