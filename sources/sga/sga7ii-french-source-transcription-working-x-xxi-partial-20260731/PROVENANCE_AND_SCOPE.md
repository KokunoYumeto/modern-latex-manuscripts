# Provenance and scope

## Controlling source

The controlling witness is the 446-page Institute for Advanced Study scan of
SGA 7 II distributed as `Number12.pdf`:

- bytes: 12,587,545
- SHA-256: `FA679DEBFC8ADA3232D7E752A1837FC6CE474488E20A44D7641CF296876E1297`
- page images: 2010 x 3000 pixels
- mapping: printed folio = zero-based scan index - 7
