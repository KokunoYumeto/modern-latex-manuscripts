# Original-print adjudication — SGA 1 Exposé I §I.3.4

French authority line 728 prints the graph morphism with target
`X times_Y X`. Line 735 then prints the pullback map with identity factor
`id_X'` while giving domain `X' times_Y X`.

The original-print Wstein scan, SHA-256
`B97308E4DA3542CDCA400FD965B59033F8E236D76D0B7102F8D76FEFB8ADB7E0`,
was inspected at physical PDF p.20 / printed Exposé I p.3. It clearly has both
same readings. They are therefore printed-source defects, not modern
transcription errors.

For a `Y`-morphism `g: X' -> X`, the graph has target `X' times_Y X`. On the
source-stated pullback domain `X' times_Y X`, the factor acting on `X` must be
`id_X`. The English body uses both type-correct formulas and immediately
discloses both source-original readings. Silent correction and unqualified
reproduction were both rejected.

The scan establishes the printed readings but not their earlier
seminar-stage origin. The scan and all scan-derived images are audit-only and
are excluded from this public checkpoint.
