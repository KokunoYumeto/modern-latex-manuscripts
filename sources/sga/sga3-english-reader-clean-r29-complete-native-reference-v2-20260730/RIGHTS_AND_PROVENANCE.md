# Rights and provenance

The controlling source witness for the reconstruction and translation is the
Polo–Gille SGA 3 re-edition and its associated public PDF components.  Those
source PDFs and private high-resolution authority crops are not included in
this package.

The English text is a source-aligned reconstruction.  Jacob C. Reinhold's
declared CC BY 4.0 English comparison lineage was used only as credited
comparison/drafting material; that declaration applies to that comparison
translation and does not create a license grant for the underlying French
work, the editor re-edition, or every item in this package.

No broader license grant is inferred or asserted here.  Public distribution
requires a separate rights and publication decision.
