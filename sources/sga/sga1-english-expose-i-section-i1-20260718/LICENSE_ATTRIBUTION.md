# License and attribution caveats

## Source attribution

The translation is based on the corrected French TeX distributed as arXiv
`math/0206203v2`, *Revêtements étales et groupe fondamental (SGA 1)*, a
re-typeset and annotated edition of Lecture Notes in Mathematics 224 by
Alexander Grothendieck et al. The source credits volunteer LaTeX typesetting
under a project directed by Bas Edixhoven, completion by the Société
mathématique de France, and updates by Michel Raynaud.

The source driver's notice asks anyone passing on a modified version to say at
the beginning and on the first output page that it is modified. This bounded
English workpass identifies itself as a task-owned working translation on its
title page and in its source/status note.

## External English controls

- `jmoellermath/translate-SGAI` contains an MIT license, but its own project
  record describes sparse, unresolved coverage; it is not the translation
  authority.
- `thosgood/sga` has no root license covering its translation body; the
  author's translation page says the material is for personal use and is not
  generally fully proofread.
- The two repositories are one Hosgood derivative lineage for Exposé I, not
  independent witnesses.
- The recovered jcreinhold file describes itself as an unproofed
  LLM-generated snapshot.

No external English wording is reproduced as a translation substrate in this
checkpoint. Candidate forms appear only as short comparative facts in the
source-control ledgers.

## Public-distribution state

No blanket license is declared for this checkpoint, and this task does not
resolve the original-work, translation, or re-typeset-source rights. The
archive-maintenance task must review the source notice, attribution, metadata,
and permissible public file set before any upload. The French source archive,
French source excerpt, external repositories, external compiled PDFs, and
recovered full-volume English control are deliberately excluded from the
checkpoint payload.
