# SGA public status

This record contains modern LaTeX working editions, source workpasses,
translations, and audit material for SGA 1, SGA 5, and SGA 6. The files have
different authority levels; broad page coverage does not imply uniform source
certification.

## Reader order

1. The 309-page SGA 5 English working translation remains the default reader.
2. The four-page SGA 1 English checkpoint covers the opening of Expose I and
   section I.1 only. It continues at French authority line 605, section I.2.
3. The 381-page corrected SGA 6 English reader covers the extant scan with
   explicitly layered authority.
4. The 374-page French SGA 6 source-rescribe workpass is frozen at source
   index 684; later material remains inherited scaffold.
5. The Spanish SGA 6 file is a bounded four-page Expose X checkpoint.

## Important boundaries

- The SGA 1 checkpoint is a machine-assisted, source-audited working unit, not
  a complete SGA 1 translation, critical edition, peer review, or independent
  scholarly certification.
- SGA 5 remains a substantial source-aware working translation whose readiness
  audit is open; known editorial and source-ambiguity items remain documented.
- The SGA 6 English reader is full-range but not uniformly certified: its
  prefix is inherited and partially synchronized, its middle layer was checked
  against the available French workpass, and its later layer is scan-checked
  draft material pending further French reconciliation.
- Historical Spanish SGA 5 checkpoints remain withdrawn pending a canonical
  rebuild and are not included in this version.
- None of these files is presented as a critical edition, proof check,
  diagram-by-diagram certification, or whole-SGA completion.

The editable TeX, public ledgers, build records, page renders, continuation
cursor, attribution caveats, and exact hashes for the SGA 1 unit are grouped in
`07_SGA1_English_ExposeI_Opening_SectionI1_TeX_Evidence_20260718.zip`.
