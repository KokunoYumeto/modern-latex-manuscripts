# Rights and provenance

The controlling source is the publicly available 540-page scan of *Groupes de
monodromie en geometrie algebrique I* (Lecture Notes in Mathematics 288),
20,827,344 bytes, SHA-256
`9CD40FF06EB1E488AF385A56899D4F492492A06A1E2E3C0ED6876B82E3E3603F`.
The scan itself is not included here.

The frozen complete French working transcription used for source alignment has
master SHA-256
`7B7394BEAF970AC724EFDE80C841B2DAACC28D64E3145538A39AA2FA915BF355`.
Consequential readings were checked against the scan rather than treating that
working transcription as an independent authority. Expose VI is English in
the source volume and is preserved here as a source-aligned English text.

No blanket license or ownership claim is asserted for the underlying volume,
French text, printed English contribution, or this package as a whole. Rights
remain with their respective holders. A prior inherited English witness is
excluded because its provenance and source alignment are insufficient for use
as authority.
