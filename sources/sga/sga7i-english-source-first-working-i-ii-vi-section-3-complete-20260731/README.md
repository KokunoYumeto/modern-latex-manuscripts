# SGA 7 I English working reader through Expose VI, Section 3

This bounded current-progress reader contains complete English Exposes I and
II and Expose VI from its opening through Remark 3.16, completing Section 3.
Expose VI was printed in English in the source volume and has been checked
against that source; Exposes I and II are source-first English translations.

The exact continuation cursor is Section 4, "Formal moduli of deformations,"
at French working-source line 2451, zero-based scan index 105, source folio 94.
Exposes VII-IX and the remainder of Expose VI are not included. This package is
therefore not a complete English SGA 7 I, critical edition, peer review,
mathematical certification, accessibility certification, or rights-clearance
decision.

## Direct reading and source

- `reader/SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Section3_Complete_20260731.pdf`
  is the 66-page A4 mathematical reader.
- `source/SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Section3_Complete_20260731.tex`
  is the build wrapper.
- `source/components/` contains the exact 37 editable components used by the
  wrapper.
- `SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Section3_Complete_Reader_and_TeX_20260731.zip`
  groups the reader, editable source, and package notices for one-click use.

The PDF starts directly with the mathematical text. Package documentation is
kept outside the reader. The public source carries forward the previously
reviewed normal-size rendering of the small comparison diagram in component
29; the mathematical text and diagram incidence are unchanged.
