This package preserves a source-checked English continuation for current-rescribe
indices 685-692, printed pages 672-679, and source-PDF pages 675-682. It covers
the continuation of Expose XIV section 3.3 through the opening of section 4.7.

The editable TeX and seven-page reader are publication-facing successors to the
producer target. The English mathematical and prose body is unchanged. Two

Unicode parentheses, removing two U+0001 bytes from layout-aware text extraction.
All seven 200-dpi successor renders are byte-identical to the producer renders.
