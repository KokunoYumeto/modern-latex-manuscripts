## Result

The scan confirms the §I.8 envelope, singular heading, hierarchy, page
markers, displayed formulas, and starred closing remark. It also confirms
that three defects/irregularities are shared with the print and were not
introduced by the English workpass:

1. `B tensor_A A_0 = A_0` has the wrong target; the construction requires
   the localization `B_0`. The English uses `B_0` with an immediate note.
2. The local ring at `z` is printed as isomorphic to `C`; the point,
   localization, étaleness, and following base change require
   `B = C_n`. The English uses `B` with an immediate note.
3. `défini pas` is the evident typographical error `défini par`; it is
   translated naturally as “induced by.”

The print also alternates `J` and calligraphic `J` for one ideal of
definition and its powers. The English uses the calligraphic form
consistently and discloses the alternation. No unresolved material ambiguity
remains in this bounded unit.

The scan and its renders remain internal audit witnesses and must not enter a
public translation payload.
