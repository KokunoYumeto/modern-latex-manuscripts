# SGA 7 I English working reader through Expose VI, Proposition 2.9

This bounded current-progress reader contains complete English Exposes I and
II and Expose VI from its opening through Proposition 2.9. Expose VI was
printed in English in the source volume and has been checked against that
source; Exposes I and II are source-first English translations.

The exact continuation cursor is Corollary 2.10 at the French working-source
line 1153, zero-based scan index 70, source folio 59. Exposes VII-IX and the
remainder of Expose VI are not included. This package is therefore not a
complete English SGA 7 I, critical edition, peer review, mathematical
certification, accessibility certification, or rights-clearance decision.

## Direct reading and source

- `reader/SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Through_2_9_20260731.pdf`
  is the 39-page A4 mathematical reader.
- `source/SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Through_2_9_20260731.tex`
  is the build wrapper.
- `source/components/` contains the exact 20 editable components used by the
  wrapper.
- `SGA7I_English_SourceFirst_Working_I_II_VI_Through_2_9_Reader_and_TeX_20260731.zip`
  groups the reader, editable source, and package notices for one-click use.

The PDF starts directly with the mathematical text. Package documentation is
kept outside the reader.
