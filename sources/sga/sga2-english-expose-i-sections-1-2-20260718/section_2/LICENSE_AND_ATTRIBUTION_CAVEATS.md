# License and attribution caveats

Assessment date: 2026-07-18

## French authority

The controlling source is the corrected/annotated French TeX distributed in
`arXiv-math0511279v1.tar.gz`. Availability from arXiv does not by itself
establish a blanket reuse license for every underlying French contribution.
This checkpoint therefore does not redistribute the French TeX archive or
French rendered pages as proposed public files. It records their hashes and
coordinates as source-control evidence.

## Recovered English comparison witness

The recovered full-volume English Markdown pins exactly to Jacob Reinhold's
public `jcreinhold/sga` commit
`069f3b6f7cc4c9c1001d0c2799d3e7c5cbd93c09`. The pinned repository states:

- copyright 2026 Jacob Reinhold;
- CC BY 4.0 for the English translation contribution;
- the work is LLM-generated and is a translation, not a critical edition.

Any reuse of inherited English expression must attribute Jacob Reinhold,
link or identify the CC BY 4.0 license, and indicate material changes. It must
not be relabeled CC0. The full recovered witness is not included in this
checkpoint payload.

## New source-aligned English unit

This lane has not assigned a new standalone public license to the
source-aligned English unit. The archive maintainer must preserve the above
attribution/change notice wherever inherited expressive contribution may be
present and must apply the project's approved license metadata rather than
assuming CC0 or public domain.

not a full SGA 2 English translation. It should identify the French TeX as
