# SGA 2 English source-aligned working checkpoints

This package contains two bounded English working checkpoints for SGA 2, Expose I. Both were reconciled against the corrected French TeX in arXiv `math/0511279v1`. They are not a complete SGA 2 translation, an official critical edition, peer review, or full-volume source certification.

## Included scope

- Section 1: Expose I heading/opening and section 1; full French source envelope lines 87-279, substantive section lines 90-279; printed pages 5-12; 6-page English PDF. The next cursor is section 2 at French line 280.
- Section 2: French lines 280-503, including core section 2 at lines 280-489 and the corrected terminal bibliography at lines 490-503; printed pages 13-18; 5-page English PDF. The next cursor is Expose II at French line 505.

The package therefore contains two editable target units and 11 rendered English pages. French source files and source-page renders are not redistributed.

## Authority and comparison material

The sole source authority is the corrected French TeX archive, SHA-256 `26C1C38F668A414A9A1535FC4A2EAD7C5E2CBA4BF8938DAC5CE3651DDE2A0476`; its main TeX has SHA-256 `C2F899E92A904E312B550C6452A117FF23D30AF984B2254A0961D2DF0DACD042`.

Jacob Reinhold's explicitly LLM-generated CC BY 4.0 English corpus was used only as a comparison witness and is not redistributed here. It is not treated as authority or as a globally proofed substrate. The included license and provenance notes preserve that distinction and attribution.

## Public-package hygiene

The producer workspaces contain internal handoff notes, host-specific build paths, and raw MiKTeX logs. Those private controls are deliberately excluded. This public package preserves the checked TeX/PDF pair, clean source-alignment and terminology evidence, target-render evidence, attribution notes, and independent-review summaries. `PUBLIC_MANIFEST.csv` and `SHA256SUMS.csv` bind the exact public subset.

Corrections can be proposed through GitHub issues or pull requests at <https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/modern-latex-manuscripts>.
