# SGA 2 Expose VIII bounded unit L12C

This directory is an off-PC GitHub backup of an independently sealed bounded
English working unit. It covers corrected French TeX lines 2618--2638,
including the close of the proof of Lemma 1.2. Line 2639 and Section 2 are
excluded; the next cursor is line 2640.

The editable TeX and one-page PDF are direct producer artifacts. The unit
transparently emends the French line 2632 reading `H^q` to `H^{-q}` while
leaving the French authority unchanged. The source audit and independent
review seal explain the mathematical evidence and the limited editorial
status of that choice.

This is a bounded unit, not a cumulative Expose VIII reader, a complete SGA 2
translation, a critical edition, source certification, peer review,
independent human review, mathematical certification, or rights clearance. A
future cumulative Expose VIII checkpoint should become the preferred reader
while preserving this unit-level provenance.

The four French source-page renders listed in
`SOURCE_IMAGE_RIGHTS_BLOCKED.csv` are not copied into this public mirror
because their redistribution rights have not been established. Their hashes,
page coordinates, dimensions, and QA role remain recorded. Project-generated
target renders and the associated review evidence are included.
