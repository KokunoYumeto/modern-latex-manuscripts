# Publication readiness

Status: `READY_FOR_WORKING_PUBLICATION`

as an honestly labeled current-progress reader.

XIX-XXVI are absent. Exposes VII, X, XII, XIII, XIV, XVI, and XVIII are
included because their translated bodies are readable and worth preserving,
while remaining standalone release-machine or native-diagram work is disclosed
in the reader and README.

edition, source-derived witnesses, English reconstruction, or package as a
whole. Rights and attribution remain with their respective holders.

The PDF is not claimed to be tagged or accessibility-remediated. No source
authority PDF or OCR corpus is included.

DOI `10.5281/zenodo.20410947`. Predecessor records remain immutable history.
