# Provenance and rights

## Authority

The component-specific Polo-Gille SGA 3 re-edition PDFs documented by the
underlying source files control French wording, formulas, numbering, notes,
page order, and diagram appearance. Those authority PDFs and private review
crops are not redistributed in this package.

The user-supplied OCR corpus pre-existed this integration and was consulted
read-only as locator or drafting evidence where useful. No OCR generation or
rerun is represented by this release. Source images, not OCR, control any
disputed reading.

## Comparison lineage

Jacob C. Reinhold's English Markdown in
`https://github.com/jcreinhold/sga`, revision
`e7a259f3f8608ad3edf9bf6eead3fd504dd2d23e`, was used as comparison and
drafting lineage, not as source authority or independent corroboration.
Reinhold describes that contribution as LLM-generated and states CC BY 4.0
for his translation contribution. That license does not extend to the
underlying French work or to unrelated project material.

The integration and editorial workflow also involved human-directed
OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT and Anthropic Claude. These production details are
recorded here rather than presented inside the mathematical reader.

underlying rights is asserted for the French source, the Polo-Gille edition,
the English reconstruction, editorial additions, or this package as a whole.

This is a scholarly working translation and TeX edition, not a new critical
edition or an official publication of the original seminar.
