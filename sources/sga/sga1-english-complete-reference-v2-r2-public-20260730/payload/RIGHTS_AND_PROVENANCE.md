# Rights and provenance

## Controlling source

The translation is based on the corrected French TeX distributed as arXiv
`math/0206203v2`, *Revêtements étales et groupe fondamental (SGA 1)*, a
re-typeset and annotated edition of Lecture Notes in Mathematics 224 by
Alexander Grothendieck et al.  The frozen source archive has SHA-256
`0A33C9C06908705A2525690FAAE02F6F07980A4AA069A8B6CFF9B1D9BC39ACD3`.
The French archive and original-print scans are not included in this package.

The source notice credits volunteer LaTeX typesetting under a project directed
by Bas Edixhoven, completion by the Société mathématique de France, and updates
by Michel Raynaud.  It asks anyone passing on a modified version to identify it
as modified at the beginning and on the first output page.  This package and
its first output page identify the work as an English translation.

## English lineage

The cumulative English source was produced and extended as a machine-assisted
working translation from the official French TeX.  Recovered/community English
materials were comparison controls only, never source authority.  In
particular:

- `jmoellermath/translate-SGAI` has an MIT license for its sparse project
  material but does not establish complete source alignment;
- the Hosgood/Thosgood SGA material does not supply a blanket license for a
  complete translation body and describes itself as personal-use/not generally
  fully proofread;
- the current jcreinhold comparison lineage declares CC BY 4.0 for its own
  translation only and describes it as a readable LLM translation rather than
  a critical edition.

No external PDF, source archive, scan, OCR corpus, or scan-derived image is
redistributed here.
