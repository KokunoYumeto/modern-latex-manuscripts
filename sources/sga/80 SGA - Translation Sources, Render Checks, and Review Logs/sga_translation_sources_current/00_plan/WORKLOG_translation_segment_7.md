This range completes the technical cardinal-filtration section of Exposé I. It covers:

- stability of cardinal filtrations under accessible functors;
- comparison of different cardinal filtrations;
- ind-presentation of accessible categories through sufficiently large cardinal slices;
- accessible functors as left Kan extensions from small subcategories;
- the section category of a fibred category over a small base;
- construction of small strict-epimorphic generating subcategories for categories of sections and cartesian sections;
- cardinal filtrations on those section categories.
