# Rights and provenance

## FAC source authority

The controlling source is the publicly available journal scan of Jean-Pierre
Serre, *Faisceaux algebriques coherents*, Annals of Mathematics (2) 61 (1955),
197-278, DOI 10.2307/1969915:

- 83 PDF pages
- 6,305,726 bytes
- SHA-256
  `B8C6508249308F5D8BC886B9E6360F1EC4FDC8F0F18EFAA03EE0E36B591551CA`

The full source PDF is not redistributed here. Four bounded source-image
excerpts that were actually used for title, contents, typography, and opening
text checks are included as scholarly verification evidence. Their exact
parent, page, rasterization scale, crop box, pixel dimensions, and SHA-256 are
recorded in `visual-evidence/VISUAL_EVIDENCE_INDEX.csv`.

These are the actual scan-derived pixels, not reader screenshots and not a
metadata-only substitute. Their inclusion does not assert ownership of the
underlying article or create a blanket license for the article, transcription,
or package. Rights remain with their respective holders.

## GAGA source custody

The separate GAGA ZIP preserves a first-pass TeX transcription of Serre's
*Geometrie algebrique et geometrie analytique* (1956), printed pages 1-42.
Its authority is a 43-page NUMDAM scan, 4,134,102 bytes, SHA-256
`9898F985DD6932496E26450BFE0BECA8655A937032F545B97CEBD88D1BC8EB98`.
That scan is not included. The GAGA source snapshot is source survival only;
its reader remains held until visible join sentinels are removed and checked.

No blanket license grant or rights-clearance decision is asserted for either
work.
