# Jean-Pierre Serre: FAC complete working transcription

This package preserves a complete readable French working transcription of
Jean-Pierre Serre's *Faisceaux algébriques cohérents*, Annals of Mathematics
(2) 61 (1955), 197-278.

## Scope

- All 82 printed source pages, 197-278, are represented in source order.
- The reader is 63 A4 pages.
- Source indices 1-82 map to printed pages by the rule that the printed page
  equals the source index plus 196.
- Twelve pages that were absent from the preceding 70/82 checkpoint were
  transcribed directly from rendered source leaves. Their exact files,
  resolutions, bytes, and hashes are recorded in
  evidence/MANUAL_PAGE_LEDGER.csv.

This is a complete working transcription of the represented article, not a
critical edition, mathematical certification, peer review, rights clearance,
or accessibility-remediated edition. Citation-critical formulas and unusual
readings should still be checked against the source.

## Public files

- reader/Serre_FAC_French_Complete_Working_Transcription_20260731.pdf is the
  direct 63-page reader and preferred preview.
- source/fac.tex and source/fac_body.tex are the complete editable build
  surface. source/manual/ preserves the twelve page-level recovery units.
- evidence/SOURCE_NOTES.md records source defects and uncertain readings.
- visual-evidence/ contains four actual scan-derived crops used during source
  checking, with parent-page, rasterization, bounding-box, dimension, and hash
  provenance.
- release/02_Serre_FAC_French_Complete_Source_and_Visual_Evidence_20260731.zip
  is the compact complete FAC reader/source/evidence download.
- release/03_Serre_GAGA_French_FirstPass_TeX_Custody_20260731.zip is retained
  byte-identically from the preceding release. It preserves the first-pass
  GAGA TeX source for printed pages 1-42; its existing PDF is not fronted
  because visible page-join sentinels remain.

The authority PDF, raw logs, auxiliary files, private caches, and production
status notes are not included.
