# Rights and provenance

## FAC source authority

The controlling source is the publicly available journal scan of Jean-Pierre
Serre, *Faisceaux algébriques cohérents*, Annals of Mathematics (2) 61
(1955), 197-278, DOI 10.2307/1969915:

- 83 PDF pages
- 6,305,726 bytes
- SHA-256
  B8C6508249308F5D8BC886B9E6360F1EC4FDC8F0F18EFAA03EE0E36B591551CA

The full source PDF is not redistributed here. Four bounded source-image
excerpts actually used for title, contents, typography, and opening-text
checks are included as scholarly verification evidence. Their exact parent,
page, rasterization scale, crop box, pixel dimensions, and SHA-256 identities
are recorded in visual-evidence/VISUAL_EVIDENCE_INDEX.csv.

These are source-scan pixels, not reader screenshots and not a metadata-only
substitute. Their inclusion does not assert ownership of the underlying
article or create a blanket license for the article, transcription, or
package. Rights remain with their respective holders.

## Transcription lineage

The complete working transcription represents all printed pages 197-278.
Twelve pages were transcribed directly from rendered source leaves to close
the exact gaps in the preceding 70/82 checkpoint. The public TeX retains
source-page markers, reading decisions, source defects, and review resolution
without production-system commentary.

## GAGA source custody

The retained GAGA ZIP preserves first-pass TeX for Serre's *Géométrie
algébrique et géométrie analytique* (1956), printed pages 1-42. Its authority
is a 43-page NUMDAM scan, 4,134,102 bytes, SHA-256
9898F985DD6932496E26450BFE0BECA8655A937032F545B97CEBD88D1BC8EB98.
That scan is not included. The GAGA source snapshot is source survival only;
its reader remains held until visible join sentinels are removed and checked.

No blanket license grant or rights-clearance decision is asserted for either
work.
