# FAC editorial decision logbook

## Purpose and audit rule

This is the human-readable rationale ledger for the FAC French and English work. It complements, but is not replaced by, the machine-readable CSV controls. A successor task or human mathematician should be able to identify what was changed, which witness controlled the decision, why the selected reading or English form was chosen, and what was deliberately rejected.

Every intervention is classified as one of the following:

1. **French transcription repair (`FAC-FR-T…`)** — the inherited French TeX disagrees with the printed 1955 authority. Both working French layers are repaired to the print. This is not called a source correction.
2. **Printed-source correction (`FAC-FR-C…`)** — the printed authority itself contains an evident typo or internally impossible reference. The diplomatic French layer preserves the print; the corrected French layer and English translation use the mathematically coherent reading; the English reader discloses the intervention.
3. **English normalization** — a recurring target-language or TeX convention that changes no mathematical content. The convention rationale is recorded below, but that general rationale does not erase occurrence-level accountability: every actual nonliteral application must remain locatable by unit and target occurrence. Any exception receives its own entry.
4. **Typesetting/build repair** — a defect introduced in the English TeX or build, such as a missing backslash or misleading operator glyph. Rejected output is kept as adverse history where material; only the repaired successor is admitted.

No OCR reading is authority. Direct rendered views of the controlling PDF decide uncertain source readings. Routine FAC pages are inspected at approximately 1100 dpi; detail is escalated toward 5000–9000 dpi only when the ordinary view does not resolve a small symbol, arrow, index, prime, or punctuation mark.

If a later review shows that an editorial choice or English rendering was wrong, the repair is never silent or merely local. The task must: (a) append a row to `controls/EDITORIAL_SELF_CORRECTION_LEDGER.csv`; (b) identify the original decision and all affected occurrences; (c) search the full active FAC French/English scope for propagation; (d) repair every affected occurrence copy-on-write; (e) rebuild and recheck the changed surfaces; and (f) identify which artifact is superseded. A later discovery that an attempted correction to Serre was itself mistaken must explicitly restore the authorial reading and explain the reversal here.
