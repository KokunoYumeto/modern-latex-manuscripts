# Deligne and Mumford - Irreducibility of the Space of Curves, Pages 75-99

Date staged: 2026-06-02

This packet contains a source-checked working segment for Pierre Deligne and David Mumford, "The Irreducibility of the Space of Curves of Given Genus", covering the supplied page range 75-99.

It should not be read as a complete replacement for the existing full paper-005 reader. The existing full reader remains available, but this segment is cleaner and more source-grounded for the covered range.

## Contents

- `Cumulative_p075_099/PDF/`: English and French working PDFs for pages 75-99.
- `Cumulative_p075_099/TEX/`: English and French TeX for pages 75-99.
- `Cumulative_p075_099/SCAN/`: source scan slice for pages 75-99.
- `Installment_p090_099/`: the latest installment-only English/French TeX, PDFs, and scan slice for pages 90-99.

## Coverage

The English cumulative segment has 17 rendered pages and reaches section 4.10, "Some results on algebraic stacks." The French working segment has 18 rendered pages.

The segment includes reconstructed TikZ diagrams and does not use screenshots as diagram substitutes.

## Use

Use this packet as a high-fidelity repair/source layer for paper 005. Once the remaining end of the paper has been source-checked at the same standard, the full paper-005 reader can be replaced by a single clean complete English/French pair.
