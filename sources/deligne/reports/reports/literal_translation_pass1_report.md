# Deligne LaTeX Translation Pass 1 - Literal Mathematical Draft
## Scope
- Input: pass-2 cleaned TeX sources for Deligne papers 30-90 plus 12 letters.
- TeX sources processed: 88.
- Files selected for French-to-English literal glossary translation: 73.
- Papers 1-29 remain absent from this source batch.

## Method
This pass preserves LaTeX commands, inline math, display math, and standard math environments, and translates surrounding prose by a conservative French mathematical glossary and phrase table. It is intended as a full-corpus English working draft, not as a polished final translation or clean-room recast.

## Known limitations
- This is a literal mechanical pass. It preserves the mathematics but leaves residual French and awkward phrase order in many places.
- The pass is intentionally conservative around equations, macros, labels, and citations.
- Paper 76 remains structurally malformed at the TeX level, as in pass 2; translation did not attempt to repair its equation/prose boundary errors.
- English-language papers were mostly left as-is except for local French/theorem-name repair if detected.
