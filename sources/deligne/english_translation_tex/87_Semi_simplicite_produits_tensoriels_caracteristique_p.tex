\title{Semisimplicity of tensor products in characteristic p}
\author{Pierre Deligne}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Resume.}
Let $G$ a scheme en groups affine over a field $k$ of caracteristique $p \neq 0$, and $(V_i)$ a family finite of representations semi-simples of $G$. We montrons that si $\sum (\dim V_i - 1) < p$, then the representation of $G$ tensor product of the $V_i$ is again semisimple. Under the hypothese additionnelle that $G$ is smooth, this theorem a ete proves by J.-P.\ Serre en 1994. We we ramenerons a this cas. One can more generally prendre for $V_i$ of the objects of a Tannakian category on $k$.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Abstract.}
Let $G$ be an affine group scheme over a field $k$ of characteristic $p \neq 0$. If $(V_i)$ is a finite family of semisimple representations of $G$ for which $\sum (\dim V_i - 1) < p$, we show that the tensor product of the $V_i$ is a semisimple representation of $G$. For a smooth $G$, this theorem is due to J.-P.\ Serre. We proceed by reduction to this case. More generally, one can take the $V_i$ to be in a tannakian category over $k$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:0}

Let $T$ a Tannakian category over a field $k$ of caracteristique $p \neq 0$. For the definition of the Tannakian categories, we renvoyons a \cite{Saavedra} p.~193 or a \cite{Deligne1990}~2.1, 2.8. Recall that parmi the axiomes figure the existence of duaux, and celle of a functor fiber $\omega$ with values in the vector spaces on a extension suitable $K$ of $k$. The dimension $\dim(V)$ of a object $V$ of $T$ is $\dim_K \omega(V)$. Deux functors fibers devenant isomorphes on a extension more grande suitable (\cite{Deligne1990}~1.12, 1.13), this dimension ne depend not of the choix of the functor fiber $\omega$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:0.1}
Let $(V_i)_{i \in I}$ a family finite of objects of $T$. Si the $V_i$ are semi-simples and that $\sum (\dim(V_i) - 1) < p$, then the tensor product of the $V_i$ is a object semisimple of~$T$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{example}\label{ex:0.2}
Si $T$ is the category of representations lineaires of dimension finite of a scheme en groups affine on $k$, one obtains the theorem annonce in the resume.

Other examples: the category of representations of dimension finite of a algebra of Lie on $k$, or celle of the $p$-representations of dimension finite of a $p$-algebra of Lie on $k$. By passage a enveloppe algebraic, these cas se ramenent of ailleurs to the precedent.
\end{example}

\begin{example}\label{ex:0.3}
Assume that a group $\Gamma$ agisse over a field commutative $K$ of caracteristique $p$, and let $k := K^\Gamma$ the under-field of the invariants. Let $T$ the category of the $K$-vector spaces of dimension finite, equipped with a action semi-lineaire of $\Gamma$. Equipped of the tensor product on $K$, this category is tannakienne on $k$: it admet the functor fiber on $K$ ``space vector under-jacent''. Si of the representations semi-lineaires $V_i$ of $\Gamma$ on $K$ are semi-simples and that $\sum (\dim_K(V_i) - 1) < p$, the representation semi-lineaire tensor product of the $V_i$ is therefore semisimple.
\end{example}

\subsection{}
Let $V$ a space vector of dimension finite on $k$ of caracteristique $p$. As explique in \cite{Serre1994}~\S4, a automorphism $g$ of $V$ such that $g^p = 1$ defines a morphism of algebraic groups $\Ga \to \GL(V):\ t \mapsto g^t$, with $g^t$ defined by the formule of the binome
\begin{equation}\label{eq:0.4.1}
g^t := \sum_{n < p} \binom{t}{n} (g - 1)^n.
\end{equation}
A endomorphism $N$ of $V$ such that $N^p = 0$ defines similarly a morphism of algebraic groups $\Ga \to \GL(V):\ t \mapsto \exp(tN)$, with $\exp(tN)$ defined by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:0.4.2}
\exp(tN) := \sum_{n < p} \frac{(tN)^n}{n!}.
\end{equation}
Indeed, after every extension of scalars a $k$-algebra commutative $\Lambda$, si for $t$ in $\Lambda$ one defines $\exp(tN)$ by \eqref{eq:0.4.2}, the hypothese that $N^p = 0$ assure that $\exp((t_0 + t_{00})N) = \exp(t_0 N) \exp(t_{00} N)$.

\begin{definition}\label{def:0.5}
Supposons $k$ algebriquement clos of caracteristique $p$, and let $G$ a under-scheme en groups of $\GL(V)$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $G$ is \emph{sature} si for every $g \in G(k)$ such that $g^p = 1$, the morphism $g \mapsto g^t$ of $\Ga$ in $\GL(V)$ se factorise by $G$.
\item $G$ is \emph{infinitesimalement sature} si for every $N \in \Lie(G)$ such that $N^p = 0$ (to the sens of the structure of $p$-algebra of Lie of $\Lie(G)$, or as endomorphism of $V$, that revient to the same) the morphism $t \mapsto \exp(tN)$ of $\Ga$ in $\GL(V)$ se factorise by $G$.
\end{enumerate}
One will say that $G$ is \emph{doublement sature} s'it is sature and infinitesimalement sature.
\end{definition}

Let $G$ a scheme en groups affine of type finite over a field algebriquement clos $k$ of caracteristique $p$. In the states \ref{thm:0.1}, prenons for $T$ the category of representations of dimension finite of $G$, assume that the family $(V_i)$ of representations is faithful, and posons $V := \bigoplus V_i$. For prouver \ref{thm:0.1} lorsque $G$ reduit, Serre \cite{Serre1994} commence by ramener to the cas or $G$ is sature in $\GL(V)$. Si tel is the cas, the group finite $\pi_0(G)$ of the composantes connected of $G$ is of ordre prime a $p$, and this permet of se ramener to the cas or $G$ is connected. Let $G^0$ the composante neutre of $G$, $G^0_\red$ the under-scheme en groups reduit of $G^0$ and $R_u G^0_\red$ the radical unipotent of $G^0_\red$. Tant $G^0_\red$ that $R_u G^0_\red$ are smooth, i.e.\ of the algebraic groups lineaires to the sens of the seminaire Chevalley of 1956/58.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:0.7}
Let $(V_i)_{i \in I}$ a family finite faithful of representations of $G$. Let $V$ the sum of the $V_i$. Assume that each representation $V_i$ is of dimension $< p$ and that, as under-group of $\GL(V)$, $G$ is infinitesimalement sature. Under these hypotheses:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $G^0_\red$ and $R_u G^0_\red$ are of the under-schemes en groups invariants of $G$, and $G^0/G^0_\red$ is of type multiplicatif.
\item Si the representations $V_i$ are semi-simples, $G^0_\red$ is reductive.
\item Si $G^0_\red$ is reductive, there exists in $G^0$ a under-scheme en groups central connected of type multiplicatif $M$ such that the morphism $M \times G^0_\red \to G^0$ fasse of $G^0$ a quotient of $M \times G^0_\red$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\section{Proof of the theorem~\ref{thm:0.7}}\label{sec:1}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:1.1}
In a scheme en groups affine $G$ of type finite over a field, all under-scheme en groups connected of type multiplicatif is contenu in a under-scheme en groups connected of type multiplicatif maximal.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof (of after a lettre of M.\ Raynaud a the auteur)]
It is enough to montrer that in the set of the under-schemes en groups connected of type multiplicatif of $G$, ordonne by inclusion, every part totalement ordonnee $\mathcal{M}$ is majoree (Bourbaki, Ens III \S2 no.4 Theorem 2). For $M$ in $\mathcal{M}$, the centralisateur $Z_G(M)$ of $M$ in $G$ is a under-scheme en groups (automatiquement closed), and the map $M \mapsto Z_G(M)$ is decroissante. the set ordonne of the under-schemes fermes of $G$ is noetherien. the set of the $Z_G(M)$ for $M$ in $\mathcal{M}$ a therefore a more petit element $C$. Each $M$ in $\mathcal{M}$ is contenu in the centre $Z(C)$ of $C$. This centre is affine and commutative. The composante neutre of son more grand under-scheme en groups of type multiplicatif (SGA3 XVII 7.2.1) majore $\mathcal{M}$. C'is the majorant requis.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:1.9}
Let $f: V \to W$ a morphism of schemes of type finite on $k$, $v \in V(k)$, and $(df)_v$ the morphism induces by $f$ of the space tangent of Zariski of $V$ en $v$ toward the space tangent of Zariski of $W$ en $f(v)$. \end{proof}

En caracteristique $p$, the algebra of Lie of a scheme en groups is a $p$-algebra of Lie. We shall denote $\gamma \mapsto \gamma^p$ the operation of power $p$-ieme, souvent notee $\gamma^{[p]}$. For every representation lineaire $\rho$ of the group, one has $\rho(\gamma^p) = \rho(\gamma)^p$.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:1.10}
For every $\gamma$ in $\Lie U$, one has $\gamma^p = 0$.
\end{lemma}

As explique under \ref{lem:1.7}, one regarde ici $U$ as a under-group of $Z^0_G(H) \subset G$.

\begin{proof}
Puisque $U$ is a scheme en groups unipotent, for each $i$ the image $\gamma_i$ of $\gamma$ in $\End(V_i)$ is nilpotente. Puisque $\dim(V_i) < p$, one has $\gamma_i^p = 0$. The family $(V_i)$ of representations being faithful, one has $\gamma^p = 0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:1.11}
The group unipotent $U$ is smooth.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}

Let $E_A$ the affine space of dimension $A$ and $f: E_A \to U: (t_a) \mapsto \prod_a \rho_a(t_a)$, the product being to the sens of the loi of group of $U$. By construction, $df$ is a isomorphism en $0$. Appliquant \ref{lem:1.9}, one deduces that $U$ is smooth to the neighborhood of the element neutre, therefore partout by homogeneite.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

\section{Tensor products of powers exterieures}\label{sec:5}

The results which suivent have ete suggeres by the referee.

The theorem \ref{thm:0.7} permet of generaliser to the cas of objects of a Tannakian category the theorem of Serre \cite{Serre2003} on the semisimplicity of tensor products of powers exterieures of representations.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:5.1}
Let $T$ a Tannakian category over a field $k$ of caracteristique $p > 0$, $(V_i)_{i \in I}$ a family finite of objects semi-simples of $T$, and $(m_i)_{i \in I}$ a family of integers $\geq 0$. Si
\begin{equation}\label{eq:5.1.1}
\sum m_i(\dim V_i - m_i) < p,
\end{equation}
then the tensor product of the $\bigwedge^{m_i} V_i$ is semisimple.
\end{theorem}

Recall that the power exterieure $m$-ieme of a object $V$ of $T$ is the image of the antisymetrisation $a: V^{\otimes m} \to V^{\otimes m}$. For every functor fiber $\omega$, one has $\omega \bigwedge^m V = \bigwedge^m \omega(V)$, and the dual of $\bigwedge^m V$ is $\bigwedge^m (V^\vee)$.

We ramenerons the proof of \ref{thm:5.1} to the cas particulier or, for each $i$, $\dim(V_i) < p$. The arguments of \ref{cor:3.3}, \ref{cor:4.11} and \ref{sec:4.12} ramenent then a supposer en outre that $k$ is algebriquement clos, that $T$ is the category of representations of a scheme en groups affine $G$ on $k$, and that the family of representations $(V_i)$ is faithful, i.e. that $G$ is a under-scheme en groups of $\prod \GL(V_i)$, and therefore of $\GL(V)$, for $V := \bigoplus V_i$.
