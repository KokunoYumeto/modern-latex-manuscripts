Cher Illusie,

\vspace{0.3cm}

Let $X$ a space analytic complex, and
\[
\varepsilon : X_{*} \longrightarrow X
\]
a space analytic simplicial which Let hyperrecouvrement proper of $X$.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Conjecture.} \quad $R\varepsilon_{**}(\Omega^{p}_{X_{*}}) \in D^{b}_{\coh}(X,\mathcal{O})$ and independant of the choix of $X_{*}$.

\noindent [Notation: $\underline{\Omega}^{p}_{X_{*}}$]

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Rmq.} this clarifie beaucoup this that je fais in Hodge~III. one has mis on $X$ a double complex, with $d'_{1}$ bivariant and $d'_{2}$ operateur differentiel of prime ordre, and bien defined a quasi-isomorphism pres (c'is $R\varepsilon_{**}\,\Omega^{\bullet}_{X_{*}}$, of gradue the $R\varepsilon_{**}\,\Omega^{p}_{X_{*}}$).

\vspace{0.3cm}

For $X$ projective, the spectral sequence degenere en $E_{1}$, which aboutit a the Hodge filtration and
\[
H^{q}(X, \underline{\Omega}^{p}) \Longrightarrow H^{p+q}(X^{\an}, \mathbb{C}).
\]

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Evidence.} It is enough without doute, by devissage, of see this:

Let $X$ smooth, $\widetilde{X} \xrightarrow{\pi} X$ the eclatement of $Y \subset X$ smooth of codim $c$, and $\widetilde{Y} = \pi^{-1}(Y)$.

Let the systeme of the $(\widetilde{X}/X)^{\Delta_{n}} \xrightarrow{\varepsilon_{n}} X$.

one has $(\widetilde{X}/X)^{k} = \widetilde{X}$ recolle selon $\widetilde{Y}$ a $(\widetilde{Y}/Y)^{k}$ (inclusion diagonale of $\widetilde{Y}$).

Let $\underline{\Omega}^{p}_{(\widetilde{X}/X)^{k}}$ defined as
\[
\Ker\bigl(\mathrm{pr}_{0}^{*}\, \underline{\Omega}^{p}_{\widetilde{X}} \oplus \underline{\Omega}^{p}_{(\widetilde{Y}/Y)^{k}} \longrightarrow \underline{\Omega}^{p}_{\widetilde{Y}}\bigr).
\]

\noindent\textbf{Then}
\[
\underline{\Omega}^{p}_{X} \xleftarrow{\;\approx\;} R\varepsilon_{**}\, \underline{\Omega}^{p}_{(\widetilde{X}/X)^{\bullet}}.
\]

\noindent\textbf{Example.} For $X$ projective, si $\underline{\Omega}^{\circ}_{X} = 0$, then $H^{*}(X,\mathcal{O}) \to H^{*}(X,\mathcal{O})$ is surjective.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Example.} $\mathcal{X}^{*}(\underline{\Omega}^{\circ}_{X})$ is the under-sheaf (nu $X$) of the sheaf of rings of normalisee of $X$, form of the $f$ constants in each fiber reduite. Notant by $\widetilde{\phantom{X}}$ the normalise, one has
\[
0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{X}^{*}(\underline{\Omega}^{\circ}_{X}) \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{X^{\sim}} \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{(X^{\sim} \times_{X} X^{\sim})_{\mathrm{red}}}
\]

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Example.} One must avoir, for $G$ group finite agissant on $X$ and $\pi : X \to X/G$:
\[
\underline{\Omega}^{p}_{X/G} = \bigl(\pi_{*}\,\underline{\Omega}^{p}_{X}\bigr)^{G}.
\]
Si $Z = \text{lisse}/\text{groupe fini}$, one must therefore avoir $\underline{\Omega}^{\circ} = \mathcal{O}$. For a $V$-variety $X$, posant locally $X = X_{1}/G$, and $\underline{\Omega}^{p}_{X} = (\pi_{*}\,\underline{\Omega}^{p}_{X_{1}})^{G}$, with the confort $\underline{\Omega}^{\bullet}_{X}$ given lieu a theorie of Hodge standard.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Example.} Si $A = B \cup_{C} D$, one must avoir a triangle
\[
0 \longrightarrow \underline{\Omega}_{A} \longrightarrow \underline{\Omega}_{B} \oplus \underline{\Omega}_{D} \longrightarrow \underline{\Omega}_{C} \longrightarrow 0
\]

\newpage

si $\underline{\Omega}^{\circ} = 0$ for $B$, $D$ and $C$, this must therefore be pareil for $A$.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Example.} Si $\widetilde{X} \to X$ is birationnel, and a isomorphism hors of $Y \subset X$, of image converse $\widetilde{Y}$: $\pi' : \widetilde{Y} \to Y$, one must avoir a triangle
\[
0 \longrightarrow \underline{\Omega}_{X} \longrightarrow \underline{\Omega}_{Y} \oplus R\pi_{*}\,\underline{\Omega}_{\widetilde{X}} \longrightarrow R\pi'_{*}\,\underline{\Omega}_{\widetilde{Y}} \longrightarrow 0
\]

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Example.} Let $X$ the cone on a projective variety $V \subset \mathbb{P}^{n}$. one has,

a) $\Gamma\, \mathcal{X}^{*}(\underline{\Omega}^{\circ}_{X}) = \mathbb{C} \oplus \displaystyle\bigoplus_{n > 0} H^{0}(V, \mathcal{O}(n))$

b) $\Gamma\, \mathcal{X}^{i}(\underline{\Omega}^{\circ}_{X})$ is the module gradue suivant on this algebra graduee:
\[
\bigoplus_{n > 0} H^{i}(V, \mathcal{O}(n)).
\]

one has $\underline{\Omega}^{\circ}_{X} = 0$ ni and only si, for $n > 0$, $H^{*}(\mathbb{P}^{n}, \mathcal{O}(n)) \to H^{*}(V, \mathcal{O}(n))$ is surjective.

\noindent\textbf{Ex.} $V = V_{m}(d)$, $\sum a_{i} < n+2$ (FAC 78 Prop 5).

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{Fonctorialite:}

a) $\underline{\Omega}_{X}$ must be fonctoriel en $X$.

b) one has a ``cup-product'', to the minimum: $\mathcal{X}^{i}\,\underline{\Omega}^{p} \otimes \mathcal{X}^{j}\,\underline{\Omega}^{q} \to \mathcal{X}^{i+j}\,\underline{\Omega}^{p+q}$.

c) Si $X_{*}$ is a hyperrecouvrement proper of $X$, one must avoir
\[
R\varepsilon_{**}\,\underline{\Omega}^{p}_{X_{*}} = \underline{\Omega}^{p}_{X}.
\]

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{Generalisation.}

a) Ideal: one devrait avoir a telle theorie for of other sheaves (complexes of sheaves) that $\underline{\mathbb{C}}$.

b) To the less: $U \hookrightarrow X$; $Rj_{*}\,\underline{\mathbb{C}}$ \quad (cf Hodge~III).
