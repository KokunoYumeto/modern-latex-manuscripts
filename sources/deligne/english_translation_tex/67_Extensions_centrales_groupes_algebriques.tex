\section*{Introduction}

Let $G$ a algebraic group lineaire absolument simple simply connected over a field $F$. Si $G$ is deploye and $F$ infini, the group $G(F)$ of the points rationnels of $G$ is egal a son group of the commutateurs (the analogue algebraic of ``connected'') and admet therefore a central extension universelle
\begin{equation}\label{0.1}
0 \to \pi_1(G(F)) \to \widetilde{G(F)} \to G(F) \to 0.
\end{equation}

Matsumoto (1969) determine the kernel $\pi_1(G(F))$ by generateurs and relations. For $G$ of type other that $C_n$ ($n \geq 1$), c'is the quotient $K_2(F)$ of $F^* \otimes F^*$ by the under-group generated by the $x \otimes (1-x)$ for $x \neq 1$ in $F^*$. For $G$ of type $C_n$, c'is a group $L$ of which $K_2(F)$ is quotient. See 3.8 for more of details.

A map
\[
(\, , \,) : F^* \times F^* \to A
\]
bimultiplicative: $(xy,z) = (x,z) + (y,z)$ and $(x,yz) = (x,y) + (x,z)$, and verifiant
\begin{equation}\label{0.2}
(x, 1-x) = 0
\end{equation}
(a symbol) defines therefore a central extension of $G(F)$ by $A$.

Let $\overline{F}$ a cloture separable of $F$, $n$ a integer inversible in $F$, $\mathbf{Z}/n(1)$ the group of the racines $n$-iemes of the unite of $\overline{F}$, $\mathbf{Z}/n(k)$ sa power tensor $k$-ieme on $\mathbf{Z}/n$ and $\partial_n : F^* \to H^1(\Gal(\overline{F}/F), \mathbf{Z}/n(1))$ the cobord of Kummer (0.N.3). One sait (Tate (1976)) that the cup-product of the cobords of Kummer
\begin{equation}\label{0.3}
(\, , \,)_n : F^* \otimes F^* \to H^2(\Gal(\overline{F}/F), \mathbf{Z}/n(2)) : x, y \mapsto \partial_n x \smile \partial_n y
\end{equation}
is a symbol (symbol galoisien). We donnons in the present article a interpretation (3.5 and 3.7) of the central extension corresponding en termes of étale cohomology, the construction reposant finalement on the fait that for $K$ a Lie group compact presque simple simply connected, one has (see 1.7)
\begin{equation}\label{0.4}
H^4(BK, \mathbf{Z}) = \mathbf{Z}.
\end{equation}

Notre construction 3.5 n'exige not that $G$ let deploye. For $G$ absolument simple simply connected on $F$, it fournit a central extension
\begin{equation}\label{0.5}
0 \to H^2(\Gal(\overline{F}/F), \mathbf{Z}/n(2)) \to \widetilde{G(F)}_n \to G(F) \to 0.
\end{equation}

We n'affirmons not that this central extension let universelle parmi the central extensions of kernel annule by $n$, ni that it let not trivial. En fait, for $F = \mathbf{R}$ and $G(\mathbf{R})$ compact, the space topologique $G(\mathbf{R})$ is connected and simply connected and the central extension (0.5), being topologique (2.9), is trivial.

The vertu of the central extension (0.5) is of be canonical: defined a isomorphism unique pres. It is fonctorielle en $F$, and sa fonctorialite en $G$ is controlee by celle of (0.4). See 3.9 (ii). For every under-group unipotent $U$ of $G$, it is canonically trivialisee on $U(F)$ (2.11). For $T$ a tore in $G$, the commutateur
\[
(t_1, t_2) := \tilde{t}_1 \tilde{t}_2 \tilde{t}_1^{-1} \tilde{t}_2^{-1} \in H^2(\Gal(\overline{F}/F), \mathbf{Z}/n(2))
\]
of liftings $\tilde{t}_1$ and $\tilde{t}_2$ in $\widetilde{G(F)}_n$ of $t_1, t_2 \in T(F)$ is decrit en termes of Galois cohomology (3.5.3). Si $T^{\ad}$ is the image of $T$ in the group adjoint $G^{\ad}$, the action of $G^{\ad}(F)$ on $\widetilde{G}$ fournit by transport of structures a action of $G^{\ad}(F)$ on the extension (0.5). For $t_1 \in T^{\ad}(F)$ and $t_2 \in T(F)$, the ``commutateur''
\[
(t_1, t_2) := t_1(\tilde{t}_2) \tilde{t}_2^{-1} \in H^2(\Gal(\overline{F}/F), \mathbf{Z}/n(2))
\]
admet a description analogue (4.9).

Si $F$ is a field local and that $n$ is sufficiently divisible, the extension (0.5) is independante of $n$ (5.2). C'is a central extension
\begin{equation}\label{0.6}
0 \to \mu(F) \to \widetilde{G(F)} \to G(F) \to 0
\end{equation}
of $G(F)$ by the group $\mu(F)$ of the racines of the unite in $F$ si $F$ is not complex (5.4.1), by the group trivial for $F$ complex. Si $F$ is not archimedien, of ring of valuation $\mathcal{O}$, that $G$ a ``bonne reduction'' and that $\mu(F)$ is of ordre prime a the caracteristique residuelle, one dispose of a scindage
\begin{equation}\label{0.7}

\end{equation}
of (0.6) to the-dessus of the under-group compact maximal hyperspecial $G(\mathcal{O})$. See 5.12.

Si $F$ is a field global, of completes the field local $F_v$, the product restreint of the $\widetilde{G(F_v)}$ of (0.6) (product restreint relativement to the $G(\mathcal{O}_v)^{\sim}$ of (0.7), which are defined for presque every place $v$) is a central extension of the group adelique $G(\mathbb{A})$ by the sum on the places not complexes of the $\mu(F_v)$. Poussons-the by

\oplus \mu(F_v) \to \mu(F) : (x_v) \mapsto \prod x_v^{[F_v:\mathbf{Q}_v]/[F:\mathbf{Q}]}
\end{equation}
(presque all the factors egaux a $1$, and $\mu(F)$ identifie a under-group of $\mu(F_v)$ for $F_v$ not complex). One obtains a central extension $\widetilde{G(\mathbb{A})}$ of $G(\mathbb{A})$ by $\mu(F)$ and the fonctorialite of (0.5) en the field of base shows (see 6.4) that it is canonically trivialisee on $G(F)$:

\end{equation}

The fonctorialite of (0.6) and (0.9) en $G$ is controlee by celle of (0.4). The fonctorialite en $F$ is celle of the identification of $\mu(F)$ a group of Galois cohomology. See (5.5).

\section{Spaces classifiants simpliciaux and leur cohomology}

\subsection{}\label{1.1}

the space classifiant $BG$ of a Lie group $G$ is defined classiquement as being the base of a $G$-torseur $EG$ which let contractile. It is defined a equivalence of homotopie pres. Si $K$ is a under-group compact maximal of $G$, the inclusion of $K$ in $G$ is a equivalence of homotopie, $EG$ is a $K$-torseur on $EG/K$, which is therefore a version of $BK$ and $BK \to BG$ is a equivalence of homotopie. It is of usage, chez the topologues, of considerer $BK$ of preference a $BG$.

of after Milnor (1956) and Segal (1968), a version of $BG$ is the realisation geometric of the space topologique simplicial suivant:
\begin{equation}\label{1.1.1}
\Delta_n \mapsto G^{n+1}/G.
\end{equation}
In (1.1.1), one has note $\Delta_n$ the set totalement ordonne $\{0, 1, \ldots, n\}$ and the one regarde a object simplicial as a contravariant functor on the category of the $\Delta_n$. the action line by laquelle one divise is the action diagonale by translations a line. The fiber $EG$ is the realisation geometric of $\Delta_n \mapsto G^{n+1}$.

For the maps a the geometrie algebraic, it is commode of omettre every mention of the realisations geometriques and of define the classifiant (simplicial) $B_\bullet G$ (or simplement $BG$) as being the space topologique simplicial (1.1.1). The cohomology of the realisation geometric of a space topologique simplicial $X_\bullet$ can se define directement, without passer by the intermediaire of the realisation geometric. Voici deux definitions possibles. Leur equivalence is prouvee in Deligne (1974), 5.2 or SGA4 (t.~2), V~bis, 2.3.5, 2.3.8. The seconde definition, more calculatoire that the premiere, is celle which we servira.

\textbf{(A)} A sheaf $\mathscr{F}$ on $X_\bullet$ is a systeme of sheaves $\mathscr{F}_n$ on the $X_n$ and of morphisms $\mathscr{F}(\varphi) : X(\varphi)^* \mathscr{F}_m \to \mathscr{F}_n$ for $\varphi : \Delta_n \to \Delta_m$, with $\mathscr{F}(\psi\varphi) = \mathscr{F}(\psi) \circ \mathscr{F}(\varphi)$. The sheaves of sets on $X_\bullet$ forment a topos and the one prend the cohomology of this topos.

\textbf{(B)} Let $\mathscr{F}$ a sheaf abelien on $X_\bullet$. Let $\mathscr{F}^*$ a resolution of this sheaf, with each $\mathscr{F}^m$ acyclique on $X_n$: $H^i(X_n, \mathscr{F}^m) = 0$ for $i > 0$. By example: prendre for $\mathscr{F}^*$ the resolution flasque canonical (Godement (1958), II, 4.3) of $\mathscr{F}_n$ on $X_n$; it is fonctorielle en $(X_n, \mathscr{F}_n)$. The systeme of the $\Gamma(X_n, \mathscr{F}^m)$ is cosimplicial en $n$ and differentiel gradue en $m$. It fournit a complex double. The cohomology of $\mathscr{F}$ is celle of the complex simple associated:
\begin{equation}\label{1.1.2}
H^*(X_\bullet, \mathscr{F}) := H^*(s\Gamma(X_\bullet, \mathscr{F}^*)).
\end{equation}
For identifier the composante of degree $k$ of $s\Gamma(X_\bullet, \mathscr{F}^*)$ a the sum of the $\Gamma(X_n, \mathscr{F}^m)$ for $n + m = k$, one regarde $n$ as premiere variable and $m$ as seconde. Si $d'$ is the differentielle simpliciale and $d''$ the differentielle of $\mathscr{F}^*$, the differentielle of $s\Gamma(X_\bullet, \mathscr{F}^*)$ is then $d' + (-1)^n d''$. The filtration by $n$ fournit a spectral sequence
\begin{equation}\label{1.1.3}
E_1^{p,q} = H^q(X_p, \mathscr{F}) \Rightarrow H^{p+q}(X_\bullet, \mathscr{F}).
\end{equation}
The differentielle $d_1$ of this spectral sequence is the differentielle simpliciale $\sum (-1)^i \partial_i$.

Prenons for $\mathscr{F}$ the sheaf constant $A$. The cohomology obtained is then celle of the realisation geometric of $X_\bullet$ with coefficients in $A$, and the spectral sequence (1.1.3) is given by the filtration by the squelettes successifs.

\subsection{}\label{1.2}

To verify that the cohomology of the classifiant simplicial $B_\bullet G$ coincide with celle of the space classifiant $BG$, one can, as suggere by 1.1, ne not passer by the realisation geometric.

Indeed, let $(EG/BG)_n$ the power $(n+1)$-ieme fiber product of $EG$ on $BG$. It follows of the existence of sections local of $EG \to BG$ that the space simplicial $(EG/BG)_\bullet$ a same cohomology that $BG$ (SGA4 (t.~2), V bis (3.3.2) and (4.1.9)).

Let $(EG)_x$ the fiber of $EG$ en $x \in BG$. For $q \in (EG)_x$, $g \mapsto qg$ is a isomorphism $G \xrightarrow{\sim} (EG)_x$. Notons $p \mapsto q^{-1}p$ son inverse. the map $(EG)_x \to G^{n+1}/G : (p_i) \mapsto (q^{-1}p_i)$ is independante of the choix of $q$. It fournit a morphism of spaces topologiques simpliciaux $(EG/BG)_\bullet \to (G^{n+1}/G) = B_\bullet G$.

En each degree $n$, this morphism is isomorphic a $\pr_2 : EG \times G^n \to G^n$, therefore is a equivalence of homotopie. By (1.1.3), it induces a isomorphism en cohomology: one has
\begin{equation}\label{1.2.1}
H^*(BG) \xrightarrow{\sim} H^*((EG/BG)_\bullet) \xrightarrow{\sim} H^*(B_\bullet G).
\end{equation}

\subsection{}\label{1.3}

Si the one identifie $G^{n+1}/G$ a $G^n$ by
\begin{equation}\label{1.3.1}
(g_0, \ldots, g_n) \longmapsto (g_0 g_1^{-1}, g_1 g_2^{-1}, \ldots, g_{n-1} g_n^{-1}),
\end{equation}
the fleches simpliciales are data by the formules familieres of the cohomology of the groups. Ainsi
\begin{equation}\label{1.3.2}
\partial_0, \partial_1, \partial_2 : G^2 \to G \quad \text{sont} \quad (g,h) \mapsto h, gh, g,
\end{equation}
and $\sigma_0, \sigma_1 : G \to G^2$ are $g \mapsto (e,g), (g,e)$.

\subsection{}\label{1.4}

The construction 1.1 s'applique also for define the étale cohomology of a scheme simplicial: en 1.1 (A) or (B), remplacer ``sheaf $\mathscr{F}$'' by ``sheaf for the topologie étale''.

The cas which we interesse is celui of the classifiant simplicial $BG$ of a scheme en groups $G$ on a scheme $S$, of composantes $G^{n+1}/G$. One identifie $G^{n+1}/G$ a the power fibree $n$-ieme $G^n$ of $G$ on $S$ via (1.3.1).

For $X_\bullet$ a scheme simplicial on $\mathbf{C}$, with $X_n$ of type finite on $\mathbf{C}$, and $A$ a group abelien of twisting, the spectral sequence 1.1.3 and the theorem of comparaison SGA4 (t.~3), XVI, 4.1, appliques to the $X_n$, montrent that the étale cohomology $H^*(X_\bullet, A)$ coincide with the cohomology $H^*(X_\bullet(\mathbf{C}), A)$ of the space topologique simplicial $X_\bullet(\mathbf{C})$.

In particular, for $G$ a algebraic group on $\mathbf{C}$, and for $K$ a under-group compact maximal of the Lie group $G(\mathbf{C})$, the étale cohomology $H^*(B_\bullet G, A)$ coincide with son analogue topologique:
\begin{equation}\label{1.4.1}
H^*(B_\bullet G, A) \xrightarrow{\sim} H^*(B_\bullet G(\mathbf{C}), A) = H^*(BG(\mathbf{C}), A) = H^*(BK, A).
\end{equation}

\subsection{}\label{1.5}

We shall have need of the analogue, for $G$ a scheme en groups reductive on a scheme $S$, of the results classiques on the cohomology of the Lie groups and of leurs spaces classifiants. For $S = \Spec(\mathbf{C})$, they se deduisent of the results classiques by the theorem of comparaison. En general, locally for the topologie étale on $S$, $G$ is the inverse image of a scheme en groups reductive deploye on $\Spec(\mathbf{Z})$, and one se ramene to the cas $S = \Spec(\mathbf{C})$ en utilisant

\begin{theorem}[Rappel 1.5.1]\label{1.5.1}
Let $S$ a scheme, $n$ a integer inversible on $S$, $a : G \to S$ a scheme en groups reductive on $S$ and $A$ a sheaf abelien of twisting on $S$, annule by $n$. One denotes again $A$ son inverse image on a $S$-scheme. Then
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] The formation of the sheaves $R^i a_* A$ on $S$ is compatible a all changement of base $S' \to S$.
\item[(ii)] Si $A$ is locally constant on $S$, the $R^i a_* A$ are locally constants.
\end{itemize}

\begin{proof}
The question is local for the topologie étale on $S$. \subsection{Topologie}\label{2.9}

Let $F$ a field local: locally compact not discret. Si $G$ is a algebraic group on $F$, i.e. a scheme en groups of type finite on $S = \Spec(F)$, the group $G(F)$ is a group locally compact. The construction repose on the theorem of the functions implicites: si $f : X \to Y$ is a morphism étale of schemes of type finite on $F$, the morphism of spaces locally compacts $f : X(F) \to Y(F)$ is a homeomorphisme local. Let $\mathscr{I}^*$ as en 2.1.

\begin{lemma}\label{2.10}

\begin{proof}
Let $y \in X(F)$. the henselise $X_y$ of $X$ en $y$ is the projective limit of the voisinages etale $U$ of $y$ (scheme $U$ étale on $X$ equipped with $\tilde{y} \in U(F)$ to the-dessus of $y$). There exists therefore a neighborhood étale $U$ such that the restriction of $h$ a $U$ coincide with the inverse image on $U$ of $y^*(h)$. The function $x \mapsto x^*(h)$ is then constant of value $y^*(h)$ on the image of $U(F)$ in $X(F)$. By the theorem of the functions implicites, this image contient a neighborhood of $y$ and 2.10 en resulte.
\end{proof}

Prenons $X = G$ and $h = [c_3]$ in 2.10: the morphism (2.4.3) is locally constant and son kernel $G(F)^c$ is therefore open.

\subsection{}\label{7.1}

Let $S$, $n$, $T$, $X$, $Y$ as en (0.N.7) and $c \in H^4(BT, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$. The class $c$ defines a morphism of $T(S)$ in $H^3(S, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$ and a class of isomorphie of central extensions of the kernel $T(S)^c$ by $H^2(S, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$ (2.6). For avoir a central extension defined a isomorphism unique pres, it faut donner $c$ ``to the niveau of the cocycles''.

By analogie with Deligne (1991), j'espere that the one y parvient as suit.

\begin{espoir}[7.2]\label{7.2}
Let $Q$ a form quadratique a values entieres on $Y$, of form bilineaire associated $B$. Se donner ``to the niveau of the cocycles'' a systeme projective of classes $c_n$ in the $H^4(BT, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$ ($n$ inversible on $S$), donnant locally on $S$ a $Q$ mod $n$ (1.9.3, $i = 2$, 1.6.6), revient a se donner a central extension of sheaves on $S$
\begin{equation}\label{7.2.1}
0 \to \mathbf{G}_m \to \mathscr{E} \to Y \to 0,
\end{equation}
such that the commutateur $(Y_1, Y_2) : Y \otimes Y \to \mathbf{G}_m$ let given by
\begin{equation}\label{7.2.2}
(Y_1, Y_2) = (-1)^{B(Y_1, Y_2)}.
\end{equation}
\end{espoir}

Voici, of 7.3 a 7.6, of the indications en faveur of 7.2.

\subsection{}\label{7.3}

Let $T^\vee$ the tore on $S$ for lequel $\mathscr{H}om_S(\mathbf{G}_m, T^\vee)$ is the dual $Y^\vee$ of $Y$. The sheaf $\mathscr{H}om(Y, \mathbf{G}_m)$ of the automorphismes (trivial on $Y$ and $\mathbf{G}_m$) of a extension (7.2.1) is celui of the sections of $T^\vee$. For $t \in T(S)$ and $t' \in T^\vee(S)$, the product of the cobords defined by (3.3.1):
\[
\partial t \smile \partial t' : T(S) \otimes T^\vee(S) \to H^2(S, Y \otimes \mathbf{Z}/n(1)) \otimes H^1(S, Y^\vee \otimes \mathbf{Z}/n(1)) \to H^3(S, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))
\]
can s'interpreter as a morphism $T^\vee(S)$ in $\Hom(T(S), H^2(S, \mathbf{Z}/n(2)))$, a group which agit on every central extension of $T(S)^c$ by $H^2(S, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$.

\subsection{}\label{7.4}

Si $Q$ is datum under the form $C(y, y)$, for $C$ a form bilineaire not necessarily symmetric, the cocycle $(-1)^{C(Y_1, Y_2)}$ defines a extension (7.2.1). Parallelement, a central extension of $T(S)$ is given by the cocycle $C(\partial t_1, \partial t_2)$.

\subsection{}\label{7.5}

Let $\mathscr{P}(Y)$ the sheaf of the paires $(A, q)$: $A$ form alternee integer on $Y$, $q$ map $Y \to \mathbf{G}_m$ verifiant
\[
q(y_1 + y_2) q(y_1)^{-1} q(y_2)^{-1} = (-1)^{A(y_1, y_2)}.
\]
The form quadratique $Q$ defines a $\mathscr{A}lt(Y)$-torseur $P_Q$ under the sheaf $\mathscr{A}lt(Y)$ of the forms alternees on $Y$: celui of the forms bilineaires entieres $C$ such that $Q(y) = C(y, y)$. A extension (7.2.1) defines a lifting of $P_Q$ en a $\mathscr{P}(Y)$-torseur: the paires $(C, s)$ with $C$ as ci-dessus and $s$ a section of $\mathscr{E} \to Y$ such that
\[
s(y_1 + y_2) = s(y_1) s(y_2) (-1)^{C(y_1, y_2)}.
\]
En fait, the datum of a extension (7.2.1) equivaut a celle of a tel lifting.

For $(A, q)$ in $\mathscr{P}(Y)$ and $t$ in $T$, it devrait be possible, en imitant Deligne (1991), of define a champ $(A, q)(t)$ of bande $\mathbf{Z}/n(2)$, this construction definissant in the derived category a morphism
\begin{equation}\label{7.5.1}
\Phi : \mathscr{P}(Y) \otimes^L T \to \mathbf{Z}/n(2)[2].
\end{equation}
A morphism (7.5.1) induces
\begin{equation}\label{7.5.2}
H^1(S, \mathscr{P}(Y)) \otimes T(S) \to H^3(S, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))
\end{equation}
and, fixant in the $H^1$ the class of the $\mathscr{P}(Y)$-torseur defined by a extension (7.2.1), a morphism of $T(S)$ in $H^3(S, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$. This devrait be celui defined by the class $c$ corresponding.

\subsection{}\label{7.6}

Let $G$ a scheme en groups simple simply connected on $S$, $T$ a under-scheme en tores maximal and $X$, $Y$, $W$ as en (0.N.7). Uniformement en $n$, the generateur natural (3.6) $c_n$ of $\widetilde{H}^4(BG, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$ induces a class in $H^4(BT, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$. Puisque $H^i(BG, \mathbf{Z}/n(2)) = 0$ for $i < 4$, this class induite is a considerer as defined to the niveau of the cocycles. En all cas, the extension canonical of $G(S)^c$ by $H^2(S, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$ defined by $c$ induces a central extension of $T(S)^c$ defined a isomorphism unique pres.

Let $Q$ the form quadratique integer $W$-invariante on $Y$, verifiant $Q(H_\alpha) = 1$ for $\alpha$ a racine longue. For $S$ the spectre of a field, we construirons a extension (7.2.1) adaptee a $Q$ (7.2.2), which devrait donner the restrictions of $c$ a $BT$. Puisque, locally on $S$, $G$ and $T$ are deployes, it is enough to construct this extension for $G$ and $T$ deployes, pourvu that the construction let fonctorielle en $S$.

We donnerons deux constructions of the extension desiree. The seconde is a traduction terre-a-terre of the premiere and it a sens for every $S$.

Let therefore $G$ and $T$, deployes, on $S = \Spec(F)$, and construisons a central extension
\[
0 \to F^* \to \mathscr{E} \to Y \to 0
\]
satisfaisant (7.2.2).

\textbf{Premiere construction.} --- Etendons the scalaires a $F((t))$. The tame symbol
\[
F((t))^* \otimes F^* \to F^*
\]
fournit by Matsumoto (1969) a central extension
\[
0 \to F^* \to W \to G(F((t))) \to 0,
\]
of which one prend the inverse image by the map composee
\[
Y \to T(F((t))) \to G(F((t))).
\]

\textbf{Deuxieme construction.} --- the extension $\mathscr{E}$ is caracterisee a isomorphism unique pres by (7.2.2) and as being equipped with maps
\[
[\,] : U_\alpha - \{0\} \to \{0\}^{-1}\mathscr{E} \subset \mathscr{E}(H_\alpha)
\]
verifiant the conditions (7.6.1) and (7.6.2) suivantes.

(7.6.1) For $a \in F^*$ and $e_\alpha \in U_\alpha - \{0\}$, $[a e_\alpha] = a \alpha^{\vee}[e_\alpha]$.

Let $\SL(2)_\alpha$ the under-group $\SL(2)$ generated by $H_\alpha(\mathbf{G}_m)$ and the under-groups radiciels $U_\alpha$ and $U_{-\alpha}$. Let $N_\alpha$ the normalisateur of $H_\alpha(\mathbf{G}_m)$ in $\SL(2)$, and $N_\alpha^* := N_\alpha - H_\alpha(\mathbf{G}_m)$. For enoncer (7.6.2) we shall use the trijection of J. Tits (On the constants of structure and the theorem of existence of the algebras of Lie semi-simples, Publ. Math. IHES, 31 (1966), prop. 1, p. 25), between $U_\alpha - \{0\}$, $U_{-\alpha} - \{0\}$ and $N_\alpha^*$.

(7.6.2) For $w_\alpha, e_\alpha, e_{-\alpha}$ in the trijection of Tits and $e_3 \in U_{-\alpha} - \{0\}$,
\[
[w_\alpha(e_\alpha)] = [e_\alpha] [e_{-\alpha}]^{-1} w_\alpha^{\Phi(H_\alpha)} \eta(\Phi(H_\alpha))
\]
with $\eta(n) = (-1)^{n(n-1)/2}$. In particular,
\[
[e_{-\alpha}] [e_\alpha] = (-1)^{\Phi(H_\alpha)}.
\]

\subsection{}\label{7.7}

\begin{example}\label{7.10}
Prenons $G = \GL(N)$ over a field $F$ and prenons for $T$ a tore maximal deploye. Let $x_i$ the weight of $T$ in the representation standard and prenons
\[
Q = \sum_{i < j} x_i x_j = \frac{1}{2}\Bigl((\sum x_i)^2 - \sum x_i^2\Bigr).
\]
The restriction of $Q$ a $Y^{\der}$ is the generateur positive of the forms quadratiques entieres $W$-invariantes on $Y^{\der}$.

A representation lineaire $V$ of $G$, isomorphic a the representation tautologique, defines a extension (7.8.1): plonger $G$ in $\SL(V \oplus \det(V)^\vee)$, by $g \mapsto (g, \det(g)^{-1})$ and appliquer 7.6 a $T$ vu as tore maximal of $\SL$. Concretement, let $e_i$ the base of $Y$ duale of the base $x_i$ of the group of the characters of $T$ and let $V_i$ the under-space of weight of $V$ of weight $x_i$. the extension $\mathscr{E}$ is caracterisee, a isomorphism unique pres, as satisfaisant (7.2.2) and as being equipped with maps
\[
[\,] : V_i \otimes V_j^\vee \to \mathscr{E}(e_i - e_j) \quad \text{pour } i \neq j
\]
verifiant
\[
[ae_i \otimes e_j^\vee] = a^{e_i - e_j} [e_i \otimes e_j^\vee].
\]
The $H_\alpha$ are the $e_i - e_j$, and $U_\alpha = \Hom(V_i, V_j) \cong V_i \otimes V_j^\vee$. One identifie the extension $Y \supset Y^{\der} \hookrightarrow \mathscr{E}$ en the munissant of the
\[
[\,] : V_i \otimes V_j^\vee \to \mathscr{E}(e_i - e_j).
\]
This construction, $V \mapsto \mathscr{E}$, induces a isomorphism on the groups of automorphismes: of $V$, as representation of $\GL_N$, and of $\mathscr{E}$, as central extension prolongeant $\mathscr{E}'$. C'is therefore a equivalence of categories: it revient to the same of se donner a representation isomorphic a the representation tautologique, or a extension (7.8.1).

Prenons maintenant for $G$ a form interieure of $\GL(N)$: the group multiplicatif of a algebra simple central $D$ on $F$. Let $T$ a tore maximal. The question being local, it revient again to the same of se donner $\mathscr{E}$, or of se donner a representation lineaire $V$ of $G$ geometriquement isomorphic a the representation tautologique of $\GL(N)$: the existence of $\mathscr{E}$ is obstruee by the class of $D$ in the group of Brauer.

Si the one croit 7.8, the central extensions (3.6) of $G^{\der}(F)$ by the $H^2(F, \mathbf{Z}/n(2))$ n'have therefore not en general of extension natural a $G(F)$. By against, si $D$ is of ordre $k$ in the group of Brauer, one can esperer that the datum of a $D^\otimes$-module simple fournisse a extension natural of leur multiple by $k$.
\end{example}
