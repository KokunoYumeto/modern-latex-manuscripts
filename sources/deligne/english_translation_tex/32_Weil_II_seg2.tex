\begin{corollary}[1.8.10]
Let $\mathscr{F}_0$ a sheaf smooth on $X_0$ of type finite on $\FF_q$, and $j : U_0 \hookrightarrow X_0$ a open dense of $X_0$. Si the restriction of $\mathscr{F}_0$ a $U_0$ is ponctuellement $\iota$-pur, then $\mathscr{F}_0$ lui-same is ponctuellement $\iota$-pur.
\end{corollary}

Let $\beta$ the weight of $\mathscr{F}_0|U_0$. one has $\mathscr{F}_0 \hookrightarrow j_*j^*\mathscr{F}_0$ and $\mathscr{F}_0^\vee \hookrightarrow j_*j^*\mathscr{F}_0^\vee$, puisque $\mathscr{F}_0$ and son dual $\mathscr{F}_0^\vee$ are smooth. of after (1.8.9), $\mathscr{F}_0$ is therefore $\iota$-mixte of weight ponctuels $\leq \beta$. Puisque son dual is similarly $\iota$-mixte of weight ponctuels $\leq -\beta$, ses weight ponctuels are also $\geq \beta$, and (1.8.10) en resulte.

It n'is en fait not necessary of invoquer (1.8.9): by restriction a of the curves and normalisation, one se ramene facilement to the cas or $X_0$ is a curve smooth, and one invoque directement (1.8.7).

\begin{corollary}[1.8.11]
All sheaf smooth $\iota$-mixte $\mathscr{F}_0$ on $X_0$ normal of type finite on $\FF_q$ is extension successive of sheaves smooth ponctuellement $\iota$-purs.
\end{corollary}

One can supposer $X_0$ connected, and one se ramene a supposer the sheaf irreducible. Puisque $X_0$ is normal, sa restriction a quelconque open not vide $U_0$ is again irreducible --- therefore ponctuellement $\iota$-pur for $U_0$ suitable, by definition of the sheaves $\iota$-mixtes --- and one applique (1.8.10).

We verrons more tard (3.4.1) that (1.8.11) vaut without hypothese of normalite.

\begin{corollary}[1.8.12]
Let $\mathscr{F}_0$ a sheaf smooth $\iota$-mixte on $X_0$ connected and of type finite on $\FF_q$. Si $\mathscr{F}_0$ is $\iota$-pur en a point of $X_0$, it is ponctuellement $\iota$-pur.
\end{corollary}

Supposons of abord $X_0$ normal. It follows then of (1.8.11) that the weight of $\mathscr{F}_0$ se lisent on sa fiber en a point arbitraire of $X_0$. In the cas general, en appliquant (1.8.11) a the inverse image of $\mathscr{F}_0$ on the normalise of $X_0$, one trouve that the set of the points of $X_0$ or $\mathscr{F}_0$ is pur of a weight given $\beta$, contient with a point closed $x_0$ all the composantes irreductibles of $X_0$ passant by $x_0$. It is therefore open and closed.

\paragraph{1.8.14}
In the dictionnaire heuristique of [2], I, the theorem (1.8.4) correspond a the theorie of W.~Schmid of the comportement asymptotique of the variations of Hodge structures polarisables on the disque epointe $D^*$. The corollary (1.8.5) suggere the probleme suivant:

Appelons \emph{variation of mixed Hodge structures} on a space analytic smooth $S$ the datum of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item a systeme local $V_\ZZ$ of $\ZZ$-modules libres of type finite;
\item a filtration finite croissante $W$ of $V_\QQ = V_\ZZ \otimes \QQ$ by of the under-systemes local of vector spaces on $\QQ$;
\item a filtration finite decroissante $F$ of $V = V_\ZZ \otimes \CC$ by of the under-fibers vector (i.e. the filtration $F$ varie in such a way holomorphic); one exige that $F$ satisfies the axiome of transversalite: $\nabla F^i \subset \Omega^1 \otimes F^{i-1}$ and that $W$ and $F$ definissent en each point of $S$ a mixed Hodge structure.
\end{enumerate}
The variations which apparaissent en geometrie algebraic have of the properties additionnelles; by example, the variations of Hodge structures $\Gr^W(V)$ are polarisables.

Let $(V, W, F)$ a variation of mixed Hodge structures on $D^*$, $t \in D^*$, $\overline{V}$ the fiber of $V$ en $t$ and $T \in \End(\overline{V})$ the transformation of monodromy. One assumed for simplifier that $T$ is unipotente, and one sets $N = \log T$.

\begin{probleme}[1.8.15]
Degager a class of ``bonnes'' variations of mixed Hodge structures on $D^*$ such that, for $(V, W, F)$ bonne (and unipotente), there exists on $\overline{V}$ a filtration finite croissante $M$, such that $NM_i \subset M_{i-2}$, and that $N$ induise of the isomorphisms:
\[ N^b : \Gr_{a+b}^W \Gr_a^M \overline{V} \xrightarrow{\sim} \Gr_{a-b}^W \Gr_a^M \overline{V}. \]
One aimerait also that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item the variation $V$ let asymptotique, en a sens suitable, a ``orbite nilpotente'': a bonne variation for which $F_t \circ \exp(uN)$ is the transforme by $\exp(-uN)$ of the filtration deduite of $F_t$ by transport horizontal of $t$ a $t \cdot \exp(u)$;
\item for a orbite nilpotente, $(\overline{V}, M, F)$ let again a variation of mixed Hodge structures.
\end{enumerate}
\end{probleme}


\subsection{Local monodromy of the sheaves mixtes}

In this numero, which ne will be not utilise by the sequence, we generalisons (1.8.7) to the cas of the diviseurs a croisements normaux.

\paragraph{1.9.1}
Let $X$ a scheme smooth on $\FF_q$, $D$ a diviseur a croisements normaux of $X$, reunion transverse of diviseurs smooth $D_i$ ($i \in I$) and $E$ the intersection of the $D_i$. One assumed each $D_i$ defined by a equation global $t_i = 0$, for pouvoir appliquer the construction (1.7.8) (cf. toutefois (1.7.10)).

Let moreover $\mathscr{F}$ a sheaf smooth on $X - D$, moderement ramifie the long of $D$. One assumed that it admet a filtration finite croissante $W$ by of the under-sheaves smooth, such that $\Gr_i^W(\mathscr{F})$ let ponctuellement $\iota$-pur of weight $i$ (we verrons en (3.4.1) that all sheaf smooth $\iota$-mixte of weight ponctuels integers admet a telle filtration). Let $L$ the set of all the nombres prime $\ell \neq p$, and definissons $\mathscr{F}[E]$ as en (1.7.8). C'is a sheaf smooth on $E$, equipped with a action of $\ZZ_L(1)^I$; this action is quasi-unipotente, of or of the ``endomorphisms'' $N_i : \mathscr{F}[E](i) \to \mathscr{F}[E]$ (1.7.8).

Being donnes a sheaf $\mathscr{N}$, deux filtrations finite croissantes $W'$ and $W''$ of $\mathscr{N}$ and a family finite of ``endomorphisms'' $N_\lambda : \mathscr{N}(1) \to \mathscr{N}$ ($\lambda \in \Lambda$), we shall have a considerer the condition:

$\mathbf{L}(W', W'', (N_\lambda)_{\lambda \in \Lambda})$: The $N_\lambda$ stabilisent $W'$, satisfy $N_\lambda W'_i(1) \subset W'_{i-2}$ and quels that let the nombres rationnels $c_\lambda > 0$, the endomorphism $N = \sum c_\lambda N_\lambda$ induces of the isomorphisms:
\[ N^b : (\Gr_{a+b}^{W'} \Gr_a^{W''} \mathscr{N})(b) \xrightarrow{\sim} \Gr_{a-b}^{W''} \Gr_a^{W'} \mathscr{N}. \]

It follows of (1.6.13) that, $W'$ and the family of the $N_\lambda$ being donnes, there exists to the more a filtration $W''$ verifiant this condition. In son states, it is loisible of remplacer ``nombre rational $> 0$'' by ``nombre integer $> 0$'': remplacer the $c_\lambda$ by $nc_\lambda$ and $N$ by $nN$.


\begin{theorem}[1.9.2]
With the hypotheses and notations of (1.9.1), there exists on $\mathscr{F}[E]$ a and a seul systeme of filtrations finite croissantes $W(J)$ ($J \subset I$) by of the under-sheaves smooth stables by $\ZZ_L(1)^I$ (therefore by the $N_i$), such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item $W(\emptyset)$ is the filtration deduite of $W$ by fonctorialite;
\item for $J \subset K$, the condition $\mathbf{L}(W(J), W(K), (N_k)_{k \in K-J})$ is verifiee.
\end{enumerate}
En outre, $\Gr_a^{W(\emptyset)} \mathscr{F}[E]$ is ponctuellement $\iota$-pur of weight $a$.
\end{theorem}

the unicite of the $W(J)$ resulte aussitot of (1.6.13) (and it is enough to disposer of the conditions a), and b) for $\emptyset \subset K$). For prouver the existence, we traiterons of abord a cas particulier.

\begin{lemma}[1.9.3]
Let $(c_k)_{k \in I}$ a systeme of nombres integers $> 0$. The sheaf $\mathscr{F}[E]$ admet a (unique) filtration finite croissante $W(I)$ by of the under-sheaves smooth, such that $N = \sum c_k N_k$ satisfies the condition $\mathbf{L}(W, W(I), N)$.

En outre $\Gr^{W(I)} \mathscr{F}[E]$ is ponctuellement $\iota$-pur of weight $a$.
\end{lemma}

Vu the unicite (1.6.13), si for every point geometric $\overline{\xi}$ of $E$, localise en a point closed $x$, there exists a filtration finite croissante $M$ on $\mathscr{F}[E]_{\overline{\xi}}$ satisfaisant a $\mathbf{L}(W, M, N)$ then these filtrations se recollent en a filtration $M$ of $\mathscr{F}[E]$ by of the under-sheaves smooth, satisfaisant a $\mathbf{L}(W, W(I), N)$. It is enough same of prouver the existence of $M$ en a point of each composante connected of $E$.

This we ramene, for prouver (1.9.3), a montrer that for $\overline{\xi}$ and $x$ as ci-dessus, $M$ exists and that $\Gr^M \mathscr{F}[E]_{\overline{\xi}}$ is pur of weight $a$. We the verifierons by reduction a (1.8.5). A extension preliminaire of the finite field of base we ramene a supposer that $x$ is a point rational. One complete then the $t_i$ en a systeme of coordonnees local $(t_i, u_\alpha)$, centre en $x$ (a map étale of a neighborhood of $x$ in the affine space type $\AA^n$ ($n$ suitable) envoyant $x$ on the origine). Restreignons $\mathscr{F}$ a the curve of equation parametree $u_\alpha = 0$, $t_i = t^{c_i}$. More precisely: one envoie the line affine (coordonnee $t$) in the affine space $\AA^n$ of coordonnees $t_i$, $u_\alpha$ by $t \mapsto (t^{c_i}, 0)$, and one prend the inverse image of $\mathscr{F}$ on the curve $C$ fiber product of a neighborhood of $x$, and of the line affine, to the-dessus of $\AA^n$. Notons again $x$ (resp. $\overline{\xi}$) the point (resp. the point geometric) of $C$ to the-dessus of $x$ (resp. $\overline{\xi}$) in $X$, and to the-dessus of $0$ in the line affine, and passons to the localise strict $C_{(\overline{\xi})}$. The fiber generique geometric on $C_{(\overline{\xi})}$ of $\mathscr{F}$ s'identifie a $\mathscr{F}[E]_{\overline{\xi}}$. Via this identification, the logarithme of the monodromy devient $N$, and (1.9.3) resulte of (1.8.5).

\paragraph{1.9.4}
Deduisons the theorem of son cas particulier (1.9.3). One remark of abord that the filtration $W(I)$ of (1.9.3) ne depend not of the choix of the $c_k$, puisque caracterisee independamment of ceux-ci as being the weight filtration.

For $J \subset I$, let $X_J$, $D_J$ and $D_J^\circ$ as en (1.7.9), and appliquons (1.9.3) a $X_J$, to the traces of the $D_j$ ($j \in J$), and a leur intersection $D_J^\circ$. one has $E = D_\emptyset^\circ$, and, on $\mathscr{F}[E]$, one defines enfin $W(J)$ as deduces by fonctorialite of the filtration $W(J)$ of $\mathscr{F}[D_J^\circ]$, via the isomorphism $\mathscr{F}[E] = \mathscr{F}[D_J^\circ][E]$. one has a) by definition, and the condition b) se deduces of $\mathbf{L}(W(J), W(K), (N_k)_{k \in K-J})$ for $\mathscr{F}[D_K^\circ]$.

\subsection{Values absolues not archimediennes}

The conventions (0.7) are en vigueur.

Let $X_0$ a curve smooth absolument irreducible on $\FF_q$, $j : U_0 \hookrightarrow X_0$ a open of $X_0$, complement of the set finite of points $S_0$, and $\mathscr{F}_0$ a sheaf smooth on $U_0$. To the n$^\circ$ (1.8), we have exploite the formule of the traces, under the form (1.4.7.2), and the structure of the local monodromy, for etudier the value absolute archimedienne of the values proper of Frobenius on $\mathscr{F}_0$, en the points of $S_0$ (to the sens (1.10.2) ci-dessous). In this numero, we etudierons of the values absolues not necessarily archimediennes.

Choisissons therefore Let) a isomorphism $\iota$ of $\QQ_\ell$ with $\CC$ (cas archimedien), let b) a nombre prime $\ell'$, a algebraic closure $\QQbar_{\ell'}$ of $\QQ_{\ell'}$, and a isomorphism $\iota$ of $\QQ_\ell$ with $\QQbar_{\ell'}$ (cas not archimedien). One normalisera the value absolute natural of $\QQbar_{\ell'}$ by $\|\ell'\| = 1/\ell'$, by example.

The lemma (1.8.1) se generalise en the

\begin{lemma}[1.10.1]
Let $b > 0$. Si, for every point $x$ of $U_0$ and every value proper $\alpha$ of $F_x$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$ one has $\|\alpha\| \leq b^{\deg(x)}$, then, for every point $x$ of $|S_0|$, and every value proper $\alpha$ of $F_x$ on $j_*\mathscr{F}_0$, one has again $\|\alpha\| \leq b^{\deg(x)}$.
\end{lemma}

Reprenons the proof of (1.8.1). We we placerons in the cas not archimedien, and indiquerons under form of resp. the modifications a apporter for traiter the cas archimedien (which is a legere generalisation of (1.8.1)).

In (1.8.1.1), the prime factor to the prime membre converge for $|t| < 1/b$ (resp. $|t| < 1/q^b$ (1.4.6)). The second membre n'a not of pole for $|t| < 1/\|q\|^b$, therefore for $|t| < 1/b$, by (1.4.4) (resp. for $|t| < 1/q^b$). The factors restant to the prime membre:
\[ \det(1 - F_x t^{\deg(x)}, j_*\mathscr{F}_0) \quad (x \in |S_0|) \]
are therefore without pole for $|t| < 1/b$ (resp. $|t| < 1/\sqrt{b}$), this which proves (1.10.1) (resp. proves the result of which one deduces (1.10.1) en remplacant $\mathscr{F}_0$ by ses powers tensorielles, as en (1.8.1)).

\paragraph{1.10.2}
Let $s \in |S_0|$, $\overline{\eta}$ a point geometric of $s$, and $\mathscr{F}_{\overline{\eta}}$ the representation $\iota$ of the group of Weil $W(\overline{\eta}/\eta)$. We appellerons ``values proper of $F_s$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$'' the values proper of a lifting of $F_s \in W(\overline{\eta}/\eta)$ in $W(\overline{\eta}/\eta)$, agissant on $\mathscr{F}$. This terminologie ne will be utilisee that lorsque the choix of the lifting n'importe not (cf. (1.7.4)).


\begin{theorem}[1.10.3]
One se place in the cas not archimedien. Let $0 < b < c < \infty$. Si for every point $x \in |U_0|$ and for every value proper $\alpha$ of $F_x$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$, one has $b^{\deg(x)} \leq \|\alpha\| \leq c^{\deg(x)}$, then for every point $s \in S_0$ and for every value proper $\alpha$ of $F_s$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$, one has again:
\[ b^{\deg(s)} \leq \|\alpha\| \leq c^{\deg(s)}. \]
\end{theorem}

Let $s \in |S_0|$. As en (1.8.4), one passe a covering finite for supposer the local monodromy unipotente: the action of $I \subset W(\overline{\eta}/\eta)$ on $\mathscr{F}_{\overline{\eta}}$ s'ecrit:
\[ \sigma \mapsto \exp(\iota(\sigma) \cdot N). \]
The lemma (1.10.1) assure that the values proper of $F_s$ on $\Ker(N)$ satisfont $\|\alpha\| \leq c^{\deg(s)}$. Puisque $\|q\| < 1$, it en va similarly of the values proper of $F_s$ on $\mathscr{F}_{\overline{\eta}}$ all integer (1.6.14.3). the inegalite $b^{\deg(s)} \leq \|\alpha\|$ s'obtient by passage to the sheaf dual.

\paragraph{1.10.4}
Si the one applique the theorem (1.10.3) to the powers exterieures of $\mathscr{F}_0$, one obtains a relation between the polygones of Newton attached to the $F_x$ ($x \in |U_0|$), and to the $F_s$ ($s \in |S_0|$). We n'enoncerons the result that for $\ell' = p$ (1.10.7): for $\ell' \neq p$, the values proper of Frobenius are en pratique toujours of the unites $\ell'$-adiques, and (1.10.3) n'is jusqu'ici utile that under the following form:


\begin{corollary}[1.10.5]
Si, for every point $x \in |U_0|$, the values proper $\alpha$ of $F_x$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$ are of the unites $\ell'$-adiques (i.e. si $\|\alpha\| = 1$), then, for every point $s \in |S_0|$, the values proper of $F_s$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$ are again of the unites $\ell'$-adiques.
\end{corollary}

En rapetissant $U_0$, one can remplacer the hypothese ``for every $x \in |U_0|$'', by ``for all sauf a nombre finite'', and recuperer gratuitement the $x$ manquants.

\paragraph{1.10.6}
For $\ell' = p$, it is utile of passer of the langage of the values absolues a celui of the valuations. For $N$ a power of $p$, let therefore $v_N$ the valuation of $\QQ_p$, normalisee by $v_N(N) = 1$.

Let $(\alpha_i)_{i \in I}$ a family finite of elements of $\QQ_p$. Son polygone of Newton rel.~$N$ is the graphe of the function continue lineaire by morceau $n$ of $[0, |I|]$ in $\RR$ caracterisee as suit: $n(0) = 0$, and si the one range the $v_N(\alpha_i)$ en a sequence croissante, indexee by the integers between $1$ and $|I|$, the pente of $n$ between $j-1$ and $j$ is the $j$-ieme of these valuations. The value of $n$ en the integer $j$ ($0 < j < |I|$) is therefore the more petite valuation of a product of $j$ $\alpha_i$ of indices distincts.

For $x \in |X_0|$, the polygone of Newton of $\mathscr{F}_0$ en $x$, rel.~$\ell$, is the polygone of Newton, rel.~$N(x)$, of the image by $\iota$ of the systeme of the values proper of $F_x$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$. The value en the integer $j$ ($0 \leq j \leq \rank \mathscr{F}_0$) of the function corresponding is the more petit of the nombres $v_{N(x)}(\wedge^j \alpha)$, for $\alpha$ value proper of $F_x$ on $\wedge^j \mathscr{F}_0$.


\begin{corollary}[1.10.7]
Assume that, for presque all $x \in |U_0|$, $\mathscr{F}_0$ ait en $x$ the same polygone of Newton $n$, rel.~$\ell$. Then, for $s \in |S_0|$, the polygone of Newton $n_s$ of $\mathscr{F}_0$ en $s$ is to the-dessus of $n$: $n_s \geq n$, and a the same extremites.
\end{corollary}

The minoration $n_s \geq n$ reformule the majoration (1.10.3) of the values absolues of the values proper of $F_s$, for the sheaves $\wedge^j \mathscr{F}_0$. The complement ``same extremite'': $n_s(\rank \mathscr{F}_0) = n(\rank \mathscr{F}_0)$, resulte of this that for a sheaf of rank a (en the occurrence, the power exterieure maximale of $\mathscr{F}_0$), the valuation $v_N(\alpha)$ of $\alpha_x$, for $\alpha$ the value proper of $F_x$, is independante of $x$ (cf. (1.3.3)).

\paragraph{1.10.8}
One obtains of the results more precise en tenant compte of the filtration of monodromy $M$. By example:


\begin{corollary}[1.10.9]
Let $\beta \leq \gamma$, and assume that, for presque all $x \in |U_0|$, the values proper $\alpha$ of $F_x$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$ satisfont a $\beta \leq v_{N(x)}(\alpha) \leq \gamma$. Then, si $n$ is the part integer of $\gamma - \beta$, the logarithme $N$ of the part unipotente of the local monodromy, en $s \in |S_0|$, satisfait $N^{n+1} = 0$.
\end{corollary}

Indeed, si $m$ is the more petit integer such that $N^{m+1} = 0$, $F_s$ admet of the values proper $\alpha$ and $\alpha \cdot N(s)^m$, and one applique (1.10.3).


\subsection{Specialisation of the monodromy}

\begin{proposition}[1.11.1]
Let $S$ a scheme irreducible, $f : X \to S$ a morphism smooth purement of dimension relative $1$, a fibers geometriquement connected, $g$ a section of $f$ and $\mathscr{F}$ a $\ZZ_\ell$-sheaf smooth on $X$. \end{proposition}

Let $I_n$ the covering finite étale $\Isom(\mathscr{F}/\ell^n \mathscr{F}, f^*g^*\mathscr{F}/\ell^n \mathscr{F})$ of $X$. one has:
\[ g^* I_n = \Aut(g^*\mathscr{F}/g^*\mathscr{F}). \]
the automorphism identique we fournit a section $g_n$ of $I_n$ to the-dessus of $g$. After avoir rapetisse $S$, we construisons simultanement the $M^n$, and a tour of coverings finite etale $X_n \subset I_n$, contenant the section $g_n$, stables by $M^n$, and such that, fiber geometric a fiber geometric, $X_n$ let the covering galoisien of $X$ which trivialise $\mathscr{F}/\ell^n$. One aura $M^n = g^*(X_n/X)$.

Let $F$ the fiber of $\mathscr{F}$ en a point geometric. The proof repose on the faits that $\Aut(F/g^*F)$ is finite, tandis that the group of the automorphismes of $F$ trivial on $F/g^*F$ is a pro-$\ell$-group.

Prenons the composante neutre of the section $g_n$ of $I_1$. To the-dessus of a open suitable $U_1$ of $S$, this construction is compatible to the changement of base (cf. EGA IV (9.7.7)). It we fournit, on $U_1$, the covering $X_1$ cherche. One prend $M^1 = g^* X_1$.

\paragraph{2.1.1}
Let $\Gamma$ a group isomorphic a $\ZZ$ or a $\RR$, $\omega$ a quasi-character not trivial $\omega : \Gamma \to \RR_{+}^*$, $G$ a group locally compact extension of $\Gamma$ by a group compact $G^0$, $\Sigma$ a set infini denombrable, and $(x_\nu)_{\nu \in \Sigma}$ a family indexee by $\Sigma$ of classes of conjugaison in $G$. One fait the hypotheses (A), (B) suivantes. The reader trouvera of the examples en (2.1.2), (2.1.3), (2.1.9) and to the numero suivant.

(A$'$) Si $\Gamma$ is isomorphic a $\RR$, the extension $G$ is trivial.

This hypothese is en fait automatiquement verifiee.

(A$''$) Si $\Gamma$ is isomorphic a $\ZZ$, the centre of $G$ s'envoie on a under-group of indice finite of $\Gamma$.

For demontrer (2.2.10), seul utilise in the proof of the theorem principal, one pourrait se contenter of the cas or $G$ is the product of $\Gamma = \ZZ$ by a Lie group compact $G^0$ of composante connected $G^{00}$ semisimple.

The condition (A$''$) n'is not necessary for prouver the theorem (2.1.4) ci-dessous: the group n'y apparait that via ses representations lineaires (continues complexes, of dimension finite), and si $G$ admet a representation lineaire of which the restriction a $G^0$ a kernel finite, (A$''$) is automatiquement satisfaite (1.3.10).

For $s \in \CC$, posons $\omega_s = \omega^s$. Notant again $\omega_s$ the morphism compose $G \to \Gamma \xrightarrow{\omega_s} \CC^*$, one sets $N_\nu = \omega_{-1}(x_\nu)$. Si $\Gamma$ is isomorphic a $\ZZ$, one denotes $q$ and $\deg$ the nombre $> 1$ and the isomorphism of $\Gamma$ with $\ZZ$ such that $\omega_1(\gamma) = q^{-\deg(\gamma)}$. one has $\omega_s = \omega \cdot q^{-s \deg}$. Notant again $\deg$ the morphism compose $G \to \Gamma \xrightarrow{\deg} \ZZ$, one sets $\deg(\nu) = \deg(x_\nu)$.

Let $g$ a element of the centre of $G$ of image not trivial in $\Gamma$. It en exists by hypothese. A representation lineaire complex $v : G \to GL(V)$ is unitarisable if and only if $v(g)$ the is: of a structure hermitienne invariante by $g$ one deduces a invariante by $G$ by integration on the group compact $G/g^\ZZ$. Si $v$ is irreducible, therefore $v(g)$ scalaire, there exists a unique nombre reel $a$ such that $|v(g)| = \omega_a(g)$, and $v \cdot \omega_{-a}$ is unitarisable. One appelle $a$ the part reelle $\Re(v)$ of $v$. one has $\Re(v \cdot \omega_s) = \Re(v) + \Re(s)$.

Let $\widehat{G}$ the set of the classes of isomorphie of representations irreductibles of $G$, and $\widehat{G}_u$ the set of celles which are unitaires. The sets $\{v \cdot \omega_s \mid s \in \CC\}$ forment a partition of $\widehat{G}$, and the map $s \mapsto v \cdot \omega_s$ identifie $\{v \cdot \omega_s \mid s \in \CC\}$ to the quotient of $\CC$ by a under-group discret of $i\RR$ si $\Gamma \simeq \ZZ$, and a $\CC$ si $\Gamma \simeq \RR$. We munirons $\widehat{G}$ of the structure of surface of Riemann for which it is the sum disjointe of these quotients.


(B$'$) For every $\nu \in \Sigma$, one has $N_\nu \geq 1$.

(B$''$) The product infini $\prod_{\nu \in \Sigma} (1 - N_\nu^{-s})^{-1}$ converge absolument for $\Re(s) > 1$.

For $\Gamma$ isomorphic a $\ZZ$, these conditions can se reecrire: $\deg(\nu) \geq 0$, and, for every $\varepsilon > 0$:
\[ \#\{\nu \mid \deg(\nu) = n\} = O(q^{n(1+\varepsilon)}). \]

the hypothese (B$''$) assure that, for $v \in \widehat{G}$, the product infini:
\[ L(v) = \prod_{\nu \in \Sigma} \det(1 - v(x_\nu))^{-1} \]
converge absolument for $\Re(v) > 1$. Each factor is holomorphic en $v$ for $\Re(v) > 0$, and $L(v)$ is function holomorphic of $v$ for $\Re(v) > 1$. One sets $L(v, s) = L(v \cdot \omega_s)$.

\begin{theorem}[2.1.4]
With the hypotheses and notations of (2.1.1), assume that the function $L(v)$ se prolonge en a function meromorphe of $v$ for $\Re(v) > 1$ and that, in this region $\Re(v) > 1$, it is holomorphic sauf for a pole simple en $\omega_1$. Then, the function $L(v)$ ne s'annule not for $\Re(v) = 1$, sauf for to the more a representation $v_0$, of dimension $1$ and defined by a character $\omega_{1/2}$ with $\omega$ of ordre $2$.
\end{theorem}

For a representation $v$ not necessarily irreducible, one definira again $L(v)$ and $L(v,s)$ as en (2.1.1). One sets $L'(v) = \frac{\partial}{\partial s} L(v,s)|_{s=0}$. one has in the domaine of convergence:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:2.1.4.1}
\frac{L'}{L}(v) = \sum_{\substack{\nu \in \Sigma \\ n > 0}} \log N_\nu \cdot \Tr v(x_\nu^n).
\end{equation}

One can more generally prendre for $v$ a representation virtuelle, i.e. a element of the group of Grothendieck of the category of representations of $G$. For $v$ unitaire and $a$ reel $\geq 1$, $\omega_a \cdot v$ is in the domaine of convergence and (2.1.4.1), applique a $\omega_a \cdot v$, se recrit:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:2.1.4.2}
\frac{L'}{L}(\omega_a \cdot v) = \sum_{\substack{\nu \in \Sigma \\ n > 0}} (\log N_\nu \cdot (N_\nu)^{-na}) \Tr v(x_\nu^n).
\end{equation}

Let $\mu$ a mesure on $G$, voire on the set of the classes of conjugaison of $G$. For every representation unitaire virtuelle $v$ of $G$, we poserons:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:2.1.4.3}
\widehat{\mu}(v) = \int \Tr v(g)\, d\mu.
\end{equation}
the integral converge si the masse totale of $|\mu|$ is finite. We appellerons then the function $\widehat{\mu} : v \mapsto \widehat{\mu}(v)$ the transformee of Fourier of $\mu$. Si the one n'impose not a $v$ of be unitaire, one parle similarly of transformee of Fourier-Laplace. Si $\mu$ is positive, of masse totale finite, one has for every representation unitaire virtuelle $v$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:2.1.4.4}
\widehat{\mu}(v \otimes \overline{v}) \geq 0 \quad \text{(pour } v \neq 0 \text{)}.
\end{equation}

The formule (2.1.4.1) exprime that, for $\Re(s) > 1$, $\Lambda_0(v) = \frac{L'}{L}(\omega_0 \cdot v)$ is the transformee of Fourier of the mesure positive of masse totale finite:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:2.1.4.5}
\mu_0 = \sum_{\substack{\nu \in \Sigma \\ n > 0}} \log N_\nu \cdot N_\nu^{-n} \delta_{[x_\nu^n]}
\end{equation}
on $G$ (one ecrit $\delta_{[a]}$ for the mesure of Dirac en $a$). For every representation unitaire virtuelle $v$ of $G$, of character reel and $\geq 0$, one has therefore $\Lambda_0(v) \geq 0$ for $\Re(s) > 1$; in particular, for every representation unitaire virtuelle $v$, one has:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:2.1.4.6}
\Lambda_0(v \otimes \overline{v}) \geq 0 \quad \text{(pour } \Re(s) > 1 \text{)}.
\end{equation}

For $v \in \widehat{G}$, let $r(v)$ the ordre of the pole (the oppose of the ordre of the zero) of $L$ en $v \cdot \omega_1$. One prolonge $r$ by additivite to the group of Grothendieck of the category of representations unitaires of $G$. For $v$ in this group, the function $\frac{L'}{L}(v \cdot \omega_s)$ n'a that of the poles simples, and son residu en $v \cdot \omega_1$ ($v \in \widehat{G}$) is $r(v)$. Faisant tendre $s$ toward $1$ in (2.1.4.6), one trouve therefore that for every representation unitaire virtuelle of $G$:
\[ r(v \otimes \overline{v}) \geq 0. \]

The theorem resulte of the lemma suivant:

\begin{lemma}[2.1.5]
Let $r$ a function a values entieres on $\widehat{G}$. One assumed that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item for the representation trivial $1$, $r(1) = 1$;
\item $r(\overline{v}) = r(v)$;
\item $r(v) \leq 0$ for $v \neq 1$;
\end{enumerate}
prolongeant $r$ by additivite to the group of Grothendieck of the representations unitaires of $G$, one has, for every representation unitaire $v$ of $G$:
\[ r(v \otimes \overline{v}) \geq 0. \]
Then, $r(v) = 0$ for $v \neq 1$, sauf for to the more a representation $v_0$ of dimension a and defined by a character of ordre deux.
\end{lemma}

In this lemma, $G$ n'apparait that via ses representations unitaires. This we permet of the remplacer by a group compact (son adherence in the product of ses representations unitaires) and of ramener (2.1.5) a the variante suivante.
