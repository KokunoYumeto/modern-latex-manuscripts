This article expose some r\'{e}sultats of finitude on the repr\'{e}sentations of monodromy. The more frappant is the

\begin{theorem}[0.1]
Fixons $S$, $o \in S$ and a integer $N$. The repr\'{e}sentations of monodromy $\sigma_{\ZZ} : \pi_{1}(S,o) \to \Aut(H^{i}(X_{o}, \ZZ))$ ($i \leq N$, $X$ variable) which are of dimension $N$ ne forment that a nombre finite of classes of isomorphie.
\end{theorem}

We shall prove also the r\'{e}sultat more pr\'{e}cis:

\begin{variante}[0.2]
Fixons $S$, $o \in S$ and a integer $N$. The repr\'{e}sentations rationnelles of dimension $N$ of $\pi_{1}(S,o)$, which are direct summands of a repr\'{e}sentation of monodromy ne forment that a nombre finite of classes of isomorphie.
\end{variante}

These \'{e}nonc\'{e}s are for the repr\'{e}sentations rationnelles fournies by the monodromy. The lemmas bien connus suivants permettent of traiter also of repr\'{e}sentations on the integers.

\begin{lemma}[0.3]
Let $\sigma : \Gamma \to \GL(V)$ a repr\'{e}sentation semisimple of a group $\Gamma$ on a space vector rational of dimension finite $V$. The repr\'{e}sentations $\rho : \Gamma \to \Aut(L)$, for $L \subset V$ a r\'{e}seau integer stable by $\Gamma$, ne forment that a nombre finite of classes of isomorphie.
\end{lemma}

The proof is rappel\'{e}e en 2.6.

\begin{lemma}[0.4]
Let $H$ a $\ZZ$-module of type finite, $T$ son under-group of twisting, $L = H/T$ and $\sigma : \Gamma \to \Aut(L)$ a repr\'{e}sentation of a group $\Gamma$ on $L$. Si $\Gamma$ is of type finite, it n'y a that a nombre finite of rel\`{e}vements of $\sigma$ en a action of $\Gamma$ on $H$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The kernel of $\Aut(H) \to \Aut(L)$ is finite, car contenu in the set finite $1 + \Hom(H, T)$. For each g\'{e}n\'{e}rateur $\gamma$ of $\Gamma$, $\sigma(\gamma)$ n'a therefore that a nombre finite of rel\`{e}vements in $\Aut(H)$.
\end{proof}

\section{Variations Complexes}

\subsection*{1.1}
Let $S$ a vari\'{e}t\'{e} analytic complex smooth. A \emph{variation complex} of weight $0$ on $S$ is the donn\'{e}e of a syst\`{e}me local complex $V$ on $S$, of fibers $V_{s}$ ($s \in S$) equipped with a d\'{e}composition $V_{s} = \bigoplus_{p \in \ZZ} V_{s}^{p}$, satisfaisant to the axiomes suivants.

\begin{itemize}
\item[(V.C.1)] The under-spaces $F^{p} := \bigoplus_{i \geq p} V_{s}^{i}$ (resp. $\bar{F}^{q} := \bigoplus_{i \leq q} V_{s}^{i}$) of $V_{s}$ varient holomorphiquement (resp. antiholomorphiquement) with $s$.
\item[(V.C.2)] Si a section local diff\'{e}rentiable $v$ of $V$ is en each point in $F^{p}$ (resp. $\bar{F}^{q}$), sa d\'{e}riv\'{e}e with respect to} a champ of vecteurs on $S$ is in $F^{p-1}$ (resp. $\bar{F}^{q-1}$).
\end{itemize}

Si $\mathcal{V}$ is the sheaf of the sections $C^{\infty}$ of $V$ and that $\nabla$ is the connexion, vue as morphism of sheaves $\nabla : \mathcal{V} \to \Omega^{1}(\mathcal{V})$, the d\'{e}compositions of the $V_{s}$ en $\bigoplus V_{s}^{p}$ fournissent a d\'{e}composition $\mathcal{V} = \bigoplus \mathcal{V}^{p}$ of $\mathcal{V}$, and (V.C.1), (V.C.2) \'{e}quivalent \`{a} this that $\nabla$ envoie $\mathcal{V}^{i}$ in $\Omega^{1,0}(\mathcal{V}^{i-1}) \oplus \Omega^{1,0}(\mathcal{V}^{i}) \oplus \Omega^{0,1}(\mathcal{V}^{i}) \oplus \Omega^{0,1}(\mathcal{V}^{i+1})$.

In what follows, we ne consid\'{e}rerons that of the variations complexes of weight $0$ and omettrons of pr\'{e}ciser ``of weight $0$''.

For $S$ connected, the \emph{nombres of Hodge} $h^{p}$ are the dimensions $h^{p} := \dim V_{s}^{p}$, ind\'{e}pendantes of $s \in S$. Of m\^{e}me, the dimension of $V$ is celle of the $V_{s}$.

A \emph{polarisation} of the variation complex $V$ is a form hermitienne horizontale $\psi$ on $V$ such that en each point $s \in S$ the d\'{e}composition $V_{s} = \bigoplus V_{s}^{p}$ let orthogonale, and that the restriction of $\psi$ \`{a} $V_{s}^{p}$ let d\'{e}finite positive for $p$ pair and d\'{e}finite n\'{e}gative for $p$ impair. One posera $\mathcal{Q} = \bigoplus_{p} (-1)^{p} \psi|V^{p}$, c'is a form hermitienne d\'{e}finite positive on the fibr\'{e} vector $V$.

\subsection*{1.3}
Sp\'{e}cialisons 1.1 to the cas o\`{u} $S$ is r\'{e}duit \`{a} a point: a \emph{Hodge structure complex} on a space vector complex $H$ is a d\'{e}composition $H = \bigoplus H^{p}$; ses nombres of Hodge are $h^{p} := \dim H^{p}$.

A \emph{polarisation} is a form hermitienne $\psi$ for which the $H^{p}$ are deux \`{a} deux orthogonaux and of restriction \`{a} $H^{p}$ d\'{e}finite positive for $p$ pair, d\'{e}finite n\'{e}gative for $p$ impair.

Let $H$ a space vector complex, $\psi$ a form hermitienne on $H$ and $(h^{p})$ a family of integers $\geq 0$. One assumed that $\dim H = \sum h^{p}$ and that $\sgn(\psi) = \sum (-1)^{p} h^{p}$. One notera $M(H, \psi, (h^{p}))$ --- or simplement $M$ --- the space of the Hodge structures complexes on $H$, of nombres of Hodge $h^{p}$, polaris\'{e}es by $\psi$. the map which \`{a} a point of $M$ associated the Hodge filtration of $H$ by the $F^{p} := \bigoplus_{i \geq p} H^{i}$ is a injection of $M$ in a vari\'{e}t\'{e} of drapeaux of $H$. Indeed, $\bar{F}^{q} := \bigoplus_{i \leq q} \overline{H^{i}}$ is the orthogonal of $F^{1-q}$ and $H^{p} = F^{p} \cap \bar{F}^{-p}$. This injection identifie $M$ \`{a} a open of this vari\'{e}t\'{e} of drapeaux. One munit $M$ of the structure complex induite.

En each point of $M$, $\End(H)$ h\'{e}rite of a d\'{e}composition of Hodge, and of a Hodge filtration corresponding, with
\[
F^{p}\End(H) = \{f : H \to H \mid f(F^{i}) \subset F^{i+p}\}.
\]
the space tangent \`{a} $M$ (i.e. \`{a} the vari\'{e}t\'{e} of drapeaux) s'identifie \`{a} $\End(H)/F^{0}\End(H)$, and h\'{e}rite of the filtration image of $F$ of the fibr\'{e} tangent, with $F^{-1} = F$. C'is a filtration holomorphic $F^{0} = 0$.

Let $U$ the group unitaire $U(H, \psi)$. It agit transitivement on $M$. Si $m \in M$ correspond \`{a} a d\'{e}composition $H = \bigoplus H^{p}$ of $H$, the stabilisateur of $m$ is the under-group compact
\[
K := \prod_{p} U(H^{p}, \psi|H^{p}) \subset U.
\]
The compacit\'{e} of $K$ assure the existence on $M \cong U/K$ of m\'{e}triques riemanniennes (voire hermitiennes) $U$-invariantes.

\subsection*{1.4 Remark}
Si $(H', \psi')$ is isomorphic \`{a} $(H, \psi)$, a m\'{e}trique $U$-invariante on $M$ en d\'{e}termine a on $M' := M(H', \psi', (h^{p}))$: transporter the m\'{e}trique donn\'{e}e by the isomorphism $M \to M'$ induces by a isomorphism $u : (H, \psi) \to (H', \psi')$. The $U$-invariance assure that the m\'{e}trique obtained on $M'$ ne d\'{e}pend not of the choix of $u$.

\subsection*{1.5 Remark}
For every m\'{e}trique riemannienne $U$-invariante $d$ on $M$, the vari\'{e}t\'{e} riemannienne $(M, d)$ is homog\`{e}ne; it is therefore compl\`{e}te and ses boules ferm\'{e}es are compactes. Si $o \in M$ and that $C$ is a constant, the set of the $g \in U$ such that $d(g.o, o) \leq C$ is compact.

\subsection*{1.6}
Let $(V, \psi)$ a variation complex polaris\'{e}e on $S$, suppos\'{e} connected, and $(h^{p})$ ses nombres of Hodge. Choisissons a point base $o \in S$ and let $(\widetilde{S}, \tilde{o})$ the rev\^{e}tement universal of $(S,o)$. The syst\`{e}me local under-jacent is constant. It s'identifie to the syst\`{e}me local constant of fibers $V_{o}$ and this identification permet of regarder the variation as a family, param\'{e}tris\'{e}e by $\widetilde{S}$, of Hodge structures complexes on the space vector fixe $V_{o}$. They are polaris\'{e}es by $\psi_{o}$ and of nombres of Hodge $(h^{p})$. One attached ainsi \`{a} $(V, \psi)$ a map
\[

Reprenons the hypoth\`{e}ses and notations of 1.6, and let $\sigma : \pi_{1}(S,o) \to \GL(V_{o})$ the repr\'{e}sentation of monodromy. We shall say that a base $e$ of $V_{o}$ is \emph{adapt\'{e}e} \`{a} the d\'{e}composition of Hodge $P(\tilde{o}) : V_{o} = \bigoplus V_{o}^{p}$ of $V_{o}$ si it is the r\'{e}union of bases orthonorm\'{e}es for $(-1)^{p}\psi$ of the $V_{o}^{p}$.

\begin{corollary}[1.8]
Fixons $(S,o)$ and the nombres of Hodge $(h^{p})$. For every $\gamma \in \pi_{1}(S,o)$, there exists $C$ such that, for every variation complex polaris\'{e}e $V$ on $S$, of nombres of Hodge $(h^{p})$, and every base adapt\'{e}e $e$ of $V_{o}$, the coefficients of the matrice $\sigma(\gamma)$, in the base $e$, are born\'{e}s en value absolute by $C$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Let $H = \bigoplus \CC^{h^{p}}$, $\psi$ the form hermitienne on $H$ sum of forms hermitiennes $(-1)^{p}\sum z_{i}\bar{w}_{i}$ on the $\CC^{h^{p}}$, $M := M(H, \psi, (h^{p}))$ and $o$ the point of $M$ corresponding \`{a} the d\'{e}composition of $H$ en the $\CC^{h^{p}}$.

For every variation $V$ as en 1.7, the choix of a base adapt\'{e}e of $V_{o}$ identifie $V_{o}$ \`{a} $H$ and permet of regarder the map $P$ d\'{e}finite by $V$ as a map
\[
P_{V} : \widetilde{S} \to M
\]
v\'{e}rifiant $P_{V}(\tilde{o}) = o$.

Let $d_{K}$ the pseudo-m\'{e}trique of Kobayashi on $\widetilde{S}$: the more grande pseudo-m\'{e}trique $d$ such that for every map holomorphic $\varphi$ of the disque unit\'{e} in $\widetilde{S}$, $d(\varphi(x), \varphi(y))$ let inf\'{e}rieur or \'{e}gal \`{a} the distance of Poincar\'{e} of $x$ \`{a} $y$. Puisque $\widetilde{S}$ is connected, $d_{K}(x,y)$ is finite for every $x$ and $y$. For $M$ equipped with a m\'{e}trique riemannienne $U$-invariante $d_{M}$ suitable, ind\'{e}pendante of $(S,o)$ and of $V$, it r\'{e}sulte of 1.7 that
\[
d_{M}(o, \sigma(\gamma).o) = d_{M}(P_{V}(\gamma.\tilde{o}), P_{V}(\tilde{o})) \leq d_{K}(\gamma.\tilde{o}, \tilde{o}).
\]
of apr\`{e}s 1.5, $\sigma(\gamma)$ is therefore in a compact of $U$ ind\'{e}pendant of $V$, this which proves 1.8.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[1.9]
Fixons $(S,o)$ and a integer $N$. For each $\gamma \in \pi_{1}(S,o)$, there exists $C$ such that, for every variation polaris\'{e}e $V$ on $S$, of dimension $N$, and every base adapt\'{e}e $e$ of $V_{o}$, the coefficients of the matrice $\sigma(\gamma)$, in the base $e$, are born\'{e}s en value absolute by $C$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Proc\'{e}dons by r\'{e}currence on $N$. The cas $N = 0$ is trivial.

Let $p$ such that $h^{p} \neq 0$. Notons $V_{<p}$ (resp. $V_{>p}$) the sum of the $V^{i}$ for $i < p$ (resp. $i > p$). It r\'{e}sulte of (V.C.1)(V.C.2) that the under-fibr\'{e}s $V_{<p}$ and $V_{>p}$ are horizontaux. S'there exists $i < p < j$ with $h^{i} \neq 0$, $h^{p} = 0$, $h^{j} \neq 0$, one obtains ainsi a d\'{e}composition of the variation complex $V$ en the sum of deux variations complexes of dimensions strictement more petites that celle of $V$. A base adapt\'{e}e of $V_{o}$ is r\'{e}union of bases adapt\'{e}es of $(V_{<p})_{o}$ and $(V_{>p})_{o}$, and one conclut by r\'{e}currence.

It reste \`{a} traiter the cas o\`{u} the $i$ such that $h^{i} \neq 0$ forment a intervalle. Let $V(n)$ the variation complex of m\^{e}me syst\`{e}me local under-jacent that $V$, d\'{e}duite of $V$ by the renum\'{e}rotation:

It is polaris\'{e}e by $(-1)^{n}\psi$, a base adapt\'{e}e of $V_{o}$ is again a base adapt\'{e}e of $V(n)_{o}$, and the coefficients of the matrice $\sigma(\gamma)$ are the m\^{e}mes for $V$ and $V(n)$. One ne restreint therefore not the g\'{e}n\'{e}ralit\'{e} en ne traitant that of the $V$ such that $\{p \mid h^{p} \neq 0\}$ Let intervalle commencant en $0$. It n'y a then that a nombre finite of possibilit\'{e}s for the syst\`{e}me of the $h^{p}$, and one conclut by 1.8.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{3.2}

Let $X$ a sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien on $S$ of which one assumed that it se prolonge en a sch\'{e}ma semi-ab\'{e}lien $\bar{X}$ on $\bar{S}$ (r\'{e}duction semi-stable). Let $n$ the dimension relative of $X$ on $S$, $e$ the section zero and posons $\omega := e^{*}\Omega^{n}_{\bar{X}/\bar{S}}$. C'is the sheaf inversible on $\bar{S}$ of which the fiber en $s \in \bar{S}$ is the power ext\'{e}rieure maximale of the dual of the alg\`{e}bre of Lie of the fiber of $\bar{X}$. In \cite{Faltings}, G. Faltings commence by borner the degr\'{e} of $\omega$, ind\'{e}pendamment of $X$. Si one simplifie son argument by a r\'{e}f\'{e}rence \`{a} S. Zucker \cite{Zucker}, one obtains the estimation suivante.

\begin{lemma}[3.2]
With the notations pr\'{e}c\'{e}dentes, si $-\chi(S) > 0$, one has
\[
\deg \omega \leq \tfrac{1}{2} n \cdot (-\chi(S)).
\]
In this formule, $\chi(S)$ is the caract\'{e}ristique of Euler-Poincar\'{e} topologique: for $\bar{S}$ of genre $g$, $-\chi(S) = 2g - 2 + |T|$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let $H$ the variation of Hodge structures on $S$ of fibers $H^{1}(X_{s})$ ($s \in S$). It given lieu \`{a} a syst\`{e}me local complex $H_{\CC}$, and \`{a} a fibr\'{e} vector complex $H$ equipped with a connexion $\nabla$, of which $H_{\CC}$ is the syst\`{e}me local of the sections horizontales. On $H$, one dispose of the Hodge filtration $F$, r\'{e}duite ici \`{a} a under-fibr\'{e} $F^{1}(H)$ of $H$. The fiber $H^{0}(X_{s}, \Omega^{1})$ of $F^{1}(H)$ en $s$ s'identifie to the dual of the alg\`{e}bre of Lie of $X_{s}$.

the hypoth\`{e}se that $X$ is \`{a} r\'{e}duction semi-stable \'{e}quivaut \`{a} the unipotence of the local monodromy of $H_{\CC}$ en each $t \in T$. Let $H_{\can}$ the prolongement canonical (\cite{DeligneEDR}, 5.2) of the fibr\'{e} vector $H$ \`{a} $\bar{S}$ and let $F^{1}(H_{\can})$ the under-fibr\'{e} locally direct factor of $H_{\can}$ which prolonge $F^{1}H$. Every polarisation of $X$ (therefore of $H$) induces a dualit\'{e} parfaite between $H/F^{1}H$ and $F^{1}H$, and this dualit\'{e} se prolonge en a dualit\'{e} parfaite between $H_{\can}/F^{1}H_{\can}$ and $F^{1}H_{\can}$. We admettrons of the th\'{e}orie of the mod\`{e}the of N\'{e}ron that
\[
e^{*}\Omega^{1}_{\bar{X}/\bar{S}} = F^{1}H_{\can}.
\]

We traiterons of abord of the cas o\`{u} $X/S$ is without part fixe. This \'{e}quivaut \`{a} $H^{0}(S, H_{\CC}^{\vee}) = 0$ and implique that $H^{i}(S, H_{\CC}) = 0$ for $i \neq 1$, of o\`{u}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:3.2.1}
\dim H^{1}(S, H_{\CC}) = -\chi(S, H_{\CC}) = -\rank(H_{\CC}) \cdot \chi(S) = -2n\chi(S).
\end{equation}

The cohomology $H^{*}(S, H)$ can se calculer as the hypercohomologie, on $\bar{S}$, of the de Rham complex
\[
H_{\can} \xrightarrow{\nabla} \Omega^{1}_{\bar{S}}(\log T) \otimes H_{\can}.
\]
This complex, not\'{e} $K$, is filtr\'{e} by the under-complexes
\[
F^{i}K := (F^{i}H_{\can} \xrightarrow{\nabla} \Omega^{1}_{\bar{S}}(\log T) \otimes F^{i-1}H_{\can})
\]
and, of apr\`{e}s S. Zucker \cite{Zucker}, \S13, the spectral sequence corresponding d\'{e}g\'{e}n\`{e}re en $E_{1}$. In particular,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:3.2.2}
\dim E_{1}^{0,1} + \dim E_{1}^{1,0} \leq \dim H^{1}(S, H).
\end{equation}

one has $E_{1}^{0,1} = H^{0}(\bar{S}, \Omega^{1}(\log T) \otimes H_{\can}/F^{0}H_{\can})$ and $E_{1}^{1,0} = H^{1}(\bar{S}, F^{1}H_{\can})$, of dual of Serre $H^{0}(\bar{S}, (F^{1}H_{\can})^{\vee})$.

Si $F^{1}H_{\can}$ is of degr\'{e} $d$, i.e. si $d := \deg(\omega)$, one has therefore
\begin{align*}
\dim E_{1}^{0,1} + \dim E_{1}^{1,0} &\geq \chi((F^{1}H_{\can})^{\vee}) + \chi(S, \Omega^{1} \otimes H_{\can}/F^{1}H_{\can}) \\
&= [n(g-1) + d] + [n(g-1+|T|) + d] \\
&= -n\chi(S) + 2d.
\end{align*}

Combin\'{e} with \eqref{eq:3.2.1} and \eqref{eq:3.2.2}, this given
\[
-n\chi(S) + 2d \leq -2n\chi(S),
\]
i.e. the assertion of 3.2.

A sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien $X$ on $S$ is isog\`{e}ne \`{a} the sum of a sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien constant $\bar{S} \times B$ and of a sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien $Y$ without part fixe. Si $X$ is \`{a} r\'{e}duction semi-stable, $Y$ the is also. one has
\[
\omega \text{ (pour } X) = \omega \text{ (pour } \bar{S} \times B) \otimes \omega \text{ (pour } Y),
\]
o\`{u} the prime factor is trivial. This ram\`{e}ne 3.2 for $X$ \`{a} 3.2 for $Y$, and termine the proof of 3.2.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[3.4]
In the cas o\`{u} $X$ is a family of curves elliptiques ($n=1$), one dispose of a morphism ``of Kodaira--Spencer''
\begin{equation}\label{eq:3.3.1}
\omega^{\otimes 2} \to \Omega^{1}_{\bar{S}}(\log T).
\end{equation}
S'it is trivial, the invariant modulaire of $X$ is constant and $\deg(\omega) = 0$ ($X$ is toujours suppos\'{e} \`{a} r\'{e}duction semi-stable---therefore, en the occurrence, \`{a} bonne r\'{e}duction). S'it is not trivial, son existence reprouve 3.2: it implique that $2\deg(\omega) \leq \deg(\Omega^{1}_{\bar{S}}(T))$ and one has $\chi(S) = \deg \Omega^{1}_{\bar{S}}(T)$. For $X$ a family modulaire, \eqref{eq:3.3.1} is a isomorphism and one has \'{e}galit\'{e} in 3.2.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[3.5]
Assume that $\chi(S) < 0$, i.e. that the rev\^{e}tement universal of $S$ is the disque unit\'{e} $\mathbb{D}$. C'is the cas int\'{e}ressant (cf. 3.3). A polarisation of the sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien $X/S$ d\'{e}finit a polarisation of the variation of Hodge structures $H$ ($H^{1}$ of the fibers), and in particular a m\'{e}trique hermitienne on the fibr\'{e} vector $F^{1}H = e^{*}\Omega^{1}_{X/S}$. The variation $H$ given lieu \`{a} a map of p\'{e}riodes $P : \mathbb{D} \to M$ as en 1.7. The th\'{e}or\`{e}me of Griffiths 1.7 that $P$ d\'{e}croit the distances entra\^{i}ne a majoration of the tenseur of courbure of the fibr\'{e} m\'{e}tris\'{e} $e^{*}\Omega^{1}_{X/S}$ and in particular of sa 2-form of Chern. A \'{e}tude local \`{a} the infini shows that the int\'{e}grale of this 2-form is the degr\'{e} of $\omega$ on $\bar{S}$ (C. Peters \cite{Peters}). Int\'{e}grant the majoration ci-dessus, one retrouve 3.2.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[3.6]
Let $H$ a variation complex of Hodge structures polarisable on a curve $S = \bar{S} - T$, $H$ the fibr\'{e} vector \`{a} connexion corresponding, $H_{\can}$ the prolongement canonical of $H$, and $F$ the Hodge filtration of $H_{\can}$: $F^{p}H_{\can}$ is the under-fibr\'{e} locally direct factor of $H_{\can}$ which prolonge the under-fibr\'{e} $F^{p}H$ of $H$. The th\'{e}or\`{e}me of Griffiths 1.7 and a \'{e}tude local \`{a} the infini \cite{Zucker} permettent again of majorer the $|\deg \Gr_{F}^{p}H_{\can}|$ en function of the genre of $\bar{S}$, of $|T|$ and of the nombres of Hodge of the variation $H$.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{3.7}
Let $n$ a integer $\geq 3$ and $\mathcal{M}_{d,n}$ the space of the modules of the vari\'{e}t\'{e}s ab\'{e}liennes principalement polaris\'{e}es of dimension $d$ equipped with a structure of niveau $n$. C'is a space fin of modules: the donn\'{e}e of a sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien principalement polaris\'{e} $X$ on $S$, of dimension relative $d$, equipped with a structure of niveau $n$, \'{e}quivaut \`{a} celle of a morphism $f : S \to \mathcal{M}_{d,n}$. For $S = \bar{S} - T$ as en 3.1, this morphism se prolonge en a morphism $\bar{f}$ of $\bar{S}$ in the compactification of Satake $\bar{\mathcal{M}}_{d,n}$ of $\mathcal{M}_{d,n}$. Faltings d\'{e}duit of 3.2 that the graphe $\Gamma(\bar{f}) \subset \bar{S} \times \bar{\mathcal{M}}_{d,n}$ of $\bar{f}$ is of degr\'{e} born\'{e} ind\'{e}pendamment of $X/S$, for a embedding projective suitable of $\bar{S} \times \bar{\mathcal{M}}_{d,n}$. Of l\`{a} r\'{e}sulte that the sch\'{e}mas ab\'{e}liens of the type consid\'{e}r\'{e} are param\'{e}tr\'{e}s by a sch\'{e}ma of type finite $Y$: there exists a sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien $K_{Y}$ on $S \times Y$ such that each sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien of the type dit on $S$ let isomorphic \`{a} the restriction $K_{y}$ of $K_{Y}$ \`{a} $S \times \{y\} \sim S$. The monodromy \'{e}tant a invariant discret, the class of isomorphie of the repr\'{e}sentation of monodromy of $\pi_{1}(S)$ d\'{e}finite by $K_{y}$ ne d\'{e}pend that of the composante connected of $Y$ o\`{u} se trouve $y$. This reprouve the th\'{e}or\`{e}me 0.1, restreint to the cas particulier of the sch\'{e}mas ab\'{e}liens of dimension relative $d$, principalement polaris\'{e}s and equipped with a structure of niveau $n$.

\subsection*{3.8}
Let $Z$ a sch\'{e}ma connected of type finite and $u : S_{Z} \to Z$ a family of curves param\'{e}tris\'{e}e by $Z$. One assumed that $\bar{S}_{Z} = \bar{S}_{Z} - T_{Z}$ with $\bar{S}_{Z}$ proper and smooth on $Z$, of fibers of the curves of genre $g$, and with $T_{Z}$ finite \'{e}tale on $Z$, of fibers of the sets of $t$ points.

For $z \in Z$, posons $S_{z} := u^{-1}(z)$. For $x \in S_{z}$, the fundamental groups $\pi_{1}(S_{u(x)}, x)$ of the fibers of $u$ formant a syst\`{e}me local on $S_{Z}$. Of the chemin of $x$ \`{a} $y$ d\'{e}finit therefore a isomorphism of $\pi_{1}(S_{u(x)}, x)$ \`{a} $\pi_{1}(S_{u(y)}, y)$. Si, for $i = 1, 2$, $S_{i}$ is the compl\'{e}ment in a curve $\bar{S}_{i}$ of genre $g$ of a set $T_{i}$ of $t$ points, and that $x_{i} \in S_{i}$, a isomorphism of $\pi_{1}(S_{1}, x_{1})$ with $\pi_{1}(S_{2}, x_{2})$ will be dit \emph{permis} s'there exists a family as ci-dessus, of which the $S_{i}$ let of the fibers and for which the isomorphism let d\'{e}duit of a chemin of $x_{1}$ \`{a} $x_{2}$.

the moduli space $\mathcal{M}_{g,t}$ of the curves of genre $g$ \`{a} $t$ points \'{e}tant connected, there exists toujours a isomorphism permis of $\pi_{1}(S_{1}, x_{1})$ with $\pi_{1}(S_{2}, x_{2})$.

\subsection*{3.9}
The arguments 3.6 s'appliquent again with of the param\`{e}tres. For $d$, $n \geq 3$, there exists a sch\'{e}ma of type finite $Y$ on $Z$ and a sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien $\mathcal{X}$ on $S_{Z} \times_{Z} Y$ ayant the propri\'{e}t\'{e} suivante: for every $z \in Z$ and all sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien principalement polaris\'{e} $A_{z}$ of dimension $d$, on $S_{z}$, equipped with a structure of niveau $n$, there exists $y \in Y$ to the-dessus of $z$ such that $A_{z}$ let the inverse image of $\mathcal{X}$ by $S_{z} \to S_{Z} \times_{Z} Y : s \mapsto (s, y)$.

Prenant a family universelle $S_{Z}$, one en d\'{e}duit the r\'{e}sultat suivant.

\begin{proposition}[3.10]
Let $\Gamma$ the fundamental group of a curve of genre $g$ with $t$ points fix\'{e}s. Fixons $d$ and $n \geq 3$. For every curve of genre $g$ with $t$ points fix\'{e}s $S$, for every sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien $A$ on $S$, principalement polaris\'{e}, of dimension $d$, equipped with a structure of niveau $n$, and for every isomorphism permis $\gamma : \Gamma \to \pi_{1}(S,o)$, the repr\'{e}sentation of monodromy of $\pi_{1}(S,o)$ on $H^{1}(A_{o}, \ZZ)$ fournit a repr\'{e}sentation $\sigma_{V}$ of $\Gamma$. The classes of isomorphie of repr\'{e}sentations of $\Gamma$ ainsi obtained ne forment that a nombre finite of orbites under the automorphismes permis of $\Gamma$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[3.11]
the analogue of 3.9, without structure of niveau impos\'{e}e, is again vrai. All sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien of dimension $d$ on a curve $S$ acquiert a structure of niveau $n$ on a rev\^{e}tement of $S$ of degr\'{e} $\leq 3^{4nd^{2}}$, and one d\'{e}crit a sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien on $S$ by the donn\'{e}e of a rev\^{e}tement $S'$ of $S$, of degr\'{e} $\leq 3^{4nd^{2}}$, of a sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien with structure of niveau $n$ on $S'$, and of a donn\'{e}e of descent of $S'$ \`{a} $S$ for this sch\'{e}ma ab\'{e}lien.
\end{remark}
