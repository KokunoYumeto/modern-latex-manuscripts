\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We definissons the category of the mixed Tate motives on the ring of the $S$-integers of a number field, and the motivic fundamental group (rendu unipotent) of a variety unirationnelle over a field of nombres. We considerons more en detail the motivic fundamental group of the projective line less $0$, $\infty$ and the racines $N$-iemes of the unite.
\end{abstract}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} We define the category of mixed Tate motives over the ring of $S$-integers of a number field. We define the motivic fundamental group (made unipotent) of a unirational variety over a number field. We apply this to the study of the motivic fundamental group of the projective line punctured at zero, infinity and all $N$-th roots of unity.

Beilinson-Soule is vraie on $k$, and c'is
this which permet of define the $t$-structure
requise.
By Levine (1998) and Huber (2000), one
dispose on this Tannakian category of

corresponding to the theories of cohomology
usuelles (sauf that the cohomology cristalline
n'a jusqu'a present not ete
consideree).

In such a way can-\^{e}tre artificielle, but

subcategory of $\MT(k)$.
See 1.6.

Other variante: by descent of a extension
of $k$ a $k$, one defines the Tannakian category $\MAT(k)$ of the motives  of Artin-Tate 
mixte, which deviennent

(representations rationnelles of
$\Gal(\kbar/k)$).

In Deligne (1989), the a of we a

realisations'' of the fundamental group
$\pi_1(X,0)$ rendu unipotent: a algebra of

plut\^{o}t son spectre.
Si $X$ is unirationelle, we construisons a

In the fin of the article, we utilisons these

\subsection*{1. Mixed Tate motives on the ring of the}

\subsection*{1.1 Let $k$ a field.}
Nombre of results cites dependant of
the resolution of the singularities, we assume $k$ of caracteristique $0$.
Hanamura (1995), Levine (1998) and Voevodsky
(2000) have chacun defined a triangulated category of motives on $k$, and Levine
(1998) VI 2.5.5 constructs a equivalence
between sa triangulated category and celle of
Voevodsky.
This derniere will be for we the more
commode.
We the noterons $\DM(k)$ and noterons
$\DM(k)_{\dbQ}$ celle which s'en deduces by
tensorisation with $\dbQ$.
One dispose in $\DM(k)$ of objects of Tate
$\dbZ(n)$ ($n\in\dbZ$), of which the images in
$\DM(k)_{\dbQ}$ will be notees $\dbQ(n)$.
Seule we importera the subcategory
triangulee $\DMT(k)_{\dbQ}$ of
$\DM(k)_{\dbQ}$ generated by the $\dbQ(n)$:
celle of the ``extensions iterees'' of
$\dbQ(n)[m]$.
Recall that $E$ is {\it extension} of $B$
by $A$ s'there exists a triangle distingue
$A\to E\to B\to A[1]$.

One dispose in $\DM(k)$ of a tensor product associatif and commutative a unite
$\otimes$, compatible a the structure
triangulee.
the automorphism of symetrie of
$\dbZ(1)\otimes\dbZ(1)$ is the identity
(Voevodsky (2000) 2.1.5), $M\mapsto
M(1):=M\otimes \dbZ(1)$ is a equivalence
(loc. cit. 4.1.3), and $\dbZ(n)$ is
$\dbZ(1)^{\otimes n}$ ($n\in\dbZ$).
The tensor product $\otimes$ is rigide
(loc. cit. 4.3.7): existence for every object
$M$ of a dual $M^*$ equipped with $\ev\colon\,
M^*\otimes M\to \dbZ(0)$ and $\delta\colon\,
\dbZ(0)\to M\otimes M^*$ such that the composes
$M\to M\otimes M^*\otimes M\to M$ and $M^*\to
M^*\otimes M\otimes M^*\to M^*$ let the identity.

Recall, of after Levine (1993), comment,
lorsque the conjecture of annulation of
Beilinson Soule is verifiee
for $k$, one can extraire of $\DMT(k)_{\dbQ}$
a Tannakian category $\MT(k)$ which
merite the nom of category of the mixed Tate motives on $k$.

Ecrivons $\Hom^j(M,N)$ for $\Hom(M,N[j])$.
The $\Hom^*(\dbZ(a),\dbZ(b))$ ne dependant
that of $i=b-a$.
They are donnes by the groups of Chow
superieurs convenablement numerotes of
$\Spec(k)$ (loc. cit. 4.2.9 for
$X=\Spec(k)$).
For $i< 0$ they are nuls. 
For $i=0$,
\begin{align}
\Hom^j(\dbZ(0),\dbZ(0))
   &= \dbZ &&\text{si } j=0,\\
   &= 0 &&\text{sinon}.\tag{1.1.1}
\intertext{Pour $i=1$,}
\Hom^j(\dbZ(0),\dbZ(1))
   &= k^* &&\text{si } j=1,\\
   &= 0 &&\text{sinon}.\tag{1.1.2}
\end{align}

After tensorisation with $\dbQ$, these groups
are again data by the under-spaces
proper of the operations of Adams in the
groups of $K$-theorie of $k$ (Levine
(1998) II 3.6.6; reference originale:
Bloch (1986) complete by Bloch (1994);
see also Levine (1994)).
The conjecture of annulation of
Beilinson-Soule is that
\begin{equation}
\Hom^j(\dbQ(0),\dbQ(i))=0\quad
\text{pour}\quad i>0\quad\text{et}\quad
j\Le 0.
\tag{1.1.3}
\end{equation}
Lorsque tel is the cas, Beilinson Bernstein
Deligne (1982) 1.3.14, applique a the
full subcategory of $\DMT(k)_{\dbQ}$
of objects the $\dbQ(n)$, shows that the
extensions iterees of $\dbQ(n)$
($n\in\dbZ$) forment a abelian category, coeur of a $t$-structure on
$\DMT(k)_{\dbQ}$.
One the appelle the category
$\MT(k)$ of the mixed Tate motives on $k$.
It is stable by tensor product and
passage to the dual.
These functors being exacts, that resulte
of this that $\dbQ(n)\otimes\dbQ(m)=\dbQ(n+m)$ and
that $\dbQ(n)^*=\dbQ(-n)$.
One obtains ainsi on $\MT(k)$ a tensor product exact en each variable,
associatif, commutative, a unite and rigide.

Of this that $\MT(k)$ is the coeur of a
$t$-structure on $\DMT(k)_{\dbQ}$ resulte
that for $A$ and $B$ in $\MT(k)$, one has
\begin{align}
\Ext^1(A,B) &= \Hom^1(A,B)\qquad\text{et}\tag{1.1.4}\\
\Ext^2(A,B) &\hookrightarrow \Hom^2(A,B).\tag{1.1.5}
\end{align}
In particular,
\begin{equation}
\Ext^1(\dbQ(n),\dbQ(m))=0\quad
\text{pour}\quad m\Le n.\tag{1.1.6}
\end{equation}
For $A$ in $\MT(k)$, it follows of
(1.1.6), by induction on a description of
$A$ as extension iteree, that $A$ admet
a unique weight filtration''
$W$, finite, croissante and indexee by the
integers pairs, such that
\begin{equation}
\Gr_{-2n}^W(M):= W_{-2n}(M)/W_{-2(n+1)}(M)
\end{equation}
Let sum of copies of $\dbQ(n)$.
Posons
\begin{equation}
\omega_n(M):=\Hom(\dbQ(n),\Gr_{-2n}^W(M)).
\end{equation}

one has therefore
\begin{equation}
\Gr_{-2n}^W(M)=\dbQ(n)\otimes_\dbQ
\omega_n(M).\end{equation}
The filtration W is fonctorielle, exact and
compatible to the tensor product.
Parce that the automorphism of symmetrie of
$\dbQ(1)\otimes\dbQ(1)$ is the identity, one
dispose of isomorphisms
\[
\dbQ(n)\otimes\dbQ(m)\simover\dbQ(n+m)
\]
compatible a the associativity and a the
commutativity of $\otimes$.
The exact functor $M\mapsto
\omega(M):=\oplus\omega_n(M)$ is therefore
a $\otimes$-functor: c'is a functor fiber
and the category $\MT(k)$ is tannakienne.
\subsection*{1.2 For $M$ a Tate motive mixte, the under-quotient}
$W_{-2n}(M)/W_{-2(n+2)}(M)$ of
$M$ is a extension $M_{n,n+1}$ of
$\Gr_{-2n}^W(M)=\dbQ(n)\otimes\omega_n(M)$ by
$\Gr_{-2(n+1)}^W(M)=\dbQ(n+1)\otimes\omega_{n+1}(M)$.
The class of this extension is a element
\begin{equation}
e_n\in \Ext^1(\dbQ(n),\dbQ(n+1))\otimes\Hom
(\omega_n(M),\omega_{n+1}(M)).
\end{equation}
The tensorisation with $\dbQ(n)$ is a
isomorphism
