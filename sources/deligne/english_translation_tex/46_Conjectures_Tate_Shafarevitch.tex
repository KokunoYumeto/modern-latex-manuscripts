\vspace{12pt}
\textbf{Dans cet exposé,} nous expliquons comment G. Faltings prouve la conjecture de Tate (2.7) et celle de Shafarevitch (3.3, 3.4). Nous traitons en détail des propriétés de la ``hauteur'' d'une variété abélienne, introduite par G. Faltings. C'est la partie la plus technique, et la plus longue, de la preuve. Le lecteur qui, après avoir lu les définitions 1.1, 1.2, est prêt à admettre 1.3, peut passer directement aux paragraphes 2 et 3.

\subsection*{Notations et terminologie}

Certains termes seront employés dans un sens restreint :

\textsc{Corps} := corps commutatif.

\textsc{Corps de nombres} := extension finie de $\QQ$.

\textsc{Schéma} := schéma noethérien séparé.

\textsc{Schéma en groupes sur} $S$ : toujours de type fini sur $S$. En général, plat sur $S$ et commutatif.

Pour $k$ un corps, on notera $\overline{k}$ une clôture algébrique de $k$. Pour $A$ une variété abélienne sur $k$ et $\ell$ un nombre premier, premier à la caractéristique, on note $T_\ell(A)$ le module de Tate de $A$ :
\[
T_\ell(A) := \varprojlim A_{\ell^n}(\overline{k}).
\]
It is equipped with a action of the Galois group $\Gal(\overline{k}/k)$ and of a structure of module on the ring $\End(A)$ of the $k$-endomorphisms of $A$. One sets $V_\ell(A) = T_\ell(A) \otimes_{\ZZ_\ell} \QQ_\ell$.

\section{HAUTEURS}

\subsection{}

Let $k$ a number field and $L$ a space vector of rank a on $k$ equipped for each place $v$ of $k$ of a value absolute $v$-adique: $\|\lambda \cdot \ell\|_v = |\lambda|_v \cdot \|\ell\|_v$ for $\lambda \in k$, $\ell \in L$. One assumed that for $\ell \in L^* := L - \{0\}$, one has $\|\ell\|_v = 1$ sauf en a nombre finite of places. of after the formule of the product $\prod_v |\lambda|_v = 1$ for $\lambda \in k^*$, the product $\prod_v \|\ell\|_v$ is independant of the choix of $\ell \in L^*$. One appelle \emph{degree} of $L$ the logarithme of son inverse:
\[
\deg(L) := -\log \prod_v \|\ell\|_v.
\]

Let $\cO$ the ring of integers of $k$. For $v$ a place of $k$, one denotes by a $v$ en indice a completion en $v$. A $\cO$-form $L_{\cO}$ of $L$, i.e. a $\cO$-module projective of rank a such that $L_{\cO} \otimes_{\cO} k = L$, fournit for each place finite $v$ of $k$ a value absolute $v$-adique on $L$; transporter a $L_v$ the value absolute of $k_v$ by a isomorphism of $k_v$-vector $V \xrightarrow{\sim} L_v$ which envoie $L_{\cO} \otimes_{\cO} \cO_v$ on $\cO_v$. For $\ell \in L^*$ one has $\|\ell\|_v = 1$ for presque all $v$. Si $\ell \in L_{\cO}$,
\[
\deg(L) = -\log \#(L_{\cO}/\cO \cdot \ell) + \sum_{v \text{ archimédienne}} \log \|\ell\|_v.
\]

Donnons-we moreover, for each embedding complex $\sigma : k \hookrightarrow \CC$, a structure hermitienne (:= a form hermitienne defined $> 0$) $\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle_\sigma$ on the space vector complex $L_\sigma := L \otimes_{k,\sigma} \CC$. Deux embeddings complexes conjugues fournissent of the vector spaces $L_\sigma$ complexes conjugues, and one assumed that for $z \in L_\sigma$ one has $\langle \bar{z}, \bar{z}\rangle_{\bar{\sigma}} = \overline{\langle z, z\rangle_\sigma}$. Si, for $v$ archimedienne induite by a embedding complex $\sigma$, one sets $\|\ell\|_v := \langle \ell, \ell\rangle_\sigma^{1/2}$, one obtains a systeme of values absolues of the type voulu, and $\deg L$ is defined.

For $k = \QQ$, one has $\cO = \ZZ$ and si $e$ is the a of the deux generateurs $\pm e$ of $L_{\ZZ}$,
\[
\deg(L) = -\log \|e\|_\infty.
\]

\subsection{}

Let $A$ a abelian variety over a field $k$. One denotes $\omega(A)$ the space vector of rank a suivant on $k$: si $A$ is of dimension $g$,
\[
\omega(A) := \overset{g}{\bigwedge} H^0(A, \Omega^1).
\]
Si $k$ is a number field, one dispose for each place $v$ of $k$ of a value absolute $v$-adique natural on $\omega(A)$.

(a) Let $\cO$ the ring of integers of $k$ and $\fA$ the model of Neron of $A$ on $\Spec(\cO)$. The $\cO$-module projective of rank a
\[
\omega(\fA) = e^* \Omega^g_{\fA/\Spec(\cO)}
\]
is a $\cO$-form of $\omega(A)$. It fournit the values absolues cherchees for the places finite.

(b) Let $\sigma : k \hookrightarrow \CC$ a embedding complex and $A_\sigma$ the abelian variety on $\CC$ deduite of $A$ by extension of scalars. the space vector complex
\[
\omega(A)_\sigma := \omega(A) \otimes_{k,\sigma} \CC
\]
is the space of the $g$-forms differentielles holomorphic on $A_\sigma$. One the munit of the structure hermitienne for which
\[
\langle \alpha, \alpha \rangle_\sigma = \frac{1}{(2\pi)^g} \int_{A_\sigma(\CC)} \alpha \wedge \bar{\alpha}.
\]
This fournit the $\|\cdot\|_v$ for $v$ archimedien. Faltings utilise $\frac{1}{(2\pi)^g}$; peu importe.

En terme of these values absolues, one defines the \emph{hauteur} $h(A)$ of $A$ as being
\[
h(A) := \frac{1}{[k:\QQ]} \deg \omega(A).
\]
For $k = \QQ$, si $\alpha$ is the a of the deux generateurs $\pm \alpha$ of $\omega(A)_{\ZZ}$ (a form of Neron), one has
\[
h(A) = -\frac{1}{[k:\QQ]} \log \prod_v \|\alpha\|_v.
\]

For $\fG : G \to S$ a scheme en groups plat purement of dimension relative $g$ on a base $S$, of section neutre $e$, one denotes $\omega(G)$ the sheaf inversible on $S$ of which the sheaf dualisant relative is the inverse image:
\[
\fG^! \cO_S = \fG^* \omega(G)[g], \quad \omega(G) = e^* \Omega^g_{G/S}.
\]
For $G$ smooth on $S$, one retrouve $e^* \Omega^g_{G/S}$.

For $k$ a number field of ring of integers $\cO$ and $S = \Spec(\cO)$, si the fiber generale of $G$ is proper on $k$, i.e. extension of a group finite by a abelian variety, the integration on $G(\CC)$ fournit again, for each embedding complex $\sigma : k \hookrightarrow \CC$, a structure hermitienne on $\omega(G) \otimes_{\cO} \CC$. The degree $\deg \omega(G)$ is therefore defined. The cas $G$ quasi-finite will be utilise to the ~2.

A scheme en groups commutatifs plat on a base $S$ is dit \emph{abelien} si ses fibers are of the extensions of abelian varieties by of the tores. One dit that $A$ is \emph{a reduction semi-stable} si son model of Neron connected $\fA^0$ (of fibers the composantes neutres of the model of Neron) is semi-abelien. It revient to the same of exiger that $A$ let the fiber generale of a scheme semi-abelien on $\cO$; this dernier is unique. Si $A$ is a reduction semi-stable, the formation of $\fA^0$ and therefore celle of $\omega(\fA^0)$ commute a extension of scalars $k \to k'$, and the factor $1/[k:\QQ]$ in the definition of $h(A)$ assure that $h(A)$ is invariant by extension of scalars. Si a abelian variety $A$ on $k$ acquiert a reduction semi-stable on a extension $k'$ of $k$, one defines the \emph{hauteur geometric} of $A$ by
\[
h_{\text{geom}}(A) := h(A_{k'}).
\]
Of the fait that $\omega(\fA^0)$ s'envoie in $\omega(\fA)$ one deduces that $h(A) \leq h_{\text{geom}}(A)$, with equality if and only if the reduction is semi-stable.

Recall that a \emph{polarisation} of $A$ is a class of equivalence algebraic (defined on $k$) of sheaves inversibles amples on $A$. Son degree is the integer $c_1(\cL)^g/g! = \chi(\cL)$. A \emph{polarisation principal} is a polarisation of degree 1.

\begin{proposition}
Quels that let the number field $k$, the integer $g \geq 1$ and $n \geq 1$, and $h$, it n'y a that a nombre finite of classes of isomorphie of abelian varieties principalement polarisees $(A,\theta)$ on $k$, of dimension $g$, of degree of polarisation $n$ and of hauteur $h(A) \leq h$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{example}[The cas $k = \QQ$, $g = 1$]
Let $E$ a curve elliptique on $\QQ$ and $\omega$ a differentielle of Neron. The curve $E$ admet a equation
\[
y^2 + a_1xy + a_3y = x^3 + a_2x^2 + a_4x + a_6 = 0
\]
a coefficients integers, such that, notant $F(x,y)$ the prime membre, the form $\omega$ let the form $dx/F_y = -dy/F_x$. Let $\Lambda \subset \CC$ the reseau such that $(E,\omega)$ let isomorphic, on $\CC$, a $(\CC/\Lambda, dz)$. Let $\omega_1, \omega_2$ a base positive of $\Lambda$, $\tau = \omega_2/\omega_1$ ($\Impartie(\tau) > 0$) and $q = \exp(2\pi i \tau)$. one has on $\CC$
\[
\Delta = q \prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1-q^n)^{24} \quad \text{et} \quad j = (c_4^3 - c_6^2)/1728
\]
 are independants of the choix of the base $\omega_1, \omega_2$ of $\Lambda$. This are of the polynomes a coefficients integers en the $a_i$ (see by example the formulaire of Tate, mis to the gout of the jour by P.~Deligne, in Antwerp IV (Springer Lecture Notes 476)). This are therefore of the integers. The curve $E$ admet the equation of Weierstrass
\[
y^2 = 4x^3 - c_4x - c_6.
\]
Posons $H(E) = \exp h(E)$ and $H^*(E) = \max(|c_4^3|, |c_6^2|)$. For montrer that it n'y a that a nombre finite of curves elliptiques on $\QQ$ a $h(E)$ given, it is enough to borner $H^*(E)$ en terme of $H(E)$.

Prenons a base $\omega_1, \omega_2$ of $\Lambda$ with $|\omega_1|$ minimal. one has then $\Impartie(\tau) \geq \sqrt{3}/2$. For $\Impartie(\tau) > a$ with $a > 0$, the relation $j = q^{-1} + 744 + \cdots$ between $\tau$ and the invariant modulaire $j$ fournit that
\[
\Sup(1, \log |j|) / 2\pi \leq 1/\Impartie(\tau) \leq C(a) \cdot \Sup(1, \log |j|) / 2\pi.
\]
Parce that $\Delta$ is integer, one has also $|\Delta| \leq H^*(E)^{1/2}$, of or
\[
H(E) \leq H^*(E) \leq C \cdot H(E)^N
\]
for of the constants $C$ and $N$ convenables.
\end{example}

\begin{remark}
For $\tau$ in the Poincare half-plane, let $q = e^{2\pi i \tau}$, $\Lambda_\tau = \ZZ + \ZZ\tau$, $E(\tau) = \CC/\Lambda_\tau$ the curve elliptique equipped with the form differentielle $dz$, $\alpha$ the form differentielle
\[
\alpha = \Delta(\tau)^{1/12} \cdot (dz)^{\otimes 2}
\]
(peu importe the choix of the racine douzieme) and
\[
V(j) = |\Delta(\tau)| \cdot (\Impartie(\tau))^6.
\]
This function of $\tau$ is invariante by $\SL(2,\ZZ)$, therefore provient of a function $V(j)$ of the invariant modulaire $j$. For $E$ a curve elliptique on $k$, of invariant modulaire $j \in k$, posons $H(E) = V(j)^{1/2}$ (resp.\ $H(E) = V(j)$) for $k$ reel (resp.\ complex).

The differentielle logarithmique $d\log V$ is, a factor pres, the serie of Eisenstein not holomorphic $E_2$; D.~Masser a shows that it ne s'annule that for $j = 0$ or $j = 1728$ (Springer, Lecture Notes 437, lemma 3.2). One deduces that the maximum of $V(j)$ is atteint en $j = 0$ and that the minimum of $H(E)$ is atteint by the curves elliptiques of invariant modulaire $0$. This fournit the minimum of $h(E)$.

For $E$ a curve elliptique a multiplication complex by the ring of integers $\cO$ of the field quadratique imaginaire of discriminant $-D$, the formule of Chowla-Selberg (J.~Crelle 227(1967), 86-110) given the value of $H(E)$; si $\varepsilon$ is the character of Dirichlet quadratique of conducteur $D$, one has
\[
\log H(E) = \frac{\pi \sqrt{D}}{6h} \sum_{a=1}^{D-1} \varepsilon(a) \cdot a \cdot \log \sin\frac{\pi a}{D}
\]
($w = \#\cO^*$, $h =$ nombre of classes). The cas particulier $D = 3$ fournit the value minimum of $h(E)$:
\[
h(E) \geq -\frac{1}{2} \log\frac{\sqrt{3}\Gamma(1/3)^6}{2\pi \cdot 3^3} \approx 0.0255\ldots
\]
\end{remark}

We shall prove all of abord the lemma suivant, which suffirait for the maps.

\begin{lemma}
Let $m$ a integer, product of deux nombres prime $\geq 3$. It n'y a that a nombre finite of classes of isomorphie of abelian varieties principalement polarisees $(A,\theta)$ on $k$, of dimension $g$, equipped with a structure of niveau $m$:
\[
\theta : (\ZZ/(m))^{2g} \xrightarrow{\sim} A_m
\]
and of hauteur $h(A) \leq h$.
\end{lemma}

Parce that $m \geq 3$, the triples $(A,\theta,\xi)$ n'have not of automorphism not trivial. The classes of isomorphie, on $k$, of these triples, s'identifient to the $k$-points of a moduli space $M$ suitable. One prouvera 1.6 en comparant $h(A)$ a the hauteur, en a sens more classique, of the point of $M$ corresponding a $(A,\theta,\xi)$. the hypothese on $m$ assure that $A$ a reduction semi-stable, of sorte that the hauteur is invariante by extension of scalars.

\subsection{}

Let $X$ a projective variety on $\QQ$. A sheaf inversible $\cL$ on $X$ defines a class $\bmod\ \cO(1)$ of functions hauteur $h_{\cL}$ on $X(\overline{\QQ})$. One can construct a telle function as suit:

(a) One choisit a projective variety $X_{\ZZ}$ on $\Spec(\ZZ)$, and a sheaf inversible $\cL_{\ZZ}$ on $X_{\ZZ}$ such that $\cL$ se deduise of $\cL_{\ZZ}$ by extension of scalars of $\ZZ$ a $\QQ$.

(b) One choisit a structure hermitienne on $\cL_\CC$, i.e. on the fiber en lines $\cL_\CC^{\text{an}}$ on $X(\CC)$ deduces of $\cL$. For simplifier, one the assumed invariante by the conjugaison complex of $(X(\CC), \cL_\CC)$.

Let then $k$ a number field, of ring of integers $\cO$, and $x \in X(k)$. Parce that $X_{\ZZ}$ is proper on $\Spec(\ZZ)$, $x$ se prolonge en
\[
x_\cO \in X_{\ZZ}(\cO) = \Hom(\Spec(\cO), X_{\ZZ}).
\]
Let $\cL_x$ the fiber of $\cL_{\ZZ}$ en $x$; c'is a $k$-space vector of rank 1. The choix (a) fournit the $\cO$-form $\cL_{x,\cO}$ of $\cL_x$. The choix (b) fournit, for each embedding complex $\sigma$ of $k$, a form hermitienne on $\cL_x \otimes_{k,\sigma} \CC$. One prend
\[
h_{\cL}(x) := \frac{1}{[k:\QQ]} \deg(\cL_{x,\cO}).
\]
The factor $1/[k:\QQ]$ in the definition assure that for $k \subset k'$, the function $h_{\cL}$ on $X(k)$ is the restriction a $X(k)$ of the function $h_{\cL}$ on $X(k')$; by passage a the limite, one obtains $h_{\cL}$ on $X(\overline{\QQ})$.

A version savante of the fait that it n'y a that a nombre finite of integers $n$ with $|n| \leq C$ is:

\begin{theorem}[Rappel 1.8]
Si $\cL$ is ample, it n'y a that a nombre finite of $x \in X(k)$ with $h_{\cL}(x) \leq h$.

\begin{theorem}
Let $k$ a number field, $\overline{k}$ a algebraic closure of $k$, $S$ a set finite of places finite of $k$, $\ell$ a nombre prime and $d$ a integer. There exists a set finite $T$ of places of $k$, disjoint of $S$, such that a representation $\ell$-adique semisimple of dimension $d$, $\rho : \Gal(\overline{k}/k) \to \GL(d, \QQ_\ell)$, not ramifiee en dehors of $S$, is uniquement determinee (a isomorphism of representations pres) by the $\Tr \rho(\varphi_v)$ for $v \in T$.
\end{theorem}

One sait (Hermite) that it n'exists that a nombre finite of extensions galoisiennes $k'$ of $k$, not ramifiees en dehors of $S$ and of degree borne. of after Cebotarev, there exists therefore a set finite $T$ of places of $k$, disjoint of $S$, such that the classes of conjugaison of the Frobenius $\varphi_v$ ($v \in T$) remplissent all $\Gal(k'/k)$, for every extension galoisienne $k'$ of $k$, not ramifiee en dehors of $S$, of degree $\leq \ell^{2d^2}$. Prouvons that $T$ convient.

Let therefore $\rho_1$, $\rho_2$ deux representations $\ell$-adiques of the type dit, with $\Tr \rho_1(\varphi_v) = \Tr \rho_2(\varphi_v)$ for $v \in T$. We voulons montrer that $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$ have same character, therefore, being semi-simples, are isomorphes. Let $M$ the image of the algebra $\ZZ_\ell[\Gal(\overline{k}/k)]$ by
\[
\rho_1 \times \rho_2 : \ZZ_\ell[\Gal(\overline{k}/k)] \longrightarrow M_d(\QQ_\ell) \times M_d(\QQ_\ell).
\]
It s'agit of verify that, on $M$, the form lineaire $\delta(m_1, m_2) := \Tr(m_1) - \Tr(m_2)$ is identiquement zero. the algebra $M$ is a $\ZZ_\ell$-module of rank $\leq 2d^2$. the image of $\Gal(\overline{k}/k)$ in the quotient $(M/\ell M)^*$ a therefore less of $\ell^{2d^2}$ elements and, by hypothese, each element of this image is a $(\rho_1 \times \rho_2)(\varphi_v)$ for $v \in T$. By Nakayama, the $(\rho_1 \times \rho_2)(\varphi_v)$ ($v \in T$) engendrent $\ZZ_\ell$-lineairement $M$. On eux, the form lineaire $\delta$ s'annule by hypothese, of or $\delta = 0$ on $M$.
