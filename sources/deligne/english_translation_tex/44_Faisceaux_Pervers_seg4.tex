Let $j: U_o \hookrightarrow X_o$ a embedding affine. Si $F_o$ is a perverse sheaf mixte of weight $\leq w$ (resp. $\geq w$) on $U_o$, the perverse sheaf $j_{!*}F_o$ is again mixte of weight $\leq w$ (resp. $\geq w$). In particular, si $F_o$ is pur, $j_{!*}F_o$ is pur of the same weight.

\begin{theorem}[5.3.5]

\end{theorem}

Si the perverse sheaves simples mixtes (and therefore purs) $U_o$ and $V_o$ are of weight $u$ and $v$, with $u > v$, $\Ext^1(V_o, U_o) = 0$ (5.1.15(ii) for $i=1$; recall that $\Ext^1 = \Hom^1_{\cD}$). \begin{lemma}[5.3.6]
Let $A$ a abelian category of which all object is of longueur finite. Let $(S^+, S^-)$ a partition en deux classes of the set of the classes of isomorphie of objects simples of $A$, and $A^+$ (resp. $A^-$) the full subcategory of $A$ formee of the $X$ of which the under-quotients simples are in $S^+$ (resp. $S^-$). Assume that for $U$ in $S^+$ and $V$ in $S^-$, one ait $\Ext^1(V,U) = 0$. Then, all object $F$ of $A$ admet a unique under-object $WF$ in $A^-$, such that $F/WF$ let in $A^+$. For every morphism $f: F \to G$, one has $f(WF) = f(F) \cap WG$.
\end{lemma}

For $X$ in $A^+$ and $Y$ in $A^-$, the exact sequence of the $\Ext$ shows that $\Ext^1(Y,X) = 0$. The classes $S^+$ and $S^-$ being disjointes, one has also $\Hom(X,Y) = \Hom(Y,X) = 0$.

Prouvons the existence of $WF$ by recurrence on the longueur $\ell$ of $F$. For $\ell = 0$, $F = 0$ and the assertion is clear. Si $\ell > 0$, let $S$ a under-object simple of $F$. the hypothese of recurrence s'applique a $F/S$, of or the existence of a $W(F/S)$. Let $E$ son inverse image in $F$. C'is a extension of $W(F/S)$ by $S$. Si $S$ is in $S^-$, one prend $WF = E$. Si $S$ is in $S^+$, the nullite of $\Ext^1(W(F/S), S)$ shows that the extension $E$ is trivial, and one prend for $WF$ a lifting of $W(F/S)$ in $E$.

Prouvons the unicite. Si the under-objects $W'$ and $W''$ of $F$ are in $A^-$, and that $F/W'$ and $F/W''$ are in $A^+$, the nullites of $\Hom(W', F/W'')$ and $\Hom(W'', F/W')$ montrent that the morphisms $W' \hookrightarrow F \twoheadrightarrow F/W''$ and $W'' \hookrightarrow F \twoheadrightarrow F/W'$ are nuls, i.e. that $W' = W''$.

Enfin, for every morphism $f: F \to G$, tant $f(WF)$ that $f(F) \cap WG$ satisfy the property caracteristique of $WfF$: one has $WfF = fWF = fF \cap WG$.

\begin{corollary}[5.3.7]
For that a perverse sheaf mixte $F_o$ on $X_o$ let of weight $\leq w$, it is necessary and sufficient that every under-variety irreducible $Y_o$ of $X_o$ (of dimension notee $d$) admette a open dense $U_o$ such that $\HH^{-d}(F_o)|_{U_o}$ let of weight ponctuels $\leq w-d$.
\end{corollary}

\noindent\textbf{Proof.} The condition is necessary. Prouvons that it is sufficient. Si $F_o$ n'etait not of weight $\leq w$, of after 5.3.5, it admettrait a quotient simple $G_o$ of weight $w_1 > w$. Let $j: Y_o \hookrightarrow X_o$ smooth connected, of dimension $d$, and $L_o$ a sheaf smooth on $Y_o$, such that $G_o \simeq j_{!*}(L_o[d])$. One can prendre $Y_o$ affine, and $L_o$ ponctuellement pur of weight $w_1-d$. The exact sequence longue of cohomology attached a
\[
0 \to \Ker(F_o \to G_o) \to F_o \to G_o \to 0
\]
fournit, on a open dense $V_o$ of $Y_o$, a epimorphisme
\[
\HH^{-d}(F_o)|_{V_o} \twoheadrightarrow \HH^{-d}(G_o)|_{V_o} = L_o|_{V_o}\;.
\]
the hypothese assure that for $V_o$ sufficiently petit, the weight ponctuels of $\HH^{-d}(F_o)|_{V_o}$ are $\leq w-d$. Ceux of $L_o$ the are therefore also, of or $w_1 \leq w$: contradiction.

\begin{theorem}[5.3.8]
Si $F_o/X_o$ is a perverse sheaf pur, the perverse sheaf $F$ on $X$ is direct sum of perverse sheaves simples, therefore of the form $j_{!*}L[d]$ for $j: U \hookrightarrow X$ the inclusion of $U$ smooth connected purement of dimension $d$ and $L$ a $\overline{\QQ}_{\ell}$-sheaf smooth irreducible on $U$.
\end{theorem}

The proof s'obtient en recopiant [1](3.4.1(iii)). Let $F'$ the sum in $F$ of the under-perverse sheaves simples of $F$. C'is the more grand under-object semisimple of $F$, and it is enough to verify that $F' = F$. The under-sheaf $F'$ of $F$ is stable by Frobenius. It provient therefore of $F'_o \subset F_o$ (5.1.2). Let $F''_o = F_o/F'_o$. The class of the extension $0 \to F'_o \to F_o \to F''_o \to 0$ a image zero in $\Ext^1(F'', F')$ (5.1.15(iii)), puisque $F'_o$ and $F''_o$ are purs of the same weight (5.3.1). one has therefore $F \simeq F' \oplus F''$. Si $F''$ etait not zero, it admettrait a under-object simple; this dernier se releverait in $F$, contredisant the maximalite of $F'$. one has therefore $F'' = 0$, and $F' = F$.

Let $E_n$ the $\overline{\QQ}_{\ell}$-sheaf on $\Spec(\FF_q)$, of fiber $\overline{\QQ}_{\ell}^n$ (base $(e_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n}$), on laquelle the Frobenius $F$ agit in such a way unipotente with a seul bloc of Jordan: $Fe_n = e_n$, and, for $i < n$, $Fe_i = e_i + e_{i+1}$. C'is a sheaf pur indecomposable of weight $0$, not semisimple si $n > 1$. Notons again $E_n$ son inverse image on $X_o$.

\begin{proposition}[5.3.9]
(i) The perverse sheaves purs indecomposables are ceux of the form $S_o \otimes E_n$, with $S_o$ simple.

(ii) Si $S_o$ is simple, there exists $d$ and $S_1$ on $X_1 := X_o \otimes \FF_{q^d}$, of which $S_o$ let the direct image by $\pi: X_1 \to X_o$, restant simple on $X$, and not isomorphic a ses conjugues under $\Aut(\FF_{q^d}/\FF_q)$.
\end{proposition}

Let $F_o$ a perverse sheaf such that $F$ let semisimple. C'is the cas for $F_o$ pur (5.3.8), and for $F_o$ simple. Let $A$ the set of the classes of isomorphie of constituants simples of $F$, and ecrivons $F$ as sum of ses constituants isotypiques: $F = \bigoplus F_a$ ($a \in A$). The decomposition of $A$ en orbites fournit a decomposition stable by $F^*_q$ of $F$ -- therefore a decomposition of $F_o$ (5.1.2): $F_o = \bigoplus F_{oC}$ ($C$ orbite of $\Fr_q^*$ in $A$). For $C$ a orbite a $d$ elements and $a \in C$, $F_a \subset F_C \subset F$ is stable by $F^{*d}_q = (F^*_q)^d$, therefore defines a under-perverse sheaf $F_{1a}$ of the perverse sheaf $F_{1C}$ deduces of $F_{oC}$ by inverse image on $X_1 = X_o \otimes \FF_{q^d}$. For $\pi$ the projection of $X_1$ on $X_o$, one has $F_{oC} \simeq \pi_*F_{1a}$. Let $G_a$ simple of class $a$, and choisissons a isomorphism $\phi: F^{*d}_q G_a \simeq G_a$. One deduces a action $F^*_q$ of Frobenius on $V_a := \Hom(G_a, F_a) = \Hom(G_a, F)$, and the isomorphism $F_a \simeq G_a \otimes V_a$ is $F^{*d}_q$-equivariant.

Si $F_o$ is indecomposable, it n'y a that a orbite and $(V_a, F^{*d}_q)$ is indecomposable: $F^{*d}_q$ agissant on $V_a$ n'a that a seul bloc of Jordan. Changeant the isomorphism $\phi: F^{*d}_q G_a \simeq G_a$, one can supposer $F^{*d}_q$ on $V_a$ unipotent; in this case, $(G_a, \phi)$ is isomorphic a under-object of $(F_a, F^{*d}_q)$, therefore correspond a perverse sheaf $G_1$ on $X_1$, and $F_{1a} \simeq G_1 \otimes E_{\dim(V_a)}$. One utilise ici that the inverse image on $\Spec(\FF_{q^d})$ of $E_n$ is isomorphic to the sheaf analogue on $\Spec(\FF_{q^d})$. One obtains
\[
F_o \simeq \pi_*(G_1 \otimes E_n) \simeq \pi_*(G_1) \otimes E_n\;,
\]
and $\pi_*(G_1)$ is simple. This proves (i).

For $F_o$ simple, it n'y a that a orbite, and the action of $F^{*d}_q$ on $V_a$ is irreducible: $V_a$ is of dimension $1$, and one obtains (ii).

\begin{corollary}[6.2.8]\label{cor:6.2.8}(Theorem global of the cycles invariants).
Let $f: X \to Y$ a morphism proper (of schemes of type finite on $\CC$) and $K$ in $D^b_c(X, \CC)$. Let $V$ a open (of Zariski) of $Y$ on lequel $\HH^if_*K$ is locally constant. Si $K$ is semisimple of origine geometric, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:6.2.8.1}
\HH^i(X, K) \longrightarrow \HH^0(V, \HH^if_*K)\;.
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

In particular, for $V$ connected and $y \in Y$, posant $X_y = f^{-1}(y)$, $\pi_1(V, y)$ agit on $(\HH^if_*K)_y \xrightarrow{\sim} \HH^i(X_y, K)$, and (6.2.8.1) se recrit
\begin{equation}\label{eq:6.2.8.2}
\HH^i(X, K) \longrightarrow \HH^i(X_y, K)^{\pi_1(V, y)}\;.
\end{equation}

\begin{corollary}[6.2.9]\label{cor:6.2.9}(Theorem local of the cycles invariants).
Let $f: X \to Y$ a morphism proper, $K$ in $D^b_c(X, \CC)$ and $y \in Y$. Let $V$ a open (of Zariski) of $Y$, on lequel $\HH^if_*K$ is locally constant. For $B$ a boule sufficiently petite of centre $y$, and $j$ the inclusion of $B \cap V$ in $B$, one has
\[
\HH^0(B \cap V, \HH^if_*K) = (j_*j^*\HH^if_*K)_y\;,
\]
and
\[
\HH^0(B, \HH^if_*K) \xrightarrow{\sim} (\HH^if_*K)_y = \HH^i(X_y, K)\;.
\]
Si $K$ is semisimple of origine geometric, for a tel $B$, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:6.2.9.1}
\HH^i(X_y, K) \longrightarrow \HH^0(B \cap V, \HH^if_*K)\;.
\end{equation}
for $B \cap V$ connected and $z \in B \cap V$, that s'ecrit again
\begin{equation}\label{eq:6.2.9.2}
\HH^i(X_y, K) \longrightarrow \HH^i(X_z, K)^{\pi_1(B \cap V, z)}\;.
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

In 6.2.9, the existence of boules $B$ of the type indique is connue. These boules ne are ici that a commodite of exposition: one pourrait prendre of other voisinages formant a systeme fondamental of voisinages (defined by example en terme of a triangulation). This dit, and compte tenu of 6.2.5, the proof is the same that en 5.4.7-5.4.9.

\begin{theorem}[6.2.10]\label{thm:6.2.10}(Lefschetz difficult relative).
Let $f: X \to Y$ a morphism projective, $\ell$ the premiere class of Chern of a sheaf inversible relativement ample, and $F$ a perverse sheaf semisimple of origine geometric on $X$. For $i \geq 0$, one has
\[
\ell^i: {}^{p}\HH^{-i}f_*F \xrightarrow{\sim} {}^{p}\HH^if_*F(i)\;.
\]
\end{theorem}

This states se deduces of 6.2.6 and of 5.4.10. A cas particulier utile is celui or $Y$ is reduit a point. It fournit the Lefschetz theorem difficult en cohomology of intersection.

\begin{remark}[6.2.11]
One aimerait pouvoir, in the enonces which precedent remplacer ``of origine geometric'' by ``of the form $j_{!*}L[d]$, for $j$ the inclusion of $Y$ smooth connected of dimension $d$ and $L$ the systeme local under-jacent a variation of Hodge structures polarisable''. One aimerait also pouvoir remplacer the algebraic varieties by of the varieties kahleriennes compactes.

Si $f$ is a morphism of varieties kahleriennes compactes, we ne savons demontrer ni that $Rf_*\CC$ is sum of the ${}^{p}\HH^iRf_*\CC[i]$, ni that the perverse sheaves ${}^{p}\HH^iRf_*\CC$ are semi-simples, ni the analogue of 6.2.10 for $Rf_*(\CC[\dim X])$.
\end{remark}
