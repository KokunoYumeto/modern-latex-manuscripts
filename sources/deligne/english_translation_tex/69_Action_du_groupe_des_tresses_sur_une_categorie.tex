\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} We describe how to define by generators and relations an action of the braid group on a category. More generally, how to define a functor from a generalized positive braids monoid, corresponding to a finite Coxeter group, to a monoidal category. Applications to constructions of Bondal (exceptional systems) and of Brou\'e--Michel (correspondences on flag manifolds) are given.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the inclusion of $W_n$ in $W_{n+1}$ admet a adjoint a line;
\item the functor of passage to the quotient $W_{n+1} \to W_{n+1}/W_n$ admet a adjoint a line;
\item all object $Y$ of $W_{n+1}$ figure in a triangle distingue $(X, Y, Z)$ with $X$ in $W_n$ and $Z$ in the orthogonal a line of $W_n$ in $W_{n+1}$.

\subsection*{1.2}\label{sec:1.2}

The \emph{mono\"ide} (without unite) $B_n^+$ \emph{of the braids strictement positives} a $n$ brins ($n \geq 2$) is the mono\"ide generated by of the generateurs $s_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq n-1$) soumis to the relations
