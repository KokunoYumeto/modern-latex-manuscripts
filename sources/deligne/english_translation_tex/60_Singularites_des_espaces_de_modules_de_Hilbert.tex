Let $K$ a number field totalement reel of degree $g$ on $\mathbb{Q}$ and $R$
the ring of ses integers. Fixons a integer $n\geq 3$ (the niveau), let
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n]\subset\mathbb{C}$ generated by the racines niemes of the unite and let
$\Gamma(n)$ the under-group of congruence of niveau $n$.

In~\cite{Rapoport}, M.~Rapoport considered a probleme of modules for
of the abelian varieties of dimension $g$, a multiplication by $R$, equipped with
data auxiliaires convenables. the moduli space $\mathscr{M}_R$ corresponding
is a space algebraic on $\Spec(\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n][1/n])$. the space algebraic
$\mathscr{M}_R$ on $\mathbb{C}$ which s'en deduces by the extension of scalars
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n][1/n]\to\mathbb{C}$ is the variety of Shimura $\mathfrak{X}^g/\Gamma(n)$,
or $\mathfrak{X}$ is the Poincare half-plane, on lequel $\SL(2,K)$ agit via
the $g$ embeddings of $\SL(2,K)$ in $\SL(2,\mathbb{R})$ deduits of the embeddings
reels of $K$.

A fois choisie a datum combinatoire of type suitable, Rapoport
defines a compactification $\bar{\mathscr{M}}_R$ of $\mathscr{M}_R$, which on $\mathbb{C}$ se
reduit a compactification toro\"  + "{i}dale a the Mumford and al~\cite{AMRT} of $\mathscr{M}_R$.

In~\cite{Rapoport}, Rapoport affirme that $\bar{\mathscr{M}}_R$ is proper and smooth
on $\Spec(\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n][1/n])$. A corollary, exploite in
Deligne-Ribet~\cite{DeligneRibet}, is the irreductibilite of the fibers geometriques
of $\bar{\mathscr{M}}_R$ on $\Spec(\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n][1/n])$. the assertion of proprete is
fausse en the caracteristiques (premieres a $n$) divisant the discriminant $\Delta$ of $K$
on $\mathbb{Q}$. The probleme is a distance finite: not-proprete of $\mathscr{M}_R$ on the moduli space of the abelian varieties polarisees, en the caracteristiques divisant $\Delta$.
In the map~\cite{Rapoport} pages~322--323 of the critere valuatif of proprete, the cas
or there is bonne reduction n'is not traite.

For $[K : \mathbb{Q}] > 1$, that $\bar{\mathscr{M}}_R$ let proper and smooth contredisait of ailleurs

$l$-adique of $\bar{\mathscr{M}}_R$ is ramifiee en the nombres prime $p$ (prime a $l$ and $n$)
divisant $\Delta$.

For retablir the proprete, it faut modifier the probleme of modules considered,
remplacant $\mathscr{M}_R$ by a moduli space of abelian varieties of which $\mathscr{M}_R$
is a open fiber a fiber dense. The scheme $\bar{\mathscr{M}}$ on $\Spec(\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n][1/n])$ n'is more

Rapoport for compactifier $\mathscr{M}$ en $\bar{\mathscr{M}}$ proper on $\Spec(\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n][1/n])$: one prend
for $\bar{\mathscr{M}}$ the gluing of $\bar{\mathscr{M}}_R$ and $\mathscr{M}$ the long of $\mathscr{M}_R$.

In the present article, we definissons $\bar{\mathscr{M}}$ and determinons ses singularities.
The fibers of $\bar{\mathscr{M}} \to \Spec(\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n][1/n])$ are normales and of the resulte en every

shows that $\Im(u)$ is totalement isotrope, therefore lagrangien. Similarly for
$\Im(\,\!^t u)$. This proves (5.5.2).

For $S$ spectre dun field $k$ of caracteristique $p$, the assertions (5.5.1), (5.5.2)
deviennent vraies after extensions of the scalaires a extension parfaite of $k$.
By descent, they are deja vraies on $k$.

Supposons maintenant that $s \in S$ a field residuel of caracteristique $p$ and
prouvons~5.5 to the neighborhood of $s$. En $s$, one dispose of the under-space lagrangien
$\Im(\,\!^t u)$ in $H'$. Let $L_s$ a supplementaire lagrangien and relevons $L_s$ en a
under-space lagrangien $L'$ of $H'$. Cest possible in a neighborhood of $s$. Permutant
the roles of $H'$ and $H''$, one constructs similarly $L''$ lagrangien in $H''$. The
morphism

\begin{equation}
L' \oplus L'' \longrightarrow H'
\end{equation}

is a isomorphism en $s$, therefore in a neighborhood of $s$. Similarly for

\begin{equation}
L' \oplus L'' \longrightarrow H''.
\end{equation}

En terme of these decompositions $L' \oplus L''$ of $H'$ and $H''$ as sum of
under-spaces lagrangiens, the matrice of $u$ is $\diag(1, p)$. The forms alternees
mettent $L'$ and $L''$ en duality, the same for $H'$ and $H''$:

\begin{equation}
H' \cong L_0 \otimes \mathscr{O}_S \oplus L_0^* \otimes \mathscr{O}_S, \quad H'' \cong L_0 \otimes \mathscr{O}_S \oplus L_0^* \otimes \mathscr{O}_S.
\end{equation}

For obtenir the form canonical voulue, it reste a choisir a isomorphism
between $L'$ and $L_0 \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathscr{O}_S$.

For $p$ inversible on $S$, lassertion~5.5 is trivial: a isomorphism symplectique
of $H'$ with $H_0 \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathscr{O}_S$ se prolonge uniquement en a isomorphism of type
voulu. This conclut the proof of~5.5.

\begin{remark}
Let $k$ a ring, and $R_g$ the $k$-algebra generated by of the
indeterminees $x_{ij}$, $y_{ij}$ ($1 \leq i, j \leq g$) soumises to the relations $x_{ij} = x_{ji}$, $y_{ij} = y_{ji}$,
$\sum_j x_{ij}y_{jk} = 0$ ($1 \leq i, k \leq g$). The scheme $\Spec(R_g)$ is lespace of modules of the
paires of matrices carrees symmetric $X$, $Y$ of taille $g \times g$ verifiant $XY = 0$.

In the premiere part of~\cite{ChaiNorman}, Chai and Norman prouvent that, for $k$ a field,
$\Spec(R_g)$ is of Cohen-Macaulay, purement of dimension $g(g+1)/2$, and reduit.
Lassertion `reduit' is due independamment a B.~Kotsev (J.~Alg.~139 (1991),
p.~484--504). For $k$ quelconque, G.C.M.~Ruitenberg (Comm.~Alg.~16 (1988),
p.~1993--2011), a given a base explicite of $R_g$ on $k$. Lespace $\Spec(R_g)$ is
therefore plat on $\Spec(k)$ and, ses fibers geometriques being of Cohen-Macaulay and
reduites, is of Cohen-Macaulay (resp.~is reduit) si $k$ lest.

On $\Spec(\mathbb{Z}_p)$, considerons maintenant lespace of modules $\mathscr{M}_1 =
\mathscr{M}_0 \times_{\Spec(\mathbb{Z})} \Spec(\mathbb{Z}_p)$ of the paires of matrices carrees symmetric $X$, $Y$ of taille
$g \times g$ verifiant $XY = pI$. The group $\GL(g)$ agit on this space, by $(X, Y) \mapsto$
$(uX\,\!^t u, \,\!^t u^{-1}Yu)$. The fiber speciale is $\Spec(R_g)$, for $k = \mathbb{F}_p$. Geometriquement,
i.e.~on the algebraic closure of $k$, $\GL(g)$ a on $\Spec(R_g)$ a nombre finite of orbites.
One checks that the orbites ouvertes have for points of the paires $(X, Y)$ which, after
a changement of coordonnees suitable $u$, se mettent under the form

\begin{equation}
X = \begin{pmatrix} A & 0 \\\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad Y = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\\ 0 & B \end{pmatrix},
\end{equation}

with $A$ and $B$ not degenerees. Of tels points se relevent en caracteristique~0: si
$\tilde{A}$ and $\tilde{B}$ relevent $A$ and $B$, $(X, Y)$ se relevent en $(\tilde{X}, \tilde{Y})$ with

\begin{equation}
\tilde{X} = \begin{pmatrix} \tilde{A} & 0 \\\ 0 & p\tilde{B}^{-1} \end{pmatrix}, \quad \tilde{Y} = \begin{pmatrix} p\tilde{A}^{-1} & 0 \\\ 0 & \tilde{B} \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}

It follows that the fiber of caracteristique~0 of $\mathscr{M}_1$ is dense in $\mathscr{M}_1$. The fiber
speciale being reduite, $\mathscr{M}_1$ is plat on $\mathbb{Z}_p$ (EGA IV 2e part 3.4.6.1). It follows
that $\mathscr{M}_0$ is plat on $\Spec(\mathbb{Z})$ a fibers geometriques reduites and of Cohen-
Macaulay. The fiber generique being smooth, the critere of Serre EGA~IV~5.8.6
shows that $\mathscr{M}_0$ is normal.

By~5.4, one deduces the same properties for $\mathscr{M}$: lespace of modules $\mathscr{M}$
normal plat on $\Spec(\mathbb{Z})$ a fibers geometriques reduites and of Cohen-
Macaulay. These results are egalement obtained by Chai and Norman, in the
deuxieme part of leur article~\cite{ChaiNorman} (of the less for $p > 2$), by a methode
more compliquee.
\end{remark}
