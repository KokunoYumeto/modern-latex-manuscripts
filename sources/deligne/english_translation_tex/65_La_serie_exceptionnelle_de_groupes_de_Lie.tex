\noindent\textbf{Resume.} Numerologie of the groups exceptionnels and a interpretation conjecturale.\\[2pt]
\textbf{Abstract.} Numerology of exceptional Lie groups and a conjectural explanation.

\bigskip

Let $G_0$ the group deploye adjoint of the a of the types suivant: $A_1$, $A_2$, $G_2$, $D_4$, $F_4$, $E_6$, $E_7$, $E_8$.
One fixe a epinglage of $G_0$. One denotes $\Gamma$ the group of the automorphismes of $G_0$. For $\Gamma$ the group of the automorphismes of the diagram of Dynkin, identifie to the group of the automorphismes of $G_0$ respectant the epinglage, c'is a product semi-direct $\Gamma \ltimes G_0$. The group $G$ is a algebraic group on $\Q$.

Considerer the Lie group complex $G(\C)$, voire a form compacte of $G(\C)$, we suffirait. The possibilite, for notre propos, of inclure $D_4$ in the liste a ete decouverte by Cohen and of~Man.

Let $T$ the tore maximal of $G$ fourni by the epinglage and $\langle \, , \, \rangle$ the form bilineaire canonical on $\Lie(T)^\vee$: the form inverse of the restriction a $\Lie(T)$ of the form of Killing $\Tr(\ad x \ad y)$. Let $\alpha$ the more grande racine and $k = \langle \alpha, \alpha \rangle$. Values of $k$: $1/2$, $1/3$, $1/4$, $1/6$, $1/9$, $1/12$, $1/18$, $1/30$. Let enfin $h$ the nombre of Coxeter, egal Let $k$, Let $-(1/6) - k$. One sets $a := 1/h$. \`A this liste, ajoutons enfin the group trivial, with $a = 5/6$ or $a = -1$.

Let $\frg$ the algebra of Lie of $G$, $U$ son algebra enveloppante and $C \in U$ the element of Casimir corresponding a the form of Killing. For $V$ a representation of $G$, we appellerons endomorphism of Casimir of $V$ the action of $C$. For $V$ irreducible, c'is the multiplication by a scalaire, appele Casimir of $V$. Recall that the Casimir of the representation adjointe vaut $1$.

\textbf{Notre observation is that for $(G, a)$ as ci-dessus, $G$ admet of the representations virtuelles}
$X_i$ ($0 \leq i \leq 4$), $Y_i$ ($0 \leq i \leq 3$), $Y_i^*$ ($0 \leq i \leq 3$), $A$, $C$ and $C^*$
\textbf{ayant the properties (A) a (G) suivantes.}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(A)} Chacune of these representations is let irreducible, let $0$, let the opposee of a representation irreducible. one has $X_0 = Y_0 = Y_0^* = 1$, the representation trivial, and $X_1 = Y_1 = Y_1^* = \frg$, the representation adjointe.

\medskip
\noindent For each representation $R$ of the symmetric group $S_n$, one dispose of a functor $V \mapsto [R](V) := \Hom_{S_n}(R, V^{\otimes n})$. For $V$ a representation of a group, the class of $[R](V)$ in the group of Grothendieck of the representations is a $\lambda$-polynome (polynome en the $\wedge^i V$) en the class of $V$. For $R$ irreducible corresponding a partition $p$ of $n$, one ecrira $[p]_V$ for the class $[R](V)$. Examples:
$$
[(n)]_V = \Sym^n V, \quad [(1, \ldots, 1)]_V = \wedge^n V, \quad [(2,1)]_V = V \otimes \wedge^2 V - \wedge^3 V, \quad [(3)]_V = V^3 - 2V \cdot \wedge^2 V + \wedge^3 V.
$$

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(B)} In the $\lambda$-ring of the representations of $G$, one has:
\begin{align*}
\text{(B1)} \quad & [(2)]_{\frg} = \Sym^2 \frg = 1 + Y_2 + Y_2^* \\
\text{(B2)} \quad & [(1,1)]_{\frg} = \wedge^2 \frg = \frg + X_2 \\
\text{(B3)} \quad & [(3)]_{\frg} = \Sym^3 \frg = \frg + X_2 + A + Y_3 + Y_3^* \\
\text{(B4)} \quad & [(2,1)]_{\frg} = 2\frg + X_2 + Y_2 + Y_2^* + A + C + C^* \\
\text{(B5)} \quad & [(1,1,1)]_{\frg} = \wedge^3 \frg = 1 + X_2 + Y_2 + Y_2^* + X_3 \\
\text{(B6)} \quad & Y_2 \otimes \frg = \frg + X_2 + Y_2 + A + Y_3 + C \\
\text{(B7)} \quad & Y_2^* \otimes \frg = \frg + X_2 + Y_2^* + A + Y_3^* + C^*
\end{align*}

One notera that these formules are permutees by the involution $*$, si the one convient that $A$ and the $X_i$ (in particular $\frg$) are fixes by $*$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(C)} As consequence of \textbf{(B)}, one has in the $\lambda$-ring of the representations of $G$:
\begin{align*}
\text{(C1)} \quad & X_2 = \wedge^2 \frg - \frg \\
\text{(C2)} \quad & X_3 = \wedge^3 \frg - \frg \cdot \wedge^2 \frg + \Sym^2 \frg + \frg - 1
\end{align*}
Moreover,
$$
X_4 = \wedge^4 \frg - \frg \cdot \wedge^3 \frg + \Sym^2 \frg \cdot \wedge^2 \frg - \wedge^2 \frg - (\frg + A) \cdot \frg + \frg.
$$

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(D)} The Casimir a on these representations the values suivantes:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|cccccc}
 & $X_n$ & $Y_n$ & $Y_n^*$ & $A$ & $C$ & $C^*$ \\
\hline
$n$ & $n + (n^2 - n)a$ & & $n + (n^2 - n)a^*$ & $8/3$ & $3 + 3a$ & $3 + 3a^*$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Lorsque the representation consideree is $0$, this states is a interpreter as being vide.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(E)} The dimensions of these representations are data by the formules suivantes, or the one has fait $\lambda := -6a$:
\begin{align*}
\dim X_1 &= -2(\lambda + 5)(\lambda - 6)/\lambda(\lambda - 1) \\
\dim X_2 &= 5(\lambda + 3)(\lambda + 5)(\lambda - 4)(\lambda - 6)/\lambda^2(\lambda - 1)^2 \\
\dim X_3 &= -10(\lambda + 2)(\lambda + 4)(\lambda + 5)(\lambda - 3)(\lambda - 5)(\lambda - 6)/\lambda^3(\lambda - 1)^3 \\
\dim X_4 &= 5(\lambda + 3)(\lambda + 4)(\lambda + 5)(\lambda + 3)(2\lambda - 4)(\lambda - 5)(\lambda - 6)(2\lambda - 5)/\lambda^4(\lambda - 1)^4 \\
\dim Y_2 &= -90(\lambda + 5)(\lambda - 4)/\lambda^2(\lambda - 1)(2\lambda - 1) \\
\dim Y_3 &= -10(\lambda + 5)(5\lambda - 6)(\lambda - 4)(\lambda - 5)(\lambda - 6)/\lambda^3(\lambda - 1)^2(2\lambda - 1)(3\lambda - 1) \\
\dim A &= -27(\lambda + 3)(\lambda + 4)(\lambda + 5)(\lambda - 4)(\lambda - 5)(\lambda - 6)/\lambda^2(\lambda - 1)^2(3\lambda - 1)(3\lambda - 2) \\
\dim C &= 640(\lambda + 3)(\lambda + 5)(\lambda - 3)(\lambda - 5)/\lambda^3(\lambda - 1)(2\lambda - 1)(3\lambda - 2)
\end{align*}

For $Y_n^*$ and $C^*$, the dimension is given by the same formule that for $Y_n$, $C$ with $\lambda$ remplace by $\lambda^* := 1 - \lambda = -6a^*$. Noter that the formules for $X_i$ and $A$ are invariantes by $\lambda \mapsto \lambda^*$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(F)} For chacune of the egalites (B1) a (B7), (C1) and (C2), the formules (E) fournissent a expression of the dimension of the prime (resp.~second) membre as being the value en $\lambda$ of a function rational $R_1$ (resp.~$R_2$). one has $R_1 = R_2$ (equality of functions rationnelles).

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(G)} For chacune of the egalites (B1) a (B7), (C1) and (C2), the formules (D), (E) and (1), (2) fournissent a expression of the trace of Casimir on the prime (resp.~second) membre as being the value en $\lambda$ of a function rational $R_1$ (resp.~$R_2$). one has $R_1 = R_2$.

\bigskip

For $R$ a representation of $S_n$ and $V$ a representation of a group semisimple, the trace of the endomorphism of Casimir of $[R](V)$ is deduite of the trace of the endomorphism of Casimir of $V$ by the formule suivante: si $\chi$ is the character of $R$, $n(\sigma)$ the nombre of cycles of a permutation $\sigma \in S_n$ and $m(\sigma)$ the sum of the carres of the longueurs of the cycles of $\sigma$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:casimir-trace}
\Tr(C, [R](V)) = \frac{1}{n!} \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} \chi(\sigma) \, m(\sigma) \, (\dim V)^{n(\sigma)-2} \, \Tr(C, V).
\end{equation}
For deux representations $V$ and $W$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:casimir-tensor}
\Tr(C, V \otimes W) = \Tr(C, V) \dim(W) + \dim(V) \Tr(C, W).
\end{equation}

Je ne connais not of proof satisfaisante of this which precede. The enonces (B), (C) and (D) are obtained by the force brutale of the programme LiE. That the dimensions and Casimir of $\frg$, $Y_2$ and $Y_2^*$ let of the form (D), (E) for $a$ suitable is deduces in Vogel [2] \S\,6.9 of the fait that the seuls elements invariants of $\Sym^4(\frg)$ are the multiples of the carre of the element invariant of $\Sym^2(\frg)$. The dimension of the $X_i$ se deduces of celle of $\frg$ by (C). Si $n = k$, $Y_n$ is the representation of weight dominant $n\alpha$, for $\alpha$ the more grande racine, and (D) for $Y_n$ can s'en deduire. the assertion (D) for $X_n$ can se deduire of (C): to the second membre, the trace of the endomorphism of Casimir se calcule by (1).

Admettons (D), and the formules of dimension for $Y_2$, $Y_2^*$, and the $X_i$. Si the one exprime the equality of the dimensions and of the traces of Casimir for the deux membres of (B3), (B4), (B6), and (B7), one obtains a systeme of equations lineaires en $\dim Y_3$, $\dim Y_3^*$, $\dim A$, $\dim C$ and $\dim C^*$ a coefficients to the second membre functions rationnelles of $\lambda$. One deduces a expression for these dimensions as a function rational of $\lambda$. We have suivi this methode for trouver a polynome en $\lambda$ multiple of the denominateur, and for estimer the degree of the numerateur, the numerateur being ensuite obtained by interpolation a partir of the values connues corresponding to the $(G, a)$. That the functions rationnelles obtained se factorisent as en (E) is a miracle. The identites $R_1 = R_2$ of (F), (G) resultent simplement of this that for a polynome suitable $P$ en $\lambda$, $R_1 P$ and $R_2 P$ are deux polynomes en $\lambda$ egaux en a nombre of values of $\lambda$ strictement more grand that leur degree.

\section*{The conjecture}

Je conjecture that there exists a abelian category semisimple $\Q(t)$-lineaire $\mathcal{C}_t$, equipped with a tensor product $\otimes$ and of contraintes of associativity, of commutativity and of unite (Saavedra [1], chap.~1), ayant the properties suivantes.

\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] The groups $\Hom(X, Y)$ are of dimensions finite on $\Q(t)$.

\item[(ii)] Each object admet a dual: $\mathcal{C}_t$ is rigide. Recall that this permet of define for every object $X$ sa dimension $\dim(X) \in \End(1) = \Q(t)$: the compose $1 \to X^\vee \otimes X \to 1$ of the morphisms of evaluation and of coevaluation $1 \to X^\vee \otimes X$.

\item[(iii)] The tensor category $\mathcal{C}_t$ is of type adjoint to the sens suivant. There exists a object $\frg$, equipped with a crochet of Lie $[\, , \,]: \frg \otimes \frg \to \frg$, agissant functorially on each object $X$: action $\rho_X: \frg \otimes X \to X$. the action is compatible to the tensor product: $\rho_{X \otimes Y} = \rho_X \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes \rho_Y$. For $X = \frg$, $\rho_X$ is $[\, , \,]$. the action $\rho_X$ se transpose en a coaction $^t\!\rho_X: X \to \frg^\vee \otimes X$. For every $X$, $X^\frg := \Ker({}^t\!\rho_X)$ is the more grand under-object of $X$ sum of copies of $\mathbf{1}$. Enfin, all object is under-quotient of a sum of $\frg^{\otimes n}$.

\item[(iv)] $\frg$ is a object simple, and the form of Killing is not degeneree.

\item[(v)] There exists of the objects simples $X_i$ ($0 \leq i \leq 4$), $Y_i$ ($0 \leq i \leq 3$), $Y_i^*$ ($0 \leq i \leq 3$), $A$, $C$ and $C^*$ verifiant (A) a (E), with $a = t$.

\item[(vi)] All object simple $S$ is absolument simple, i.e.~$\End(S) = \Q(t)$, and is isomorphic a son dual, more precisely admet a form bilineaire symmetric not degeneree $S \otimes S \to \mathbf{1}$. The dimension $\dim(S) \in \Q(t)$ of a object simple admet a factorisation en factors lineaires.

\item[(vii)] The category $\mathcal{C}_t$ admet a $\otimes$-autoequivalence $*$, semi-lineaire relativement a $t \mapsto -(1/6) - t$, envoyant $A$ and the $X_i$ on eux-same, $Y_i$ on $Y_i^*$, $C$ on $C^*$; the carre of $*$ is isomorphic a the identity.

\item[(viii)] En a sens a preciser, the category of representations of $G$ is a specialisation of $\mathcal{C}_t$ en $t = a$.
\end{itemize}

\section*{A model}

Voici a model for this conjecture. Let $\mathcal{B}_n^-[t]$ the tensor category $\Z[t]$-lineaire rigide suivante.

Objects: sets finite.

$\Hom(X, Y)$: let $\mathcal{C}$ the set of the classes of isomorphie of varieties compactes of dimension a bord, of bord $X \amalg Y$ (bordismes of $X$ a $Y$). The $\Z[t]$-module $\Hom(X, Y)$ is the quotient of the $\Z[t]$-module free $\Z[t]^{(\mathcal{C})}$ of base $[c]$ ($c \in \mathcal{C}$) by $[c \cup \text{un cercle}] = t[c]$.

Composition: composition of the bordismes.

$\otimes$: sum disjointe.

For $\lambda \in \Q$, let $\mathcal{B}_\lambda$ the tensor category $\Q$-lineaire rigide deduite of $\mathcal{B}_n^-[t]$ by the extension of scalars $\Z[t] \to \Q$, $t \mapsto \lambda$. Disons that a morphism $f: X \to Y$ of $\mathcal{B}_\lambda$ is negligeable si for every $g: Y \to X$, $\Tr(gf) = 0$. The morphisms negligeables ferment a ideal $I_\lambda$ and, si $f$ is negligeable, $f \otimes g$ the is also. The category quotient $\mathcal{B}_\lambda/I_\lambda$ is therefore again tensor rigide. Let $(\mathcal{B}_\lambda/I_\lambda)^{\text{kar}}$ son Karoubian envelope, obtained en adjoignant formally the direct summands corresponding to the endomorphisms idempotents.

Si $\lambda$ is a integer $n \geq 0$, this Karoubian envelope n'is other that the category $\Rep(O(n))$ of the representations of the group orthogonal $O(n)$. More precisely, let $V$ a space vector on $\Q$ equipped with a form bilineaire symmetric not degeneree $B$. There exists then a $\otimes$-functor unique a isomorphism unique pres, of $\mathcal{B}_n^-$ in $\Rep(O(V))$, envoyant the object $\{1\}$ on $V$ and the bordisme

of $\{1, 2\}$ a $\emptyset$ on $B: V \otimes V \to \mathbf{1}$. This $\otimes$-functor induces a equivalence of $(\mathcal{B}_n/I_n)^{\text{kar}}$ with $\Rep(O(V))$. C'is the a traduction of the theorie of Weyl of the invariants of $O(V)$ in $V^{\otimes N}$ (cf.~[3], chap.~V, \S\,5).

Si $\lambda$ is a integer pair $-2p \leq 0$, the Karoubian envelope $(\mathcal{B}_\lambda/I_\lambda)^{\text{kar}}$ is a variante of the category $\Rep(\Sp(2p))$ of the representations of the group symplectique $\Sp(2p)$: the element central $-1 \in \Sp(2p)$ defines a $\Z/(2)$-graduation of each representation $V$ of $\Sp(2p)$, and one defines the contrainte of commutativity by the regle of Koszul. En of other termes, in the category of the super-representations of $OSp(0|2p)$, one considers the subcategory of the under-quotients of sums of $V^{\otimes N}$, for $V$ the super-representation evidente.

Si one considers similarly the sets $S$ finite orientes, i.e.~equipped with $\varepsilon: S \to \{\pm 1\}$, and the bordismes orientes, one obtains a tensor category $\Z[t]$-lineaire rigide $\mathcal{A}_n^+[t]$. For $\lambda \in \Q$, let $\mathcal{A}_\lambda$ the category $\Q$-lineaire rigide deduite of $\mathcal{A}_n^+[t]$ by the extension of scalars $\Z[t] \to \Q$, $\lambda \mapsto t$. One defines as precedemment the ideal $I_\lambda$ of the morphisms negligeables of $\mathcal{A}_\lambda$. Lorsque $\lambda$ is a integer $n \geq 0$, $(\mathcal{A}_\lambda/I_\lambda)^{\text{kar}}$ n'is other that $\Rep(\GL(n))$. Si $\lambda$ is a integer $-n \leq 0$, one obtains the variante of $\Rep(\GL(n))$ defined by $-1 \in \GL(n)$: in the category of the super-representations of $\GL(0|n)$, the subcategory of the under-quotients of sums of $V^{\otimes N} \otimes V^{\vee \otimes M}$, for $V$ the super-representation evidente.

In these deux cas, si one etend the scalaires of $\Z[t]$ a $\Q(t)$, the Karoubian envelope of the category $\mathcal{A}_t$ or $\mathcal{B}_t$ obtained is a tensor category rigide semisimple. Ses objects simples $S$ are absolument simples, of dimension $\dim(S) \in \Q(t)$ a polynome en $t$ product of factors lineaires.

\section*{The formules}

Voici the formules.

Let $V_\lambda^-$ the object ``a point'' of $\mathcal{B}_\lambda^-$ and $V$ son image in $\mathcal{B}_\lambda^{-\text{kar}}$ (``representation evidente''). For etudier the subcategory of $\mathcal{B}_\lambda^{-\text{kar}}$, formee by the under-quotients of sums of $V^{\otimes i}$ ($i \leq N$), a methode is of proceder by ``prolongement polynomial'' a partir of the category $\Rep(O(n))$, $n$ grand. The objects irreductibles of $\mathcal{B}_\lambda^{-\text{kar}}$ correspondent ainsi to the sequences decroissantes of integers $\ell_i \geq 0$, with $\ell_i$ zero for $i$ sufficiently grand. The representation corresponding of $O(n)$ ($n$ grand) a for weight dominant $\sum_i (\ell_i - \ell_{i+1})\omega_i$, in the notation of the tables of Bourbaki. The formule of dimension of Weyl fournit by prolongement the dimension of this object irreducible. Notons $h((\ell_i))$ or simplement $h$ the integer such that the representation of the symmetric group defined by the partition $(\ell_i)$ of $\sum \ell_i$ let of dimension $(\sum \ell_i)!/h$. Si $\ell_i = 0$ for $i > a$, one has
$$
h = \prod_{i=1}^a (\ell_i + a - i)! \; \prod_{1 \leq i < j \leq a} ((\ell_i - i) - (\ell_j - j)).
$$

Let $a(n)$ (resp.~$b(n)$) the nombre of paires $i, j$ ($1 \leq i \leq j$) such that $n = (\ell_i - i) + (\ell_j - j)$ (resp.~$n = -i - j$). With these notations,
$$
\dim V((\ell_i)) = (1/h) \cdot \prod (t + n)^{a(n) - b(n)}.
$$
Formally: $(1/h) \cdot \prod (t + \ell_i - i + \ell_j - j) / \prod (t - i - j)$. C'is a polynome of degree $\sum \ell_i$.

the object $\frg := \wedge^2 V$ joue the role of the representation adjointe: it is equipped with a crochet of Lie, and agit functorially and in such a way compatible to the tensor product on each object. The form bilineaire $\Tr(\rho(x)\rho(y), V)$ on $\frg$ is not degeneree. On $V((\ell_i))$, the Casimir corresponding is
$$
\sum_i \ell_i(\ell_i - 2i) + t \sum_i \ell_i
$$
ainsi that one the satisfies by prolongement a partir of the cas of $O(n)$ ($n$ grand).

Let $V_\lambda^+$ the object ``a point positivement oriente'' ($\varepsilon = 1$) of $\mathcal{A}_\lambda^+$, and $V$ son image in $\mathcal{A}_\lambda^{+\text{kar}}$ (``representation evidente''). For etudier the subcategory of $\mathcal{A}_\lambda^{+\text{kar}}$ formee of the under-quotients of sums of $V^{\otimes i} \otimes V^{\vee \otimes j}$ ($i, j \leq N$), a methode is of proceder by ``prolongement polynomial'' a partir of the category $\Rep(\GL(n))$, $n$ grand. The objects irreductibles of $\mathcal{A}_\lambda^{+}$ correspondent ainsi to the couples of sequences decroissantes of integers positifs $((\ell_i), (m_i))$, with $\ell_i$ and $m_i$ nuls for $i$ sufficiently grand. Si one identifie the group of the characters of the tore maximal ``matrices diagonales'' of $\GL(n)$ a $\Z^n$ of the facon usuelle, the representation corresponding of $\GL(n)$ ($n$ grand) a for weight dominant
$$
(\ell_1, \ell_2, \ldots, 0, \ldots, 0, -m_2, -m_1).
$$
the object $\frg := V \otimes V^\vee$ joue the role of the representation adjointe, and the form bilineaire $\Tr(\rho(x)\rho(y), V)$ is not degeneree.

Let $c(n)$ (resp.~$d(n)$) the nombre of paires $i, j$ ($1 \leq i, j$) such that $n = \ell_i - i + m_j - j + 1$ (resp.~$n = -i - j + 1$). By prolongement of the formule of Weyl,
$$
\dim V((\ell_i), (m_i)) = (1/h((\ell_i)) \cdot h((m_i))) \cdot \prod (t + n)^{c(n) - d(n)}.
$$
Formally: $(1/h((\ell_i)) \cdot h((m_i))) \cdot \prod (t + \ell_i - i + m_j - j + 1) / \prod (t - i - j + 1)$. C'is a polynome en $t$ of degree $\sum \ell_i + \sum m_i$. The Casimir relative a $\Tr(\rho(x)\rho(y), V)$ is
$$
\sum_i \ell_i(\ell_i - 2i + 1) + \sum_i m_i(m_i - 2i + 1) + t\bigl(\sum_i \ell_i + \sum_i m_i\bigr).
$$
