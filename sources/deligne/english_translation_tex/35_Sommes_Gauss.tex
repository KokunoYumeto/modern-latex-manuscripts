\section*{O. Introduction.}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{O. Introduction}

A Gauss sum is the product scalaire of a character additif and of a character multiplicatif of a finite field. Si $\FF_q$ is a finite field a $q$ elements, $\chi$ a character of $\FF_q^*$, and $\psi$ a character of the group additif of $\FF_q$, one sets
\[
g(\chi, \psi) = \sum_{x \in \FF_q^*} \chi(x) \psi(x).
\]
One assumed en principe $\chi$ and $\psi$ not trivial; the sum $g(\chi, \psi)$ is then of value absolute $q^{1/2}$. One parle of sum $n$-ique si $\chi$ is of ordre $n$. For $a \in \FF_q^*$, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:0.1}
g(\chi, \psi(ax)) = \chi(a)^{-1} g(\chi, \psi).
\tag{0.1}
\end{equation}
In particular, si $\chi$ is of ordre $n$, $g(\chi, \psi)^n$ is independant of $\psi$.

A each nombre prime $p \neq 2$, one can attacher the Gauss sum quadratique
\[
g_p = \sum_{x \in \FF_p^*} \left(\frac{x}{p}\right) \exp(2\pi i x/p)
\]
 or $\left(\frac{x}{p}\right)$ is the symbol of Legendre: $+1$ si $x$ is a carre mod $p$ and $-1$ otherwise. This sum a ete calculee by Gauss: c'is $\sqrt{p}$ or $i\sqrt{p}$ selon that $p \equiv 1$ or $-1 \pmod 4$.

Si one veut, similarly, attacher a Gauss sum $n$-ique a prime ideal $\frakp$ of the ring of integers $\cO$ of a number field $F$, the point is to define a character multiplicatif, and of a character additif of the field residuel $\cO/\frakp$.

Assume that $F$ contienne a racine primitive $n$-ieme of the unite, and posons $\mu := \mu_n(\cO)$. Si $\frakp$ is prime a $n$, one has $\mu \xrightarrow{\sim} \mu_n(\cO/\frakp)$. Notons $t$ the isomorphism converse, and posons $q = N\frakp$. One denotes $(x/\frakp)_n$, or simplement $(x/\frakp)$, the character $t(x^{(q-1)/n})$ of $(\cO/\frakp)^*$ with values in $\mu$. More generally, for $\fraka$ a ideal of $\cO$ prime a $x$, of factorisation en prime ideals $\fraka = \prod \frakp_i^{a_i}$, one denotes $(x/\fraka)_n$ or simplement $(x/\fraka)$ the product $\prod (x/\frakp_i)^{a_i}$ (for $x$ prime a $\fraka$). For $\fraka = (a)$, one ecrit also $(x/a)$. The lien with the symbol of Hilbert (\S 2) is the formule
\[
(x/a) = \prod_v (x, a)_v
\]
The loi of reciprocite for the symbol of Hilbert fournit a loi of reciprocite for these symbols.

The symbol $(x/\fraka)$ is a character of $(\cO/\fraka)^*$ (and deja of $(\cO/\prod \frakp_i)^*$) with values in $\mu$. For en deduire a character a values complexes, it is enough to the composer with a isomorphism $\epsilon$ of $\mu$ with the group of the racines $n$-iemes of the unite of $\CC$.

We shall denote $\epsilon$ the character $\exp(2\pi i x)$ of $\QQ/\ZZ$, the character induces on $\QQ_p/\ZZ_p$, ceux which s'en deduisent on the $\cO_{\frakp}/\partial_{\frakp}^{-1}$, via the isomorphism $\QQ_p/\ZZ_p \xrightarrow{\sim} \cO_{\frakp}/\partial_{\frakp}^{-1}$, and ceux which s'en deduisent by of the traces: by example the character $\exp(2\pi i \Tr_{F/\QQ} x)$ of $F/\cO$.

For construct of the characters additifs of the field residuels, one dispose of deux methodes.

\textbf{$1^{\text{ère}}$ methode}: For $\fraka$ a ideal of $\cO$, of field residuel $\cO/\fraka$ of caracteristique $p$, one defines the character additif $\psi_{\fraka}$ as compose of the trace: $\cO/\frakp \to \ZZ/p\ZZ$, and of the character $\epsilon(x/p)$ of $\ZZ/p\ZZ$.

\textbf{$2^{\text{ème}}$ methode}: We ne the expliciterons that in the cas particulier suivant: $n = 3$, and $F$ is the field $\QQ(\sqrt[3]{1})$ of the racines cubiques of the unite. In every the introduction, sauf mention expresse of the contraire we ne considererons more that this cas. Each ideal $\frakp$ prime a $3$ a unique generateur $\alpha$ congru a $1 \pmod 3$ and one prend as character additif of $\cO/\frakp$ the character $\epsilon(\alpha^{-1} x)$.

For $p$ prime, the Gauss sums cubiques obtained en utilisant the characters additifs of the a or the other methode coincident: for $\frakp = (\alpha)$, with $\alpha \equiv 1 \pmod 3$, a calcul easy (cf.\ \eqref{eq:0.1}) shows that the a is the product of the other by $(-1/\varpi)$ si $\frakp$ is of degree 2, and by $(\varpi/\frakp)$ si $\frakp$ is of degree 1. These racines cubiques of the unite are triviales: $(-1/\frakp)$ parce that $(-1)^2 = 1$, and $(\varpi/\frakp)$ for be reelle: one has a the fois $(\varpi/\frakp)^\sigma = (\varpi/\frakp)$ (by transport of structure) and $(\varpi/\frakp)^- = (\varpi/\frakp)$ (reciprocite cubique).

The seconde methode permet of attacher a sum, again dite ``of Gauss'', a ideal $\fraka$ not necessarily prime:

For $\fraka = (\alpha)$, with $\alpha \equiv 1 \pmod 3$, one sets
\begin{equation}\label{eq:0.2}
g(\fraka) = \sum_{x \in (\cO/\fraka)^*} (x/\fraka) \epsilon(\alpha^{-1} x).
\tag{0.2}
\end{equation}

one has:

\begin{equation}\label{eq:0.3}
\text{Si $\fraka$ est sans facteur carré, } g(\fraka) = (N\fraka)^{1/2}. \text{ Sinon, } g(\fraka) = 0.
\tag{0.3}
\end{equation}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Multiplicativite tordue.} Si $\fraka = \fraka' \cdot \fraka''$, with $\fraka'$ and $\fraka''$ prime between eux, of generateurs $\alpha'$ and $\alpha''$ congrus a $1 \pmod 3$, one has
\[
g(\fraka) = (\alpha'/\fraka'')(\alpha''/\fraka') g(\fraka') g(\fraka'').
\]
\end{quote}

The reciprocite cubique assure that $(\alpha'/\fraka'') = (\alpha''/\fraka')$. One can therefore remplacer the factor $(\alpha'/\fraka'')(\alpha''/\fraka')$ by $(\alpha'/\fraka'')^2$.

For $F$ and $n$ quelconques, of after Weil [22], the power $n$-ieme of the Gauss sum $g(\frakp)$, as function of $\frakp$, is a character of Hecke algebraic. One notera that this power $n$-ieme ne depend not of the choix of the character additif. Si one considers this character of Hecke as connu, it s'agit, for calculer the sum $g(\frakp)$ it-same, of determiner quelle racine $n$-ieme of this character of Hecke is $g(\frakp)$.

C'is this that fait Gauss in the cas quadratique, and, for $n$ pair, $n = 2m$, son result ramene the calcul of $g(\frakp)^m$ a celui of a character of Hecke algebraic.

One dispose also of results $p$-adiques (Stickelberger, recemment precise by Gross--Koblitz [4]), and, for $n = 3, 4$ and $F = \QQ(\sqrt[n]{1})$, of a expression en terme of product of values of division of functions elliptiques (Matthews [13]).

In [19], Patterson shows that the argument $g(\fraka)/|g(\fraka)|$ of $g(\fraka)$, for $\fraka$ prime a $3$ and without factor carre, is equidistribue on the cercle unite. It utilise the critere of Weyl, and must therefore prouver that for $T$ integer not zero, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:0.5}
\sum_{N\fraka < x} (g(\fraka)/|g(\fraka)|)^T = o(x).
\tag{0.5}
\end{equation}
The theorem tauberien of Wiener--Ikehara permet of deduire a telle estimation of the holomorphie for $\Re s \geq 1$ of the serie of Dirichlet
\begin{equation}\label{eq:0.6}
\sum (g(\fraka)/|g(\fraka)|)^T N(\fraka)^{-s},
\tag{0.6}
\end{equation}
the etude of these series is the coeur of the probleme.

En principe, the same arguments s'appliquent to the product $g(\fraka) \chi(\fraka)$, for $\chi$ a character of Hecke. The conclusion devient the suivante. Ne considerons that the ideals without factor carre $\fraka = (\alpha)$, with $\alpha \equiv 1 \pmod 3$, such that the argument of $\alpha$ let in a intervalle $I$ of the cercle unite. the argument of $g(\fraka)^3 = -\alpha^2 \cdot \overline{\alpha}$ is then in the intervalle oppose $(-1) \cdot I$, and celui of $g(\fraka)$ in $\{z \mid z^3 \in (-1) \cdot I\}$, i.e.
\ in the reunion of $3$ intervalles disjoints $I_i$ ($i = 1, 2, 3$). One trouve that the argument of $g(\fraka)$ is a fois on trois in chacun of eux: for $N\fraka < X$, the nombre of fois that it is in the a of eux is $\frac{1}{3}(\text{nombre de } \fraka) + o(X)$. The same conclusion vaut si one impose moreover a $\alpha$ a condition of congruence. One can interpreter this result as signifiant that it n'y a not of regle ``simple'', same statistique, for determiner the argument of the Gauss sum.

In [5], Heath-Brown and Patterson prouvent of the results analogues for the Gauss sum cubique $g(p)$ ($p$ prime); this contredit a suggestion of Kummer, imprudemment elevee to the rank of conjecture by Hasse. A role essential is again joue by the serie of Dirichlet \eqref{eq:0.6} for $T = 1$. After a decalage en $s$, it se recrit
\begin{equation}\label{eq:0.7}
\sum_{\fraka} g(\fraka) N(\fraka)^{-s};
\tag{0.7}
\end{equation}
c'is of it that we parlerons.

The serie of Dirichlet \eqref{eq:0.7} n'is not multiplicative. We shall donner a the multiplicativite tordue \eqref{eq:0.4} a ecriture adelique. Notations: see 0.0 and the \S 2. Let $\Af_f$ the ring of the adeles a distance finite of $F$ and $(\ ,\ )$ the symbol of Hilbert cubique on $\Af_f^*$. One defines a central extension $\Af_f^\sim$ of $\Af_f^*$ by $\mu$, equipped with a section $a \mapsto [a] = (a, 1)$, en posant $(a, \zeta')(b, \zeta'') = (ab \cdot (a, b), \zeta' \zeta'')$. one has

\tag{0.8}
\end{equation}
C'is the extension defined by the $2$-cocycle $(a, b)$. The loi of reciprocite for the symbols of Hilbert assure that $[\xi][\eta] = [\xi\eta]$ for $\xi, \eta \in F^*$: $[\ ]$ scinde the extension to the-dessus of $F^*$.

For $\epsilon$ a character of $\mu$, a function $f$ on $F^\sim$ (resp.\ $\Af_f^\sim$) will be appelee a $\epsilon$-\emph{function} si $f(\xi x) = \epsilon(\xi) f(x)$ for $\xi \in \mu$. A $\epsilon$-function is uniquement determinee by sa restriction a $[F^*]$ (resp.\ $[\Af_f^*]$), and one the identifiera souvent a sa restriction: one decrira son support as a part of $F^*$ (resp.\ $\Af_f^*$).

Let, for each $\frakp$, a $\epsilon$-function $f_\frakp$ on $F_\frakp^*$, invariante a line by a under-group open of $F_\frakp^*$. One assumed that, for presque all $\frakp$, $f_\frakp$ is invariante a line by $[\cO_\frakp^*]$, and vaut $1$ on $[\cO_\frakp^*]$. Under these hypotheses, the product of the $f_\frakp$ --- a function on the product restreint of the $F_\frakp^*$ --- provient of a $\epsilon$-function $f$ on $\Af_f^*$, invariante a line by a under-group open. One appellera \emph{multiplicative} a $\epsilon$-function ainsi obtained, and one ecrira $f = \prod f_\frakp$.

\sum_{x \in F^* / \cO^*} f([x]) N(x)^{-s}.
\]
\end{example}
