\noindent
One shows ici comment a theorie suitable of the ``motives'' permet of retrouver certain of the results of Shimura on the periods of the abelian varieties of type C-M \cite{Shimura2}. It a paru instructif of commencer by a description informelle of the properties of the motives and of leurs realisations~: c'is this which is fait to the section~1. The section~2 interprete certain results of Shimura a the aide of motives. The section~3 defines the absolute Hodge cycles, and the theorie of the motives which leur is subordonnee. \section{THE LANGAGE OF THE MOTIVES}

Let $k$ a field of caracteristique $0$. There exists of the ``motives on $k$'' plusieurs definitions, of which one ignore si they are equivalentes, and ayant chacune leurs vertus. We preciserons to the section~3 laquelle we is utile.

The motives on $k$ forment an additive category, equipped with a tensor product. Each projective variety and smooth $X$ on $k$ defines a motive $h(X)$ on $k$, the \emph{cohomology motivic} of $X$, and all motive on $k$ se deduces of a motive of this form (by decoupage en morceaux and twist a the Tate). Enfin, a various theories of cohomology for the algebraic varieties correspondent of the functors ``realisation'' for the motives.

Each abelian variety $A$ on $k$ defines a motive $T(A)$ on $k$, son $H_1$ \emph{motivic}, and the functor $A \mapsto T(A)$ is fully faithful. Plonger the category of the abelian varieties in celle of the motives a for interet of pouvoir prendre of the $\otimes$. But the objects obtained ne are not geometriques~: it n'y a not of ``functions'' on a motive.

The \emph{rank} of a motive is the dimension of the a quelconque of ses realisations, that we shall maintenant decrire.

Si $k = \CC$, the \emph{Betti realization} of a motive $M$ is a group abelien free of rank finite $H_B(M)$, which is equipped with a \emph{Hodge structure}, that is of a decomposition~:
\[
H_B(M) \otimes_\ZZ \CC = \bigoplus_{p+q=n} H^{p,q} \qquad \text{(avec } \overline{H}^{p,q} = H^{q,p}\text{)}.
\]

Illustrons this on the motive $T(A)$, which is the $H_1$ of a abelian variety $A$. One defines $H_B(T(A)) = H_1(A, \ZZ)$, homology of $A = A(\CC)$ vu as space topologique.

Let $\Lie(A)$ the algebra of Lie of the group analytic $A$, and let $J : H_1(A, \ZZ) \to \Lie(A)$ the homomorphisme ``integration on a $1$-cycle''. one has a exact sequence of groups analytiques complexes~:
\begin{equation*}
\begin{CD}
0 @>>> H_1(A, \ZZ) @>{J}>> \Lie(A) @>{\exp}>> A @>>> 0
\end{CD}
\end{equation*}

The Hodge structure of $H_1(A, \CC)$ is such that $H_1(A, \CC) = H^{0,-1} \oplus H^{-1,0}$. One obtains $H^{0,-1}$ as kernel of the map $H_1(A, \CC) \to \Lie(A)$, prolongement $\CC$-lineaire of $J : H_1(A, \ZZ) \to \Lie(A)$.

\bigskip

Placons-we maintenant over a field of caracteristique $0$ and donnons-we a algebraic closure $\bar{k}$ of $k$. For every nombre prime $\ell$, the \emph{realisation $\ell$-adique} of a motive $M$ is a $\ZZ_\ell$-module free of type finite $H_\ell(M)$ (equipped with ailleurs of a action of the Galois group of $\bar{k}$ on $k$). Lorsque $k = \CC$, one dispose of a isomorphism~: $H_\ell(M) \cong H_B(M) \otimes_\ZZ \ZZ_\ell$. Si $A$ is a abelian variety on $k$, si $\bar{k}$ is a algebraic closure of $k$, the realisation $\ell$-adique of $T(A)$ is notee $T_\ell(A)$~; c'is the $\ZZ_\ell$-module~:
\[
T_\ell(A) = \varprojlim_n A_{\ell^n}(\bar{k}) \qquad \text{(où } A_{\ell^n}(\bar{k}) \text{ est le groupe des points d'ordre } \ell^n \text{ de } A(\bar{k})\text{)}.
\]

Si $k = \CC$, the isomorphism $T_\ell(A) \cong H_B(T(A)) \otimes_\ZZ \ZZ_\ell$ is easy a decrire. For every $n$, the projection of $\gamma \otimes x \in H_B(T(A)) \otimes_\ZZ \ZZ_\ell$ on $A_{\ell^n}(\CC)$ se calcule ainsi~; si $m \in H_B(T(A))$ is such that $x - m \in \ell^n (H_B(T(A)) \otimes_\ZZ \ZZ_\ell)$, the exponentielle of the element $\dfrac{m}{\ell^n} \in H_1(A, \QQ)$ is the point of ordre $\ell^n$ voulu.

The \emph{de Rham realization} of $M$ is a space vector on $k$ of dimension finite $H_{DR}(M)$, equipped with a filtration decroissante $F^\bullet$ (the \emph{Hodge filtration}). Lorsque $k = \CC$, one has a isomorphism $H_B(M) \otimes_\QQ \CC \xrightarrow{\sim} H_{DR}(M)$. By this isomorphism, the Hodge filtration is such that $F^p = \bigoplus_{p' \geq p} H^{p',q}$. In the cas of the motive $T(A)$, recall \cite{Weil2} the existence of a ``extension universelle'' $E$ of $A$ by a group vector, which s'inscrit in a exact sequence of algebraic groups~:
\begin{equation*}
\begin{CD}
0 @>>> H^1(A, \mathcal{O}_A)^* @>>> E @>>> A @>>> 0.
\end{CD}
\end{equation*}
one has $H_{DR}(T(A)) = \Lie(E)$, and the Hodge filtration is given by~:
\[
0 = F^1 \subset F^0 = H^1(A, \mathcal{O}_A)^* \subset F^{-1} = \Lie(E).
\]
Si $k = \CC$, one has $H_1(E, \ZZ) \xrightarrow{\sim} H_1(A, \ZZ)$, of or a morphism~:
\[
H_1(A, \CC) \xleftarrow{\sim} H_1(E, \CC) \xrightarrow{J} \Lie(E) = H_{DR}(T(A)).
\]
C'is the isomorphism voulu.

\subsection*{Examples}

\textbf{1)} Ici, $k = \QQ$. One va define the \emph{Tate motive} $\ZZ(1)$~; c'is the $H_1$ of the algebraic group $\Gm$. For trouver ses various realisations, one substitue $\Gm$ a $A$ in the definition of $T(A)$. One obtains~:
\begin{itemize}[label={--}]
\item $H_B(\ZZ(1)) = 2\pi i \cdot \ZZ$, kernel of $\exp : \CC \to \CC^*$
\item $H_B(\ZZ(1)) \otimes_\ZZ \CC = H^{-1,-1}$
\item $H_\ell(\ZZ(1)) = \ZZ_\ell(1) = T_\ell(\Gm) = \varprojlim_n \mu_{\ell^n}(\bar{\QQ})$
\item $H_{DR}(\ZZ(1)) = \QQ$.
\end{itemize}

the isomorphism $H_B(\ZZ(1)) \otimes_\QQ \CC \xrightarrow{\sim} H_{DR}(\ZZ(1)) \otimes_\QQ \CC$ envoie $(2\pi i) \otimes 1$ on $2\pi i$. We recall the isomorphisms $H_1(\Gm) \cong H_1(\PP^1 \setminus \{0, \infty\}) \cong H_2(\PP^1)$. Moreover, for every curve algebraic proper smooth and connected on $\QQ$, $H_2(X)$ is isomorphic a $H_2(\PP^1)$ (therefore a $\ZZ(1)$). For every integer $d$, one defines a motive $\ZZ(d)$ of sorte that $\ZZ(d) = \ZZ(d-1) \otimes_\ZZ \ZZ(1)$. one has $\ZZ(m+n) \cong \ZZ(m) \otimes_\ZZ \ZZ(n)$ for $m, n \in \ZZ$.

\textbf{2)} Let $E$ a curve elliptique on $\QQ$. The motive $T(E)$ is of rank $2$. Therefore the motive $\bigwedge^2 T(E)$ is of rank $1$, and one has $\bigwedge^2 T(E) \cong \bigwedge^2 H_1(E) \cong H_2(E) \cong \ZZ(1)$.

Precisons the isomorphism $\bigwedge^2 T(E) \cong \ZZ(1)$ in the differentes realisations.

\textbf{3)} Let $A$ a abelian variety on $\QQ$. A the cohomology class of a section hyperplane of $A$ in a embedding projective, correspond a morphism of motives $\bigwedge^2 T(A) \to \ZZ(1)$. En cohomology of Betti, this correspond a form alternee $2\pi i \cdot E$ on $H_B(T(A))$, with values in $2\pi i \cdot \ZZ$. En de Rham cohomology, one has similarly a form alternee $E_{DR}$ on $H_{DR}(T(A))$ with values in $\QQ$. It is classique that $F^0$ is a under-space totalement isotrope. One deduces that one can trouver of the differentielles of premiere espece $\omega_1, \ldots, \omega_g$ on $A$, of the differentielles of deuxieme espece $\eta_1, \ldots, \eta_g$ on $A$, such that the one ait~:

\section{MOTIVES LIES TO THE ABELIAN VARIETIES OF TYPE C-M}

Let $k$ a under-field algebriquement clos of $\CC$ --- by example $\bar{\QQ}$ --- and let $\mathcal{A}$ the subcategory of the category of the motives on $k$, generated by the $T(A)$ of the abelian varieties (for the operations of tensor product, passage to the dual and direct summands). The functor ``Betti realization of motive deduces of $M/k$ by extension of scalars a $\CC$'' envoie $\mathcal{A}$ in the category of the Hodge structures. It follows formally of the Theorem~1 of the \S\,3 that this functor is fully faithful.

Let $E$ a field CM, $S$ the set of these embeddings complexes and $- : S \to S$ the conjugaison complex. A each function $\varphi : S \to \ZZ$ verifiant
\begin{equation}\tag{*}
\varphi(\sigma) + \varphi(\bar{\sigma}) \quad \text{est indépendant de } \sigma \in S,
\end{equation}
attachons a Hodge structure $H_\varphi$, equipped with a structure of module on the ring $\mathcal{O}_E$ of the integers of $E$~:
\[
H_\varphi = \mathcal{O}_E \text{, muni de la structure de module évidente, et dans la décomposition } H_\varphi \otimes_\ZZ \CC = E \otimes_\QQ \CC \cong \CC^S \text{, le facteur } \CC \text{ d'indice } \sigma \in S \text{ est de type de Hodge } (\varphi(\sigma), \varphi(\bar{\sigma})).
\]

One checks that the $H_\varphi$ are in the image of $\mathcal{A}$, and same in the image of the subcategory generated by the abelian varieties of type CM. This permet of define the motive $M_\varphi$ on $\bar{\QQ}$ as being celui of Betti realization $H_\varphi$.

The de Rham realization $H_{DR}(M_\varphi)$ is a space vector on $\bar{\QQ}$, equipped with a action of $E$. For every $\sigma \in S$, let $H_{DR}(M_\varphi)_\sigma$ the under-$\bar{\QQ}$-space vector on lequel $x \in E$ agit as $\sigma(x) \in \bar{\QQ}$. Chacun of these vector spaces is of dimension $1$ generated by a element $\omega_\sigma$, and $H_{DR}(M_\varphi)$ en is the sum. Via the isomorphism~: $H_B(M_\varphi) \otimes_\QQ \CC \xrightarrow{\sim} H_{DR}(M_\varphi) \otimes_{\bar{\QQ}} \CC$, a element $e$ of $E = H_B(M_\varphi) \otimes_\ZZ \QQ$ correspond a family $(e_\sigma)$ with $e_\sigma \in H_{DR}(M_\varphi)_\sigma$. One verifies immediatement that the nombre complex $p(\sigma, \varphi)$ such that $p(\sigma, \varphi) \cdot e_\sigma = \omega_\sigma$ ne depend, a multiplication pres by $\bar{\QQ}^*$, ni of $e$ ni of the choix of $\omega_\sigma$. This justifie the notation.

Between the $p(\sigma, \varphi)$ and the \emph{invariants of Shimura} $p_E(\sigma, \varphi)$ (see \cite{Shimura2}), one has the relation~:
\[
p_E(\sigma, \varphi) = \frac{p(\sigma, \varphi)}{(2\pi i)^{\varphi(\sigma)}} \quad (\text{modulo } \bar{\QQ}^*)
\]
(which n'is that a traduction of the definitions).

\section{ABSOLUTE HODGE CYCLES AND MOTIVES}

Let $X$ a projective variety and smooth on $\CC$. A \emph{cycle of Hodge} of codimension $d$ on $X$ is a element of $H^{2d}(X, \QQ)$ of type $(d,d)$, or, this which revient to the same, a element of $H^{2d}(X, \QQ)(d) \overset{\text{def}}{=} H^{2d}(X, \QQ) \otimes_\ZZ \ZZ(d)$ of type $(0,0)$. Remarquons that for $x \in H^{2d}(X, \QQ)(d)$, it is equivalent of dire that $x$ is of type $(0,0)$ or that $x$ is in $F^0$. A cohomology class of a under-algebraic variety of $X$ is a cycle of Hodge. Hodge a conjecture that all cycle of Hodge serait combinaison lineaire of cohomology classes of under-varieties. This conjecture paraissant inaccessible, one introduit a notion which ``arithmetise'' celle of cycle of Hodge.

The cycles that one defines auront all the attributs visibles of the cycles algebraic, namely of the realisations en cohomology $\ell$-adiques and of Rham. A \emph{absolute Hodge cycle} of codimension $d$ on a projective variety smooth $X$, over a field $k$ of caracteristique $0$, will be a collection of cohomology classes in the groups $H^{2d}_{et}(X_{\bar{k}}, \QQ_\ell)(d)$ (resp.~$H^{2d}_{DR}(X)(d)$) satisfaisant the deux conditions
\begin{itemize}
\item the element of $H^{2d}_{DR}(X)(d)$ is in $F^0$~;
\item for every embedding $k \hookrightarrow \CC$, the differentes cohomology classes correspondent (via the isomorphisms of the \S\,1) a class rational of the cohomology of Betti of $X \otimes_k \CC$.
\end{itemize}

one has the inclusions~: $(\text{cycles algébriques}) \subset (\text{cycles de Hodge absolus}) \subset (\text{cycles de Hodge})$. Utilisons this definition for preciser the category of the motives on laquelle one has travaille in the \S\,1 and 2. Let of abord $\mathcal{V}(k)$ the category of the smooth projective varieties on $k$. One constructs a category \emph{additive} $\mathcal{M}(k)$, in laquelle the groups $\Hom(M,N)$ are of the vector spaces on $\QQ$, which is equipped of the data suivantes~:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item a tensor product $\otimes$, associatif, commutative, distributive with respect to the addition of the objects
\item a contravariant functor $H^*$ of $\mathcal{V}(k)$ in $\mathcal{M}(k)$, bijective on objects, compatible to the sums, sending products to tensor products.
\end{enumerate}
In $\mathcal{M}(k)$, one has, for $X$ and $Y$ connected~:
\[
\Hom(H^*(X), H^*(Y)) = Z^{\dim(Y)}_{h.a}(X \times Y),
\]
or one denotes $Z^d_{h.a}(Z)$ the space vector on $\QQ$ of the absolute Hodge cycles of codimension $d$ on $Z$. The construction of the category of motives $\mathcal{M}(k)$ se fait then all as in \cite{Demazure}, the cycles algebraic being remplaces by the absolute Hodge cycles. Grace a this new definition, the composantes of Kunneth of the diagonale of $X \times X$ being of Hodge absolues, one has in $\mathcal{M}(k)$ a decomposition~:
\[
H^*(X) = \bigoplus_i H^i(X).
\]

The result which suit a fait the object of exposes a the I.H.E.S. en 1979.

\begin{theorem}
All cycle of Hodge on a abelian variety is of Hodge absolute.
\end{theorem}

Signalons the

\begin{corollary}[Borovoi]
Let $A$ a abelian variety over a field $k$ of caracteristique $0$, $\xi$ a cycle of Hodge on $A$. The correspondants $\ell$-adiques $\xi_\ell$ of $\xi$ are fixes by a under-group open of $\Gal(\bar{k}|k)$.
\end{corollary}

\section{PERIODS OF THE INTEGRALS OF THE ABELIAN VARIETIES}

Let $A$ a abelian variety complex, $H_1(A, \CC) = H^{0,-1} \oplus H^{-1,0}$ the decomposition of Hodge of $H_B(A)$ (cf.~\S\,1). Considerons $\GL(H_1(A))$ as algebraic group on $\QQ$. one has a morphism of algebraic groups complexes~:
\[
\mu : \Gm \to \GL(H_1(A, \CC)) \text{ tel que } \mu(z) \text{ agisse par l'homothétie de rapport } z \text{ sur } H^{-1,0},
\]
by the identity on $H^{0,-1}$. The \emph{group of Hodge} $G$ of $A$ is the adherence of Zariski of the under-group $\mu(\CC^*)$ of $\GL(H_1(A))$. En of other termes, $G$ is a under-algebraic group on $\QQ$ of $\GL(H_1(A))$. A function regular on a open of Zariski of $G$, and $\QQ$-rational, is zero on $G$ if and only if it is zero on $\mu(\CC^*)$. One verifies aisement that $G$ contient the homotheties of $H_1(A)$. Voici a seconde description of $G$, tres utile~: one considers all the tenseurs rationnels of type $(-\frac{N}{2}, -\frac{N}{2})$ in the tensor products $\bigotimes^N H_1(A)$. Then $G$ is the under-group of $\GL(H_1(A))$ form of the transformations lineaires $g$ for lesquelles there exists a scalaire $\mu$ such that~: $g \cdot t = \mu^m t$, for every tenseur $t$ of type $(m,m)$. Enfin, one shows that $G$ is the group which fixe all the absolute Hodge cycles of type $(0,0)$ in the spaces of tenseurs mixtes on $H_1(A)$ (mixtes = covariants and contravariants).

Supposons maintenant $A$ defined on a under-field $k$ of $\CC$. Posons $g = \dim(A)$.

\begin{theorem}
Let $\omega_1, \ldots, \omega_g$ of the differentielles of premiere espece on $A$, $\eta_1, \ldots, \eta_g$ of the differentielles of deuxieme espece on $A$, such that $(\omega_1, \ldots, \omega_g, \eta_1, \ldots, \eta_g)$ Let base of $H^1_{DR}(A)$. Let $K$ the under-field of $\CC$ generated by $k$ and by the periods $\int_\gamma \omega_i$, $\int_\gamma \eta_i$ $(1 \leq i \leq g$, $\gamma \in H_1(A, \ZZ))$~; the degree of transcendance of $K$ on $k$ is majore by the dimension of $G$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
$K$ is the field of rationalite of the isomorphism $H_{1,B}(A) \otimes_\QQ \CC \xrightarrow{\sim} H_{1,DR}(A) \otimes_k \CC$ given by ``integration'' on a cycle. This isomorphism preserve the cohomology classes of the absolute Hodge cycles. Let $P$ the algebraic variety formee of the isomorphisms which have this property. Of the definition of $G$, and of the theorem~1 resulte that $P$ is a space principal homogene under $G$, defined on $k$, therefore $\dim(P) = \dim(G)$. One conclut by the lemma suivant~:

\begin{lemma}
Let $z$ a point of $\AA^N(\CC)$~; the degree of transcendance of $k(z)$ on $k$ is egal a the dimension of the $k$-adherence of Zariski of $\{z\}$~;
\end{lemma}
\noindent C'is a tautologie. \hfill C.Q.F.D.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Of the example 3 of the \S\,1, resulte that $2\pi i \in K$. One can of ailleurs montrer that the degree of transcendance of $K$ on $k(2\pi i)$ is majore by $\dim(G) - 1$.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}
With the notations of the Theorem~2, supposons moreover $A$ simple of type C-M. The degree of transcendance of $K$ on $k$ is majore by $g + 1$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
It is enough to montrer~: $\dim(G) \leq g + 1$. Or $\mu(\CC^*)$ commute a the action on $H_1(A, \QQ)$ of the field $E$. It follows that $G$ commute also a this action, and that $G$ is a tore algebraic. Moreover $G$ preserve, a factor pres, the form alternee associated a section hyperplane (see the example 3 of the \S\,1). of or the assertion.
\end{proof}
