More precisely, with the notations of 3.2.8, filtrer a complex $K$ by of the under-complexes $F^p K$ revient a se donner of the complexes $K^{n,m}$ ($n \geqslant 0$) with $K^{n,*} = \Gr_F^n(K)[n]$, and of the $d(\alpha)$ for $\alpha$ the injections $\Delta_n \hookrightarrow \Delta_{n+1}$ and leurs composes. The identites $d \circ d = 0$ fournissent of the relations between these $d(\alpha)$. For that $K$ let in $D^b$, it is necessary and sufficient that the $K^{n,*}$ let in $D^b$ and nuls for presque all $n$.

\noindent\textbf{3.2.13.}\quad The condition of 3.2.9 is verifiee in the cas which we interesse parce that the $K^n$ are a cohomology bornee and that the morphisms of transition are of the isomorphisms. It is also verifiee si the $K^n$ are concentres en degrees bornes independants of $n$.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{3.2.14.}\quad The results 3.2.2 and 3.2.4 s'etendent to the categories derivees filtrees. Si $(K, F)$ and $(L, F)$ are of the objects filtres of $D^b(S)$, with $\underline{\Ext}^i(\Gr_F^p K, \Gr_F^q L) = 0$ for $i < 0$ and all $p, q$, then the prefaisceau of the morphisms filtres $U \mapsto \Hom_{DF(U)}((K, F)|U, (L, F)|U)$ is a sheaf.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{3.2.15.}\quad The theorem 3.2.4 admet the variante suivante for the objects filtres: si $(K_U, F)$ is a object filtre of $D^b(S)$ given $C$-locally, with the $\Gr_F^p K_U$ in $D^{[a,b]}$ for of the bornes independantes of $U$ and $p$, and si $\underline{\Ext}^i(\Gr_F^p K_U, \Gr_F^q K_U) = 0$ for $i < 0$ and all $p, q, U$, then there exists a object filtre $(K, F)$ of $D^b(S)$ (unique of after 3.2.14) donnant naissance a $(K_U, F)$.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{3.2.16.}\quad These results s'appliquent in particular to the $t$-structures. Let $\mathcal{D}(U)$ ($U \in S$) of the $t$-categories, formant a champ for the functors $t$-exacts. One assumed given, for each $U$, a $t$-structure on $\mathcal{D}(U)$, compatible to the restrictions. Then, for $K$ in $\mathcal{D}(S)$, the tronques $\tau_{\leqslant 0} K|U$ se recollent en a object $\tau_{\leqslant 0} K$ of $\mathcal{D}(S)$. The functors $\tau_{\leqslant 0}$ and $\tau_{\geqslant 0}$ definissent a $t$-structure on $\mathcal{D}(S)$.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{3.2.17.}\quad \underline{Map.}\quad Let $f : X \to S$ a morphism proper and smooth of schemes, of dimension relative $d$. For each $s \in S$, the fiber $X_s$ is proper and smooth on $k(s)$, of dimension $d$. The categories $D_c^b(X_s, \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ forment a champ on $S$ (for the topologie étale). The $t$-structure of perversite autoduale on each $D_c^b(X_s)$ se recolle en a $t$-structure on $D_c^b(X)$. C'is the $t$-structure of \emph{perversite relative} on $X/S$.
