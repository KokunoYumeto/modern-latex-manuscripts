The present article must beaucoup a.~Grothendieck. It a invente the
philosophie of the motives, which is notre fil directeur. There is some cinq
ans, it m'a also dit, with force, that the complete profini $\pi_1$ of the fundamental group of $X := \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C}) - \{0, 1, \infty\}$, with son action of
$\Gal(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ is a object remarquable, and that it faudrait the etudier.

All covering finite of $X$ can be decrit by of the equations a coefficients
of the nombres algebraic. Appliquant a these coefficients a element of
$\Gal(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$, one obtains the equations of a other covering. Comprendre
comment $\Gal(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ permute the classes of isomorphie of coverings finite
revient essentiellement a comprendre the action of Galois on $\pi_1$. ``Essentiellement'':
car j'ai omis of mentionner the points base, and that the coverings galoisiens n'have not
ete traites as $G$-coverings, for $G$ leur group of automorphismes.

Jusqu'ici, one ne dispose not of a langage for etudier the action of Galois on
$\pi_1$. A.~Grothendieck and ses eleves have developpe a description combinatoire
(``cartes'') of the coverings finite of $X$, basee on the decomposition of
$\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$ en the deux ``triangles spheriques'' $\operatorname{Im}(z) \geq 0$ and
$\operatorname{Im}(z) \leq 0$, of cotes $[\infty, 0]$, $[0,1]$ and $[1,\infty]$.
It n'a not aide a comprendre the action of Galois. One n'a that peu of examples of
coverings not resolubles of which the conjugues galoisiens aient ete calcules.

In the article, we ne considerons that the $\pi_1$ rendu nilpotent, i.e. that the
quotients $\pi_1^{(N)}$ of $\pi_1$ by the under-groups of sa serie central descendante.
The group profini $\pi_1^{(N)}$ is a product on $\ell$ prime of pro-$\ell$-groups
nilpotents: $\pi_1^{(N)} = \prod_\ell \pi_1^{(N)\ell}$. Each $\pi_1^{(N)\ell}$ is a
Lie group $\ell$-adique. It admet a algebra of Lie $\Lie\pi_1^{(N)\ell}$, which is
a algebra of Lie on $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$. Si one has choisi a point base
$x \in X(\mathbb{Q}) = \mathbb{Q} - \{0, 1\}$, $\Gal(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ agit on these algebras of
Lie. the action a automorphismes interieurs pres ne depend not of the choix of $x$.
One aimerait comprendre these actions.

The $\pi_1$ rendu nilpotent is tres proche of the cohomology. This apparait to the
mieux in the theorie of D.~Sullivan ([Su],[Mo]). Notations: for $\Gamma$ a group
of generation finite, let $Z^i\Gamma$ the serie central descendante,
$\Gamma^{(N)} = \Gamma/Z^{N+1}\Gamma$ and $\Gamma^{[N]} = \Gamma^{(N)}/\text{torsion}$ (9.3).
The theorie of Malcev ([Mal]) attached to $\Gamma^{[N]}$ a algebra of Lie nilpotente
on $\mathbb{Q}$, notee $\Lie\Gamma^{[N]}$, such that $\Gamma^{[N]}$ Let under-group of
congruence of the algebraic group unipotent on $\mathbb{Q}$ of algebra of Lie
$\Lie\Gamma^{[N]}$. of after D.~Sullivan, for $X$ a variety differentiable,
$\Lie\pi_1(X)^{[N]} \otimes \mathbb{R}$, a automorphismes interieurs pres, is
determine by the algebra differentielle graduee $\Omega_X^*$, prise a
quasi-isomorphisms pres.

This relation etroite with the cohomology indique that the etude of the $\pi_1$ rendu
nilpotent is loin of the reve ``anabelien'' of A.~Grothendieck. It permet by against
of s'appuyer on sa philosophie of the motives.

Let $k$ a number field. Si $X$ is a algebraic variety on $k$, one dispose
for $X$ of every a serie of theories of cohomology paralleles: the cohomology
classique of $X(\mathbb{C})$ (for each embedding complex of $k$), the cohomology
cristalline (= of Rham for $X$ smooth), the cohomology $\ell$-adique, $\cdots$.
The groups obtained are equipped with various structures additionnelles (of Hodge mixte,
action of Galois, $\cdots$) and relies by of the isomorphisms of comparaison. To the section~1,
we axiomatisons the situation by the definition of the ``systemes of realisations on $k$''.
The definition exact n'is not a prendre to the serieux: selons the maps --- and this that one
is capable of faire --- it can y avoir interet a ajouter or a soustraire tant to the
data that to the axiomes. the essential for we is that
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item The category of the systemes of realisations is equipped with a $\otimes$ with the
properties usuelles: c'is a Tannakian category on $\mathbb{Q}$.
\item Conjecturalement, the category of the motives is a full subcategory of
celle of the systemes of realisations.
\end{enumerate}

The condition (ii) exige in particular that for every variety $X$ on $k$ and all $i$,
the theories of cohomology disponibles, appliquees a $X$, fournissent a systeme of
realisations on $k$ $H^i(X)$ (that one notera $H^i(X)_{\mot}$ and appellera the $H^i$
motivic of $X$).

Of the idees analogues have ete developpees independamment by U.~Jannsen [J].
In [J], U.~Jannsen defines the motives (mixtes) on $k$ as formant the subcategory
tannakienne of celle of the systemes of realisations generated by the $H^i(X)$, for
$X$ quasi-projective smooth. Ici, we resterons imprecis, en disant that a motive on $k$
is a systeme of realisations ``of origine geometric''. For $X$ on $k$ and
$x \in X(k)$, we voulons by example regarder $\Lie\pi_1(X(\mathbb{C}),x)^{[N]}$ as
a realisation of a motive on $k$.

the article must beaucoup a travail not publie of Z.~Wojtkoviak. For
$X = \mathbb{P}^1 - \{0,1,\infty\}$ and $x \in X(\mathbb{C})$, je lui avais propose a
definition of the mixed Hodge structure of $\Lie\pi_1(X(\mathbb{C}),x)^{[N]}$. It the a
calculee en part, for $N$ petit, and a mon extreme surprise, a shows that for $N=4$,
sa description faisait apparaitre $\zeta(3)$. A form decantee of the calculs apparait
to the section~19. En fait, all the article a for origine mon desir of comprendre the
result of Z.~Wojtkoviak. J'ai also ete aide by the reponse of O.~Gabber a ma
question ``Comment construct a extension of $\mathbb{Z}$ by $\mathbb{Z}(3)$,
uniformement en $\ell$ ?'': ``A partir of a class in $K_5(\mathbb{Q})$'', ainsi that by
the conjectures of A.~Beilinson on the values of functions $L$.

For $X$ a algebraic variety over a field of nombres $k$, $x \in X(k)$ and $N$ a
integer, one veut disposer of a systeme of realisations
$\Lie\pi_1(X,x)^{[N]}_{\mot}$. We n'arriverons a the construct that under of the hypotheses
additionnelles on $X$: in the cas general, certain realisations we manquent.
The cas of $\mathbb{P}^1$ less some points --- more generally of the varieties rationnelles
smooth --- is toutefois couvert.

Let $k = \mathbb{Q}$, $X = \mathbb{P}^1 - \{0,1,\infty\}$ and $x \in X(\mathbb{Q})$. The gradue
for the weight filtration of $\Lie\pi_1(X,x)^{[N]}_{\mot}$ is the algebra of Lie free
on $H^1(X)_{\mot}$, modulo son $Z^{N+1}$ (serie central descendante). The $H^1(X)_{\mot}$
is the sum of deux copies of the Tate motive $\mathbb{Q}(1)$. One deduces that
$\Lie\pi_1(X,x)^{[N]}_{\mot}$ is extension iteree of motives of Tate $\mathbb{Q}(n)$. That of the
extensions not triviales apparaissent is this which fait son charme.

Je conjecture that over a field of nombres $k$, the group of the extensions motiviques of
$\mathbb{Q}$ by $\mathbb{Q}(n)$ ($n > 0$) is $K_{2n-1}(k) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$. For a cadre
general in lequel s'insere this conjecture, see the \S5 of [B]. In particular, for
$k = \mathbb{Q}$, one veut avoir $\Ext^1(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Q}(n))$ zero for $n$ pair, of dimension
a for $n$ impair $\geq 3$. It s'agit of the $\Ext^1$ motivic: the extensions en tant that
systeme of realisations which ``proviennent of the geometrie algebraic''. This conjecture

verifiees. This that one connait ne concerne jusqu'ici that the quotient by the second group
derive. A grande part of the article is consacree a elaborer a langage in lequel
the consequences on $\Lie\pi_1(X,x)^{[N]}_{\mot}$ of the conjecture s'enoncent clairement.

\section{Terminologie and notations}\label{sec:0}

\textbf{(0.1)} One denotes $\limind$ and $\limproj$ the limites inductives and projective.

\textbf{(0.2)} For $\ell$ a nombre prime, one denotes $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$ and $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$
the completes of $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Q}$ for the topologie $\ell$-adique:
\[
\mathbb{Z}_\ell = \limproj \mathbb{Z}/\ell^n\mathbb{Z}, \qquad
\mathbb{Q}_\ell = \mathbb{Z}_\ell \otimes \mathbb{Q}.
\]
One denotes $\hat{\mathbb{Z}}$ the complete profini of $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{A}^f$
the ring of the adeles finite:
\[
\hat{\mathbb{Z}} = \prod_\ell \mathbb{Z}_\ell, \qquad
\mathbb{A}^f = \hat{\mathbb{Z}} \otimes \mathbb{Q}.

\begin{proposition}[1.10]\label{prop:1.10}
The systemes of realisations forment a Tannakian category.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
As in \cite{J}, the point cle is that a morphism of $\mathbb{Q}$-mixed Hodge structures is strictement compatible to the filtrations $W$ and $F$ and that ses noyaux and conoyaux
are of the $\mathbb{Q}$-mixed Hodge structures (\cite{D2} 3.3.5). One deduces that all
morphism of systemes of realisations is strictement compatible $W$ and $F$. For $W$,
one can plutot utiliser (AM4).

It is then clear that the noyaux and conoyaux of a morphism forment again a systeme
of realisations and that a morphism bijective is a isomorphism. One dispose of direct sums and the category is therefore abelienne.

One dispose of a tensor product evident, associatif and commutative, and of a passage to the
dual. One dispose also of a functor fiber, same of deux: $B$ and $\DR$, with values in
the $\mathbb{Q}$-vector spaces. The category of the systemes of realisations is therefore
tannakienne and neutre: the functor fiber $B$ (resp. $\DR$) the identifie a the category of representations of the scheme en group $G_B$ (resp. $G_\DR$) of ses automorphismes:
\cite{Sa} or \cite{DM} 2.11.
\end{proof}

One espere that the functors realisations definissent a functor fully faithful
of the category of the motives on $\mathbb{Q}$ in celle of the systemes of realisations. Si
tel n'etait not the cas, the philosophie of the motives perdrait beaucoup of son interet.
This mene a the ``definition'' provisoire suivante:

\subsection*{4.1}

Let $\mathcal{C}$ a category. One can donner of the category of the
$\Ind$-objects of $\mathcal{C}$ the definitions equivalentes suivantes.

\textbf{(4.1.1)} The category of the functors contravariants $\mathcal{C} \to
(\text{Ens})$ which are the petite filtered colimit filtrante of functors representables

\textbf{(4.1.2)} The category of the petits systemes inductifs filtrants of objects of
$\mathcal{C}$, with the $\Hom$ defined by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:4.1.3}
\Hom((X_\alpha), (Y_\beta)) = \limproj_\alpha \limind_\beta \Hom(X_\alpha, Y_\beta).
\end{equation}

It revient to the same of considerer the petits systemes inductifs filtrants as
indexes by a petite category filtrante, or by a petit set ordonne filtrant
(SGA 4 I 8.1.6). One denotes ``$\limind$'' $X_\alpha$ the $\Ind$-object defined by the petit
systeme inductif $(X_\alpha)$. One passe of (4.1.2) a (4.1.1) by the equivalence of
categories
\[
\text{``}\limind\text{''} X_\alpha \longmapsto \limind h_{X_\alpha}.
\]
Si the category $\mathcal{C}$ is abelienne, celle of ses $\Ind$-objects the is also
(SGA4 I 8.9.9c) and the functor canonical fully faithful $\mathcal{C} \to
\Ind\mathcal{C}$ is exact (SGA4 I 8.8.2). The limites inductives filtrantes exist
in $\Ind\mathcal{C}$ and are exact (SGA4 I 8.9.1).

\subsection*{4.2}

The cas which we interessera is celui or $\mathcal{C}$ is a abelian category
of which all the objects are noetheriens.

\begin{lemma}[4.2.1]\label{lem:4.2.1}
Under the hypothese precedente,
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $\mathcal{C}$ is stable by under-quotients in $\Ind\mathcal{C}$.
\item All object $X$ of $\Ind\mathcal{C}$ is the filtered colimit of a systeme inductif
filtrant a morphisms of transition monomorphiques $(X_\alpha)_{\alpha \in A}$ of objects of
$\mathcal{C}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

For $(X_\alpha)_{\alpha \in A}$ and $X$ as en (ii),
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\setcounter{enumi}{2}
\item each $X_\alpha$ is a under-object of $X$
\item $\alpha \mapsto X_\alpha$, of $A$ in the set ordonne filtrant (by (i)) of the
under-objects of $X$ is cofinal.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proof}
Prouvons (ii). Let $X = \text{``}\limind\text{''} X_\alpha$. For each $\alpha$, the
$\Ker(X_\alpha \to X_\beta)$ ($\beta > \alpha$) forment a systeme croissant of
under-objects. Parce that $X_\alpha$ is noetherien, it is stationnaire. Let
$K_\alpha := \Ker(X_\alpha \to X_\beta)$ for $\beta$ sufficiently grand. one has
\[
\text{``}\limind\text{''} X_\alpha \xrightarrow{\sim}
\text{``}\limind\text{''} X_\alpha/K_\alpha
\]
and $(X_\alpha/K_\alpha)$ satisfies (ii).

Prouvons (iii). The morphism $X_\alpha \to X$ is the filtered colimit filtrante of the
monomorphismes $X_\alpha \to X_\beta$ ($\beta > \alpha$), therefore a monomorphisme by
exactitude of the limites in $\Ind\mathcal{C}$.

Prouvons (i). It s'agit of verify that all under-object $Z$ of $X$ in $\mathcal{C}$
is again in $\mathcal{C}$. By (ii), (iii) $Z$ is filtered colimit filtrante of
under-objects $Y_\alpha$ in $\mathcal{C}$. The $Y_\alpha$ are of the under-objects of $X$. Leur
systeme is therefore stationnaire and $Z$ is in $\mathcal{C}$.

Prouvons (iv). For $Z \subset X$ in $\mathcal{C}$, the morphism $Z \to X$ se
factorise by definition of $\Hom(Z,X)$ (\ref{eq:4.1.3}) by a $X_\alpha$.
\end{proof}

Reciproquement, let $\mathcal{V}$ a abelian category and $\mathcal{C}$ a
full subcategory stable by sums finite and under-quotients.

\begin{lemma}[4.2.2]\label{lem:4.2.2}
Under the hypotheses precedentes, si
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item all object of $\mathcal{C}$ is noetherien,
\item the petites limites inductives filtrantes exist in $\mathcal{V}$ and are exact
and
\item all object of $\mathcal{V}$ is petite filtered colimit filtrante of objects of
$\mathcal{C}$,
\end{enumerate}
then
\[
\Ind\mathcal{C} \longrightarrow \mathcal{V}:
\text{``}\limind\text{''} X_\alpha \longmapsto \limind X_\alpha
\]
and
\[
\mathcal{V} \longrightarrow \Ind\mathcal{C}: X \longmapsto \text{le syst\`eme
inductif filtrant de ses sous-objets dans } \mathcal{C}
\]
are of the equivalences inverses the a of the other.

In a langage less choisi: $\mathcal{V}$ is the category of the $\Ind$-objects of
$\mathcal{C}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
As en \ref{lem:4.2.1}(ii),(iii), one deduces of (iii) that each $X$ in
$\mathcal{V}$ is petite filtered colimit filtrante of under-objects $X_\alpha$ in
$\mathcal{C}$. The $X_\alpha$ are cofinaux in all the under-objects of $X$ in
$\mathcal{C}$: si $Z \subset X$, $Z$ in $\mathcal{C}$, passant a the limite in the
\[
0 \longrightarrow Z \cap X_\alpha \longrightarrow X_\alpha \longrightarrow X/Z,
\]
one trouve that the $Z \cap X_\alpha$ have for filtered colimit $Z$. Puisque $Z$ is
noetherien, they forment a systeme stationnaire and $Z$ is contenu in a
$X_\alpha$.

One deduces that $X$ is the filtered colimit of ses under-objects in $\mathcal{C}$:
$\mathcal{V} \to \Ind\mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{V}$ is the identity. Reciproquement, all
$X$ in $\Ind\mathcal{C}$ is a ``$\limind$'' $X_\alpha$ as en
\ref{lem:4.2.1}(ii), the $X_\alpha$ are cofinaux in the under-objects in
$\mathcal{C}$ of $\limind X_\alpha$ and $\Ind\mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{V} \to
\Ind\mathcal{C}$ is the identity.
\end{proof}
