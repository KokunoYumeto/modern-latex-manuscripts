\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent Cher Luc,

\vspace{0.5cm}

Voici a proof global of the existence of a complex of DR donnant lieu a the theorie of Hodge mixte.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent \textbf{Objects consideres}: the cat. derivee of the cat. suivante of complexes bifiltres $(K^{*}, d, W, F)$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2cm]
    \item[$K^{i}$:] sheaf \underline{quasi-coherent}, ainsi that the $\Gr_{j}^{W} K^{i}$ and the $F^{i} K^{i}$
    \item[$d$:] op. diff of the $1^{\text{er}}$ ordre (which respecte $W$ and $F$)
    \item[$\Gr_{j}(d)$:] $\mathcal{O}$-lineaire
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent \textbf{Variantes}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2cm]
    \item[a)] demande only $\Gr_{j}^{W} K$ quasi-coherent
    \item[b)] one s'en tient, in this lettre, a $F$, plutot that a $W$
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent \textbf{Remarks}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2cm]
    \item[a)] one has of the $Rf_{*}$
    \item[b)] for $X_{*} \xrightarrow{\varepsilon} X$, one defines as suit $R\varepsilon_{*}$:
    
    after avoir resolu and pis $\varepsilon_{*}$, one trouve a double complex $K^{i,j}$ on $X$.
    
    ($i =$ indice of $X_{i}$, $j =$ degree), with $W, F$. One prend
    \begin{center}
    \begin{tabular}{ll}
    complex simple associated & \\
    $F$: `` '' `` '' to the $F^{i}$ & \\
    $W$: diagonal between ``$i$'' and $W$: & $W^{\ell} = \bigoplus\limits_{i'+i''=i} W^{i'} K^{i'',*}$
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}
    \item[c)] one has a inverse image by a morphism smooth $f : Y \to X$:
    \[
    f^{*} K \underset{dfn}{=} \Omega_{Y}^{*} \otimes_{\Omega_{X}^{*}}^{\mathbb{L}} K
    \]
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3cm}

One veut construct functorially, for $U \subset X$ \underline{$X$ proper}, a tel object on $X$, of which the $R\Gamma$ ``let'' a complex of Hodge mixte and given lieu a the mixed Hodge structure of $R\Gamma H(U, \mathbb{C})$. In particular the assertions 8.19 in Hodge III are vraies.

\vspace{0.3cm}

Je m'apercois that je demande trop: seul $(K^{*}, d, F)$ can be ainsi canonical. \underline{Pourquoi ?}

\newpage

The construction is datum in Hodge III \,\, \raisebox{0.5ex}{\small 9.3}: one prend a resolution...

$(U_{*}, X_{*})$ as en Hodge III, and, for $D_{*} = X_{*} - U_{*}$,
\[
R\varepsilon_{*}\bigl(\Omega_{X_{*}}^{*}(\log D_{*}), W, F\bigr)
\]

The probleme is celui of the independance of the choix of $U_{*}, X_{*}$.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*)]
    \item $R\Gamma(X, K) \xrightarrow{\quad\sim\quad} R\Gamma(X, K')$ \hfill (c'is $H^{*}(X, \mathbb{C})$)
    \item $R\Gamma(X, \Gr_{F}^{i} K) \xrightarrow{\quad\sim\quad} R\Gamma(X, \Gr_{F}^{i} K')$ \hfill (c'is $\Gr_{F}^{i} H^{*}(X, \mathbb{C})$)

\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}

One emploie maintenant the methode of [4?], finitude. En procedant by recurrence on $\dim X$ and en notant that this which is vrai for $\mathbb{C}$ vaut also on all field of car. $0$. One trouve that
\[
\Gr_{F}^{i}(K) \longrightarrow \Gr_{F}^{i}(K')
\]
