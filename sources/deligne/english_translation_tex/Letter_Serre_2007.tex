\medskip
\noindent Cher Serre,

\smallskip
Ta lettre me given the occasion of essayer of rassembler mes id\'{e}es. Je commence by
\'{e}num\'{e}rer of the obstacles quant \`{a} saisir the relation between forms automorphes and motives.

\bigskip
\noindent \textbf{A.} For avoir a correspondence with a fonctorialit\'{e} raisonnable between repr\'{e}sentations of
$G(F)$ ($F$ local not archim\'{e}dien) and repr\'{e}sentations of the group of Weil ($=$ Deligne) $W'(/F)$,
Langlands is amen\'{e} \`{a} utiliser the correspondence that j'appelle ``unitaire'' in Anvers~II
(forms \ldots, \S3, p.~Del42--45). The fonctorialit\'{e} \'{e}tant ici cruciale, \`{a} mon field d\'{e}fendant, je
m'y rallie. The probl\`{e}me appara\^{i}t d\'{e}j\`{a} for $\GL(2)$.

\smallskip
\textbf{Example:} si $\pi$ is a repr\'{e}sentation admissible irr\'{e}ductible of $\GL(2,F)$, son caract\`{e}re central $\omega_\pi$ is a caract\`{e}re of $F^*$, i.e.~of $W(/F)$ by the field of class. The dual $G^\vee$ of $\GL(2,F)$ is $\GL(2,\mathbb C)$ [car one consid\`{e}re of the repr\'{e}sentations complexes of $\GL(2,F)$] and one veut that $\pi$ corresponde \`{a} a class of conjugaison of repr\'{e}sentations $\sigma \colon W'(/F) \to \GL(2,\mathbb C)$. One veut a relation between $\omega_\pi$ and $\det(\sigma)$, of the type $\omega_\pi = \det(\sigma)\,\norm{\,\cdot\,}^k$. One veut also pouvoir consid\'{e}rer a group $G$ isomorphic \`{a} $\GL(2,F)$, but for lequel one n'a not fix\'{e} quelle \'{e}tait the repr\'{e}sentation of dimension~2 ``\'{e}vidente'': $V$ or $V^\vee$? En of other termes, one veut a compatibilit\'{e} with the automorphism ext\'{e}rieur $\tau$ of $\GL(2,F)$ (which induces itou on the dual $G^\vee$): si $\pi$ correspond \`{a} $\sigma$, $\pi \circ \tau$ (isomorphic \`{a} $\pi^\vee$) must correspondre \`{a} $\tau \circ \sigma$ (isomorphic \`{a} $\sigma^\vee$). Puisque $\omega_\pi$ and $\det(\sigma)$ are ainsi remplac\'{e}s by leur inverse, this n'is possible that si $k=0$.

Si $\chi_1$ and $\chi_2$ are of the caract\`{e}res of $F^*$, the repr\'{e}sentation $\chi_1 \oplus \chi_2$ of $W'(/F)$ must, for $\chi_1\chi_2^{-1} \neq \norm{\,\cdot\,}^{\pm 1}$, correspondre \`{a} a repr\'{e}sentation induite, ne d\'{e}pendant not of the ordre in lesquels $\chi_1$ and $\chi_2$ are pris, and of caract\`{e}re central $\chi_1\chi_2$. These contraintes are v\'{e}rifi\'{e}es by the induction unitaire of the caract\`{e}re $(\chi_1,\chi_2)$ of a tore d\'{e}ploy\'{e}. Unitaire: tordre by $\delta^{1/2}$.

the inconv\'{e}nient, is that, bien that the notion of repr\'{e}sentation admissible irr\'{e}ductible $\pi$ let purement alg\'{e}brique (one can remplacer $\mathbb C$ by $K$ alg\'{e}briquement clos of caract\'{e}ristique $0$ quelconque, $G^\vee$ devenant a group on $K$), the correspondence ne the is not: it utilise a racine carr\'{e}e of $q$ (the nombre of \'{e}l\'{e}ments of the field r\'{e}siduel). A analyste as Langlands croit that $q$ a racine carr\'{e}e meilleure that the other. Moi not. This me force \`{a} affirmer that one n'a not a correspondence between the $\pi$ and the $\sigma$, but deux. For pouvoir en nommer a, it faut avoir choisi a caract\`{e}re of $F^*$ racine carr\'{e}e of $\norm{\,\cdot\,}$ (\`{with values in $K$ quand $\pi$ is $/K$). Je supposerai the racine carr\'{e}e choisie not ramifi\'{e}e, but this n'is without doute not n\'{e}cessaire. In the cas of $\GL(n)$, si one remplace a choix of $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$ by a other, the rapport between the deux is $\varepsilon$ of ordre~$2$, and si $\pi$ correspond \`{a} $\sigma$ for the a, then $\pi \cdot \varepsilon(\det)^{(n-1)/2}$ correspond \`{a} $\sigma$ for the other. Note that the caract\`{e}re central n'a not chang\'{e}.

In the cas global, of m\^{e}me, je ne voudrais a correspondence between motives and certain repr\'{e}sentations automorphes that for $F$ \emph{equipped with a caract\`{e}re of the group of the classes of id\`{e}the racine carr\'{e}e of $\norm{\,\cdot\,}$.

\smallskip
\textbf{Example:} the repr\'{e}sentation trivial of $\GL(2,F)$ correspond \`{a} the repr\'{e}sentation $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2} \oplus \norm{\,\cdot\,}^{-1/2}$ of $W'(/F)$.

\smallskip
\textbf{Example:} a form automorphe classique of weight $2k$ for $\GL(2,\mathbb Z)$ is a function of r\'{e}seau $\Lambda \subset \mathbb C$ v\'{e}rifiant between other $f(\lambda\Lambda) = \lambda^{-2k}f(\Lambda)$. The group is ici $\GL_{\mathbb R}(\mathbb C)$ and the caract\`{e}re central is $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{2k}$ (or $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{-2k}$ selon the conventions). The d\'{e}terminant of the repr\'{e}sentation $\ell$-adique corresponding ``is'' $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{2k-1}$. This shows that the correspondence of which one has the habitude between modular forms and motives n'is not compatible with the automorphism ext\'{e}rieur of $\GL(2)$. Si one has equipped $\mathbb Q$ of the racine carr\'{e}e positive of $\norm{\,\cdot\,}$ on $\mathbb A^*$, je suis contraint of affirmer that c'is $\vol(\mathbb C/\Lambda)^{1/2} f$ that one veut faire correspondre \`{a} a motive. Je ne vois not comment one pourrait esp\'{e}rer rien dire of sens\'{e} for a group unitaire si the correspondence for $\GL(n)$ n'\'{e}tait not compatible to the automorphismes ext\'{e}rieurs.

\smallskip
\textbf{Question of signe:} the $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{\pm 2k}$ is li\'{e} to the cauchemar suivant: si $T$ is a tore, and that one fait agir $T(\mathbb A)$ on $T(\mathbb A)/T(\mathbb Q)$ by translations \`{a} gauche, then on the functions on $T(\mathbb Z)/T(\mathbb Q)$ by transport of structures, one has $[t*f](u) = f(t^{-1}u)$. A caract\`{e}re $\chi$ d\'{e}finit the repr\'{e}sentation automorphe $\mathbb C.\chi$; the action of $T(\mathbb A)$ on celle-ci is by $\chi^{-1}$.

\smallskip
\textbf{Compl\'{e}ment.} For $G$ a group r\'{e}ductif over a field $k$, one has a morphism canonical $G(k) \to H^1(\Gal(/k),\mu_2) = k^*/k^{*2}$, d\'{e}finite as suit. It se factorise by $G^{\mathrm{ad}}$, is compatible to the product, and for $R_{k'/k}(G')$, se d\'{e}duit by $N \colon k'^* \to k^*$ of son analogue for $G'(k')$. This ram\`{e}ne to the cas $G$ absolument simple adjoint. Let $G_{\mathrm{sc}}$ the group simply connected corresponding, and $Z$ son centre. The morphism voulu is d\'{e}duit of $\gamma \colon Z \to \mu_2$, \`{a} d\'{e}finir. The question is g\'{e}om\'{e}trique, and it is enough to d\'{e}finir this $\gamma$ for $G$ d\'{e}ploy\'{e}. One choisit a tore $T_{\mathrm{sc}} \subset G_{\mathrm{sc}}$ and a Borel $B$ the contenant, and one prend by $\gamma$ the restriction of the caract\`{e}re $\delta_B^{1/2} = \prod \omega_i$ of $T_{\mathrm{sc}}$ \`{a} $Z$. The carr\'{e} of this caract\`{e}re se factorise by the image of $T_{\mathrm{sc}}$ in $G$; sa restriction \`{a} $Z$ is therefore \`{with values in $\mu_2$. Changer $B$ change $\delta_B^{1/2}$ by a caract\`{e}re of the tore of $G$, and $\gamma$ is therefore bien d\'{e}finite [other argument: deux $B$ are conjugu\'{e}s, then that $Z$ is central].

For $G$ quelconque, this d\'{e}finit en fait a central extension of $G^{\mathrm{ad}}$ by $\mu_2$, of which one can prendre the pull-back \`{a} $G$:
\[
1 \to \mu_2 \to G_1 \to G \to 1.
\]

For the group $G^\vee$, this correspond \`{a} a \'{e}l\'{e}ment central canonical $c$ of ordre divisant~$2$. For $G$ adjoint (absolument simple for simplifier), $G^\vee$ is simply connected, and the \'{e}l\'{e}ment en question is $\prod\limits_{\alpha>0} \alpha^\vee(-1)$. Si $E$ is a repr\'{e}sentation irr\'{e}ductible autoduale of $G^\vee$, $c$ agit by $\pm 1$ selon that $E$ is orthogonale or symplectique (Bourb. Lie~VIII \S7 prop.12, p.~152). one has en fait a propri\'{e}t\'{e} a peu more pr\'{e}cise that Bourb. loc.~cit, which caract\'{e}rise $c$. Epinglons $G^\vee$ and let $P = \mathbb Z/2 \ltimes G^\vee$ the product semi-direct for the action of $\mathbb Z/2$ which respecte the \'{e}pinglage and agit on the diagram of Dynkin by the involution of opposition. Each repr\'{e}sentation irr\'{e}ductible $E$ of $P$ is auto-duale, $c$ is central in $P$, and $c$ agit on $E$ by $\pm 1$ selon that $E$ is orthogonale or symplectique.

En of other termes, si one regarde the repr\'{e}sentations of $P$ as of the super vector spaces, gr\^{a}this \`{a} the action of $c$, and that one modifie the commutativit\'{e} of the tensor product en cons\'{e}quence, all the repr\'{e}sentations of $P$ admettent a form bilin\'{e}aire (super) sym\'{e}trique invariante.

This s'\'{e}tend \`{a} $G$ r\'{e}ductif quelconque and fournit $c$ central of ordre divisant $2$ in $G^\vee$. These caract\`{e}re/\'{e}l\'{e}ments must, as for $\GL(n)$, contr\^{o}ler the effet of changer of $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$.

\bigskip
\noindent \textbf{B.} Si $f$ is a form automorphe classique of weight $2k$, by example on $\GL(2,\mathbb Z)$, and that $E$ is the under-field of $\mathbb C$ engendr\'{e} by ses coefficients, this which lui correspond is a motive $M$ \`{with coefficients in $E$, or more concr\`{e}tement a syst\`{e}me compatible of repr\'{e}sentations $E_\lambda$-adiques of dimension~$2$. A motive \`{with coefficients in $E$ is a motive $M$ equipped with $E \to \End(M)$, and one has a functor of extension of the coefficients $M \mapsto E' \otimes_E M$ for $E \subset E'$. Passant \`{a} the limite, one d\'{e}finit the cat\'{e}gorie of the motives \`{with coefficients in a extension alg\'{e}brique $\mathcal E$ of $\mathbb Q$ by
\[
(\mathcal M \text{ coeff } \mathcal E) = \varinjlim \ind(\mathcal M \text{ coeff } E \subset \mathcal E).
\]

J'aimerais me limiter, for commencer, \`{a} avoir a correspondence between certain repr\'{e}sentations automorphes and of the objects motiviques, rel.~to the motives \`{with coefficients in the cl\^{o}ture alg\'{e}brique $\bar{\mathbb Q}$ of $\mathbb Q$ in $\mathbb C$. The field $\mathbb C$ appara\^{i}t car one consid\`{e}re of the repr\'{e}sentations automorphes contenues in the space of the functions complexes on $G(\mathbb A)/G(F)$.

In the cas of the field of functions, one can plut\^{o}t consid\'{e}rer of the functions \`{with values in $K$ alg\'{e}briquement clos of caract\'{e}ristique~$0$, and it faudrait consid\'{e}rer the motives \`{with coefficients in the cl\^{o}ture alg\'{e}brique of $\mathbb Q$ in $K$. the histoire devrait \^{e}tre invariante under $\Aut(K)$ (which agit on this cl\^{o}ture alg\'{e}brique ainsi that on $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$).

In the cas of the number field, one pourrait esp\'{e}rer that the repr\'{e}sentations automorphes ayant \`{a} see with the motives are celles auxquelles one can attacher of the nombres of Hodge, but c'is a espoir inaccessible: as one ne comprend not pourquoi exist the forms of Maass with a value proper of the Laplacien other that the bonne, $\lambda_0$, corresponding to the functions $L$ of field quadratique r\'{e}els, comment pourrait-one montrer that for $\Delta = \lambda_0$ they are all motiv\'{e}es?

It ne faut not esp\'{e}rer that every repr\'{e}sentation automorphe motiv\'{e}e, ``\`{with coefficients in $E$'' corresponde \`{a} a object motivic \`{with coefficients in $E$. Si $F'/F$ is Galois of group quaternionien, the repr\'{e}sentation of dim~$2$ of $\Gal(F'/F)$ given a repr\'{e}sentation automorphe ``\`{with coefficients in $\mathbb Q$'', but one n'a not of motive \`{a} coefficient in $\mathbb Q$ corresponding (only \`{a} coefficient in $L$ si $L$ d\'{e}ploie the quaternions). Of m\^{e}me, conjecturalement, for the vari\'{e}t\'{e}s ab\'{e}liennes of dimension~$2$ \`{a} alg\`{e}bre of quaternions of endomorphisms.

\bigskip
\noindent \textbf{C.} Comment comprendre the product semi-direct ${}^LG = \Gal(F'/F) \ltimes G^\vee$ that introduit Langlands? In the cas local not archim\'{e}dien, this which joue the r\^{o}the of $\sigma$ in the explications pr\'{e}c\'{e}dentes is
\[
\varphi \colon W'(/F) \longrightarrow {}^LG
\]
compatible to the projections on $\Gal(F'/F)$ and pris \`{a} $G^\vee$-conjugaison pr\`{e}s. Si je me permets of remplacer $G^\vee$, group complex, by $G^\vee(E_\lambda)$, group on a extension $E_\lambda$ of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, $W'$ by $\Gal$, and $\varphi$ by a homomorphisme continu (this which restaure the part unipotente of $W'$), this that one consid\`{e}re is
\[
H^1(\Gal(/F), G^\vee(E_\lambda)),
\]
in the d\'{e}finition duquel between the action of $\Gal(/F)$ on $G^\vee$. This which correspond \`{a} $\pi$ is a class of isomorphie of $G^\vee(E_\lambda)$-torseurs $\Gal(/F)$-\'{e}quivariants (the \'{e}quivariance touchant also $G^\vee(E_\lambda)$). Quand the choses are dites ainsi, the analogue motivic devient clear.

\smallskip
\textbf{Expliciter:} $\varphi \colon \Gal(/F) \to {}^LG(E_\lambda)$ versus $H^1$: let $P$ the $G^\vee(E_\lambda)$ trivial. Si $\Gamma$ is a group of automorphismes, of $G^\vee(E_\lambda)$, the group of the automorphismes of the syst\`{e}me (torseur $P$ under $G^\vee(E_\lambda)$) induisant on $G^\vee(E_\lambda)$ a \'{e}l\'{e}ment of $\Gamma$ is the product semi-direct $\Gamma \ltimes G(E_\lambda)$: $\gamma$ agit by $(\gamma,\gamma)$, $g \in G(E_\lambda)$ agit by (translation \`{a} gauche $g$, $\Id$). By this isomorphism, $\varphi$ fournit a torseur \'{e}quivariant. Si one change the trivialisation of the torseur, $\varphi$ change by a automorphism $G(E_\lambda)$-int\'{e}rieur.

All this m'am\`{e}ne \`{a} the suggestion suivante. Let $F$ a field global and $G$ r\'{e}ductif on $F$. One assumed donn\'{e}e a racine carr\'{e}e $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$ of the caract\`{e}re $\norm{\,\cdot\,}$ of the group of the classes of id\`{e}the. Si $F$ is a number field, $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$ is \`{with values in $\mathbb R$. Si $F$ is a field of functions and that one d\'{e}finit the forms automorphes as \'{e}tant of the functions \`{with values in $K$ alg\'{e}briquement clos of caract\'{e}ristique~$0$, $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$ is \`{with values in $K$.

One regarde the dual $G^\vee$ of $G$ as \'{e}tant a syst\`{e}me local, on $\Spec(F)_{\text{\'et}}$, of groups r\'{e}ductifs on $\bar{\mathbb Q}$. Concr\`{e}tement, a tel object is the donn\'{e}e, for each extension \'{e}tale sufficiently grande $F'$ of $F$, of a group r\'{e}ductif on $\bar{\mathbb Q}$ \,$G^\vee(F')$, fonctoriel en $F'$, and this of sorte that for $F' \to F''$, the morphism $G^\vee(F') \to G^\vee(F'')$ Let isomorphism.

\textbf{d\'{e}finition of $G^\vee$:} dire that $F'/F$ is sufficiently grand si $F'$ d\'{e}ploie $G$. Partant of $G \otimes_F F'$, one d\'{e}finit son dual $G^\vee$ (d\'{e}ploy\'{e} \'{e}pingl\'{e}) as of habitude. Je n'aurai en fait that \`{a} utiliser the syst\`{e}me local $G^\vee_{\bar{\mathbb Q}}$ of groups r\'{e}ductifs on $\bar{\mathbb Q}$ corresponding, $\bar{\mathbb Q}$ \'{e}tant the cl\^{o}ture alg\'{e}brique of $\mathbb Q$ in $\mathbb C$ (resp.~$K$ for a field of functions). It n'is not n\'{e}cessaire for disposer of $G^\vee(F')$ that $F'$ d\'{e}ploie $F$: it is enough that $\Gal(/F')$ agisse trivialement on the diagram of Dynkin and that $F'$ d\'{e}ploie the centre connected.

This \'{e}tant donn\'{e}, this that must correspondre \`{a} certain repr\'{e}sentations automorphes $\pi$ is a class of isomorphie of $G^\vee_{\bar{\mathbb Q}}$-torseurs motiviques $\sigma$, i.e.~of objects of the type suivant: for $F'/F$ sufficiently grand for that $G^\vee(F')$ let d\'{e}finite, a $\otimes$-functor $\bar{\mathbb Q}$-lin\'{e}aire
\[
\Rep(G^\vee_{\bar{\mathbb Q}}(F')) \longrightarrow (\text{motifs \`{a} coefficients dans } \bar{\mathbb Q} \text{ sur } F'),
\]
fonctoriel en $F'$.

\bigskip

\[
1 \to \mu_2 \to \widetilde{G} \to G \to 1.
\]
Poussons by $\mu_2 \hookrightarrow \mathbb G_m$:

\bigskip
\noindent \textbf{Changer the choix of $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$ (bis).} one has also constructs a \'{e}l\'{e}ment canonical $c$ of ordre divisant $2$ of $G^\vee$. The caract\`{e}re $\varepsilon$ fournit a motive (of Artin) $L_\varepsilon$ of rank~$1$ on $F$, equipped with $L_\varepsilon^{\otimes 2} \xrightarrow{\sim} 1$. the \'{e}l\'{e}ment $c$ permet of tordre $\sigma$ by $L_\varepsilon$: si $\sigma$ associated the motive $\sigma(E)$ \`{a} the repr\'{e}sentation $E$ on laquelle $c$ agit by $(-1)^n$, $\sigma \otimes \varepsilon$ associated \`{a} $E$ the motive $\sigma(E) \otimes L_\varepsilon$. Si, for $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$, $\pi$ correspond \`{a} $\sigma$, $\pi$ must again correspondre \`{a} a torseur motivic quand $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$ is remplac\'{e} by $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}\varepsilon$: it must correspondre \`{a} $\sigma \otimes \varepsilon$.

These deux r\`{e}gles devraient bien s\^{u}r \^{e}tre compatible.

\bigskip
\noindent \textbf{Loose ends:} (i) For $G$ r\'{e}ductif on $F$ d\'{e}ploy\'{e} on $F'/F$, it is can-\^{e}tre more natural of consid\'{e}rer $[G(\mathbb A_{F'})/G(F')]^{\Gal(F'/F)} =: G(\mathbb A/F)$ that $G(\mathbb A)/G(F)$. In the cas of the field of functions, c'is requis si one veut the points of a champ alg\'{e}brique. For the tores, the group of the caract\`{e}res of $T(\mathbb A/F)$ semble more sympatique that celui of $T(\mathbb A)/T(F)$.

(ii) That faire si $\pi$ appara\^{i}t in $L(G(\mathbb A)/G(F))$ with a multiplicit\'{e} $> 1$? Can-one esp\'{e}rer that the composante $\pi$-isotopique let canonically direct sum of repr\'{e}sentations isomorphes \`{a} $\pi$ (and, in the cas of the field of functions, chacun of these sommands contenant of the functions corresponding \`{a} of the sheaves $\ell$-adiques)? And that each sommand given lieu \`{a} a $\sigma$?

(iii) Comment caract\'{e}riser $\sigma$ si sa class of isomorphie n'is not uniquement d\'{e}termin\'{e}e by the classes of isomorphie local correspondantes? Si a telle ind\'{e}termination is responsable of a multiplicit\'{e} $> 1$ as en (ii), comment relier of the $\pi \subset L(G(\mathbb A)/G(F))$ \`{a} of the $\sigma$?

\bigskip
\textbf{Example:} $\GL(2)$. Ici, \`{a} $\pi$ and \`{a} $\norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}$, one can associer $\pi \cdot |\det|^{1/2}$, and this repr\'{e}sentation automorphe associ\'{e}e is the m\^{e}me for $(\pi, \norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2})$ and for $(\pi\varepsilon, \norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2}\varepsilon)$ ($\varepsilon$ of ordre~$2$). This rend natural of attacher a $\sigma$ \`{a} $\pi|\det|^{1/2}$ plut\^{o}t that \`{a} $(\pi, \norm{\,\cdot\,}^{1/2})$. C'is this which is usuel, bien that not compatible \`{a} the dualit\'{e}. One can faire of m\^{e}me for $\GL(2n)$.

Je pensais expliciter of other examples, but they retardent trop this lettre. This will be for the prochaine.
