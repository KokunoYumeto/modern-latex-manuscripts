\CanonHeading{0. INTRODUCTION}
\CanonText{Let \(X_{0}/k\) be an ordinary elliptic curve. By Serre-Tate theory [14, \(V]\), the formal moduli space \(M\) of deformations of \(X_{0}\) over local Artinian W-algebras with residue field \(k\) is isomorphic to the formal multiplicative group \(\widehat{G}_{m}/W\). More precisely, the p-divisible group}
\CanonDisplay{G_{0}\,=\,\cup{}\,\operatorname{Ker}(p^{n}\,:\,X_{0}\,\to{}\,X_{0})}
\CanonText{is isomorphic to \((\mathbb{Q}_{p}/\mathbb{Z}_{p})\) \(\times{}\) \(\mu{}_{p^\infty{}}\), and the choice of an isomorphism \(\alpha{}\) between the étale part of \(G_{0}\) and \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}/\mathbb{Z}_{p}\) canonically identifies, for every local Artinian W-algebra \(R\) with residue field \(k\), the set of liftings of \(X_{0}\) over \(R\) with the group \(\operatorname{Ext}^{1}((\mathbb{Q}_{p}/\mathbb{Z}_{p})_{R},(\mu{}_{p^\infty{}})_{R})\), which in turn is canonically isomorphic to the group of units of \(R\) congruent to 1 modulo the maximal ideal.
