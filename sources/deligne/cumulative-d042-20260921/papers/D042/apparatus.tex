\section*{Scope and source}
The controlling source is the fifteen-page Number49 scan, printed pages
129--143. The original language is French; the English edition is a standalone
translation. The article comprises the introduction, sections 1--3, the
\emph{Appendice / Signes}, three bibliography entries and the terminal receipt
\emph{Oblatum 30-X-1983}. P. Deligne is the sole printed author. Bibliographic
references to other authors are not authorship credits.

The separate collected-paper scan is comparison-only; it never overrides the
controlling scan. All supplied audits and prior-work materials remain preserved
as inherited evidence. No prior-work branch is accepted on its own authority.
The exact archive, source and output identities are recorded in the machine-readable
gate inventories accompanying these editions.

\section*{Page and copy-matter policy}
Each language edition has one output page for each of the fifteen source pages,
in source order. The source folio is supplied in the output footer as a locator.
Running titles, running bylines, journal masthead and publisher copyright are
excluded from the article body. No cover page, missing page, additional author,
continuation or terminal publisher matter is invented. The title, sole byline,
institutional address, section labels, formulas, appendix, bibliography and
receipt are article matter and remain in the readers. Paragraphs continue across
source-page boundaries when the source does. Blank remainder after the receipt
marks the actual end of the article; it is not a missing continuation.

The first source page prints both ``Invent. math. 76, 129--143 (1983)'' and
``\textcopyright\ Springer-Verlag 1984'', with the masthead
\emph{Inventiones mathematicae}. Both year statements are preserved here;
neither is silently reconciled. The address reads ``Institut des Hautes Etudes
Scientifiques 35, route de Chartres, F-91440 Bures-sur-Yvette, France''.
The unaccented \emph{Etudes} and absence of a comma after \emph{Scientifiques}
are retained.

\section*{Source-local readings}
The following notes record potentially surprising source readings and the
sign-sensitive or boundary-sensitive passages. They are not mathematical
emendations or proof certification.

\section*{Independent normalization notes}
All modifications made after receipt of the complete supplied state are recorded
below. Native diagram reconstruction and layout normalization do not authorize
silent changes to printed mathematics.
\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N001 (physical 1).} The generic multi-index is printed as bold Latin $n$, not Greek $\nu$; the vector $x$ is bold. The native expression restores these distinctions in both languages.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N002 (physical 1).} The diagonal display has no index attached to the summation sign. The supplied subscript $n$ is removed and the source\textquotesingle s bold multi-index $\mathbf1$ is restored in the coefficient.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N003 (physical 3).} The defining relation in the Notations paragraph retains the source\textquotesingle s colon-equals sign $X_T:=X\times_ST$.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N004 (physical 9).} The left-facing arrow in the three-object chain of Lemma 2.6 carries a printed isomorphism marker $\sim$. It is restored; the separate trace-compatibility diagram is not given an additional label.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N005 (physical 6).} The Serre-duality prose prints $u:T\to C$, followed by a section of $C_T/T$. The unprinted subscript $T$ in the first codomain is removed, preserving the source-local discrepancy.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N006 (physical 7).} The construction of $\mathscr L_\lambda$ prints the indexed trivial sheaf $\mathscr L'_\lambda=\OO$. The omitted $\lambda$ subscript is restored at this occurrence.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N007 (physical 7).} The English construction restores the explicit forward reference ``see below'', translating the source's ``cf. ci-dessous''.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N008 (physical 8).} In paragraph (b), the linear map is printed with the Greek capital $\Gamma$, not Latin $F$. Both languages restore $\Gamma:V\to W$.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N009 (physical 14).} The source distinguishes bold upright $\mathbf H$ from ordinary $H$. Bold $\mathbf H$ is restored at exactly three degree-zero supported-hypercohomology occurrences: (S.7) and the first two terms of the following comparison chain. The third term $H^0\Gamma_{\{x\}}$ and later $H^1$ terms remain ordinary.\par

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}\noindent\textbf{D042-N010 (physical 15).} The shifted integration morphism and both terms of the displayed $\varepsilon$ map use bold upright $\mathbf H^0$ in the source; these three instances are restored. The following GAGA equality prints two ordinary italic $H^0$ instances. That source-local inconsistency is deliberately retained.\par
