\section*{Editorial policy}
English is the source language. The French text is a separate translation. Repeated journal running heads, folios, publisher copyright and price copy-matter are excluded from reader prose but remain in the untouched authority. Inherited candidates, failed V1 receipts and all web audit claims remain ZERO\textunderscore{}ACCEPTED evidence until the independent corpus gate. This apparatus records substantive readings and explicit exclusions, not repeated production status.\par
\DAppAnchor{1}{227}
The original language is English. The journal citation, title, both-author byline, IHES address, and Ribet permanent-address footnote are retained. The journal logo/imprint, publisher copyright, and price/ISSN line are excluded from the reader as copy-matter; all remain in the unchanged source PDF. The French edition is a translation, not a relabelled English candidate.\par
