Then

|Σ_{x₁,...,x_n ∈ F_q} ψ(Q(x₁,...,x_n))| <= (d-1)^n q^{n/2}.

Replacing Q by a scalar multiple if necessary, we may assume (and shall assume) that

(8.4.1)  ψ(x) = exp(2πi Tr_{F_q/F_p}(x)/p).

Let X₀ be the étale covering of the n-dimensional affine space A₀ over F_q with equation T^p - T = Q, and let σ be the projection from X₀ to A₀:

σ : X₀ -> A₀

X₀ = Spec(F_q[x₁,...,x_n,T]/(T^p-T-Q)).

The covering X₀ is Galois, with Galois group Z/p; i ∈ Z/p = F_p acts by T ↦ T+i.

Let x ∈ A₀(F_q), and let us compute the Frobenius endomorphism of the fiber of X₀/A₀ over x. Set q = p^f, and let F̄_q be an algebraic closure of F_q. For (x,T) ∈ X₀(F̄_q) lying over x, we have F((x,T)) = (x,T^q), and

T^q = T + Σ_{i=1}^f (T^{p^i}-T^{p^{i-1}}) = T + Σ_i Q(x)^{p^{i-1}} = T + Tr_{F_q/F_p}(Q(x)).

Thus Frobenius acts as the element Tr_{F_q/F_p}(Q(x)) of the Galois group.

Let E be the field of the p-th roots of unity and let λ be a finite place of E not dividing p. We shall work in λ-adic cohomology. For j ∈ Z/p, let ℱ_{j,0} be the rank-one E_λ-local system on A₀ defined by X₀ and ψ(-jx) : Z/p -> E* -> E_λ*: there is a map ι : X₀ -> ℱ_{j,0}, and ι(i*x) = ψ(-ji)ι(x). Denote without the subscript 0 the objects obtained from A₀, X₀, ℱ_{j,0} by extension of scalars to F̄_q. The trace formula (1.12.1) for ℱ_{j,0} reads

(8.4.2)  Σ_{x₁,...,x_n ∈ F_q} ψ(Q(x₁,...,x_n)) = Σ_i (-1)^i Tr(F*, H_c^i(A,ℱ₁)).

We have σ_*E_λ = ⊕_j ℱ_j, and hence

(8.4.3)  H_c^*(X,Q_ℓ) ⊗_{Q_ℓ} E_λ = ⊕_j H_c^*(A,ℱ_j).

For j = 0, ℱ_j is the constant sheaf E_λ; this summand corresponds, via pullback, to the inclusion of the cohomology of A into that of X.

Lemma (8.5). - (i) For j ≠ 0, H_c^i(A,ℱ_j) vanishes for i ≠ n; for i = n, this cohomology space has dimension (d-1)^n.

(ii) For j ≠ 0, the cup product

H_c^n(A,ℱ_j) ⊗ H_c^n(A,ℱ_{-j}) -> H_c^{2n}(A,E_λ) ->^{Tr} E_λ(-n)

is a perfect pairing.

(iii) X₀ is an open subvariety of a nonsingular projective variety Z₀.
