Si one sait traiter this cas, one posera

\label{eq:6.1.1}
I_{X/S} P(V_1, \ldots, V_{N+1}) := I_{X/S} P(\det(V_1), \ldots, \det(V_{N+1})) \ .
\end{equation}

For notre theorem principal, one has need of the cas or $N = 1$ and or $X$ is a family of curves smooth parametrees by $S$. In this case, the functor requis a ete constructs in SGA4 (XVIII 1.3). As in SGA4, we the noterons $\langle L, M \rangle$. For $S$ spectre of a field $k$, one can en donner the description direct suivante. One defines $\langle L, M \rangle$ as the space vector of rank a on $k$, generated by of the symbols $\langle \ell, m \rangle$ for $\ell$ and $m$ of the sections rationnelles of $L$ and $M$, a diviseurs disjoints, with the relations
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:6.1.2}
\begin{aligned}
\langle \ell, fm \rangle &= f(\divm(\ell)) \, \langle \ell, m \rangle \\
\langle f\ell, m \rangle &= f(\divm(m)) \, \langle \ell, m \rangle \ .
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
That the construction ne s'effondre not se ramene to the theorem of Weil that, for $f$ and $g$ deux functions rationnelles on $X$ a diviseurs disjoints, one has ([16] III \S 4)
\[
f(\divm(g)) = g(\divm(f)) \ .
\]
The construction of SGA4 is more compliquee, more instructive and reprouve the theorem of Weil.

On a base quelconque, the same construction marche, for autant that one se localise for the topologie étale: the sheaf of the sections local of $\langle L, M \rangle$ is the sheaf of $\calO$-modules generated by of the sections local $\langle \ell, m \rangle$ with the relations \eqref{eq:6.1.2}, with $f(\divm(\ell))$ interprete as a norme: for $D$ a diviseur of Cartier relative, one sets $f(D) := N_{D/S}(f)$. one has $f(D_1 + D_2) = f(D_1) \cdot f(D_2)$. One ecrit $\divm(\ell) = D_1 - D_2$ and one sets $f(\divm(\ell)) = f(D_1) \cdot f(D_2)^{-1}$.

For $L = \calO(D)$, and $1$ the section canonical of $\calO(D)$, one has $\langle 1, fm \rangle = N_{D/S}(f) \cdot \langle 1, m \rangle$. Of the, a isomorphism
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:6.1.3}
\langle \calO(D), M \rangle = N_{D/S} M
\end{equation}
with $\langle 1, m \rangle \mapsto N_{D/S}(m)$. For the definition of $N_{D/S} M$, see 7.1.

\section{$\langle L, M \rangle$ and cohomology}

\noindent\textbf{7.1}\quad Let $f: X \to S$ proper and plat purement of dimension relative a, $D$ a diviseur of Cartier relative and $M$ a sheaf inversible on $X$. One sets
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:7.1.1}
N_{D/S}(M) := \det f_*(\calO_D \otimes M) \otimes \det f_*(\calO_D)^{-1} \ .
\end{equation}
Si $\calO(D)$ is equipped with sa metrique singular canonical (for which $\|1\| = 1$, cf 1.3.4), the isomorphism 6.1.3 is compatible to the metriques. one has
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:7.1.2}
\langle \calO(D), M \rangle = N_{D/S}(M) \ .
\end{equation}
Si $M$ is equipped with a metrique, the metrique corresponding of $N_{D/S}(M)$ is caracterisee by the formule
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:7.1.3}
\log \|\det f_*(\calO_D \otimes M)\| - \log \|\det f_*(\calO_D)\| = \int_{X/S} c_1(M) \wedge \delta_D
\end{equation}
or $\delta_D$ is the courant of integration on $D$. This formule resulte of 6.6.1 and of this that $c_1(\calO(D))$ is the courant $\delta_D$.

More generally, si $E$ is a fiber virtuel on $X$, one sets
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:7.1.4}
\det f_*(E) := \bigotimes_i \det R^i f_*(E)^{(-1)^i} \ .
\end{equation}
C'is a fiber en lines virtuel (a object of the category of Picard defined en 4.2). The datum of compatibility a $+$ evidente on this functor is compatible a the commutativity and a the associativity. For $E$ a fiber vector on $S'$, directement or by (7.1.1), one has a isomorphism
\[
\det f_* E \simeq N_{E/S} \det E
\]
and this isomorphism is compatible a the additivite en $E$ of the deux membres. It se prolonge therefore en a isomorphism fonctoriel en $v$ virtuel:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:7.1.5}
\det f_* v \simeq N_{E/S} \det(v)
\end{equation}
which, for $v$ partout of rank 0 given (7.1.3).

Similarly, (7.1.2) provient of
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:7.1.6}

\section{The theorem}

\noindent\textbf{11.1}\quad Let $X$ a curve projective not singular of genre $g$ on $\bbC$, $\mu$ a densite positive of masse totale 1 on $X$, and $E$ a fiber vector on $X$, equipped with a metrique admissible (6.7). \begin{theorem}

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:11.1.1}

the isomorphism (11.1.1) is a isometrie, a factor $\exp(a(X) \cdot \rg(E))$ pres, or $a(X)$ is a constant ne dependant that of the curve $X$.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\textbf{11.2}\quad For prouver the theorem, one verifies of abord that the deux membres of (11.1.1) se comportent of the same maniere by rapports to the sequences exact courtes $0 \to E' \to E \to E'' \to 0$. one has
