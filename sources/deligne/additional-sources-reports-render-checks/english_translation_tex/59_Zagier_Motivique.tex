\begin{aligned}

&\quad +\sum_{\ell=3}^N A_{\ell-1}(z)(\ad e_0)^{\ell-1}(e_1).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.4.1}
\end{equation}

The crochet of (4.4.1) with $\sum e_0+\sum e_1$ ne depend that of $A_2$. It follows that $A_\ell(z_\alpha)$ se modifie by the addition of a combinaison lineaire of termes of the form suivante:

&\qquad\text{avec }b+c=\ell-2;\\
&A_b(z_\alpha)a(z_\alpha)(\log z_\alpha)^c(\log z_\alpha+a(z_\alpha))^d\\
&\qquad\text{avec }b+1+c+d=\ell.
\end{align*}

the invariance of the membre of gauche of (4.3.5)$_\ell$, $2\leq\ell\leq k$, resulte then of (4.3.1)$_k$ and (4.3.5)$_m$, $2\leq m<\ell$, and (i) en resulte.

\textup{(iii)} $\sum\lambda_\alpha A_k(z_\alpha)=\reg(\varphi_k(\sum\lambda_\alpha\{z_\alpha\}_k^*))$ in $\C/(2\pi i)^k\Q$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On the Tannakian category $\mathcal{T}(S)$, one dispose of the deux functors fibers $\omega$ and $M\mapsto\real(M)_\Q$. Parce that $G=\Gm\ltimes U$, son $H^1$ is zero and, by the theorie generale of the Tannakian categories, there exists a isomorphism of functors fibers $a:\omega\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\real(\ )_\Q$.

One defines as suit a isomorphism between the complexifies of these functors fibers:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:4.6.1}
\begin{aligned}
\real(M)_\C &= \bigoplus_k\real(M)_\C^k = \bigoplus_k\operatorname{Gr}^W_{-2k}\real(M)_\C = \bigoplus_k\omega(M)^k\otimes_\Q\real(\Q(k))_\C\\
&= \omega(M)\otimes_\Q\C.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.6.1}
\end{equation}

Comparant these isomorphisms, one obtains a automorphism of $M\mapsto\omega(M)\otimes\C$, i.e., $g\in G(\C)$, such that $\real(M)$ (of complexifie identifie {a} $\omega(M)\otimes\C$ by 4.6.1) let of space vector complex gradue under-jacent (cf. 1.2) $\omega(M)\otimes\C$, and of reseau rational $g\omega(M)$. This element $g$ is unique {a} $g\mapsto g\gamma$ pres, $\gamma\in G(\Q)$. Prenant $M=\Q(1)$, one sees that $g$ se projette in the quotient $\Gm$ of $G$ on a multiple rational of $2\pi i$. Changeant $g$ by $\gamma\in G(\Q)$, one can supposer that $g$ is of the form
\begin{equation*}

\end{equation*}

The choix of $u$ determine a determination generalisee of $\log z$ ($z\in F^*$): the matrice of $u$ agissant on $\omega(E)$, for $E$ a extension of $\Q(0)$ by $\Q(1)$, is of the form $(\frac{1}{2\pi i}\cdot a)$, with $a$ additif en $E$ and one prend $\log z=a$ for $E=[z]$.
