one has $(k_F \otimes F^J)^* = (k_F^*)^J$, $(k_{0F} \otimes F^J)^* \cong (k_{0F}^*)^J$ and the maps verticales ``norme'' are the maps product. The premiere ligne horizontale is the product of copies indexees by $J$ of the seconde ligne horizontale. The commutativity of 3.16.2 en resulte.

\section{Formule of the traces}

\subsection{}
In [DF], the probleme of comptage 2.3 (i) is resolu lorsque $|S_0| \geq 2$ and that the monodromies local imposees are unipotentes, with a seul bloc of Jordan. In the langage automorphe, that signifie that one demande to the composantes local $\pi_{s_0}$ ($s_0 \in S_0$) of be of the form
\begin{equation}
\text{repr\'esentation sp\'eciale } \otimes \chi \det g,
\tag{4.1.1}
\end{equation}
for $\chi$ a character not ramifie of $K_{s_0}^*$.

the hypothese $|S_0| \geq 2$ permet of passer of the representations automorphes for $GL(r, K)$ to the representations automorphes for the group multiplicatif of a algebra a division $D$ of dimension $r^2$ on $K$. It is rassurant of n'utiliser the formule of the traces that in a cas a quotient compact (modulo the centre), but it devrait be possible of utiliser plutot the formule of the traces for $GL(n)$, for a function test suitable, with the simplification that apporte the fait that one veut detecter of the representations automorphes which en deux places are of the serie discrete. After all, c'is ainsi that one relie $GL(r, K)$ and $D^*$. One simplifiera the explications which suivent en restant with $GL(r, K)$, but en admettant that the seuls termes not nuls in the formule of the traces requise are ceux associes to the classes of conjugaison of elements elliptiques of ordre finite of $GL(r, K)$.

Choisissons a embedding $\F_{q^r} \hookrightarrow M_r(\F_q)$. It defines a morphism $\F_{q^r}^* \hookrightarrow GL(r, \F_q) \hookrightarrow GL(r, K)$, by lequel the set of the orbites of $\Gal(\F_{q^r}/\F_q)$ in $\F_{q^r}^*$ s'envoie bijectivement on the set of the classes of conjugaison a considerer.

Let $T(\gamma)$ the terme of the formule of the traces (donnant the nombre of points fixes of $V$) ainsi associated a $\gamma$ in $\F_{q^r}^*$. It ne depend that of the under-extension $\F_{q^m}$ of $\F_q$ generated by $\gamma$. Posons $T(m) := T(\gamma)$. The nombre $c_m$ of elements of $\F_{q^m}^*$ which engendrent the extension $\F_{q^m}$ of $\F_q$ is
\[
c_m = \sum_{a|m} \mu(a)(q^{m/a} - 1).
\]

The nombre of points fixes of $V$ is
\begin{equation}
N_1 = \sum_{m|r} c_m T(m) = \sum_{m|r} \sum_{ab=m} \mu(a)(q^b - 1) T(ab)
\tag{4.1.2}
\end{equation}
\[
\phantom{N_1} = \sum_{b|ab|r} \sum_{a} \mu(a)(q^b - 1) T(ab).
\]

The nombre of points fixes of $V^n$ ($n \geq 1$) is obtained similarly, after avoir etendu the scalaires of $\F_q$ a $\F_{q^n}$.

\begin{surprise}
Quand one laisse varier $n$, each $T(m)$ n'is en general not, as function of $n$, of the form (1.2.1). By against, in (4.1.2), each terme of the sum on $b$ is of this form.
\end{surprise}

Je n'ai aucune explication, ni geometric, ni automorphe, of 4.2, only a proof. Je n'ai not not more of explication a the

\begin{surprise}
Under the hypotheses of 4.1, the nombre $N_1$ of points fixes of $V$ is divisible by $q$.
\end{surprise}

\subsection{}
Si $r = 2$, the formule of the traces is a peu less effrayante. Drinfeld [Dr] the a utilisee for traiter of the cas or $S_0$ is vide. Flicker (not publie) a traite of the cas or $|S_0| = 1$ and or the local monodromy en each $s \in S$ is unipotente with a seul bloc of Jordan (that is, puisque $r = 2$, not trivial). Rassemblant the informations ainsi obtained, one obtains the

\begin{surprise}
Let $E$ the set of the classes of isomorphie of $\Ql$-sheaves smooth irreductibles of rank $2$ on $X - S$, a local monodromy unipotente (tant not trivial that trivial). The nombre of points fixes of $V := \Frob^*$ on $E$ ne depend that of $X_0/\F_q$ and of $|S_0|$.
\end{surprise}

C'is a this surprise that 2.16 fait allusion. Si, as in [DF], one exige that the local monodromy en each $s \in S$ let unipotente with a seul bloc of Jordan the nombre of points fixes depend of $X_0$, of $S$, and also of the action of Frobenius on $S$ (vue as class of conjugaison in the symmetric group $\mathfrak{S}_{|S_0|}$).

\tag{5.1.1}
\end{equation}
Si $|S_0| \geq 2$, (5.1.1) is a map of the result principal of [DF]. The curieux result suivant ([DF] \S 7) permet of en deduire the cas or $S_0$ is reduit a point closed of degree $4$.

The proof is by reduction a theorem analogue on $\C$.

\subsection{}

\end{equation}

the analogue complex of $\overline{E}$ is the moduli space $\overline{M}_\C$ of the systemes local complexes of rank $2$ on $\mathbf{P}^1 - S$, for $|S| = 4$, a local monodromy unipotente. \subsection{}
Notons $E(1, \emptyset)$ the set $E(R)$ of 2.1 for $r = 1$ and $S_0 = \emptyset$. The tensor product the munit of a structure of group abelien. The group $\Pic(X_0)$ s'envoie on $\Z$ by the map degree, with for kernel the group finite $\Pic^0(X_0)(\F_q)$ of the $\F_q$-points of the jacobienne $\Pic^0(X_0)$. The classes of isomorphie of $\Ql$-sheaves $F_0$ smooth of rank a on $X_0$ s'identifient ainsi to the characters $\chi$ of $\Pic(X_0)$ with values in $\Zl^*$, the $\F_q$-twisting by $\lambda$ correspond to the product by the character $\lambda^n$ of $\Z$, and, attachant a $F_0$ the restriction of $\chi$ a $\Pic^0(X_0)(\F_q)$, one obtains a bijection between classes of $\F_q$-twisting of $F_0$ smooth of rank $1$ on $X_0$ and characters of $\Pic^0(X_0)(\F_q)$.

\subsection{}
Quels that let $r \geq 1$ and $R$ as en 2.1, the tensor product induces a action of the group abelien $E(1, \emptyset)$ on $E(R)$, and of $E(1, \emptyset)^V$ on $E(R)^V$. \begin{equation}
|E(R)^V| = |E(1, \emptyset)^V| = |\Pic^0(X_0)(\F_q)| \quad \text{(si } r = 1 \text{ et (2.10.1))}.
\tag{6.2.1}
\end{equation}

Si $r > 1$, prendre the power exterieure $\bigwedge^r$ fournit a map
\[
\det: E(R) \longrightarrow E(\textstyle\bigwedge^r R)
\]
such that, for the action, notee $\otimes$, of $E(1, \emptyset)$, one ait
\[
\det(\mathcal{L} \otimes F) = \mathcal{L}^r \otimes \det(F).
\]
Similarly, after passage to the points fixes of $V = \Frob^*$, for $\det: E(R)^V \to E(\bigwedge^r R)^V$ and the action of $E(1, \emptyset)^V$.

the action of $E(1, \emptyset)^V$ on $E(R)^V$ n'is not necessarily free, and the fibers of
\[
\det: E(R)^V \longrightarrow E(\textstyle\bigwedge^r R)^V
\]
n'have not necessarily all the same nombre of elements. Neanmoins, in all the cas which have pu be calcules $|E(1, \emptyset)^V|$ divise $|E(R)^V|$. As function of $n$, $|\Pic^0(X_0)(\F_{q^n})|$ is of the form (2.15.1):

Je conjecture the form precisee suivante of the conjecture 2.15 (i)

\begin{conjecture}
As function of $n$, the nombre of points fixes $N_n(R)$ of $V^n$ is of the form

\subsection{}
Voici a analogue of 6.3 en geometrie algebraic. Let $A_0$ a abelian variety on $\F_q$ and $f_0: X_0 \to A_0$ a morphism proper and smooth. Bien that the fibers of $f: X_0(\F_{q^n}) \to A_0(\F_{q^n})$ n'aient not necessarily the same nombre of elements (example: $X_0 = A_0$ and $f = \text{multiplication par un entier premier \`a } p$), $|A_0(\F_{q^n})|$ divise $|X_0(\F_{q^n})|$. Indeed, the $R^i f_! \Q_\ell$ are of the $\Q_\ell$-sheaves smooth on $A$, and the fundamental group (abelien) of $A$ agit on these sheaves. One sait that this action is semisimple. Let $(R^i f_! \Q_\ell)^0$ the under-sheaf of the invariants. It provient of a under-sheaf $(R^i f_{0!} \Q_\ell)^0$ of $R^i f_{0!} \Q_\ell$ on $A_0$, and this dernier is the inverse image on $A_0$ of a sheaf on $\Spec(\F_q)$, that we shall denote $H^i(X_0/A_0)$. One sait that
\begin{equation}
H^i(A, (R^j f_! \Q_\ell)^0) \xrightarrow{\sim} H^i(A, R^j f_! \Q_\ell).
\tag{6.4.1}
\end{equation}
The formule of the traces of Grothendieck for the nombre of $\F_q$-points of $X_0$ can therefore se reecrire
\begin{equation}
|X_0(\F_{q^n})| = \Tr\Bigl(\Frob^*, \sum_i (-1)^i H^i(A, \Q_{\ell'}) \otimes \sum_j (-1)^j H^j(X_0/A_0)\Bigr).
\tag{6.4.2}
\end{equation}
Si the $\gamma_k$ are the values proper of Frobenius on the $H^j(X_0/A_0)$, and the $c_k$ the sum alternee of leurs multiplicites, one deduces of (6.4.2) that
\begin{equation}

\tag{6.4.3}
\end{equation}
The divisibilite promise of $|X_0(\F_{q^n})|$ by $|A_0(\F_{q^n})|$ resulte of a formule analogue a (6.3.1). The values proper of Frobenius on $H^j(X_0/A_0)$, contenu in the cohomology of a fiber of $X_0 \to A_0$, are of the integers algebraic.
