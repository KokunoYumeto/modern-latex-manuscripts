\vspace{0.5cm}

Cher Monsieur Weil,

\vspace{0.3cm}

Voici the determination, in the cas of a field of functions, of the facon of which varie the constant of the functional equation of which we avions parle. Je joins a copie of a lettre of Langlands, and of some calculs which s'y rapportent. Je n'en utiliserai that the annotation (L) ci-more bas.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{1. Representations $\lambda$-adiques, of after Serre and Grothendieck.}

\vspace{0.3cm}

Let $K$ a field of functions of a variable of field of the constants $\mathbb{F}_q$, $X$ the set of ses places, $E$ a number field, $\lambda$ a place of $E$ ne divisant not $q$ and $E_\lambda$ the field local corresponding, of ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$ and of field residuel $k_\lambda = \mathcal{O}_\lambda/\lambda$.

A \emph{representation $\lambda$-adique} of $K$ is a representation continue of $\Gal(\overline{K}/K)$ in a vector of dimension finite on $E_\lambda$, strictement c'is a telle representation such that there exists a part finite $S$ of $X$ en dehors of laquelle $\sigma$ let not ramifie.

Si $v \in X$, one designe by $V_v$ a uniformisante of $K_v$ corresponding, by $F_v = \varphi_v^{-1}$ a Frobenius geometric, by $I_v$ the group of inertie of the Galois group local en $v$, by $\sigma_v$ the representation local defined by $\sigma$.

\vspace{0.5cm}

One sets
\[
Z_v(\sigma, T) = \det(1 - F_v T, \sigma_v^{I_v})^{-1}
\]
(action of Frobenius on the invariants of the inertie)
\[
Z(\sigma, T) = \prod_v Z_v(\sigma, T) \quad \in E_\lambda[[T]]
\]

\noindent\textbf{Example.} A representation $\lambda$-adique of rank $1$ of $K$ n'is other that a homomorphisme of the group of the classes of ideals of $K$ in $\mathcal{O}_\lambda^\times$, continu for the topologie discrete of $\mathcal{O}_\lambda^\times$. One designe by $1(n)$ the representation corresponding a $x \mapsto |x|^{-n}$ $(n \in \mathbb{Z})$ and one sets $\sigma(n) = \sigma \otimes 1(n)$.

A theorem of Grothendieck affirme that si $\sigma$ is a representation $\lambda$-adique of $K$, then

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item $Z(\sigma, T)$ is a fraction rational (in $E_\lambda(T)$)
\item It satisfies a functional equation
\[
Z(\check{\sigma}(1), T) = \varepsilon(\sigma, T) \, Z(\sigma, T)
\]
or $\varepsilon(\sigma, T)$ is a monome. Je poserai
\[
\varepsilon(\sigma, T) = q^{(1-g)\dim \sigma} \, \varepsilon_0(\sigma, T)
\]
$\varepsilon_0(\sigma) = \varepsilon_0(\sigma, 1)$; this quantite is a unite $\lambda$-adique.
\item Si $\sigma$ designe again the sheaf $\lambda$-adique defined by $\sigma$ (direct image of sa restriction to the point generique), one has again
\[
\varepsilon(\sigma, T) = \det(-FT, H^*(\sigma)) = \prod_i \det(-FT, H^i(\sigma))^{(-1)^i}
\]
\end{enumerate}

\newpage

Si $S$ is a part finite of $X$, one sets
\[
Z_S(\sigma, T) = \prod_{v \notin S} Z_v(\sigma, T)
\]
and
\[
\varepsilon_S(\sigma, T) = \varepsilon(\sigma, T) \cdot \prod_{v \in S} \det(-F_v T, \sigma_v^{I_v})^{-1}
\]
\begin{align*}
&= \text{mon\^ome asymptotique \`a } Z_S(\sigma, T) \text{ pour } T \to \infty \\
&= \det(-FT, H^*(\sigma_S)) \quad \text{o\`u } \sigma_S \text{ est le faisceau}
\end{align*}
$\lambda$-adique $\sigma$, restreint a $X - S$ and prolonge by $0$ en the $v \in S$.
\[
\varepsilon_{0,S}(\sigma, T) = q^{(g-1)\dim \sigma} \, \varepsilon_S(\sigma, T)
\]

One defines the conducteurs
\[
m_v(\sigma_S) = @@MATH5@@
\]
or one sets by example
\[
\dim \Hom_{I_v}(\Sw_v, \sigma_v) = \dim \Hom_{I_v}(\Sw_v, \sigma_v \text{ r\'eduit mod } \lambda)
\]
or $\Sw_v$ is the representation of Swan of a quotient sufficiently grand $I_v'$ of $I_v$ (c'is a $\mathcal{O}_\lambda[I_v']$-module projective).

One omet the mention of $S$ lorsque $S = \emptyset$.

\vspace{0.5cm}

Let maintenant $\mathbb{L}$ a set not vide of \emph{prime} $\neq q$ of places of $E$, and $S$ a part finite of $X$. A \emph{systeme compatible} of representations $\lambda$-adiques ($\lambda \in \mathbb{L}$), of set exceptional $S$, is the datum, for each $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}$, of a representation $\lambda$-adique $\sigma_\lambda$ of $K$, the conditions suivantes being verifiees:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item Each $\sigma_\lambda$ is not ramifie en dehors of $S$
\item For $v \notin S$, $Z_v(\sigma_\lambda, T)$ se trouve in $E(T)$ and is independant of $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}$
\end{enumerate}

One sets
\begin{align*}
Z_v(\sigma, T) &= Z_v(\sigma_\lambda, T) \qquad (v \notin S) \\
Z_S(\sigma, T) &= \prod_{v \notin S} Z_v(\sigma, T) \\
\varepsilon_S(\sigma, T) &= \varepsilon_S(\sigma_\lambda, T)
\end{align*}
these quantites ne dependent not of $\lambda$ (indeed, $\varepsilon_S$ can se define en terme of $Z_S$).

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{2. (L)} \quad One admettra the states suivant:

\smallskip

\textit{Let $\sigma$ and $\rho$ deux representations complexes of a quotient finite of $\Gal(\overline{K}/K)$. Supposons leurs conducteurs disjoints. With the notations precedentes, one has then}
\begin{equation}\tag{L}
\varepsilon_0(\sigma \otimes \rho, T) = \varepsilon_0(\sigma, T)^{\dim \rho} \cdot \varepsilon_0(\rho, T)^{\dim \sigma} \cdot \prod_{v \in X} \det \sigma(F_v)^{-m_v(\rho)} \cdot \prod_{v \in X} \det \rho(F_v)^{-m_v(\sigma)}
\end{equation}

\newpage

\noindent\textbf{3. Variation of the constants}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\begin{theorem}
Supposons $\mathbb{L}$ infini, and let $\sigma$ and $\rho$ deux systemes compatible of representations $\lambda$-adiques ($\lambda \in \mathbb{L}$), of sets exceptionnels disjoints. Then, for every $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}$,
\begin{equation}\tag{*}
\varepsilon_0(\sigma_\lambda \otimes \rho_\lambda, T) = \varepsilon_0(\sigma_\lambda, T)^{\dim \rho} \cdot \varepsilon_0(\rho_\lambda, T)^{\dim \sigma} \cdot \prod_{v \in X} \det \sigma_\lambda(F_v)^{-m_v(\rho_\lambda)} \cdot \prod_{v \in X} \det \rho_\lambda(F_v)^{-m_v(\sigma_\lambda)}
\end{equation}
i.e. the formule (L) reste valable.
\end{theorem}

\smallskip

Let $S$ and $T$ of the sets exceptionnels disjoints for $\sigma$ and $\rho$.

\begin{lemma}
For $\lambda$ given, the formule $(*)$ equivaut a
\begin{equation}\tag{*$_{S,T}$}
\varepsilon_{0,S \cup T}(\sigma_\lambda \otimes \rho_\lambda, T) = \varepsilon_{0,S}(\sigma_\lambda, T)^{\dim \rho} \cdot \varepsilon_{0,T}(\rho_\lambda, T)^{\dim \sigma} \cdot \prod_{v \in T} \det \sigma_\lambda(F_v)^{-m_v(\rho_\lambda)} \cdot \prod_{v \in S} \det \rho_\lambda(F_v)^{-m_v(\sigma_\lambda)}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

Si (A) (resp. (B)) is the rapport of the deux membres of $(*)$ (resp. of $(*)_{S,T}$), the quotient $(A)/(B)$ apparait as a product of termes local (for $v \in S \cup T$), of which one va montrer that they are all $1$. Si $v \in S$, it faut verify:
\[
\det(-F_v T, \sigma_v^{I_v} \otimes \rho_v) = \det(-F_v T, \sigma_v^{I_v})^{\dim \rho} \cdot \det \rho(F_v)^{\dim \sigma_v^{I_v}}
\]
this which is evident.

Si $\sigma$ is a representation of $\Gal(\overline{K}/K)$ in a vector of dimension finite on $k_\lambda$, one defines $Z(\sigma, T)$ and $Z_S(\sigma, T)$ in $k_\lambda[[T]]$ by the same formules that precedemment. It is trivial by reduction mod $\lambda$ that, si $\sigma$ is a representation of $\Gal(\overline{K}/K)$ in a $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$-module free of type finite, of conducteur contenu in $S$, and si $\sigma/\lambda$ is the $k_\lambda$-representation which s'en deduces by reduction mod $\lambda$, one has:

\begin{lemma}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $Z_S(\sigma/\lambda, T)$ is a fraction rational; one designe by $q^{(1-g)\dim \sigma} \cdot \varepsilon_0(\sigma/\lambda, T)$ the monome asymptotique a $Z(\sigma/\lambda, T)$ for $T \to \infty$.
\item Si $\sigma$ and $\rho$ are as more haut, a conducteurs contenus in of the parties finite disjointes $S$ and $T$ of $X$, the conditions suivantes are equivalentes:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item $\varepsilon_{0,S \cup T}(\sigma \otimes \rho, T) = \varepsilon_{0,S}(\sigma, T)^{\dim \rho} \cdot \varepsilon_{0,T}(\rho, T)^{\dim \sigma} \cdot \prod_{v \in T} \det \sigma(F_v)^{-m_v(\rho_S)} \cdot \prod_{v \in S} \det \rho(F_v)^{-m_v(\sigma_S)} \pmod{\lambda}$
\item $\varepsilon_{0,S \cup T}(\sigma/\lambda \otimes \rho/\lambda, T) = \varepsilon_{0,S}(\sigma/\lambda, T)^{\dim \rho} \cdot \varepsilon_{0,T}(\rho/\lambda, T)^{\dim \sigma} \cdot \prod_{v \in T} \det \sigma_\lambda(F_v)^{-m_v(\rho_S)} \cdot \prod_{v \in S} \det \rho_\lambda(F_v)^{-m_v(\sigma/\lambda_S)}$ \quad (in $k_\lambda$)
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

One va deduire of (L) that

\begin{corollary}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item Si $\sigma$ is a representation of $\Gal(\overline{K}/K)$ in a $k_\lambda$-vector of dimension finite, the functions $Z_S(\sigma, T) \in k_\lambda[[T]]$ are rationnelles.
\item Si $\sigma$ and $\rho$ are deux telles representations, a conducteurs contenus in of the sets finite disjoints $S$ and $T$, the formule (b) is toujours valable.
\end{enumerate}
\end{corollary}

For prouver (i), one denotes that, quitte a agrandir $E$ and $k_\lambda$, one sait (Brauer) that $\sigma$ se releve virtuellement en a representation of a quotient finite of $\Gal(\overline{K}/K)$ in a vector of dimension finite on $E$; one applique then 2.(i) a this representation.

the assertion (ii) se proves similarly, a partir of (L) and of the lemma 1.

It follows that the assertion 2.(ii) a) is toujours vraie, therefore that $(*)_{S,T}$ is vraie mod $\lambda$.

\newpage

The same technique of reduction to the cas of the series L usuelles, or a other methode, permet of verify that
\[
\deg_T(\varepsilon_0(\sigma_\lambda, T)) = \dim(\sigma)(2 - 2g) - \sum_v m_v(\sigma_\lambda, S)
\]
(formule of Ogg--Shafarevitch--Grothendieck). The prime membre of this formule is independant of $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}$, of sorte that $\sum_v m_v(\sigma_\lambda, S)$ is independant of $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}$. There exists therefore a part infinie $\mathbb{L}_1$ of $\mathbb{L}$ such that the $m_v(\sigma_\lambda, S)$ (and $m_v(\rho_\lambda, S)$) let independants of $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}_1$. The deux membres of the formule $(*)_{S,T}$ are then independants of $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}_1$; puisqu'they are egaux mod $\lambda$ for a infinite of $\lambda$, they are egaux for $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}_1$. For achever the proof, it is enough to verify:

\begin{lemma}
Supposons $\mathbb{L}$ infini, and let $\sigma$ a systeme compatible of representations $\lambda$-adiques, of set exceptional $S$. Then, the integers $m_v(\sigma_\lambda, S)$ are independants of $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}$.
\end{lemma}

One appliquera the results which precedent a $\sigma$ and a character $\chi$ of ordre finite of the group of the classes of ideals of $K$, with values in a finite extension of $E$, of conducteur $T$ disjoint of $S$. One sait deja that there exists of the integers $m_v$ (the $m_v(\sigma_\lambda, S)$ for $\lambda \in \mathbb{L}_1$) such that

$\varepsilon_{0,S \cup T}(\sigma \otimes \chi, T) = \varepsilon_{0,S}(\sigma, T) \cdot \varepsilon_{0,T}(\chi, T)^{\dim \sigma} \cdot \prod_{v \in T} \det \sigma(F_v)^{-m_v(\chi_T)} \cdot \prod_{v \in X} \chi(F_v)^{-m_v}$

and that, of other part

$\varepsilon_{0,S \cup T}(\sigma \otimes \chi, T) \equiv \varepsilon_{0,S}(\sigma, T) \cdot \varepsilon_{0,T}(\chi, T)^{\dim \sigma} \cdot \prod_{v \in T} \det \sigma(F_v)^{-m_v(\chi_T)} \cdot \prod_{v \in X} \chi(F_v)^{-m_v(\sigma_\lambda, S)} \pmod{\lambda}$

of sorte that
\[
\prod_{v \in S} \chi(F_v)^{m_v - m_v(\sigma_\lambda, S)} \equiv 1 \pmod{\lambda}
\]
This, being vrai for every $\chi$, implique that $m_v(\sigma_\lambda, S) = m_v$, this which acheve the proof.
