\section*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Introduction}

In \cite{WeilI}, cite \textbf{I} by the sequence, we have demontre the Weil conjecture donnant the value absolute complex of the values proper of Frobenius agissant on the cohomology of a projective variety and smooth defined over a field finite. We etudions ici the cohomology a value in a sheaf; it s'agit of passer of the properties ponctuelles of the sheaf a celles of sa cohomology.

Let therefore $X_0$ a scheme of type finite on $\mathbb{F}_q$ and $\mathscr{F}_0$ a $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$-sheaf on $X_0$. One assumed choisie a algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb{F}}$ of $\mathbb{F}_q$, and one indique by the suppression of the indice $0$ the extension of the field of base of $\mathbb{F}_q$ a $\overline{\mathbb{F}}$ (cf.~(0.7)). For $x_0 \in |X_0|$ a point closed of $X_0$ and $x \in X(\overline{\mathbb{F}})$ to the-dessus, one dispose of the automorphism of Frobenius $F_{x_0}^*$ of the fiber $\mathscr{F}_x$ of $\mathscr{F}$ en $x$ (\textbf{I}, (1.11) a (1.13), or (1.18)). One appellera ses values proper the \emph{values proper of $F_{x_0}^*$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$}. One dit that $\mathscr{F}_0$ is \emph{ponctuellement pur of weight $n$} si, for every $x_0 \in |X_0|$, the values proper of $F_{x_0}^*$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$ are of the nombres algebraic of which all the conjugues complexes are of value absolute $N(x_0)^{n/2}$. One dit that $\mathscr{F}_0$ is \emph{mixte} s'it is extension iteree of sheaves ponctuellement purs; the weight of ceux-ci are the weight of $\mathscr{F}_0$. Notre result essential is the

\begin{theorem}[3.3.1]
Let $f: X_0 \to S_0$ a morphism of schemes of type finite on $\mathbb{F}_q$, and $\mathscr{F}_0$ a sheaf mixte of weight $\leq n$ on $X$. Then, for each $i$, the sheaf $R^i f_! \mathscr{F}_0$ on $S_0$ is mixte of weight $\leq n+i$.
\end{theorem}

For $S_0 = \Spec(\mathbb{F}_q)$, this theorem dit that, for each value proper $\alpha$ of Frobenius on $H_c^i(X, \mathscr{F})$, there exists a integer $m \leq n+i$ (the weight of $\alpha$) such that the conjugues complexes of $\alpha$ let all of value absolute $q^{m/2}$.

The duality of Poincare permet parfois of completer these majorations by of the minorations (3.3.5). By example, si $X_0$ is proper and smooth, and that the sheaf $\mathscr{F}_0$ is smooth ($=$ constant tordu, in a other terminologie) and ponctuellement pur of weight $n$, then the values proper of Frobenius on $H^i(X, \mathscr{F})$ are all of weight $n+i$ --- this that we exprimerons again en disant that $H^i(X, \mathscr{F})$ is pur of weight $n+i$.

For $\mathscr{F}_0 = \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ (of weight $0$), one retrouve the result principal of \textbf{I} (with the hypothese projective and smooth remplacee by proper and smooth).

A reduction easy, parallele a the proof of the finiteness theorem for the $R^i f_!$ (cf.~SGA~4, XIV, ?) ramene the theorem 1 to the theorem suivant, and a etude local a the infini of the sheaves smooth ponctuellement purs on a curve (C~ci-dessous).

\begin{theorem}[cf.~(3.2.3)]
Let $X_0$ a curve proper and smooth on $\mathbb{F}_q$, $j: U_0 \hookrightarrow X_0$ the inclusion of a open dense, and $\mathscr{F}_0$ a sheaf smooth ponctuellement pur of weight $n$ on $U_0$. Then, $H_c^i(X, j_! \mathscr{F})$ is pur of weight $n+i$.
\end{theorem}

Voici the grandes lignes of the proof.

\medskip
\textbf{A) Nettoyages.} --- (i) Si $u: \widetilde{X}_0 \to X_0$ is a morphism finite surjective of source a curve proper and smooth $\widetilde{X}_0$, and that one designe by $\widetilde{\;}$ the changement of base by $u$, the $H_c^i(X, j_! \mathscr{F})$ are of the direct summands of the $H_c^i(\widetilde{X}, \tilde{j}_! \widetilde{\mathscr{F}})$. This argument permet of se ramener to the cas or the local monodromy of $\mathscr{F}$ en the points of $X \setminus U$ is unipotente.

(ii) A theorem of duality assure that $H_c^i(X, j_! \mathscr{F})$ and $H^{2-i}(X, j_* \mathscr{F}^\vee)$ are en duality parfaite, a value in $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell(-1)$. This ramene a verify that the conjugues complexes $\overline{\alpha}$ of the values proper $\alpha$ of Frobenius on $H_c^i(X, j_! \mathscr{F})$ are of value absolute $|\overline{\alpha}| \leq q^{(i+n)/2}$. The cas difficult is celui of the $H^1$.

\medskip
\textbf{B) Embeddings complexes.} --- Let $0 \neq \alpha \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ a value proper of Frobenius on $H_c^i(X, j_! \mathscr{F})$. It is commode of reformuler as suit the estimations a verify: for each isomorphism $\iota: \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathbb{C}$, one has
\[|\iota\alpha| \leq q^{(i+n)/2}.\]
In the proof, each isomorphism $\iota$ will be traite separement; this conduit a introduire the notions of sheaves ponctuellement $\iota$-pur or $\iota$-mixte (it s'agit of ne not regarder all the conjugues complexes of the values proper $\alpha$, but only $\iota\alpha$). It is moreover commode of permettre to the weight of be of the nombres reels quelconques. See (1.2.6). One prouvera the theorem~2, with pur remplace by $\iota$-pur. Je refer the reader which, as moi, repugne a the axiome of the choix, implicite in the usage of isomorphisms between $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ and $\mathbb{C}$, a (1.2.11).

\medskip
\textbf{C) Local monodromy of the sheaves $\iota$-purs.} --- Posons $S_0 = X_0 \setminus U_0$. One commence by montrer that, si $\mathscr{F}_0$ is smooth and ponctuellement $\iota$-pur of weight $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the weight $w_{\iota(x_0)}(\alpha) = 2\log_{N(x_0)}|\iota\alpha|$ of a value proper $\alpha$ of $F_{x_0}^*$ on $j_* \mathscr{F}_0$, for $x_0 \in S_0$, is of the form $\beta - m$, with $m$ integer $\geq 0$, and one determine $m$ en terme of the local monodromy of $\mathscr{F}_0$ en $x_0$ (1.8.4). More generally, one determine $w_{\iota(x_0)}(\alpha)$ for $\alpha$ value proper of $F_{x_0}^*$ on $\mathscr{F}_0$, to the sens (1.7.4).

Premiere etape: montrer that $w_{\iota(x_0)}(\alpha) \leq \beta + 1$. One the fait en exploitant the formule of Grothendieck for the function $Z(U_0, \mathscr{F}_0, t)$: appliquant $\iota$, one trouve a gauche a product infini convergent for $w_\iota(t) < -\beta - 2$, a line a fraction rational of numerateur $\det(I - Ft, H_c^1(U, \mathscr{F}))$, and one utilise the fait that the $(j_* \mathscr{F})_x$ for $x \in S$ contribuent a $H_c^2(U, \mathscr{F})$. Deuxieme etape: appliquer this result to the powers tensorielles of $\mathscr{F}_0$ and of son dual, en tenant compte of the structure connue of the local monodromy.

\[H_c^i(U \times U, \mathscr{F} \boxtimes \mathscr{F}) = H_c^i(X \times X, j_! \mathscr{F} \boxtimes j_! \mathscr{F})\]
a the aide of a pinceau of sections hyperplanes of $X_0 \times X_0$, assumed en position generale. This for a prolongement projective suitable of $X_0 \times X_0$. One assumed effectue the nettoyage A(i).

The but is of prouver that the weight of a value proper $\alpha$ of Frobenius on $H^2(X \times X, j_! \mathscr{F} \boxtimes j_! \mathscr{F})$ satisfies $w_q(\alpha) \leq 3 = 2+1$. This acquis, si $\alpha$ is maintenant a value proper of Frobenius on $H^1(X, j_! \mathscr{F})$, the formule of K\"unneth assure that $\alpha^2$ is value proper of Frobenius on $H^2(X \times X, j_! \mathscr{F} \boxtimes j_! \mathscr{F})$, and one obtains $w_q(\alpha) \leq 1+1/2$. More generally, supposons the theorem deja satisfies a $\delta$ pres, to the sens suivant: for every curve, and all sheaf (again of weight zero) the values proper of Frobenius on $H^1(X, j_! \mathscr{F})$ are of weight $w_q(\alpha) \leq 1+\delta$. \begin{para}[1.1.1]
Recall the definition of a $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$-constructible sheaf on a scheme (0.1) $X$ (cf.~SGA~5, VI (1.4.2)).

a) Let $A$ a ring noetherien of twisting. A sheaf of $A$-modules $\mathscr{F}$ is dit \emph{constructible} (SGA~4, IX, \S~2) s'there exists a partition finite of $X$ en parties locally fermees $X_i$ such that the restrictions $\mathscr{F}|X_i$ let locally constants, of fibers of the $A$-modules of type finite.

b) Let $R$ a ring local noetherien of caracteristique residuelle $\ell$, and $\mathfrak{m}$ son ideal maximal. One assumed $R$ complet for the topologie $\mathfrak{m}$-adique. The category of the $R$-constructible sheaves is the category 2-projective limit of the categories of sheaves of $R/I$-modules, for $I$ a ideal open of $R$. For the notion generale of projective limit (resp.~inductive) 2-categorique, the reader can consulter SGA~4, VI (6.10) (resp.~(6.3)). We the expliciterons a each usage, for that it puisse se dispenser of the faire. Ici: A $R$-constructible sheaf $\mathscr{F}$ on $X$ is a systeme projective of sheaves of $R$-modules, indexe by the ideals ouverts $I$ of $R$, such that:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item[$\alpha$)] $\mathscr{F}_I$ is annule by $I$, and is constructible en tant that sheaf of $R/I$-modules;
\item[$\beta$)] for $I \supset J$, the morphism of transition of $\mathscr{F}_J$ in $\mathscr{F}_I$ induces a isomorphism of $\mathscr{F}_J \otimes_{R/J} R/I$ with $\mathscr{F}_I$.
\end{itemize}

the map $n \mapsto \mathfrak{m}^n$ of the integers $> 0$ in the set of the ideals ouverts of $R$ is cofinale. This permet, in the definitions precedentes, of remplacer the projective limit on $I$ by a projective limit on $n$, with $R/I$ remplace by $R/\mathfrak{m}^n$.

The functor $\mathscr{F} \mapsto \ll \limproj \gg \mathscr{F}_I$ is fully faithful, of the category of the $R$-constructible sheaves in celle of the pro-sheaves of $R$-modules (pro-objects of the category of the sheaves of $R$-modules), and one appellera again $R$-constructible sheaf all pro-sheaf in the image essential. Justification of this notation: for $(\mathscr{F}_I)$ as before, the systeme projective en $J$ of the $\mathscr{F}_I \otimes_{R/I} R/J$ is essentiellement constant, of value $\mathscr{F}_J$.

One dit that $\mathscr{F}$ is \emph{smooth} (in a other terminologie, \emph{constant tordu}) si the $\mathscr{F} \otimes_R R/I$ are locally constants.

c) Let $E$ a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$ and $R$ the cloture integral of $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$ in $E$. The category of the $E$-constructible sheaves is the quotient of the category of the $R$-constructible sheaves by the subcategory epaisse of the sheaves of twisting. En of other termes:

\item[$\beta$)] one has $\Hom(\mathscr{F} \otimes_R E, \mathscr{G} \otimes_R E) = \Hom(\mathscr{F}, \mathscr{G}) \otimes_R E$.
\end{itemize}

A $E$-constructible sheaf is smooth s'it is locally of the form $\mathscr{F} \otimes_R E$, with $\mathscr{F}$ smooth.

\[\Hom(\mathscr{F} \otimes_E F, \mathscr{G} \otimes_E F) = \Hom(\mathscr{F}, \mathscr{G}) \otimes_E F.\]
For a extension iteree, one has a isomorphism canonical:
\begin{equation}\tag{1}
(\mathscr{F} \otimes_E F) \otimes_F G \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathscr{F} \otimes_E G.
\end{equation}

The category of the $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$-constructible sheaves is the category $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$-lineaire 2-filtered colimit of the categories of the $E$-constructible sheaves, for $E \subset \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ a finite extension moreover en more grande of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$. \[\Hom(\mathscr{F}, \mathscr{G}) \otimes_E \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell \xrightarrow{\sim} \Hom(\mathscr{F} \otimes_E \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell, \mathscr{G} \otimes_E \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell).\]
\item[$\beta$)] one has of the isomorphisms canonical $(\mathscr{F} \otimes_E F) \otimes_F \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathscr{F} \otimes_E \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$, compatible a (1).
\item[$\gamma$)] All $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$-constructible sheaf is of the form $\mathscr{F} \otimes_E \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ for $E$ and $\mathscr{F}$ convenables.
\end{itemize}

A $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$-constructible sheaf is smooth s'it is locally of the form $\mathscr{F} \otimes_E \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$, with $\mathscr{F}$ smooth.

\begin{definition}[1.2.1]

\begin{proposition}[1.6.1]
Let $N$ a endomorphism nilpotent of a object $V$ of a abelian category. There exists then a and a seule filtration finite (0.10) $M$ of $V$, croissante, such that $NM^i \subset M^{i-2}$ and that $N^k$ induise a isomorphism $\Gr_k^M V \xrightarrow{\sim} \Gr_{-k}^M V$.
\end{proposition}

Prouvons the existence (the unicite se proves similarly). One procede by recurrence on a integer $d$ such that $N^{d+1} = 0$. For $d = 0$ ($N = 0$), one prend a filtration $M$ trivial ($\Gr_i^M V = 0$ for $i \neq 0$).

Ensuite, one prend $M^d = V$, $M^{d-1} = \Ker N^d$, $M^{-d} = \Imm N^d$ and $M^{-d-1} = 0$, of sorte that $\Gr_d^M V$ and $\Gr_{-d}^M V$ let the co-images and images of $N^d$. On $\Ker N^d / \Imm N^d$, the power $(d-1)$-ieme of $N$ induces $0$. This permet of define $M$ on $\Ker N^d / \Imm N^d$ by recurrence, and one defines $M^i$ on $V$ ($d-1 > i > -d$) as the inverse image in $\Ker N^d$ of the under-object $M^i$ for $\Ker N^d / \Imm N^d$.

\begin{remark}[1.6.2]
The caracterisation (1.6.1) of $M$ shows sa compatibility to the passage a the category duale.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[1.6.3]
The \emph{part primitive} $P_i(V)$ (or simplement $P_i$) of $\Gr_i^M V$ is the kernel of the morphism induces by $N$ of $\Gr_i^M V$ in $\Gr_{i-2}^M V$.
\end{definition}

\begin{para}[1.6.4]
Si $i > 0$, $N: \Gr_i^M V \to \Gr_{i-2}^M V$ is injective, puisque $N^{i-1} \circ N$ is a isomorphism, and $P_i = 0$. Si $i > 0$, and si the one ecrit that $N \circ N^{i+1}: \Gr_{i+2}^M V \to \Gr_{-i}^M V \twoheadrightarrow \Gr_{-i-2}^M V$ is a isomorphism, one trouve that $\Gr_{-i}^M V$ is direct sum of $P_{-i}$ and of the image of $\Gr_{i+2}^M V$ by $N^{i+1}$; the morphism $N$ induces a isomorphism of this image with $\Gr_{-i-2}^M V$.

Rep'being this construction, one constructs by recurrence decroissante on $i > 0$ a isomorphism of $\Gr_{-i}^M V$ with the sum of the $P_{-j}$ for $j > i$ and $j$ congru a $i$ mod $2$. Utilisant moreover the isomorphisms $N^i: \Gr_i^M V \xrightarrow{\sim} \Gr_{-i}^M V$, one obtains of the isomorphisms:
\begin{equation}\tag{1.6.4.1}
\Gr_i^M V \simeq \bigoplus_{\substack{j > |i| \\ j \equiv i (2)}} P_{-j}.
\end{equation}
\end{para}

Via these isomorphisms, the morphism $N$ of $\Gr_i^M V$ in $\Gr_{i-2}^M V$ is the inclusion evidente for $i > 1$ ($i-2 \geq -1$), the projection evidente for $i - 2 \leq -1$.

\begin{lemma}[1.6.5]
The morphism $N: (V, M) \to (V, M \text{ decal\'e de } 2)$ is strictement compatible to the filtrations.
\end{lemma}

It faut prouver that $NM^{i+2} \supset \Imm(N) \cap M^i$. Distinguons deux cas. Si $i < 0$, the morphism $N: M^{i+2} \to M^i$ a gradue surjective, therefore is surjective and $NM^{i+2} = M^i$, of or a fortiori the assertion. Si $i + 2 > 0$, the morphism $N: V/M^{i+2} \to V/M^i$ a gradue injective, therefore is injective, and $N^{-1} M^i \subset M^{i+2}$, of or a fortiori the assertion.

This permet of echanger the constructions $\Ker N$ and $\Gr^M$:

\begin{corollary}[1.6.6]
the inclusion of $\Ker N$ in $V$ induces of the isomorphisms:
\[\Gr_i^M(\Ker N) \xrightarrow{\sim} P_i.\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{para}[1.6.7]

\begin{proposition}[1.11.1]
Let $S$ a scheme irreducible, $f: X \to S$ a morphism smooth purement of dimension relative $1$, a fibers geometriquement connected, $g$ a section of $f$ and $\mathscr{F}$ a $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-sheaf smooth on $X$. Then, there exists a open $U$ of $S$ such that, for every $n$, and all point geometric $\overline{s}$ of $U$, $M_{\overline{s}}$ let the fiber en $\overline{s}$ of a under-sheaf smooth $\mathscr{M}^n$ of $\Aut(g^* \mathscr{F}/\mathfrak{m}^n)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{para}[1.11.4 --- Proof of 1.3.1]
