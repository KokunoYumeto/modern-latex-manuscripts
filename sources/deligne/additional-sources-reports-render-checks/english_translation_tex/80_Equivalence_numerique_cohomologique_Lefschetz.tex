\begin{abstract}\noindent
We determine the group of automorphisms generated, in the endomorphisms of the cohomology of an Abelian variety one has finite field, by the Lefschetz operators and the complex multiplications.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Algebraic geometry (Abelian varieties, étale cohomology); 14F20, 14K22

\section*{1.}

Let $k$ a algebraic closure of the field $\mathbf{F}_p$ a $p$ elements. One designera by $\ell$ a nombre prime different of $p$. One fixe, on $k$, a isomorphism $\mathbf{Q}_\ell \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathbf{Q}_\ell(1)$.

Let $A$ a abelian variety on $k$. One denotes $Z$, or $Z(A)$, the group of the cycles a coefficients rationnels on $A$, gradue by the codimension:
\[
Z = \bigoplus_i Z^i .
\]
One dispose on $Z^i$ of the relations of numerical equivalence and of cohomological equivalence, for the étale cohomology with coefficients in $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$. Deux cycles cohomologiquement equivalents are numeriquement equivalents.

Let $A_j$ the factors simples of $A$, $E_j$ the centre of $\End(A_j) \otimes \mathbf{Q}$ and $F$ the compose, in a algebraic closure of $\mathbf{Q}$, of the clotures galoisiennes of the $E_j$; $F$ is therefore a field totalement reel or a field CM. Si $\ell$ is a nombre prime, let $D_\ell \subset \Gal(F/\mathbf{Q})$ a group of decomposition en $\ell$; let $c \in \Gal(F/\mathbf{Q})$ the conjugaison complex.

\begin{theorem}
Si $c \in D_\ell$, the numerical equivalence and the cohomological equivalence, for the étale cohomology $\ell$--adique, co\"incident for $A$.
\end{theorem}

A theorem less precise, or the condition on $\ell$ dependait, for a variety simple, of the choix of a field maximal of multiplication complex, etait demontre in [2].

The remarks suivantes are evidentes:

(a) $A$ being datum, the condition of the Theorem 1 is verifiee, of after the theorem of \v{C}ebotarev, for a set $\mathcal{L}$ of nombres prime $\ell$ of densite (analytic or na\"ive) not zero.

(b) Si the condition of the Theorem 1 is verifiee for $A$, it the is also for the powers $A^n$ of $A$. There exists therefore a set $\mathcal{L}$, of densite $> 0$, such that the conclusion let vraie for every power $A^n$. This had ete remark by Milne [5].

Let $H = \bigoplus_{i=0}^{2g} H^i(A, \mathbf{Q}_\ell) = \bigoplus_{i=0}^{2g} H^i$, or $g = \dim(A)$. Let $D = \End(A) \otimes \mathbf{Q}$.

The group $D^\times$ opere on $H$, ainsi that the group, isomorphic a $\SL(2, \mathbf{Q})$, given by the Lefschetz theory. All deux preservent the cohomology classes algebraic. Let $G \subset \GL(H)$ the adherence of Zariski of the group generated by $D^\times$ and $\SL(2)$. The point--cle of notre proof of the Theorem 1 is a description of $G$, or plutot of the group $G_{\mathbf{Q}_\ell}$ on $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$ which s'en deduces by extension of scalars. We montrerons to the \S\,4 comment this description se ramene to the deux cas suivants.

Let of abord $E$ a curve elliptique supersinguliere and $A = E^n$. The group $\End(A)^\times$ agissant on $H \otimes \mathbf{Q}_\ell = \Lambda^\bullet(H^1 \otimes \mathbf{Q}_\ell)$ a for adherence of Zariski the group $\GL(H^1 \otimes \mathbf{Q}_\ell)$. Let $V = H^1 \otimes \mathbf{Q}_\ell$ and $V^*$ son dual.

\begin{theorem}
The group $G_{\mathbf{Q}_\ell}$ is isomorphic to the group $\CSpin(V \oplus V^*)$ of the similitudes spinorielles, $V \oplus V^*$ being equipped with the form quadratique natural.

It opere on $H \otimes \mathbf{Q}_\ell = \bigoplus_i H^i(A, \mathbf{Q}_\ell)$ by the sum of ses deux representations demi--spinorielles. In particular, the representation on the classes of degree pair (resp.\ impair) is irreducible.
\end{theorem}

\`A the oppose, let $A$ a variety irreducible such that $\End(A) \otimes \mathbf{Q} = D$ Let algebra a division, of degree reduit $n$, over a field quadratique imaginaire $E$; $A$ is of dimension $n$.

Let $c \in \Gal(E/\mathbf{Q})$ the conjugaison complex, and choisissons a embedding $j$ of $E$ in $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$. The structure of $E$--module of $H^1(A, \mathbf{Q}_\ell)$ fournit a decomposition $H^1 = H^{10} \oplus H^{100}$, or $\lambda \in E$ agit on $H^{10}$ (resp.\ $H^{100}$) by multiplication by $j(\lambda)$ (resp.\ $j(c\lambda)$). Let $V = V_1 \oplus V_2$ the decomposition duale of $H^1(A, \mathbf{Q}_\ell)$. the adherence of Zariski of $D^\times$ devient, on $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$, the group $\GL(V_1) \times \GL(V_2)$.

Munissons
\[
H^\bullet(A, \mathbf{Q}_\ell) = \bigoplus_{p,q} \Lambda^p V_1 \otimes \Lambda^q V_2
\]
of the graduation given by $k := p - q$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:grad}
\mathrm{gr}^k = \bigoplus_{p-q=k} \Lambda^p V_1 \otimes \Lambda^q V_2 .
\end{equation}
The part of degree $(-n)$ is therefore $\Lambda^0 V_1 \otimes \Lambda^n V_2$, of dimension $1$, and celle of degree $(-n+1)$ is $V_1 \otimes \Lambda^n V_2 \oplus \Lambda^0 V_1 \otimes \Lambda^{n-1} V_2$, of dimension $2n$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:3}
\emph{(i)} For a isomorphism of $\bigoplus_k \Lambda^p V_1 \otimes \Lambda^q V_2$ with $\Lambda^\bullet(\mathrm{gr}^{-n+1})$, sending $\mathrm{gr}^k$ en $\Lambda^{\frac{n+k}{2}} \mathrm{gr}^{-n+1}$, $G_{\mathbf{Q}_\ell}$ s'identifie to the group generated by $\GL(\mathrm{gr}^{-n+1})$ agissant on $\Lambda^\bullet \mathrm{gr}^{-n+1}$, and by the homotheties $x \mapsto \lambda x$.

\emph{(ii)} The $\mathrm{gr}^k$ ($-n \leq k \leq n$) are therefore of the representations irreductibles not isomorphes of $G$. The under--spaces $\mathrm{gr}^k$ and $\mathrm{gr}^\ell$ are orthogonaux for the cup--product si $k + \ell \neq 0$, and en duality parfaite si $k + \ell = 0$.
\end{theorem}

Montrons comment reduire the Theorem 1 (and same a variante a more more forte, cf.\ \S\,5) a the analyse of the group $G$.

(a) Let $Z_0$ the $\mathbf{Q}$--under--space vector of $H^\bullet(A, \mathbf{Q}_\ell)$ image of $Z$, and $Z_1$ the $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$--under--space vector generated by $Z_0$. Puisque $G$ contient the group $\mathbf{G}_m$ corresponding a the graduation of $H^\bullet(A)$ (action of $(\mathbf{Q} \cdot 1)^\times \subset \End(A) \otimes \mathbf{Q}$), ainsi that celui which correspond a the same graduation, decalee of $\dim(A)$ (matrices diagonales in $S$), it contient the homotheties $x \mapsto \lambda x$.

(d) Assume that $\mathbf{Q}_\lambda$ is a extension galoisienne sufficiently grande of $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$. One verifiera that $H_\lambda$ is a sum of representations absolument irreductibles not isomorphes $W$ of $G$, and that the form of intersection defines of the accouplements not degeneres on $W \times W'$, $W \mapsto W'$ being a involution of the factors. Assume that there exists $\sigma \in \Gal(\mathbf{Q}_\lambda/\mathbf{Q}_\ell)$ echangeant the factors en duality. Puisque $Z_\lambda$ is stable by the Galois group, the form of intersection is then not degeneree: this terminera the proof.

Let $E$ the centre of $\End(A) \otimes \mathbf{Q}$: $E$ is a product of field CM $E_i$ (one convient that a field totalement reel is CM\ldots). Assume that $E$ a the property suivante, equivalent a the hypothese ``$c \in D_\ell$'' of the Theorem 1:

\text{Il existe une extension galoisienne $\mathbf{Q}_\lambda$ de $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$ et $\sigma \in \Gal(\mathbf{Q}_\lambda/\mathbf{Q}_\ell)$ tels}
\end{equation}
\[
\text{que, pour tout $i$ et pour tout plongement $\tau : E_i \to \overline{\mathbf{Q}}_\ell$, on ait $\sigma\tau = \tau \circ c$,}
\]

\]

One verifiera then ci--dessous the existence of $\sigma$ echangeant $W$ and $W'$. Under this hypothese, therefore, the Theorem 1 s'ensuit.

\section*{3.}

\[

C'is a algebra graduee by $\mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z}$ [3]; let $C = C^+ \oplus C^-$ this graduation. Faisons agir $W$ on the algebra exterieure $\Lambda^\bullet V$ of $V$ by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:clifford}
v \cdot x = v \wedge x \quad (v \in V, x \in \Lambda^\bullet V) \qquad
\lambda \cdot x = \lambda \rfloor x \quad (\lambda \in V^*, x \in \Lambda^\bullet V)
\end{equation}
or $\lambda \rfloor x = i(\lambda)x$. The relation which defines $C$ is verifiee, and \eqref{eq:clifford} se prolonge therefore en a structure of $C$--module
\begin{equation}\label{eq:clifford-end}
C \longrightarrow \End(\Lambda^\bullet V) .
\end{equation}
The morphism \eqref{eq:clifford-end} is a isomorphism: see [1], Thm.~2; or remarquer that si $V = V' \oplus V''$, $C$ is the tensor product (to the sens $\mathbf{Z}/2$--gradue) of the algebras analogues $C'$ and $C''$: one is therefore ramene to the cas, easy, or $V$ is of rank 1.

\]

Son algebra of Lie is celle of the endomorphisms pairs $T$ of $\Lambda^\bullet V$ such that, for $W$ identifie a under--space of $\End(\Lambda^\bullet V)$, one ait $[T, W] \subset W$. \begin{lemma}\label{lem:4.1}
One munit $\Lambda^\bullet V$ of the product $*$, and of the graduation \eqref{eq:grad}.

\emph{(i)} $\Lambda^\bullet V$ is generated by $\mathrm{gr}^{1-n} = V_1 \otimes \Lambda^n V_2 \oplus \Lambda^0 V_1 \otimes \Lambda^{n-1} V_2$.

\emph{(ii)} The product $* : \mathrm{gr}^i \times \mathrm{gr}^j \to \mathrm{gr}^{i+j}$ is invariant by $\SL(2)$, $\SL(V_1)$ and $\SL(V_2)$.

\emph{(iii)} The graduation of $\Lambda^\bullet V$ is preservee by $G$.
\end{lemma}

the image of $G$ in $\GL(\mathrm{gr}^{n-1})$ contient $\GL(V_1)$, $\GL(V_2) \simeq \GL(\Lambda^{n-1} V_2)$ ainsi that a isomorphism $\Lambda^0 V_1 \otimes \Lambda^n V_2 \xrightarrow{\sim} V_1 \otimes \Lambda^n V_2$ (given by $L$) and a isomorphism inverse (given by $\Lambda$). C'is therefore $\GL(\mathrm{gr}^{n-1})$. The property (i) identifie $\Lambda^\bullet V$ a $\Lambda^\bullet(\mathrm{gr}^{n-1})$, in such a way compatible a the action of $\SL(2) \times \SL(V_1) \times \SL(V_2)$. The group generated by $\{zs_1, z^{-1}s_2\}$, or $z$ is a scalaire and $s_i \in \SL(V_i)$, in $\GL(V_1) \times \GL(V_2)$ opere on $\Lambda^\bullet V$ by the action of $\GL(\mathrm{gr}^{1-n})$ decrite by the Theorem 3. The group $G$ is therefore generated by $\GL(\mathrm{gr}^{1-n})$, isomorphic a $\GL(2n)$, and by a tore of dimension $1$, which opere by homotheties globales on $\Lambda^\bullet V$, of or the Theorem 3 (i). It is clear that, deja for the action of $\GL(\mathrm{gr}^{1-n})$, the representations obtained are absolument irreductibles and not isomorphes; enfin, si $k + \ell \neq 0$, the spaces $\mathrm{gr}^k$ and $\mathrm{gr}^\ell$ ne are not of the representations duales, same for the action of $\GL(2n) \subset G$. The cup--product being not degenere, $\mathrm{gr}^k$ and $\mathrm{gr}^\ell$ are therefore en duality parfaite.

\section*{5.}

A fois acquise the description of $G$, voici the proof of the Theorem 1.

Ecrivons
\[
H_\lambda = H^\bullet(A, \mathbf{Q}_\lambda) = \bigotimes_{v \in \Sigma/c} H_v^\bullet
\]
or $H_v^\bullet$ is as in the \S\,4. One assumed $\mathbf{Q}_\lambda$ sufficiently grand, and galoisien on $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$.

Under the action of the product (desormais note $G$) of the groups decrits in the \S\,4, $H_\lambda$ se decompose as direct sum indexee by the functions $\sigma \mapsto k(\sigma)$ ($\sigma \in \Sigma$) or
\begin{align}
k(\sigma) &\in \mathbf{Z} \text{ si } \sigma \neq \sigma c \text{; alors } k(\sigma c) = -k(\sigma) \label{eq:k1}\\
k(\sigma) &\in \mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z} \text{ si } \sigma = \sigma c \text{ (facteur supersingulier \'eventuel)} . \label{eq:k2}
\end{align}

In \eqref{eq:k1}, $k(\sigma)$ is given by the Theorem \ref{thm:3}, etendu, as in the \S\,4, to the cas or $E$ is CM plutot that quadratique imaginaire. One remarquera that si one change $\sigma$ en $\sigma c$, the spaces $V_1$ and $V_2$ of the \S\,4 are echanges, of or the relation \eqref{eq:k1}.

Let $K$ the group of the functions verifiant \eqref{eq:k1} and \eqref{eq:k2}. Puisque the space vector on $\mathbf{Q}_\lambda$ generated by the cycles algebraic is stable by $G$ ainsi that by cup--product, it correspond a under--mono\"ide $\mathrm{Alg}$ of $K$. Moreover,
\begin{align}
&\mathrm{Alg} \subset K_0 = \{k : \sum_{\sigma \mod c} k(\sigma) \text{ pair}\} \label{eq:alg1}\\
&H_\lambda(k) \text{ est orthogonal \`a } H_\lambda(\ell) \text{ si } k + \ell \neq 0 \text{;} \label{eq:alg2}\\
&H_\lambda(k) \text{ et } H_\lambda(-k) \text{ sont en dualit\'e} \label{eq:alg3}\\
&\mathrm{Alg} \text{ est stable par } \Gal(\mathbf{Q}_\lambda/\mathbf{Q}_\ell) . \label{eq:alg4}
\end{align}

Assume that the Galois group contienne a element agissant as $(-1)$ on $K$. Then, on all under--space $\mathbf{Q}_\ell$--rational of $H_\lambda$, invariant by $G$, the duality given by the form of intersection is not degeneree. C'is in particular the cas si (with the notations of the \S\,1) the conjugaison complex $c \in \Gal(E/\mathbf{Q})$ appartient to the group of decomposition of $\ell$, of or the Theorem 1.

A peu less suffit: for that all under--mono\"ide stable Let under--group of $K$ (therefore that the space of the cycles algebraic contienne, with the space associated a $k$, son dual associated a $-k$), it is enough that $K \otimes \mathbf{Q}$ n'admette not of line fixe under the action of $\Gal(\mathbf{Q}_\lambda/\mathbf{Q}_\ell)$. En of other termes, si $\ell$ is not ramifie in the factors of $E$, it is enough that $\mathrm{Frob}_\ell$ agissant on $\Sigma$ satisfies:
\[
\text{Pour tout } \sigma \in \Sigma\text{, il existe } a \in \mathbf{Z} : \mathrm{Frob}_\ell^a(\sigma) = \sigma c .
\]
