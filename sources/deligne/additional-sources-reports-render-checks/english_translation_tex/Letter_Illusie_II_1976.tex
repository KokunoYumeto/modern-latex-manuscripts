\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent Cher Luc,

\vspace{0.5cm}

The pinceaux donnent the conjecture of ma lettre a Katz in the cas general.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Let:}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{5cm}p{7cm}}
$X$ projective smooth & $\mathcal{F}$ sheaf smooth of $\mathbb{Z}/\ell\mathbb{Z}$ on $U = X - D$ \\
$D$ diviseur smooth & ramifie the long of $D$, locally defined by \\
$D' \subset D$ diviseur smooth in $D$ & $\chi : \mathbb{F}_p \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^*$ and a covering \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

of Artin--Schreier se ramifiant as suit:
\begin{center}
for $x = 0$ equation local for $D$, \quad $x = y^p = 0$ for $D'$:
\[
T^p - T = g/x^d \text{ \quad , \quad avec } (d,p) = 1.
\]
\end{center}

\vspace{0.3cm}

Let $\mathcal{F}'$ the sheaf virtuel
\[
\mathcal{F}' = j_! \mathcal{F} - j_! \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell + d(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)_{D-D'}.
\]
Then
\[
\chi(\mathcal{F}') = 0.
\]

Proof by recurrence. One prend a pinceau of Lefschetz general

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item $\chi$ ne change not by passage a the eclate associated to the pinceau [utilise the nullite of $\chi$ for the intersection of $X$ with the axe: one the a by recurrence]

\item $\chi$ of the section hyperplane generale is $0$ [by recurrence]

\item it ne se passe a accident that quand the section devient tangente a $X$, $D$ or $D'$.

Ailleurs, one has indeed a situation product [loc.\ en haut, $(X,\mathcal{F})$ is product of the section by the parametre of the section]

\item for a pinceau general of haut degree, chacun of these accidents apporte a contribution:
\[
\chi = \alpha\,(\#~\text{tang \`a}~X) + \beta\,(\#~\text{tang \`a}~D) + \gamma\,(\#~\text{tang \`a}~D').
\]
\end{enumerate}

One fait maintenant varier the degree of the pinceau.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{Lemma:} Let $X \subset \mathbb{P}$ projective smooth. The class of $X$ in the $n$-ieme embedding of Segre (by $\mathcal{O}(n)$) is a polynome en $n$, of degree \underline{egal} a $\dim(X)$.

\vspace{0.5cm}

A calcul easy shows that the coefficient of $n^{\dim X}$ is $\deg_X \mathcal{O}(1) = [c_1\,\mathcal{O}(1)]^{\dim X}$.

(methode of the pinceaux, and calcul of $\chi$ of a section hyperplane...)

\vspace{0.5cm}

Of the lors, one has, variant the degree
\[
\chi = \alpha\,(\text{pol d}^\circ \dim X) + \beta\,(\text{pol d}^\circ (\dim X - 1)) + \gamma\,(\text{pol d}^\circ (\dim X - 2)).
\]

\vspace{0.3cm}

of or si $\dim X \geq 3$, one has $\alpha = \beta = \gamma = 0$ and $\chi = 0$.

The cas $\dim X \leq 2$ ayant deja ete traite, that acheve the proof.
