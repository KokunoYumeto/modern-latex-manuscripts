\subsection*{12.5}

Let $V$ a space vector on $k$, and $\nabla = d + \omega$ a connexion integrable a poles logarithmiques the long of $D$ on $V \otimes \cO_X$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.1}
\nabla : V \otimes \cO_X \to V \otimes \Omega_X^1(\log D).
\end{equation}
Every form differentielle global a poles logarithmiques is exact ([D2] 3.2.14). the integrabilite of the connexion: $R = d\omega + \frac{1}{2}[\omega,\omega] = 0$ s'ecrit therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.2}
[\omega, \omega] = 0,
\end{equation}
or the crochet, with values in $H^0(X, \Omega^2(\log D)) \otimes \End(V)$, is defined by
\[
[\alpha \otimes u, \beta \otimes v] = (\alpha \wedge \beta) \otimes [u,v].
\]

The same formule defines a crochet, note $[\omega, \omega]_-$, with values in $\bigwedge^2 H^0(X, \Omega^1(\log D)) \otimes \End(V)$. Let $K$ the kernel of the morphism natural
\[
\wedge : \bigwedge^2 H^0(X, \Omega^1(\log D)) \to H^0(X, \Omega^2(\log D)).
\]
the integrabilite \eqref{eq:12.5.2} s'ecrit again
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.3}
[\omega, \omega]_- \in K \otimes \End(V).
\end{equation}

Dualisons. Let $H$ the dual of $H^0(X, \Omega^1(\log D))$ and $K^\perp \subset \bigwedge^2 H$ the orthogonal of $K$. The datum of $\omega$ equivaut a celle of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.4}
\varphi : H \to \End(V).
\end{equation}
For the commodite of calculs ulterieurs, je prefere modifier the choix evident of \eqref{eq:12.5.4} by a signe. The duality between $H$ and $H^0(X, \Omega^1(\log D))$ fournit a $1$-form canonical $\alpha$ with values in $H$: si $(h_i)$ and $(\alpha_i)$ are of the bases en duality,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.5}
\alpha = \sum \alpha_i h_i.
\end{equation}
One prend for morphism \eqref{eq:12.5.4} the morphism $\varphi$ verifiant
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.6}
\varphi(\alpha) = -\omega.
\end{equation}

Let $\varphi_2 : \bigwedge^2 H \to \End(V)$ defined by $\varphi_2(\alpha \wedge \beta) = [\varphi(\alpha), \varphi(\beta)]$. The condition of integrabilite \eqref{eq:12.5.3} se recrit
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.7}
\varphi_2(K^\perp) = 0.
\end{equation}

Let $\Lib(H)$ the algebra of Lie librement generated by the space vector $H$. It is graduee. one has
\begin{align*}
\Lib(H)_1 &= H \\
\Lib(H)_2 &= \textstyle\bigwedge^2 H \quad (\text{par } x \wedge y \mapsto [x,y]).
\end{align*}
The datum of \eqref{eq:12.5.4} equivaut a celle of a morphism of algebras of Lie
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.8}
\rho : \Lib(H) \to \End(V),
\end{equation}
with $\rho|_H = \varphi$, and the condition \eqref{eq:12.5.7} se recrit
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.5.9}
\rho(K^\perp) = 0.
\end{equation}
The datum of the connexion integrable $\nabla = d + \omega$ on $V \otimes \cO_X$ \eqref{eq:12.5.1} equivaut therefore a celle of a action on $V$ of the algebra of Lie $L(H, K^\perp)$ quotient of $\Lib(H)$ by the ideal generated by $K^\perp$. One laisse to the reader the soin of verify that this construction is compatible to the tensor product.

\subsection*{12.15}

Let $G$ a Lie group and $\alpha = \lambda(t)dt$ a $1$-form on the intervalle $(0,1]$ with values in the algebra of Lie $\Lie G$. Definissons the integral $I_0^1(\alpha)$, with values in $G$, of $\alpha$. One considers the solution $g(t)$ of the equation differentielle
\[
dg \cdot g^{-1} = \alpha,
\]
with the condition initiale that $g(0)$ is the element neutre $e$, and one sets
\[
I_0^1(\alpha) := g(1).
\]

More generally, let $M$ a variety differentiable, $\alpha$ a $1$-form on $M$ with values in $\Lie G$, $\gamma : (0,1] \to M$ a chemin in $M$ of extremites $x = \gamma(0)$ and $y = \gamma(1)$. One sets
\[
I_\gamma(\alpha) := I_0^1(\gamma^* \alpha).
\]
S'it n'y a not of ambiguite on $\gamma$, one ecrira also $I_x^y(\alpha)$. This ``integral'' is invariante by reparametrisation of the chemin. For a chemin compose, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.15.1}
I_x^z(\alpha) = I_y^z(\alpha) \cdot I_x^y(\alpha).
\end{equation}
For a chemin infiniment petit, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.15.2}
I_x^{x+dx}(\alpha) = \alpha.
\end{equation}

Munissons the $G$-torseur trivial $T = G \times M$ on $M$ of the connexion for which
\[
\nabla e = -\alpha.
\]
For every section local $g$, one has then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.15.3}
\nabla g \cdot g^{-1} = dg \cdot g^{-1} - \alpha,
\end{equation}
and $I_\gamma(\alpha)$ s'obtient en transportant the fiber of $T$ en $x$ en celle of $T$ en $y$ by transport parallele the long of $\gamma$, and en prenant the image of $e \in T_x = G$ in $T_y = G$. Si the connexion is integrable: $d\alpha - \frac{1}{2}[\alpha,\alpha] = 0$, $I_\gamma(\alpha)$ ne depend therefore that of the class of homotopie of the chemin $\gamma$ of $x$ a $y$.

For usage ulterieur, notons that \eqref{eq:12.15.3} can se recrire
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12.15.4}
\nabla g = dg - g \cdot \alpha.
\end{equation}

\subsection*{13.3 Betti realization}

\subsection*{15.12}

Voici a other construction of the functor $R \mapsto R_{(s)}$, inspiree by the deformation to the cone normal en theorie of the vanishing cycles.

Let $\bA$ the line affine standard (coordonnee $t$). Eclatons $(0, s)$ in $\bA \times \overline{X}$ and let $E$ the complement of the transforme pur of $\{0\} \times \overline{X}$.

Si $\overline{X}$ is a line of origine $s$ and $z$ a coordonnee on $\overline{X}$ (a form lineaire), $(t, z/t)$ is a systeme of coordonnees for $E$. the isomorphism $(\bA - \{0\}) \times \overline{X} \to (\bA - \{0\}) \times \overline{X}: (t, x) \mapsto (t, x/t)$ se prolonge therefore en a isomorphism of $E$ with $\bA \times \overline{X}$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:15.12.1}
E \cong \text{fibré trivial } \overline{X} \quad (\overline{X} \text{ une droite}).
\end{equation}

For $\overline{X}$ quelconque, appelons \emph{structure lineaire to the neighborhood of $s$} the choix of a class privilegiee of coordonnees local, such that one passe of the a the other by a transformation lineaire affine. Se donner a germe of structure lineaire en $s$ revient a se donner a germe $\varphi : (T_s, 0) \to (\overline{X}, s)$ with $\varphi'(0) = 1$: a $\varphi$, attacher the coordonnees local $a \circ \varphi^{-1}$, with $a$ lineaire affine. a coordonnee local $z$ centree en $s$ attacher $\varphi$ such that $z \circ \varphi = dz$.

The choix of a structure lineaire en $s$ we ramene, pres of $s$, to the cas or $\overline{X}$ is a line. One trouve that $E$ is smooth on $\bA$ and that the section $s$ of $E$ to the-dessus of $\bA - \{0\}$ se prolonge a $\bA$. One obtains also a isomorphism of the fiber of $E$ en $0$ with the space tangent $T_s$. This isomorphism ne depend not of the structure lineaire (or of the coordonnee local) choisie.

Let $\overset{\circ}{E}$ the complement of the section $s$, prolongee a $\bA$. the space $E$ (resp. $\overset{\circ}{E}$) is smooth on $\bA$; to the-dessus of $\bA - \{0\}$ c'is the fiber trivial of fiber $\overline{X}$ (resp. $X$) and sa fiber en $0$ is $T_s$ (resp. $T_s^0$).

Let $((\bA - \{0\})^\sim, 1)$ the covering universal of $(\bA - \{0\}, 1)$ and completons this space en $\bA^\sim$ to the-dessus of $\bA$ by adjonction of a point $\tilde{0}$ to the-dessus of $0$. As topologie on $\bA^\sim$ one prend the more grossiere for which $(\bA - \{0\})^\sim$ is a open, with sa topologie habituelle, and $\bA^\sim \to \bA$ is continu. Concretement: $\bA^\sim = \bC \cup \{\infty\}$; systeme fondamental of voisinages of $\infty$: $\{\infty\} \cup \{z \mid \Re z < a\}$; $\bA^\sim \to \bA: \exp$; $\tilde{1} = 0$.

Let $E^\sim := E \times_{\bA} \bA^\sim$ the inverse image of $E$ on $\bA^\sim$. For every covering $R$ of $X$, let $R_1$ son inverse image on $(\bA - \{0\})^\sim \times X \hookrightarrow E$. Si $\overline{X}$ is a line, the isomorphism \eqref{eq:15.12.1} identifie $R_1$ a the inverse image of $R$ on $\bC \times \overline{X}$ by the map $(z, x) \mapsto \exp(z) \cdot x$. This map is homotope a $(z, x) \mapsto x$, by the homotopie $(z, x) \mapsto \exp(\lambda z) \cdot x$ ($0 \leqslant \lambda \leqslant 1$) and $R_1$ is therefore isomorphic a $\pr_2^* R$. The covering $R_1$ se prolonge therefore en $R^\sim$ on $E^\sim$ and the restriction of $R^\sim$ a the fiber $T_s^0 \cong X$ of $\overset{\circ}{E} \to \bA$ en $0$ is $R$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:15.12.2}
R^\sim|_{T_s^0} \cong R \quad (\overline{X} \text{ une droite; on utilise que } T_s^0 \cong \overline{X} \text{ par } \eqref{eq:15.12.1}).
\end{equation}

For $\overline{X}$ quelconque, the choix of a structure lineaire en $s$ we ramene, pres of $s$, to the cas or $\overline{X}$ is a line. One trouve that $R_1$ se prolonge en a covering $R^\sim$ of $E^\sim$. The \emph{deformation to the cone normal} $r(R)$ of $R$ is the restriction of $R^\sim$ a the fiber $T_s^0$ of $\overset{\circ}{E}^\sim \to \bA^\sim$ en $\tilde{0}$. Si a structure lineaire is defined by $\varphi : (T_s^0, 0) \to (X, s)$, $\varphi'(0) = 1$, \eqref{eq:15.12.2} fournit a isomorphism of germes of coverings of $T_s^0$ pres of $0 \in T_s^0$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:15.12.3}
r(R) \cong \varphi^* R \quad \text{(germe près de } 0 \in T_s^0\text{)}.
\end{equation}

The isomorphisms \eqref{eq:15.12.3} definissent a isomorphism between the functor $R \mapsto r(R)$ and the functor $R \mapsto R_\varphi$. That the space of the $\varphi$ is connected assure that it ne depend not of $\varphi$.
