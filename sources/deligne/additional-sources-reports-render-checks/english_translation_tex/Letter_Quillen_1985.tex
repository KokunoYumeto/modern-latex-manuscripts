\textbf{\Large Lettre a Quillen}
\end{center}

\vspace{1cm}

\noindent\hfill Bures-on-Yvette, the 20 juin 1985

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent Cher Quillen,

\medskip

I thought about how to compute, a the Riemann-Roch, the analytic twisting of which you computed the curvature in your IHES preprint. I succeeded only for virtual bundles of rank~0: the case of $\cO$, or the meaning of $\mathrm{Td}_2$, eludes me. I expect that a lot of what follows is well known to you. Please tell me.

Once the statement to be proved is constructed, the proof is essentially a proof of uniqueness of a metric one $\det Rf_*(X,E)$ for metrized vector bundle $E$, such that:
\begin{itemize}
\item compatibility with $\oplus$ (gauge invariance is implicit)
\item the curvature property you prove.
\end{itemize}
I should say: uniqueness up to a scalar $c(X, \text{metric on } X)^{\rg E}$.

As I have to think, I continue in French.

\section{Motivation}

Let $X/S$ a fiber en curves. Riemann-Roch dit, for $E$ virtuel of rank~0:
\begin{equation}
c_1 Rf_* E = \frac{1}{2} \int_{X/S} c_1(E)^2 - c_1(E) c_1(\omega_{X/S}) - 2c_2(E).
\end{equation}

This formule is a equality in $\Pic(S) \otimes \Q$. Je veux the relever en the construction of fibers en lines, uniques a isomorphism unique pres corresponding to the differents termes, and en the construction of a isomorphism canonical remplacant the $=$ of the formule.

Voici the liste of the constructions a faire:

For $E$: fiber vector on $X$, $X$ curve on $S$:

\begin{enumerate}
\item $[c_1 Rf_* E]$: $\det Rf_* E$: a fiber en lines gradue on $S$ (en degree $\chi(X_s, E_s)$, $s \in S$) --- C'is connu.

\item $[c_1(E)]$: $\det E$, a fiber en lines on $X$.

\item $\left[\int_{X/S} c_1 \cdot c_1\right]$: a deux fibers en lines $L$, $M$ on $X$, attacher a fiber en lines $\langle L, M \rangle$ on $S$ --- C'is in SGA~4~XVIII and recopie more bas.

\item $\left[\int_{X/S} c_2\right]$: a fiber vector $E$ on $X$, attacher a fiber en lines $\IC_2(E)$ on $S$. This will be fait more bas. A exact sequence $0 \to E' \to E \to E'' \to 0$ given lieu a isomorphism
\begin{equation}
\IC_2(E) \xrightarrow{\sim} \IC_2(E') \otimes \IC_2(E'') \otimes \langle \det E', \det E'' \rangle
\end{equation}
(cf $c_2(E) = c_2(E') + c_2(E'') + c_1(E')c_1(E'')$).

\item This fait, it faudra define a isomorphism canonical
\begin{equation}
\frac{(\det Rf_* E)^{\otimes 2}}{(\det Rf_* \cO)^{\otimes \rg E}} \xrightarrow{\sim} \langle \det E, \det E \rangle \otimes \langle \det E, \omega_{X/S} \rangle^{-1} \otimes \IC_2(E)^{\otimes -2}
\end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

Assume that $S = \Spec(\C)$, and that $E$ let equipped with a metrique hermitienne. One sets $\langle s, s \rangle = \|s\|^2$. The twisting analytic fournit a metrique on $\det Rf_* E$. The plan will be of continuer:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[6)] a metrique on the space vector of dimension~1 $\langle L, M \rangle$, for $L$ and $M$ equipped with metriques.
\item[7)] a metrique on $\IC_2(E)$, for $E$ equipped with a metrique.
\end{enumerate}

Noter that 6) and 7) are independants of all choix of a metrique on $X$. A metrique hermitienne on $X$ is by definition a metrique hermitienne on the fiber tangent and defines a metrique duale on $\Omega^1$.

The but is of prouver that, for this metrique on $\Omega^1$, one has:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[8)] the isomorphism 5) is a isometrie.
\end{enumerate}

\`A priori, in 5), the metrique a gauche is $h(E)$ fois the metrique a line, with $h(E) \in \R_+^*$ function of $(E$ with sa metrique$)$. One prouvera of abord various properties fonctorielles and of commune of $h$, for montrer ensuite that they suffisent a the examiner. The necessary confidence for effectuer the calculs is fournie by the analogie with the cas not archimedien: si $X$ is a curve over the field of the fractions of a ring of valuation discrete $A$, is prolonge en $X_A$ regular proper on $A$, and that a fiber $E$ on $X$ is prolonge en a fiber vector $E_A$ on $X_A$ (the analogue of a metrique), one dispose of a $A$-structure on $\det Rf_* E$ (savoir, $\det Rf_* E_A$), and similarly of $A$-structures on $\langle \det E, \det E \rangle$, etc., and si 5) respecte the $A$-structures, c'is vrai on every base $S$, by example $\Spec(A)$.

Si one se place in the cas of a surface arithmetic $X \to \Spec \cO$, the theorem fournit again a isomorphism between fibers en lines metriques on $\Spec \cO$; the equality of the classes of isomorphie is a theorem a the Riemann-Roch.

\section{Analogies}

Let $X$ a curve projective not singular over the field of the fractions $K$ of a ring of valuation discrete complet $A$ a field residuel finite. One denotes $|A|$ the value absolute of $K$.

For $V$ a $K$-space vector and $M$ a $A$-structure, one sets $\|v\| = \langle v, v \rangle^{1/2}$. one has $\|\lambda v\| = \inf\{\|v\| : v \in M\}$, or defines $\|v\|_M = \inf\{\|m\| : m \in M\}$ for $v \in V$.

Let $E$ a fiber vector on $X$. Si $X_A$ is a model proper of $X$ on $A$, one se given a model $E_A$ of $E$ on $X_A$ (the analogue of a metrique hermitienne).

\medskip

one has a metrique hermitienne on $E$ defines, for each $x \in X$, a structure hermitienne on the fiber of $E$ en $x$. The fiber vector on $X_A$ prolongeant $E$ en $x \in X(K)$, of or $E_x$ and $x^* E_A$ is a $A$-structure on the fiber of $E$ en $x$.

one has a function $L_E$: a diviseur $D$ on $X_A$ regular, one attached $L_E(D) := \det(\Gamma(X_A, E_A(D))/\Gamma(X_A, E_A))$.

\section{$\langle L, M \rangle$}

Let $f: X \to S$ proper, plat, purement of dimension relative 1. For $L$ and $M$ of the fibers en lines on $X$, one defines $\langle L, M \rangle$ as the fiber en lines on $S$ generated by of the symbols $\langle \ell, m \rangle$ ($\ell$ and $m$ sections rationnelles of $L$ and $M$ of which the diviseurs are disjoints), with the relations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\langle \ell, m_1 m_2 \rangle = \langle \ell, m_1 \rangle \otimes \langle \ell, m_2 \rangle$
\item[(ii)] $\langle \ell_1 \ell_2, m \rangle = \langle \ell_1, m \rangle \otimes \langle \ell_2, m \rangle$
\item[(iii)] Si $\Div(\ell) = \sum n_i P_i$, then $\langle \ell, m \rangle = \bigotimes m(P_i)^{\otimes n_i}$
\end{enumerate}

The relation (iii) a sens car $m$ is a section rational of $M$, inversible on the support of $\Div(\ell)$. The loi of reciprocite of Weil assure the compatibility of (i), (ii), (iii).

\begin{proposition}
With the notations precedentes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Si $L$ or $M$ is trivial, $\langle L, M \rangle$ is trivial.
\item[(ii)] For $D$ a diviseur relative effective, $\langle \cO(D), M \rangle = N_{D/S}(M)$, the norme of $M$ of $D$ a $S$.
\item[(iii)] The functor $(L, M) \mapsto \langle L, M \rangle$ is symmetric and biadditif.
\item[(iv)] one has $\langle L, M \rangle \otimes \langle L, N \rangle \xrightarrow{\sim} \langle L, M \otimes N \rangle$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
These properties resultent directement of the definition.
\end{proof}

\section{Metrique on $\langle L, M \rangle$}

Supposons $S = \Spec(\C)$ and $X$ a surface of Riemann compacte equipped with a metrique hermitienne. Let $L$ and $M$ of the fibers en lines equipped with metriques hermitiennes. One defines a metrique on $\langle L, M \rangle$ by:
\begin{equation}
\log \|\langle \ell, m \rangle\| = \int_X \log \|\ell\| \cdot c_1(M) + \int_X \log \|m\| \cdot c_1(L) + \int_X \log \|\ell\| \cdot dd^c \log \|m\|
\end{equation}
or $c_1(M) = \frac{i}{2\pi} \partial \bar{\partial} \log \|m\|^2$ is the form of Chern of $M$, and the derniere integral is regularisee si the diviseurs of $\ell$ and $m$ ne are not disjoints.

\begin{theorem}
The metrique defined ci-dessus is compatible with the isomorphisms of symetrie and of additivite of the Proposition 3.1. Sa courbure is given by:
\begin{equation}
c_1(\langle L, M \rangle) = \int_{X/S} c_1(L) \wedge c_1(M).
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\section{Commune of $\langle L, M \rangle$}

For $f: X \to S$ as en \S3, the fiber en lines $\langle L, M \rangle$ is defined on $S$ and fonctoriel by changement of base. The properties fonctorielles are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Symetrie: $\langle L, M \rangle \xrightarrow{\sim} \langle M, L \rangle$.
\item[(ii)] Biadditivite: $\langle L_1 \otimes L_2, M \rangle \xrightarrow{\sim} \langle L_1, M \rangle \otimes \langle L_2, M \rangle$.
\item[(iii)] For $u: S' \to S$, one has $u^* \langle L, M \rangle \xrightarrow{\sim} \langle u^* L, u^* M \rangle$.
\item[(iv)] Si $L = f^* N$ for $N$ a fiber en lines on $S$, then $\langle L, M \rangle \xrightarrow{\sim} N^{\otimes \deg(M)}$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Generalisation a the dimension $n$}

Let $f: X \to S$ proper, plat, purement of dimension relative $n$. For $L_0, \ldots, L_n$ of the fibers en lines on $X$, one veut define $\langle L_0, \ldots, L_n \rangle_{X/S}$, a fiber en lines on $S$. One procede by recurrence on $n$. For $n = 0$, $X$ is finite on $S$ and $\langle L_0 \rangle = N_{X/S}(L_0)$. For $n > 0$, one se ramene to the cas or the a of the $L_i$ is of the form $\cO(D)$ for a diviseur relative $D$, and one sets:
\begin{equation}
\langle L_0, \ldots, L_n \rangle = \langle L_0|_D, \ldots, L_n|_D \rangle_{D/S}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Esquisse]
This definition is correcte and fournit a functor symmetric multiadditif en the $L_i$, compatible to the changement of base.
\end{theorem}

\section{RR for the fibers en lines (result partiel)}

Let $f: X \to S$ proper and plat, purement of dimension relative 1. For $L$ a fiber en lines on $X$, one veut construct a isomorphism canonical:
\begin{equation}
(\det Rf_* L)^{\otimes 2} \xrightarrow{\sim} \langle L, L \otimes \omega_{X/S}^{-1} \rangle^{-1} \otimes (\det Rf_* \cO)^{\otimes 2 \cdot \deg(L)}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}
This isomorphism exists, is unique (a signe pres), and is a isometrie for the metriques of Quillen and of Deligne.
\end{theorem}

\section{Construction of $\IC_2$}

Let $f: X \to S$ proper and plat, purement of dimension relative 1. One defines a category of Picard commutative $\cP(X/S)$ as suit:
\begin{itemize}
\item The objects are the couples $(\rg(E), \det E)$ for $E$ a fiber vector on $X$.
\item The morphisms $(r, L) \to (r', L')$ exist only si $r = r'$, and are then the isomorphisms $L \xrightarrow{\sim} L'$.
\end{itemize}

One veut define $\IC_2(E)$, a object of $\cP(S)$ (i.e., a fiber en lines on $S$), such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\IC_2(E)$ is additif en $E$ for the sequences exact courtes.
\item[(ii)] For $E$ of rank 1, $\IC_2(E) = \cO_S$.
\item[(iii)] For every exact sequence $0 \to E' \to E \to E'' \to 0$:
\begin{equation}
\IC_2(E) \xrightarrow{\sim} \IC_2(E') \otimes \IC_2(E'') \otimes \langle \det E', \det E'' \rangle.
\end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}
The functor $E \mapsto \IC_2(E)$ exists and is unique (a isomorphism unique pres).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Esquisse of proof]
All fiber vector admet a filtration a quotients successifs of rank 1. the unicite resulte of this that $\IC_2(E)$ is then determine by sa value on the fibers of rank 1 (trivial) and by the data of gluing for the sequences exact. the existence se proves en verifiant that the data of gluing are coherentes.
\end{proof}

\section{The metrique of $\IC_2(E)$}

Let $X$ a variety analytic complex, $E$ a fiber vector holomorphic on $X$, equipped with a metrique hermitienne $h$. One defines the metrique of $\IC_2(E)$ by additivite a partir of the metrique on $\langle \det E', \det E'' \rangle$ and of the formule (8.1).

More precisely, si $0 \to E' \to E \to E'' \to 0$ is a exact sequence of fibers vector metrises, the metrique on $\IC_2(E)$ is defined by:
\begin{equation}
\|\cdot\|_{\IC_2(E)} = \|\cdot\|_{\IC_2(E')} \otimes \|\cdot\|_{\IC_2(E'')} \otimes \|\cdot\|_{\langle \det E', \det E'' \rangle}.
\end{equation}

One checks that this definition ne depend not of the choix of the exact sequence (or of the filtration), grace a the compatibility of the metriques with the isomorphisms of symetrie and of additivite.

\begin{theorem}
The metrique on $\IC_2(E)$ ainsi defined satisfait the properties suivantes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] It is compatible with the sequences exact (isomorphism (8.1) is a isometrie).
\item[(ii)] For $E = L_1 \oplus L_2$, the metrique on $\IC_2(E) = \langle L_1, L_2 \rangle$ coincide with the metrique of Deligne defined to the \S4.
\item[(iii)] The courbure of the metrique on $\IC_2(E)$ is given by:
\begin{equation}
c_1(\IC_2(E)) = \int_{X/S} c_2(E, h),
\end{equation}
or $c_2(E, h)$ is the deuxieme form of Chern for the connexion of Chern of $(E, h)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\section{The theorem}

Let $X$ a curve projective and smooth on $\C$ and $E$ a fiber vector holomorphic on $X$, equipped with a metrique hermitienne $h$. Let $g$ a metrique hermitienne on $X$. The twisting analytic of Ray-Singer fournit a metrique $\|\cdot\|_Q$ on $\det H^*(X, E)$.

\begin{theorem}[Theorem principal]
the isomorphism canonical
\begin{equation}
(\det H^*(X, E))^{\otimes 2} \xrightarrow{\sim} \langle \det E, \det E \otimes \omega_X^{-1} \rangle^{-1} \otimes \IC_2(E)^{\otimes -2} \otimes (\det H^*(X, \cO))^{\otimes 2 \rg E}
\end{equation}
is a isometrie for the metrique of Quillen a gauche and the tensor product of the metriques of Deligne a line.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Esquisse]
By unicite, it is enough to verify that the deux metriques have same courbure (calculee by Quillen for the metrique of Quillen, and by the formules of Bott-Chern for the metriques of Deligne), and that they coincident for of the fibers particuliers. One se ramene to the cas or $E$ is a sum of fibers en lines, then by deformation to the cas or $E$ is trivial. The cas $E = \cO$ is traite by the theorie of the modular forms (formule of Kronecker).
\end{proof}
