\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} Except for a few corrections added in 2010, the article reproduces notes, sent to Nagata in the 1970s, which were a translation in the language of schemes of his articles [2], [3]. The proofs are a ``constructive'' version of his proofs. Making the proofs constructive allowed us to proceed without using valuations. A more detailed exposition and additional results are in Conrad [1]. Unless otherwise mentioned, all the schemes considered are supposed to be Noetherian.

Moreover, $D^+(x) \cap (X - V(\mathfrak{a})) = \Spec(A[x^{-1}])$.

\section*{1. The theorems}

\begin{definition}
Let $X$ and $Y$ deux $S$-schemes, with $Y$ separe on $S$. A quasi-domination $f: X \dashrightarrow Y$ is a couple form of a open dense $U$ of $X$ and of a morphism of $S$-schemes $f: U \to Y$ of which the graphe let closed in $X \times_S Y$.

Let $X$ and $Y$ deux $S$-schemes, with $Y$ separe on $S$, $U$ a open (quasi-compact) of $X$ and $f: U \to Y$. One dit that $X$ is quasi-dominant (rel. a $U$ and $f$) on $Y$ s'there exists a quasi-domination of the adherence schematique $\overline{U}$ of $U$, with values in $Y$, which prolonge $f$. There exists to the more a telle quasi-domination (of graphe the adherence of the graphe of $f$).

A quasi-domination $f$ is dite proper (or complete) si the morphism $f: U \to Y$ is proper.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[{[2, Theorem 3.2]}]
Let $X$ and $Y$ deux $S$-schemes and $U \subset V \subset X$ deux ouverts denses of $X$. One assumed $Y$ separe of type finite on $S$. Let $f: U \to Y$ a quasi-domination of $V$ in $Y$. There exists a ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ with $V(\mathfrak{a}) \subset X - V$ such that the eclate $\widetilde{X}$ of $X$ selon $V(\mathfrak{a})$ quasi-domine $Y$.
\end{theorem}

By the changement of base $X \to S$, one se ramene a supposer that $X = S$. $Y$ is then a scheme on $X$, $f$ a section of $Y$ to the-dessus of $U$, and by hypothese $f(U)$ is closed to the-dessus of $V$.

\section*{Commentaires}

J'espere that en being more constructifs again, one pourra prouver the theorem 1.6 for $f: X \to S$ separe of type finite, $S$ quasi-compact quasi-separe. The regles a suivre are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item considerer uniquement of the ouverts quasi-compacts and of the fermes defined by of the ideals of type finite
\item ne jamais prendre of adherence schematique (one sortirait of (a)), but prendre only a closed contenant the part datum and sufficiently petit for the constructions ulterieures
\item ne not parler of open quasi-projective dense (cf. (b)). Se rappeler that, for construct of tels ouverts, one pourrait partir of a cover affine $U_i$, and prendre $U_i' = U_i - \bigcup_{j<i} U_j$. Traduire the arguments en termes of the $U_i$ of depart, and appliquer (a)+(b).
\end{enumerate}

Je n'ai by against aucune idee in the cas of the spaces algebraic.
