\subsection*{9.1}

Si $G$ is a algebraic group unipotent over a field $k$ of caracteristique 0, the map exponentielle
$$\exp : \Lie G \longrightarrow G$$
is defined, and is a isomorphism of $\Lie G$, equipped with the loi of Hausdorff (Bourbaki, Lie II \S 6), with $G$. The functor $G \mapsto \Lie(G)$: (groups unipotents on $k$) $\to$ (algebras of Lie nilpotentes of dimension finite on $k$) is therefore a equivalence of categories, of inverse $\mathcal{L} \mapsto (\mathcal{L}$, equipped with the loi of Hausdorff).

\subsection*{9.2}

Malcev ([Mal]) ne s'exprime not en terme of algebraic groups unipotents on $\Q$. It utilise the notion voisine of group nilpotent without twisting divisible. In a tel group $\Gamma$, the map $x \mapsto x^n$ ($n$ integer $\neq 0$) is bijective and one can define $x^r$ for $x \in \Gamma$, $r \in \Q$.

Si $\mathcal{L}$ is a algebra of Lie nilpotente on $\Q$, the loi of Hausdorff fait of $\mathcal{L}$ a group nilpotent without twisting divisible. This functor (algebras of Lie nilpotentes on $\Q$) $\to$ (groups nilpotents without twisting divisibles) is a equivalence of categories (Bourbaki Lie II \S 6 ex 4). One denotes $\Gamma \mapsto \Lie \Gamma$ son inverse.

\subsection*{9.3}

The functor of inclusion
$$\text{(groupes nilpotents uniquement divisibles)} \longrightarrow \text{(groupes nilpotents)}$$

\subsection*{9.4}

Disons that a representation of a group $\Gamma$ on a space vector $V$ on $k$ of caracteristique 0 is nilpotente si $V$ admet a filtration finite $F$, respectee by $\Gamma$, such that the action of $\Gamma$ on $\Gr_F(V)$ let trivial. $$\Lie \Gamma \longrightarrow \{u \in \gl(V) \mid u \text{ respecte } F \text{ et } \Gr_F(u) = 0\}.$$

\begin{align*}
&\text{(repr\'esentation nilpotente de $\Gamma$ sur $V$)} \\
&\qquad \xrightarrow{\sim} \text{(repr\'esentation nilpotente de $\Lie \Gamma$ sur $V$)}.
\end{align*}
