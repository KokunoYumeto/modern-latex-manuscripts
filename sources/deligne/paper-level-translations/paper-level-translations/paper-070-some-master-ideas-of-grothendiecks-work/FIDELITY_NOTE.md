# Fidelity note: Deligne paper 70

This package contains a cleaned French TeX layer and a full English translation TeX layer for:

**Quelques idées maîtresses de l'œuvre de A. Grothendieck** / **Some Master Ideas in the Work of A. Grothendieck**.

The translation is not a summary. It preserves the article structure:
- title and author line;
- abstract;
- opening quotation and contextual paragraphs;
- Sections 1--4: schemes, topoi, six operations, motives;
- all displayed diagrams/formulas used in the article;
- bibliography.

The French layer is a cleaned working re-typesetting, not a raw verbatim/OCR dump. OCR artifacts were normalized in ordinary mathematical French: accents, \(\ell\), \(\pi_1\), \(Rf_!\), \(\otimes^L\), \(R\mathcal Hom\), \(Lf^*\), \(Rf^!\), and the schematic diagrams.
