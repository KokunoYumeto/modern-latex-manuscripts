# Fidelity note - Deligne Paper 88

This package contains a full working English translation and a French working transcription layer for Pierre Deligne's **Comptage de faisceaux l-adiques**.

The translation was checked against the original article text and rendered article images.  The package avoids source-page framing and keeps the working French layer as an alignment/audit aid.

Coverage checklist:

- title, dedication, all section headings;
- Sections 1-6;
- Lemma 2.4, Lemma 2.6, Lemma 2.11, Remark 2.12, Lemma 2.13, Conjecture 2.15, Variant 2.16, Conjecture 2.18, Example 2.19, Proposition 2.20, Theorem 3.5, Assertion 3.8, Lemma 3.11, Lemma 3.13, Proposition 3.15, Lemma 3.16, Surprises 4.2, 4.3, 4.5, Proposition 5.2, Proposition 5.7, Conjecture 6.3, Conjecture 6.7;
- numbered displayed formulas (1.1.1) through (6.4.3), as present in the source;
- commutative diagrams (1.1.1), (3.16.1), and (3.16.2), reconstructed in TikZ-CD;
- bibliography.

Status: audited working translation.  The English PDF and French working PDF compile under XeLaTeX and were render-checked.
