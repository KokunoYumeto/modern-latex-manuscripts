\section*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Introduction}

\colheaders

\paralleltext{%
एतत् पाठान्तरं भास्कराचार्यकृतस्य बीजगणितस्य अन्तिमभागं समाविशति । एतत् पृष्ठसंख्याः ११५--१७० संवृणोति । अस्मिन् भागे सर्वोन्नतगणितविषयाः समाविष्टाः --- बहुवर्णमध्यमाहरणानि, भावितसमीकरणानि, आसन्नमूलविधयः, च उपसंहारश्लोकाः ।
}{%
This portion comprises the final section of Bhāskarācārya's \textit{Bījagaṇita}, covering pages 115--170 of the original Chowkhamba edition. It contains the most advanced topics in the treatise: equations with multiple unknowns (\textit{aneka-varṇa-madhyamāharaṇa}), product equations (\textit{bhāvita-samīkaraṇa}), approximate root methods (\textit{āsanna-mūla-vidhi}), and the concluding verses (\textit{upasaṃhāra}).
}

\paralleltext{%
भास्करः स्वलक्षणकसंज्ञां प्रयुङ्क्ते । अज्ञातराशयः \dev{यावत्तावत्} (या) इत्यादिवर्णैः संज्ञायन्ते । गुणकाः तदुपसर्गाः भवन्ति । अन्तिमभागेषु च प्रकाशकस्य पुस्तकसूची अपि विद्यते ।
}{%
Bhāskara employs his characteristic notation where unknown quantities are denoted by the initial syllable \dev{याव} (\textit{yāva-}, from \textit{yāvattāvat}, ``as much as''), with coefficients prefixed to these symbols. The final sections also include a publisher's catalogue listing the Sanskrit texts available from the Chowkhamba Sanskrit Book Depot, Vārāṇasī.
}

\paralleltext{%
अस्य द्विभाषापाठस्य संरचना एतादृशी --- वामपार्श्वे संस्कृतमूलं देवनागर्यां, दक्षिणपार्श्वे तदनुवादं आङ्ग्लभाषया सह आइएएस्टी (IAST) लिप्यन्तरेण गणितसंज्ञानां च ।
}{%
This bilingual edition is arranged in parallel columns: the Sanskrit original in Devanagari on the left, and its English translation with IAST transliteration of mathematical terminology on the right. May this arrangement serve students and scholars alike in their study of India's rich algebraic heritage.
}

\section{From the Varga-prakṛti Section (p.\ 115)}

\colheaders

\paralleltext{%
अयं भागः वर्गप्रकृतिचक्रवालविधेः प्रवृत्तिं समाविशति । वर्गप्रकृतिसमीकरणं $Nx^2 + k = y^2$ रूपेण भवति । अत्र नवानि सूत्राणि, उदाहरणानि च प्रदर्शितानि ।
}{%
This section continues the discussion of the \textit{Varga-prakṛti} (square-nature) equation, i.e., equations of the form $Nx^2 + k = y^2$, known in modern mathematics as Pell's equation. The text presents further rules and worked examples for finding solutions to such indeterminate equations.
}

\subsection*{Continuation of the Square-nature Method}

\parallelverse{%
समशोधने हृते पक्षौ यावच्चावत्तावकस्य रूपं प्रक्षिप्य प्रथमपक्षमूलं या २ रू १ । परपक्षस्य काव १२ रू १ । वर्गप्रकृत्या मूले क २ उचे ७ वा क २८ उचे ९७ । कनिष्ठं कालकमानम् । उच्चिष्टस्य या २ रू १ समं हृत्वा लब्धं यावच्चावकमानम् ३ वा ४८ । स्वच्छानेनोत्थापने हृते जाती राशी १, ५ वा २०, ७६ इत्यादि ॥
}{%
After equal subtraction (\textit{sama-śodhana}), the two sides being reduced, adding the original constant to the first side, its root is $\sqrt{4y^2 + 1}$. For the second side: $\sqrt{12y^2 - 1}$. By the \textit{varga-prakṛti} [square-nature method], the roots (\textit{mūla}) are $(2, 7)$ or $(28, 97)$. The lesser (\textit{kaniṣṭha}) is the time-value (\textit{kālaka-māna}). The residue (\textit{ucciṣṭa}) from $\sqrt{4y^2 + 1}$ gives the \textit{yāvattāvat} value $3$ or $48$. By self-substitution (\textit{sva-uddhāpana}) and reduction, the series of values $1, 5$ or $20, 76$, etc.
}

\bigskip

\parallelverse{%
अथापरं सूत्रं साध्येऽस्तु ॥\\[0.5em]
(१) द्वितीयपक्षे सति सममेव तु हत्यापवर्त्योन पदे प्रसाध्ये । उच्चिष्टं कनिष्ठेन तदा निहत्याच्छेदं वर्गेण हतोऽपवर्तः ॥ ६ ॥\\[0.3em]
कनिष्ठवर्गेण तदा निहत्योच्चिष्टं ततः पूर्ववदेव शेषम् । इत्यर्थात् ॥
}{%
Now another rule is to be accomplished: Rule 6: ``When the second side exists [and is not unity], having made [the two sides] equal (\textit{sama}), multiplying and reducing (\textit{apavartana}), the remainder (\textit{ucciṣṭa}) [is obtained]; having multiplied the residue by the lesser [root] (\textit{kaniṣṭha-mūla}), dividing by the square [nature] (\textit{varga-prakṛti}), the reduced [equation is obtained]. Then having multiplied by the square of the lesser [root], the residue --- from that, as before, the remainder.'' Thus the meaning.
}

\subsection*{Worked Example (p.\ 115)}

\parallelverse{%
(१) वि०---कल्प्यते समी पक्षौ\\[0.3em]
\hspace*{2em} काव १\\
\hspace*{2em} यावव्। ई १ याव।ई १
}{%
(1) The two sides are assumed equal: \\[0.3em]
\hspace*{2em} [Side 1:] coefficient $12$ \\[0.3em]
\hspace*{2em} [Side 2:] $\sqrt{\text{yāva} \cdot 1} \cdot \sqrt{\text{yāva} \cdot 1}$
}

\paralleltext{%
अत्र प्रथमपक्षस्य मूले क १ द्वितीयपक्षस्य यावव्।ई १ याव।ई १ मूलेन सममिति । तत्र द्वितीयपक्षस्य मूले च का।
}{%
Here the root of the first side is $1$. The second side, having its root equal to $\sqrt{\text{yāva} \cdot 1 \cdot \text{yāva} \cdot 1}$, is equivalent to that. There, for the second side, let the root be \textit{kā}.
}

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{center}
\fbox{$\displaystyle \text{का} = \sqrt{\text{यावव्।ई १ याव।ई १}} = \text{या}\sqrt{\text{याव।ई १ ई १}}$}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.5em}

\paralleltext{%
अत इदं याव।ई १ ई १ मूलदं ततो वर्गप्रकृतिविषयो यथा का वर्गः ई, गुणः ई, युतो मूलदं इति हत्वा यावच्चावकमानं उच्चिष्टं चास्य याव।ई १ ई १ मूलेन सममिति।
}{%
Therefore this gives the root of ``\textit{yāva} 1, constant 1''; then by the \textit{varga-prakṛti} method, as the square (\textit{kā-varga}) of \textit{kā} is $1$, the multiplier (\textit{guṇa}) is $1$, and when the additive is added, the root is obtained. Having multiplied, the \textit{yāvattāvat} value and the residue from $\sqrt{\text{yāva} \cdot 1 \cdot \text{constant} \cdot 1}$ are equal.
}

\subsection*{Summary Rule (p.\ 115 bottom)}

\parallelverse{%
एवं बहुधा बुद्धिमत्त्वविचिन्त्यामिति सर्वमुपपन्नम् ॥
}{%
``Thus in many ways, by the thoughtful application of intelligence (\textit{buddhimat-tva-vicintya}), all [is demonstrated].'' Everything is proved (\textit{upapanna}).
}

\section{Aneka-varṇa-madhyamāharaṇa-bhedāḥ (pp.\ 116--133)}
\label{sec:aneka-varna}

\paralleltext{%
अयं विभागः समग्रस्यान्तिमभागस्य महत्तमः अस्ति । अत्र बहुवर्णसमीकरणानां व्यवस्थितविधयः प्रदर्श्यन्ते । संस्कृते अज्ञातराशयः \textit{वर्णैः} (रङ्गैः) संज्ञायन्ते --- भिन्नेभ्यो वर्णेभ्यो भिन्नाः अज्ञातराशयः संबद्धाः भवन्ति ।
}{%
This is the largest section of the final portion, dealing systematically with equations involving multiple unknown quantities (\textit{aneka-varṇa}, ``many colours''). In Indian algebra, the term \textit{varṇa} (colour) is the traditional designation for an unknown quantity --- different unknowns being assigned different colours such as \textit{yāvattāvat} (black), \textit{nīla} (blue), \textit{pīta} (yellow), \textit{lohita} (red), etc.
}

\subsection{General Rules for Multiple Unknowns (pp.\ 116--117)}

\parallelverse{%
अथ सूत्रं साध्येऽस्तु ॥\\[0.5em]
(१) द्वितीयपक्षे सति सममेव तु हत्यापवर्त्योन पदे प्रसाध्ये । उच्चिष्टं कनिष्ठेन तदा निहत्याच्छेदं वर्गेण हतोऽपवर्तः ॥ ६ ॥\\[0.3em]
कनिष्ठवर्गेण तदा निहत्योच्चिष्टं ततः पूर्ववदेव शेषम् । इत्यर्थात् ॥
}{%
Now the rule is to be accomplished: ``When the second side exists, having made [the two sides] equal (\textit{sama}), multiplying and reducing (\textit{apavartana}), the remainder (\textit{śeṣa}) [is obtained] for the root; having multiplied the residue (\textit{ucciṣṭa}) by the lesser [root] (\textit{kaniṣṭha}), dividing by the square [coefficient], the reduced [value is obtained]. Having multiplied by the square of the lesser [root], the residue --- from that, as before, the remainder.'' Thus the meaning.
}

\subsection*{Worked Example 1 (p.\ 117)}

\parallelverse{%
उदाहरणम् ॥\\[0.3em]
यस्य वर्गेऽद्वितिः पञ्चगुणा वर्गशतोनिता । मूलद्वा जायते राशिः गणितज्ञ वदाशु तम् ॥ १ ॥
}{%
Example 1: ``A number (\textit{rāśi}) whose square minus twice [itself], diminished by a hundred times five [i.e., 500], has two roots (\textit{mūla-dvā}) --- tell me that number quickly, O mathematician (\textit{gaṇita-jña}).''
}

\paralleltext{%
अत्र राशिः=या १ । अस्य वर्गेऽद्वितिः पञ्चगुणा वर्गशतोनिता यावव ५ याव १०० । अयं वर्ग इति कालकवर्गसमं हृत्वा गृहीतं कालकवर्गस्य मूलं का १ । द्वितीयपक्षस्य यावव ५ याव १०० । यावच्चावकगुणापवत्यं वर्गप्रकृत्या मूले क १० उचे २० वा क १७० उचे ३८० ।
}{%
Here the number = \textit{yā} 1. Its square minus twice, five multiplied, diminished by a hundred [times the square]: [coefficient] 5, [square] 100. This being equal to the time-square, taking the root of the time-square as \textit{kā} 1. For the second side: 5 \textit{yāva}, 100 \textit{yāva}. By \textit{yāvattāvat}-multiplier-reduction and the \textit{varga-prakṛti} method, the roots are $(10, 20)$ or $(170, 380)$.
}

\subsection*{Worked Example 2 (p.\ 117)}

\parallelverse{%
उदाहरणम् ॥\\[0.3em]
कयोः स्यादन्तरे वर्गौ वर्गयोगो ययोर्घनः । तौ राशी कथयामित्रो वहुधा बोज्ञवित्तम ॥ २ ॥
}{%
Example 2: ``Two numbers (\textit{rāśī}) whose difference of squares [equals] the sum of their squares --- tell me those numbers, O friend (\textit{mitra}), in many ways, O best of algebraists (\textit{bīja-jña-vittama}).''
}

\paralleltext{%
अत्र राशी या १, का १ । अनयोरन्तरं या १ का १ नीलकवर्गसमं हृत्वा लब्धं यावच्चावकमानं का १ नीलं ई १ । अनेन यावच्चावकद्वितीयाप्य जाती राशी का १ नीलं ई, का १ । अनयोर्वर्गयोगः काव २ नीलका २ नीलव १ । एष घन इति नीलकवर्गयनसमं हृत्वा शोधने हृते जाते प्रथमपक्षे नीलव १ नीलव १ । द्वितीयपक्षे काव २ नीलका २ ।
}{%
Here the numbers are \textit{yā} 1, \textit{kā} 1. Their difference, \textit{yā} 1 minus \textit{kā} 1, being equal to the blue-square (\textit{nīla-kṛti}), the \textit{yāvattāvat} value obtained is \textit{kā} 1, blue (\textit{nīla}) = constant 1. By this, the two \textit{yāvattāvat} values [produce] the numbers: \textit{kā} 1, blue = constant; and \textit{kā} 1. The sum of their squares: $2$ \textit{kāva}, $2$ \textit{nīla-kā}, $1$ \textit{nīla-varga}. This is the cube (\textit{ghana}), equal to the blue-square product; in equal subtraction, the first side becomes blue $1$, blue $1$; the second side: $2$ \textit{kāva}, $2$ \textit{nīla-kā}.
}

\subsection*{Rule for Three Unknowns (pp.\ 119--120)}

\parallelverse{%
(१) साध्यकल्पो यदि वर्गवर्गेषद्वित्र्यादिवर्णस्य हते समं तम् । हृत्वा पदं तस्य तदन्वपक्षे वर्गप्रकृत्यैवकदैव मूले ॥\\[0.3em]
कनिष्ठमाच्छाद्य पदेन तुल्यं उच्चिष्टं द्वितीयेन समं विधायात् ॥ ८ ॥
}{%
Rule 8: ``If the square of the square of an assumed [quantity] (\textit{sādhya-kalpa}), diminished by the square of one, two, three, etc., unknown quantities (\textit{varṇa}), having made [the two sides] equal, taking the square root (\textit{pada}) of that, then by the \textit{varga-prakṛti} method, the roots (\textit{mūle}) [are found]; covering the lesser root (\textit{kaniṣṭha-mūla}) by an equal root, making the residue (\textit{ucciṣṭa}) equal to the second [unknown]...''
}

\subsection*{Worked Example with Three Unknowns (p.\ 121)}

\parallelverse{%
उदाहरणम् ॥\\[0.3em]
त्रिकादिचतुरश्रेणां नच्छेदं क्वापि च यत् कलम् । तदेव त्रिगुणं क्षेत्रमष्टयुगच्छेदं भवेद्व्र ॥ ९ ॥
}{%
Example: ``That fractional part which, when subtracted from the series beginning with three [i.e., 3, 5, 7, ...], nowhere leaves a remainder; the same tripled, the field [becomes] one with remainder eight --- [find it], O sage (\textit{vṛddha}).''
}

\paralleltext{%
अत्र श्रेण्योन्या इः । आदि=३, चयः=२, गच्छः=या १ । आदि=३, चयः=२, गच्छः=का १ । अनयोः (क) कलं=याव १ या २, काव १ का २ । अनयोरन्यत् त्रिगुणं परस्मिं हृत्वा शोधनार्थं
}{%
Here the series (\textit{śreṇī}) is an arithmetic progression. First term = 3, common difference = 2, number of terms = \textit{yā} 1. Another: first = 3, difference = 2, terms = \textit{kā} 1. Of these, the part = \textit{yāva} 1 \textit{yā} 2, \textit{kāva} 1 \textit{kā} 2. One of these, tripled, subtracted from the other, for equalization:
}

\begin{center}
\fbox{$\displaystyle \text{न्यासः} \longrightarrow \text{याव ३ या ६} \qquad \text{काव १ का २}$}
\end{center}

\paralleltext{%
शोधने हृते पक्षौ त्रिगुणीहृत्व नव प्रक्षिप्य प्रथमपक्षस्य मूलं या ३ रू ३ । द्वितीयपक्षस्य काव ३ का ६ रू ९ । नीलकवर्गेण
}{%
The two sides being reduced by equal subtraction, having tripled, adding nine, the root of the first side: $\sqrt{3\text{yā} + 3}$. For the second side: $\sqrt{3\text{kāva} + 6\text{kā} + 9}$. By the blue-square [method]...
}

\subsection*{Rule for Equal-square Unknowns (p.\ 121)}

\parallelverse{%
(९) प्रथमपक्षस्यद्विगुणीतच्छेदस्य मूलं नीलं प्रकल्प्य तद्वर्गेण समं परं पक्षं हृत्वा पूर्वोक्तर्यस्य वासना चातिसरति ॥
}{%
``Having assumed the root of twice the divisor of the first side as the `blue' (\textit{nīla}) [unknown], having divided the other side by its square, the desire (\textit{vāsanā}) is satisfied more than previously stated.''
}

\subsection{The Method of Substitution (pp.\ 125--128)}

\parallelverse{%
अथ सूत्रं सूचयम् ॥\\[0.5em]
(१) सकृपमयकमल्पकं वा विभोगमूलं प्रथमं प्रकल्प्य । योगान्तरशेषकमाजिताच्छेदान्तरशेषकतः पदं स्यात् ॥ १२ ॥
}{%
Now the rule of instruction: Rule 12: ``Having assumed the root of the difference (\textit{vibhoga-mūla}) of [the two sides] as the first [unknown], or a small quantity, [and] the residue of the difference of sums (\textit{yoga-antara-śeṣa}) as the square [of another unknown], the root (\textit{pada}) [is obtained].''
}

\paralleltext{%
वि०---अत्र कल्प्यते योगान्तरशेषमानम् = क्षे$_1$ वर्गान्तरशेषमानम् = क्षे$_2$, वर्गयोगशेषमानम् = क्षे$_3$ विभोगमूलम् = या, योगमूलम् = का
}{%
Here we assume: residue of difference of sums = \textit{kṣe}$_1$, residue of difference of squares = \textit{kṣe}$_2$, residue of sum of squares = \textit{kṣe}$_3$; root of difference = \textit{yā}, root of sum = \textit{kā}.
}

\begin{center}
\fbox{$\displaystyle \text{विभोगः} = \text{या}^2 - \text{क्षे}_1, \quad \text{योगः} = \text{का}^2 - \text{क्षे}_1$}
\end{center}

\begin{center}
\fbox{$\displaystyle \text{अल्पराशिः} = \frac{\text{का}^2 - \text{या}^2}{2}, \quad \text{वृहद्राशिः} = \frac{\text{का}^2 + \text{या}^2 - 2\text{क्षे}_1}{2}$}
\end{center}

\paralleltext{%
अल्पराशिः (छोटसंख्या) वृहद्राशिः (महत्संख्या) इत्येतयोः सूत्रेण लब्धिः । एतत् ``प्रक्षेपकविधिः'' इति प्रसिद्धम् ।
}{%
The lesser number (\textit{alpa-rāśi}) and the greater number (\textit{vṛhada-rāśi}) are thus obtained by the rule. This is known as the ``method of insertion'' (\textit{prakṣepaka-vidhi}).
}

\subsection*{Rule 13 (p.\ 129)}

\parallelverse{%
शेषं ततः शेषकमूलमन्यच्च मूले विद्ध्वादसकृत् सम्मते ॥ ९ ॥\\[0.3em]
समाविते वर्गकृती तु यत्र तन्मूलमादाय च शेषकस्य । इष्टोद्धृतस्येर्विवर्जितस्य दलैन समं तद्विह तदैव कार्यम् ॥ १० ॥
}{%
``Then the remainder (\textit{śeṣa}), the root of the remainder-residue (\textit{śeṣaka-mūla}), and another, having found the roots repeatedly, is approved. Where the square-forms (\textit{varga-kṛtī}) are combined (\textit{samāvita}), taking its root, and of the residue (\textit{śeṣaka}), diminished by what is raised by the desired [quantity], made equal by halves --- that indeed is the operation (\textit{kārya}).''
}

\subsection*{Worked Example on the Product Method (p.\ 130)}

\parallelverse{%
उदाहरणम् ॥\\[0.3em]
तौ राशी वद यत्कृत्योः समस्तगुणयोर्युतिः । मूलद्वा स्याद्‌योगमष्टौ मूलद्वा रूपसंयुतः ॥ १ ॥
}{%
Example: ``Tell those two numbers (\textit{rāśī}) of which the sum of the product of all the multipliers of the squares [equals]; [the root of] twice the sum [is] eight, or twice the root plus unity.''
}

\paralleltext{%
अत्र राशी या १, का १ । अनयोर्वर्गयोः समस्तगुणयोर्युतिः यावव ७ काव ८ । अयं वर्ग इति नीलकवर्गसमं हृत्वा पक्षयोः कालकधनं प्रक्षिप्य नीलकपक्षस्य मूलं नी १ । परपक्षस्य याव ७ काव ८ ।
}{%
Here the numbers are \textit{yā} 1, \textit{kā} 1. Of their squares, the sum of the products of all the multipliers: 7 \textit{yāva}, 8 \textit{kāva}. This being a square, equal to the blue-square, adding the time-value to both sides, the root of the blue side is \textit{nī} 1. For the other side: 7 \textit{yāva}, 8 \textit{kāva}.
}

\subsection*{Summary Verses (p.\ 131)}

\parallelverse{%
अथ सूत्रं सूचयम् ॥\\[0.5em]
(१) सकृपमयकमल्पकं वा विभोगमूलं प्रथमं प्रकल्प्य । योगान्तरशेषकमाजिताच्छेदान्तरशेषकतः पदं स्यात् ॥ १२ ॥\\[0.3em]
वर्गान्तरशेषकसंमितिर्युता क्षेपेण हृत्वोर्ध्वितेन तै ततः । द्विधा पदं तत्पदयुगविभोगाज् मूलं युतमूलमतस्तयोरिति ॥
}{%
``Having assumed the root of the difference as the first [unknown], or a small quantity, the residue of the difference of sums as the square, the root [is obtained] from the residue of the difference of divisors. The difference of squares having a residue equal to the divisor, divided by the additive (\textit{kṣepa}), then by the quotient, [the root] divided in two; from the difference of the pair of roots, the root [of the lesser] from the sum of the roots, thus [proceeding for] those two.''
}

\subsection{Equations with Product Terms (pp.\ 134--138)}

\paralleltext{%
अयं विभागः \textit{भावितसमीकरणानि} (घातफलयुक्तानि) व्यभचरति । अत्र अज्ञातराशीनां घातफलानि सन्ति । इदं विधानं बहुवर्णमध्यमाहरणस्य विधिं विस्तारयति ।
}{%
This section deals with \textit{bhāvita} (product) equations, where the unknowns appear in product form. The method extends the techniques for multiple unknowns to handle more complex algebraic structures involving products (\textit{ghāta} or \textit{bhāvita}) of unknown quantities.
}

\parallelverse{%
अथ वा स्वर्णकल्पनायां मन्दबुद्धीनां पुनः किञ्चित् पठितः । तत् सूचयामि ॥\\[0.5em]
हरमका यस्य हृति सूक्ष्मति सोऽपि द्वितीयपदयुतातः । तेनाहतोऽस्यवर्णो रूपपदेनान्वितः कल्पः ॥ १६ ॥
}{%
``Now, for the assumption of colours (\textit{svarṇa-kalpanā}) for those of dull intellect, something further is stated; that I indicate: Rule 16: The divisor (\textit{hara}) multiplied by [some quantity] whose [result] is fine (\textit{sūkṣma}), added to the second term; having multiplied by that, the unknown (\textit{asya-varṇa}) [is obtained], combined with a root term [as] the assumption (\textit{kalpa}).''
}

\subsection*{Worked Example (p.\ 137)}

\parallelverse{%
उदाहरणम् ॥\\[0.3em]
न यदि पदं रूपाणां शिष्येत्तं तेन हरतोऽपि । नावच्छद्‌गुणो भवति न चेदेवमपि किल तथै ॥ १७ ॥
}{%
Example: ``If no root of the constants remains, then even by that [method] from the divisor; the enclosed multiplier does not become [known], and if not thus indeed, [proceed] accordingly.''
}

\paralleltext{%
हरमतिः । यस्याङ्कस्य हृतिहरमका सती सूक्ष्यतीति निश्शेषा भवति अपि च सोऽप्यङ्को हर्यो रूपपदेन च गुणितो हरमकः सन् सूक्ष्यति तदा तेनाहतोऽस्यवर्णस्तेन रूपाणां घनमूलं न लभ्यते तदा तेन रूपेषु हरतोऽपि तावद्‌गुणो भवेत् तन्मूलं रूपपदं स्यात् ।
}{%
The divisor is to be considered. That number by which the divisor, being multiplied, becomes exhausted (\textit{niśśeṣa}) --- fine (\textit{sūkṣma}) --- that number, combined with the constant term (\textit{rūpa-pada}) multiplied by the divisor, being exhausted, then having multiplied by that, the unknown; if the cube-root of the constants is not obtained by that, then by that from the constants of the divisor, a multiplier of that extent [is obtained]; its root becomes the constant term.
}

\subsection{The Bhāvita Rule (pp.\ 140--141)}

\parallelverse{%
भावितम् ॥\\[0.5em]
वर्णद्वयातिकूपेऽस्य मकरवेष्टनैकतः । एताम्या संयुताबुद्धि कल्पयो र्वैच्छया च तौ ॥ ३ ॥\\[0.3em]
वर्णद्वयो वर्गयोरन्ते क्रियते ते विपर्ययात् । समयोः पक्षयोरेकमाध्यमिकमपास्य तौ वर्णौ रूपाणि च
}{%
The section on ``Product'' (\textit{Bhāvita}): ``Two unknowns (\textit{varṇa-dvaya}), their product having a coefficient (\textit{atikuṭa}), from one side having a single enclosure (\textit{makara-veṣṭana} = bracket), having assumed any sum of their values (\textit{abhuddhi}), [proceeding] with the pair of unknowns reversed (\textit{viparyaya}), [taking] the square of two unknowns, the means (\textit{mādhyamika}) [being] equal, having removed one middle term, the two unknowns and the roots (\textit{rūpāṇi})...''
}

\subsection*{Worked Example on Bhāvita (p.\ 142)}

\parallelverse{%
उदाहरणम् ॥\\[0.3em]
चतुःषष्टिगुणयो राश्योः संयुतिर्द्वियुता तयोः । राशिघातेन तुल्येति ॥
}{%
Example: ``The sum of two numbers multiplied by sixty-four, increased by two --- that [equals] the product (\textit{rāśi-ghāta}) of those two numbers.''
}

\paralleltext{%
तत्र यथोक्तं हृते पक्षौ $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \text{या ४ का ३ रू २} \\ \text{या।का।भा १} \end{array} \right.$
}{%
There, as stated, the two sides [are]: $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \text{yā } 4 \text{, kā } 3 \text{, constant } 2 \\ \text{yā. kā. product } 1 \end{array} \right.$
}

\paralleltext{%
वर्णद्वयातिकूपेऽस्य १४ एतदेकेनाहतं हते जाते द्विके १, १४ । एते वर्णद्वया ४, ३ स्वैच्छया युते जाते यावच्चावककालकमाने ५, १८ वा १७, ५ । द्विकेन ५, ११ वा १०, ६ ॥
}{%
Of the two unknowns' coefficient 14, multiplied by one of these, results 1, 14 for the pair. These two unknowns 4, 3, combined at will, produce the \textit{yāvattāvat}-time values: $(5, 18)$ or $(17, 5)$. For the pair: $(5, 11)$ or $(10, 6)$.
}

\subsection{The Method of Mean Elimination (pp.\ 145--147)}

\parallelverse{%
माध्यमाविगमः ॥\\[0.5em]
आसन्नमानस्य हराशमानम् अप्राप्तिगुण्ये सहिते क्षेपेण । पूर्णे स्याताम् अन्तरराशिकाभ्यां तदा हरांशो भवतीश्रमस्य ॥ १ ॥\\[0.3em]
आसन्नमानयोरन्तरमेव भवेत् । अंशस्थाने सदा रूपं चिन्त्येत तथा धीमता ॥ २ ॥\\[0.3em]
सर्वेषामासन्नमानेषु हरांशो भवति षट् । तथोत्तरोत्तरं सूक्ष्मतामासन्नानि भवन्ति हि ॥ ३ ॥
}{%
The method of mean-elimination (\textit{mādhyamāvigama}): ``The divisor-measure (\textit{harā-śamāna}) of the approximate value (\textit{āsanna-māna}), added to the non-obtained quantity (\textit{aprāpti}) and the additive (\textit{kṣepa}), being complete (\textit{pūrṇa}) by two numbers with difference (\textit{antara-rāśi}), then the divisor-fraction (\textit{harāṃśa}) [gives] the effort. The difference of two approximate values alone [suffices]. The numerator [is] always unity (\textit{rūpa}) [at first], thus by the intelligent. For all approximate values, the divisor-fraction is six. Thus gradually (\textit{uttarottaraṃ}), more and more subtle (\textit{sūkṣmataraṃ}) approximate values are obtained.''
}

\paralleltext{%
अयं माध्यमाविगमः ``उभयनिषेधविधिः'' इत्यपि प्रसिद्धः । अत्र मध्यमपदं निष्कास्य समीकरणं सरलीक्रियते ।
}{%
This method of mean-elimination is also known as ``\textit{ubhaya-niṣedha-vidhi}'' (method of double suppression). Here, the middle term is eliminated and the equation is simplified.
}

\subsection{Navīna-mati-viśeṣaḥ (pp.\ 153--157)}

\paralleltext{%
अयं ``नवीनमतिविशेषः'' नामकः विभागः समीकरणसाधनायाः सन्निकृष्टमूलविधानं (आसन्नमूलम्) समाविशति । अत्र अनिर्मितवर्गसंख्यानां मूलानि सन्निकृष्टतया ज्ञातुं पुनरावृत्तिविधिः प्रदर्श्यते --- इदं न्यूटनस्य पद्धतेः पूर्वगामी अस्ति ।
}{%
The section on ``Special Methods for New Understanding'' (\textit{navīna-mati-viśeṣaḥ}) presents the \textit{āsanna-mūla} (approximate root) method --- an iterative technique for finding successively better approximations to irrational square roots, a remarkable precursor to what is now known as Newton's method.
}

\parallelverse{%
नवीनमतिविशेषः ॥\\[0.5em]
निरक्ष पदं यद्‌द्विगुणात् स्यात् फलार्थं धनार्थं तदेवात्र शेषं तदस्यम् । पदद्वयं धनं शेषहृतप्रमन्यत् फलं तद्‌भवत् शेषमूलं धनेन ॥ १ ॥\\[0.3em]
धनार्थं नवं तस्य कृत्या विहीनो गुणः शेषमकोऽस्यवर्णस्य मानम् । मूद्‌गुसेव मते यदा शेषमानं भवेद्‌द्वितीयं तदा लव्धितौ ते ॥ २ ॥
}{%
Rule 1: ``A number (\textit{nirakṣa pada}), when doubled (\textit{dviguṇāt}), becomes the root; for the fruit (\textit{phala}) [result], for wealth (\textit{dhana}) [coefficient], that same residue (\textit{śeṣa}) here --- its two terms (\textit{pada-dvayaṃ}) [are] the wealth divided by the residue (\textit{śeṣa-hṛta-pramāṇa}); the fruit [is] the root of the residue (\textit{śeṣa-mūla}) multiplied by the wealth. Rule 2: The new wealth (\textit{dhana-artha}) is diminished by nine times its square (\textit{kṛtyā vihīna}); the multiplier (\textit{guṇa}), diminished [thus], [gives] the measure (\textit{māna}) of the unknown (\textit{asya-varṇa}). When by the opinion of Mūdgu [i.e., by the method of approximation], the residue-value becomes the second [term], then the quotient (\textit{labdhi}) for both.''
}

\subsection{Worked Examples on Special Methods (pp.\ 157--159)}

\paralleltext{%
अत्र कतिपयानि उदाहरणानि दत्तानि सन्ति । एतानि विभिन्नप्रकारस्य समीकरणानि सरलतया साधयन्ति ।
}{%
Here several examples are given. These demonstrate the solution of various types of equations by simplified methods.
}

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{center}
\fbox{%
\begin{minipage}{0.9\textwidth}
\centering
\textbf{Example 1:} $\text{अ} + \text{क} = 5760$, $\text{अ} - \text{क} = \dfrac{\text{अ}}{2}$\\[0.3em]
Then अ $= 3840$, क $= 1920$ --- quickly (\textit{kṣipram}).

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Example 2:} $\dfrac{2\text{क}+1}{2} = \dfrac{7\text{क}+5}{6}$\\[0.3em]
Then क-mānam $= 1$ --- quickly.

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Example 3:} $\dfrac{\text{क}+1}{2} + \dfrac{3\text{क}-4}{5} + \dfrac{9}{8} = \dfrac{6\text{क}+7}{8}$\\[0.3em]
Then क $= 20$ --- quickly.

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Example 4:} $\left(\text{क}+\dfrac{5}{4}\right)\left(\text{क}-\dfrac{5}{4}\right) - (\text{क}+5)(\text{क}-3) + \dfrac{3}{8} = 0$\\[0.3em]
Then क $= 12$ --- quickly.
\end{minipage}%
}
\end{center}

\subsection{The Method of Approximate Roots (pp.\ 160--165)}

\paralleltext{%
अयं विभागः ``आसन्नमूल'' विधिं विस्तारेण व्याख्याति । अनिर्मितवर्गसंख्यायाः वर्गमूलं ज्ञातुं इयं पुनरावृत्तिपद्धतिः उपयुज्यते । एषा ``चक्रवालपद्धतिः'' (cyclic method) इति अपि प्रसिद्धा ।
}{%
This section explains the \textit{āsanna-mūla} (approximate root) method in detail. This iterative procedure is used to find the square root of a non-square number. It is also known as the ``\textit{cakravāla-paddhati}'' (cyclic method) of Bhāskara.
}

\parallelverse{%
आसन्नमूलानि सूत्राणि ॥\\[0.5em]
आसन्नमानस्य हराशमानम् अप्राप्तिगुण्ये सहिते क्षेपेण । पूर्णे स्याताम् अन्तरराशिकाभ्यां तदा हरांशो भवतीश्रमस्य ॥ १ ॥
}{%
Rules for approximate roots (\textit{āsanna-mūlāni sūtrāṇi}): ``The divisor-measure of the approximate value, added to the non-obtained quantity and the additive, being complete by two numbers with difference, then the divisor-fraction [gives] the effort.''
}

\paralleltext{%
अस्य गणितीयप्रक्रिया एतादृशी --- अनिर्मितवर्गसंख्या $n$ दत्ता, $\sqrt{n}$ ज्ञातुं:
}{%
The mathematical procedure is as follows: given a non-square number $n$, to find $\sqrt{n}$:
}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item Choose an initial approximation $a_1$ (the ``approximate value,'' \textit{āsanna-māna})
  \item Compute the residue (\textit{śeṣa}): $r = n - a_1^2$
  \item Form the next approximation: $a_2 = a_1 + \dfrac{r}{2a_1}$
  \item Iterate for successively better approximations
\end{enumerate}

\paralleltext{%
एषा पद्धतिः आसन्नमूलानां क्रमशः सूक्ष्मतराणि मूलानि प्रदातुं शक्नोति ।
}{%
This method can provide successively more subtle (\textit{sūkṣmatara}) approximations to the root.
}

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{center}
\fbox{%
\begin{minipage}{0.85\textwidth}
\centering
\dev{%
कल्प्यते---\\[0.3em]
(१) $0$, अ, अ', अ'', $\cdots\cdots\cdots$\\[0.2em]
(२) $1$, सो, सो', सो'', $\cdots\cdots\cdots$\\[0.2em]
(३) अ, क$_१$, क$_२$, क$_३$, क$_₄$, $\cdots\cdots\cdots$}

\vspace{0.5em}
\textit{Here:} क$_1$, क$_2$, क$_3$ --- are the approximate measures\\
of the approximate root: $\dfrac{\text{प}}{\text{ल}}$, $\dfrac{\text{प}'}{\text{ल}'}$, $\dfrac{\text{प}''}{\text{ल}''}$, etc.
\end{minipage}%
}
\end{center}

\paralleltext{%
अत्र क$_१$, क$_₂$, क$_₃$,---अयबवदासन्नमूलस्यासन्नमानानि $\frac{\text{प}}{\text{ल}}$, $\frac{\text{प}'}{\text{ल}'}$, $\frac{\text{प}''}{\text{ल}''}$ चेति ।
}{%
Here \textit{kā}$_1$, \textit{kā}$_2$, \textit{kā}$_3$ --- are the approximate values of the approximate root: $\frac{\text{pa}}{\text{la}}$, $\frac{\text{pa}'}{\text{la}'}$, $\frac{\text{pa}''}{\text{la}''}$, etc.
}

\begin{center}
\fbox{$\displaystyle \sqrt{n} = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{n+\text{अ}_₁}}{\text{सो}'} \cdot \text{प} + \text{प}}{\frac{\sqrt{n+\text{अ}_₁}}{\text{सो}''} \cdot \text{ल} + \text{ल}} = \frac{\text{प}(\sqrt{n+\text{अ}_₁}) + \text{सो}''\text{प}}{\text{ल}(\sqrt{n+\text{अ}_₁}) + \text{सो}''\text{ल}}$}
\end{center}
