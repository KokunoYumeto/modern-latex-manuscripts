\bilingualpair{%
測圓海鏡為金元數學家李冶（1192--1279）於1248年所著，是中世紀代數學之傑作。
全書共一百七十問，論一圓城與直角三角形之關係，皆以天元術立一算、
布列方程求解。此處所錄卷七至卷九包含：
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,topsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{卷七}：明前一十八問
  \item \textbf{卷八}：明後一十六問
  \item \textbf{卷九}：大斜四問、大和八問
\end{itemize}
每問依古典格式：或問述題、法曰列術、草曰詳演。
}{%
The \textit{Ceyuan Haijing} (Sea Mirror of Circle Measurements), completed in 1248
by the Chinese mathematician Li Ye (1192--1279), is one of the masterpieces of
medieval Chinese algebra. The work contains 170 problems concerning a circle
inscribed in or related to a right triangle, all solved by \textit{tian yuan shu}
(天元術). Volumes 7--9 contain:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,topsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Volume 7}: 18 problems on ``front'' configurations (明前)
  \item \textbf{Volume 8}: 16 problems on ``rear'' configurations (明後)
  \item \textbf{Volume 9}: 4 problems on great oblique (大斜) and 8 on great sums (大和)
\end{itemize}
Each problem follows the classical structure: \textbf{或問} (problem statement),
\textbf{法曰} (computational formula), and \textbf{草曰} (detailed derivation).
}

\bilingualpair{%
原書中以籌算布列，太極、天元之位以方塊（■）記中間多項式。
此處保留方塊記號，以存古法原貌。
\textbf{天元術術語對照：}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,topsep=0pt]
  \item 天元一 = Heavenly Element (the unknown $x$)
  \item 立天元一 = Let $x$ be\ldots
  \item 寄左 = Store on the left (for later)
  \item 相消 = Mutual cancellation (equating)
  \item 開平方 = Extract square root
  \item 開立方 = Extract cube root
  \item 開三乘方 = Extract fourth root
\end{itemize}
}{%
In the original text, counting-rod notation and intermediate polynomial
expressions are represented by placeholders (■); these correspond to the
``Tai Ji'' (太極) and ``Tian Yuan'' (天元) positions on the counting board.
\textbf{Key terminology in tian yuan shu:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,topsep=0pt]
  \item \textit{tian yuan yi} = the unknown $x$
  \item \textit{li tian yuan yi} = Let $x$ be\ldots
  \item \textit{ji zuo} = store on the left (for later comparison)
  \item \textit{xiang xiao} = mutual cancellation (set equal)
  \item \textit{kai ping fang} = extract square root
  \item \textit{kai li fang} = extract cube root
  \item \textit{kai san cheng fang} = extract fourth root
\end{itemize}
}

\owenCN{出南門東行七十二步有樹，出東門南行三十步見之。問答同前。}
}{%

Question and answer as before (find the city diameter).}

\bilingualpair{%
\fayueCN{倍南行以乘倍東行為平實，並二行又倍之為從，一虛隅。得城徑。}
}{%
\fayueEN{Double the southward walk and multiply by double the eastward walk

\bilingualpair{%
\tsaoyueCN{%
識別得此問名為弦外容圓，又為內率求虛積。其二行步相並為虛弦，若以相減即虛較也。
又倍東行為弦較和，倍南行即弦較較，此二數相乘則兩虛積也。若直以二行相乘，則半個虛積也。
又倍東行減於城徑，餘即二虛勾也。倍南行減於城徑則二虛股也。虛積上三事和即城徑也。
乃立天元一為圓徑，便以為三事和也。倍二行步減之，得$ \; \square \; $為黃方一，天元乘之得$ \; \square \; $為二虛積（寄左）。
然後倍東行以乘倍南行，得八千六百四十為同數，與左相消得$ \; \square \; $。
益積開平方得二百四十步，即城徑也。合問。}
}{%
\tsaoyueEN{%
By identification: this problem is called ``circle contained outside the
hypotenuse.'' The two walks summed give the empty hypotenuse; their difference
gives the empty difference. Double the eastward walk is the sum of hypotenuse
and difference; double the southward walk is their difference. The product of
these two quantities is twice the empty area. Multiplying the walks directly
gives half the empty area. Subtracting double the eastward walk from the
diameter leaves twice the empty base; subtracting double the southward walk
gives twice the empty leg. The three-element sum of the empty area is the
city diameter.

Let $x$ be the diameter, which is the three-element sum. Subtracting twice
the two walks gives $\square$ as one yellow square; multiplying by $x$ gives
$\square$ as twice the empty area (store left). Then double the eastward walk
times double the southward walk gives 8640 as the matching number. Cancelling
with the left gives $\square$. Extracting by accumulated opening of the square
gives 240 paces, the city diameter. QED.}
}

此二數相乘得下式$ \; \square \; $為半段黃方冪（寄左）。
然後立天元以自之，又二之，與左相消得$ \; \square \; $。
益積開平方得一百二十步，即半城徑也。}
}{%
\tsaoyueEN{%
Let $x$ be the radius, set aside in two positions. Adding the eastward walk
to the upper gives $\square$ as the large-difference base; adding the leg to
the lower gives $\square$ as the small-difference leg. Multiplying these two
quantities gives $\square$ as half the yellow-square power (store left).
Then squaring $x$ and doubling, cancelling with the left gives $\square$.
Accumulated opening of the square gives 120 paces, the half-diameter.}
}

gives 90 paces, the empty leg. Summing base and leg gives $\square$ as the
empty sum; adding the sum of the two walks $\square$ gives $\square$, which
is the diameter.}
}

Adding the two walks gives 240 paces, the city diameter. QED.}

\bilingualpair{%
\textbf{測圓海鏡}為金元數學家李冶（1192--1279）於1248年所著，
乃現存最早系統發展天元術之著作。
全書以十五勾股形圍繞圓城，設問一百七十道，
皆求城徑（通常二百四十步，半徑一百二十步）。
書中方塊（■）記太極、天元之位，即古代中國之多項式記法。
此雙語本並排原文與英譯，以期有助於中西數學史之研究。
}{%
\textit{Ceyuan Haijing} (Sea Mirror of Circle Measurements) was written by
the Chinese mathematician Li Ye (1192--1279) and completed in 1248. It is
the earliest extant work that systematically develops the \textit{tian yuan
shu} algebraic method for solving polynomial equations. The work uses a
sophisticated system of 15 right triangles formed by lines through and
around a circular city, with 170 problems asking for the city's diameter
(typically 240 paces, with the radius being 120 paces). The mathematical
expressions denoted by ■ represent intermediate polynomial expressions on
the counting board. This bilingual edition presents the original text
alongside its English translation to facilitate research in the history of
mathematics across Eastern and Western traditions.
}
