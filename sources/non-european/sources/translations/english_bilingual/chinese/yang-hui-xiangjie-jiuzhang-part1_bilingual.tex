\section{Introduction / 引言}

\bilingual{
  \zh{《詳解九章算法》}乃南宋楊輝所著之重要數學著作。成書於一二六一年，詳解古代《九章算術》之各題算法。
}{
  \textit{Xiangjie Jiuzhang Suanfa} (\zh{詳解九章算法}, ``Detailed Analysis of the Mathematical Methods in the Nine Chapters'') is one of the most important mathematical works of the Southern Song Dynasty, written by the mathematician \textbf{Yang Hui} \zh{楊輝} (c.~1238--1298 CE). Completed in 1261, this work provides detailed explanations and commentaries on the classic \textit{Jiuzhang Suanshu} (Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art).
}

\subsection{Historical Context / 歷史背景}

\bilingual{
  原《九章算術》成書於漢代（約公元前二百年至公元二百年），凡二百四十六題，分九章。至宋代，原文因\zh{靖康之變}（一一二七年）而散佚，傳抄訛誤，題隱法晦，學者難以入門。
}{
  The original \textit{Jiuzhang Suanshu} dates back to the Han Dynasty (c.~200 BCE--200 CE) and contains 246 mathematical problems organized into nine chapters. By the Song Dynasty, the original text had become difficult to understand due to the loss of manuscripts during the \textit{Jingkang Incident} (\zh{靖康之變}, 1127 CE), corruption of transmitted texts through repeated copying, and obscure problem statements with difficult methods.
}

\bilingual{
  \zh{輝嘗聞學者，謂九章題問頗隱，法理難明，不得其門而入。於是以答參問，用草考法，因法推類，然後知斯文非古之全經也。}
}{%
  ``I have heard scholars say that the problems in the Nine Chapters are rather obscure, and the methods difficult to understand, so one cannot find the door to enter. Therefore, using the answers to examine the questions, employing the worked solutions to investigate the methods, and following the methods to extend by analogy---only then do I know that this text is not the complete classic of antiquity.''
}

\subsection{Structure of the Work / 全書結構}

\bilingual{
  全書凡十二章：一、\zh{乘除}（四十一題）；二、\zh{方田}（三十八題）；三、\zh{粟米}（四十六題）；四、\zh{衰分}（二十題）；五、\zh{少廣}（二十四題）；六、\zh{商功}（二十八題）；七、\zh{均輸}（二十八題）；八、\zh{盈不足}（二十題）；九、\zh{方程}（十八題）；十、\zh{勾股}（三十八題）；十一、\zh{篡類}。
}{
  The original work comprised \textbf{12 chapters}: (1) Multiplication and Division --- 41 problems; (2) Field Measurement --- 38 problems; (3) Grain Conversion --- 46 problems; (4) Proportional Distribution --- 20 problems; (5) Short Width / Root Extraction --- 24 problems; (6) Commercial Calculations / Engineering --- 28 problems; (7) Equitable Distribution --- 28 problems; (8) Excess and Deficit --- 20 problems; (9) Systems of Linear Equations --- 18 problems; (10) Right Triangles --- 38 problems; (11) Classification / Compilation.
}

\subsection{Editorial Method / 編纂之法}

\bilingual{
  楊輝解題之法，分為四部：\zh{問}（設題）、\zh{答}（給出數值答案）、\zh{術}（說明一般算法）、\zh{草}（詳細逐步演算）。此四部結構，為中國數學著述之典範。
}{
  Yang Hui's approach to explaining the Nine Chapters was systematic: \textbf{Problem (\zh{問})} states the mathematical problem; \textbf{Answer (\zh{答})} gives the numerical answer; \textbf{Method (\zh{術})} states the general algorithm; \textbf{Worked Solution (\zh{草})} provides a detailed step-by-step calculation. This four-part structure became a model for Chinese mathematical exposition.
}

\subsection{The Method Source for Square Root Extraction / 開方作法本源}

\bilingual{
  楊輝所錄之\zh{開方作法本源圖}（今稱楊輝三角或賈憲三角），源出北宋賈憲\zh{（約一〇一〇至一〇七〇年）}之《釋鎖算書》。其圖列二項式係數至六次冪，用於開方運算。
}{
  One of Yang Hui's most celebrated contributions is preserving what we now call \textbf{Yang Hui's Triangle} (also known as Pascal's Triangle). In his work, he attributes this to the earlier mathematician \textbf{Jia Xian} (c.~1010--1070 CE), who called it the ``Method Source for Square Root Extraction'' (\zh{開方作法本源圖}). The triangle provides binomial coefficients used in root extraction. Yang Hui wrote: \zh{出釋鎖算書，賈憲用此術} (``This comes from the \textit{Shisuo Suan Shu}; Jia Xian used this method'').
}

\section{Original Preface / 原序}

\bilingual{
  \zh{黃帝九章古序云：國家嘗設算科取士，選九章以為算經之首，蓋猶儒者之六經，醫家之難素，兵法之孫子歟。}
}{%
  ``The ancient preface to the Yellow Emperor's Nine Chapters states: The state once established an examination in mathematics for selecting officials, choosing the Nine Chapters as the foremost mathematical classic---this is like the Six Classics for Confucian scholars, the \textit{Nanjing} and \textit{Suwen} for physicians, or the \textit{Art of War} for military strategists.''
}

\bilingual{
  \zh{昔聖宋紹興戊辰，算士榮棨謂靖康以來，罕有舊本，間有存者，狃於末習，向獲善本，得其全經，復起於學。以魏景元元年劉徽等、唐朝議大夫、行太史令上輕車都尉李淳風等注釋，聖宋右班直賈憲撰草。}
}{%
  ``In the past, during the Wuchen year of the Shaoxing reign of our Sacred Song Dynasty, the mathematician Rong Qi said that since the Jingkang period, old copies have been rare, and those occasionally surviving have been corrupted by inferior practices. Having obtained a good copy and recovered the complete classic, study was revived. The annotations were by Liu Hui et al.\ (Jingyuan first year of Wei) and by Li Chunfeng et al.\ (Senior Compiler of Tang Dynasty, Acting Grand Astrologer), while the detailed solutions were composed by Jia Xian of the Song Dynasty.''
}

\bilingual{
  \zh{輝嘗聞學者，謂九章題問頗隱，法理難明，不得其門而入。於是以答參問，用草考法，因法推類，然後知斯文非古之全經也。將後賢補贅之文，修前代已廢之法，刪立題術，又篡法問，詳著於後，儻得賢者，改而正諸，是所願也。}
}{%
  ``I have heard scholars say that the problems in the Nine Chapters are rather obscure, and the principles difficult to understand, so one cannot find the door to enter. Therefore, using the answers to examine the questions, employing the worked solutions to investigate the methods, and following the methods to extend by analogy---only then do I know that this text is not the complete classic of antiquity. Taking the supplementary texts added by later worthies and repairing the methods abandoned by earlier generations, I have reorganized the problem-methods and compiled the method-questions, recording them in detail below. If worthy scholars would correct and improve them, that is my wish.''
}

\section{Chapter 1: Multiplication and Division / \zh{乘除}}

\bilingual{
  第一章論乘除之基本運算，凡四十一題，多取\zh{方田}章及\zh{粟米}之題。
}{%
  The first chapter covers fundamental operations of multiplication and division, with 41 problems drawn mostly from the ``Field Measurement'' (\zh{方田}) chapter and grain conversion problems.
}

\subsection{Rectangular Field Method / \zh{直田法}}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{術：}以廣乘從得積。以\zh{畝法}（一畝二百四十平方步）除之。
}{%
  \textbf{Method:} Multiply the width (\zh{廣}) by the length (\zh{從}) to obtain the area (\zh{積}). Divide by the unit conversion (\zh{畝法}, 1 mu = 240 square bu).
}

\begin{biproblem}[1]
\bilingual{
  \zh{廣十五步，從十六步，問田幾何？答曰：一畝。}
}{%
  A field has width 15 bu and length 16 bu. What is the area? \textbf{Answer:} 1 mu.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  廣十五步，從十六步。積 = $15 \times 16 = 240$ 平方步。一畝 = 二百四十平方步，故為\textbf{一畝}。
}{%
  Width = 15 bu, Length = 16 bu. Area = $15 \times 16 = 240$ square bu. Since 1 mu = 240 square bu, the area is \textbf{1 mu}.
}
\end{bisolution}

\begin{biproblem}[2]
\bilingual{
  \zh{廣十二步，從十四步，問田幾何？答曰：一百六十八步。}
}{%
  A field has width 12 bu and length 14 bu. What is the area in square bu? \textbf{Answer:} 168 square bu.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  積 = $12 \times 14 = \textbf{168}$ 平方步。
}{%
  Area = $12 \times 14 = \textbf{168}$ square bu.
}
\end{bisolution}

\subsection{Square Field Method / \zh{方田法}}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{術：}方田之積，以里自乘，復以三百七十五畝乘之。
}{%
  \textbf{Method:} Square the side length to obtain the area in square li. Multiply by 375 mu per square li for conversion.
}

\begin{biproblem}[3]
\bilingual{
  \zh{方田一里，問為田幾何？答曰：三頃七十五畝。}
}{%
  A square field has side 1 li. What is its area? \textbf{Answer:} 3 qing 75 mu.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  積 = $1 \times 1 = 1$ 平方里。$1 \times 375 = \textbf{375\ 畝} = \textbf{三頃七十五畝}$。
}{%
  Area = $1 \times 1 = 1$ square li. $1 \times 375 = \textbf{375\ mu} = \textbf{3 qing 75 mu}$.
}
\end{bisolution}

\subsection{Fraction Multiplication / \zh{乘分}}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{術：}分母乘全步，并子以為實。分母相乘為法。以法除實。
}{%
  \textbf{Method:} For fractions with whole numbers: multiply each whole number by its denominator and add the numerator. Multiply these results to form the dividend (\zh{實}). Multiply the denominators to form the divisor (\zh{法}). Divide the dividend by the divisor.
}

\begin{biproblem}[Fraction Multiplication]
\bilingual{
  \zh{田廣七步、四分步之三，從十五步、九分步之五，問：為田幾何？答曰：一百二十步，九分步之五。}
}{%
  A field has width $7\,\frac{3}{4}$ bu and length $15\,\frac{5}{9}$ bu. What is the area? \textbf{Answer:} $120\,\frac{5}{9}$ square bu.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  廣：$7\,\frac{3}{4} = \frac{31}{4}$ 步。從：$15\,\frac{5}{9} = \frac{140}{9}$ 步。\\
  積 = $\frac{31}{4} \times \frac{140}{9} = \frac{4340}{36} = 120\,\frac{5}{9}$ 平方步。
}{%
  Width: $7\,\frac{3}{4} = \frac{31}{4}$ bu. Length: $15\,\frac{5}{9} = \frac{140}{9}$ bu.\\
  Area = $\frac{31}{4} \times \frac{140}{9} = \frac{4340}{36} = 120\,\frac{5}{9}$ square bu.
}
\end{bisolution}

\subsection{Division with Fractions / \zh{除分}}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{術：}以人數為法，錢數為實。有分者通之。實如法而一。
}{%
  \textbf{Method:} Use the number of people as the divisor (\zh{法}) and the amount of money as the dividend (\zh{實}). If there are fractions, convert them. Divide the dividend by the divisor.
}

\begin{biproblem}[Division with Fractions]
\bilingual{
  \zh{七人，均八錢三分錢之一，問人得幾何？答曰：人得一錢二十一分錢之四。}
}{%
  Seven people share $8\,\frac{1}{3}$ qian equally. How much does each person receive? \textbf{Answer:} $1\,\frac{4}{21}$ qian each.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  總數：$8\,\frac{1}{3} = \frac{25}{3}$ 錢。\\
  每人得：$\frac{25}{3} \div 7 = \frac{25}{21} = 1\,\frac{4}{21}$ 錢。
}{%
  Total: $8\,\frac{1}{3} = \frac{25}{3}$ qian.\\
  Each receives: $\frac{25}{3} \div 7 = \frac{25}{21} = 1\,\frac{4}{21}$ qian.
}
\end{bisolution}

\section{Chapter 2: Field Measurement / \zh{方田}}

\bilingual{
  方田章專論面積之計算與分數之運算。凡三十八題，涉及各種田形及分數四則運算。
}{%
  The Field Measurement chapter focuses on area calculations and fraction operations. It originally contained 38 problems covering various field shapes and operations with fractions.
}

\subsection{Reduction of Fractions / \zh{約分}}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{術：}可半者半之。不可半者，副置分母子之數，以少減多，更相減損，求其等也。以等數約之。（即輾轉相除之歐幾里得算法。）
}{%
  \textbf{Method:} If both numerator and denominator are even, halve them. If not, place the numerator and denominator separately and repeatedly subtract the smaller from the larger (Euclidean algorithm) until the greatest common divisor is found. Divide both by this common measure.
}

\begin{biproblem}[Reduction]
\bilingual{
  \zh{十八分之十二。問：約之得幾何？答曰：三分之二。}
}{%
  Reduce $\frac{12}{18}$. \textbf{Answer:} $\frac{2}{3}$.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  十二與十八之等數：$18 - 12 = 6$，$12 - 6 = 6$，$6 - 6 = 0$。等數 = 6。\\

\bilingual{

}{%

\subsection{Explanation of the Structure / 結構釋義}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{左袤}（\zh{積數}）：斜邊之常數項（皆為一），即$(x+a)^n$展開式之常數係數。\\
  \textbf{右袤}（\zh{隅算}）：最高次冪之係數（亦皆為一）。\\
  \textbf{中藏者}（\zh{廉}）：中間各數為\zh{廉}，即各中間項之係數。
}{%
  \textbf{Left diagonal} (\zh{左袤}): The constant terms (1, 1, 1, \ldots) represent the ``accumulated numbers'' (\zh{積數})---the coefficients of the constant term in $(x+a)^n$.\\
  \textbf{Right diagonal} (\zh{右袤}): The coefficients of the highest power---also all 1's---represent the ``corner calculations'' (\zh{隅算}).\\
  \textbf{Interior numbers} (\zh{中藏者}): The intermediate numbers are the ``flanks'' (\zh{廉})---the coefficients of the intermediate terms.
}

\vspace{0.5em}
\bilingual{
  每行給出$(x+a)^n$展開之係數：
  \begin{align*}
  (x+a)^0 &= 1 \\
  (x+a)^1 &= x + a \\
  (x+a)^2 &= x^2 + 2xa + a^2 \\
  (x+a)^3 &= x^3 + 3x^2a + 3xa^2 + a^3
  \end{align*}
}{%
  Each row gives the coefficients for expanding $(x+a)^n$:
  \begin{align*}
  (x+a)^0 &= 1 \\
  (x+a)^1 &= x + a \\
  (x+a)^2 &= x^2 + 2xa + a^2 \\
  (x+a)^3 &= x^3 + 3x^2a + 3xa^2 + a^3
  \end{align*}
}

\subsection{Application to Root Extraction / 開方之應用}

\bilingual{
  楊輝與賈憲用此係數進行開平方與開立方。開平方之術：一、置積為實。二、商估首位。三、以三角之係數為方法、廉法、隅法。四、乘減遞推。
}{%
  Yang Hui and Jia Xian used these coefficients to perform square root and cube root extraction. To extract $\sqrt{A}$: (1) Set $A$ as the dividend (\zh{實}). (2) Estimate the first digit of the root as the quotient (\zh{商}). (3) Use the coefficients from the triangle to form the ``method'' (\zh{法}), ``flank'' (\zh{廉}), and ``corner'' (\zh{隅}). (4) Multiply and subtract iteratively.
}

\subsection{Jia Xian's Generating Method / 賈憲增乘方求廉法}

\bilingual{
  \zh{增乘方求廉法：以一次乘二次，以二次乘三次，遞增一位，至求終。}
}{%
  ``To seek the flanks by augmented multiplication: multiply once by twice, multiply twice by thrice, increasing by one position each time, until the end is reached.''
}

\bilingual{
  此法由前行生成後行，相鄰兩數相加得之---即今所謂帕斯卡法則：
  \[
  \binom{n}{k} = \binom{n-1}{k-1} + \binom{n-1}{k}
  \]
}{%
  This method generates each row from the previous one by adding adjacent entries---exactly the rule we now call Pascal's rule:
  \[
  \binom{n}{k} = \binom{n-1}{k-1} + \binom{n-1}{k}
  \]
}

\subsection{The Equitable Distribution Method / \zh{均輸法}}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{術：}以一斛之運費與各縣距離相乘，加粟價，得各縣之比率。以戶數為衰，副并為法。以賦粟乘未并之衰為實。實如法而一。
}{%
  \textbf{Method:} Multiply the distance and transport cost per unit for each county. Add the grain price to obtain the proportional rate. Simplify the county household numbers to form the distribution rates. Set out the rates, combine as the divisor. Multiply the total grain tax by each uncombined rate for the dividends. Divide each by the divisor.
}

\begin{biproblem}
\bilingual{
  \zh{五縣均賦粟一萬石，每車載二十五石，行道一里，出雇錢一文。各縣到輸所遠近不等，粟價高下，欲令縣勞費相等。}
}{%
  Five counties are to share equally the tax burden of 10,000 dan of millet. Each cart carries 25 dan, and for each li traveled, the transport cost is 1 qian. The counties differ in distance from the delivery point and in grain prices. How much does each county contribute?
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  每石每里運費 = $\frac{1}{25}$ 錢。\\
  各縣率：\\
  甲：$20 + 0 = 20$；乙：$10 + \frac{200}{25} = 18$；\\
  丙：$12 + \frac{150}{25} = 18$；丁：$17 + \frac{250}{25} = 27$；\\
  戊：$13 + \frac{150}{25} = 19$。\\
  按戶數 $\times$ 率分配各縣賦額。
}{%
  Transport cost per dan per li = $\frac{1}{25}$ qian.\\
  Rates: A: $20 + 0 = 20$; B: $10 + \frac{200}{25} = 18$;\\
  C: $12 + \frac{150}{25} = 18$; D: $17 + \frac{250}{25} = 27$;\\
  E: $13 + \frac{150}{25} = 19$.\\
  Distribution proportional to households $\times$ rate.
}
\end{bisolution}

\subsection{The Pursuit Problem / 相遇問題}

\begin{biproblem}
\bilingual{
  \zh{甲發長安，五日日至齊，乙發齊，七日至長安。今乙先行二日。問：幾日相逢？答曰：二日，十二分日之一。}
}{%
  Person A departs from Chang'an and reaches Qi in 5 days. Person B departs from Qi and reaches Chang'an in 7 days. Now B has already departed 2 days ahead. \textbf{Answer:} $2\,\frac{1}{12}$ days.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  甲速 = $\frac{1}{5}$（每日行程）；乙速 = $\frac{1}{7}$。\\
  乙先行二日，已行$\frac{2}{7}$。餘程 = $\frac{5}{7}$。\\
  合速 = $\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{7} = \frac{12}{35}$。\\
  時 = $\frac{5/7}{12/35} = \frac{175}{84} = 2\,\frac{1}{12}$ 日。
}{%
  A's speed = $\frac{1}{5}$ (of the distance per day). B's speed = $\frac{1}{7}$.\\
  In 2 days, B covers $\frac{2}{7}$ of the distance. Remaining = $\frac{5}{7}$.\\
  Combined speed = $\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{7} = \frac{12}{35}$.\\
  Time = $\frac{5/7}{12/35} = \frac{175}{84} = 2\,\frac{1}{12}$ days.
}
\end{bisolution}

\subsection{The Excess and Deficit Method / \zh{盈不足法}}

\bilingual{

}

  人數 = $\frac{3+4}{8-7} = \textbf{7}$。
}{%
  Dividend = $8 \times 4 + 7 \times 3 = 32 + 21 = 53$.\\
  Divisor = $3 + 4 = 7$.\\
  Price = $\frac{53}{1} = \textbf{53}$ qian.\\
  Number of people = $\frac{3+4}{8-7} = \textbf{7}$.

\end{bisolution}

\subsection{The Fang Cheng Method / \zh{方程法}}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{術：}置行數相乘，以少減多，反覆相消，直至一未知數孤立。錢數為實，物數為法。
}{%
  \textbf{Method:} Set up the coefficients in an array. For the quantities sought, cross-multiply adjacent rows and repeatedly subtract the smaller from the larger until one variable is isolated. The money amounts form the dividend, the quantities form the divisor.
}

\begin{biproblem}[Three Grains / 三禾]
\bilingual{
  \zh{上禾三秉，中禾二秉，下禾一秉，共實三十九斗。上禾二秉，中禾三秉，下禾一秉，共實三十四斗。上禾一秉，中禾二秉，下禾三秉，共實二十六斗。問：上、中、下禾一秉各幾何？}
}{%
  3 bundles of top-grade grain, 2 bundles of medium-grade, and 1 bundle of low-grade yield 39 dou. 2 bundles of top-grade, 3 bundles of medium-grade, and 1 bundle of low-grade yield 34 dou. 1 bundle of top-grade, 2 bundles of medium-grade, and 3 bundles of low-grade yield 26 dou. \textbf{Answer:} Top $9\,\frac{1}{4}$; Medium $4\,\frac{1}{4}$; Low $2\,\frac{3}{4}$ dou per bundle.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  方程組：
  \begin{align*}
  3x + 2y + z &= 39 \\
  2x + 3y + z &= 34 \\
  x + 2y + 3z &= 26
  \end{align*}
  用方程術（高斯消去）：\\
  由一、二式得：$x - y = 5$。\\
  由二、三式得：$5x - 7y = 1$。\\
  解之：$x = 9\,\frac{1}{4}$，$y = 4\,\frac{1}{4}$，$z = 2\,\frac{3}{4}$。
}{%
  System:
  \begin{align*}
  3x + 2y + z &= 39 \\
  2x + 3y + z &= 34 \\
  x + 2y + 3z &= 26
  \end{align*}
  Using the fangcheng method (Gaussian elimination):\\
  From equations (1) and (2): $x - y = 5$.\\
  From equations (2) and (3): $5x - 7y = 1$.\\
  Solving: $x = 9\,\frac{1}{4}$, $y = 4\,\frac{1}{4}$, $z = 2\,\frac{3}{4}$.
}
\end{bisolution}

\subsection{Jia Xian's ``Unlocking'' Square Root Method / 賈憲釋鎖開平方法}

\bilingual{
  \textbf{術：}一、置積為實。二、借一算步之，超一等。三、商所得。四、以商乘下法為方法，而以除。五、除已，倍方法為廉法。六、復置下法，退位，復商。七、以商乘廉法，命實除之。
}{%
  \textbf{Method:} (1) Set the number whose root is sought as the dividend (\zh{實}). (2) Place a counting rod called the ``lower divisor'' (\zh{下法}) below the dividend, separated by one position per digit pair. (3) Estimate the first digit of the root and place it above. (4) Multiply this digit by the lower divisor to form the ``square divisor'' (\zh{方法}). (5) Subtract from the dividend. (6) Double the square divisor to form the ``flank divisor'' (\zh{廉法}). (7) Shift positions and repeat for subsequent digits.
}

\begin{biproblem}
\bilingual{
  \zh{積五萬五千二百二十五步，問為方幾何？答曰：二百三十五步。}
}{%
  An area of 55,225 square bu. What is the side of the square? \textbf{Answer:} 235 bu.
}
\end{biproblem}

\begin{bisolution}
\bilingual{
  $\sqrt{55225} = \textbf{235}$ 步。\\
  步驟：\\
  一、分節：$5\,|\,52\,|\,25$。\\
  二、首商：$2^2 = 4 \leq 5$，首商為二。\\
  三、餘：$5 - 4 = 1$，下推$52$得$152$。\\
  四、倍法：$2 \times 2 = 4$。求$d$使$(40+d) \times d \leq 152$。$d=3$：$43 \times 3 = 129$。\\
  五、餘：$152 - 129 = 23$，下推$25$得$2325$。\\
  六、倍法：$23 \times 2 = 46$。求$d$使$(460+d) \times d \leq 2325$。$d=5$：$465 \times 5 = 2325$。\\
  七、餘零。故$\sqrt{55225} = 235$。
}{%
  $\sqrt{55225} = \textbf{235}$ bu.\\
  Step-by-step:\\
  (1) Divide into groups: $5 \mid 52 \mid 25$.\\
  (2) First digit: $2^2 = 4 \leq 5$, so first digit is 2.\\
  (3) Remainder: $5 - 4 = 1$. Bring down 52: 152.\\
  (4) Double the current result: $2 \times 2 = 4$. Find $d$ such that $(40 + d) \times d \leq 152$. $d = 3$: $43 \times 3 = 129$.\\
  (5) Remainder: $152 - 129 = 23$. Bring down 25: 2325.\\
  (6) Double current result: $23 \times 2 = 46$. Find $d$ such that $(460 + d) \times d \leq 2325$. $d = 5$: $465 \times 5 = 2325$.\\
  (7) Remainder: 0. So $\sqrt{55225} = 235$.
}
\end{bisolution}

\section{Conclusion / 結論}

\bilingual{
  楊輝《詳解九章算法》為中國數學史之里程碑。其貢獻包括：\\
  \textbf{一、保存賈憲著作：}經楊輝引述，後人得知賈憲之二項式三角、開方方法及代數貢獻---否則此等著作將完全失傳。\\
  \textbf{二、系統闡述：}楊輝之四部結構（問、答、術、草）成為數學著述之典範。\\
  \textbf{三、批判性學術：}其按方法而非按主題分類問題，揭示不同應用領域中數學技巧之統一性。\\
  \textbf{四、教學創新：}楊輝明言其著述為助「不得其門而入」之學子。
}{%
  Yang Hui's \textit{Xiangjie Jiuzhang Suanfa} represents a landmark in the history of Chinese mathematics. Its contributions include:\\
  \textbf{(1) Preservation of Jia Xian's work:} Through Yang Hui's citations, we know of Jia Xian's binomial triangle, his root extraction methods, and his contributions to algebra---works that would otherwise be completely lost.\\
  \textbf{(2) Systematic exposition:} Yang Hui's four-part structure (problem, answer, method, worked solution) became a model for mathematical writing.\\
  \textbf{(3) Critical scholarship:} His classification of problems by method rather than by topic revealed the underlying unity of mathematical techniques across different application domains.\\
  \textbf{(4) Pedagogical innovation:} Yang Hui explicitly stated that he wrote to help students who ``could not find the door to enter'' the difficult Nine Chapters.
}
