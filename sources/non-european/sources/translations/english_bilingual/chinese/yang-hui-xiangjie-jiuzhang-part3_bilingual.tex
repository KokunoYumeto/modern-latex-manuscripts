\subsection{基本方法 \textbar\ Basic Methods}

\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
\textbf{【中文原文】}

\textbf{直田法}曰：廣從相乘為實，如畝法而一。

\switchcolumn[1]
\textbf{【English Translation】}

\textbf{Method for Rectangular Fields:} Multiply the width by the length to obtain the dividend; divide by the unit (240 square paces per mu).

\subsection{圓田法 \textbar\ Method for Circular Fields}

\subsection{互換法 \textbar\ Exchange Method}

\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
\textbf{【中文原文】}

\textbf{互換法}曰：以所求率乘所有數，為實；以所有率為法，實如法而一。

\switchcolumn[1]
\textbf{【English Translation】}

\textbf{Exchange Method:} Multiply the desired rate by the quantity possessed to obtain the dividend; the rate of what is possessed is the divisor; divide the dividend by the divisor.

Arrangement method: Cash, cash; goods, goods; numbers, numbers; rates, rates---arrange in corresponding columns by kind, with the original rates of each item laid out below.
\end{paracol}

\subsection{少廣法 \textbar\ Short Width Method}

\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
\textbf{【中文原文】}

少廣反合分並率。設諸分母子併而為廣，借田求縱，立少廣章，易合分之術而為之法。用副置分母自乘，以乘全步及諸子，各以本母除其子而併之，免互乘之繁。又得此術，兼助合分，使法術引伸，不亦善乎？

\textbf{少廣法}曰：列置全步及分母子，而副置分母自乘，以乘全步及子，各以本母除子，併之為法，以全步積分乘畝步為實，實如法而一。
\switchcolumn[1]
\textbf{【English Translation】}

Short Width is the inverse of combining fractions and rates. Setting up various fractional denominators and numerators combined as width, borrowing a field to find the length---this establishes the Short Width chapter, converting the technique of combining fractions into a method. Place the denominators separately, squaring them, to multiply whole paces and numerators; each divides its numerator by its own denominator and combines them---avoiding the complexity of cross-multiplication. Having obtained this method, it also assists in combining fractions, extending and developing the methods---is this not excellent?

\textbf{Short Width Method:} Arrange whole paces and fractional denominators and numerators; separately place the squared denominator to multiply whole paces and numerators; each divides its numerator by its own denominator; combine them as divisor; multiply the whole-pace integral by the mu-conversion (240) as dividend; divide the dividend by the divisor.
\end{paracol}

\subsection{盈朒適足法 \textbar\ Excess-Deficit-Exact Method}

\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
\textbf{【中文原文】}

\textbf{盈朒適足法}曰：置所出率，盈朒各居其下。副置出率，以少減多餘為約法。盈朒適足，令維乘所出率實，以盈朒之數乘人積為人實，實皆如約法而一。

其一法曰：以盈或不足之數為實，置所出率，以少減多餘為法，實如法而一約人，以適足出率乘人，為物價也。
\switchcolumn[1]
\textbf{【English Translation】}

\textbf{Excess-Deficit-Exact Method:} Set out the proposed rates, with excess and deficit placed below each. Separately set out the rates; subtract the smaller from the larger, the remainder being the simplified divisor. For excess-deficit-exact: cross-multiply the rates by the excess/deficit amounts as dividends; multiply the excess/deficit numbers by the accumulated persons as person-dividend; divide each dividend by the simplified divisor.

Alternative method: Take the excess or deficit number as dividend; set out the proposed rates, subtract smaller from larger as divisor; divide to obtain the number of people; multiply by the exact rate to obtain the price of goods.
\end{paracol}

Sell 2 cattle and 5 sheep to buy 13 pigs, with 1,000 cash remaining. Sell 3 cattle and 3 pigs to buy 9 sheep, cash exactly sufficient. Sell 6 sheep and 8 pigs to buy 5 cattle, 600 cash short. What are the prices of cattle, sheep, and pigs?

\textsf{Answer:} Cattle 1,200 cash; Sheep 500 cash; Pigs 300 cash.
\end{paracol}
\end{problemenv}

\begin{problemenv}{方程五（金銀易重）\textbar\ Equations 5 (Gold and Silver Weights)}{9 pieces of gold and 11 of silver weigh equally; exchange 1 piece, gold becomes 13 liang lighter. Find weight of each?}
\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
今有黃金九，白銀一十一，稱之重適等，交易其一，金輕十三兩。問：金、銀一枚各重幾何？
\switchcolumn[1]
9 pieces of yellow gold and 11 pieces of white silver weigh exactly the same. Exchange one piece between them, and the gold becomes 13 liang lighter. What is the weight of one piece of each?

\textit{[Let $g$ = weight of one gold piece, $s$ = weight of one silver piece. Then $9g = 11s$ and $8g + s = 10s + g - 13$, giving $g = \frac{143}{4}$ liang, $s = \frac{117}{4}$ liang.]}
\end{paracol}
\end{problemenv}

\subsection{勾股基本方法 \textbar\ Basic Right-Triangle Methods}

\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
\textbf{【中文原文】}

\textbf{勾股法}曰：勾自乘，股自乘，併之為弦實，開方除之即弦。其勾股自乘為實，如股弦較而一，加較半之即弦。
\switchcolumn[1]
\textbf{【English Translation】}

\textbf{Right-Triangle Method:} Square the base (gou), square the height (gu); combine them as the dividend for the hypotenuse (xian); extract the square root to obtain the hypotenuse. Alternatively: square the base and height as dividend; divide by the difference between height and hypotenuse; add half the difference to obtain the hypotenuse.
\end{paracol}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\begin{problemenv}{勾股一 \textbar\ Right Triangle 1}{Rope on upright post: trailing 2 chi on ground, pulled taut 8 chi from base. Find rope length?}
\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
其三，勾自乘為實，如股弦較而一，加較半之即弦。

立木垂索，委地二尺，引索斜之，拄地去木八尺。問索長幾何？

\textsf{答曰：}一十七尺。
\switchcolumn[1]
Third method: square the base as dividend; divide by the height-hypotenuse difference; add half the difference to obtain the hypotenuse.

An upright post with a hanging rope, trailing 2 chi on the ground; pull the rope taut to a point 8 chi from the post. What is the rope length?

\textsf{Answer:} 17 chi.
\end{paracol}
\end{problemenv}

\begin{problemenv}{勾股二 \textbar\ Right Triangle 2}{Wall 1 zhang high, pole leaning against it; moved past mark by 1 chi. Find distance from base?}
\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
其四，勾自乘為實，如股弦較而一，以較減之餘半之即股。

垣高一丈，倚木齊垣，木腳去本以畫記之，臥而過畫一尺。問去本幾何？

\textsf{答曰：}四丈九尺半。
\switchcolumn[1]
Fourth method: square the base as dividend; divide by the height-hypotenuse difference; subtract the difference and halve the remainder to obtain the height.

A wall 1 zhang high, a pole leaning to match it; mark where the foot stands; lay it down and it passes the mark by 1 chi. What is the distance from the base?

\textsf{Answer:} 4 zhang 9.5 chi.
\end{paracol}
\end{problemenv}

\begin{problemenv}{勾股三 \textbar\ Right Triangle 3}{Circular timber in a wall: saw cut 1 cun deep, saw path 1 chi long. Find diameter?}
\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
其五，半勾自乘為實，如半股弦較而一，加半較即弦。

圓材埋在壁中，不知大小，鋸深一寸，道長一尺。問徑幾何？
\switchcolumn[1]
Fifth method: square half the base as dividend; divide by half the height-hypotenuse difference; add half the difference to obtain the hypotenuse.

A circular timber is embedded in a wall; its size is unknown. Sawing 1 cun deep, the saw path is 1 chi long. What is the diameter?

\textit{[This is the famous ``sawing the circular timber'' problem: given chord length (1 chi) and sagitta (1 cun), find the diameter.]}
\end{paracol}
\end{problemenv}

\subsection{複雜勾股問題 \textbar\ Complex Right-Triangle Problems}

\begin{problemenv}{勾股五（竹折）\textbar\ Right Triangle 5 (Broken Bamboo)}{Bamboo 1 zhang high, top breaks and touches ground 3 chi from base. Find break height?}
\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
今有竹高一丈，末折抵地，去本三尺。問：折者高幾何？

\textsf{答曰：}四尺五十五分尺之十一。
\switchcolumn[1]
A bamboo is 1 zhang high; the top breaks and reaches the ground, 3 chi from the base. What is the height of the break?

\textsf{Answer:} $4\frac{11}{55}$ chi (or $4\frac{1}{5}$ chi).
\end{paracol}
\end{problemenv}

\begin{problemenv}{勾股六（葭生中央）\textbar\ Right Triangle 6 (Reed in Pond)}{Square pond 1 zhang; reed grows at center, protrudes 1 chi above water. Pulled to shore, just reaches. Find depth and reed length?}
\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
今有池方一丈，葭生其中央，出水一尺。引葭赴岸，適與岸齊。問：水深、葭長各幾何？
\switchcolumn[1]
A square pond 1 zhang across; a reed grows at its center, protruding 1 chi above the water. Pull the reed to the shore: it just reaches level with the bank. What are the water depth and the reed length?

\textit{[Let depth = $d$, reed = $d+1$. Then $(d+1)^2 = d^2 + 5^2$, giving $d = 12$ chi, reed = 13 chi.]}
\end{paracol}
\end{problemenv}

\begin{problemenv}{勾股七（邑方）\textbar\ Right Triangle 7 (City Dimensions)}{Square city with central gates: tree 20 paces north of north gate; walk 14 paces south, then 1775 paces west to see tree. Find city side?}
\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
今有邑方不知大小，各開中門。出北門二十步有木，出南門十四步折而西行一千七百七十五步見木。問：邑方幾何？
\switchcolumn[1]
A square city of unknown size, each side having a central gate. Go out the north gate 20 paces to a tree. Go out the south gate 14 paces, then turn west and walk 1,775 paces to see the tree. What is the side of the city?

\textit{[Let city side = $s$. Similar triangles give $\frac{s/2}{20} = \frac{1775}{s/2 + 14+20}$, yielding $s = 250$ paces.]}
\end{paracol}
\end{problemenv}

\section*{Appendix: The Nine Classification Categories \textbar\ 附錄：九類分門}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix \textbar\ 附錄：The Nine Classification Categories}

\begin{paracol}{2}
\switchcolumn[0]
\textbf{【中文原文】}

楊輝纂類將《九章算術》二百四十六問重新分為九類：

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{乘除}——基本田畝計算與單位換算，四十一問。
\item \textbf{分率}——分數運算（乘分、除分），九問。
\item \textbf{合率}——分數併合之術，包括少廣開方，二十問。
\item \textbf{互換}——物品比例交換，包括粟米換算與賦稅問題，五十六問。
\item \textbf{衰分}——按比例分配，包括均輸法，一十八問。
\item \textbf{疊積}——土方工程與立體體積計算，二十七問。
\item \textbf{盈不足}——雙假設法（盈朒術），十一問。
\item \textbf{方程}——線性方程組，以算籌布列矩陣求解，二十問。
\item \textbf{勾股}——勾股定理及相關幾何方法，三十八問。
\end{enumerate}
\switchcolumn[1]
\textbf{【English Translation】}

Yang Hui's \textit{Reclassification} (\textit{Zuanlei}) reorganizes the 246 problems from the \textit{Nine Chapters} into nine categories based on their mathematical methods:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Chéngchú (Multiplication \& Division)} -- 41 problems dealing with basic field measurements and unit conversions.
\item \textbf{Fēnlǜ (Fractional Rates)} -- 9 problems on operations with fractions (multiplication, division, and comparison).
\item \textbf{Hélǜ (Combined Rates)} -- 20 problems on combining fractional quantities, including the ``short width'' (shǎo guǎng) root-extraction method.
\item \textbf{Hùhuàn (Exchange \& Barter)} -- 56 problems on proportional exchange of goods, including grain conversion and taxation.
\item \textbf{Shuāifēn (Proportional Distribution)} -- 18 problems on distributing quantities proportionally, including the ``equitable transport'' (jūnshū) method.
\item \textbf{Diéjī (Stacked Accumulations)} -- 27 problems on volumes of earthworks and other three-dimensional calculations.
\item \textbf{Yíngbùzú (Excess \& Deficit)} -- 11 problems solved by the double false position method.
\item \textbf{Fāngchéng (Simultaneous Equations)} -- 20 problems on systems of linear equations, solved by matrix methods on counting boards.
\item \textbf{Gōugǔ (Right Triangles)} -- 38 problems applying the Pythagorean theorem and related geometric methods.
\end{enumerate}
\end{paracol}
