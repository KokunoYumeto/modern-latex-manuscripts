# Arabic translations of Chinese mathematical classics — Claude output

17 TeX files + 17 compiled PDFs (379 pages total).

These are Arabic translations of the major Chinese mathematical works, produced to complete
the trilingual (Chinese-English-Arabic) reader set.

## Works covered
- Nine Chapters (Jiuzhang Suanshu) vols 1-3, 4-6, 7-9 — 67pp
- Li Ye, Ceyuan Haijing vols 1-3, 4-6, 7-9, 10-12 — 104pp
- Qin Jiushao, Shuxue Jiuzhang fasc 1, 2, 3-4, 4, 5-6, 7-9 — 127pp
- Yang Hui, Xiangjie Jiuzhang parts 1, 2, 3 — 74pp
- Sunzi Suanjing — 7pp

Each PDF has parallel Chinese original + Arabic translation columns.
