Chinese Mathematical Classics - Arabic Translation Completion Note

This update adds a complete Arabic translation-draft surface for the current
Chinese mathematical classics corpus:

- Nine Chapters / Jiuzhang Suanshu, vols. 1-9
- Li Ye, Ceyuan Haijing, vols. 1-12
- Qin Jiushao, Shuxue Jiuzhang, fascicles 1-9
- Sunzi Suanjing
- Yang Hui, Xiangjie Jiuzhang, parts 1-3

The five `30-* Arabic Translation` PDFs are the reader-facing files. The
`80-01` ZIP preserves the lower-level TeX/PDF files used to assemble them,
including overlap and source-check artifacts that are useful for repair.

The translations are working drafts: readable and inspectable, but not a
proofed critical Arabic edition.
