                                                     ・ Yan shu (衍数) — Product of all ding numbers

                                                       (the LCM)
                                                     ・ Qi shu (奇数) — Remainder of yan shu divided
                                                       by ding shu

                                                     ・ Yong shu (用数) — Key constructed number for
