九歸除法門・Division by the Nine Returns

     實少法多從法歸，實多滿法進前居，                     When the dividend is small and divisor
     常存除數專心記，法實相停九十餘。                     large, follow the return method;
     但遇無除還頭位，然將釋九數呼除，                     When the dividend fills the divisor, advance
     流傳故泄真消息，求一穿韜總不如。                     to the next position;
                                          Keep the divisor always in mind;
九歸除法者，以九歸訣為除法之要術也。實少於法，則從                 When divisor and dividend match, the re-
法歸之；實多於法，則進位而居之。常記除數於心，法實                 mainder is in the nineties.
相等則餘九十餘。凡二十九問。                            When a digit cannot be divided, return to
                                          the head position;
                                          Then use the nine-explanations method to
                                          call the division;
                                          This tradition reveals the true secret;
                                          The “seeking unity” method cannot com-
                                          pare.

                                     The “nine returns” division uses the division rhymes
                                     given in the General Principles. When the dividend
                                     is smaller than the divisor, use the “return” method;
                                     when the dividend is larger, advance the quotient.
                                     The practitioner must memorize the divisor and keep
                                     it in mind. When the division is exact, the remainder
                                     is in the nineties. If a digit cannot be divided, return
                                     to the head position and use the “nine explanations”
                                     method. This method, though not the orthodox ap-
                                     proach (商除), is easier for beginners.

                                                              算, Japanese mathematics).
