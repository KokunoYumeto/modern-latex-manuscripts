\noindent\small\textbf{Status of this leaf.} Names, Ptolemaic identifications, and \textbf{coordinates}
are complete, collated against Nallino's critical edition (Pars II, 1907, regional table, regions 1--12).
Longitude is reckoned from the Fortunate Isles; latitudes are northern throughout. Two minute-cells read
anomalously in the print (Gallia Lugdunensis 48°\,2′, Gallia Belgica 47°\,1′) and are reproduced exactly
as printed, flagged \CHECK. For Germania Magna the codex longitude is 36° where Nallino adopts 34°
(his note). This is leaf~1 of the regional block; al-Battānī's separate \emph{city} gazetteer
(\textit{Tabula latitudinum et longitudinum urbium}, $\approx$270 localities) follows in the same source
and is the next geographical work.
