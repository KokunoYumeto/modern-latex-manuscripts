Luc Illusie — Complexe cotangent et déformations I–II: French and English working editions

Authors/Creators

  • Illusie, Luc

Description

Luc Illusie, Complexe cotangent et déformations I–II. This is the dedicated working-edition lineage for Springer LNM 239 (DOI 10.1007/BFb0059052) and LNM 283 (DOI 10.1007/BFb0059573).

Start with english.pdf, the cumulative standalone English edition. french.pdf is the separate cumulative French edition. Their stable cumulative masters are en.tex and fr.tex. There is no combined, side-by-side, or interleaved reader.

Each visible reader opens on a clean author-and-work cover containing only Luc Illusie and the language-appropriate work title. The LNM masthead, series number, editors, Springer device, and publisher imprint remain only in the internal reconstructed source-cover witness.

Current admitted coverage is LNM 239 physical pages 1–23. The front matter is complete. Chapter I section 1.1 is present through printed page 5: the simplicial indexing category; simplicial, cosimplicial, mixed, diagonal, constant, and augmented objects; equations (1.1.1)–(1.1.6.2); the representable coequalizer pi_0 and its adjoint triple; homotopy and its native-TeX diagrams; the homotopy category Hotsimpl(C); homotopy equivalence; the cosimplicial analogue Hotcosimpl(C); relative homotopy; the fibre categories Hotsimpl(E)_S; base-change homotopies and functors; and the fibred or cofibred total category Hotsimpl(E_{/B}). The final B-functor statement continues on physical page 24 / printed page 6 after induit un foncteur / induces a functor and is not completed by inference. Direct 1,100- and bounded 5,000-dpi-equivalent inspection controls p.23; no OCR was generated. The internal diplomatic layer preserves (Giraud [12] 1.16) . and l'exten-/sion; corrected French removes exactly that pre-full-stop space and joins the carrier split. The direct forms fg-homotopie, hg-homotopie, Simpl(p), Fl Delta, and Hotsimpl(E_{/B}) are deliberately preserved. Bibliography key 12 remains pending terminal-bibliography review. LNM 283 has not started.

The first p.23 Poppler high-detail attempt produced ten identical blank strips after a memory-allocation diagnostic; they remain preserved and noncontrolling. P23r1 was rejected for materially compressed vertical density. P23r2 repaired density but was superseded before admission to add the stable component-formula anchor and exact operator spacing. P23r3 is admitted after clean three-pass builds, 300/600-dpi lead review, and zero-raster checks. All predecessors remain preserved no-overwrite evidence.

source.zip contains the active editable TeX, internal diplomatic French evidence, semantic and reference tables, decisions, and compact validation receipts. The Springer scans and direct page images are identified by exact hashes but are not redistributed. This is an incomplete scholarly working edition, not a critical edition, peer review, mathematical certification, or claim of redistribution permission for the underlying publication.

Notes
