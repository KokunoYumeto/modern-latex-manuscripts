\phantomsection\hypertarget{illusie:I:ch1:s1:1:p1}{}%
Let $\Delta$ denote the category whose objects are the nonempty finite totally
ordered sets and whose arrows are the order-preserving maps. For
$n\in\mathbb{N}$ and $i\in[0,n]$, denote by

\phantomsection\hypertarget{illusie:I:ch1:s1:1:d1}{}%
\[
  \delta_i^n\; (\text{or }\delta_i):[0,n-1]\longrightarrow[0,n]
\]
the order-preserving injection that omits $i$, and by

\phantomsection\hypertarget{illusie:I:ch1:s1:1:d2}{}%
\[
  \sigma_i^n\; (\text{or }\sigma_i):[0,n+1]\longrightarrow[0,n]
\]
the order-preserving surjection that repeats $i$.

\phantomsection\hypertarget{illusie:I:ch1:s1:1:p2}{}%
Let $C$ be a category. A simplicial object of $C$ is a functor from
$\Delta^{\circ}$ to $C$; a morphism of simplicial objects of $C$ is a morphism
of the corresponding functors. If $X$ is a simplicial object of $C$, for
$n\in\mathbb{N}$ we write $X_n$ in place of $X([0,n])$, and we sometimes
denote simply by $d_i^n$ (or $d_i$) the $i$-th face map $X(\delta_i^n)$ and by
$s_i^n$ (or $s_i$) the $i$-th degeneracy map $X(\sigma_i^n)$. We denote by

\phantomsection\hypertarget{illusie:I:ch1:s1:1:eq1}{}%
\[
  \makebox[\linewidth][s]{\text{(1.1.1)}\hfill$\operatorname{Simpl}(C)$\hfill\phantom{\text{(1.1.1)}}}
\]
the category of simplicial objects of $C$. Analogously, for every integer
$p\geq1$ we define the category $p\text{-}\operatorname{Simpl}(C)$ of
$p$-simplicial objects of $C$, that is, functors from $(\Delta^p)^{\circ}$ (*)
to $C$. Dually, we define the category $\operatorname{Cosimpl}(C)$ of
cosimplicial objects of $C$, that is, functors from $\Delta$ to $C$, and, for
$p\geq1$, the category $p\text{-}\operatorname{Cosimpl}(C)$ of
$p$-cosimplicial objects of $C$, that is, functors from $\Delta^p$ (*) to $C$.
