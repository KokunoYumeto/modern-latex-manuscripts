# Luc Illusie — Complexe cotangent et déformations I–II

This is the dedicated archive lineage for Luc Illusie's two-volume
Complexe cotangent et déformations (LNM 239 and 283).

Start with english.pdf, the cumulative standalone English edition. The
separate cumulative French edition is french.pdf. Their stable cumulative
masters are en.tex and fr.tex. No combined, side-by-side, or interleaved
reader is present.

Each reader opens on a clean author-and-work cover: Luc Illusie and the
language-appropriate title only. The visible covers contain no LNM masthead,
series number, editors, Springer device, or publisher imprint. The historical
source-cover reconstruction remains internal diplomatic evidence.

Current admitted scope is LNM 239 physical pages 1–22. The front matter is
complete. Chapter I section 1.1 is present through printed page 4: simplicial,
cosimplicial, mixed, diagonal, constant, and augmented objects; equations
(1.1.1)–(1.1.5.1); the representable coequalizer and adjoint triple for pi_0;
homotopy, the category Hotsimpl(C), homotopy equivalence, the cosimplicial
analogue Hotcosimpl(C), and the opening of relative homotopy. The final
sentence continues on physical page 23 / printed page 5 after French la
relation d'équivalence / English the equivalence relation. LNM 283 has not
started.

The internal diplomatic French preserves the physical carrier split
homo-/topisme. The French edition joins it as homotopisme; p.22 has zero
textual correction. The direct form Simp(p) is deliberately preserved.

source.zip contains the active editable TeX, the internal diplomatic French
evidence, semantic/reference tables, decisions, and compact validation
receipts. Springer authority scans and direct page images are identified by
hash but are not redistributed.

This is an immediate working checkpoint, not a complete translation, critical
edition, peer review, mathematical certification, or claim of permission to
redistribute the underlying Springer publication.
