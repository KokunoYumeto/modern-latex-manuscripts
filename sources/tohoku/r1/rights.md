# Rights and provenance

The controlling images are official J-STAGE scans of Tohoku Mathematical
Journal (Second Series), volume 9 (1957). J-STAGE marks the articles as free
access. Free access is not treated here as an independently verified license
grant for unrestricted redistribution.

The complete French TeX and local French readers are preserved as private
archival/scholarly working artifacts. A public French projection requires a
separate rights determination. Source scans and private local paths are never
bundled into a public package by default. English readers and machine indexes
also require the ordinary final privacy, provenance, validation, and archive
gates before release.

Public-facing provenance should use semantic aliases and the two article DOIs,
not private filesystem paths.
