# Dirichlet Round 22 - XXXVII-XLI plus volume-I errata

Sequential continuation after Band II item XXXVI. This package completes the remaining correspondence/list/errata tail of Werke Band II after the Gauss correspondence.

Completed this round:

- XXXVII. Briefwechsel zwischen G. Lejeune Dirichlet und Leopold Kronecker, mitgetheilt von Ernst Schering.
- XXXVIII. Auszug aus dem Briefwechsel zwischen Alexander von Humboldt und G. Lejeune Dirichlet.
- XXXIX. Bemerkung zu Dirichlet's Werken Bd. I. S. 348. Z. 7, von Herrn R. Dedekind.
- XXXX. Bemerkung des Herrn Heinrich Weber.
- XXXXI. Verzeichniss der Uebersetzungen Dirichlet'scher Abhandlungen, die nicht in die Gesammelten Werke aufgenommen worden sind.
- Druckfehler-Verzeichniss zum 1. Bande.

The layout uses compact paths for Windows path-length safety. Original-language and English reading PDFs are typed TeX output. Source scans are retained only as witnesses; no page screenshot is used as a substitute for mathematical notation, tables, symbols, or formula systems in the reading PDFs. Planetary signs, the Dedekind note, the Weber note, the translation list, and the volume-I errata are all reset directly in TeX.

Top-level layout:

- new/orig/pdf and new/orig/tex
- new/en/pdf and new/en/tex
- src/pages, src/scans, src/text, src/notes
- checks
- cum/pdf and cum/index
