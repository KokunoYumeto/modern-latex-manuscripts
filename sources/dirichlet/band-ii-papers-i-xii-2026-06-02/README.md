# Dirichlet Round 09 - Rigid Body Motion and Gauss Reciprocity

This package continues the Dirichlet Band II sequence after Papers IX-X and adds Papers XI-XII.

Completed in this round:

- XI. Über die Bewegung eines festen Körpers in einem incompressibeln flüssigen Medium.
- XII. Über den ersten der von Gauss gegebenen Beweise des Reciprocitätsgesetzes in der Theorie der quadratischen Reste.

The package keeps separate paper-level TeX/PDF outputs and also includes cumulative original-language and English PDFs through Papers I-XII.

The visible PDFs are clean reading copies. Scan and render evidence are included as auxiliary material, not inserted into the reading PDFs.

## Main files

- `new_work_this_round/original_language/pdf/` - German scan-checked PDFs for Papers XI-XII.
- `new_work_this_round/translation/english/pdf/` - English translation PDFs for Papers XI-XII.
- `new_work_this_round/original_language/tex/` - German TeX source.
- `new_work_this_round/translation/english/tex/` - English TeX source.
- `new_work_this_round/scan_evidence/` - extracted Werke Band II scan pages used for checking.
- `new_work_this_round/render_checks/` - rendered PNG checks of the new PDFs.
- `cumulative_current/combined_pdf/` - combined PDFs through Papers I-XII.

## Source-control note

The selected reader remains useful as a navigation aid, but these papers were rebuilt against the Werke Band II scan where the selected reader was incomplete or formula-unreliable.
