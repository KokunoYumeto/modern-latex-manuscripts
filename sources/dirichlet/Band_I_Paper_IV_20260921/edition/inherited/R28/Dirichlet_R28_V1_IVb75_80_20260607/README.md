# Dirichlet R28 — Vol. I Paper IVb pp. 75--80

Sequential continuation of Dirichlet Werke Band I Paper IV:
`Recherches sur les diviseurs premiers d'une classe de formules du quatrième degré`.

This package completes printed pages 75--80 only. It does not close Paper IV; printed pages 81--98 remain open for the next pass.

Layout:
- `src/` source witnesses and source witness PDF.
- `new/orig/` French TeX/PDF.
- `new/en/` English TeX/PDF.
- `cum/` cumulative PDFs through I--III + IVa + IVb.
- `idx/` machine-readable unit, formula, symbol, scan, and audit inventories.
- `audit/` logs and recursive audit note.
- `checks/` render checks.
