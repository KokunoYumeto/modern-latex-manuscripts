# Dirichlet: Recherches sur les diviseurs premiers d’une classe de formules du quatrième degré

Complete French transcription, English translation and separate editorial apparatus for Paper IV in *Gesammelte Werke*, Band I (1889), printed pp. 63–98. P. G. Lejeune Dirichlet is the author of the mathematics. The reprint identifies the earlier publication in Crelle’s *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik*, volume 3, pp. 35–69. This edition follows the attached 1889 witness; it does not establish a critical text across editions.

The French and English readers each contain all 34 author pages, pp. 65–98. The title leaf and blank verso, pp. 63–64, are preserved separately. Paper V is excluded. Wording, mathematical tokens, paragraph structure, source page boundaries, footnote and headings are retained; line wrapping is re-typeset. Printed anomalies remain in the text and are discussed only in the five-page apparatus.

This is an AI-assisted transcription and translation. ChatGPT Pro produced and revised the text against source images and performed the final fresh source pass. Codex preserved the returned files, checked archive and evidence bindings, independently rebuilt all three readers, compared text and reviewed every rendered page. It is not independent human peer review or mathematical certification. Retained earlier drafts and their old success labels are historical evidence, not the current edition.

The final source pass made 21 further corrections, including the printed b on p. 89, absent terminal product ellipses on pp. 72–73, and explicit conditional clauses in English. The contextual delta on p. 89 is now discussed only as an editorial inference. The source’s readings on pp. 66, 70, 77, 82, 85 and 89 remain visible.

## Rebuild and verify

Use XeLaTeX with the packages and TeX-distributed Latin Modern/Computer Modern fonts named in each `main.tex`. Run `python build_public_readers.py` from this directory; it copies editable inputs to a new build directory and runs two passes for each reader without changing the preserved sources. No font files are redistributed. PDF byte identity can vary with the TeX installation.

`PUBLIC_MANIFEST.tsv` binds the public package bytes. `PUBLIC_PROVENANCE.json` records their relationship to the saved full production return. All production-state files are preserved, except private local paths in one intake receipt are replaced by an explicit omission marker. Original source, reader and audit bytes are otherwise unchanged. The original manifest’s hash for that receipt describes the private original; the public manifest describes this redistribution. Historical production tools are retained for provenance and should not be used to regenerate the final text.
