\par\indent
It was seen above $(\vartheta')$ that, when $u$ is even, $a$ is or is not a biquadratic
residue with respect to $p$, according as one has $\leg{t}{a}=1$, or $\leg{t}{a}=-1$.
On the other hand, it follows from what precedes, that one has, in this same case,
$\leg{\psi s\pm t}{a}=1$. One concludes from this that, if $u$ is even, $a$ is or is not a biquadratic
residue with respect to $p$, according as one has:
\[
\leg{t}{a}=\leg{\psi s\pm t}{a}\quad\text{or}\quad
\leg{t}{a}=-\leg{\psi s\pm t}{a}.
\]
