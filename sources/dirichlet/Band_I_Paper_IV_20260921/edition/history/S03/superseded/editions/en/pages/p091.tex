The numbers $t+\psi s$, $t-\psi s$ cannot both be divisible by $b$,
for then their half-sum, which is $t$, would also be so, which was seen above
to be impossible. If one denotes by $t\pm\psi s$ that one of these two numbers which is

that $\leg{t\mp\psi s}{b}=1$. Indeed $t\mp\psi s$ is composed of two factors $E$ and $K$ of which
the first $E$ divides $m^2$, the second $g^2+bh^2$.

\par\indent
Now, one ascertains, as in the case of $\varphi$ not divisible by $b$, that $E$
is a square, so that one has $\leg{E}{b}=1$. One also has $\leg{K}{b}=1$, $K$ being a divisor
of $g^2+bh^2$. One concludes from this by multiplying:
\[
\leg{EK}{b}=\leg{t\mp\psi s}{b}=1.
\]
