# Printed p. 66

Authority: exact-scope leaf 4; full-PDF page 83; `qa/source/p066_authority.png`.

**Unqualified converse retained.** The second paragraph asserts that solvability for every prime factor of B implies solvability for B. That unrestricted assertion is reproduced in both editions. As an editorial check, A=3 and B=9 give a counterexample: x^4≡3 (mod.3) is solvable, whereas fourth powers modulo 9 are 0, 1, 4, 7, never 3. No qualification is silently inserted.
