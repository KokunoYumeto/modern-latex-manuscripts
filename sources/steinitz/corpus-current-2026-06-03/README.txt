Steinitz_16 corpus package.

Short-path, chronological layout. Reader files are separated by work and language:
- de: German TeX/PDF/TXT
- en: English TeX/PDF/TXT
- source/scans: source witness material
- qa: rendered PDF check images

New in this package:
- 01_1894_dissertation: German and English files for the opening tranche of the 1894 dissertation, through Section 7.
- 06_1905_polyhedron_source: corrected local source slice for the 1905 one-sided-polyhedron paper, printed pp. 281-307.
