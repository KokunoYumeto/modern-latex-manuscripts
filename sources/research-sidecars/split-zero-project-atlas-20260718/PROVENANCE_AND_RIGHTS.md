# Provenance and rights

## Public scope

This release contains:

- mathematics and conceptual direction supplied by the repository maintainer;
- generated formalizations, calculations, visualizations, and explanatory
  project prose;
- exact Lean and Python checks;
- the source-free Part 8-C2B residual-Niemeier facade, checker, and finite
  replay/status/boundary ledgers;
- rights-screened derivatives and finite data;
- citations and short factual descriptions of external literature.

The release is licensed CC BY 4.0. External papers, software, and source
materials retain their original licences and copyrights. The archives cite or
hash-pin those sources; they do not relicense them.

## JT boundary

Jacolm Tobley (JT) is credited as a researcher and project relationship. This
does not make the repository owner of JT-authored work.

The release excludes:

- raw JT transcripts, Discord exports, private chat, and source dumps;
- direct JT prose not separately cleared for publication;
- protected or irrelevant personal material;
- model prose placed in an `author_jt_verbatim` field;
- Kimi, Claude, GPT, Codex, or other assistant prose represented as JT source.

Where a mixed transcript informed a check, the public file contains only the
generated mathematical reconstruction, exact finite data, or independently
stated theorem. The private provenance ledger retains the source distinction.

The Part 8-C2B bundle is a curated generated audit with no direct JT witness
and no JT theorem entry. It excludes the raw Branch32-A compendium, master
inventories, model-reasoning spans, private run material, and local copies of
the cited literature. Its public facade corrects the source's literal-equality
wording to lattice isometry.

Packet 201 also preserves a bounded Claude N16b note, checker, and output as
hash-pinned adjacent model inputs. They are included so a reader can audit the
correction history. They are not classified as JT-authored prose and are not
the proof authority for the release theorem statements.

## Authorship classes

The release manifest uses these classes:

- `generated_project_work`: produced by project AI/tooling and checked in the
  repository;
- `repository_maintainer_work`: supplied or directed by the repository
  maintainer;
- `external_literature_reference`: cited third-party work, not republished;
- `jt_research_relationship`: credit/provenance relationship without transfer
  of source ownership;
- `historical_exploration`: preserved superseded material, not a current claim.

## Mathematical status

Every central item is classified as an exact theorem, checked calculation,
generated construction, obstruction, diagnostic, evidence-backed open problem,
investigation prompt, or historical exploration. The class is part of the
claim. It must not be removed when quoting a result.

In particular:

- the exponential C6 bridge is exact as a generated finite construction, not
  source-selected;
- the kernel-collapse bridge is a diagnostic counterexample, not a candidate;
- common output series do not establish an internal object morphism;
- the physical Wilson half-index identification remains open;
- the predatum/complementary-nullity programme has exact local blind-plane,
  ternary-recovery, and K4/XOR support mechanisms;
- the full ordered source datum canonically selects the projective mixed-mock
  ray `[c_2]` and its arithmetic odd projector, but no algebra-valued natural
  intertwiner from the predatum/Jordan object has been proved;
- the cited `L=4` cancellation lemma does not directly establish aggregate
  cancellation for the displayed `L=8` Sigma(2,3,5) datum;
- the cowlick/sedenion packet exactly proves a rank-12, nullity-4 finite
  left-multiplication calculation, but does not claim that the hairy-ball
  theorem forces exactly two zeros, that `S^15` lacks nonvanishing fields, or
  that an Adams eight-field bound is the same object as any ghost-kernel
  dimension;
- the coordinate half-turns form a Klein four-group; they are not promoted to
  canonical zeta flows or to a global uniqueness theorem.
- the residual Part 8-C2B construction exactly verifies an even unimodular
  rank-24 extension with no new roots among its 72 glue classes; Niemeier
  identification, theta modularity, and the geometric `g4` theorem retain
  their stated literature dependencies, while level-six/`R=107` compatibility
  and “instanton-sensitive datum” remain non-theorem boundaries.

## Public proof closure

The release is designed to stand without access to the private repository.
Packet-number references in the public navigation files, packet TeX, and
machine-readable packet extracts are scanned against the compendium and all
three working-text representations. Named proof members are checked against the
formal archive. This broader gate adds the previously omitted Packet 076
dependency of Packet 077 and rejects missing Packet 198--202 proof material,
the adjacent Packet 201 audit trail, the Packet 201 Lean facade, the revised D3
handoff, or the public C6/Sigma atlas. The public charter integration preserves project
definitions and provenance without publishing its mixed source note or any
JT-authored corpus dump. External mathematical theorems remain normal
literature citations and are not republished.

The same gate requires all six Part 8-C2B files under one formal-archive
directory and the human facade in the results compendium. A partial facade-only
publication is rejected.

## Reuse

When reusing a theorem, dataset, or visual, cite the concept DOI plus the stable
ID or packet number. When reproducibility matters, also cite the version DOI and
the SHA-256 digest from `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
