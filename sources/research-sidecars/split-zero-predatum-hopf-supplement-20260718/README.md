This bounded supplement preserves the 17 July 2026 N16-N18 working-note
sequence and its executable checks for the evolving split-zero research
sidecar. It is additive to the Project Atlas record under concept DOI
`10.5281/zenodo.20822444`; the Project Atlas PDF remains the recommended
reader-facing entry point.

## Contents

- `k4_sphere_definer_note16_20260717.md`: K4/sphere working note, including
  the later N16b errata section.
- `k_charter_note17_20260717.md`: terminology and typing charter. It explicitly

  title date is historical; later N16-N18 entries occur near the end.
- `verify_*.py` and `*_output.txt`: executable symbolic checks and recorded
  outputs for N16, N16b, its errata, and N18.
- `CODEX_RERUN_20260718.txt`: a fresh UTF-8 rerun of all four scripts on this
  machine.
- `AUDIT_STATUS.md`, `MANIFEST.csv`, and `SHA256SUMS.txt`: scope, inventory,
  and integrity information.
