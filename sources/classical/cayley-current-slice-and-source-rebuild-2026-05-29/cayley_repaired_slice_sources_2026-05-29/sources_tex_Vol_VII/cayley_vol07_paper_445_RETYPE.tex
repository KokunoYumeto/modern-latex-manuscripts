S_y, & T_y, & U_y, & V_y\\
S_z, & T_z, & U_z, & V_z\\
S_w, & T_w, & U_w, & V_w
\end{vmatrix} = 0,
\]
which is the Hessian; and the equation may equally be regarded as a Jacobian equation
\[
J(S, T, U, V) = 0,
\]
a form which puts in evidence the properties just referred to.

\subsection*{Quartic Surface through three given Lines.}

\noindent\textbf{110.}\ To find the equation to the quartic surface through the three lines

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
