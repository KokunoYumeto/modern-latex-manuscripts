\paragraph{8.}
Substituting   in  the  equation  $D^2 U - \mathrm{II} \cdot DU = 0$,  we   obtain   as   the   conditions  of
a  five-pointic  contact
\begin{align*}
&-(m-2)\, \partial_1^2 U + P \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}\partial_1^2 U = 0, \\
&-\tfrac{1}{3}\bigl[(m-1)\, \partial_1^3 + 3(m-2)\, \partial_1 \partial_2\bigr] U
   + P \cdot \tfrac{1}{6}(\partial_1^3 + 3\partial_1 \partial_2) U
   + \partial_1 P \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} \partial_1^2 U = 0, \\
&-\tfrac{1}{12}\bigl[(m-1)(\partial_1^4 + 6\partial_1^2 \partial_2) + (m-2)(4\partial_1 \partial_3 + 3\partial_2^2)\bigr] U \\
&\quad + P \cdot \tfrac{1}{24}\bigl[\partial_1^4 + 6\partial_1^2 \partial_2 + 4\partial_1 \partial_3 + 3\partial_2^2\bigr] U
   + \partial_1 P \cdot \tfrac{1}{6}(\partial_1^3 + 3\partial_1 \partial_2) U
   + \tfrac{1}{2}\partial_2 P \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}\partial_1^2 U = 0;
\end{align*}
or  reducing
\[
P = 2(m-2),
\]
\[
\partial_1 P = \tfrac{2}{3}\, \frac{\partial_1^3 U}{\partial_1^2 U},
\]
\[
\partial_2 P = \tfrac{1}{2}\, \frac{\bigl[\partial_1^4 + 6\partial_1^2 \partial_2\bigr] U}{\partial_1^2 U}
              - \tfrac{4}{3}\, \frac{\partial_1^3 U}{\partial_1^2 U} \cdot \frac{\bigl[\partial_1^3 + 3\partial_1 \partial_2\bigr] U}{\partial_1^2 U},
\]
which  are  the  conditions  of  a  five-pointic  contact:  it  is  to  be  remarked  that  if  only
the  first  and  second  conditions  are  satisfied,  we  have  a  four-pointic  contact,  and  if  only
the  first  condition  is  satisfied,  a  three-pointic  contact.
