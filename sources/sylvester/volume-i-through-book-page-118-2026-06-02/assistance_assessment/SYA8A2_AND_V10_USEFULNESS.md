# Sylvester assist-package usefulness

The SYA8A2 and v10 assist packets are useful as checking/localisation layers, not as direct edition text.

Coverage: both packets target Sylvester Volume I book pp. 50-75. This batch extends the cumulative edition from book p. 90 to book p. 118, so the packets did not overlap the new transcription range. They were therefore not pasted into the edition.

Useful parts:

- Witness crops: useful for checking whether formula-heavy regions were noticed and for comparing candidate TeX against a local scan crop.
- Accepted/rejected CSV split: useful for audit triage on pp. 50-75 and for identifying recognition failure modes.
- Crop classification: useful when the task is annoying table/array/formula localisation rather than normal prose transcription.

Not sufficient:

- The TeX candidates are not reliable enough to promote without scan comparison.
- False positives and formula/prose boundary errors remain.
- Source scans remain authoritative.

Use pattern going forward: request this kind of assist only for dense formulas, arrays/tables, diagrams, or pages where visual localisation is the bottleneck. For ordinary Sylvester prose plus short displays, manual source reading is faster and cleaner.
