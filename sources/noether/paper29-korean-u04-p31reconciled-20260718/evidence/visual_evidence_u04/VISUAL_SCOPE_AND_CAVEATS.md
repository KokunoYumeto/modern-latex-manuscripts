# U04 visual scope and caveats

The canonical index covers every raster used or created for U04: printed source pages 31 and 32, the German control render, the preserved Korean draft render, and the accepted Korean final render. No crop, segmentation artifact, contact sheet, diagram, model overlay, or additional page render was created for this unit.

Printed page 31 carries the opening five lines of U04. Printed page 32 carries the continuation, display, conclusion, and Hilbert note. The two source-page records therefore contain explicit pixel-coordinate regions rather than treating the whole pages as undifferentiated locators. The printed page-break hyphenation is witness material; the sealed TeX remains the textual authority.

Redistribution rights for the two source JPEGs are unresolved. Their exact binaries remain in the private continuity root and are excluded from the public ZIP. The public-safe layer preserves names, SHA-256 hashes, byte counts, dimensions, 400-DPI metadata, rotation state, page/source indices, bounding boxes, structural links, QA state, and `manifest_only_rights_blocked` disposition without exposing a private filesystem path.

The German control, Korean draft, and Korean final PNGs are project-generated from project TeX and contain no source-scan pixels. They are included as `open_payload`. The draft is retained as a superseded before/after witness; it was not lost or reconstructed. Direct original-resolution session inspection found no clipping, overlap, missing glyph, formula damage, note defect, or pagination defect in the final render. This is not human review, translation certification, source certification, rights clearance, publication, or community endorsement.
