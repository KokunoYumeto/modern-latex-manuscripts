# Noether collected pp. 747-764 source audit

## Scope and method

All 18 assigned collected-volume pages were opened and compared against the live German TeX. The complete-page witnesses were rendered from the included IA-derived source PDF at 650 dpi. Dense or ambiguous loci on pp. 747, 748, and 763 were reopened at 1300 dpi. The OCR text is retained only as a navigation aid; it was not used as textual authority.

This pass checks prose, formulas, symbol families, indices, accents, products, punctuation, headings, footnotes, and page continuity. It closes this bounded band against the best complete local witness. It does not certify the entire collected works or the complete author.

## Confirmed body repairs

1. Collected p. 763: the exact source glyph in the p-adic norm-class relation is congruence, with a tilde above two horizontal bars. The live TeX had simeq. It now has cong.
2. Collected p. 764: the Kapferer title page was restored to the printed title ending ": von H. Kapferer" and the printed subtitle "(Mit einem Zusatz, gemeinsam mit E. Noether)". The non-source separate expanded author/location line and "in Goettingen" addition were removed.

Both changed cumulative output pages were rendered before and after, reopened visually, and passed.

## Source-error apparatus, no body patch

The determinant equation immediately above structurally suggests delta_ij in the denominator. The diplomatic German body preserves the printed delta_ik and records the locus as a probable original-print mathematical denominator error.

### Collected p. 748

The source omits superscript (k) on the second alpha_ij in the homomorphism chain while retaining the superscript on the surrounding corresponding occurrences. The diplomatic body preserves the printed omission and records it as a probable original-print superscript omission.
