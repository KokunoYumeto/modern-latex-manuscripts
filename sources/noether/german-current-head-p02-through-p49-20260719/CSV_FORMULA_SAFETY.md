# CSV formula-safety projection

The producer files remain hash-addressed in `provenance/SHA256_FILES.csv`.
One public CSV copy required spreadsheet-safe escaping: a mathematical
source-reading cell in `ledgers/NOETHER_HARD_MATH_ERROR_LEDGER_current.csv`
began with an equals sign.

The public projection prefixes that cell with a single apostrophe. This is a
spreadsheet-safety encoding only; no mathematical reading, page disposition,
status, identifier, or source claim changed.
