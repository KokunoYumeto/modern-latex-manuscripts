# Noether Paper 4 Section 8 bounded English checkpoint

This release adds a source-audited English working checkpoint for Emmy
Noether's Paper 4, Section 8. It covers the editable German control span
R823 lines 4270--4475, original printed pages 146--152, and physical scan
pages 29--35. The next source cursor is R823 line 4477, Section 9.

## Reader, source, and evidence

- The four-page PDF and editable TeX are available as direct downloads.
- The source-evidence ZIP preserves the exact 38-file independently audited
  package, including source/formula controls, machine-readable structural and
  difficulty ledgers, build evidence, target renders, and validation receipts.
- The visual-evidence ZIP contains four target-page renders and one contact
  sheet. It contains no original-scan pixels.
- The original 1911 print was used as audit-only symbol and structure control.
  It is not redistributed in these files.

The bounded review records four print-adjudicated source defects and their
disclosed repairs. Specialist review of the translation and mathematics
remains outside the claim made here.

## Claim boundary

This is a bounded, machine-assisted, source-audited English working
translation. It is not complete Paper 4 or a complete corpus, a critical
edition, mathematical certification, proof verification, peer review,
independent human review, accessibility conformance, or a rights-clearance
determination. The PDF is untagged. Record-level CC0 metadata does not establish
rights in the underlying German work or this translation.
