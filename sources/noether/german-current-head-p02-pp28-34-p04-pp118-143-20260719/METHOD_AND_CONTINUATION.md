# Method and continuation

## Current cursor

## Required page workflow

1. Open the original full source page.
2. Enlarge and reopen every dense equation, matrix, schema, diagram-like array, note marker, and ambiguous symbol family.
3. Compare prose continuity, displayed mathematics, labels, punctuation, emphasis, notes, and page boundary.
4. Record a page disposition even when no patch is needed.
5. For every confirmed defect, record the source reading, inherited reading, mathematical force, evidence, finder, and adjudicator.
6. Edit only the live cumulative. Keep a bounded exact diff.
7. Compile twice and scan the logs.
8. Render every changed output page and reopen it visually.
9. Update detailed QC, canonical per-page QC, correction-origin, hard-math, source-apparatus, provenance, and publication logbooks.
10. Recompute the completion matrix before sealing.

## Known Paper 2 fragility classes

- continuation stars, braces, diagonal arrays, and terminal rows;
- primes and Greek/Latin lookalikes in coefficient families;
- exponents in worked invariant examples;
- centered multiplication dots that separate dense factors;
- page-local note identities and note-call placement;
- source emphasis that scopes definitions, theorem statements, or contrasted terms;
- apparent line-break artifacts that can be mistaken for subscripts or symbol families.
