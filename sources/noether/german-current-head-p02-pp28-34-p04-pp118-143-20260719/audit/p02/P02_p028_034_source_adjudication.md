# P02 printed pp. 28-34 source adjudication

## Authority and method

The authority was the staged original Paper 2 witness, not OCR and not the inherited TeX. Every assigned page was opened as a complete labeled page and as three enlarged overlapping strips. The strips are enlargements of the best locally staged witness; they were used to inspect dense formulas, schema continuation marks, note identities, exponents, primes, and centered multiplication dots.

The active cumulative was compared directly to the visible source. All promoted edits are listed in `P02_p028_034_confirmed_fixes.csv`. The cumulative was then compiled twice with XeLaTeX, the changed output band was rendered at 400 dpi, and output pp. 5-10 were reopened visually. The final build has 466 pages and no fatal, emergency-stop, undefined-control-sequence, rerun, overfull-box, underfull-box, or missing-character flags.

## Controlling conclusions

- Printed p. 28 required a real schema reconstruction: both continuation stars, the fifth continuation column, and the terminal row belong to the source.
- Printed p. 29 contains the coefficient family `C'_{pr\varrho}`. The missing prime was not a stylistic issue.
- Printed p. 33 contains `a_\eta^3`, not `a_\eta^4`.
- The centered multiplication dots on pp. 29-34 were retained where the source visibly uses them to separate factors.
- Page-local `*)` and `**)` calls were restored as source apparatus; they are not interchangeable generic footnotes.

No additional source-backed defect was found after those repairs. The seven pages are closed on this current head, subject only to a future concrete source contradiction.
