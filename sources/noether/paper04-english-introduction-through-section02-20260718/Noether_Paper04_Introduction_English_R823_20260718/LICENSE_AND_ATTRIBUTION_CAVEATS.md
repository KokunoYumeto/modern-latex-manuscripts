# Attribution and rights caveats

Author: Emmy Noether. Original paper: “Zur Invariantentheorie der Formen von
n Variabeln,” *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* 139 (1911),
118–154.

This package supplies a new English working translation of one bounded unit
plus editorial provenance and QA material. It does not claim ownership of the
underlying German work, scans, transcriptions, or comparison witnesses. The
original scans and source/candidate control bodies are deliberately excluded.

The legal status of the underlying source and the appropriate license for a
derivative translation may depend on jurisdiction and repository policy. A
repository's record-level license metadata is not treated as proof of rights in
the underlying work. This file records attribution and uncertainty; it is not
legal advice or a rights determination.
