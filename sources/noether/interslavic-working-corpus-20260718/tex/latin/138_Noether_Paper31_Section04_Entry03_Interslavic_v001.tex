\setcounter{footnote}{24}

Druga baza $\bar\alpha_1,\ldots,\bar\alpha_s$ togo samogo ideala tvori matrično kolco, izomorfno k $R_{\mathfrak a}$, pri tom ekvivalentno jemu:
\[
        R_{\bar{\mathfrak a}}=P^{-1}R_{\mathfrak a}P.
\]
Bo nehaj $\bar\alpha_1,\ldots,\bar\alpha_s$ bude još jedna linearno nezavisna $\mathfrak Z$-modulna baza; sledovateljno $(\bar\alpha_1,\ldots,\bar\alpha_s)=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_s)P$, gde determinant matrice $P$ jest jedinica iz $\mathfrak Z$. Togda
\[
\begin{aligned}
(\gamma\bar\alpha_1,\ldots,\gamma\bar\alpha_s)
&=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_s)CP\\
&=(\bar\alpha_1,\ldots,\bar\alpha_s)P^{-1}CP,
\end{aligned}
\]
to jest $R_{\bar{\mathfrak a}}=P^{-1}R_{\mathfrak a}P$.
