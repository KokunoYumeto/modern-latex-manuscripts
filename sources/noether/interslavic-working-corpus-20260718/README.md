## Status boundary

These are internally normalized working translations. They are not a critical
edition, native-speaker or community certification, peer review, or proof that
every mathematical symbol is source-faithful. Check important mathematics
against the German R823 source control and source scans. The method and exact
normalization evidence are mirrored at
[`interlanguage-sidecar/methodology/interslavic-normalization-002ab-20260718`](../../../interlanguage-sidecar/methodology/interslavic-normalization-002ab-20260718/).
