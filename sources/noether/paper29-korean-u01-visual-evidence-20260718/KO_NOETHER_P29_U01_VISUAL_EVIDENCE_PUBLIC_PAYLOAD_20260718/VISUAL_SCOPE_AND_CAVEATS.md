# Korean Noether Paper 29 U01 visual-evidence scope and caveats

This archive covers every visual asset used or generated by the Korean lane for bounded unit U01 of Noether Paper 29: two complete source-page witnesses (printed pages 28 and 29) and two project-generated, full-page German/Korean renders. No separate equation crops, source crops, diagram crops, contact sheets, before/after images, segmentation artifacts, masks, or model overlays were used or created for this unit.

The two source pages belong to an eight-file, 11,431,303-byte private continuity root covering printed pages 28--35. Only pages 28 and 29 were used for U01. Page 29 also contains the beginning of § 1 below the U01 boundary; the indexed asset is the complete page image because no derivative crop was used. Redistribution rights for all eight source images have not been established. The render checks covered clipping, overlap, missing glyphs, black boxes, margin overflow, blank output, unreadable mathematics, and footnote collision; no visible defect was found in the sealed renders.
