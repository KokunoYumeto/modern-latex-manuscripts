# Emmy Noether R823 source-synchronized components

This bundle collects bounded translation components that were reconciled to
the current R823 German authority and passed their stated build and visual-QA
gates. It does not replace any full multilingual cumulative reader.

## English components

Standalone English components are included for Papers 10, 20, 26, 28, 29,
and 37. Each directory includes editable TeX, a compiled PDF, source/control
material, correction or disposition records, build/visual evidence, and a
verified SHA-256 manifest. These six papers are component-ready. The complete
English cumulative reader remains the older RA10 working branch and has not
yet been rebased paper by paper to R823.

## CJK components

Papers 26 and 36 are included in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese,
Japanese, and Korean, with German R823 controls. Simplified Chinese and
Japanese were recovered from existing cumulative readers and reconciled for
these units. Traditional Chinese is a controlled generic script/register
adaptation, not a Taiwan- or Hong-Kong-localized edition. Korean is new working
translation coverage. No external native-language signoff is claimed.

## Slavic book-front component

The title/introduction unit of Noether's lecture-book material is included in
Ukrainian, Russian, Interslavic Latin, and deterministic Interslavic Cyrillic.
All four three-page outputs built and passed the package's internal structural
and visual checks. This is 1 of 32 core book units; the next cursor is
`BOOK_S01`. It is not a complete book translation or community certification.

## Boundaries

The French and Spanish R823 cumulative rebases, English Paper 07, and the SGA
English synchronization lanes were actively changing when this bundle was
sealed. They are intentionally excluded. Terms such as `complete`, `closed`,
or `ready` inside component logs apply only to the explicitly bounded unit.
