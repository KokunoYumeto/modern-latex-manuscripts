# Paper 17 sections 5-9 method and special-character note

Scope: continuation through Paper 17 §9, including the units/idempotents of residue groups, reducibility into k subgroups, finiteness theorem, the unique-decomposition case, additive decomposition of completely reducible groups, the isomorphism theorem, and the equivalence between same-kind modules and residue-group isomorphism.

Translation policy: preserve residue-class language literally enough for algebraic checking. Spanish uses `grupo residual`, `clase residual`, `módulo unilateral`, `completamente reducible`, and mostly `del mismo tipo` for `von gleicher Art`. Japanese uses `剰余群`, `剰余類`, `片側加群`, `完全可約`, and `同種`.

Formula policy: formulas (12)-(30), all congruence conditions, idempotent/orthogonality relations, finite-basis contradiction arguments, and theorem statements V-VII remain editable TeX. No formula has been converted into an image.

Title policy: the first source scan/title witness has `insbesondere aus Differenzenausdrücken`; the batch title history sometimes broadens this to differential-and-difference expressions. The Spanish/Japanese display title in this package follows the scan-visible title, while §1 and the introduction retain the differential/difference discussion where it belongs.
