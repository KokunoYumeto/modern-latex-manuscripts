# Paper 13 methodology and notation note

Scope: Noether Paper 13, *Invariante Variationsprobleme*, translated complete into Spanish and Japanese.

Paper 13 introduces dense variational-calculus notation and Lie-group notation. The following local policy is cumulative for downstream agents:

- Preserve `\dd`, `\Div`, `\bd`, `\G`, and `\p` as editable TeX macros.
- Preserve formula numbering `(1)` through `(20b)` exactly as display tags.
- Distinguish finite continuous groups depending on finitely many parameters from infinite continuous groups depending on arbitrary functions.
- Translate conservation-law terminology consistently: Spanish `leyes de conservación`, Japanese `保存則`; translate the proper/improper distinction as `propias/impropias` and `真性/非真性`.
- In one-variable examples, primes such as `u'` and `u''` are derivatives, not Greek nu; do not normalize them to `\nu`.
