# P33-KO-U02 translation choices

This file records translator choices, not checking or certification.

- `Modulklassen` and `Idealklassen` are rendered `가군류` and `아이디얼류`.
- `Gruppen mit Operatoren` is rendered descriptively as `연산자를 가진 군`; the later defined `Operatorenbereich` will be distinguished explicitly.
- `sich ... unterordnen` is rendered `... 아래에 포섭하다`, preserving the claim that the module and ideal classes fall under the generalized group concept.
- `Reduktion einer gegebenen Darstellung` is provisionally rendered `주어진 표현의 환원`.
- Historical `Darstellungskörper` is rendered `표현체`; the commutative restriction is retained and later noncommutative usage remains for checker adjudication.
- This is an unchecked ko-KR translation. No ko-KP, source, language-review, specialist, or external validation is claimed.
