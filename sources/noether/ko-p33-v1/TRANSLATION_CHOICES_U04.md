This file records translator choices, not checking or certification.

- `Reduktion` retains U02's provisional `환원`; `irreduzible Bestandteile` is rendered `기약 성분`.
- `Gesamtheit der möglichen Darstellungen` is rendered `가질 수 있는 표현 전체`.

- `Darstellungskörper` retains `표현체`; `Matrizen eines festen Grades` is rendered `고정된 차수의 행렬들`.
