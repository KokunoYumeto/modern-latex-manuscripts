# P33-KO-U05 translation choices

This file records translator choices, not checking or certification.

- `Gruppe mit Operatorenbereich` is rendered `연산자 영역을 가진 군`; `Operator` is `연산자`.
- `operatorisomorph` and `Operatorisomorphie` are rendered `연산자 동형` and `연산자 동형의 의미`.
- `zulässige` subgroups/normal divisors are rendered `허용되는`; `Einheit` in the subgroup clause is made explicit as `항등부분군`.
- `Kompositionsreihe` and `Faktorgruppen` are rendered `합성열` and `인자군`.
- `direkt unzerlegbare Faktoren` is rendered `직접분해 불가능한 인자들`.
- This is an unchecked ko-KR translation. No ko-KP, source, Korean-review, specialist, or external validation is claimed.
