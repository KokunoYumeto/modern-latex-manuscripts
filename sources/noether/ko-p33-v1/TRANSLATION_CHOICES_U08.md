# P33-KO-U08 translation choices

This file records translator choices, not checking or certification.

- `hyperkomplexe Systeme` is rendered `초복소수계`, aligned with U01's `초복소수`; `초복소계` remains a checker alternative.
- `Doppelkettensatz` is rendered `이중 사슬 조건`.
- `irreduzible (einfache) Modulklasse` is `기약(단순) 가군류`; `Idealklasse` retains `아이디얼류`.
- `Restklassenring nach dem Radikal` is rendered `라디칼로 나눈 잉여류환`; the source does not further classify the radical.
- `vollständig reduzibel` is rendered `완전가약`, not modernized to `반단순`.
- `Automorphismenring` is `자기동형환`; historical `Automorphismenkörper` is `자기동형체`. Where the source says the ring becomes a `Körper`, the target adds `곧 나눗셈환` to expose the historical noncommutative sense.
- `einseitig`, `Rechtsideal`, and `Linksideal` are rendered `한쪽`, `오른쪽 아이디얼`, and `왼쪽 아이디얼`.
- `Wedderburnsche Struktursatz`, `nichtkommutative Körper`, and `Zerfällungskörper` are rendered `베더번 구조정리`, `비가환체`, and `분해체`.
- `unzerlegbare Ideale` is `직접분해 불가능한 아이디얼`, kept distinct from `기약(단순)`.
- This is an unchecked ko-KR translation. No ko-KP, source, Korean-review, specialist, or external validation is claimed.
