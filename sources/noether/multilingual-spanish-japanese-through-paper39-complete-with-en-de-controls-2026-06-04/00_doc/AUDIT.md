## Global terminology/refinement

`Differente` is now standardized as Spanish `diferente` and Japanese `ディファレント（差異イデアル）` in algebraic-number-theory contexts.  This is a methodology-level correction of an older too-literal Japanese gloss.

`Hauptordnung` is kept as `orden principal` / `主オーダー` in Paper 37 rather than silently modernizing the historical source wording.

`Galoismodul`, `operatorisomorph`, `Faktorensystem`, `verschränktes Produkt`, `Normrestsymbol`, and `Führer` were added to the cumulative methodology aids for later Takagi/class-field-theory integration.

## Mathematical notes
