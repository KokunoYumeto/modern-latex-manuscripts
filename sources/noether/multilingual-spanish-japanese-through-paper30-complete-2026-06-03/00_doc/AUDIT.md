# Audit note - Paper 30 ES/JA

No declared translation gaps in Paper 30. No tables or diagrams occur in the paper. Displayed formulas, chains, quotient expressions, ideal symbols, and the Jordan--Hölder comparison are preserved as editable TeX.

Cumulative branch choice: the cumulative TeX/PDF files in this packet are rebuilt from the checked Papers 25--29 cumulative branch before appending complete Paper 30. They are not based on older partial Paper 30 cumulative drafts, to avoid branch regression.

Terminology/consistency decisions:
- `Teilerkettensatz` / `Teilerkettenbedingung`: `condición de cadena de divisores` / `約イデアル鎖条件`.
- `Vielfachenkettensatz` / `Vielfachenkettenbedingung`: `condición de cadena de múltiplos` / `倍イデアル鎖条件`.
- `Doppelkettensatz`: `doble condición de cadena` / `二重鎖条件`.
- `teilerfremd` remains symmetric coprimality: `coprimo` / `互いに素`.
- directional `prim zu` remains `primo respecto de` / `に対して素`.
- `Einheitsideal` and `Einselement` are kept distinct: `ideal unitario` / `単位イデアル` versus `elemento identidad` / `単位元`.
- `Kompositionsreihe` is `serie de composición` / `組成列`; it is not silently recast as subgroup-series language.
