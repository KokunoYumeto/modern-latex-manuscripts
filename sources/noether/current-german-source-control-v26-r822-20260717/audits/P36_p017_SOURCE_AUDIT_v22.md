# P36 Printed p. 17 Source Audit

Date: 2026-07-11

## Scope

This pass reopened Emmy Noether's one-page meeting announcement, *Idealdifferentiation und Differente*, in the original *Jahresbericht der Deutschen Mathematiker-Vereinigung* 39 (1930), p. 17.

The GDZ volume has two canvases labeled p. 17 because the volume contains separately paginated sections. Canvas `00000312` is the annual-meeting page containing Noether's entry; canvas `00000025` is unrelated. The authority used here is the full 3280x5046 IIIF image for canvas `00000312`.

## Result

The active P36 TeX is complete and source-correct. It preserves:

- the numbered author/title line;
- the complete two-sentence mathematical description;
- source emphasis on `Differentialquotient` and `Ideals`;
- the final sentence `Eine ausführliche Darstellung soll in den Math. Ann. erscheinen.`

No TeX patch was required.

## Ledger correction

An old survival-only ledger row attributed `Der Beweis soll in den Math. Ann. erscheinen.` to Web R719 and claimed it survived in the then-current head. Direct original-page audit disproves that reading. The later cumulative already contained the correct source sentence, so v22 corrects the provenance and page-QC records rather than changing the TeX.

This distinction matters: archaeology claims can be wrong even when the current cumulative happens to be right. The page ledger must describe the actual source and actual current head, not a remembered intermediate report.
