## Status boundary

This record is a working corpus. It is **not** a critical edition, author-wide source-faithfulness certification, paper-by-paper certification, complete multilingual synchronization, or publication-grade proofreading. Source-audit depth varies by paper. The English and multilingual readers predate some German repairs and must not be assumed to contain every current German correction.

Terms such as `COMPLETE`, `closure`, or `closed` inherited inside older packets refer to the bounded task named by that packet, not to certification of the full corpus.

Corrections and source comparisons are welcome through issues or pull requests at https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/modern-latex-manuscripts.
