# Scope and authority

## Represented repair checkpoints

1. **Paper 4 Introduction and Sections 2-4.** Sixteen post-repair target units
   distinguish original-print defects, R823 transcription defects, and
   inherited target-lineage regressions. The package preserves all sixteen
   post-repair TeX/PDF pairs and their immediate TeX preimages.
2. **Paper 4 Section 5.** Four post-repair target units restore the required
   `rho_1` term in equation (35) while retaining four explicitly recorded
   print-to-R823 editorial deltas. Unchanged Section 4 body copies are not
   duplicated.
3. **Paper 37.** Four post-repair target units restore the printed author line,
   the basis endpoint `v_t`, and the cross-reference to 2a).

The bodies are exact July 18 repair survivors. Later Interslavic normalization
may supersede them as reader text; these files remain the evidence-bearing
source-repair checkpoints.

## Authority

The bounded decisions compare the historical Noether print, the R823 German
transcription/control, and the target bodies. The historical source pixels are
identity-bound but excluded from this public projection. The exact findings,
hashes, and decision classifications remain in the audit ledgers and the
rights-blocked visual-witness manifest.

## Claim limits

No blanket license or redistribution right is asserted for the historical
German sources. The target files are preserved as working translations and
source-repair evidence. This package does not claim human linguistic approval,
community acceptance of Interslavic terminology, complete Paper 4 or Paper 37
source fidelity, mathematical proof verification, accessibility remediation,
or critical-edition status.
