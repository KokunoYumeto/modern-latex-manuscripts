# Exclusions and rights

Historical source pixels are important authority witnesses, but their public
redistribution rights are not established here. They are therefore represented
by exact hash, byte count, dimensions, DPI, and audit role without publishing
the pixels.

Target-PDF renders and contact sheets are omitted because the target PDFs are
already included and remain directly renderable. Their exact identities are
retained in the original-package receipt.

Raw LaTeX logs, auxiliary files, compile transcripts, and the original repair
script contain host-local paths or are replaceable build intermediates. They
are hash-bound but not redistributed. The path-clean build tables preserve the
substantive compile and PDF results.

Original package manifests are retained so every original private package file
remains independently identifiable. This public projection does not claim to
be byte-identical to those private packages.
