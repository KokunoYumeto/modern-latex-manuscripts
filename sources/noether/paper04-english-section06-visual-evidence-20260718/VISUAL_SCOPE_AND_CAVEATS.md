# Visual evidence scope and caveats

This package contains three project-generated PNGs for the bounded English Paper 4 Section 6 reader: two complete 200-dpi page renders and one ordered contact sheet. No original-scan pixels, German-source images, inherited-English images, or diagnostic source crops are included.

The images document rendering and layout QA only. They do not constitute source rights clearance, mathematical certification, peer review, independent human scholarly review, or a claim that Paper 4 is complete. Stable visual IDs bind each image to the exact two-page parent PDF and the machine-readable structural units it covers.
