# U03 visual scope and caveats

The canonical index inventories every source page and project render used or created in U03 production, including the overwritten initial-render loss event and its exact-TeX reconstruction.

Printed pp.28, 31, and 32 have unresolved redistribution rights. Their binaries remain under the private source root and are excluded from the public ZIP. Public-safe metadata retains names, hashes, bytes, page coordinates, geometry, structural links, QA state, rights status, and disposition without private filesystem paths.

The German control, accepted Korean render, and reconstructed superseded Korean render are project-generated and included in the open payload. The reconstruction is not the unavailable original overwritten PNG. No visual item has external human certification.
