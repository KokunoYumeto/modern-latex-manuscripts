# U03 initial-render reconstruction

The first U03 Korean render was created and visually inspected before its PNG was overwritten by the accepted rerender. Its binary was not hashed before overwrite. The exact initial TeX was reconstructed from the recorded production patch and must match the recorded pre-review TeX SHA-256 379C3A064823F94FDACD2419F5BCF9DAA54002FC7AA99F99A231DA0DE5FBE877.

The reconstructed PDF/PNG are project-generated reconstruction evidence, not recovered historical binaries and not the accepted target. Their hashes cannot prove pixel identity with the unavailable overwritten PNG because no original PNG hash survives. The accepted target supersedes the wording, shared-footnote treatment, and target-only display shown here.
