## Unit-specific choices

- **U01:** `분할 교차곱`, `극대 오더`, `구역`, `주구역`, and `대각합-쌍대 가군` establish the paper's producer lexicon. Long historical sentences are divided only where Korean syntax requires; claims and footnotes remain in source order.
- **U02:** `E_\Gg` generates the `자명표현`; `Matrizeneinheiten` is `행렬단위`; complementary bases are rendered as trace-dual bases.
- **U03:** `\bar k` is `갈루아 폐포`; the one-sided/full distinction for relations (1)/(2) is retained.
- **U04:** historical algebra similarity is `상사`; `Automorphismenring` is provisionally `자기준동형환`, not `자기동형군`.
- **U05:** `Ordnung von a` is `a의 오더`; reciprocal left/right ideals are phrased as ideals `서로 역인` under the displayed products.
- **U06:** old `Quotientenring nach p` is translated by its explicitly defined localization sense. `Komponente` remains `성분`.
- **U07:** source symbols `<` are preserved without silently normalizing them to `\subseteq`; `Sp(\mathfrak c)` is a trace ideal.
- **U08:** the four displayed ideal-product identities are preserved as target TeX without mathematical normalization.
- **U09:** `regulär` is retained as provisional `정칙인`; the accompanying full-rank consequence is stated separately.
- **U10:** the source's overloaded symbol $t$ is retained; `Rechtsmultiplikatorenbereich` is `오른쪽 승수환`.
- **U11:** `Verzweigungsstelle` is `분기소`; extension ideals are `확장아이디얼`; `Divisionsalgebra` is `나눗셈대수`.
- **U12:** the two `Hauptordnung` occurrences in the quaternion example are rendered `정수환`, consistent with rings of integers in the displayed subfields.

## Adverse evidence / held questions

2. A narrow web search found no authoritative Korean primary source resolving `maximal order`; a secondary machine-translated review used `극대 오더`. It was not treated as validation. The producer choice remains provisional.
3. `Ordnung`, `normal`, `regulär`, `Automorphismenring`, and `Differente` carry historical senses that a Korean algebra/number-theory checker must review.
4. No North-Korean mathematical standard evidence was consulted. South-/North-Korean divergence remains unresolved rather than silently collapsed.
