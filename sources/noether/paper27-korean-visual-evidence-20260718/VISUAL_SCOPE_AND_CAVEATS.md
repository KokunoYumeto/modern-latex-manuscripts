# Korean Noether P27 visual-evidence scope and caveats

This archive covers every visual asset used or generated by the Korean lane for the bounded Paper 27 production unit. It contains one source-page witness and two project-generated full-page renders. No separate equation crops, diagram crops, contact sheets, before/after images, segmentation artifacts, or model overlays were used or created for this unit.

The GDZ page image is retained at its exact private source root because it is archival research evidence. Its redistribution rights have not been established. The public-safe layer therefore reports its stable ID, filename, hash, dimensions, DPI, page coordinates, linked structures, QA state, and rights-blocked disposition but does not include its pixels or private absolute path. This is not a claim that the source image is copyrighted, public domain, licensed, or forbidden; it is an explicit statement that the lane has no verified redistribution basis.

The German and Korean PNGs were generated by `pdftoppm -png -r 180 -singlefile` from the project PDFs and contain no source-scan pixels. They were inspected at original resolution for clipping, overlap, missing glyphs, black boxes, margin overflow, blank output, and unreadable formulae. No visible defect was found. This was internal model inspection, not human or community certification.

The public payload is a handoff candidate only. It does not create a GitHub release, Zenodo draft, DOI, or publication claim. Archive maintenance remains the sole owner of incorporation into an existing canonical handoff/deposit.
