# Paper 34 §§15--26 methodology notes

Scope: this packet completes Paper 34 in Spanish and Japanese, from §15 through §26.

Global coherence choices applied in this block:

- `Körper` remains `cuerpo` / `体` in commutative field contexts, but in the representation-module and automorphism-field context the first occurrence explicitly records the division-ring reading: `cuerpo no conmutativo / anillo de división` and `非可換体／斜体`.
- `Automorphismenkörper` continues as `anillo de división de automorfismos` / `自己同型斜体`.
- `Hauptspur` and `reduzierte Spur` are standardized as `traza principal` / `主トレース` and `traza reducida` / `被約トレース`. This is important for later algebraic-number-theory and central-simple-algebra packets.
- `Charakter` is `carácter` / `指標`; `Klassensumme` is recorded as `suma de clase` / `類和`.
- The source-visible product table in the discriminant computation of §25 is restored as editable TeX from the existing Paper 34 product-table patch and appears in the Spanish/Japanese outputs as well as the German/English control files. It is not represented by a screenshot.
- The wording `finite group` in §26 is ordinary finite-group language, not the finite-parameter Lie-group sense flagged earlier for Paper 21.

No retroactive body rewrite outside Paper 34 was needed in this packet. The cumulative branch is built from the checked Paper 34 §14 cumulative branch and then appends §§15--26.
