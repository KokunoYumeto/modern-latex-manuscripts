# Emmy Noether public corpus status - 2026-07-18

This record is the reader-facing public surface for the project's Emmy Noether
material. It preserves the current German R823 LaTeX source-control reader and
editable source package; cumulative English, Spanish, French, and Interslavic
working readers; bounded translation components in additional languages; and
the source-repair, provenance, build, render, and audit material needed to
inspect how those artifacts were produced.

## Current readers and components

- German R823 source control, including a bounded Paper 30 hard-mathematics
  audit and support package;
- cumulative English working material and standalone English paper drafts;
- 473-page Spanish and 494-page French R823 working translations with editable
  source and evidence packages;
- Interslavic Latin and Cyrillic working corpora with 221 editable units in
  each script, all 442 unit PDFs, cumulative 527- and 551-page readers, and
  normalization evidence through Tranche 007;
- bounded Slavic, Arabic, Persian, Indonesian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional
  Chinese, Japanese, and Korean working components; and
- source-repair, OCR-locator, provenance, checksum, render-QA, and audit
  packages retained for reproducibility.

## New bounded checkpoints in this version

This version adds a two-page source-audited English working translation of
Paper 3 and source-current working translations for Paper 27 in Korean, Paper
28 in Simplified Chinese, controlled generic Traditional Chinese, Japanese,
and Korean, and Paper 29 in Simplified Chinese and controlled generic
Traditional Chinese. Their grouped source/evidence archive contains editable
TeX, authority controls, terminology and decision records, structural and
difficulty indexes where supplied, build records, and render-QA material.

Paper 3 is one closed source-audited translation unit, not certification of the
whole English corpus. The CJK files are internally source-checked working
translations without external native-language or community signoff. Generic
Traditional Chinese is not a Taiwan, Hong Kong, or Macao localization.

## Visual evidence

The visual-evidence archive adds 3,024 unique project-generated images that
were not already present by exact hash inside the existing Spanish, French,
and Interslavic evidence packages. It preserves the frozen 494-page French
render baseline, the frozen Spanish full-page and high-resolution review
baselines, and missing Interslavic normalization review images through Tranche
007. Its machine-readable index also records 169 already-public instances and
57 duplicate instances that were deliberately not repeated.

These images are witnesses to rendering and review activity. They do not by
themselves certify mathematical correctness, translation quality, source
fidelity, or native-language acceptance.

## Authority limits

These files are substantial machine-assisted working editions, translations,
and source-control artifacts. They are not official critical editions, peer
review, mathematical proof certification, native-language or community
certification, rights determinations, or guarantees that every symbol and
translation choice is correct. Build and render checks establish artifact
integrity; bounded source audits establish only the scopes stated in their
evidence. OCR and VLM outputs are locator or comparison witnesses unless a
file explicitly records direct source reconciliation.

The French corpus is a modern editorial translation of the German R823
authority, not a historical French edition. The Interslavic work is a typed
editorial normalization and working translation corpus, not an independent
sentence-by-sentence German-source audit or unified-v6.2 readiness claim. Its
remaining 353 `važi*|važe*` mathematical sense-extension probes in 101 files
remain an explicit external-authority blocker.
