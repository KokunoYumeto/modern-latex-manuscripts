# Korean terminology status

Running prose is Hangul-first on a declared `ko-KR` working baseline. No Hanja authority is asserted, and no DPRK/North-Korean localization is claimed.

Internally usable Korean-source forms include `다항식환`, `으뜸 아이디얼`, `소 아이디얼`, and `합성열`. Proper-name evidence supports `매콜리` in a compound name and directly supports `오스트롭스키`.

The following working forms remain held or context-limited: `힐베르트 수`, `특성 함수`, `잉여류`, `생성 함수`, `잉여류체`, `기약 성분`, and `유한 오더`. The TeX retains German or English controls for the historically or semantically exposed items.

Row-level evidence and limitations are in `evidence/KOREAN_NATIVE_EXAMPLE_CORPUS_SLICE.csv`; selections and questions are in `terminology/TERMINOLOGY_LEDGER.csv`; adverse, absence, and veto findings are in `terminology/ADVERSE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.csv`. The Hilbert-numbers decision validates against the operational decision schema and remains `held`.

Under `CJK-D011`, `evidence/CJKV_CROSSWALK_P27_KO.csv` records an explicit sense window, excluded senses, trap class, qualitative Mandarin-Simplified dominance risk/debt, underrepresented-cohort effect, and provisional lexical-attractor basin for every P27 term. These basin labels describe transmission history only; they are not evidence weights, cognacy findings, semantic-equivalence claims, or readiness scores.

No Chinese or Japanese form is counted as Korean evidence, and no external or community certification is claimed.
