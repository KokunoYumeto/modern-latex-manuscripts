# Korean script and standard policy

- Running prose is Hangul-first and uses contemporary South-Korean mathematical spelling as the working baseline (`ko-KR`).
- Latin-script source controls remain visible only where the Korean technical form is held or historically ambiguous: `Hilbertsche Anzahlen`, `charakteristische Funktion`, `residue field; Restklassenkörper`, `irreduzible Komponenten`, and `order`.
- Hanja is not inserted into the running text. Local sources expose occasional Hanja glosses such as `소(素)` in terminology metadata, but this tranche does not assert a Hanja-based Korean authority or a deterministic Hangul-Hanja conversion.
- Proper names use locally attested Hangul where available: `매콜리` and `오스트롭스키`. `E. Noether` remains in the source's bibliographic form.
- No North-Korean mathematical-standard corpus was recovered in the bounded lane search. The output must not be described as North-Korean localized or North/South unified. A DPRK-standard reviewer should decide any terminological divergence before such a claim is made.
- Formulae and Fraktur identifiers remain in math mode and are not transliterated.
