# Methodology note for Paper 34 §§5--14

Scope: Paper 34 §§5--14 in Spanish and Japanese.

The main linguistic refinement in this block is the consistent treatment of noncommutative `Körper` language. Where Noether explicitly allows noncommutativity, the translations use division-ring terminology at first occurrence: Spanish `cuerpo no necesariamente conmutativo, es decir, anillo de división`; Japanese `必ずしも可換とは限らない体、すなわち斜体`. Later occurrences may shorten to `cuerpo` / `体` when the historical wording is harmless, but `Automorphismenkörper` and matrix-ring structure statements are kept as `anillo de división de automorfismos` / `自己同型斜体`.

The order of noncommutative products was preserved in all formulas, including `\mr\ma_i`, `\ml_i\mr_i`, `\mr_i\ml_k`, and conjugation formulas involving `c_{ik}`. No commutative simplification was introduced.

Contraction and extension of ideals over the center are now standardized as Spanish `ideal contraído` / `ideal extendido` and Japanese `収縮イデアル` / `拡張イデアル`, to support later integration with ideal-theoretic and class-field material.
