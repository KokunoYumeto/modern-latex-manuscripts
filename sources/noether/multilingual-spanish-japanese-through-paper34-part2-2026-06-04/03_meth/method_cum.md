# Cumulative translation-method update through Paper 34 §14

The cumulative Spanish/Japanese branch through Paper 34 §14 retains prior global standardizations: `dominio íntegro` / `整域`, `base íntegra` / `整基底`, finite-parameter Lie-group usage only where flagged, and representation-theoretic `Körper` as division-ring language in noncommutative contexts.

Added in this packet:

- `Automorphismenkörper`: `anillo de división de automorfismos` / `自己同型斜体`.
- `Körper` in explicitly noncommutative module/matrix contexts: division-ring language at first occurrence.
- `Kontraktionsideal` and `Erweiterungsideal`: `ideal contraído` / `ideal extendido`; `収縮イデアル` / `拡張イデアル`.
- Radical vocabulary: `Radikal` remains `radical` / `根基`; it is not silently modernized to nilradical/Jacobson radical unless the local argument requires a note.
- `Matrixeinheiten`: `unidades matriciales` / `行列単位`.

No retroactive body rewrite outside Paper 33--34 was necessary in this packet. The cumulative TeX received macro additions for Fraktur ideal letters required by Paper 34 §§5--14.
