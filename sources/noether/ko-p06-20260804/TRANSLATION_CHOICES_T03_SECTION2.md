## Sense windows

- `Unbestimmte / unbestimmt`: `미정원 / 부정형`. The capitalized noun means algebraic indeterminates in the rational-function setting; the predicate means that a specialized value or quotient is indeterminate, especially `$0/0$`. These senses must not be collapsed merely because the German forms share a stem.
- `algebraischer Rang`: provisionally `대수적 계수(階數)`, the defined number `$\rho$` of algebraically independent functions. Hangul `계수` can attract the unrelated coefficient sense; `대수적 랭크` or another Korean rank term remains a checker alternative.
- `Rang der Funktionalmatrix`: provisionally `함수행렬의 계수(階數)`. Its window is the rank witnessed by the nonzero derivative determinant in equation (2), not a coefficient count. Whether `함수행렬`, `야코비 행렬`, or another historically faithful Korean expression is appropriate remains held.
- `Spezialisierung / Substitution`: provisionally `특수화 / 대입`. The first describes assigning values while retaining the required algebraic properties; the second names the actual substitutions such as `$x_i=a$`. The two are related but not automatically interchangeable.
- `sukzessiv`: `잇따른`, with `순차적` retained as an alternative. It denotes the ordered chain `$x_n=a_n,\ldots,x_{\rho+1}=a_{\rho+1}$`, not merely simultaneous evaluation.
- `Rang erhalten`: `계수(階數)가 보존되다`, meaning that algebraic rank remains `$\rho$` under the permitted substitution.
- `gemeinsamer Nenner`: `공통분모`, specifically `$G(x)$` for the functions `$g_1,\ldots,g_\rho$`.
- `teilbar / Teilbarkeit`: `나누어떨어지다 / 나누어떨어짐`, polynomial divisibility by `$(x_i-a)$`, not numerical divisibility alone. More nominal Korean wording remains a checker choice.
- `irreduzible Gleichung`: `기약방정식`, the equation of `$h(x)$` over the functions `$g_1,\ldots,g_\rho$`; the precise relative/base-field sense must be checked.
- `reduzierte Darstellung / teilerfremd`: `기약표현 / 서로소`, a numerator-denominator presentation with coprime `$H_1,H_2$`, not a generic simplified display.
- `homogene Formen`: `동차형식`, referring to the `$B_k$` expressions in `$G,G_i$`; do not pull the phrase toward canonical/normal form.
- `identisch Null`: `항등적으로 영`, a function or polynomial identity, not merely zero at the chosen specialization.
- `Hilfssatz`: `보조정리`, the lemma concluded after iterating the one-variable specialization argument.
- `Zähler / Nenner / $0/0$`: `분자 / 분모 / 부정형 $0/0$`. The conclusion concerns neither numerator nor denominator becoming identically zero under the stipulated sequential procedure; it does not license a general claim about all specializations.
