## Sense windows

- `Körper / System`: `체 / 계`. `Körper` is the algebraic field defined by closure in Definition I; `System` is the broader collection and need not be a field.
- `Unbestimmte`: provisionally `미정원`; the intended window is an indeterminate/variable of the rational-function setting, not an equation's unknown. The lane also contains `부정원`; harmonization is held for the checker.
- `reduzierte Darstellung`: `기약표현`, specifically coprime numerator and denominator, not an arbitrary simplified presentation.
- `ganze rationale Funktionen (Polynome)`: U08 has `정(整) 유리함수(다항식)` and U13 has `정칙 유리함수`. The source window is polynomial/integral rational functions, not holomorphic regularity. The inconsistency is exposed and held rather than silently harmonized.
- `Koeffizientenbereich / Zahlkörper`: `계수영역 / 수체`, but the source explicitly allows all complex numbers and finitely many parameters. `수체` must not import only the modern finite-extension-of-`$\mathbb Q$` sense.
- `Größen aus $\Omega$`: `$\Omega$의 원소`, meaning coefficient-field elements/scalars and hence degree-zero functions, not physical magnitudes.
- `Adjunktion / spezieller Körper`: `첨가 / 특수한 체`, meaning the field generated over `$\Omega$` by finitely many adjoined rational functions; not physical attachment or an unexplained modern special-field class.
- `Zwischenkörper`: `중간체`, with the two inclusion directions fixed by U10.
- `wirklich enthält`: provisionally `실질적으로 포함한다`; the sense is that at least one function actually depends on/contains all `$n$` indeterminates, not that the field is a proper subset.
- `algebraischer Rang`: provisionally `대수적 계수(階數)` in the explicitly defined sense of the number `$\rho$` of algebraically independent functions. `계수` must not be read as coefficient; `대수적 랭크` remains a checker alternative.
- `Doppelindex`: `이중 첨자`, the ordered pair of indices `$n,\rho$` in `$\Ssys_{n\rho}$` and `$\Kfield_{n\rho}$`.
- `Lagrangescher Gattungsbereich`: `라그랑주 종영역`, here the field of rational/polynomial functions invariant under a permutation group; not a generic genus domain.
- `symmetrische Elementarfunktionen`: `기본대칭함수`, specifically the elementary symmetric functions.
- `projektiver Invariantenkörper / Grundformen`: `사영불변식체 / 기본형식`, in the historical invariant-theory window.
- `homogene, isobare Invarianten`: `동차인 등중(等重) 불변식`, where `isobar` means equal weight, not a pressure/isobar sense. These alone form a system, not a field.
