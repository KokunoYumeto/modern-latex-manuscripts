## Sense windows

- `ein-eindeutig`: `일대일 대응`. Here the constructed association is injective and later supports relation preservation in both directions. `일대일 사상` may understate the converse/surjectivity nuance; modern `전단사` may over-modernize the register. All remain held.
- `Abbildung / Abbildungsfunktion / Abbildungssystem`: `사상 / 사상 함수 / 사상계`. The first is the construction, the second an image function `$f^*$`, and the third the resulting system `$\Ssys^*_{\rho\rho}`. These related senses must not be flattened mechanically.
- `algebraischer Rang / eigentlich charakteristische Zahl`: `대수적 계수(階數) / 본질적으로 특징적인 수`. `階數` disambiguates rank from coefficient `係數` only provisionally; `랭크` and a more idiomatic rendering of the second phrase remain alternatives.
- `Unbestimmte durch Zahlen ersetzen / Substitution`: `미정원을 수로 바꾸다 / 대입`. This is the sequential specialization construction inherited from §2, not arbitrary evaluation without the lemma's nonvanishing/rank-preservation conditions.
- `Relation / identisch`: `관계식 / 항등적으로`. Relation means a functional algebraic relation among system elements; identity scopes differ between `$x_1,\ldots,x_\rho$` and `$x_1,\ldots,x_n$`.
- `rationale Verbindung`: `유리 결합`. It means a rational expression/combination of the selected functions, not merely a logical connection.
- `Übertragungsprinzip`: `전이 원리`. It transfers relation-characterized properties from an image system back to the original system. It is not the operator `Überschiebung`, provisionally `전이연산`, despite the shared Korean attractor `전이`.
- `Körper`: `체(體)`. The Hanja separates mathematical field from body, but its historical South-/North-Korean acceptability remains unverified.
- `algebraische Invarianten / Grundform`: `대수적 불변식 / 기본형식`. `Grundform` is the basic form whose coefficients are specialized after linear transformation; `기저형식` and other historically attested alternatives require evidence.

## Lexical-attractor basins and adverse evidence

- Established/native Korean mathematical shelf: `일대일 대응`, `대수적으로 독립/종속`, `유리 결합`, `관계식`, `항등적으로`, `불변식`, `선형 변환`.
- Sino-xenic inherited or established: `사상(寫像)`, `체(體)`, `계수(階數)`, though the exact Hanja display and historical register are not thereby authorized.
- Modern calque/coinage risk: `사상계`, `본질적으로 특징적인 수`, `전이 원리`, and compounds copied too literally from neighboring-language evidence.
- Global modern-loan alternatives: `랭크`, `전단사`; these may clarify modern mathematics while shifting register.
- Mixed/contested: `사상 함수`, `유리 결합`, `기본형식`, `두루 나타내다` for `durchlaufen`.
- False-friend risks: Hangul `계수`→coefficient, `체`→body, `전이`→the unrelated Überschiebung operator, `대응`→mere association without bijectivity, and `사상 함수`→redundant modern phrasing.

Mandarin-Simplified dominance on the accessible CJK evidence shelf is recorded only as qualitative retrieval/adverse-evidence debt. It must never become a readiness, confidence, or certification scalar. Korean choices require Korean evidence.
