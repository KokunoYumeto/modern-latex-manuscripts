## Sense windows

- `Rationalbasis`: `유리기저`. In Definition III this is a finite family whose rational combinations over `\Omega` generate every function of the system or field. It is not a linear-algebra basis over the rational numbers. `유리적 기저`, `유리 생성기저`, and `유리생성기저` remain alternatives for Korean evidence and register comparison.
- `Übertragungsprinzip`: `전이 원리`. Here the principle transfers relation-characterized existence of a rational basis from the image system to the original system. `이행 원리` remains an alternative. It must not be collapsed with `Überschiebung`, provisionally `전이연산`, despite the shared Korean attractor `전이`.
- `Körper / algebraischer Körper über`: `체(體) / ... 위의 대수적 확대체`. Bare `체` means a mathematical field; in U43 and U46 the relational phrase denotes an algebraic field extension. `體` disambiguates body only provisionally. `대수적 체`, `대수확대체`, and other historically attested possibilities require Korean evidence.
- `algebraischer Rang`: `대수적 계수(階數)`. It is the number of algebraically independent functions, closely related to transcendence degree here, not matrix rank and not coefficient `係數`. `대수적 랭크` and a transcendence-degree-explicit paraphrase remain alternatives.
- `Adjunktion / adjungieren`: `첨가 / 첨가하다`. It is field adjunction of elements or generators. `부가`, `접합`, and an explicit `원소를 첨가하여 생성하다` paraphrase remain alternatives; ordinary-language addition must not obscure the field-extension operation.
- `Zwischenkörper`: `중간체`. It is a field intermediate between the displayed base and overfield. Spaced `중간 체` and a more explicit `중간 확대체` remain alternatives; it must not be read as a physical intermediary body.
- `algebraisches Element / algebraische Erweiterung`: `대수적 원소 / 대수적 확대체`. The element is algebraic over the displayed rational-function field; the extension claims have specific base/overfield directions and finite qualifiers. `대수원소`, `대수확대`, and spacing variants remain evidence holds.
- `Elimination / Relation`: `소거 / 관계식`. U42 eliminates the variables to obtain polynomial relations in which each `$x_i$` actually occurs. `관계` may be more idiomatic in prose but can blur the displayed equation sense.
- `birationale Transformation`: `쌍유리 변환`. In U45 it is the reciprocal rational change between two generating bases, expressed by both `$\chi_i$` and `$\psi_j$`; it is not a generic reversible map. `쌍유리변환`, `이유리 변환`, and an explicative reciprocal-rational paraphrase remain alternatives.
- `ein beliebiges System / durchlaufen`: `임의의 계 / 두루 나타내다`. Quantification ranges over arbitrary independent systems or all field functions, not merely a representative sample. `전부 취하다`, `모두 달리다`, and more formal quantifier wording remain checker alternatives.
- `direkte Verallgemeinerung`: `직접 일반화`. The Steinitz citation supplies the historical referent, but the strength and direction of the claimed generalization require citation and mathematical checking.
