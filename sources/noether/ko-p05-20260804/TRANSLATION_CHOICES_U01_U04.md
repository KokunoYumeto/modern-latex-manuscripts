# Noether Paper 5 Korean producer translation choices, U01--U04

These are producer choices and held questions, not Korean validation. No external Korean reviewer, local-language corpus certification, formula review, or canon adjudication has occurred.

## Governing language controls

- Running prose uses Hangul-first South-Korean mathematical spelling provisionally.
- Hanja is not inserted into the target without Korean evidence and reviewer approval. Possible explanatory Hanja forms such as 有理函數體, 基底, 不變式, 判別式, and 終結式 are held metadata only, not endorsed target forms.
- A North-Korean standard was not selected. Forms, spacing, loanword treatment, and technical vocabulary for ko-KP remain a checker hold; ko-KR wording does not authorize ko-KP.
- Chinese, Japanese, and Korean evidence do not authorize one another. No Chinese target was inspected.
- Mandarin-Simplified dominance debt is a qualitative hard control: the broader CJK evidence shelf may overrepresent Mandarin-Simplified modern calques, so apparent cross-CJK familiarity cannot count as Korean readiness. This debt is not a score and cannot be averaged away.
