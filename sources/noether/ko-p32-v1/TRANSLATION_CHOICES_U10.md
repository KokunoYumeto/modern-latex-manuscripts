# P32-KO-U10 translation choices

This file records translator choices, not checking or certification.

- `idempotentes Element` is rendered `멱등원` (`冪等元`); the zero and unit exclusions are explicit.
- `Adjunktion` is rendered by the verb `첨가하다`; `algebraischer Zahlkörper` is `대수적 수체`.
- `Summe von drei/zwei Quadraten` is rendered `세/두 제곱의 합`.
- `Normen-Produktsatz der Quaternionen` and `verschwindende Norm` are `사원수의 노름곱 정리` and `노름이 0`.
- All displayed identities and equivalences are preserved without algebraic normalization.
- This is an unchecked ko-KR translation. No ko-KP, source, Korean-review, specialist, or external validation is claimed.
