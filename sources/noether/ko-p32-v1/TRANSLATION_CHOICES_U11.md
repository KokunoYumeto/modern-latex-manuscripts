# P32-KO-U11 translation choices

This file records translator choices, not checking or certification.

- `einseitig einfaches Ideal` and `Idealklasse` are rendered `한쪽 단순 아이디얼` and `아이디얼류`.
- `direkte Summenzerlegung`, `primitives idempotentes Element`, and `Komponenten der Einheit` are rendered `직접합 분해`, `원시 멱등원`, and `단위원의 성분`.
- `absolut einfach` is rendered `절대단순`, kept distinct from `절대기약` representation classes.
- The module-action coefficient formula and matrix $(\gamma^i_k)$ are preserved.
- Historical `nichtkommutativer Körper` retains `비가환체`; `Schurscher Index` retains `슈어 지수`.
- This is an unchecked ko-KR translation. No ko-KP, source, Korean-review, specialist, or external validation is claimed.
