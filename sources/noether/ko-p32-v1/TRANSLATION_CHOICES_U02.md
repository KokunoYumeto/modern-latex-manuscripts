This file records translator choices, not checking or certification.

- `Grundkörper`, `Zahlkörper`, and `Zerfällungskörper` are rendered `기초체`, `수체`, and `분해체`.
- `Charakter`, `Körper eines Charakters`, and `Index` are rendered `지표`, `지표체`, and `지수`.
- `absolut irreduzibler Bestandteil` and `Darstellungsklasse` are rendered `절대기약 성분` and `류`/`표현류` according to syntax.

- `konjugiert` is rendered `공액`; `einfacher Charakter` is `단순지표`.
- The footnote's `vollständig reduzible hyperkomplexe Systeme` is `완전가약 초복소수계`.
