# Visual evidence rights and disposition

This package publishes two project-generated target-reader page renders: one
`zh-Hans-CN` page and one controlled-generic `zh-Hant` page. Each record binds
the parent PDF, page, full-page pixel box, dimensions, density, rotation,
image hash, structural root, review receipts, QA state, and publication
disposition.

The one German source-control render is represented by metadata, hash,
dimensions, page number, and QA disposition only. Its pixels are deliberately
absent because this release does not assert redistribution rights over that
source-control text. No source scan, source-scan crop, or native-evidence PDF
is included.

The two target images were inspected at original detail during archive intake.
Both are legible and show no clipping, overlap, missing glyph, blank or
duplicate page, broken line, formula displacement, or orphaned content. This
visual finding does not constitute native-reader review, mathematical
certification, regional localization, or rights clearance.
