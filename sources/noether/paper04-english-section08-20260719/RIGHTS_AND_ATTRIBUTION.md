# Rights and attribution

The historical work is attributed to Emmy Noether and was originally
published in *Journal fuer die reine und angewandte Mathematik* 139 (1911),
pages 118-154. This checkpoint covers only Section 8, printed pages 146-152.

The package does not assert a blanket license over the German work, the
original scan, inherited translations, or the derivative English text. The
German and inherited-English bodies and the original scan are not distributed
here. Their hashes are retained solely for provenance and comparison control.

Any archive or repository operator must independently review attribution,
underlying rights, derivative-work status, public metadata, and permitted
scope before publication. Record-level metadata is not proof of underlying
rights.
