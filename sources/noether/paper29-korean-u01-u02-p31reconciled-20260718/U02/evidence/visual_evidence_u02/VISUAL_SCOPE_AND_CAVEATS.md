# Korean Noether Paper 29 U02 visual-evidence scope and caveats

This U02-only archive is separate from and does not modify the sealed U01 archive. It indexes eight surviving visual assets: three native-400-PPI source-page witnesses, the accepted compact German and final Korean one-page renders, two surviving pages from the superseded German 11pt control, and one independently reconstructed Korean pre-review render.

The source pages are complete printed pages 29--31. Page 29 contains pre-U02 material above § 1, and page 31 contains post-U02 material below the corollary. No derivative crop was used. Their redistribution rights have not been established, so the JPEGs remain at the private eight-file source root and are represented publicly only by filenames, hashes, dimensions, DPI, page/canvas coordinates, linked structures, QA state, and `manifest_only_rights_blocked` status.

All five PNGs in the open payload are project-generated. The accepted compact German render replaces a rejected two-page 11pt control whose second page contains only the corollary's final line. Both rejected pages remain open before/after evidence. The final Korean render replaces a prior reviewed state. That prior TeX was reconstructed by reversing the three exact edits preserved in a saved patch event; the reconstructed TeX has the recorded historical SHA-256, and its 180-DPI rendered pixels exactly reproduce the recorded historical PNG SHA-256. The regenerated PDF does not reproduce the unavailable historical PDF binary and is labeled as a reconstruction.

An still earlier Korean render with a stranded footnote marker was overwritten before hashing. It has no recoverable file or defensible hash. The metadata records that loss explicitly and does not invent an asset identifier, byte count, or digest.

Every surviving visual was inspected at original resolution. The accepted renders show no clipping, overlap, missing glyphs, black boxes, margin overflow, blank output, unreadable mathematics, or footnote collision. The superseded German second page is retained precisely because its one-line pagination is a rejected QA outcome. These are internal model checks, not external human, source-critical, rights, community, or Korean-specialist certification.

The ZIP is a bounded handoff candidate for the existing GitHub/Zenodo route. It contains only the five open PNGs and public-safe metadata. It creates no release, draft, DOI, publication, rights determination, or certification claim.
