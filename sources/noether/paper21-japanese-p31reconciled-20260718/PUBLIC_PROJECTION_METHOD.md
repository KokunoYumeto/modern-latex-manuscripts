# Public projection method

This is a publication-safe projection of the internally frozen Japanese Paper 21 checkpoint.
The original 39-row manifest and checksum inventory are retained under
`INTERNAL_*_BINDING` filenames. Host-absolute paths and internal routing labels are
replaced by relative or logical identifiers. Raw path-bearing TeX logs and host-bound
validation scripts are excluded; concise public build records and a package-local
validator replace them. Exact target TeX, PDF, extracted text, source/control TeX where
included, and project-generated render bytes are otherwise preserved.

No original-print pixels are present. The three included PNGs are project-generated target renders.
