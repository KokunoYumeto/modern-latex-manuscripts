## Mathematical significance

Two clusters are mathematically significant rather than merely typographic:

- Formula (2) had become grammatically and semantically malformed when z aus K was modernized to z in K but the source preposition aus and conjunction remained. The repaired text now has the source relation and note scope.
- Three ideal-prime symbols had been flattened to plain Latin p in p-adic terminology while the adjacent subscript and Primideal used fraktur p. The source uses one coherent ideal-prime family.

The remaining corrections restore note scope, printed bibliographic content, emphasis, and whether two class-group expressions are inline or detached displays.
