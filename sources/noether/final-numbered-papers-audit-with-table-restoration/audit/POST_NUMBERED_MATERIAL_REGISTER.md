# Post-numbered material register

The final cumulative translation in this package covers the numbered Noether papers 1--43 only, source PDF pages 41--724. The source volume continues after page 724 with material that is not part of the numbered paper run:

- Source pages 725--777: `Algebra der hyperkomplexen Größen`, lecture by Prof. E. Noether, winter semester 1929/30, worked out by M. Deuring.
- Source pages 778--789: H. Kapferer, `Notwendige und hinreichende Multiplizitätsbedingungen zum Noetherschen Fundamentalsatz der algebraischen Funktionen`, with an added supplement jointly with E. Noether.
- Source pages 790--796: lists of short communications and book reviews by Emmy Noether, plus terminal bibliographic/index material.

These are logged separately because the previous sequence was scoped and labelled as the numbered-paper corpus. They should be treated before a claim of full-volume textual completion, but not as gaps in papers 1--43.
