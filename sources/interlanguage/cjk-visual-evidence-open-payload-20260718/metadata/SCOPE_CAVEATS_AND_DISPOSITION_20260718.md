# CJK visual-evidence handoff scope and caveats — 2026-07-18

This bounded package inventories every image file currently present under the CJK lane root, including target renders, source-control renders, contact sheets, before/after states, recovered QA renders, external reference-repository images, and scan-page renders. No segmentation masks or model-overlay image files were found at this cursor.

Project-generated and project-recovered render evidence is included in the open payload. External repository imagery and scan pixels remain preserved at their exact private local paths but are not redistributed: their public-safe metadata, hashes, dimensions, coordinates, QA state, and rights-blocked disposition are included instead. Rights are not inferred from filenames, a repository's mere presence, or a license-badge image.

Visual evidence documents provenance and review. It does not certify the underlying transcription, translation, mathematical content, rights determination, source fidelity, or native-language correctness. Parent-PDF associations are deterministic candidates and require manual provenance confirmation where the index reports uncertainty. Temporary and before-state renders are intentionally retained; known supersession is stated per record rather than implemented by deletion.

Archive maintenance owns GitHub/Zenodo publication. This package is intended for the existing concept record and must not create a competing draft.
