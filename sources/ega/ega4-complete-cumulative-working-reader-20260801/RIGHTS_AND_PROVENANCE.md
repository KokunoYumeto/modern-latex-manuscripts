# Rights and Provenance

The French source basis is the four EGA IV installments published in *Publications Mathematiques de l'IHES* and made available through NUMDAM. Those authority PDFs are not duplicated in this package.

The editable English source is a cumulative source-aligned working translation. No blanket license or redistribution right is asserted for the underlying French work, the English reconstruction, or the package as a whole. Rights remain with their respective holders.

Source corrections are disclosed in the English text rather than silently attributed to the French source. In particular, the reader identifies the printed `(4.7.14)` reference as a source slip and links the relevant Proposition 4.7.13.
