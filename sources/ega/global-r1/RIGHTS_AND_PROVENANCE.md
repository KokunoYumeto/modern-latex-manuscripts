# Rights and provenance

## Controlling French sources

The English volumes are source-aligned to Alexander Grothendieck and Jean
Dieudonné's published EGA series. The controlling witnesses were frozen NUMDAM
PDFs for EGA I, II, III (parts 1 and 2), and IV (parts 1–4). EGA 0 is the
preliminary material printed across the corresponding EGA I, III, and IV
publications. No French authority PDF is redistributed in this package.

## English and TeX lineage

The editable base descends from the public `ryankeleti/ega` community
translation lineage, preserved in local snapshots including
`fc261645438fbeb2e1b3ca1052c4ba9a489f3628` and `3d7afe28`. Contributor
records name Mateo Carmona, Tim Holzschuh, Tim Hosgood, Ryan Keleti, Thiago
Solovera & Nery, and Wayne Yang. The present sources preserve that provenance
and add source-first completion, formula/diagram reconstruction, corrections,
and a cumulative stable-link layer.

Existing OCR and comparison text were locator/drafting witnesses only. No new
OCR was generated for this cumulative reader, and no OCR witness is included.
