## English lineage

The editable base descends from the pre-existing human community translation
in the `ryankeleti/ega` project, locally frozen at the snapshot identified as
`3d7afe28`. Its contributor record names Mateo Carmona, Tim Holzschuh, Tim
Hosgood, Ryan Keleti, Thiago Solovera & Nery, and Wayne Yang. That snapshot
described EGA IV as incomplete. This successor completes and source-aligns the
whole EGA-IV reader against the four NUMDAM authorities, including substantial
manual reconstruction and correction, then adds the stable reference layer.

OCR and other inherited comparison text were locator/drafting witnesses only;
they are neither controlling sources nor redistributed here.
