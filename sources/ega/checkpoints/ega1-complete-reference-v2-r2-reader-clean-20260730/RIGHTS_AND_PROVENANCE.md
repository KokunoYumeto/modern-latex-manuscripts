# Rights and provenance

## Controlling source

The translation is source-aligned to Alexander Grothendieck and Jean
Dieudonné, *Éléments de géométrie algébrique I: Le langage des schémas*,
Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS 4 (1960). The frozen NUMDAM authority PDF
has 227 physical pages, 31,680,717 bytes, and SHA-256
`9ABA23020217535977E279BDD06A0413F48DA703086865BA4C00766C85DF4AE6`.
The inserted EGA I §1.8 addenda were checked against the EGA II NUMDAM volume,
219 physical pages / 27,414,108 bytes / SHA-256
`111834EFFFE9E90D068389D418F08925A82B4A54AE2957F080712D4180E032EB`.
Neither French authority PDF is redistributed in this package.

## English lineage

The editable English base is the pre-existing human community translation in
the `ryankeleti/ega` project. The upstream README describes EGA I as complete and proofread by Tim
Hosgood (`@thosgood`) and names Mateo Carmona, Tim Holzschuh, Tim Hosgood,
Ryan Keleti, Thiago Solovera & Nery, and Wayne Yang among the contributors.

The completed source-aligned predecessor was checked sequentially against the
rendered NUMDAM authority through EOF. This successor adds exact anchors,
local links, guarded unavailable cross-volume references, and PDF metadata,
and removes two redundant contents registrations. Reversing the reference
layer and restoring those two historical R1 lines reconstructs all 16
predecessor source files byte-for-byte.
