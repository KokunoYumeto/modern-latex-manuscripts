# Publication caveats

- Scope is two standalone readers: EGA 0 through section 13 and published EGA
  III sections 1–7. Unpublished planned EGA III sections 8–11 are not supplied.
- Standalone cross-volume references remain visible but intentionally do not
  become broken PDF actions. EGA 0 retains four external I/II/IV reference
  warnings. EGA III retains 682 external-reference warning occurrences (372
  unique targets) to EGA 0, I, II, and one external G locator.
- All 1,993 delivered GoTo actions resolve. There are no duplicate destinations,
  fatal TeX errors, missing glyph diagnostics, or rerun warnings in the final
  four-pass builds.
- The final PDFs are not claimed to be tagged or accessibility remediated.
- The NUMDAM French authorities and OCR witnesses are excluded.
- Provenance is recorded, but no license is invented for the inherited
  community translation or underlying French source.
- Publication, repository readback, and archival custody are not claimed until
  an external publication system returns exact identities.
