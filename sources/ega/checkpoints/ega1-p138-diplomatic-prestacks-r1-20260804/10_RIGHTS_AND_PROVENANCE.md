# Rights and provenance

The authority is the NUMDAM EGA corpus identified in the validators. No authority PDF, publisher scan, source-page raster, or third-party comparison file is included. Underlying-work and scan rights remain with their rightsholders; no package-wide license is invented.

French TeX is a diplomatic project transcription. English TeX is a separately ledgered source-rechecked project layer. Generated bounded readers and their QA artifacts are included as reproducibility evidence, not as critical-edition or mathematician-review certification. Raw private logs remain in separate custody; the public surfaces are minimally transformed with every replacement event recorded by token length and SHA-256.
