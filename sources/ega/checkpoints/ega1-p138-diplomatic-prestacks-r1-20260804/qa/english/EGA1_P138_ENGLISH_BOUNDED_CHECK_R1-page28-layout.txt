Corollary (5.5.7). — An afﬁne scheme is separated (and is thus a scheme, which justiﬁes the terminol-
ogy of (5.4.1)).
Corollary (5.5.8). — Let Y be an afﬁne scheme; for f : X → Y to be a separated morphism, it is necessary
and sufﬁcient for X to be separated (in other words, for X to be a scheme).
Proof. Indeed, we see that the criterion of (5.5.6) does not depend on f .                                   □

Corollary (5.5.9). — For a morphism f : X → Y to be separated, it is necessary for the induced prescheme
 f −1 (U ) to be separated for every open subset U on which Y induces a separated prescheme, and it is sufﬁcient
for this to hold for every afﬁne open subset U ⊂ Y.
Proof. The necessity of the condition follows from (5.5.4) and (5.5.1, ii); the sufﬁciency follows from
(5.5.4) and (5.5.8), taking into account the existence of afﬁne open covers of Y.                     □

   In particular, if X and Y are afﬁne schemes, then every morphism X → Y is separated.
