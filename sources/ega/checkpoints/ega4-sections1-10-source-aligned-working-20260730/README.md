# EGA IV Sections 1-10 source-aligned English working reader

This checkpoint preserves the cumulative source-aligned English working reader
for EGA IV Sections 1 through 10.

## Scope

- Continuous authority coverage through the end of Section 10.
- Continuation: EGA IV Section 11.
- Forty sealed source files plus one reader-clean public wrapper.
- No raster dependency in the reader.

The source-era mathematical and editorial content remains in the reader.
Project, model, workflow, source-status, comparison-lineage, and private-path
material is absent. This is a working scholarly translation, not complete
EGA IV, complete EGA, a critical edition, rights clearance, peer review,
mathematical certification, or tagged-PDF accessibility remediation.

## Authority and lineage

The controlling authority is the NUMDAM EGA IV Part 2 French original,
228 pages, 31,819,022 bytes, SHA-256
`C3E960AA1C5C37046E8892D8A3CAC098E2738164136B5CDAA5D5D893F89931DA`.
That PDF is already present on the EGA record and is not duplicated here.

The broader editable English tree descends from the public
[`ryankeleti/ega`](https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega) project. The frozen
comparison snapshot used during intake was commit
`3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971`. No repository-root license
grant was found in that snapshot, so this checkpoint does not infer one.
Other English comparisons and the user's pre-existing OCR were drafting or
locator witnesses only; the French source remained controlling.
