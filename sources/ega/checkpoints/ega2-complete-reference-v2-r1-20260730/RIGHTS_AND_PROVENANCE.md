# Rights and provenance

## Controlling source

The translation is source-aligned to the NUMDAM scan of Alexander Grothendieck
and Jean Dieudonné, *Éléments de géométrie algébrique II: Étude globale
élémentaire de quelques classes de morphismes*, Publications Mathématiques de
l'IHÉS 8 (1961).  The frozen authority PDF is 27,414,108 bytes, 219 physical
pages, SHA-256
`111834EFFFE9E90D068389D418F08925A82B4A54AE2957F080712D4180E032EB`.
It is the mathematical/source witness and is not redistributed here.

## English lineage

The inherited editable English base comes from the community EGA translation
tree preserved in the local `modern-latex-manuscripts` mirror.  The mirror is
at commit `fc261645438fbeb2e1b3ca1052c4ba9a489f3628`; the latest commit touching
the EGA II source set in that mirror is
`422795c4a74c98de6913e7485a20a34a7d41b353`.  Its book front matter credits
Mateo Carmona, Tim Holzschuh, Tim Hosgood, Ryan Keleti, Thiago Solovera & Nery,
and Wayne Yang.

A fresh comparison snapshot of `ryankeleti/ega` was frozen at commit
`3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971`.  The snapshot contained no root
`LICENSE` file; no license grant is inferred.  Comparison material is not a
French source authority.

The source-aligned predecessor was complete through the authority's terminal
Errata/Addenda.  This successor preserves that English body and pagination.
Its substantive source corrections are two local equation-reference repairs;
the other edits add exact anchors, ten prose links, 252 index links, guarded
unavailable cross-volume references, and PDF metadata.  Baseline and final
extracted text and word order agree on all 165 pages.
