# Rights, redistribution, and claim limits

## Included project artifacts

This deposit includes project-produced analytical evidence, decision and correction ledgers, project English readers, and editable project TeX layers. Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 applies to the project-authored analytical material and project-produced translation/source layers only to the extent that the depositor is legally able to license them.

No license is granted here to Jean-Pierre Serre's underlying 1955 French article, its journal scan, or any third-party material. Rights in those works remain with their respective holders.

## Excluded authority and comparator files

The full French authority scan is not redistributed. It is cited by DOI and bound by file identity.

The pre-existing English translation by Piotr Achinger and Łukasz Krupa is credited and bound by public URLs and hashes, but its PDF and source archive are not redistributed because no explicit redistribution license was located in the acquired source tree:

- `https://achinger.impan.pl/fac/fac.pdf`
- `https://achinger.impan.pl/fac/fac.tar.gz`

Only project-authored, locator-bound comparative analysis is included.

## Scholarly status

The readers and TeX are working scholarly artifacts, not a critical edition, certified translation, peer-reviewed publication, accessibility certification, or rights-clearance opinion. Neither English lineage is authority. Citation-critical and mathematically delicate passages should be checked against the published French source.

The record documents one bounded blind comparison. It does not claim model-wide competence, a scalar accuracy rate, universal superiority, or canonicity of the wider SGA/EGA corpus.
