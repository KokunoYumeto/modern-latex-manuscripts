# Source basis

Primary witness: `scans/full/gauss_werke_II_full.pdf`, Gauss Werke Band II.

New work: Dedekind's `Bemerkungen zur Abhandlung De nexu...`, printed pp. 292--303 / full source PDF pages 302--313.

The Archive text layer was used as a secondary OCR witness; the rendered page images and source scan are authoritative. Printed p.304 is blank/separator and p.305 begins the next unit, `Geometrische Seite der ternären Formen`; both are included only as preview scans.

No printed diagrams or figures occur on pp.292--303. Dedekind mentions geometrical figures in the manuscript material for positive determinants, but no such figure is printed in this source range.
