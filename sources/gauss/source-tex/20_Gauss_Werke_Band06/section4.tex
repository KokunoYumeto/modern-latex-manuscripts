\subsection*{2.}

Liceat his addere quaedam calculi compendia, quibus saepius in determina-
tione prima orbitae parabolicae secundum methodum clar.\ Olbers commode usus
sum, et per quae methodus ista iam per se tam expedita adhuc magis contrahi
vel ad calculum numericum magis idonea reddi videtur. Referuntur ea ad
computum radiorum vectorum, atque imprimis chordae inter locum primum
et ultimum. Clar.\ Olbers ad hunc finem adhibet expressiones huius formae
$\sqrt{f + g\rho + h\rho\rho}$, atque coefficientes $f$, $g$, $h$ per operationes satis quidem sim-
plices determinat, ita tamen comparatas, ut in plerisque casibus praecisio suffi-
ciens obtineri nequeat, nisi tabulis logarithmorum maioribus ad septem vel sal-
tem ad sex figuras decimales constructis perficiantur. Illarum expressionum loco
alias substitui, quae tum per se aliquanto magis commodae ad calculum videntur,
tum eo quoque nomine se commendant, quod omnibus operationibus quinque de-
cimales adeoque tabulae logarithmorum minores sufficiunt. Ea summa hisce
momentis innititur. Sint

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l@{\hspace{2em}}l@{}}
$\theta$, $\theta'$, $\theta''$ & longitudines Solis in observatione prima, secunda, tertia \\
$R$, $R'$, $R''$ & distantiae Solis a terra \\
$\alpha$, $\alpha'$, $\alpha''$ & longitudines atque \\
$\delta$, $\delta'$, $\delta''$ & latitudines geocentricae cometae \\
$r$, $r'$, $r''$ & distantiae eiusdem a sole \\
$\rho$, $\rho'$, $\rho''$ & eiusdem distantiae curtatae a terra \\
$t$, $t'$, $t''$ & tempora observationum \\
$k$ & linea inter cometae locum primum atque tertium
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

His positis facile perspicitur haberi
\begin{align*}
r &= \sqrt{(\rho\cos\alpha - R\cos\theta)^2 + (\rho\sin\alpha - R\sin\theta)^2 + \rho\rho\tan^2\delta} \tag{1} \\
r'' &= \sqrt{(M\rho\cos\alpha'' - R''\cos\theta'')^2 + (M\rho\sin\alpha'' - R''\sin\theta'')^2 + MM\rho\rho\tan^2\delta''} \tag{2} \\
k &= \sqrt{(M\rho\cos\alpha'' - \rho\cos\alpha - R''\cos\theta'' + R\cos\theta)^2} \nonumber \\
&\quad \sqrt{+\, (M\rho\sin\alpha'' - \rho\sin\alpha - R''\sin\theta'' + R\sin\theta)^2} \nonumber \\
&\quad \sqrt{+\, (M\rho\tan\delta'' - \rho\tan\delta)^2} \tag{3}
\end{align*}

Aequationes 1, 2 induunt formam hancce
\begin{align*}
r &= \sqrt{\frac{\rho\rho}{\cos^2\delta} - 2\rho R\cos(\alpha - \theta) + RR} \\
r'' &= \sqrt{\frac{MM\rho\rho}{\cos^2\delta''} - 2M\rho R''\cos(\alpha'' - \theta'') + R''R''}
\end{align*}

Statuendo itaque
\begin{align*}
\frac{R\cos\delta\cos(\alpha - \theta)}{\cos\delta} &= \cos\psi, \quad R\sin\psi = B \\
\frac{R''\cos\delta''\cos(\alpha'' - \theta'')}{\cos\delta''} &= \cos\psi'', \quad R''\sin\psi'' = B''
\end{align*}
habebimus
\begin{align*}
r &= \sqrt{\Bigl(\frac{\rho}{\cos\delta} - R\cos\psi\Bigr)^2 + BB} \\
r'' &= \sqrt{\Bigl(\frac{M\rho}{\cos\delta''} - R''\cos\psi''\Bigr)^2 + B''B''}
\end{align*}

Determinando porro quinque quantitates auxiliares $g$, $G$, $h$, $H$, $C$, ita ut
habeatur
\begin{align*}
R''\cos\theta'' - R\cos\theta &= g\cos G \\
R''\sin\theta'' - R\sin\theta &= g\sin G \\
M\cos\alpha'' - \cos\alpha &= h\cos C\cos H \\
M\sin\alpha'' - \sin\alpha &= h\cos C\sin H \\
M\tan\delta'' - \tan\delta &= h\sin C
\end{align*}
formula 3 transit in sequentem
\begin{align*}
k &= \sqrt{(\rho h\cos C\cos H - g\cos G)^2 + (\rho h\cos C\sin H - g\sin G)^2 + \rho\rho h h\sin^2 C} \\
&= \sqrt{\rho\rho h h - 2\rho hg\cos C\cos(G - H) + gg}
\end{align*}

Quodsi itaque statuimus
\[
\cos C\cos(G - H) = \cos\varphi, \quad g\sin\varphi = A
\]
erit
\[
k = \sqrt{(\rho h - g\cos\varphi)^2 + AA}
\]
aut si insuper statuimus $\rho h - g\cos\varphi = u$,
\[
k = \sqrt{uu + AA}
\]

Gratum fore censemus pluribus lectoribus, si non modo complexum omnium
operationum ad has transformationes pertinentium rite ordinatum hic denuo si-
stamus, sed insuper omnes reliquas operationes simul adiiciamus, ita ut omnia,
quae ad calculum primum orbitae parabolicae requiruntur, hic iuncta invenian-
tur. Simul haec praecepta per numeros a cometa nostro desumtos illustrabimus.
Eligimus itaque observationes nostras dierum 7, 14, 21 Aprilis, quarum reductio
sequentia data administrat:

\subsection*{3.}

I.\ Operatio prima consistit in determinatione valoris approximati ipsius $M$,
quem suppeditat formula
\[
M = \frac{t'' - t'}{t' - t} \cdot
\frac{\tan\delta'\sin(\alpha - \theta) - \tan\delta\sin(\alpha' - \theta')}
     {\tan\delta''\sin(\alpha' - \theta') - \tan\delta'\sin(\alpha'' - \theta'')}
\]
In exemplo nostro invenitur $\log M = 9.75799$.

II.\ Tunc determinare oportet quantitates $g$, $G$, $h$, $H$, $C$ per formulas
sequentes, quas supra traditis aequivalentes sed ad calculum commodiores esse
patet.
\begin{align*}
R''\cos(\theta'' - \theta) - R &= g\cos(G - \theta) \\
R''\sin(\theta'' - \theta) &= g\sin(G - \theta) \\
M - \cos(\alpha'' - \alpha) &= h\cos C\cos(H - \alpha'') \\
\sin(\alpha'' - \alpha) &= h\cos C\sin(H - \alpha'') \\
M\tan\delta'' - \tan\delta &= h\sin C
\end{align*}
In exemplo nostro provenit
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l@{\hspace{3em}}l@{}}
$G = 113\textdegree\ 43\textquotesingle\ 57\textquotedbl$ & $\log g = 9.38029$ \\
$H = 109\textdegree\ 5\textquotesingle\ 49\textquotedbl$ & \\
$C = 44\textdegree\ 13\textquotesingle\ 9\textquotedbl$ & $\log h = 9.81477$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

III.\ Postea statuemus
\begin{align*}
\cos C\cos(G - H) &= \cos\varphi \\
\cos\delta\cos(\alpha - \theta) &= \cos\psi \\
\cos\delta''\cos(\alpha'' - \theta'') &= \cos\psi'' \\
R\sin\psi &= B \\
R''\sin\psi'' &= B''
\end{align*}
In hoc calculo si forte cosinus angulorum $\varphi$, $\psi$, $\psi''$ parum ab unitate diversi eva-
dant, figuras sex vel adeo septem adhibere conveniet. Ceterum angulos $\varphi$, $\psi$, $\psi''$
ipsos gradibus minutis et secundis describere non necessarium est, quum suffi-
ciat, statim a logarithmis cosinuum ad logarithmos sinuum transire.

In exemplo nostro fit
\begin{align*}
\log B &= 9.22527 \\
\log B' &= 9.98706 \\
\log B'' &= 9.86038
\end{align*}

IV.\ Denique statuatur
\begin{align*}
h\cos\psi &= b \\
h\cos\psi'' &= b'' \\
b - R\cos\varphi &= c \\
b'' - R''\cos\varphi &= c''
\end{align*}
In exemplo nostro
\begin{align*}
\log h &= 9.75645 \\
\log b'' &= 0.05028 \\
c &= +0.31365 \\
c'' &= +0.95443
\end{align*}

V.\ His ita praeparatis radii vectores $r$, $r''$ atque chorda $k$ pendent ab in-
cognita $u$ sequenti modo:
\begin{align*}
r &= \frac{A}{\cos\delta} \\
r'' &= \frac{MA}{\cos\delta''} \\
k &= \sqrt{uu + AA}
\end{align*}
quam incognitam ita per tentamina determinare oportet, ut satisfiat aequationi
\[
(r + r'' + k)^{\frac{3}{2}} - (r + r'' - k)^{\frac{3}{2}} = m
\]
ubi notandum, $m$ designare tempus $9.6887401$ dierum atque esse
\[
\log m = 0.9862673
\]
Quantitati $(r + r'' - k)^{\frac{3}{2}}$ signum $+$ praefigere oporteret, si motus heliocentricus
cometae intra tempus $t'' - t$ angulum $180\textdegree$ superaret, sed hic casus in supposi-
tionibus, quibus haecce prima orbitae determinatio innititur, occurrere nequit.
Ceterum vix opus erit monere, calculum numericum pro $r$ ita instituendum esse,
ut introducatur angulus auxiliaris $\vartheta$, talis ut sit
\[
\frac{A}{\cos\delta} = \frac{u + c}{\cos\vartheta}
\]
hincque statuendo
\[
\frac{u + c}{\cos\vartheta} = \tan\eta
\]
fieri $r = A\sec\eta$ ac perinde pro $r''$ et $k$. Nec non quisque sponte videbit,
in omnibus his operationibus percommode adhiberi posse tabulam nostram pro
logarithmis summarum atque differentiarum immediate inveniendis.

In exemplo nostro habetur $\log A = 0.16139$, paucisque tentaminibus
eruitur $u = 0.24388$.

VI.\ Inventa quantitate $u$, habemus
\[
\rho = \frac{u + c}{h\cos\psi}, \quad \rho'' = \frac{u + c''}{h\cos\psi''}
\]
(in exemplo nostro $\log\rho = 9.80364$, $\log\rho'' = 9.56163$).

Operationes reliquae satis quidem sunt notae: sed ut omnia hic adsint, for-
mulas reliquas quoque, quibus uti solemus, apponere visum est. Sint itaque

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l@{\hspace{2em}}l@{}}
$\lambda$, $\lambda''$ & longitudines heliocentricae cometae in observatione prima atque tertia \\
$\beta$, $\beta''$ & latitudines heliocentricae \\
$\omega$, $\omega''$ & longitudines in orbita \\
$\beta$ & longitudo nodi ascendentis \\
$i$ & inclinatio orbitae inter $0$ et $90\textdegree$ accipienda, si secundum modum vul- \\
& garem motum directum retrogradumque distinguimus \\
$\varpi$ & longitudo perihelii \\
$T$ & tempus transitus per perihelium \\
$q$ & distantia in perihelio
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

VII.\ Positiones heliocentricae invenientur per formulas
\begin{align*}
\rho\cos(\alpha - \theta) - R &= r\cos\beta\cos(\lambda - \theta) \\
\rho\sin(\alpha - \theta) &= r\cos\beta\sin(\lambda - \theta) \\
\rho\tan\delta &= r\sin\beta \\
\rho''\cos(\alpha'' - \theta'') - R'' &= r''\cos\beta''\cos(\lambda'' - \theta'') \\
\rho''\sin(\alpha'' - \theta'') &= r''\cos\beta''\sin(\lambda'' - \theta'') \\
\rho''\tan\delta'' &= r''\sin\beta''
\end{align*}

Consensus valorum pro radiis vectoribus $r$, $r''$ hinc prodeuntium cum iis,
qui antea ex $u$ deducti fuerant, calculo confirmando inserviet. Motus directus
erit vel retrogradus, prout $\lambda''$ maior evadit quam $\lambda$, vel minor.

In exemplo nostro invenimus
\begin{align*}
\lambda &= 225\textdegree\ 4\textquotesingle\ 22\textquotedbl, \quad \beta = +14\textdegree\ 51\textquotesingle\ 39\textquotedbl, \quad \log r = 0.13896 \\
\lambda'' &= 223\textdegree\ 6\textquotesingle\ 55\textquotedbl, \quad \beta'' = +2\textdegree\ 49\textquotesingle\ 28\textquotedbl, \quad \log r'' = 0.11068
\end{align*}
Cometae itaque motus est retrogradus.

VIII.\ Ad inveniendam longitudinem nodi atque inclinationem, adhibean-
tur formulae:
\begin{align*}
\tan i\sin(\lambda - \beta) &= \tan\beta \\
\tan i\cos(\lambda - \beta) &= \tan\beta''\sec(\lambda'' - \lambda)
\end{align*}
ubi signa superiora referuntur ad motum directum, inferiora ad retrogradum.

Dein longitudines in orbita eruuntur per formulas:
\begin{align*}
\sin(\omega - \beta) &= \sin(\lambda - \beta)\sec i \\
\cos(\omega - \beta) &= \cos(\lambda - \beta)\cos\beta\sec\beta'
\end{align*}
ubi $\omega - \beta$, $\omega'' - \beta$ in iisdem resp.\ quadrantibus accipere oportet, in quibus sunt
$\lambda - \beta$, $\lambda'' - \beta$.

Pro cometa nostro invenimus
\begin{align*}
\beta &= 42\textdegree\ 40\textquotesingle\ 8\textquotedbl \\
i &= 81\textdegree\ 1\textquotesingle\ 3\textquotedbl \\
\omega &= 237\textdegree\ 43\textquotesingle\ 7\textquotedbl \\
\omega'' &= 225\textdegree\ 31\textquotesingle\ 32\textquotedbl
\end{align*}

IX.\ Longitudinem perihelii atque distantiam in perihelio dant formulae
\begin{align*}
\tan\tfrac{1}{2}(\omega'' - \omega)\tan\tfrac{1}{2}(\omega'' + \omega - 2\varpi) &= \frac{r - r''}{r + r''} \\
q &= r\sin^2\tfrac{1}{2}(\omega - \varpi) = r''\sin^2\tfrac{1}{2}(\omega'' - \varpi)
\end{align*}
Pro cometa nostro $\varpi = 197\textdegree\ 37\textquotesingle\ 51\textquotedbl$, $\log q = 0.08469$.

X.\ Denique e tabula Barkeri desumantur motus medii anomaliis veris
$\omega - \varpi$, $\omega'' - \varpi$ vel $\varpi - \omega$, $\varpi - \omega''$ respondentes, qui sint $M$, $M''$. Tunc erit
\[
T = t - \frac{r\sqrt{r}}{n}(M - \sin M) = t'' - \frac{r''\sqrt{r''}}{n}(M'' - \sin M'')
\]
ubi signa superiora valent, si in motu directo $\omega > \varpi$, $\omega'' > \varpi$ vel in motu retro-
grado $\omega < \varpi$, $\omega'' < \varpi$; inferiora in casibus oppositis. Quantitas $n$ est constans,
ipsiusque logarithmus $= 0.0398723$. Consensus duorum valorum ipsius $T$ se-
cundam calculi confirmationem subministrat.

In exemplo nostro invenimus
\begin{align*}
T &= 49.518 \\
T &= 49.517
\end{align*}
ita ut pro tempore transitus per perihelium adoptari possit Maii d.\ 19.517.

Quodsi ex hisce elementis locus geocentricus pro tempore observationis me-
diae computatur, prodit longitudo $266\textdegree\ 27\textquotesingle\ 15\textquotedbl$, latitudo $22\textdegree\ 52\textquotesingle\ 18\textquotedbl$ bor., illa
$7\textquotedbl$ ab observata discrepans, haec ex asse consentiens.
