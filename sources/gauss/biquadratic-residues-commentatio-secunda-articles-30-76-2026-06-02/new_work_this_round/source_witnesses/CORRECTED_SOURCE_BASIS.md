# Corrected source basis for Articles 66-76

Primary source witness: C. F. Gauss, *Werke*, Band II, Internet Archive scan `werkecarlf02gausrich`, printed pp.137-148 / PDF pages 147-158.

Working text basis: the Latin Wikisource transcription was used as a navigational and text-entry aid. The source scan range packaged here is the controlling witness for article boundaries, formulas, tables, and page placement.

Scan-controlled corrections / normalizations applied:
- Article 71: in the product-exponent formula the final term is read and typeset as `3c'''`, consistently with the theorem statement and with the immediately following character formula.
- Article 73: a stray terminal letter sometimes present in extracted text after `-\frac18(bb+2b)` is not part of the formula and is omitted.
- Article 76: printed p.148 closes the memoir text. Printed p.149 begins subsequent non-article material and is outside the Article 66-76 work range.

Scope boundary:
- The new work begins at Article 66. Because Article 66 begins on printed p.137 after the end of Article 65, that page is included in the new source-scan witness even though it also contains the previous article's end.
- The cumulative work now covers Articles 30-76.
