\begin{center}\Large ON CALCULATING COMMON LOGARITHMS.\end{center}
Calculate the logarithms of
\[
\begin{aligned}
\log\frac{1025}{*}&=a\\
\log\frac{1024^2}{*}&=b\\
\log\frac{81^2}{*}&=c\\
\log\frac{125^2}{*}&=d\\
\log\frac{99^2}{*}&=e
\end{aligned}
\]
($*$ denotes a denominator that is $1$ less than the numerator) then, as is easily checked:
\[
\log 2=\frac{14\frac34+\frac54a+2b-\frac34c-2d+e}{49}=f
\]
It is also easy to derive
\[
\begin{aligned}
\log 41&=a+12f-2=g\\
\log 3&=\frac{1+c+4f+g}{8}
\end{aligned}
\]
