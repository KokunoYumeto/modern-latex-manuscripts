This selection contains the historical title matter and first five complete papers of Volume II: A New Proof of an Arithmetical Theorem (Articles 1–7); Summation of Certain Special Series (Articles 1–36); New Proofs and Extensions of the Fundamental Theorem in the Theory of Quadratic Residues, with its introduction, fifth proof (Articles 1–3), sixth proof (Articles 1–6) and new algorithm (Articles 1–6); and both papers on the Theory of Biquadratic Residues (Articles 1–23 and 24–76).
