This selection contains the historical title matter and first three complete papers of Volume II: A New Proof of an Arithmetical Theorem (\mbox{Articles 1–7}), presented on January 15, 1808; Summation of Certain Special Series (\mbox{Articles 1–36}), presented on August 24, 1808; and New Proofs and Extensions of the Fundamental Theorem in the Theory of Quadratic Residues, presented on February 10, 1817. The third paper contains the introduction, fifth proof (\mbox{Articles 1–3}), sixth proof (\mbox{Articles 1–6}) and new algorithm (\mbox{Articles 1–6}). The fourth paper, Theory of Biquadratic Residues, First Paper (Articles 1–23), presented on April 5, 1825, is included in full. The fifth paper, Theory of Biquadratic Residues, Second Paper, presented on April 15, 1831, is included in full, with its historical title and Articles 24–76. The self-reviews of May 12 and September 19, 1808, March 10, 1817, and April 11, 1825 are also included. Their German originals are supplied separately; the German reader presents modern German renderings. Later texts of Volume II lie beyond this selection. Volume I accompanies it in the collected reader.

Historical source: Carl Friedrich Gauss, Werke, Volume II, second printing, Göttingen, 1876.
