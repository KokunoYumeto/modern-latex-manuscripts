\begin{center}\textbf{GAUSS TO DIRICHLET.}\end{center}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\hline
To Monsieur&\\
\quad Monsieur \textsc{Lejeune Dirichlet}&\quad in Paris.\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
I would have thanked you earlier for the memoir you so kindly sent me and for the great pleasure it gave me, had I not wished first to hear something of the outcome of my efforts in Berlin concerning your wishes and, I may add, my own. I am extremely pleased now to see, from a letter received from the Secretary of the Academy in Berlin, that we may hope that they will soon be willing to secure a suitable permanent position for you in your homeland.
