\begin{center}\emph{Distribution of Quadratic Residues in Eighths.}\end{center}
$p$ a prime; $(r)$ the number of quadratic residues of $p$ that lie between $(r-1)\frac p8$ and $r\frac p8$.

First case; $p=8n+1$.
\[
\begin{aligned}
2t&\quad\text{number of classes for the determinant }-p;\\
2u&\quad\text{number of classes for the determinant }-2p.
\end{aligned}
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
(1)=(8)&=\tfrac14(2n+t+u)\\
(2)=(4)=(5)=(7)&=\tfrac14(2n+t-u)\\
(3)=(6)&=\tfrac14(2n-3t+u)
\end{aligned}
\]
