For a negative determinant $-p$, which\begingroup\renewcommand{\thefootnote}{***)}\footnote{[that is, when $p$ is a positive prime of the form $4n+1$.]}\endgroup\ is a prime of the form $4n+1$, the number of classes is\begingroup\renewcommand{\thefootnote}{$\dagger$)}\footnote{[the number of classes is $=2(\alpha-\beta)$.]}\endgroup\ $=(\alpha-\beta)$, where $\alpha$ is the number of quadratic residues in the first quarter
\[
1\pdot2\pdot3\dotsfour\tfrac14(p-1)
\]
and $\beta$ the number of nonresidues.
