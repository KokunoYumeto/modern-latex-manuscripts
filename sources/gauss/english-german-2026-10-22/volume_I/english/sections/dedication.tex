\textsc{MOST SERENE PRINCE}

\noindent I consider it a great happiness that \textsc{You} allow me to dedicate this work to \textsc{Your} most exalted name; a sacred duty of gratitude binds me to offer it to \textsc{You}. For if \textsc{Your} favour, Most Serene Prince, had not first opened the way to learning for me, and if \textsc{Your} constant support had not sustained my studies, I could not have devoted myself entirely to mathematics, to which I have always been drawn by a passionate love. Indeed, it was \textsc{Your} kindness alone that enabled me to begin the very investigations of which this volume presents a part, continue them for several years, and put them in writing, by freeing me from other concerns so that I could give them my chief attention. When at last I wished to publish them, \textsc{Your} generosity removed every obstacle delaying their appearance. I can reflect on these great services \textsc{You} have rendered me and my efforts with a grateful heart and silent admiration \SourceLeaf{9}more readily than I can celebrate them with fitting and worthy praise. I not only feel unequal to that task, but also believe that everyone knows how customary it is for \textsc{You} to show such exceptional generosity to all who seem to contribute to the cultivation of the finest studies. Even those sciences commonly thought harder to understand and less useful in everyday life are not excluded from \textsc{Your} protection: \textsc{You Yourself}, with a mind of such wisdom and such knowledge of everything that promotes the prosperity of human society, have fully recognised the close and necessary bond joining all the sciences. If \textsc{You}, Most Serene Prince, judge this book, a witness both to my deep gratitude towards \textsc{You} and to work devoted to a most noble science, not unworthy of the exceptional favour with which You have embraced me for so long, I will congratulate myself on having spent my efforts usefully and attained the honour I desired above all others

\begin{center}
\textsc{MOST SERENE PRINCE}
\end{center}

\noindent\hspace*{1.5em}Brunswick, July 1801.

Your Highness's most devoted servant
