\clearpage\thispagestyle{empty}\noindent{\Large\bfseries Scope of this volume\par}\medskip
\begingroup\raggedright
The historical title matter and the complete 1799 dissertation, Articles 1–24, follow, with Gauss’s ten historical footnotes and all four figures. The complete second proof, Articles 1–20 with both historical footnotes, follows with its title matter. It was presented in 1815 and published in 1816. The complete third proof, presented on 30 January 1816 and published that year, follows with its title matter, introduction, Articles 1–4 and historical footnote. The complete 1828 paper Proof of an Algebraic Theorem follows, on positive and negative roots and changes and continuations of coefficient signs, including missing terms. The complete Contributions to the Theory of Algebraic Equations, read on 16 July 1849 and published in 1850, follow with their title, introduction, Articles 1–20, two historical footnotes and figure. The complete reviews of 23 December 1815, 2 March 1816 and 30 July 1849 follow under Reviews, including the handwritten remarks accompanying the review of 1816. The complete reviews of Coytier of 6 March 1813 and Fourier of 25 February 1833 follow. The complete First Part of the General Investigations of the Infinite Series (1812/1813) follows with its title, introduction, Articles 1–37, both authorial footnotes and the historical numerical table. The complete A New Method for Finding the Values of Integrals by Approximation (presented in 1814, published in 1816) follows with its title, Articles 1–23, both authorial footnotes, coefficient tables and seven numerical examples. The complete reviews of 10 February 1812 and 26 September 1814 follow under Reviews. The complete posthumous Determination of Our Series by a Differential Equation of the Second Order follows with its heading, Articles 38–57 and Schering’s historical editorial remarks. Other Volume-III works are outside this reader. Historical references retain the source numbering.
\par\medskip Historical source: Carl Friedrich Gauss, \emph{Werke}, Volume III, Göttingen, 1866; dissertation first published at Helmstedt by C. G. Fleckeisen, 1799. The original German title matter and the papers of 1828 and 1849 and the reviews of 1815, 1816 and 1849 and the reviews of Coytier and Fourier and the reviews of 10 February 1812 and 26 September 1814 and Schering’s editorial remarks are rendered in modern German in the German reader; their historical originals are retained separately.
