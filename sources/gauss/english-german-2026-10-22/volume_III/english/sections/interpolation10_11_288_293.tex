\InterpolationArticle{10}
Let \(X\) be a function of the indeterminate arc \(x\) of the form
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha+\alpha'\cos x+\alpha''\cos 2x+\alpha'''\cos 3x+\text{etc.}\\
&\quad+\beta'\sin x+\beta''\sin 2x+\beta'''\sin 3x+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
which does not continue to infinity, but breaks off at \(\cos mx\) and \(\sin mx\), so
that the number of coefficients (unknowns) is \(2m+1\). For the same number of distinct values of \(x\), namely \(a,b,c,d\) etc., let the corresponding values of the function \(X\) be given,
namely \(A,B,C,D\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\) (Values of \(x\) whose difference is an entire circumference or a multiple of it clearly cannot here be considered distinct). From
these data a formula is sought for the value \(T\) which the function \(X\) takes for any other
value of \(x\), namely \(t\). Thus there are \(2m+2\) equations
\SourceLeaf{289}
\[
\begin{aligned}
A&=\alpha+\alpha'\cos a+\alpha''\cos 2a+\alpha'''\cos 3a+\text{etc.}\\
 &\quad+\beta'\sin a+\beta''\sin 2a+\beta'''\sin 3a+\text{etc.}\\
B&=\alpha+\alpha'\cos b+\alpha''\cos 2b+\alpha'''\cos 3b+\text{etc.}\\
 &\quad+\beta'\sin b+\beta''\sin 2b+\beta'''\sin 3b+\text{etc.}\\
C&=\alpha+\alpha'\cos c+\alpha''\cos 2c+\alpha'''\cos 3c+\text{etc.}\\
 &\quad+\beta'\sin c+\beta''\sin 2c+\beta'''\sin 3c+\text{etc.}\\
D&=\alpha+\alpha'\cos d+\alpha''\cos 2d+\alpha'''\cos 3d+\text{etc.}\\
 &\quad+\beta'\sin d+\beta''\sin 2d+\beta'''\sin 3d+\text{etc.}\\
 &\text{etc.}\\
T&=\alpha+\alpha'\cos t+\alpha''\cos 2t+\alpha'''\cos 3t+\text{etc.}\\
 &\quad+\beta'\sin t+\beta''\sin 2t+\beta'''\sin 3t+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
Multiplying these equations respectively by
\[
\begin{gathered}
\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(a-b)\sin\tfrac12(a-c)\sin\tfrac12(a-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(a-t)}\\[6pt]
\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(b-a)\sin\tfrac12(b-c)\sin\tfrac12(b-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(b-t)}\\[6pt]
\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(c-a)\sin\tfrac12(c-b)\sin\tfrac12(c-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(c-t)}\\[6pt]
\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(d-a)\sin\tfrac12(d-b)\sin\tfrac12(d-c)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(d-t)}\\
\text{etc.}\\[6pt]
\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.}}
\end{gathered}
\]
and adding the products, let the result be
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{A}{\sin\tfrac12(a-b)\sin\tfrac12(a-c)\sin\tfrac12(a-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(a-t)}\\[6pt]
+{}&\frac{B}{\sin\tfrac12(b-a)\sin\tfrac12(b-c)\sin\tfrac12(b-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(b-t)}\\[6pt]
+{}&\frac{C}{\sin\tfrac12(c-a)\sin\tfrac12(c-b)\sin\tfrac12(c-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(c-t)}\\[6pt]
+{}&\frac{D}{\sin\tfrac12(d-a)\sin\tfrac12(d-b)\sin\tfrac12(d-c)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(d-t)}\\
+{}&\text{etc.}\\[6pt]
+{}&\frac{T}{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}=W
\end{aligned}
\]
\SourceLeaf{290}
Then, from Article 2, where $m$ was what here is $2m+2$, so that the second case applies, $W=0$ holds; hence multiplication by
\[
\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.}
\]
gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
T={}&\frac{\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}
{\sin\tfrac12(a-b)\sin\tfrac12(a-c)\sin\tfrac12(a-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}A\\[5pt]
&+\frac{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}
{\sin\tfrac12(b-a)\sin\tfrac12(b-c)\sin\tfrac12(b-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}B\\[5pt]
&+\frac{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}
{\sin\tfrac12(c-a)\sin\tfrac12(c-b)\sin\tfrac12(c-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}C\\[5pt]
&+\frac{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}
{\sin\tfrac12(d-a)\sin\tfrac12(d-b)\sin\tfrac12(d-c)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}D\\[5pt]
&+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
Since this formula holds without restriction for any value of $t$, it is evident that, on expanding the products of sines in the numerators into cosines and sines of multiple
arcs, the result and
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha+\alpha'\cos t+\alpha''\cos2t+\alpha'''\cos3t+\text{etc.}\\
&\quad+\beta'\sin t+\beta''\sin2t+\beta'''\sin3t+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
must be \emph{identical}; this makes the coefficients $\alpha,\alpha',\beta',\alpha'',\beta''$ etc. known. Moreover, the formula for $T$, as given here, has the property that it is immediately evident, without calculation, that substituting for $t$ respectively $a,b,c,d$ etc. duly satisfies the given values $A,B,C,D$
etc.

\InterpolationArticle{11}

If the number of given values $A,B,C,D$ etc. were one less than
we assumed, namely $=2m$, these would not suffice to determine the $2m+1$ unknown coefficients unless some relation between them were known. In this case, then, the general expression for $T$ can only be given in a form that involves an unknown constant. The reasoning of the preceding article cannot be applied here, since the sum $W=0$, taken from Article 2, rested on the assumption that the number of
quantities $A,B\mathinner{.\,.\,.}T$ is even. The formula found in the preceding article for $T$ does, indeed, generally satisfy the values $A,B$ etc., whether their number is even
or odd; but a little attention shows that, in our case, expanding the products of sines
gives an expression unlike the one proposed, of the following form
\SourceLeaf{291}
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathfrak{A}\cos\tfrac12t+\mathfrak{A}'\cos\tfrac32t+\mathfrak{A}''\cos\tfrac52t+\text{etc.}\\
&\quad+\mathfrak{B}\sin\tfrac12t+\mathfrak{B}'\sin\tfrac32t+\mathfrak{B}''\sin\tfrac52t+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
For this case, therefore, another path must be taken.

Let $X$ be multiplied by $\cos(\tfrac12x+k)$. Taking the last terms in $X$ to be $\alpha^m\cos mx+\beta^m\sin mx$ gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha\cos(\tfrac12x+k)+\tfrac12\alpha'\cos(\tfrac32x+k)\\&\qquad+\tfrac12\alpha''\cos(\tfrac52x+k)+\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\\
&\hspace{2.01em}+\tfrac12\alpha^m\cos((m+\tfrac12)x+k)\\
&+\tfrac12\alpha'\cos(\tfrac12x-k)+\tfrac12\alpha''\cos(\tfrac32x-k)\\&\qquad+\tfrac12\alpha'''\cos(\tfrac52x-k)+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\\
&\hspace{2.01em}+\tfrac12\alpha^m\cos((m-\tfrac12)x-k)\\
&\hspace{2em}+\tfrac12\beta'\sin(\tfrac32x+k)+\tfrac12\beta''\sin(\tfrac52x+k)+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\\
&\hspace{2.01em}+\tfrac12\beta^m\sin((m+\tfrac12)x+k)\\
&+\tfrac12\beta'\sin(\tfrac12x-k)+\tfrac12\beta''\sin(\tfrac32x-k)\\&\qquad+\tfrac12\beta'''\sin(\tfrac52x-k)+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\\
&\hspace{2.01em}+\tfrac12\beta^m\sin((m-\tfrac12)x-k)
\end{aligned}
\]
Now, if in this expression $x$ is replaced successively by $a,b,c,d\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}t$, the results
are multiplied respectively by
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(a-b)\sin\tfrac12(a-c)\sin\tfrac12(a-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(a-t)}\\[1ex]
&\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(b-a)\sin\tfrac12(b-c)\sin\tfrac12(b-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(b-t)}\\[1ex]
&\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(c-a)\sin\tfrac12(c-b)\sin\tfrac12(c-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(c-t)}\\[1ex]
&\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(d-a)\sin\tfrac12(d-b)\sin\tfrac12(d-c)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(d-t)}\\
&\text{etc.}\\[1ex]
&\frac{1}{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}
\end{aligned}
\]
and the sum of the products is set $=W$: from what we gave in Article 2
(in the first case, where $m$ was what here is $2m+1$), it follows easily that
\[
\begin{aligned}
W={}&\tfrac12(2i)^{2m}\alpha^m\cos(\tfrac12(a+b+c+d+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}+t)+k)\\
&+\tfrac12(2i)^{2m}\beta^m\sin(\tfrac12(a+b+c+d+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}+t)+k)
\end{aligned}
\]
On the other hand,

\SourceLeaf{292}
\[
\begin{aligned}
W={}&\frac{A\cos(\tfrac12a+k)}{\sin\tfrac12(a-b)\sin\tfrac12(a-c)\sin\tfrac12(a-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(a-t)}\\[5pt]
&+\frac{B\cos(\tfrac12b+k)}{\sin\tfrac12(b-a)\sin\tfrac12(b-c)\sin\tfrac12(b-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(b-t)}\\[5pt]
&+\frac{C\cos(\tfrac12c+k)}{\sin\tfrac12(c-a)\sin\tfrac12(c-b)\sin\tfrac12(c-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(c-t)}\\[5pt]
&+\frac{D\cos(\tfrac12d+k)}{\sin\tfrac12(d-a)\sin\tfrac12(d-b)\sin\tfrac12(d-c)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}\sin\tfrac12(d-t)}\\[5pt]
&+\text{etc.}\\[5pt]
&+\frac{T\cos(\tfrac12t+k)}{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}
\end{aligned}
\]
If we now set the quantity $k$, which is entirely arbitrary, $=-\tfrac12t$,
and the sum $a+b+c+d+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}=s$ (excluding $t$), then multiplication by
$\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}$
gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
T={}&\frac{\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}{\sin\tfrac12(a-b)\sin\tfrac12(a-c)\sin\tfrac12(a-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}A\cos\tfrac12(t-a)\\[5pt]
&+\frac{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}{\sin\tfrac12(b-a)\sin\tfrac12(b-c)\sin\tfrac12(b-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}B\cos\tfrac12(t-b)\\[5pt]
&+\frac{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}{\sin\tfrac12(c-a)\sin\tfrac12(c-b)\sin\tfrac12(c-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}C\cos\tfrac12(t-c)\\[5pt]
&+\frac{\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}{\sin\tfrac12(d-a)\sin\tfrac12(d-b)\sin\tfrac12(d-c)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}}D\cos\tfrac12(t-d)\\[5pt]
&+\text{etc.}\\[5pt]
&+2^{2m-1}i^{2m}\sin\tfrac12(t-a)\sin\tfrac12(t-b)\sin\tfrac12(t-c)\sin\tfrac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\\[5pt]
&\qquad\times\{\alpha^m\cos\tfrac12s+\beta^m\sin\tfrac12s\}
\end{aligned}
\]
We add a few more remarks on this formula

I. $i^{2m}=+1$, or $-1$, according as $m$ is even or odd.

II. Since the formula holds without restriction for any value of $t$, expanding the products of sines into sines and cosines of multiple arcs must make it
identical with the formula
for $X$ when $x$ is replaced by $t$, that is, with
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha+\alpha'\cos t+\alpha''\cos2t+\text{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\quad+\beta'\sin t+\beta''\sin2t+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
III. Thus all the coefficients $\alpha,\alpha',\beta',\alpha'',\beta''$ etc. are determined by the known quantities and the single unknown
$\alpha^m\cos\tfrac12s+\beta^m\sin\tfrac12s$; hence, if
some relation between them is given in addition, they can all readily be specified completely.
\par\SourceLeaf{293}
IV. If, however, only an expression of the same form as
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha+\alpha'\cos t+\alpha''\cos2t+\text{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\quad+\beta'\sin t+\beta''\sin2t+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
is sought (whether identical with this or not), which represents the given values
$A,B,C,D$ etc. on substituting for $t$ respectively $a,b,c,d$ etc.: the quantity
$\alpha^m\cos\tfrac12s+\beta^m\sin\tfrac12s$ changes from an unknown into an \emph{arbitrary} quantity, which may, if desired, even be set $=0$;
indeed, our formula for $T$ is arranged so that this property is immediately evident without
calculation.
