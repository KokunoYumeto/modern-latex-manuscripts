\SixthArticle{17}
The investigation of the nature of the sum of the series $F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,1)$ is thus naturally restricted to the case where $\gamma-\alpha-\beta$ is a positive quantity; the sum then always represents a finite quantity. We begin with the following observation. If the coefficients of the series $1+ax+bxx+cx^3+$ etc. $=S$ diminish towards zero from a certain term onwards, the product
\[
(1-x)S=1+(a-1)x+(b-a)xx+(c-b)x^3+\quad\text{etc.}
\]
for $x=1$ must be set $=0$, even if the sum of the series $S$ itself becomes infinitely large. For the sum of two terms is $=a$, of three $=b$, of four $=c$ etc., and so the limit of the sum continued to infinity is $=0$. Thus, whenever $\gamma-\alpha-\beta$ is a positive quantity, $(1-x)F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma-1,x)=0$ must be set for $x=1$. Hence, by equation 15 of Article 7, we have
\[
0=\gamma(\alpha+\beta-\gamma)F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,1)+(\gamma-\alpha)(\gamma-\beta)F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma+1,1),\quad\text{or}
\]
\[
[37]\qquad F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,1)=\frac{(\gamma-\alpha)(\gamma-\beta)}{\gamma(\gamma-\alpha-\beta)}F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma+1,1)
\]
Since, in the same way,
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma+1,1)&=\frac{(\gamma+1-\alpha)(\gamma+1-\beta)}{(\gamma+1)(\gamma+1-\alpha-\beta)}F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma+2,1)\\[6pt]
F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma+2,1)&=\frac{(\gamma+2-\alpha)(\gamma+2-\beta)}{(\gamma+2)(\gamma+2-\alpha-\beta)}F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma+3,1)
\end{aligned}
\]
and so on, we have in general, with $k$ denoting any positive integer,
\SourceLeaf{153}
\[
\begin{array}{@{}ll@{}}
&F(\alpha.\beta,\gamma,1)\quad\text{is equal to the product of}\quad F(\alpha,\beta,\gamma+k,1)\\[6pt]
\text{and}&(\gamma-\alpha)(\gamma+1-\alpha)(\gamma+2-\alpha)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}(\gamma+k-1-\alpha)\\[5pt]
\text{and}&(\gamma-\beta)(\gamma+1-\beta)(\gamma+2-\beta)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}(\gamma+k-1-\beta)\\[8pt]
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l@{}}{\text{divided by the product}}\\[6pt]
\text{of}&\gamma(\gamma+1)(\gamma+2)\mathinner{.\,.\,.\,.}(\gamma+k-1)\\[5pt]
\text{and}&(\gamma-\alpha-\beta)(\gamma+1-\alpha-\beta)(\gamma+2-\alpha-\beta)\mathinner{.\,.\,.}(\gamma+k-1-\alpha-\beta)
\end{array}
\]
