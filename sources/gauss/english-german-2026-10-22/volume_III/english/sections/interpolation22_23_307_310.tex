\InterpolationArticle{22}
We pass to the other case of Article 11, where the series \(X\) remains the same as in Article 20, but the number of given values is one less than
the number of unknown coefficients, namely \(=2m=\mu\). Here the following holds
\SourceLeaf{308}
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\cos\frac12(t-a)\sin\frac12(t-b)\sin\frac12(t-c)\sin\frac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\\[5pt]
&=-\frac{\sin\frac12\mu(t-a)\cos\frac12(t-a)}{2^{\mu-1}\sin\frac12(t-a)}\\[5pt]
&=-\frac{\cos\frac12(t-a)}{2^{\mu-2}}\bigl(\cos\frac12(t-a)+\cos\frac32(t-a)\\[5pt]&\qquad{}+\cos\frac52(t-a)+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}+\cos\frac{\mu-1}{2}(t-a)\bigr)\\[5pt]
&=-\frac{1}{2^{\mu-1}}\bigl(1+2\cos(t-a)+2\cos2(t-a)\\[5pt]&\qquad{}+2\cos3(t-a)+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}+2\cos(m-1)(t-a)\\[5pt]
&\qquad +\cos m(t-a)\bigr)
\end{aligned}
\]
Hence, in the formula of Article 11 for $T$, the coefficient of $A$ becomes
\[
\begin{aligned}
&=\frac1\mu\bigl(1+2\cos(t-a)+2\cos2(t-a)\\[5pt]&\qquad{}+2\cos3(t-a)+\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\\[5pt]
&\qquad +2\cos(m-1)(t-a)+\cos m(t-a)\bigr)
\end{aligned}
\]
Entirely similar expressions (with only $a$ changed to $b,c,d$ etc.) are found for the coefficients of $B,C,D$ etc. Finally, the last part of $T$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
&2^{2m-1}i^{2m}\sin\frac12(t-a)\sin\frac12(t-b)\sin\frac12(t-c)\sin\frac12(t-d)\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\\[5pt]&\qquad\times\{\alpha^m\cos\frac12s+\beta^m\sin\frac12s\}
\end{aligned}
\]
becomes
\[
\begin{aligned}
&=-i^{2m}\sin m(t-a)\{\alpha^m\cos(ma+(2m-1)90^{0})\\[5pt]&\qquad{}+\beta^m\sin(ma+(2m-1)90^{0})\}\\[5pt]
&=-\sin m(t-a)(\alpha^m\sin ma-\beta^m\cos ma)
\end{aligned}
\]
From this it is finally concluded that
\[
\begin{aligned}
&T=\frac1\mu(A+B+C+D+\mathrm{etc.})\\[5pt]
&\quad+\frac2\mu(A\cos a+B\cos b+C\cos c+D\cos d+\mathrm{etc.})\cos t\\[5pt]
&\quad+\frac2\mu(A\sin a+B\sin b+C\sin c+D\sin d+\mathrm{etc.})\sin t\\[5pt]
&\quad+\frac2\mu(A\cos2a+B\cos2b+C\cos2c+D\cos2d+\mathrm{etc.})\cos2t\\[5pt]
&\quad+\frac2\mu(A\sin2a+B\sin2b+C\sin2c+D\sin2d+\mathrm{etc.})\sin2t\\[5pt]
&\quad+\mathrm{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\quad+\frac1\mu(A\cos ma+B\cos mb+C\cos mc+D\cos md+\mathrm{etc.})\cos mt\\[5pt]
&\quad+\frac1\mu(A\sin ma+B\sin mb+C\sin mc+D\sin md+\mathrm{etc.})\sin mt\\[5pt]
&\quad+(\alpha^m\sin ma-\beta^m\cos ma)\\[5pt]&\qquad(\sin ma\cos mt-\cos ma\sin mt)
\end{aligned}
\]
If this expression, with its last part removed, is represented by
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\gamma+\gamma'\cos t+\gamma''\cos2t+\mathrm{etc.}+\gamma^m\cos mt\\[5pt]
&\quad+\delta'\sin t+\delta''\sin2t+\mathrm{etc.}+\delta^m\sin mt
\end{aligned}
\]
\SourceLeaf{309}
it is clear that all the coefficients $\gamma,\gamma',\delta',\gamma'',\delta''$ etc. have precisely the same values as in the preceding article, where $\mu=2m+1$, except for the last two $\gamma^m,\delta^m$, which were twice as large there. Furthermore, it is clear that all the coefficients
$\alpha,\alpha',\alpha''\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\alpha^{m-1},\beta',\beta''\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\beta^{m-1}$ become respectively equal to $\gamma,\gamma',\gamma''\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\gamma^{m-1},\delta',\delta''\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\delta^{m-1}$ and are therefore known, while for the last two $\alpha^m,\beta^m$ the following equations hold
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha^m=\gamma^m+(\alpha^m\sin ma-\beta^m\cos ma)\sin ma\\[5pt]
&\beta^m=\delta^m-(\alpha^m\sin ma-\beta^m\cos ma)\cos ma
\end{aligned}
\]
which, however (as could be foreseen from the nature of the problem), do not suffice to determine the coefficients $\alpha^m,\beta^m$ completely. For, since
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\cos ma=-\cos mb=\cos mc=-\cos md\ \mathrm{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sin ma=-\sin mb=\sin mc=-\sin md\ \mathrm{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
and therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\gamma^m=\frac{\cos ma}{\mu}(A-B+C-D+\mathrm{etc.})\\[5pt]
&\delta^m=\frac{\sin ma}{\mu}(A-B+C-D+\mathrm{etc.})
\end{aligned}
\]
it is readily seen that both equations are contained in the single equation
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha^m\cos ma+\beta^m\sin ma=\frac1\mu(A-B+C-D+\mathrm{etc.})
\end{aligned}
\]
Another relation between $\alpha^m$ and $\beta^m$ must therefore also be given, so that both coefficients can be fully determined.

\InterpolationArticle{23}
If the series $X$ does not end with $\cos mx$ and $\sin mx$, the value of $T$
found in the preceding article will be incomplete, and the coefficients $\gamma,\gamma',\delta'$ etc. will differ from the true values
$\alpha,\alpha',\beta'$ etc. It is readily seen that, if the coefficients of the following terms in $X$ are denoted by the same symbols that we used in Article 21,
precisely the same values that we derived there result for $\gamma,\gamma',\delta'$ etc. up to $\gamma^{m-1},\delta^{m-1}$;
the last two, however, become
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\gamma^m=\frac12\alpha^m+\frac12(\alpha^m+\alpha^{3m})\cos\mu a\\[5pt]&\qquad{}+\frac12(\alpha^{3m}+\alpha^{5m})\cos2\mu a+\mathrm{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\qquad +\frac12(\beta^m+\beta^{3m})\sin\mu a+\frac12(\beta^{3m}+\beta^{5m})\sin2\mu a+\mathrm{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\delta^m=\frac12\beta^m-\frac12(\beta^m-\beta^{3m})\cos\mu a\\[5pt]&\qquad{}-\frac12(\beta^{3m}-\beta^{5m})\cos2\mu a-\mathrm{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\qquad -\frac12(\alpha^m-\alpha^{3m})\sin\mu a-\frac12(\alpha^{3m}-\alpha^{5m})\sin2\mu a-\mathrm{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
\SourceLeaf{310}
In this case, therefore, the coefficients $\gamma,\gamma',\delta',\gamma'',\delta''\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\gamma^{m-1},\delta^{m-1}$ may be taken for the true values
$\alpha,\alpha',\beta',\alpha'',\beta''\mathinner{.\,.\,.}\alpha^{m-1},\beta^{m-1}$ insofar as the following coefficients $\alpha^{m+1},\beta^{m+1}$ etc.
can be neglected; this conclusion must not, however, be extended to the last two $\gamma^m,\delta^m$ unless they themselves can be regarded as negligible.
