Setting $\alpha=3$, $\gamma=\frac52$ in formula 30 immediately gives the continued fraction proposed in Article 90 of the \textit{Theoria motus corporum coelestium}. Two other continued fractions were given there, and it seemed appropriate to supply their derivation on this occasion. With
\[
Q=1-\cfrac{\frac{5\cdot8}{7\cdot9}x}{1-\cfrac{\frac{1\cdot4}{9\cdot11}x}{1-\frac{7\cdot10}{11\cdot13}x\ \mathrm{etc.}}}
\]
the cited passage gives $\displaystyle x-\xi=\cfrac{x}{1+\frac{2x}{35Q}}=\frac{xQ}{Q+\frac2{35}x}$, and hence
\[
\xi=\frac{\frac2{35}xx}{Q+\frac2{35}x}
\]
\Needspace{17\baselineskip}
which is the first formula. The second is obtained as follows. With
\[
R=1-\cfrac{\frac{1\cdot4}{7\cdot9}x}{1-\cfrac{\frac{5\cdot8}{9\cdot11}x}{1-\cfrac{\frac{3\cdot6}{11\cdot13}x}{1-\frac{7\cdot10}{13\cdot15}x\ \mathrm{etc.}}}}
\]
formula 25 gives
\[
\frac1R=G(\frac12,\frac32,\frac72,x),
\qquad\mbox{and}\qquad
\frac1Q=G(\frac52,-\frac12,\frac72,x)
\]
Hence
\SourceLeaf{147}
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
RF(\frac12,\frac52,\frac92,x)&=&F(\frac12,\frac32,\frac72,x)\\[4pt]
QF(\frac52,\frac12,\frac92,x)&=&F(\frac52,-\frac12,\frac72,x)
\end{array}
\]
or, interchanging the first element with the second,
\[
QF(\frac12,\frac52,\frac92,x)=F(-\frac12,\frac52,\frac72,x)
\]
\par
But by equation 21 we have
\[
F(-\frac12,\frac52,\frac72,x)-F(\frac12,\frac32,\frac72,x)
=-\frac47xF(\frac12,\frac52,\frac92,x)
\]
which gives $Q=R-\frac47x$. Substituting this value into the formula given above yields
\[
\xi=\frac{\frac2{35}xx}{R-\frac{18}{35}x}
\]
which is the second formula.
