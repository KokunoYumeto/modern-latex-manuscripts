\emph{Tables logarithmiques pour les nombres, les sinus et les tangentes, disposées
dans un nouvel ordre par M. \textup{\textsc{de Prasse}}, professeur des mathématiques à Berlin} (at
Leipzig) \emph{corrigées et précédées d’une introduction traduite de l’allemand et accompagnée de notes et d’un avertissement par} M. \textsc{Halma} 1814. Paris. 80 pages. Price
One franc.

A new printing of von \textsc{Prasse}'s handy tables, which we reviewed in these pages [1811 May 25]. Three further years of use have confirmed in every respect our earlier judgment of the arrangement of the tables, which the French editor has retained unchanged. In the beauty of its printing and paper, the French edition seems to us rather inferior to the German one; several misprints in the latter have, however, been corrected here. Even if, for a work of this kind, which the late von \textsc{Prasse} certainly undertook at his own expense to serve science rather than for profit, one chooses not to inquire further into how far Mr. \textsc{Halma} was entitled to issue a new printing, one still cannot help being surprised that, for fear of being reprinted in turn, he has signed each individual copy and threatens to take legal action against anyone who reprints it.
