\InterpolationArticle{33}
Secondly, let $X$ be a function of the form
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\beta'\sin x+\beta''\sin2x+\beta'''\sin3x+\text{etc.}+\beta^n\sin nx
\end{aligned}
\]
so that $\mu=n$ must hold. Here, by Article 30, the coefficient of $A$ in the second formula of Article 16 for $T$ becomes
\[
\begin{aligned}
&=\frac{1}{\mu\sin\mu a}\times\frac{\sin t}{\sin a}(\sin\mu a+2\sin(\mu-1)a\cos t\\[5pt]
&\qquad{}+2\sin(\mu-2)a\cos2t+\text{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\qquad+2\sin a\cos(\mu-1)t)\\[5pt]
&=\frac{1}{\mu\sin\mu a}(2\cos(\mu-1)a\sin t+2\cos(\mu-2)a\sin2t\\[5pt]
&\qquad{}+2\cos(\mu-3)a\sin3t+\text{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\qquad+2\cos a\sin(\mu-1)t+\sin\mu t)
\end{aligned}
\]
\Needspace{37\baselineskip}
Exactly similar expressions result for the coefficients of $B,C,D$ etc., merely changing the arc $a$ into $b,c,d$ etc. Thus, on setting
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\zeta'=\frac{2}{\mu\sin\mu a}\{A\cos(\mu-1)a+B\cos(\mu-1)b\\[5pt]
&\qquad{}+C\cos(\mu-1)c+D\cos(\mu-1)d+\text{etc.}\}\\[5pt]
&\zeta''=\frac{2}{\mu\sin\mu a}\{A\cos(\mu-2)a+B\cos(\mu-2)b\\[5pt]
&\qquad{}+C\cos(\mu-2)c+D\cos(\mu-2)d+\text{etc.}\}\\[5pt]
&\zeta'''=\frac{2}{\mu\sin\mu a}\{A\cos(\mu-3)a+B\cos(\mu-3)b\\[5pt]
&\qquad{}+C\cos(\mu-3)c+D\cos(\mu-3)d+\text{etc.}\}\\[5pt]
&\text{etc.}\\[5pt]
&\zeta^{\mu-1}=\frac{2}{\mu\sin\mu a}\{A\cos a+B\cos b+C\cos c+D\cos d+\text{etc.}\}\\[5pt]
&\zeta^\mu=\frac{1}{\mu\sin\mu a}\{A+B+C+D+\text{etc.}\}
\end{aligned}
\]
there will be
\[
\begin{aligned}
&T=\zeta'\sin t+\zeta''\sin2t+\zeta'''\sin3t+\text{etc.}+\zeta^\mu\sin\mu t
\end{aligned}
\]
Since this formula holds generally for any value of $t$, it will necessarily
\SourceLeaf{325}
be identical with $X$. On changing $t$ into $x$, the coefficients $\zeta',\zeta'',\zeta'''$ etc. will therefore be equal respectively to $\beta',\beta'',\beta'''$ etc.

\InterpolationArticle{34}
If, as in Article 31, all the given values have been satisfied by a function of this kind
\[
\begin{aligned}
&X'={}\gamma+\gamma'\cos x+\gamma''\cos 2x+\text{etc.}+\gamma^{m}\cos mx\\[5pt]
&+\delta'\sin x+\delta''\sin 2x+\text{etc.}+\delta^{m}\sin mx
\end{aligned}
\]
with $m=\tfrac{1}{2}\mu-\tfrac{1}{2}$ or $=\tfrac{1}{2}\mu$ according as $\mu$ is odd or even, comparing this function with the following one
\[
\begin{aligned}
&X''=\zeta'\sin x+\zeta''\sin 2x+\zeta'''\sin 3x+\text{etc.}+\zeta^{\mu}\sin\mu x
\end{aligned}
\]
which we obtained in the preceding article gives these equations:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\zeta^{\mu}=\gamma\operatorname{cosec}\mu a\\[5pt]
&\zeta'=\delta'+\gamma'\operatorname{cotg}\mu a,\\[5pt]
&\zeta^{\mu-1}=\gamma'\operatorname{cosec}\mu a\\[5pt]
&\zeta''=\delta''+\gamma''\operatorname{cotg}\mu a,\\[5pt]
&\zeta^{\mu-2}=\gamma''\operatorname{cosec}\mu a\\[5pt]
&\zeta'''=\delta'''+\gamma'''\operatorname{cotg}\mu a,\\[5pt]
&\zeta^{\mu-3}=\gamma'''\operatorname{cosec}\mu a\\[5pt]
&\text{etc., as far as}\\[5pt]
&(\tfrac{1}{2})\zeta^{m}=\delta^{m}+\gamma^{m}\operatorname{cotg}\mu a,\\[5pt]
&(\tfrac{1}{2})\zeta^{\mu-m}=\gamma^{m}\operatorname{cosec}\mu a
\end{aligned}
\]
\Needspace{40\baselineskip}
Here the factor $\tfrac{1}{2}$ placed before the coefficients $\zeta^{m},\zeta^{\mu-m}$ applies only to the case where $\mu$ is even; in the other case, where $\mu$ is odd, it must be omitted. Thus, in the latter case, where $\mu-m=m+1$, for each coefficient $\zeta',\zeta''$ etc. we have one value in terms of $\gamma,\gamma',\gamma''$ etc. and $\delta',\delta''$ etc.; in the former, however, where $\mu-m=m$, for the coefficient $\zeta^{\frac{1}{2}\mu}$ there are two equal values, in place of which the value $\delta^{\frac{1}{2}\mu}+\gamma^{\frac{1}{2}\mu}\operatorname{cotg}\mu a+\gamma^{\frac{1}{2}\mu}\operatorname{cosec}\mu a$ can also be adopted. It follows that
\[
\begin{aligned}
&X''={}X'-\gamma-\gamma'\cos x-\gamma''\cos 2x-\text{etc.}-\gamma^{m}\cos mx\\[5pt]
&+\operatorname{cotg}\mu a(\gamma'\sin x+\gamma''\sin 2x+\gamma'''\sin 3x+\text{etc.}+\gamma^{m}\sin mx)\\[5pt]
&+\operatorname{cosec}\mu a(\gamma\sin\mu x+\gamma'\sin(\mu-1)x\\[5pt]
&\qquad{}+\gamma''\sin(\mu-2)x+\text{etc.}+\gamma^{\mu-m}\sin(\mu-m)x).
\end{aligned}
\]
Hence, from $X'$ there is produced $X''$ by writing, for $\gamma$, $\gamma\operatorname{cosec}\mu a\sin\mu x=\frac{\gamma\sin\mu x}{\sin\mu a}$ and, for each term $\gamma^{\lambda}\cos\lambda x$,
\SourceLeaf{326}
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\gamma^{\lambda}\operatorname{cotg}\mu a\sin\lambda x\\[5pt]
&\qquad{}+\gamma^{\lambda}\operatorname{cosec}\mu a\sin(\mu-\lambda)x
\\[5pt]
&\text{or}\quad
\frac{\gamma^{\lambda}\cos\mu a\sin\lambda x+\gamma^{\lambda}\sin(\mu-\lambda)x}{\sin\mu a}
\end{aligned}
\]
Let the readers themselves compare this rule with what we presented in Article 24.
