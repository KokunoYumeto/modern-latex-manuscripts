\begin{center}2.\end{center}

To the list of all misprints in the stereotype edition of \textsc{Callet}'s logarithm tables given on p. 497 of the preceding issue, Dr. \textsc{Gauss} has kindly supplied the following additional errata:

\begin{center}
\footnotesize\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.30\linewidth}lll@{}}
\textit{Log. Sin. de Seconde en Seconde}
& \(4^{0}\;15'\;5''\quad\sin 8.8690096\) & read & \(8.8700096\) \\
& \(4\quad15\quad6\quad\sin 8.8690379\) & --- & \(8.8700379\) \\
\textit{Log. Sin. de} \(10\) \textit{en} \(10\) \textit{Secondes}
& \(\mathrm{Arc}\,21^{0}\;27'\;20''\) & read & \(21^{0}\;27'\;30''\).
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

For \(33^{0}\), at the bottom, instead of 59 Deg. read 56 Deg. (only in some impressions).
