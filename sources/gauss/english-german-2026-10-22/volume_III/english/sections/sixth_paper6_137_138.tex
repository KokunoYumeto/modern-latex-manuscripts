\SixthArticle{6}
The preceding functions are algebraic functions and transcendental functions depending on logarithms and the circle. We do not undertake our \textit{general} investigation for the sake of \textit{these}, however, but rather to advance the theory of higher transcendental functions, of which our series embraces a very extensive class. Among infinitely many others, this class includes the coefficients obtained by expanding the function $(aa+bb-2ab\cos\varphi)^{-n}$ into a series proceeding according to the cosines of the angles $\varphi$, $2\varphi$, $3\varphi$ etc. We shall discuss these \textit{in particular} more fully on another occasion. These coefficients can be reduced to the form of our series in several ways. Namely, setting
\[
(aa+bb-2ab\cos\varphi)^{-n}=\Omega=A+2A'\cos\varphi+2A''\cos2\varphi+2A'''\cos3\varphi+\mbox{etc.}
\]
\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
we have, \textit{first},
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A&=a^{-2n}F(n,n,1,\frac{bb}{aa})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A'&=na^{-2n-1}bF(n,n+1,2,\frac{bb}{aa})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A''&=\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}a^{-2n-2}bbF(n,n+2,3,\frac{bb}{aa})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A'''&=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{1\mathbin{.}2\mathbin{.}3}a^{-2n-3}b^3F(n,n+3,4.\ \frac{bb}{aa})
\end{array}
\]
\begin{center}etc.\end{center}
For if $aa+bb-2ab\cos\varphi$ is regarded as the product of $a-br$ and $a-br^{-1}$ (where $r$ denotes the quantity $\cos\varphi+\sin\varphi.\BareRoot{-1}$), then $\Omega$ becomes equal to the product
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
\mbox{of}&a^{-2n}
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
\mbox{and}&1+n\frac{br}{a}+\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}.\ \frac{bbrr}{aa}+\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{1\mathbin{.}2\mathbin{.}3}.\ \frac{b^3r^3}{a^3}+\mbox{etc.}
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
\mbox{and}&1+n\frac{br^{-1}}{a}+\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}.\ \frac{bbr^{-2}}{aa}+\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{1\mathbin{.}2\mathbin{.}3}.\ \frac{b^3r^{-3}}{a^3}+\mbox{etc.}
\end{array}
\]
Since this product must be identical with
\[
A+A'(r+r^{-1})+A''(rr+r^{-2})+A'''(r^3+r^{-3})
\]
the values given above follow at once.

Further, we have, \textit{second},
\SourceLeaf{138}
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A&=(aa+bb)^{-n}F(\frac12 n,\frac12 n+\frac12 .\,1,\frac{4aabb}{(aa+bb)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A'&=n(aa+bb)^{-n-1}abF(\frac12 n+\frac12,\frac12 n+1,2,\frac{4aabb}{(aa+bb)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A''&=\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}(aa+bb)^{-n-2}aabbF(\frac12 n+1,\frac12 n+\frac32,3,\frac{4aabb}{(aa+bb)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A'''&=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{1\mathbin{.}2\mathbin{.}3}(aa+bb)^{-n-3}a^3b^3F(\frac12 n+\frac32,\frac12 n+2,4,\frac{4aabb}{(aa+bb)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\begin{center}etc.\end{center}
These values are readily deduced from
\[
\Omega(aa+bb)^n=1+n(r+r^{-1})\frac{ab}{aa+bb}+\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}(r+r^{-1})^2\frac{aabb}{(aa+bb)^2}+\mathrm{etc.}
\]
\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\textit{Third},
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A&=(a+b)^{-2n}F(n,\frac12,1,\frac{4ab}{(a+b)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A'&=n(a+b)^{-2n-2}abF(n+1,\frac32,3,\frac{4ab}{(a+b)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A''&=\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}(a+b)^{-2n-4}aabbF(n+2,\frac52,5,\frac{4ab}{(a+b)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A'''&=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{1\mathbin{.}2\mathbin{.}3}(a+b)^{-2n-6}a^3b^3F(n+3,\frac72,7,\frac{4ab}{(a+b)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\begin{center}etc.\end{center}
Finally, \textit{fourth},
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A&=(a-b)^{-2n}F(n,\frac12,1,-\frac{4ab}{(a-b)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A'&=n(a-b)^{-2n-2}abF(n+1,\frac32,3,-\frac{4ab}{(a-b)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A''&=\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}(a-b)^{-2n-4}aabbF(n+2,\frac52,5,-\frac{4ab}{(a-b)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
A'''&=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{1\mathbin{.}2\mathbin{.}3}(a-b)^{2n-6}a^3b^3F(n+3,\frac72,7,-\frac{4ab}{(a-b)^2})
\end{array}
\]
\begin{center}etc.\end{center}
Both those values and these are readily obtained from
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
\Omega(a+b)^{2n}&=(1-\frac{4ab\cos\frac12\varphi^2}{(a+b)^2})^{-n}
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
&=1+n\frac{ab}{(a+b)^2}(r^{\frac12}+r^{-\frac12})^2+\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}\mathbin{.}\frac{aabb}{(a+b)^4}(r^{\frac12}+r^{-\frac12})^4+\mathrm{etc.}
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
\Omega(a-b)^{2n}&=(1+\frac{4ab\sin\frac12\varphi^2}{(a-b)^2})^{-n}
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
&=1+n\frac{ab}{(a-b)^2}(r^{\frac12}-r^{-\frac12})^2+\frac{n(n+1)}{1\mathbin{.}2}\mathbin{.}\frac{aabb}{(a-b)^4}(r^{\frac12}-r^{-\frac12})^4+\mathrm{etc.}
\end{array}
\]
