\pdfbookmark[1]{Art. 1}{transscendentes-v-art-1}\begin{center}[1.]\par\emph{General Theorem} (1827 Aug. 6)\end{center}
\[
\begin{gathered}P(x,ty).P\Bigl(x,\frac yt\Bigr)=P(xx,tt).P(xx,yy)\\[8pt]\quad{}+R(xx,tt).R(xx,yy)\end{gathered}
\]

\par\medskip\Needspace{5\baselineskip}\pdfbookmark[1]{Art. 2}{transscendentes-v-art-2}\centerline{[2.]}\nobreak\smallskip
The following is easily confirmed by factorization, if
\[
(1+x)(1+x^3)(1+x^5)(1+x^7)\ .\ .\ .\ .=M
\]
is set:
\[
\begin{gathered}
P(ix^{\frac32},\quad ix^{\frac12})=\frac{P(x,1)}{M}\\[8pt]
P(ix^{\frac32},-ix^{\frac12})=\frac{P(x^3,1)}{M}
\end{gathered}
\]

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
thus, by addition
\[
\begin{gathered}
P(x,1)+P(x^3,1)=2M.P(x^6,-x)\\[8pt]
P(x,1)-P(x^3,1)=2Mx.P(x^6,-x^5)
\end{gathered}
\]

\Needspace{24\baselineskip}
thus
\[
\begin{gathered}
P(x,1)^2-P(x^3,1)^2
\\[8pt]\quad{}=4MMx.F(x^{12}).(1-x)(1-x^5)\\[8pt]\quad{}(1-x^7)(1-x^{11})(1-x^{13})\ .\ .\ .\\[8pt]
\\[8pt]\quad{}=4MMx.\frac{Fx.F(x^6).F(x^{12})}{Fxx.F(x^3)}
\\[8pt]\quad{}=2\surd\frac{pP}{r}\ R^{\frac32}
\end{gathered}
\]
