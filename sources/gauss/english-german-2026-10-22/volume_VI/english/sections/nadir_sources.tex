\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Schumacher on the Nadir Point (31 October 1846)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the letter extract on p.~472 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher from Göttingen on 31 October 1846. The extract first appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXV, no.~579, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049450_00030/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~43--44}. The issue is dated 10 December 1846 in the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049450_00032/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{colophon on col.~48}. The Bavarian State Library catalogue dates the bound volume to 1847.
