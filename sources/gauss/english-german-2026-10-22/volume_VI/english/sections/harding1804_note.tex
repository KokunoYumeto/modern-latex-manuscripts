\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the October 1804 Report on Harding’s Discovery}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the complete excerpt of Franz Xaver von Zach’s report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~250--253. Von Zach describes Karl Ludwig Harding’s discovery and quotes Carl Friedrich Gauss and Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers. The original report appeared as article XXXI in the October 1804 issue of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~X, pp.~371--385. The collected excerpt takes its title from \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538602?page=385}{p.~371}, with the corresponding text running from \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538602?page=393}{p.~379} to \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538602?page=399}{p.~385}.
\par\smallskip\noindent Olbers refers to \href{https://archive.org/download/histoireclestef00lalagoog/page/n146.jpg}{p.~119} and \href{https://archive.org/download/histoireclestef00lalagoog/page/n158.jpg}{p.~131} of the \href{https://archive.org/download/histoireclestef00lalagoog/page/n12.jpg}{\textit{Histoire céleste française}}, vol.~I, published by Jérôme de Lalande (Paris, Imprimerie de la République, an IX [1801]). \href{https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb30722242z}{Bibliothèque nationale de France catalogue}.
