\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Letter on the Second Comet of 1805}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter of 8 July 1806 to Franz Xaver von Zach as printed in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~275--277. The letter appeared in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XIV, August 1806, article~XV, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=195}{pp.~181}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=200}{186}. The journal scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
\par\medskip
The collected edition gives the time fractions in the table and reads \textit{Aberration} after the distance comparison, where the \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=197}{journal, p.~183}, reads \textit{Nutation}. The collected edition gives 71 days between the outermost Pallas observations. The \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=200}{journal, p.~186}, gives 73 days.
\par\medskip
Adrien-Marie Legendre’s \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/10838079}{\textit{Nouvelles méthodes pour la détermination des orbites des comètes}} (Paris, Firmin Didot, an XIII--1805) includes the appendix \textit{Sur la Méthode des moindres quarrés}.
