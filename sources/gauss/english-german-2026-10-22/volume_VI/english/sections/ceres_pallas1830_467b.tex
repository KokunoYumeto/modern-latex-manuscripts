During the recent occultation of Aldebaran seen in daylight, I observed the ingress very well:

\begin{center}July 16. Ingress of $\alpha$ Tauri at the bright limb of the Moon $0^{\rm u}44^{\rm m}44^{\rm s}0$ mean time.\end{center}

Immediately afterwards clouds darkened the sky, and the Moon did not come into view again.
