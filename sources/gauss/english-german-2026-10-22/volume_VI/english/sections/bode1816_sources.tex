\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Bode’s 1816 yearbook\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~563--566. The first printing appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~40, 10 March 1814, pp.~393--400. It gives the Berlin imprint and names J.\,E. Hitzig, details omitted from the collected review.

Johann Elert Bode calculated and edited the \emph{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1816} with the approval of the Royal Academy of Sciences. It was printed in Berlin in 1813 by C.\,F.\,E. Späth, issued by Bode and sold on commission by J.\,E. Hitzig. The Heidelberg Astronomical Calculation Institute catalogue records 268 pages and one engraved plate. The digitized title and projection paper are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek copy.

Bode’s paper on the projection (pp.~222--226) describes the use of the tangents of two fifths of the angles measured from the pole and labels that proportion in the table on p.~224. The translation follows the fraction $\frac{2}{5}$ in the collected text.
