\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Schumacher’s Comet Report (17 February 1844)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the text on p.~465 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Heinrich Christian Schumacher reports observations and comparisons communicated by Gauss, with calculations by Dr. Goldschmidt. The journal issue is dated 17 February 1844. \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXI, no.~496, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049446_00132/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~247--248}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected report gives $3'34''18$ for the difference by which the comet preceded the star. The journal gives $3'44''18$ and goes on to print the comparison table mentioned at the end. The translation retains the reading and scope of the collected report.
