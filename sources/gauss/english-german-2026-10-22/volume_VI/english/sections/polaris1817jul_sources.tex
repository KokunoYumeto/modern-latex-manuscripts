\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Polaris and the Sun (18 July 1817)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter of 18 July 1817 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~393--399. The first printing is item XII, an extract from a letter to the director of the Seeberg Observatory, in Bernhard von Lindenau and Johann Gottlieb Friedrich Bohnenberger’s \textit{Zeitschrift für Astronomie und verwandte Wissenschaften}, vol.~IV, July and August 1817, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00119/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{119}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00131/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{131}. The first-print links lead to the Bavarian State Library copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00119/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{119} the first printing marks the eastward offset as $1''{,}90$, whereas the collected edition uses $1^{\mathrm s}90$. In the conjecture about the Sun’s center of gravity, the first printing gives $\frac{1}{400}$ and $\frac{1}{1000}$ on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00124/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{124}, and the collected edition gives $\frac{1}{100}$ and $\frac{1}{1000}$. On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00126/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{126} the first printing has no column for Bessel’s refraction and gives 7,2 for the April~8 barometer reading, which appears as 7~2 in the collected table.
\par\smallskip\noindent On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00128/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{128} both editions give 30,81 as the last entry of the second clock-time row. The first printing gives 61~30,15 for the June~20 reduction on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00129/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{129}, against the collected 61~30~15. On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00130/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{130} it gives $51^\circ\,31'\,45''{,}21$ for the solar result reduced to the center of the observatory. The collected edition prints $50^\circ\,31'\,45''21$. The first printing says \textit{Reduction} on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061006_00131/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{131} where the collected edition says \textit{Refraction}. The translation follows the collected edition, including its three bracketed handwritten annotations. The footnote on Westphal’s coefficients is in both editions.
