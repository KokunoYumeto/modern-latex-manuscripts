\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for The Zodiac of Juno (1805)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~119--121, including the complete table. The paper first appeared as article XX in the March 1805 issue of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde}, edited by Franz Xaver von Zach, vol.~11, pp.~225--228. \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Collected German text}. \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538603?page=237}{First printing}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The supplement refers to Gauss’s paper on the limits of the geocentric positions of the planets, on pp.~106--118 of the collected volume. The paper’s \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538602?page=181}{first printing} is vol.~10, article XIV, pp.~173--191, in the August 1804 issue.
