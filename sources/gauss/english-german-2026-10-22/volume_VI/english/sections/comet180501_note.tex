\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the First Comet of 1805}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~265--266. The report appeared as article~V in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XIII, January 1806, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=87}{pp.~79--83}. Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter of 5 December is quoted on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=90}{pp.~82}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=91}{83}. It discusses the observations of Johann Sigismund Gottfried Huth, Jacques-Joseph Thulis and Wilhelm Olbers and the elements calculated by Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel. The Marseille observations by Thulis appear in the November 1805 issue, vol.~XII, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538604?page=518}{p.~502}.
