\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the May 1802 Ceres Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the German excerpt in Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~211. Franz Xaver von Zach reports on Ceres, comparing the Seeberg and Greenwich observations with calculations by Carl Friedrich Gauss.
