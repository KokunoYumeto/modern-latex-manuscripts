\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss’s Meridian Observations of Iris and Neptune}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpt on p.~474 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss observed Iris on 21 August 1847 and Neptune on 17 August. Heinrich Christian Schumacher reports the observations in the \textit{Mittheilungen} of \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXVI, no.~611, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00095/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~173--174}. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00096/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{colophon on col.~176} gives 16 September 1847. The Bavarian State Library catalogue dates the bound volume to 1848.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected excerpt has a bracketed heading and gives both observations in its own notation. It also includes the remark on the wires, passing clouds and the missed culmination. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
