\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Comet Report (10 April 1826)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Comet} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~451--453. The unsigned report appears under \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~57 (10 April 1826), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538703_00567/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{561}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538703_00568/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{562}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538703_00569/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{563}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538703_00570/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{564} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538703_00571/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{565}. The links lead to the Bavarian State Library’s copy. The report refers to the earlier notices in \hyperlink{native-422}{no.~28} and \hyperlink{native-425}{no.~83} of 1819.
\par\smallskip\noindent The bracketed reference to pp.~417 and 420 belongs to the collected edition. The tables follow that edition, including the first longitude without a seconds sign and the abbreviation O without a period in the fourth longitude row. Both the journal and the collected edition give $4\frac{3}{4}$ and $3\frac{3}{8}$ years in their respective passages. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
