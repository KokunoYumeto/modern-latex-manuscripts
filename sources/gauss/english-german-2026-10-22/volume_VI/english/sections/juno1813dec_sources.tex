\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Juno (8, 9 and 16 December 1813)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the correspondence dated 8, 9 and 16 December 1813 in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~370--372. It first appeared as item LXIII, \textit{Auszug aus zweyen Schreiben des Hrn. Professor Gauss}, in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmelskunde}, vol.~XXVIII (December 1813), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00590/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{574}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00591/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{575}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00592/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{576}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00593/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{577}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00594/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{578}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing begins the letters of 8 and 16 December with points and includes notes signed N. on pp.~575--577. The collected edition uses dashes at those openings and omits the notes. In the Pallas comparison, the first printing ends at opposition IX. The collected edition includes opposition X and Schering’s bracketed remark. Both give 0.4267631 for the logarithm in the Juno elements. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
