\fancyhead[L]{\small GRAHAM'S PLANET.}
\SourceLeaf{482}
\begin{center}
\rule{0.55\linewidth}{0.4pt}\\[2pt]
Astronomische Nachrichten. Vol. XXVII. No. 639. pp. 235. 236. 10 June 1848.\\[2pt]
\rule{0.55\linewidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}
\begin{flushright}Göttingen 1848 May\raisebox{.35ex}{,} 22.\end{flushright}

I observed the new planet with the meridian circle on the very day I received your notice, and again two days later. The first time the planet was very faint. The second time, when the air was filled with smoke from the moors, it was so faint that little confidence can be placed in the observation. Even so, I give both observations here.
