\begin{center}\textbf{Source and translation}\end{center}
\hypertarget{connaissance1813-source-note}{}\label{connaissance1813-source-note}
\noindent AI-translated from the German text of Carl Friedrich Gauss's review of the \emph{Connaissance des tems} for 1813 and 1814, dated 3 June 1813. The translation follows \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\emph{Werke}, vol.\ VI}, Göttingen, 1874, pp.\ 554--555. The first printing in the \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue 88, pp.\ 873--876, gives the Paris and Courcier imprint before the titles, specifies octavo for 1814 and has a comma after \emph{bemerkten}. The collected edition has issue 8 and a period after \emph{bemerkten}. Its wording, coefficient signs and radical notation are retained.

\noindent The Bureau des Longitudes published both yearbooks. The title pages in the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek copies give Paris, Courcier, July 1811 for \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538233_00005/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{1813}, and Paris, Madame Veuve Courcier, April 1812 for \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538234_00005/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{1814}. The 1813 volume includes Laplace's paper on combining a large number of observations (\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538233_00217/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.\ 213}) and his paper on the long-period inequality of the Moon (\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538233_00227/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.\ 223}). Laplace prints the lunar coefficient as $-13''{,}92$ (\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538233_00229/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.\ 225}). Gauss's review gives $-13''96$. Lagrange's 1814 paper on the origin of comets begins on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538234_00215/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.\ 211}, distinguishes prograde and retrograde orbits on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538234_00217/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.\ 213}, and gives the illustrative distance and speeds on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538234_00219/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.\ 215}.
