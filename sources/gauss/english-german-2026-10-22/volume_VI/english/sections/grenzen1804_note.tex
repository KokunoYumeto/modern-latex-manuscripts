\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for On the Limits of the Geocentric Positions of the Planets (1804)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~106--118, including the three tables for Pallas and Ceres. The paper first appeared as article XIV in the August 1804 issue of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde}, edited by Franz Xaver von Zach, vol.~10, pp.~173--191. \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Collected German text}. \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538602?page=181}{First printing}.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss’s two references to his May 1804 paper point to pp.~98 and 100 of this volume. The \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538601?page=421}{May first printing} is vol.~9, article L, pp.~385--400, with the corresponding references at pp.~385\,ff.\ and 394.
\par\smallskip\noindent For orbital terminology, see Gauss’s \href{https://www2.karlin.mff.cuni.cz/~tuma/Aplikace21/theoryofmotionof00gausuoft.pdf#page=28}{\textit{Theory of the Motion of the Heavenly Bodies}, translated by Charles Henry Davis} (Boston, 1857), Book I, Section I, Arts.~1--3 and the opening of Art.~4, and Ernst Friedrich Maria Jochim’s \href{https://elib.dlr.de/94028/1/SAB-III-gesamt-2022-02-09.pdf#page=74}{\textit{Satellitenbewegung}, Band III} (PDF dated 9 February 2022), pp.~60--63.
