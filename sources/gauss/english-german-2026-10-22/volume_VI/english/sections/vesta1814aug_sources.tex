\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Vesta (13 August 1814)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report headed Vesta in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~377--379. The report appeared under Göttingen in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~129 (13 August 1814), pp.~\href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001287/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1281}, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001288/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1282}, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001289/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1283}, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001290/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1284}. The links lead to the digitized copy at the Göttingen State and University Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the latitude of the new Königsberg Observatory as $54^\circ\,42'\,50''31$ and dates Nicolai’s penultimate comparison row, with right ascension difference $+5.58$, to 26 February. The first printing gives $54^\circ\,17'\,50''31$ on p.~1282 and the day 29 in that row on p.~1283, with the difference printed as $+5{,}58$. The issue of 12 December 1814 identifies the latitude’s 17 minutes as a printing error in a \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001996/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{footnote on p.~1988} and gives 42 minutes.
