\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Tobias Mayer’s observations\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~644--645. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~160, 6 October 1827, pp.~1585--1588. The two-part edition of Mayer’s observations appeared in London in 1826 by order of the Commissioners of Longitude.
