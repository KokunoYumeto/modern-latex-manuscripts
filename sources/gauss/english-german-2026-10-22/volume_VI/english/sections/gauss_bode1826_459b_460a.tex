\begin{flushright}
Göttingen, 10 July 1826.
\end{flushright}

\begingroup\rightskip=0pt plus 1em\relax
I am sending you, my highly esteemed friend, my meridian observations of Pallas and Ceres around the time of their oppositions this year, for whatever use you wish to make of them in your Yearbook.
\par\endgroup

The planet’s faint light made the observations more difficult and reduced their precision. On the 27th I was misled by a star of the 10th magnitude only one arcminute from Pallas. As a result, the zenith distance was not set for the considerably fainter planet, and the planet itself was observed unreliably at only two wires.
