\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Herschel and South on double stars\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~642--644. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~201, 18 December 1826, pp.~2001--2004. It discusses the London editions of Herschel and South’s observations of 380 double and triple stars (1825) and South’s observations of 458 double and triple stars (1826).
