\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Regner on solar parallax}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\emph{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~523–525. The review first appeared unsigned under Göttingen in the \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~17, 29 January 1810, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1810:00000193/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~161}–\href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1810:00000198/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{166}. The \href{https://www.astro.uu.se/history/profflist_eng.html}{Uppsala Observatory’s roster} records the astronomer as Lars Regnér. The review spells his name Regner.

\noindent The first Latin title means \emph{Supplement to the history of the discovery of the solar parallax, submitted for judgment to the illustrious Royal Society of Sciences of Göttingen}. The second means \emph{Remarks on Mr. Schubert’s report to the Imperial Academy of Sciences in St Petersburg concerning a paper on solar parallax}. The translations keep the wording of the collected Latin titles, including the raised letters after \emph{D} in the second. The corresponding title appears on \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1810:00000196/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{p.~164 of the first printing}. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
