\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the letter to Bode}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter to Johann Elert Bode of 2 September 1810 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~319. Bode printed the excerpt on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538342_00245/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{237} of the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1813} (Berlin, 1810), following the ephemeris on pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538342_00244/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{236}--237. Bode’s signed note on the following page, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538342_00246/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{238}, is separate. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The bracket identifying the ephemeris belongs to the collected edition. The yearbook puts a period after Gauss says that the recipient already knows of the government’s plans. The collected text runs into the amount of 200000 francs without punctuation at that point. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
