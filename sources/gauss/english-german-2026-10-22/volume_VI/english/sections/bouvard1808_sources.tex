\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Bouvard review}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Gauss’s text on pp.~508--509 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). It appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Paris} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~177, 5 November 1808, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00745/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{1761}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00748/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{1764}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/21914082}{1808 volume} was published by the French Bureau des longitudes and printed by Courcier. Gauss discusses Alexis Bouvard’s \emph{New Tables of Jupiter and Saturn Calculated from Laplace’s Theory Using the Decimal Division of the Right Angle}. The volume also contains Jean-Baptiste Delambre’s tables of Jupiter’s satellites. The linked copy is held by the ETH Library (Rar 15631).
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing gives part~4 in the reference to p.~337 of Laplace’s \textit{Mécanique céleste} on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00746/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{p.~1762}. The collected edition gives part~8 and has an apostrophe-like mark immediately before \emph{Tafeln}. The translation follows the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
