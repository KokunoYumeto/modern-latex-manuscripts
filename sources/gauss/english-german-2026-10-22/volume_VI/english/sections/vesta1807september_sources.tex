\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the September 1807 Report on Vesta}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~290--292. It includes statements by Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers and Carl Friedrich Gauss, and the Paris observations that Bouvard sent to Gauss. The first printing is article XXII, \textit{Fortgesetzte Nachrichten über den neuen von Dr. Olbers in Bremen entdeckten Planeten Vesta}, in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmelskunde}, vol.~XVI, September 1807, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=299}{pp.~285}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=305}{291}. Olbers’s letter starts on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=300}{p.~286}, the third elements are on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=301}{p.~287}, and the comparison table and Gauss’s remarks on Harding’s atlas are on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=302}{p.~288}.
\par\medskip
The heading of the comparison table gives mean time in Paris in the collected edition and mean time in Bremen in the first printing. The two editable Vesta signs follow their outlines in the collected edition. In the time entry for 13 April, the Göttingen digitization shows a raised diagonal stroke between 6 and 2. The \href{https://archive.org/details/werkecarlf06gausrich}{University of California Libraries copy} shows an additional, staggered trace. The mark’s identity is uncertain. The \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=302}{first printing} gives the value as 6.2 seconds. The ephemeris on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=303}{p.~289}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=304}{p.~290}, and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=305}{p.~291} of the journal is described in brackets in the collected edition. The journal scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
