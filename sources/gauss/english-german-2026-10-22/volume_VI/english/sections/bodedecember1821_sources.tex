\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (26 December 1821)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter of 26 December 1821 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~439--440. Johann Elert Bode printed it under the heading \textit{Erfindung eines Heliotrops, Beobachtungen des Kometen von 1821 und Elemente seiner Bahn} in \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1825}, vol.~50, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538354_00109/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{103}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538354_00110/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{104}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538354_00111/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{105} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538354_00112/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{106}. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538354_00003/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{title page} of the Bavarian State Library copy gives Berlin 1822. The imprint names Bode as publisher, Ferd. Dümmler for distribution and C. F. E. Späth as printer. The collected source line gives Berlin 1821 and pp.~103--105. The comparison table fills p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538354_00112/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{106} of the yearbook.
\par\smallskip\noindent The letter gives 42 seconds in the first time entry, as does the yearbook. The earlier \hyperlink{native-442-part-lower}{report of 17 May 1821} gives 32 seconds. Each value is retained where it appears. The yearbook also gives $9\tfrac{1}{3}$ geographical miles. The letter credits von Staudt with calculating the elements and supplying the comparison in his own hand.
\par\smallskip\noindent On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538354_00110/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{104} Bode attached a signed footnote to the reference to the heliotrope account. In translation it reads “See the final section, \textit{Further assorted astronomical observations, remarks, etc.} B.” The collected text instead has an editorial reference to Volume IV at this point.
