\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Delambre’s tables\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~603--604. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~45, 19 March 1818, pp.~441--443. The Bibliothèque nationale de France records Delambre’s tables as a quarto printed in Paris by the widow Courcier in 1817.
