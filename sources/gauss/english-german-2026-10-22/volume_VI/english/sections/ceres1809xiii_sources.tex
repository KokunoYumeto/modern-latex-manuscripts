\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Ceres Elements XIII (3 June 1809)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the Ceres report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~312--314. It first appeared under \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue 87, 3 June 1809, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538675_00859/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{857}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538675_00860/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{858}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538675_00861/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{859}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538675_00862/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{860}. The digitized copy is from the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent Compare Bernhard August von Lindenau’s \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_caMAAAAAMAAJ/page/n518.jpg}{\textit{Fortgesetzte Nachrichten über die neuen Hauptplaneten}}, \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XIX, May 1809, pp.~504--515, in the New York Public Library copy digitized by Google. The parallel account of Ceres is on pp.~504--507. The \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_caMAAAAAMAAJ/page/n525.jpg}{Ceres table} labels the last column as the logarithm of the distance, and the \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_caMAAAAAMAAJ/page/n526.jpg}{Juno table} labels it as the distance from Earth. The collected edition’s bracket is translated as printed.
