\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Bessel on the comet of 1807}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~530--532. It first appeared unsigned under Königsberg in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~26, 16 February 1811, pp.~250--256.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected review cites Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel’s \textit{Untersuchungen über die scheinbare und wahre Bahn des im Jahre 1807 erschienenen grossen Cometen}. The book’s own title page spells the word \textit{Kometen} and gives the imprint Königsberg, Friedrich Nicolovius, 1810. The digitized copy is held by ETH-Bibliothek Zürich, Rar 33291.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected review prints \textit{Monatliche Crrespondenz}, where the first printing reads \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}. The first printing also sets the field-of-view formulas one below another and adds \textit{störenden} to the reference to each planet’s contribution. The translation follows the collected text.
