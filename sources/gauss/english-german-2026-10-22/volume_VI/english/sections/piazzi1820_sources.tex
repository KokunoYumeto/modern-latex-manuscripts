\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Piazzi’s Lezioni elementari di astronomia\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~617--618. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~4, 6 January 1820, pp.~33--37. Giuseppe Piazzi’s \emph{Lezioni elementari di astronomia} appeared in two volumes in Palermo in 1817, printed by the Stamperia Reale.
