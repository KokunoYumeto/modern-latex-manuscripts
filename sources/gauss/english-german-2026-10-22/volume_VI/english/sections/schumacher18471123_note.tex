\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Schumacher (23 November 1847)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s German letter to Heinrich Christian Schumacher of 23 November 1847 in the \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~194--196.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049452_00009/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, edited by H.~C.~Schumacher, vol.~27, no.~625, cols.~1--4. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049452_00016/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{issue is dated} 29 January 1848 at Altona. After Gauss’s signature the journal prints Carl Wolfgang Benjamin Goldschmidt’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049452_00010/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{\textit{Zodiakus der Iris}} as a separate table. Goldschmidt’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00128/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{second set of orbital elements for Iris} appeared in vol.~26, no.~615, cols.~239--240, in the issue dated 30 October 1847. The journal scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
