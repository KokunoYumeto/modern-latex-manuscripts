\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Observations of Pallas (October 1827)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Pallas} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~460. Heinrich Christian Schumacher presents the four observations that Gauss sent him. The first printing appeared as \textit{Pallasbeobachtungen} in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~VI, no.~124, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049431_00050/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~67--68}, in October 1827. The Bavarian State Library’s bound volume is dated 1828.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition puts the units in the first row and gives Schumacher’s surname where the journal has \textit{S.} The table and signature follow the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
