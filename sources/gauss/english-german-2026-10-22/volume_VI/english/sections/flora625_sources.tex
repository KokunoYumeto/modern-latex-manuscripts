\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Flora observation of 5 January 1848}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpt on p.~476 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on 10 January 1848 about the observation of Flora on 5 January.
\par\smallskip\noindent The excerpt appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXVII, no.~625, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049452_00015/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~13--14} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049452_00016/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~15--16}, issued 29 January 1848. Gauss compares the observing conditions on 3 and 5 January and gives two differences for Goldschmidt’s elements. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
