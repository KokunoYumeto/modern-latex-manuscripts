\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the solar eclipse of 28 July 1851}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report on p.~479 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). It gives the times recorded by Carl Friedrich Gauss, Westphal and Klinkerfues at the Göttingen Observatory for the beginning and end of the solar eclipse of 28 July 1851.
\par\smallskip\noindent The report appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXXIII, no.~771, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049458_00030/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~47--48}. The colophon on the same opening dates the issue 28 August 1851. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
