\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Juno (23 December 1813)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report headed Juno in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~372--373. It first appeared under Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~204 (23 December 1813), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538684_01009/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{2033}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538684_01010/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{2034}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected report uses a plural in the sentence about the calculation of the opposition. The first printing uses a singular. Both give 5 seconds for the opposition time and retain the double prime in the observation time. The separate December correspondence gives 25 seconds for the opposition. George’s Harp renders \textit{Georgsharfe}, shown as \textit{Harpa Georgii} on \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/3341782/full/2200,/0/default.jpg}{plate XII} of Johann Elert Bode’s \textit{Uranographia} (Berlin, 1801), ETH-Bibliothek Zürich, Rar 10278.
