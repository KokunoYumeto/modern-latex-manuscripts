\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (25 April 1812)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~349--351. The report appeared under the heading Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~67, 25 April 1812, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538681_00701/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{657}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538681_00702/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{658}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538681_00703/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{659} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538681_00704/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{660}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library. The report includes two communications from a letter of 26 March by Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel to Gauss.
\par\smallskip\noindent On p.~659 the journal gives 37 as the first number of the declination for Sequens and 3 seconds 321 as the annual motion in declination of the primary star. The collected text gives 47 and 2 seconds 321, respectively, with the positive sign on the annual motion. This translation retains the collected values. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
