\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Connaissance des tems for 1821\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~615--617. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~191, 29 November 1819, pp.~1905--1910. The collected edition gives Paris 1818 in the imprint of the yearbook for 1821. The first printing gives Paris 1819.
