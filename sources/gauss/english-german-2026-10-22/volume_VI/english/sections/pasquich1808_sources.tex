\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Pasquich’s observatory proposals}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s review on pp.~497--498 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The review appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Ofen} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~85, 28 May 1808, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00847/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{841}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00849/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{843}. The linked copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss quotes the title of Johann Pasquich’s pamphlet as \emph{Rechenschaft von meinen Vorschlägen zur Beförderung der Astronomie auf der königl. Universitäts-Sternwarte in Ofen}. Ofen is the German name for Buda. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
