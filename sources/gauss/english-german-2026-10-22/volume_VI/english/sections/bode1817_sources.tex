\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Bode’s Astronomical Yearbook for 1817\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~576--581. The first printing appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~28, 18 February 1815, pp.~265--277. It includes the assessment of Kramp’s method.

J. E. Bode calculated and edited the \emph{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1817}, published in Berlin in 1814 by the author and distributed on commission by J. E. Hitzig. The title page gives C. F. E. Späthen as printer. The Bavarian State Library copy is digitized by the Munich Digitization Center. Littrow’s contributions begin on p.~123. Reche’s letter to Natorp is excerpted on p.~254.

Christian Kramp’s third memoir on astronomical problems appeared on 1 July 1814 in \emph{Annales de mathématiques pures et appliquées}, vol.~5, pp.~1--28. The omission of terms is discussed on p.~13 and the numerical comparison appears on p.~27. Kramp lists 14, 19 and 22 November in that comparison. Gauss’s review gives 14, 19 and 25 November.
