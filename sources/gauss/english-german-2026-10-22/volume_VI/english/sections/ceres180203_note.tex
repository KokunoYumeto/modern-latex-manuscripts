\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the March 1802 Ceres Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report in Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~205--209. It includes Gauss’s quoted remarks, orbital calculations, tables and ephemeris, together with von Zach’s text and note.
\par\smallskip\noindent Von Zach’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00273/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{article XXIX} appeared in the March 1802 issue of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde} (Gotha, Becker), vol.~V, pp.~263--282. The collected excerpt begins with \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00277/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Gauss’s letter on p.~267} and ends with his \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00290/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{remarks on p.~280}. The journal scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
