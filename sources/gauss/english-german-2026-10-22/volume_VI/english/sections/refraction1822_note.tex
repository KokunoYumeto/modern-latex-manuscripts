\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Refraction Tables (1822)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Refractionstafeln} in the \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~185--190. It includes Heinrich Christian Schumacher’s introduction and all three tables, printed across five pages.
