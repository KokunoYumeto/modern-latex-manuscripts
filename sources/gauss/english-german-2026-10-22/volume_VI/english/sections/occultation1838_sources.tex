\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Stellar Occultation at the Göttingen Observatory (1838)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the notice on p.~463 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss and Goldschmidt observed the occultation on 27 June 1838. Heinrich Christian Schumacher published their observations in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XVI, no.~361, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049441_00011/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~5--6}, dated 18 October 1838 in the collected edition.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal uses hour, minute and second notation with primes and signs the notice \textit{S.} The collected edition uses raised letter symbols and spells out Schumacher’s surname. The translation follows the collected notation and signature. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
