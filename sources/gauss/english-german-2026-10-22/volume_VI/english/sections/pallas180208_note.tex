\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the August 1802 Pallas Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the German excerpt in Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~221--222. Franz Xaver von Zach reports on Pallas and quotes Carl Friedrich Gauss and Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers.
\par\smallskip\noindent Von Zach’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00195/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{original article}, no.~XVIII, appeared in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde} (Gotha, Becker), vol.~VI, August 1802, pp.~187--196. The collected excerpt starts on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00200/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~192} with the reports from Vilna, Prague, Kremsmünster and Padua. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00201/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Milan comparison} refers back to the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00079/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{observations in the July issue, p.~73}. The continuation of the July \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00091/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{ephemeris} is printed in the journal on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00204/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~196}. The collected editor gives a bracketed note in its place.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal tables omit the mean-time columns. The last Milan declination difference reads \(+1{,}4\) in the journal and \(-1.4\) in the collected edition. In the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00202/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Bremen table}, the journal labels the second calculated column northern declination. The collected edition labels both calculated columns right ascension. The translations follow the collected reading in each case.
\par\smallskip\noindent \href{https://doi.org/10.1098/rstl.1802.0010}{William Herschel’s paper}, pp.~216--217, describes his observations on 22 April. A separate English rendering of part of the diameter discussion appears in \href{https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-07614-0_14}{Clifford J.\ Cunningham and Wayne Orchiston, section~6.2}.
\par\smallskip\noindent Translation and typesetting produced by AI. Journal scans from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
