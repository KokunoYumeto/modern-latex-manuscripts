\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Letter of 23 August 1807}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter to Johann Elert Bode of 23 August 1807 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~284--285. In the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1810} (Berlin, 1807), the letter begins on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538339?page=222}{p.~212}, continues with the third elements on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538339?page=223}{p.~213} and ends with the ephemeris on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538339?page=224}{p.~214}. The collected edition describes the ephemeris in square brackets. The Yearbook scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
\par\medskip
Gauss’s abbreviation M.\,C. refers to the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}. His second elements and his account of their agreement with observations appear on \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0605.tif}{p.~598} of Franz Xaver von Zach’s June 1807 report on Vesta, article XLIII, vol.~XV, \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0597.tif}{pp.~590}--600. The journal scans are from the Thüringer Universitäts- und Landesbibliothek Jena. The editable altar sign in the letter follows its outline in the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
