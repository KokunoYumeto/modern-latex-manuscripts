\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the 25 August 1806 Letter}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter of 25 August 1806 to Franz Xaver von Zach in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~279--280. The letter appeared as article XXXIII in the October 1806 \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XIV, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=393}{pp.~377}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=398}{382}. The journal gives the ephemeris described in the collected edition on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=395}{pp.~379}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=397}{381}.
\par\medskip
The brightness column in the table Gauss cites gives 0,03983 for 23 and 26 February 1806, and 0,03972 for 1 March. The letter gives 0.03983 for March. \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538603?page=492}{\textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XI, May 1805, p.~478.}
\par\medskip
After the ephemeris the journal prints Gauss’s corrections to his earlier letter on the second comet. On \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=397}{p.~381} he directs the reader to read \emph{Aberration} for \emph{Nutation} on p.~183, third line from the bottom, and 71 for 73 on p.~186, fourth line from the bottom. On \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=398}{p.~382} he says the two-day reduction is of no consequence, since a second example for his work used observations of Ceres 112 days apart with equally successful results.
