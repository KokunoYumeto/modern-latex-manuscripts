\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the letter of 17 August 1810}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter of 17 August 1810 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~318--319. The first printing is item XXXI in \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_YCI4AAAAMAAJ/page/n4.jpg}{\textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXII}, September 1810, pp.~\href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_YCI4AAAAMAAJ/page/n292.jpg}{289} and \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_YCI4AAAAMAAJ/page/n293.jpg}{290}, in the University of Michigan copy digitized by Google and hosted by the Internet Archive.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected heading cites volume XVIII. The journal title page identifies volume XXII and names F. von Zach as editor. The first printing has four opening dots and a period before the sentence beginning with the hope that work will have resumed. It uses prime signs for the two quantities measured in time and spells the observing site Freytagswerder. The translation follows the collected text. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
