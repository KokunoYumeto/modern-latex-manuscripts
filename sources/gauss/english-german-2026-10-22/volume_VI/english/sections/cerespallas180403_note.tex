\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the March 1804 Ceres and Pallas Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~246--247. The report begins on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538601_00260/full/1200,/0/default.jpg}{p.~246} of article XXV in the March 1804 issue of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~IX. Gauss’s ephemeris for Pallas occupies \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538601_00261/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~247}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538601_00262/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~248} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538601_00263/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~249}. The collected edition gives a bracketed description of the ephemeris and retains the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538601_00264/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{elements and concluding paragraph on p.~250}. The journal’s list of stars from Piazzi’s catalogue begins on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538601_00265/full/1250,/0/default.jpg}{p.~251}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The report cites Gauss’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538600_00380/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{October 1803 ephemeris for Ceres}, the December notice of Karl Ludwig Harding’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538600_00547/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{chart of the path of Ceres}, and the accompanying \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538600_00548/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Ceres comparison stars}. Johann Elert Bode’s ephemeris for Pallas begins on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538335_00101/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~91 of the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1806}}.
