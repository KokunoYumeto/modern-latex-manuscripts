\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (17 March and 15 August 1817)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letters to Johann Elert Bode as printed in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~399--402. The first printing appeared under the collective title \textit{Astronomische Beobachtungen und Bemerkungen, vom Hrn. Prof. Ritter Gauß in Göttingen, aus Briefen desselben} in the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1820} (Berlin, 1817), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538349_00205/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{201}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538349_00209/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{205}. The links to the first printing lead to the Bavarian State Library copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The yearbook has two printing notes on the opening page. The first follows the mention of the ephemeris and reads “It follows below.” The second follows Gauss’s question about the winter sky and reads “See my observations on p.~90 and following.” Neither note is in the collected letters. The ephemeris mentioned in the letter is outside the collected text.
\par\smallskip\noindent On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538349_00206/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{202} the yearbook uses short dash leaders for the lunar ingresses and marks the eastward offset as $1''{,}90$ of time. The collected edition uses dotted leaders and $1^{\mathrm s}90$. The entries 16,16 for April~11 and 49,60 for May~7 on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538349_00207/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{203} and the solar value $51^\circ\,31'\,45''{,}21$ with both $4''{,}1$ differences on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538349_00208/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{204} belong to this pair of letters. The separate July letter has its own readings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
