\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of the 1817 Milan ephemerides\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~601--603. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~144, 8 September 1817, pp.~1433--1438. The volume reviewed is the 1817 Milan ephemeris calculated by Francesco Carlini and Enrico Brambilla and printed in Milan in 1816.
