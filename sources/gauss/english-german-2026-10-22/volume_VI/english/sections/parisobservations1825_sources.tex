\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of the Paris Observatory observations\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~641--642. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~129, 14 August 1826, pp.~1281--1284. The volume under review contains the Paris Observatory observations for 1810--1819, published in Paris by Bachelier for the Bureau des longitudes in 1825.
