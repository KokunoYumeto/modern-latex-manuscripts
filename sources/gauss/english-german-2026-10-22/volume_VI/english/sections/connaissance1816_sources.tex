\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Connaissance des tems for 1816\end{center}

The English translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss's review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~581--586. The review first appeared in the \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~40, 11 March 1815, pp.~385--396. The volume of \emph{Connaissance des tems} for 1816 was published by the Bureau des longitudes and issued by the widow Courcier in Paris in November 1813, as stated in the review. Gauss cites pp.~216--217 for Laplace's article on comets. He also refers to Lagrange's hypothesis in the volume for 1814.

The Venus-mass fraction follows the printed shape in the collected edition. Its denominator is uncertain.
