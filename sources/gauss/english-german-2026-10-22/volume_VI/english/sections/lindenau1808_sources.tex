\begin{center}\textbf{Source for the 25 January 1808 Letter to von Lindenau}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter to von Lindenau of 25 January 1808 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~296--298. It first appeared as \textit{Auszug aus einem Schreiben des Herrn Professors Gauss} in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XVII, February 1808, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538608?page=188}{pp.~182}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538608?page=194}{188}, in the digitized copy of the Bavarian State Library.
\par\medskip
The collected edition refers its readers to the comet observations in the notice of 25 February and prints the Milan observations of Vesta alongside their differences. The first printing gives them on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538608?page=189}{p.~183} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538608?page=190}{p.~184}. In the Pallas table the raised mark after 770 in the collected edition is retained. The first printing places a comma and seconds mark there. The letter in the collected edition ends with a bracketed description of the Juno ephemeris printed on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538608?page=192}{pp.~186}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538608?page=194}{188} in the journal.
