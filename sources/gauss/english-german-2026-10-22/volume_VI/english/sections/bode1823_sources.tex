\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Bode’s Astronomical Yearbook for 1823\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~625--628. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~5, 8 January 1821, pp.~41--48. Johann Elert Bode’s yearbook for 1823 appeared in Berlin in 1820, available from the author and on commission through Ferdinand Dümmler.

See also \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538352_00192/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{Walbeck’s article, p.~186}, and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538352_00229/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{Enke’s article on Vesta, p.~223}, in the yearbook.
