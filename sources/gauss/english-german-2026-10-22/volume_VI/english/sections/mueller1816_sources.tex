\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Müller’s guide to solar eclipses\end{center}

The English translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), p.~591. It first appeared in the \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~20, 3 February 1816, p.~200. The collected edition reads \emph{merkwürdiegen}. The translation renders its sense as “remarkable.”

Johann Wolfgang Müller’s guide was issued in Sulzbach by J.~E. Seidel in 1816. The Bavarian State Library holds the digitized copy, Astr.p.~94~r. The final comparison on p.~128 gives the eclipse magnitude in the historical units \emph{Zoll} and \emph{Minuten}, rendered here as digits and minutes.
