\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss’s review of Laplace}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Gauss’s text on pp.~500--503 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The review appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Paris} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~119, 23 July 1808, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00155/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{1185}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00162/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{1192}. The linked copy is held by the Bavarian State Library. Some running heads give 25 July.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss reviews the third edition of Pierre-Simon Laplace’s \href{https://www.loc.gov/item/17002967/}{\textit{Exposition du système du monde}} (Paris, Courcier, 1808). The title means \emph{An Account of the System of the World}. Two characters in the table of secular changes look like a small c in the collected edition. It also leaves a space within the entry for Jupiter’s semimajor axis. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00159/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} shows zeroes and a decimal comma at those places. The translated tables follow the collected text.
\par\smallskip\noindent The French words quoted from Laplace in the fifth-book paragraph are \emph{Le tableau des progrès de la plus sublime des sciences naturelles, toujours croissans au milieu même des révolutions des empires, pourra consoler des malheurs dont les récits remplissent les annales de tous les peuples.} Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
