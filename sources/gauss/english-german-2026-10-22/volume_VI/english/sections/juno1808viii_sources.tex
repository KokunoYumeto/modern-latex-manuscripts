\begin{center}\textbf{Source for Juno Elements VIII}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the 25 August 1808 notice in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~306--307. The first printing appeared under \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingischen gelehrten Anzeigen}, issue 136, pp.~\href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00001359/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1353}, \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00001360/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1354} and \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00001361/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1355}.
\par\medskip
The collected notice refers to the observation table in the 6 August letter. The notice’s first printing gives the last figure of Juno’s right ascension on July~30 as 0, while the first printing of the 6 August letter and its collected text give 9. The notice and the later letter also give different values for the geocentric latitude at opposition. Both collected readings are preserved. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
