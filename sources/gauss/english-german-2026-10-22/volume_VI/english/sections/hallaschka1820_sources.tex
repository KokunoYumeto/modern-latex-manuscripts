\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Hallaschka’s Elementa eclipsium\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), p.~619. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~17, 29 January 1820, pp.~167--168. Cassian Hallaschka’s \emph{Elementa eclipsium} was printed by Theophil Haase in Prague in 1816.
