\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Brambilla’s Milan ephemerides for 1823\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~629--630. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~64, 21 April 1823, pp.~633--637. Enrico Brambilla’s \emph{Effemeridi Astronomiche di Milano} for 1823 appeared in Milan in 1822 at the Imperial--Royal Press.
