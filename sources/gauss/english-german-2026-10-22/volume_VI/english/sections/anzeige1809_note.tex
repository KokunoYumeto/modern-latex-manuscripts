\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the 1809 Review of \textit{Theoria motus}}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the German text in Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~51 and 53--60. It includes the section title and the review published in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~96, 17 June 1809, pp.~945--955. \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb10538675?page=947}{First printing at the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek}. \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Collected German text}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The book preface is a separate text. It is available in Charles Henry Davis’s English translation of \textit{Theoria motus} (Boston, 1857). For modern German orbit-determination terminology, see Ernst Friedrich Maria Jochim, \textit{Satellitenbewegung}, vol.~III, version of 9 February 2022, pp.~342 and 363--364.
