\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the report on Steinheil’s prism photometer\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s report in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~649--652. It first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, nos.~34--35, 5 March 1835, pp.~329--337. Carl August Steinheil’s prize essay was subsequently published as \emph{Elemente der Helligkeits-Messungen am Sternenhimmel}.
