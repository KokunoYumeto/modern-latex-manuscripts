\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Connaissance des tems for 1818\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~595--597. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~54, 5 April 1817, pp.~531--536. It identifies the astronomical yearbook as the volume for 1818, issued in Paris by the widow Courcier in 1815.

In the probable-error fraction for Bouvard’s determination of the mass of Jupiter, the first printing (p.~535) reads 723 in the denominator. The collected edition gives 72 followed by an indistinct mark. The translation retains the outline of that mark. The other fractions and the decimal points follow the collected edition. The French quotation retains the collected edition’s spelling and emphasis. The spelling \emph{Connoissance} in the discussion of Regiomontanus’s problem also follows the collected edition.
