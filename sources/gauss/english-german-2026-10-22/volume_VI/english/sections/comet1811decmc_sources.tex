\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (December 1811, Monatliche Correspondenz)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~343--344. The first printing was in item LVII, \textit{IIter Comet vom Jahre 1811}, in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXIV, December 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00611/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{595}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00612/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{596}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00613/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{597} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00614/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{598}. The Göttingen extract begins on p.~596 after earlier material on Seeberg and observations by Olbers and Blanpain. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected text puts the date of Gauss’s letter in the running text, where the journal had given it in a footnote. The numbers, punctuation of the tables and both opening quotation marks follow the collected text. For 11 December this report gives seconds of 18.0 in right ascension and 46.4 in declination. The separate December report in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen} gives 20.5 and 54.7. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
