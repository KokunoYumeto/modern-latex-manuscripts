\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Hardy’s Pendulum Clock (26 June 1826)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Hardy’s Pendeluhr} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~453--454. The unsigned notice appears under \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~101 (26 June 1826), \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538704_00329/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{p.~1001} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538704_00330/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{p.~1002}. The links lead to the Bavarian State Library’s copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing puts the 21 observations in one table and reads \textit{retardirt}, where the collected edition reads \textit{retradirt}. The translation gives the reported decrease in the daily rate and follows the collected tables, including both raised seconds marks and the blank rate for 17 June. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
