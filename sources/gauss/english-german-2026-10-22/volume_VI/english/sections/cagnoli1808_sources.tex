\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Cagnoli’s star catalogue}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s review on pp.~496--497 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The review appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Modena} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~77, 14 May 1808, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00767/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{761}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00769/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{763}. The linked copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The French title means \emph{Catalogue of 501 stars, followed by the corresponding tables of aberration and nutation}, by Antoine Cagnoli. Gauss describes the catalogue and the tables. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
