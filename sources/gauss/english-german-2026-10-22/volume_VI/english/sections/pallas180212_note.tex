\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the December 1802 Pallas Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s December 1802 report on Pallas as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~232--234. It includes Gauss’s comparison with Oriani’s observations, the fifth set of elements, the comparison with Messier’s latest observation and the brightness figures.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00593/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} is article LXV of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~VI, pp.~579--583. The excerpt starts with the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00594/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{comparison on p.~580} and continues through the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00595/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{elements V on p.~581} and the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00596/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{comparison with Messier on p.~582}. The journal prints the full \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00597/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{ephemeris and four brightness figures on p.~583}. The collected edition describes the ephemeris in brackets and gives a manuscript reading of the April brightness and four additional brightness figures.
\par\smallskip\noindent The report refers to the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00404/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{elements IV} and the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00405/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{earlier ephemeris} in the October issue and to \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00516/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Messier’s observation} in the November issue.
