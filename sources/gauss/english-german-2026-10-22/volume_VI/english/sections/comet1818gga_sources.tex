\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the comet report (13 April 1818)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report printed in Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~403--404. The collected edition cites the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~60, 13 April 1818, pp.~593--594. The report refers to Gauss in the third person and speaks in the first person plural when presenting the results to readers.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition prints \textit{Dphemeride} in its bracketed sentence, rendered here as \textit{Dphemeris}. The sentence refers to the ephemeris printed in the preceding report. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
