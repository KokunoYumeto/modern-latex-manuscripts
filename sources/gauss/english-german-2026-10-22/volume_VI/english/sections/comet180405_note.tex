\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the May 1804 New Comet Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~247--248. The report appeared as article LIV in the May 1804 issue of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~IX, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538601?page=468}{p.~432}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538601?page=469}{p.~433}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538601?page=470}{p.~434} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538601?page=471}{p.~435}. Von Zach reports observations by Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers, Jean-Louis Pons, Alexis Bouvard and Charles Messier, and gives the parabolic elements and calculation by Carl Friedrich Gauss. The method cited on p.~385 is Gauss’s \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538601?page=421}{\textit{Einige Bemerkungen zur Vereinfachung der Rechnung für die geocentrischen Oerter der Planeten}}, also in \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI, p.~94.
