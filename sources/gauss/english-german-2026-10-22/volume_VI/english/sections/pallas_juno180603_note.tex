\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the March 1806 Pallas and Juno Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~269--270. The report appeared as article~XXIV, \textit{Fortgesetzte Nachrichten über die neuen Planeten}, in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XIII, March 1806, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=323}{p.~313}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=324}{p.~314} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=325}{p.~315}. The four observations of Pallas and two observations of Juno on journal p.~314 are given in \hyperlink{native-272}{Gauss’s letter to Bode} on p.~267 of the collected edition. The report quotes Carl Friedrich Gauss on perturbations and on the difficulties of observing Juno. Translation and typesetting produced by AI. The journal scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
