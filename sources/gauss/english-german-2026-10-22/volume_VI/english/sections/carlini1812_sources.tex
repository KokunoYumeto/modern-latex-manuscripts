\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Carlini review}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~543--546. It first appeared under Milan in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~193, 3 December 1812, pp.~1921--1928.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected text quotes \textit{applicuto}. The first printing and Carlini's title page read \textit{applicato}. The collected text gives the 1804 correction as 1.09 and uses decimal points in its comparison table. The first printing gives \(+1{,}09\) and uses decimal commas in that table.
