\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Humboldt and Oltmans review}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Gauss’s text on p.~509 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). It appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Paris und Tübingen} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~193, 3 December 1808, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00922/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{p.~1928}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The Latin title identifies a \emph{Survey of Geographical Longitudes and Latitudes Astronomically Observed by Alexander von Humboldt in the Equinoctial Region in the Years 1799 to 1804 and Computed by Jabbo Oltmans}. Gauss cites F. Schöll and J. G. Cotta in Paris and Tübingen and gives the extent as sixteen folio pages. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
