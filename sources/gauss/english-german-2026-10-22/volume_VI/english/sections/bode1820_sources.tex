\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Bode’s astronomical yearbook for 1820\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~605--608. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, nos.~81 and 82, 21 May 1818, pp.~801--810. Johann Elert Bode’s yearbook for 1820 was published in Berlin in 1817 by the author, on commission with Ferdinand Dümmler.
