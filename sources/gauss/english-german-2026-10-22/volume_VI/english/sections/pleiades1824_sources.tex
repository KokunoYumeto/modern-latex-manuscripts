\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Pleiades Occultation (29 August 1820)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report \textit{Sternbedeckung} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~448. The observations from Göttingen were communicated in a letter from Gauss. The collected edition identifies Bessel as the recipient in brackets. The corresponding passage appears in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~III, no.~50 (February 1824), \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049428_00023/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{col.~22}, in \textit{Berechnung verschiedener Sternbedeckungen}, a study by Rosenberger, Strehlke and Klupsz communicated by Bessel. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049428_00021/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{article heading} and the passage are from the Bavarian State Library’s copy of the volume edited by H.~C. Schumacher and published in Altona in 1825.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected table has four rows, retains two repetition dashes and places the unit marks in the first row. In the first time for $\eta$, the journal gives 3,37 in the seconds position and the collected edition gives 37.37. The translation retains the collected figures and the bracketed explanation of $x$.
