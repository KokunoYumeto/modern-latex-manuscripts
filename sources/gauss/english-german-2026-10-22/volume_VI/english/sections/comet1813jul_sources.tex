\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (5 and 12 July 1813)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpt headed Comet in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~368. The letters of 5 and 12 July 1813 were first printed together as item IX of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmelskunde}, vol.~XXVIII (July 1813), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00103/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{97}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00104/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{98}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00105/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{99}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected excerpt marks its omissions with dashes. The first printing includes Pond’s star table and the accompanying discussion on pp.~97--98. The comet observations and elements are on p.~99. The two entries for 25 April are distinct in both editions. The comet sign follows the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
