\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (letters of 8 and 16 September 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report and extracts from Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letters in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~335--337. The extracts appeared within item XXXI, \textit{Ueber den grossen Cometen von 1811}, in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXIV, September 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00314/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{304}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00315/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{305}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00316/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{306} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00317/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{307}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The dates of the letters appear in footnotes on pp.~304 and 306 of the journal and in the text of the collected edition. The first printing and the collected text both give 0 as the hour for 17 September. The point before 27 in the September ephemeris appears in the collected edition. The historical note cites the August issue, p.~180. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
