\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (report of 8 August 1812)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report of 8 August 1812 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~356--357. It first appeared under the heading Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~127, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538682_00223/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1257}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538682_00224/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1258}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538682_00225/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1259}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The true longitude at opposition on p.~1258 of the journal is $259^\circ\;27'\;51''7$. The collected report gives $259^\circ\;37'\;51''7$. The translation retains the collected figure. Gauss’s separate letter of 4 August gives its own opposition figures. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
