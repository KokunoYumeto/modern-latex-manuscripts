\begin{center}\textbf{Source for Vesta, Ceres, Comet (10 November 1808)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the 10 November 1808 report in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~308--309. The first of these leaves is printed as 380. The report first appeared under \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingischen gelehrten Anzeigen}, issue 180, pp.~\href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00001799/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1793}, \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00001800/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1794} and \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00001801/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1795}.
\par\medskip
The report distinguishes Bode’s Berlin observations, the Petersburg observations by Schubert and Wisniewsky communicated by Bode, and the Göttingen observations with Tiarks’s comparison. The July~2 declination is blank in both printings. The June~22 declination in this report and the one in the letter of 27 June are preserved as printed in the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
