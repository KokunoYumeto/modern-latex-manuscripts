\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (21 April 1824)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter of 21 April 1824 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~449--450. Bode’s \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1827} (Berlin, 1824), pp.~\href{https://books.google.nl/books?id=4C8PAAAAQAAJ&pg=PA128&redir_esc=y}{128}, \href{https://books.google.nl/books?id=4C8PAAAAQAAJ&pg=PA129&redir_esc=y}{129} and \href{https://books.google.nl/books?id=4C8PAAAAQAAJ&pg=PA130&redir_esc=y}{130}, prints it under the heading \textit{Meridianbeobachtungen des Kometen von 1824}. The links lead to the copy digitized by Google Books.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing dates the first two observation rows 31 January and 3 February. The collected edition gives 31 February and 3 January. The translation retains the collected labels, the blank seconds position and colon in the row dated the 20th, and the two groups of 16 comparison rows. The value $+963.4$ and Gauss’s suggestion of a one-minute error in the right ascension of the Altona observation remain as printed. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
