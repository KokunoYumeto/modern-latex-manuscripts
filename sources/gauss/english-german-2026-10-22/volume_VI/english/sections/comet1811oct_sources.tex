\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (October 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the editorial report and the excerpt from Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter of 14 October 1811 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~338--340. The first printing is item XL, \textit{Über den großen Cometen von 1811}, in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXIV, October 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00418/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{406}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00419/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{407}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00420/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{408}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00421/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{409}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00422/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{410} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00423/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{411}. It identifies itself as a continuation of the September item beginning on p.~289. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal dates the letter in a footnote on p.~409. The collected edition incorporates the date into the text and ends with Gauss’s remark that Olbers will write in more detail. The journal then resumes the editorial report and begins the comparisons with von Zach’s observations at Marseille. The journal also marks the 10 September entry of $-27$ in declination with a double prime, uses different time marks for the perihelion passage, marks the units in the inclination and squares the cosine in all three coordinate formulas. The translation preserves the collected edition’s signs, units and denominators. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
