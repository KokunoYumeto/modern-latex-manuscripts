\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the November 1802 Pallas Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s November 1802 report on Pallas as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~230--231. The excerpt includes von Zach’s response to William Herschel and quotes Gauss’s letter of 16 October on the classification of Ceres and Pallas and the rule for planetary distances.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00513/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} is article LII of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~VI, pp.~499--505. The collected excerpt runs from the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00516/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{notice of Herschel’s offprint on p.~502} to the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00519/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{end of Gauss’s letter on p.~505}. The offprint is Herschel’s \href{https://doi.org/10.1098/rstl.1802.0010}{\textit{Observations on the Two Lately Discovered Celestial Bodies}}. Gauss’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538595_00604/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{June 1801 reference} prints the numerical rule and attributes its general form to Wurm. The collected editors add the reference to \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538319_00180/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Wurm’s discussion in the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch} for 1790, p.~168}.
\par\smallskip\noindent An earlier English rendering of part of Gauss’s letter appears in Alexander von Humboldt’s \href{https://www.geology.19thcenturyscience.org/books/1858-Humboldt-Cosmos/Vol-IV/PDFpages/1858-Humboldt-Cosmos-IV-0118.pdf}{\textit{Cosmos}, vol.~IV, p.~118}, translated by E.~C. Otté and B.~H. Paul (\href{https://www.geology.19thcenturyscience.org/books/1858-Humboldt-Cosmos/Vol-IV/PDFpages/1858-Humboldt-Cosmos-IV-0002.pdf}{New York, Harper \& Brothers, 1858}). That excerpt omits \(4+24\). The translation here gives the full sequence and passage as printed in the \textit{Werke}. The digital copy of \textit{Cosmos} was prepared by David C. Bossard in December 2005.
