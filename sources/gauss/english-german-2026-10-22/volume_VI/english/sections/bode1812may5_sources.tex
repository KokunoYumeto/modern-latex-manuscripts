\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (5 May 1812)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter of 5 May 1812 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~351--354. Its first printing appeared as observations of Pallas, the second comet of 1811 and its orbital elements, stellar occultations, and related matters, in \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00005/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{\textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1815}}, edited by Johann Elert Bode (Berlin, 1812), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00198/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{190}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00199/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{191}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00200/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{192}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00201/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{193}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00202/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{194}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The yearbook includes a note on p.~190 signed B., which is outside the collected text. On p.~192 it prints a single prime after 39 in the perihelion longitude, and on p.~193 it gives $+5''4$ for the 9 December declination residual. The collected edition has a double prime after 39 and gives $+3''4$. The translation follows the collected readings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
