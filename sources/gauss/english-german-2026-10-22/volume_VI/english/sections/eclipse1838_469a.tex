In Göttingen, Councillor \textsc{Gauss} was able to observe only the beginning of this solar eclipse, at
\begin{center}15 March 1838.\quad $3^{\rm u}59^{\rm m}9^{\rm s}6$ mean time.\end{center}
\noindent From the middle of the eclipse onward, clouds hid the Sun.
