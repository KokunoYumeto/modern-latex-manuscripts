\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss’s Meridian Observations of Neptune (August 1847)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the observations on p.~472 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss made the three observations at Göttingen on 12, 13 and 14 August 1847 and dated the report 15 August. The first printing is in Heinrich Christian Schumacher’s \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXVI, no.~610, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00085/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~153--154}. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00088/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{colophon on col.~160} gives 30 August 1847. The Bavarian State Library catalogue dates the bound volume to 1848.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing includes Gauss’s signature, abbreviates the headings and gives the time units and decimal separators differently. The table and attribution here follow the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
