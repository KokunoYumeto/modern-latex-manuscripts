\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the December 1807 Comet Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the December 1807 comet excerpt in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~292. Franz Xaver von Zach introduces Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter of 16 November 1807. The first printing is part of article XLIV, \textit{Fortgesetzte Nachrichten über den Cometen von 1807}, in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmelskunde}, vol.~XVI, December 1807, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=576}{pp.~562}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=581}{567}. The introduction to the letter begins at the foot of \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=578}{p.~564}, and Gauss’s orbital elements and closing remarks are on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538607?page=579}{p.~565}.
\par\medskip
The collected table includes a bracketed inclination and prints \textit{duchs} in its first row, where the journal prints \textit{durchs}. The journal scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
