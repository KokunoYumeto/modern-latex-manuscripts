\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss’s Comet Observations of 8 and 10 August 1844}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the two passages dated 8 and 10 August 1844 on p.~467 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher. The passages appeared together in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXII, no.~513, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049447_00079/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~141--142} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049447_00080/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~143--144}, issued on 22 August 1844.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal includes a heading for Gauss’s correspondence and a signature. The collected extract omits both. The tables retain the collected time-unit signs, the short mark after 48, the repeated time-label dashes, the decimal points and the abbreviation G.~R. The separate journal contributions on observations from Hamburg and by Edward Cooper fall outside the collected extract. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
