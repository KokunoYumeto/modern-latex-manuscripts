\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (24 February 1810)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~317--318. It appeared in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue 32, 24 February 1810, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538677_00341/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{305}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538677_00342/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{306}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538677_00343/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{307}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538677_00344/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{308}. The digitized copy is from the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the north latitude at the 1808 opposition as 37\,33\,54. The two journal accounts give 37\,43\,54 (see \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538677_00342/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{p.~306} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00285/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{\textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, p.~277}). The collected report and the \textit{Anzeigen} give 57.3 as the last component of the declination for 29 September. On p.~278 of the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00286/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{\textit{Correspondenz}}, it reads 57.4.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected report marks the omission of Wisniewsky’s occultation table with two dashes. The table is on pp.~307--308 of the \textit{Anzeigen}. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
