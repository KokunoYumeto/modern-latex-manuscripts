\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Notice of Goldschmidt’s Comet Elements (17 August 1844)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the bracketed notice on p.~467 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The notice refers to the letter that Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on 28 July 1844, printed in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXII, no.~512, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049447_00066/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~115--116}. The collected citation dates the issue to 17 August 1844.
\par\smallskip\noindent The letter credits Dr. Goldschmidt with the calculation of the parabolic elements. The journal prints those elements, the differences from three observations, the ephemeris and the coordinate expressions. The collected text gives the bracketed notice.
