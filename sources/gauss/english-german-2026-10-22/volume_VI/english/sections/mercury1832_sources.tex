\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Schumacher (12 May 1832)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the letter on p.~463 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on 12 May 1832. The letter appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~X, no.~225, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049435_00080/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~143--144}, issued on 3 July 1832.
\par\smallskip\noindent In the declination entries, the collected edition prints a raised \textup{s} after 43, the value 44.4 on the second line and a second $\alpha$ before the abbreviation for declination. The journal prints a double prime, gives 44,3 and omits that second $\alpha$. It also gives the letter a longer heading and ends with Gauss’s signature. The translation follows the collected text.
