\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the April 1805 Pallas Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~262--263. The corresponding passages of article XXXIII, \textit{Fortgesetzte Nachrichten über den neuen Haupt-Planeten Pallas}, appeared in the April 1805 issue of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XI, on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538603?page=388}{p.~376}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538603?page=389}{p.~377}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538603?page=393}{p.~381} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538603?page=395}{p.~383}.
\par\smallskip\noindent Von Zach reports on Carl Friedrich Gauss’s eighth set of elements, names the observers and describes Karl Ludwig Harding’s work on a star chart. The journal prints the ephemeris on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538603?page=389}{p.~377} through \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538603?page=392}{p.~380}. The collected edition describes it in brackets, referring to the \hyperlink{native-267}{Ceres entry on p.~262}.
\par\smallskip\noindent Translation and typesetting produced by AI. Journal scans from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
