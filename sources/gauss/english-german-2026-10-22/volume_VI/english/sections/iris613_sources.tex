\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss’s Meridian Observations of Iris (September 1847)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpt on p.~474 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The collected text draws on a letter by Carl Friedrich Gauss to Heinrich Christian Schumacher dated 5 September 1847 (\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00105/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~193--194}), a report of later observations (\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00107/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~197--198}) and a further observation in the miscellaneous notices (\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00112/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~207--208}). All three appear in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXVI, no.~613, issued 14 October 1847 as dated on col.~208.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal prints separate sets of elements by Gould and Goldschmidt in the letter. The collected excerpt marks that passage with four dashes. Its numerical notation, five observing rows and qualifications about visibility are followed here. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
