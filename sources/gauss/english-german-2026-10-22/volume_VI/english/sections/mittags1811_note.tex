\begin{center}{\textbf{Sources for the Table for the Correction to Noon (1811)}}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss's German text in the \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~166--171. It includes the March 1811 letter, the worked example and the three pages of the table in the collected edition.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538612?page=411}{first printing} appeared as article XXXIV, \textit{Auszug aus einem Schreiben des Herrn Professor Gauß}, in the April 1811 issue of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde}, edited by Franz Xaver von Zach, vol.~23, pp.~401--409. The \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538612?page=414}{table on pp.~404--409} credits Christian Ludwig Gerling with the calculations. The first printing dates the example 2 March. The collected edition gives 20 March.
\par\smallskip\noindent The tables by von Zach mentioned in the letter appear in the \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538612?page=65}{January issue of the same volume}, after p.~56.
