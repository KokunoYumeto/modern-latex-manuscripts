\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Gerrit Moll on the transit of Mercury\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), p.~648. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~169, 21 October 1833, p.~1688. C. G. Sulpke published Moll’s observations in Amsterdam in 1833.
