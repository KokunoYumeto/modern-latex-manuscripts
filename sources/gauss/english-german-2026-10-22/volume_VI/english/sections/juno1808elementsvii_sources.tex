\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the January 1808 Juno Notice}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the collected notice \textit{Juno. Elemente VII.} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~292--293. The original notice was printed under the heading \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue 14, 23 January 1808, \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00000131/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{p.~129} and \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00000132/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{p.~130}. The Göttingen State and University Library copy has the name Gauß in the margin on p.~129. The notice refers to Gauss in the third person.
\par\medskip
The journal prints the VII. elements in the notice. The collected edition gives a bracketed reference to the separate letter to Bode of 24 January 1808 on p.~294 instead. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
