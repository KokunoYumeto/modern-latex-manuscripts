\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Juno (August 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~330--331. The original appeared as item XXI, \textit{Fortgesetzte Nachrichten über den neuen Haupt-Planeten Juno}, in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXIV, August 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00196/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{186}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00197/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{187} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00198/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{188}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The original gives vol.~XVIII, p.~270 for the September 1808 report. A footnote on p.~187 refers the opposition result to issue 92 of the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}. Wachter’s ephemeris for 1812 begins on p.~188. The collected edition gives the dates in brackets. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
