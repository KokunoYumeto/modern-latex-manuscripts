\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the letter of 23 February 1810}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter to Bernhard August von Lindenau in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~316--317. The letter appeared in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXI, March 1810, item XXVI, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00284/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{276}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00285/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{277}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00286/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{278}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00287/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{279}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00288/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{280}. The digitized journal copy is from the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal also prints a first-person account of the Pallas results on pp.~276--278 and two editorial footnotes on p.~280, the second signed v.~L. The collected edition gives the Pallas report separately and omits those footnotes. The time recorded for the third satellite’s emersion is 9\,43\,39.3 in the collected edition and 9\,43\,38.3 on p.~279 of the journal.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss refers to the method of least squares in Book II, Section III of his \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/doi/10.3931/e-rara-522?lang=en}{\textit{Theoria motus}} (Hamburg, 1809, ETH Library copy Rar 4203). See also Barnaba Oriani’s \textit{Druckfehler}, item XXVII in the same journal issue, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00289/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{281}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00290/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{282}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538611_00291/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{283}.
