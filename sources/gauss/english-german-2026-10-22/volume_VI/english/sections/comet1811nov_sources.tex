\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (November 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter as printed in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~341--342. The collected text gives Göttingen, 15 November, as its dateline. The letter appears in two excerpts in item XLIX, \textit{Über den großen Cometen von 1811}, in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXIV, November 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00521/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{507}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00531/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{517}. The first excerpt begins on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00526/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{512} and ends on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00527/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{513}. The second starts on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00529/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{515} and continues through p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00530/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{516} to the first paragraph on p.~517. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal dates the first excerpt 15 November in a footnote and gives both 10 and 15 November in the footnote to the second. Its note citing Burckhardt on the comet of 1301 is part of the journal’s editorial material. The journal uses a singular “observation” on p.~516 where the collected edition has the plural, and it gives 119 7 32 for the right ascension on 1 April where the collected edition gives 119 7 38. The translation follows the collected reading. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
