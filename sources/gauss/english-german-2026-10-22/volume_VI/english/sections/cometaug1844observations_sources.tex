\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Observations and Elements of the Comet (1 August 1844)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report on pp.~466--467 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The unsigned item in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXII, no.~511, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049447_00064/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~111--112}, issued on 1 August 1844, reports observations by Carl Friedrich Gauss. The Bavarian State Library catalogue dates the bound volume to 1845.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal also gives observations by Rümker and elements calculated by Rümker and Funk. The collected extract ends with Gauss’s comparison counts. The table follows the collected time symbols, decimal point and two comet signs. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
