\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Gerling review}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~542--543. It first appeared under Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~190, 28 November 1812, pp.~1889--1891.
\par\smallskip\noindent In the first printing, the perpendicular line runs from the center of the Earth to the center of the occulted or eclipsed body. The collected text names only the center of the occulted or eclipsed body. The review gives 46 pages and I.~C.~Baier. The \href{https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BD21343055}{catalogue entry for the Hokkaido University copy} gives 48 pages, one unnumbered folded plate and J.~C.~Baier.
