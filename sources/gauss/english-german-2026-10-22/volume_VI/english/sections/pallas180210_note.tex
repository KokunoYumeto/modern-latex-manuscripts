\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the October 1802 Pallas Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report on Pallas as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~226--227. Von Zach describes Gauss’s fourth correction of the orbital elements and the resulting ephemeris. The excerpt includes six brightness values and the comparison of two predicted positions.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00400/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{October 1802 first printing} is article XXXIX of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~VI, pp.~390--396. The passage excerpted in the \textit{Werke} begins with \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00403/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Gauss’s fourth correction on p.~393} and ends with the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00406/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{comparison and opposition paragraph on p.~396}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal prints the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00404/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{seven entries for elements IV on p.~394} and the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00405/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{ephemeris on p.~395}. The collected edition gives a bracketed reference to the September letter to Bode and a bracketed description of the ephemeris. The journal table gives \(769{,}''726\) for the daily motion and ends the aphelion entry with 41. The cited letter gives \(769''7263\) and ends the aphelion entry with \(42''\). The collected comparison adds two columns for elements V from the manuscript and a final statement of the opposition according to V.
