\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the July 1803 Pallas Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report on Pallas as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~245. The excerpt corresponds to the final paragraph and table of article VII in the July issue of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~VIII (1803), \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538600_00096/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~90--93}, on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538600_00099/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~93}. The table in the \textit{Werke} gives the Bremen mean times and labels the differences R—B. The observations are by Heinrich Wilhelm Olbers and the comparison with elements VI is by Carl Friedrich Gauss.
