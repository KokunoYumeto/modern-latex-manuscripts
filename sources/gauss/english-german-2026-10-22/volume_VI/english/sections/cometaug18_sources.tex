\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Schumacher (18 August 1844)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the letter on pp.~467--468 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on 18 August 1844. The first printing appeared in the supplement to \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXII, no.~514, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049447_00091/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~165--166}. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049447_00092/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{colophon on col.~168} dates the supplement to 5 September 1844. The collected citation gives the letter date.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal identifies Gauss as director of the Göttingen Observatory and prints his signature. The collected letter gives the address to Schumacher and ends after the single-comparison qualification. Its table uses raised letters for the time units, decimal points and three dashes for each omitted angular entry. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
