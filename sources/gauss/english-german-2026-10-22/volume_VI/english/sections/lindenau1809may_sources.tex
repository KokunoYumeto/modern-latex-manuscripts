\begin{center}\textbf{Source for the 20 May 1809 Letter to von Lindenau}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter to Bernhard August von Lindenau in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~311--312. It first appeared as item XLV, \textit{Auszug aus einem Schreiben des Herrn Prof. Gauß}, in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XIX, May 1809, pp.~\href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_caMAAAAAMAAJ/page/n515.jpg}{501}, \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_caMAAAAAMAAJ/page/n516.jpg}{502} and \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_caMAAAAAMAAJ/page/n517.jpg}{503}. The digitized copy is from the New York Public Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition adds the two bracketed passages, one on von Lindenau’s publication of the Ceres ephemeris and one on the February notice of Gauss’s pole-elevation programme. The quotation on the semicircle uses $\cos h$ in both printings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
