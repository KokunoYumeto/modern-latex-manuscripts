\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Moon observations (April 1822)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the second notice headed \textit{Mondsbeobachtungen} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~441. H. C. Schumacher first published it under the same heading in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~I, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049426_00103/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{no.~12}, April 1822, cols.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049426_00109/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{189--190}, signed S. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049426_00005/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{title page} of the Bavarian State Library copy of the bound volume gives Altona 1823 and names the Hammerich and Heineking printing house. Schumacher reports Gauss’s Göttingen measurements and adds observations made by Captain von Caroc in Copenhagen.
\par\smallskip\noindent For 309 Mayer on 3 March, the collected table gives $-13\quad3.60$ and the first print gives $-2\quad46{,}94$. For 371 Mayer on 4 March, the collected table gives $-2\quad24\;57$ without a decimal mark and the first print gives $-2\quad24{,}57$. The collected table leaves the four Moon differences empty where the first print uses dashes, and uses superscript m and s where the first print uses primes and double primes. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
