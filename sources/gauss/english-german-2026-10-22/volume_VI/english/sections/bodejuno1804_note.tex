\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss’s 1804 Letters to Bode on Juno}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpts from Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letters to Johann Elert Bode of 30 September and 12 November 1804 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~248--250. Bode printed the excerpts under the title \textit{Beobachtung der Juno und Elemente ihrer Bahn, von Hr. Doct. Gauß in Braunschweig} in the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1808}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538337?page=197}{p.~187}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538337?page=198}{p.~188}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538337?page=199}{p.~189} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538337?page=200}{p.~190}. The \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538337?page=7}{title page} gives Berlin, 1805, for the yearbook for 1808. Its editor was Johann Elert Bode.
