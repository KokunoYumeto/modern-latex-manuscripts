\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss’s Letter to Bode of 14 March 1806}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter to Johann Elert Bode in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~267--269. The letter first appeared in Bode’s \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1809} (Berlin, 1806), \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538338?page=147}{p.~137}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538338?page=148}{p.~138}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538338?page=149}{p.~139} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538338?page=150}{p.~140}. The collected edition also refers to the Ceres observations in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XIII, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=197}{p.~189}, and Gauss’s remarks on the comet of 1772 reported by Franz Xaver von Zach in article~XXIII, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=320}{pp.~310--313}, especially \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=321}{pp.~311}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538605?page=322}{312}. Translation and typesetting produced by AI. The historical journal and yearbook scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
