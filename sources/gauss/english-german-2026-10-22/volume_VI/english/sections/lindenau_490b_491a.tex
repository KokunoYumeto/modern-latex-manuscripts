On \textsc{La Place}’s expression for measuring heights with a barometer. From a letter by an \emph{earlier} reviewer [von \textsc{Lindenau}.]
\end{center}

\begin{flushright}
--- --- --- 18 May 1806.
\end{flushright}

--- --- Speaking of inconsistencies, I am reminded of \textsc{Rohde}’s latest paper reviewed in the M.\,C. N.\,N. and I differed in our judgments of it. N.\,N. declared the whole method wrong even in theory, though I did not. He [\textsc{Gauss}] now writes to me about this as follows:

‘I must return once more to a doubt you expressed in your letter before last. You find my reasons for declaring \textsc{Rohde}’s method wrong even in theory convincing, but you say that the agreement of \textsc{Rohde}’s expression with a known one made you hesitate to declare the method wrong without qualification.
\SourceLeaf{491}
It seems to me that this is due to a slight misunderstanding. What I declared wrong was not the formula \textsc{Rohde} used, but his attempt to use a formula that is indeed correct to determine an element. which cannot be determined by that formula.’

On this I now agree with N. N. as well. I would not have mentioned the matter if Captain \textsc{Rohde} had not given me another occasion to do so, etc.
