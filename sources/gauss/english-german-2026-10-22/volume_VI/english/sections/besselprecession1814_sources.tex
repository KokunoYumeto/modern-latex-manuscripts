\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the report on Bessel’s work on precession\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s report in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), p.~561. It first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~19, 31 January 1814, pp.~188--189. The Göttingen State and University Library digitized the journal.

The coefficient in the second row of the collected table starts with a character shaped like a c. The first printing starts it with a zero and uses a decimal comma.

In the preface to \emph{Untersuchung der Größe und des Einflusses des Vorrückens der Nachtgleichen} (Berlin, Realschulbuchhandlung, 1815), Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel explains that he revised the prize memoir after a new edition of Piazzi’s catalogue appeared. Page~25 distinguishes the 4585 comparisons with the older catalogue from the 4707 with the newer one. Page~26 gives the revised results.
