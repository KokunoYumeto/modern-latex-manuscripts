\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Lindenau’s 1810 solar-diameter report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated report follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\emph{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~525–526. It first appeared unsigned under Göttingen in the \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~95, 16 June 1810, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1810:00000969/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~937}–\href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1810:00000972/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{940}. Bernhard von Lindenau communicated his new results to Gauss in a letter of 23 May. The report refers back to the \hyperlink{native-524-lower}{report of 17 July 1809} and to the \hyperlink{native-518}{formula cited in the collected text}. The \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1810:00000970/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{first printing of the 1810 report} also identifies 0,9631 as a misprint for 0,09631.
