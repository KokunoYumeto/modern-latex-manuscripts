\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Graham’s planet, 22 May 1848}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpt on p.~477 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on 22 May 1848 about his first two observations of Graham’s planet.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing is in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXVII, no.~639, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049452_00126/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~235--236}. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049452_00128/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{colophon on col.~240} gives 10 June 1848. The raised mark after the month and the time notation in the table follow the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
