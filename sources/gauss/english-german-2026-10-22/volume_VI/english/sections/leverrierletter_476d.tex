\begin{flushright}Göttingen, 22 October 1846.\end{flushright}

Last night I observed \textsc{Le Verrier}’s planet with the meridian circle, and I hasten to send you the result:

\begin{center}
1846 Oct. 21.\quad $7^{\rm u}\ 52^{\rm m}\ 13^{\rm s}7$\par
R.A. $=327^\circ\ 54'\ 7''8$\qquad Decl. $-13^\circ\ 32'\ 50''1$
\end{center}

All the observations still agreed very well with a circular orbit.
