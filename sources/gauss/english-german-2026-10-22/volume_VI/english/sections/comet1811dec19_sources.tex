\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (19 December 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~342--343. The first printing appeared under the heading Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~201 of 19 December 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00977/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{2001}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00978/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{2002}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00979/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{2003} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00980/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{2004}. The date at the end of the report is 12 December. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent On p.~2002 the journal gives 35 seconds in the time of perihelion passage, while the collected text gives 55. The journal prints Nicolai’s sixteen-row ephemeris for January 1812 on p.~2003, where the collected text has three dashes. The two points in the right ascensions for 17 and 20 November in the Marseille table follow the collected printing. They have no assigned numerical meaning here.
