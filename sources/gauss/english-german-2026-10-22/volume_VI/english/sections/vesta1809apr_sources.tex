\begin{center}\textbf{Source for Vesta (8 April 1809)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the 8 April 1809 report in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~309--311. The first printing appeared under \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingischen gelehrten Anzeigen}, issue 55, pp.~\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538675?page=539}{537}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538675?page=540}{538}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538675?page=541}{539} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538675?page=542}{540}.
\par\medskip
The collected report gives the Paris and Göttingen observations, Vesta’s first observed opposition, its fourth set of elements, and relative brightness estimates. The 1811 epoch row retains the raised final dot in its leader. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
