\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of von Zach’s tables}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s review on pp.~489--492 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The review appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Gotha} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~2, 2 January 1808, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00024/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{18}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00030/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{24}. The copy linked here is from the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The Latin title means \emph{Special tables of aberration and nutation in right ascension and declination for calculating the positions of fixed stars, whether apparent or true, together with a new catalogue of 494 of the more prominent zodiacal stars, compiled at the Ernestine Observatory for the beginning of the year 1800, and other tables relating to the subject}, by Franz Xaver Freiherr von Zach. The review describes the tables and catalogues without reproducing them. The Latin phrase \emph{monumentum aere perennius} appears in the first paragraph. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
