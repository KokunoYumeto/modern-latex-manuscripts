\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Ceres Perturbation Tables}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter to Franz Xaver von Zach dated 26 December 1802, the four numbered remarks and the five tables in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~235--243. The text explains how to combine the perturbations of longitude, radius vector and latitude due to Jupiter. The tables and the letter retain their respective numbering.
\par\smallskip\noindent The letter appeared in March 1803 as article XXXIII of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, beginning on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00275/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~259}. Gauss introduces the method on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00276/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~260}, gives the formulas on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00277/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~261} and concludes the letter on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00278/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~262}. The journal prints the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00279/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{table title and four remarks on p.~263} and begins \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00280/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Table I on p.~264}.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss refers to his \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00512/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{earlier letter, vol.~VI, p.~498}, and to the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00509/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{longitude} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00510/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{latitude} formulas in the November 1802 issue.
