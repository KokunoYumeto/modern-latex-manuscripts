\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Connaissance des tems for 1820\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~609--611. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, nos.~40 and 41, 11 March 1819, pp.~402--408. Gauss identifies the volume reviewed as the yearbook for 1820, issued in Paris by the widow Courcier in 1818.
