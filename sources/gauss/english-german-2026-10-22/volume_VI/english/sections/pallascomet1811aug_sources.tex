\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas. Comet. (17 August 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~327--330. It appeared under the heading Göttingen in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue 130, 17 August 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00265/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1289}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00266/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1290}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00267/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1291}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00268/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1292} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00269/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1293}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent For the 1811 mean-longitude difference, p.~329 of the collected edition gives $-2.2$ and p.~1291 of the first printing gives $-3{,}2$. For the missing declination in the Berlin observation of 18 February the collected edition has three dashes and the first printing has three points. The collected edition spells the word for the comet’s parabolic elements \textit{parobolischen}. The first printing has \textit{parabolischen}. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
