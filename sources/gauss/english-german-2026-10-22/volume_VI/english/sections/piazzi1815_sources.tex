\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Piazzi’s star catalogue\end{center}

The English translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~590--591. It first appeared in the \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~91, 10 June 1815, pp.~903--904.

Giuseppe Piazzi’s catalogue appeared in Palermo in 1814 at the Royal Military Press. The ETH Library copy, Rar~16115, is available through e-rara. The preface prints a plus-minus sign in the right-ascension precession formula, where Gauss’s review prints a plus. The appendix gives Joseph Pilati’s reductions of Bradley’s observations for 1756. The translation retains the review’s citation and figures.
