\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Soldner and Stefenelli\end{center}

The English translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss's review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~587--590. The first printing appeared in the \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~46, 23 March 1815, pp.~449--456. The collected edition prints \emph{Stnndenwinkel} and \emph{Reichenbach'schn} where the first printing has the usual forms.

The academy printing of J. Soldner's \emph{Neue Methode beobachtete Azimuthe zu reduciren} appears in the \emph{Denkschriften der Königlichen Akademie der Wissenschaften zu München für das Jahr 1813} (Munich, 1814), pp.~365--378. The cited formulas appear on pp.~373 and 376 of the academy printing. The copy digitized by the Natural History Museum Library, London, is available at the Internet Archive. The separately printed version is the one described by Gauss. The catalogue entry for the other pamphlet, \emph{Beyträge zur Berechnung beobachteter Azimuthe}, gives the author as Anton von Steffenelli. Gauss's text spells the name Stefenelli.
