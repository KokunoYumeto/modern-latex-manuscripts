\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (10 February 1821)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Comet} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~435. The \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1821:00000217/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} appeared under \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~23, 10 February 1821, p.~217, in the Göttingen State and University Library copy. It credits Nicolet with the discovery and Gauss with finding and observing the comet at Göttingen.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing uses prime marks for the minutes and seconds of the observation time. The collected edition uses raised letters. Both editions omit the unit marks in the declination row. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
