\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (14 January 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~322--324. The report appeared under the heading Göttingen in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issues 8 and 9, 14 January 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538679_00109/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{73}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538679_00110/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{74}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538679_00111/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{75}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538679_00112/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{76} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538679_00113/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{77}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The correction table in the collected edition has a mark with a vertical stem and a right arm before the declination value 13 on 27 December. The first printing has a full plus sign. The collected mark is reproduced as an editable contour. The estimated southern declination in the Seeberg observation is $20^\circ\;58'$ in the collected edition and $21^\circ\;58'$ on p.~76 of the journal. The October ephemeris is in \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_YCI4AAAAMAAJ/page/n403.jpg}{\textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, October 1810, pp.~400--403}.
