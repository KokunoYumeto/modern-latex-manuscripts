\begin{center}\textbf{Source for von Lindenau’s letter of 17 February 1806}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpt on p.~486 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), where the letter is identified as coming from Gauss’s papers. Bernhard von Lindenau wrote to Carl Friedrich Gauss from Altenburg on 17 February 1806.
\par\smallskip\noindent Lindenau discusses Franz Xaver von Zach’s publication of Gauss’s letter and explains his own reservation about Rohde’s method. The abbreviation Obh.\ is retained as in the collected text. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
