\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of the Connoissance des tems}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s review on pp.~492--496 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The review appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Paris} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, nos.~56 and~57, 7 April 1808, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00559/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{553}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00568/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{562}. The linked copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The French title means \emph{Knowledge of the times, or of celestial motions, for the use of astronomers and navigators}, published by the Bureau of Longitudes. The collected text dates the eclipse mentioned in the passage on Lalande to 1 February 1804. The first printing gives 11 February 1804 on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538673_00561/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{p.~555}. The translation retains the collected reading.
