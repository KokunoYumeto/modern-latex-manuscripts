\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Borda’s Circle}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \textit{Borda’s Kreis} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~404--409. It first appeared as item XXIV, \textit{Ueber einige Berichtigungen an Bordaischen Wiederholungskreisen}, in Bernhard von Lindenau and Johann Gottlieb Friedrich Bohnenberger’s \textit{Zeitschrift für Astronomie und verwandte Wissenschaften}, vol.~V, March and April 1818, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061007_00208/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{198}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061007_00221/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{211}. The first-print links lead to the Bavarian State Library copy. The collected heading for the journal citation spells Lindenau as \textit{Liadenau}.
\par\smallskip\noindent On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061007_00210/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{200} the first printing defines $M$ and $N$ using the second axis as the common axis. The collected text uses the third. On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061007_00214/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{204} the first printing gives $\cos I=\sin a\sin b$ and one expression for the tangent of $\eta$ involving $\sin a\sin b$. The collected edition instead uses the product of the tangents of $b$ and $c$ with a minus sign in the cosine formula and gives two radical expressions for the tangent. On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061007_00217/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{207} the first printing names $a$ and $b$ in the discussion of the horizontal position. The collected edition names $c$ and $b$. The translation follows the collected text and retains its unbarred radicals and distinct forms for $\upsilon$ and $V$. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
