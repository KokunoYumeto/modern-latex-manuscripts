\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the stellar occultations (8 March 1818)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s occultation observations sent to Johann Elert Bode, as printed in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~402. The \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1821} (Berlin, 1818), p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538350_00238/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{232}, credits Gauss in Göttingen. The link leads to the Bavarian State Library copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The yearbook uses a raised h, a prime and a double prime in the January entry and a comma before its final zero. The collected edition uses the raised u, m and s and places the zero directly after the s. The paired observations by Gauss and Harding and the Virgo and Taurus signs are retained. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
