\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (4 November 1813)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report headed Pallas in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~369--370. It first appeared under the heading Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~176 (4 November 1813), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538684_00729/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1753}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538684_00730/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1754}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538684_00731/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1755}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The period after “opposition” in the opening paragraph follows the collected text, as do the references in square brackets. The first printing abbreviates repeated observer names in the comparison table and uses decimal commas. Both editions leave the first observed declination unsigned, whereas the separate letter of 31 August gives it with a plus sign. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
