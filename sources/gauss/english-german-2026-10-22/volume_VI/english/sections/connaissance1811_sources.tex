\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of the Connaissance des tems for 1811}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~527--528. It first appeared unsigned under Paris in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~153, 24 September 1810, pp.~1526--1528. The almanac for 1811 was issued by the Bureau des longitudes.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss’s \hyperlink{native-44}{programme on determining the elevation of the pole} and the \hyperlink{native-73}{1808 notice} cited in this review appear earlier in this volume. The page reference in the body follows the collected text.
