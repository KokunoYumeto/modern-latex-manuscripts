\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (31 January 1814)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report headed Comet in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~373--375. Its source is the Göttingen report in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~19 (31 January 1814), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538685_00191/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{185}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538685_00192/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{186}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538685_00193/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{187}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538685_00194/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{188}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538685_00195/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{189}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy. The comet account ends on p.~188. The same issue continues with a report on precession and the separate Juno addendum.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the observer’s name as Wisniwsky and prints the right ascension for comparison star IV from Bessel’s two observations as 322 44 56.1. The first printing gives Wisniewsky and 322 44 36,1. The two colons in the numerical tables follow the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
