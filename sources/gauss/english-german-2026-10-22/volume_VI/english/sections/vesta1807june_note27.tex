\expandafter\def\csname HistoricalNoteText27\endcsname{This work on determining planetary orbits will now appear shortly. Dr. \textsc{Gauss} writes to us that the \textsc{Perthes} bookshop in Hamburg has undertaken to publish it. The manuscript is quite finished. At the publisher’s request, however, Dr. \textsc{Gauss} is now publishing it in Latin so that the work can also be read by foreigners who do not know German. This will cause some further delay, but printing will be able to start in a couple of months.}
