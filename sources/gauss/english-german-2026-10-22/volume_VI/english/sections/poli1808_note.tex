\noindent This AI translation follows the Latin text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~37--49. It includes the formal title, all seven articles, Gauss’s note on azimuths, and both lecture announcements. The formal title prints \mbox{\textsc{elevationem}}, and the repeated heading prints \mbox{\textsc{elpvationem}}.
