\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Instruments (10 May 1813)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~365--367. It first appeared under the heading Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~75 (10 May 1813), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538683_00757/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{745}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538683_00758/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{746}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538683_00759/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{747}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538683_00760/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{748}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538683_00761/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{749}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538683_00762/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{750}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538683_00763/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{751}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538683_00764/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{752}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing includes the price list and subsequent institute notice on pp.~750--752. The collected edition ends with a bracketed editorial note about the list and spells the name Frauenhofer in that note. The two editions give three quarters of a second for the range of the declination determinations. The point in the sentence about measuring angles with the theodolite follows the collected text. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
