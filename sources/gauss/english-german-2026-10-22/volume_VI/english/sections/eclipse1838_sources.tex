\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Solar Eclipse Observed at Göttingen (15 March 1838)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the text on p.~464 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Heinrich Christian Schumacher reports Gauss’s observation of 15 March 1838. The journal published the report on 30 May 1839 among the \textit{Vermischte Nachrichten}. \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XVI, no.~378, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049441_00162/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{col.~303}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the observation date on the time line and signs the notice Schumacher. The journal names the date in the preceding report from Rostock and adds that no instant of the eclipse could be observed at Altona. The translation follows the collected text.
