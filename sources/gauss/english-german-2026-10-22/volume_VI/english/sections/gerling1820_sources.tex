\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the notice on Gerling’s eclipse calculations\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s notice in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~622--625. It first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~103, 26 June 1820, pp.~1025--1030. The notice reports Christian Ludwig Gerling’s calculations for the solar eclipse of 7 September 1820, communicated in his letter to Gauss of 23 May 1820.
