\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (10 September 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter and supplement in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~333--335. They appeared under the heading \textit{Beobachtungen und Elemente der Bahn des Kometen von 1811 und Zusatz zu seiner Theoria motus corp. coelest.} in the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1814}, edited by Johann Elert Bode (Berlin, 1811). The item begins on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538343_00262/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{254}, continues on pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538343_00263/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{255} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538343_00264/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{256}, and ends on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538343_00265/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{257}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The yearbook gives nine observations by von Zach and a forecast of 3 months, where the collected edition gives new observations and 4 months. The yearbook has an editorial note on the timing of the occultation and gives $z$ in place of $x$ on p.~257. The collected text gives $-z$ in place of $x$. A small mark to the upper left of the numerator 2 in the formula for $\xi$ appears in the collected edition and is reproduced as editable contours. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
