\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the November 1802 Ceres Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s November 1802 report on Ceres as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~227--230. Von Zach reports Gauss’s calculations and quotes his closing remarks. The collected editors insert part of an earlier report on Barnaba Oriani’s calculations in brackets.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00506/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{November first printing} is article LI of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~VI, pp.~492--498. The collected text draws on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00509/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~495}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00512/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{498} of the November article and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00073/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~67}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00074/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{68} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00076/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{70} of the July article. The November equations for the radius vector and latitude are on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00510/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~496}.
\par\smallskip\noindent A footnote on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00509/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~495} corrects a scribal error in the October issue (p.~387), from an increase to a decrease in eccentricity. The November journal prints \(-230''00\) as the first longitude coefficient and \(0''38\) without a sign for the annual decrease in inclination. The collected edition gives \(-231''00\) and \(+0''38\). The translation follows the collected readings.
