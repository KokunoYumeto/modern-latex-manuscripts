\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Two 1808 Letters to Bode}\end{center}
\noindent These AI translations follow Carl Friedrich Gauss’s two letters to Johann Elert Bode, dated 24 January and 23 July 1808, in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~293--296. Bode printed the letters together in the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1811} (Berlin, 1808), \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538340?page=147}{pp.~135}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538340?page=151}{139}. The Bavarian State Library copy shows the January letter on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538340?page=147}{p.~135}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538340?page=148}{p.~136} and the upper part of \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538340?page=149}{p.~137}, and the July letter at the foot of \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538340?page=149}{p.~137}, on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538340?page=150}{p.~138} and on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538340?page=151}{p.~139}.
\par\medskip
The collected edition begins the right ascension in the first row for 4 January with 325. The yearbook gives 525. In the 24 July satellite observation credited to Thiarks the collected edition gives $3\tfrac14$ f. for the Dollond instrument and the yearbook gives $3\tfrac12$ f. Both editions give 24 July within the letter dated 23 July. The collected edition describes the January ephemeris in brackets and leaves out two yearbook notes signed B. The translations follow the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
