\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of von Zach's tables}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~546--548. It first appeared under Marseille in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~196, 7 December 1812, pp.~1956--1960.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected text says \textit{Sinus-Logarithmen} (logarithms of sines). The first printing has \textit{Stern-Logarithmen}. For the first logarithm in the comparison for \(\alpha\) Tauri, the collected text has a space between 0 and 5806. The first printing has \(0{,}5806\).
\par\smallskip\noindent Franz Xaver von Zach, \textit{Nouvelles tables d'aberration et de nutation} (Marseille, Imprimerie de Mme. Mine et Comp., 1812), \href{https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_-oek6b4alfwC}{National Library of Naples copy}. The introduction defines \(A\) on \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_-oek6b4alfwC/bub_gb_-oek6b4alfwC.pdf#page=11}{p.~7}. The entries for \(\alpha\) Tauri are on \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_-oek6b4alfwC/bub_gb_-oek6b4alfwC.pdf#page=105}{p.~45} and \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_-oek6b4alfwC/bub_gb_-oek6b4alfwC.pdf#page=158}{p.~98}, where the second table lists the star as Aldébaran.
