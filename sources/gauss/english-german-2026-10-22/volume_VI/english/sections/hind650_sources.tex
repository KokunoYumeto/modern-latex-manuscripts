\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Hind’s star, 19 August 1848}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpt on p.~478 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on 19 August 1848 about his 30 July observation of Hind’s star and the mean position determined by Goldschmidt.
\par\smallskip\noindent The letter appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXVIII, no.~650, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049453_00016/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~23--24}, issued 2 November 1848 as dated in the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049453_00020/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{colophon on col.~32}. The journal gives the comparison that the collected edition marks with three dashes. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
