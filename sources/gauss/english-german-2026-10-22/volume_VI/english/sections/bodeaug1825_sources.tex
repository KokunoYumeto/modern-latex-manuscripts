\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (5 August 1825)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter of 5 August 1825 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~451. Johann Elert Bode’s \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1828} (Berlin, 1825), \href{https://books.google.nl/books?id=GDAPAAAAQAAJ&pg=PA171&redir_esc=y}{p.~171} and \href{https://books.google.nl/books?id=GDAPAAAAQAAJ&pg=PA172&redir_esc=y}{p.~172}, prints the submission under the heading \textit{Meridian-Beobachtungen der Pallas und Ceres im Jahr 1825 vom Herrn Hofrath Ritter Gauss in Göttingen}. The links lead to the copy digitized by Google Books.
\par\smallskip\noindent The Yearbook gives 5 August as the date of submission. The collected edition places the date on the Göttingen letter line. Both have fourteen Pallas observations and seven Ceres observations. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
