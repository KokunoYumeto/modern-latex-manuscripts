\begin{center}\textbf{Source for the 30 August 1808 Letter to von Lindenau}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the 30 August 1808 letter from Carl Friedrich Gauss to Bernhard August von Lindenau in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~307. The first printing appeared as item~XXXIV, \textit{Auszug aus einem Schreiben des Herrn Professors Gauß}, in the September issue of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XVIII, pp.~\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538609?page=291}{269}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538609?page=292}{270}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538609?page=293}{271}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538609?page=294}{272} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538609?page=295}{273}.
\par\medskip
The first printing repeats the orbital elements here. The collected edition refers to its separate Juno notice for the elements and places the passage on the altitude of the pole after the corresponding paper, on pp.~146--147. See also the partial English rendering in \href{https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-58118-7_12}{Clifford J. Cunningham, \textit{Scientific Papers} (Springer, 2017), pp.~283--284}. This translation follows the collected edition’s divisions and table readings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
