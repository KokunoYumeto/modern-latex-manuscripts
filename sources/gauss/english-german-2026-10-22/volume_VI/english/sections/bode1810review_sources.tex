\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Bode’s yearbook for 1810}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Gauss’s text on pp.~504--507 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). It appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Berlin} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~155, 26 September 1808, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00515/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{1545}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00522/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{1552}. The linked copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss reviews Johann Elert Bode’s \emph{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr} 1810, \emph{nebst einer Sammlung der neuesten in die astronomischen Wissenschaften einschlagenden Abhandlungen und Nachrichten}, printed in Berlin in 1807. The year 1810 is the year covered by the volume.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00521/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} gives 20 March for the occultation in item 13 and labels the Berlin Observatory entry 24. The collected edition shows a c-shaped character in the date and gives the observatory entry without a number. The translation follows the collected edition. The historical unit in the diameter of Vesta is \emph{Meilen}. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
