\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Connaissance des tems for 1826\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~638--639. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~80, 17 May 1824, pp.~793--796. The Bureau des longitudes issued \emph{Connaissance des tems} for 1826 in Paris in 1823 through Bachelier.
