\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Observation (7 March 1812)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~348. The report appeared under the heading Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~38, 7 March 1812, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538681_00413/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{369} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538681_00414/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{370}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first Mayer entry reads 16c in the collected text and 160 on p.~369 of the journal. For 162 Mayer this report gives 27.0 seconds for Gauss and 27.5 for Harding. Gauss’s letter of 1 March assigns these values the other way round. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
