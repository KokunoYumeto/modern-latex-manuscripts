\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Schumacher on the latitude of Mannheim\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~597--599. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~76, 12 May 1817, pp.~753--756. The Bibliothèque nationale de France records Schumacher’s treatise as a quarto of 56 pages printed in Copenhagen by J.~F. Schulz in 1816.

In the third correction formula, the first printing (p.~754) has $\frac{1}{23}$ and the collected edition has $\frac{1}{28}$. The first printing places the nine table entries in a single group (p.~755). The translation follows the collected edition’s two groups and retains the space in the \emph{8 3} entry for $\chi$ Ursa Major. The first printing ends the sentence before \emph{Uebrigens} with a full stop (p.~756). The collected edition leaves a gap there.
