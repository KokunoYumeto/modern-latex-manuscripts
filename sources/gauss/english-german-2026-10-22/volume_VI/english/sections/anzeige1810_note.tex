\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the 1810 Pallas Review}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the German text in Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~61--64. The review appeared in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~198, 13 December 1810, pp.~1969--1973. \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb10538678?page=939}{First printing at the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek}. \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Collected German text}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The review discusses the Latin paper on Pallas’s elliptic elements, presented on 25 November 1810. It has its own tables of the four sets of elements and six pairs of differences.
