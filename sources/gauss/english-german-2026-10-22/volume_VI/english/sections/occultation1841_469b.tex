On 23 May 1841 at Göttingen, Councillor \textsc{Gauss} observed the egress of $42\omega'$ Gemin. at $9^{\rm u}32^{\rm m}10^{\rm s}6$ mean time, as poor weather prevented him from observing the ingress. Dr. \textsc{Goldschmidt} observed this egress $0^{\rm s}3$ earlier.
\begin{flushright}\textsc{Schumacher.}\end{flushright}
