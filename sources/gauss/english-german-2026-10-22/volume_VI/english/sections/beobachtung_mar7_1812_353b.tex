\begin{center}\textbf{OBSERVATION.}\par\vspace{4pt}\hrule\vspace{4pt}\fontsize{10}{12}\selectfont Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen. No. 38, pp. 369\;..\;370, 7 March 1812.\par\vspace{4pt}\hrule\vspace{4pt}\end{center}

\par\indent An observation as rare as \emph{nine} stellar occultations on two evenings well deserves to be recorded in these pages.

\Needspace{27\baselineskip}\par\indent The following six occultations were observed at this observatory on 19 February of this year.

\par\indent Professor \textsc{Gauss} observed with the 10-foot \textsc{Herschel} telescope, Professor \textsc{Harding} with a 5-foot telescope, and Mr. Gerling with the 5-foot Dollond.
