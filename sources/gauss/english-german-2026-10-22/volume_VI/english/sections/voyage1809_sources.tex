\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Humboldt and Bonpland’s Voyage}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated English text follows the German review in Carl Friedrich Gauss, \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~512–515. The review first appeared unsigned under Paris and Tübingen in the \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~91, 10 June 1809, pp.~898–904. The Göttingen State and University Library provides the \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1809:00000900/full/1750,/0/default.jpg}{opening page}, the \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1809:00000903/full/1750,/0/default.jpg}{coordinate table}, and the \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1809:00000906/full/1750,/0/default.jpg}{closing page} of that printing. The journal repeats the units on both rows, using primes for minutes and seconds of time. The collected edition gives the units on the first row and leaves them implicit on the second. The journal reads \emph{die} and \emph{selbst} where the collected edition reads \emph{die die} and \emph{selbs}, respectively.
\par\smallskip\noindent The book title identifies the fourth part of the \emph{Voyage of Humboldt and Bonpland}, \emph{Astronomy and Magnetism}, first volume, a collection of astronomical observations, trigonometrical operations and barometric measurements made on a journey to the equinoctial regions of the New Continent from 1799 to 1803. The review concerns the two instalments of 1808 and 1809, available from Schöll and Cotta. Alexander von Humboldt made the observations, Aimé Bonpland accompanied him, and Jabbo Oltmanns worked out the results. The \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/view/bsb10868686?page=9}{bound first volume at the Bavarian State Library} bears a later Paris imprint, F.~Schoell, 1810. Humboldt's \emph{Essay on Refractions in the Torrid Zone} also appeared in the \href{https://annales.minesparis.psl.eu/articles/1808-2/89-114.pdf}{\emph{Journal des mines}, vol.~24 (1808), pp.~169–218}.
