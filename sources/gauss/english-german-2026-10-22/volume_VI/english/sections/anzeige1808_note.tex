\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the 1808 Review on Pole Elevation}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the German review in Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~68--69. The review appeared in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~195, 5 December 1808, pp.~1945--1947. \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00001951/full/1200,/0/default.jpg}{First printing}. \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Collected German text}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The review concerns Gauss’s \textit{Methodus peculiaris elevationem poli determinandi}, the lecture programme reproduced earlier in this volume. See also Siegfried Krauter, \textit{Astronomie} (January 2012), pp.~24--26, for modern terminology concerning sidereal time and pole elevation.
