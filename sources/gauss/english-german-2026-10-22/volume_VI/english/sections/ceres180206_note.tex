\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the June 1802 Ceres Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s report on Ceres as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~224. Von Zach reports on Gauss’s comparison with the observations and his calculation of the opposition.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00592/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{June 1802 first printing} is article LIX of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~V, pp.~576--590. The collected passage runs from the paragraph introducing Gauss’s comparison and the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00598/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{twelve-row table on p.~582} through the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00599/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{opposition paragraph on p.~583}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected table includes a Seeberg mean-time column and labels the differences R—B. On 1 and 2 April its calculated declinations read \(18\ 3\ 20.6\) and \(18\ 5\ 6.2\), where the journal gives \(18\ 3\ 20{,}0\) and \(18\ 5\ 0{,}2\). On 5 April its calculated right ascension reads \(179\ 40\ 15.6\), where the journal gives \(179\ 40\ 15{,}0\). The translation follows the collected edition.
