\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Pasquich's Epitome}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~535--536. The review first appeared under Vienna in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~144, 9 September 1811, pp.~1433--1436.
\par\smallskip\noindent Pasquich's \textit{Epitome elementorum astronomiae sphaerico-calculatoriae} appeared in two parts with an appendix (Vienna, 1811). The first printing of the review and Pasquich's title pages give the publisher's name as Schaumburg. The collected review gives Schaumann for the main work and Schaumburg for the appendix.
\par\smallskip\noindent In the appendix, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049414_00029/full/1250,/0/default.jpg}{table XXVIII} gives a correction for noon and midnight from corresponding solar altitudes. Gauss mentions the correction for noon in his review.
