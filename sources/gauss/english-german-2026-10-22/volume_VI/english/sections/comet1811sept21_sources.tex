\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (21 September 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~337--338. It first appeared under the heading Göttingen in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue 151, 21 September 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00473/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1497}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00474/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1498} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538680_00475/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1499}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent For 22 October, p.~338 of the collected edition gives 0 as the hour and p.~1499 of the first printing gives 9. The report gives the comet’s distances from Earth directly. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
