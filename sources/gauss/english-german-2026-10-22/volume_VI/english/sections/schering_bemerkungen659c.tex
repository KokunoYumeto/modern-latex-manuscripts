\begin{center}\textsc{Remarks.}\end{center}

This sixth volume of the works of \textsc{Gauss} contains the treatises, articles, observations and reviews that \textsc{Gauss} made public, insofar as they belong to the astronomical sciences.
As a result, the volume is already thicker than any of the other volumes of this work.
The manuscripts among his posthumous papers that concern this field, in particular a theory of planetary perturbations and the Pallas tables, could therefore hardly be included as well and must be printed separately from this volume.

Since the letters to \textsc{Zach} and \textsc{Lindenau} are no longer extant, I have had reprinted here, from the compilations of reports on the minor planets published by the editors of the Monatliche Correspondenz für Erd- und Himmelskunde, those passages that concern \textsc{Gauss} and were drawn largely from his letters, together with only such further passages as seemed necessary to understand the former.
I have reproduced the ephemerides in only a few individual examples.
In the comparisons of elements with observations, I have added the latter when they are found elsewhere in the Monatliche Correspondenz.
For reviews of writings by other authors, the name of the reviewer is confirmed by letters from \textsc{Lindenau} to \textsc{Gauss} for those taken from this journal, and by the account books of the Royal Society of Sciences for those taken from the Göttinger gelehrten Anzeigen.

Göttingen 1874 November. \hfill \textsc{Ernst Schering}.
