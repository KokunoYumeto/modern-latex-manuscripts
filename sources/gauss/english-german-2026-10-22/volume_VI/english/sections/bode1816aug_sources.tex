\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (10 August 1816)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter to Johann Elert Bode in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~392--393. The \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1819} (Berlin, 1816), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538348_00225/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{219}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538348_00227/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{221}, prints it under a heading on Pallas’s opposition for 1817, the observations and new orbital elements of Juno, and variable stars. The yearbook links lead to the Bavarian State Library copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The yearbook adds two footnotes. The one attached to the enclosed ephemerides on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538348_00225/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{219} reads \textit{Folgen nachher.} (“They follow below.”). The one attached to Harding’s ephemeris on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538348_00227/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{221} reads \textit{Folgt nachher.} (“It follows below.”). The collected letter ends after the last annual decrease in the variable-star table. Its text does not include the enclosed ephemerides.
\par\smallskip\noindent On p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538348_00226/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{220} the yearbook’s brightness table has no 1818 entry, while the collected edition gives 0.02122. The yearbook uses $27''{,}5$ in the June~12 observation and $812''{,}40187$ for the daily tropical motion. The collected text gives $27^{\mathrm s}5$ and $812''40187$. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
