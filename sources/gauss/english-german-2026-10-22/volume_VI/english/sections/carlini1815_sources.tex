\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of the Milan ephemerides for 1815\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~574--576. The first printing appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~10, 19 January 1815, pp.~89--93.

The title page of Francesco Carlini’s \emph{Effemeridi astronomiche di Milano per l’anno 1815} gives Milan, R.\,C. Stamperia di Governo, 1814. The copy from the Harvard Science Center Library, Sci 295.10, was digitized by Google and is available through Mario Carpino’s digital bibliography at the INAF Brera Observatory.

Barnaba Oriani’s paper on the latitude of Brera begins on p.~3 of the appendix. On p.~31 Oriani gives $45\quad28\quad0{,}713$ for the latitude of the tower. Gauss’s review has 49 in the corresponding latitude entry in both the first printing and the collected edition. The tables in the translation follow the collected edition, which also has the form 3c where the first printing has 30. Oriani’s paper on the 1813 opposition of Mars begins on p.~44.
