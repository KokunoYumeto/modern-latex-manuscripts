\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Bode’s Astronomical Yearbook for 1826\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~630--634. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~2, 3 January 1824, pp.~17--24. Johann Elert Bode’s yearbook for 1826 was published in Berlin in 1823, available from the author and on commission through Ferdinand Dümmler.
