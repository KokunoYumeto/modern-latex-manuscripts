\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of the Roman parallax tables\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~599--601. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~108, 7 July 1817, pp.~1073--1077. The Sapienza catalogue records the Roman astronomers’ tables as published by De Romanis in 1816.
