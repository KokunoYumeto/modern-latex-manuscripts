\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Letter of 8 May 1807}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter to Johann Elert Bode of 8 May 1807 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~283--284. Bode published it together with a second letter from Gauss dated 23 August in the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1810} (Berlin, 1807), pp.~210--214. The May letter occupies \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538339?page=220}{pp.~210}, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538339?page=221}{211} and \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538339?page=222}{212}. The scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
\par\medskip
The note signed by Bode directs the reader to the first orbital elements on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538339?page=208}{p.~198} of the Yearbook. The collected edition adds a reference to its own p.~286, where the first elements appear in the separate report from the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}. The comparison table follows the collected edition, including its mean-time column. The two editable altar signs follow their respective outlines in the collected table of apparent positions. The Göttingen digitization and the \href{https://archive.org/details/werkecarlf06gausrich}{University of California Libraries copy} both show a short trace beneath the superscript $s$ in the observation of 6 April. Its significance is uncertain. The \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538437?page=218}{yearbook printing} uses primes for minutes and seconds. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
