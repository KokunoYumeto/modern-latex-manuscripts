\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the April, May and June 1803 Pallas Reports}\end{center}
\noindent These AI translations follow two excerpts from Franz Xaver von Zach’s reports in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~244. Both reports describe Gauss’s comparison of calculated positions of Pallas with observations by Heinrich Wilhelm Olbers.
\par\smallskip\noindent For April and May, the collected edition gives a bracketed account of the April article (XLIV, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00387/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~369--375}) and a passage from the May article (LIV, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00486/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~466--469}). The April report gives the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00392/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{elements VI on p.~374} and is related to the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538335_00189/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{letter to Bode of 3 March 1803}. The May passage includes the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00488/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{comparison on p.~468} and the statement of extension of the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00597/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{December 1802 ephemeris}. The full extension appears in the journal on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00489/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~469}. The June excerpt corresponds to the comparison at the end of article LXV (\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00580/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~556--560}), on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538599_00584/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~560}. Both collected tables give Bremen mean times.
\par\smallskip\noindent A contemporary English version of the April report, \href{https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d6/The_philosophical_magazine._Volume_16%2C_1803._%28IA_s2id13415870%29.pdf#page=53}{“On the new Planet Pallas,” attributed to Baron Von Zach}, appeared in \textit{The Philosophical Magazine}, vol.~16, no.~61 (1803), pp.~49--52. The article does not identify a translator. The translations here use the German collected text.
