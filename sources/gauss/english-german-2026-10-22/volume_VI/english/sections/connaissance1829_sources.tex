\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Connaissance des tems for 1829\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~645--648. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~6, 10 January 1828, pp.~49--56. The Bureau des longitudes issued \emph{Connaissance des tems} for 1829 in Paris in 1826 through Bachelier.
