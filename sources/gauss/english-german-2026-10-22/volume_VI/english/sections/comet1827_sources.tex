\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet Report (September 1827)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Comet} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~459--460. Heinrich Christian Schumacher reports Gauss’s meridian observations and the orbit calculated by Peters. The first printing appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~VI, no.~123, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049431_00038/full/1550,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~43--44}, in September 1827. The Bavarian State Library’s bound volume is dated 1828.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the final time as 47~27.0 and prints an open-right character after the 5 in the inclination, represented here as \textup{c}. The journal prints 47~27,7 and 50 in those places. It also reads \textit{Perihels}, where the collected edition has \textit{Prihels}, and signs the notice \textit{S.} The translation follows the collected figures, ascending-node sign, table layout and expanded signature. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
