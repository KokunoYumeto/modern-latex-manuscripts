\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the letter of 14 January 1811}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter to Bernhard August von Lindenau in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~325. The first printing appeared as item VIII of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXIII, January 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538612_00105/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{97} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538612_00106/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{98}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss refers to the elements in the \href{https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_YCI4AAAAMAAJ/page/n595.jpg}{December 1810 issue, p.~592}. The daily mean tropical motion is written $768.980$ in the collected letter. The first printing uses the double prime and decimal comma. The first printing also includes the ephemeris for 3 February to 12 April 1811, represented in the collected letter by the bracketed dates. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
