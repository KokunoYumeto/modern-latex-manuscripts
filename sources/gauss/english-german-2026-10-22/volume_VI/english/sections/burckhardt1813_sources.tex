\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Burckhardt's lunar tables}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~553--554. It first appeared under Paris in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~39, 8 March 1813, pp.~388--392.
\par\smallskip\noindent Johann Carl Burckhardt's \textit{Tables de la Lune} were published by the Bureau des Longitudes de France and printed by Madame Veuve Courcier (Paris, December 1812). \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/21914185}{ETH-Bibliothek Zürich, Rar 15631}. See the \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/21914186/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{title page}, \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/21914188/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{opening of the introduction}, and \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/21914190/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{continuation of the comparison with Bürg}. The introduction gives 4182 without a double prime in the second comparison. Gauss's review gives \(4182''\). Burckhardt explains the sequence of corrections in the \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/21914193/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{instructions} and gives the syzygy formulas on \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/21914281/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{p.~87} and \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/21914282/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{p.~88}.
