\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (21 March 1820)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Gauss an Bode} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~428--429. In the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1823}, edited by Johann Elert Bode and issued in Berlin in 1820, the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538352_00166/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{first-print heading} describes observations of Saturn and Vesta in 1819 and Pallas and Mars in 1820, made with the transit telescope at Göttingen Observatory and sent in by Gauss on 21 March 1820. The report occupies pp.~160--161 in the Bavarian State Library copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the last number in the 23 January Mars entry as 8.0, where the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538352_00167/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} has 8,1. The October 1819 \hyperlink{native-427-part-lower}{instruments report} gives 36.3 for Saturn on 1 October and 58.0 for Vesta on 24 September. Both witnesses to the March letter give 36.9 and 58.1, respectively. The tables here follow the collected edition, including its arrangement of units and headings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
