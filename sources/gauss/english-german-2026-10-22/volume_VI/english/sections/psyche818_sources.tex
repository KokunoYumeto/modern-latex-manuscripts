\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the elements of Psyche}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s report on p.~479 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Gauss discusses the orbit of Psyche and gives the elements determined by Wilhelm Klinkerfues. The abbreviation Th.\ M.\ C.\ C.\ refers to Gauss’s \textit{Theoria motus corporum coelestium in sectionibus conicis solem ambientium} (Hamburg, 1809).
\par\smallskip\noindent The report appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXXV, no.~818, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049460_00013/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{col.~17}. The journal includes a comparison table and closes with Gauss’s signature and the date 8 July 1852 on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049460_00014/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{col.~20}. The collected edition leaves out the table and the signature. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049460_00020/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{colophon on col.~32} dates the issue 27 July 1852. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
