\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (10 July 1826)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter of 10 July 1826 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~454--455. Johann Elert Bode’s \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1829} (Berlin, 1826), \href{https://books.google.nl/books?id=LxwPAAAAQAAJ&pg=PA144&redir_esc=y}{p.~144} and \href{https://books.google.nl/books?id=LxwPAAAAQAAJ&pg=PA145&redir_esc=y}{p.~145}, gives the submission date and prints the letter under the heading \textit{Meridian-Beobachtungen der Pallas und Ceres im Jahr 1826}. The links lead to the copy digitized by Google Books.
\par\smallskip\noindent Each table contains eight observations. The two colons and three dashes in the Pallas table and the colon in the first Ceres row are retained from the collected edition. The reference to a star of the 10th magnitude is also present in the first printing. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
