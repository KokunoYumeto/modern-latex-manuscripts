\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the 1813 Comet Observations}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s Latin text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~25--36, including the title leaf. The paper was presented to the Royal Society of Sciences on 10 September 1813 and printed in vol.~II of the \textit{Commentationes societatis regiae scientiarum Gottingensis recentiores}. \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Göttingen digitization}.
\par\smallskip\noindent A separate \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb10538617?page=517}{German translation from the Latin} appeared in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde}, vol.~28, no.~LVIII (Gotha, December 1813), pp.~501--513. The article gives no translator’s name. The volume title credits F.~von Zach as editor. The digitized copy is held by the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
\par\smallskip\noindent Terminology was compared with Wilhelm Olbers’s \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/449756}{1797 treatise on comet orbits}, held by the ETH Library, and Andreas Kammerer and Mike Kretlow’s \href{https://fg-kometen.vdsastro.de/Publ/kometen_beobachten_2.0.pdf}{\textit{Kometen beobachten}}, March 2010 edition, pp.~252--256.
