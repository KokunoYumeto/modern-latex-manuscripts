The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~640--641. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~23, 7 February 1825, pp.~225--227. Baron de Damoiseau’s \emph{Tables de la lune} appeared in Paris in 1824 through Bachelier.
