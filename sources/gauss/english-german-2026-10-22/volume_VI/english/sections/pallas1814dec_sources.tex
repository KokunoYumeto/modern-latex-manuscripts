\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (12 December 1814)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report headed Pallas in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~379--381. It appeared under the second Göttingen heading in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~199 (12 December 1814), pp.~\href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001993/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1985}, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001994/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1986}, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001995/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1987}, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001996/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1988}, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001997/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1989}, \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1814:00001998/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1990}. The links lead to the digitized copy at the Göttingen State and University Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition has Seberg in the table heading, a space in the last declination cell for 3 November (10 6), and $-12.9$ for the mean-longitude difference in the second opposition. The first printing gives Seeberg, 10,6 and $-2{,}9$, respectively. The December report gives 50.5 seconds in the prediction of the opposition, and the separate May letter gives 50 seconds.
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{First-print footnote, p.~1988.} A printing error in no.~129, p.~1282, gave its latitude as $54^\circ\,17'\,50''3$ instead of $54^\circ\,42'\,50''3$. The note refers to the new Königsberg Observatory and belongs to the first printing. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
