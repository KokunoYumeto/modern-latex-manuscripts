\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (April 1813)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpt in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~364--365, headed Comet. It comes from item XLIV of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmelskunde}, vol.~XXVII (April 1813), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538616_00402/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{388}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538616_00403/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{389}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy. The collected excerpt begins with Gauss’s five positions and ends with Enke at work revising the elements.
\par\smallskip\noindent Both the collected text and the first printing give 71 seconds in Harding’s observation. For Gauss’s second set of elements the collected text gives May 15.93437 for the perihelion passage, and the first printing gives May 19,93437. The four comet signs follow the collected edition.
