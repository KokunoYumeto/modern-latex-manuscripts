\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the comet report (March and April 1818)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~402--403. It first appeared as item XXXIV, \textit{Auszug aus einem Briefe des Herrn Hofrath Ritter Gauß}, in Bernhard von Lindenau and Johann Gottlieb Friedrich Bohnenberger’s \textit{Zeitschrift für Astronomie und verwandte Wissenschaften}, vol.~V, March and April 1818, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061007_00286/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{276}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10061007_00287/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{277}. The links lead to the Bavarian State Library copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing gives the logarithm as 0,07937 and the comparison star’s declination as $45^\circ\,9'$. The collected edition has 0.07937 and $15^\circ\,9'$. It also prints the comet’s time offset as $14''98$, where the first printing gives $14''{,}98$. The collected edition includes the four later observations of the star marked as a manuscript addition.
