\begin{center}
VON ZACH TO GAUSS.
\end{center}

\begin{flushright}Eisenberg, 6 April 1807.\end{flushright}

My most esteemed friend, I am infinitely obliged to you for kindly sending \textsc{Güssmann}’s pamphlet, and still more for your thorough review. I will publish it in the May issue now in press, omitting your name as you wish, much as I would have liked to print it. Naturally, I must and always will respect your wishes and your peace. I am already content that you have listened to my request and find merit in my view that people as wicked and malicious as the associates of our P. \textsc{Güssmann} must be shown in their true colours, to safeguard the reputation of many a deserving man and protect him from injury and harm at the hands of people who are not properly informed. My esteemed friend, you have undertaken this good and worthy work both for the good cause and for your good friends. They and all right-minded people will be grateful to you for it forever. --- --- ---
