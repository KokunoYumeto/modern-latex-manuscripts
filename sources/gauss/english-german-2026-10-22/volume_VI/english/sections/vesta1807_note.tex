\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Vesta Notice}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the notice \emph{Vesta} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~282. The passage comes from Franz Xaver von Zach’s account of Wilhelm Olbers’s discovery of Vesta in the May 1807 \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XV, article XXXV, pp.~502--508. The exchange quoted here appears on \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0514.tif}{p.~507}. Von Zach quotes Olbers’s letter of 22 April, which in turn quotes Gauss’s reply about the name. Olbers also describes the sign Gauss chose.
\par\medskip
Gauss’s letter to Olbers of 14 April 1807 gives the naming passage in a slightly different form and discusses several designs for the sign. It is printed in Carl Schilling’s edition of their correspondence (Berlin, 1900), part~I, \href{https://archive.org/details/p1wilhelmolberss02olbeuoft/page/n351/mode/1up}{pp.~338}--\href{https://archive.org/details/p1wilhelmolberss02olbeuoft/page/n352/mode/1up}{339}. The editable sign here follows the outline printed in the collected edition. The journal scan is from the Thüringer Universitäts- und Landesbibliothek Jena. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
