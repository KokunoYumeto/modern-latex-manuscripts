\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the June 1824 Comet Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Comet} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~450. Gauss’s report first appeared under the heading \textit{Beobachtungen des Cometen von 1824 in Göttingen} in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~III, no.~59 (June 1824), \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049428_00102/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~179--180} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049428_00103/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~181--182}. The links lead to the Bavarian State Library’s copy of the volume edited by H.~C. Schumacher and published in Altona in 1825.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing gives 11 55 7,1 for the mean time on 3 February and ends with Gauss’s signature. The collected edition gives 11 35 7.1 and ends with the last observation. The translation follows the collected text and retains the blank and four points on 20 February and the detached point before the right ascension on 27 February.
