\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Occultation Notice}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the notice in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~258, on Carl Friedrich Gauss’s observation of the occultation of a star in Pisces at Brunswick on 16 October 1804. The \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~X, November 1804, gives this as the second observation in article XLI on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538602?page=495}{p.~481}.
