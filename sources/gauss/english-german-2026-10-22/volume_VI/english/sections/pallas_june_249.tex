Monatliche Correspondenz. Vol. VII, pp.\ 556—560. June 1803.


— — —

Dr.\ \textsc{Gauss} continues tirelessly to compare \textsc{Olbers}’s observations of \emph{Pallas} with his elements VI. Here is the result for the observations from 16 March to 13 April. One will be pleased to see that those elements still agree well and that the differences are scarcely greater than the uncertainties to be expected in the observations themselves, especially in the declinations, which Dr.\ \textsc{Olbers} himself describes as very uncertain:
