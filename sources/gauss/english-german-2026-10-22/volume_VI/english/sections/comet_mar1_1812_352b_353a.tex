\begin{center}\textbf{COMET.}\par\vspace{4pt}\hrule\vspace{4pt}\fontsize{10}{12}\selectfont Monatliche Correspondenz. Vol. XXV, pp. 206\;..\;207, February 1812.\par\vspace{4pt}\hrule\vspace{4pt}\end{center}

\begin{flushright}Göttingen, 1 March.\end{flushright}
\par\indent The bad weather and moonlight have allowed me to make only one observation of the comet since 4 January, and even in that observation the declination is uncertain. I now have little hope of observing it again in the next few days, since the weather is still quite unfavorable and the comet must already have become very faint.

\par\indent Professor \textsc{Harding} observed with a less powerful instrument than mine, which is why he recorded the disappearances a little earlier and the reappearances later than I did. Only for 162 \textsc{Mayer} should Professor \textsc{Harding}’s observation be preferred to mine, since I happened to be slightly disturbed just as I made the observation.
