\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Nouet on astronomical observations in Egypt}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), p.~534. The collected text is an extract from a longer review in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~79, 18 May 1811, pp.~780--781.
\par\smallskip\noindent The review discusses Nouet's \textit{Observations astronomiques faites en Egypte pendant les années VI, VII et VIII}, in \textit{Description de l'Egypte}, \textit{État moderne}, vol.~I, first instalment (Paris, Imprimerie impériale, 1809), pp.~1--20. The \href{https://archive.org/details/bibliographicala00thom}{London Institution collation}, pp.~26--27, lists the memoir. The \href{https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb35129066r}{Bibliothèque nationale de France catalogue} identifies the volume.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing gives a clear final zero in 3430. The translation retains the collected edition's spelling Ssalehhiyeh and its full stop before the continuation of the sentence on magnetic declination.
