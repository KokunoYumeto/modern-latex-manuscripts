\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (20 March 1815)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report headed Comet in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~382. The report appeared under Göttingen in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~45 (20 March 1815), \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538686_00449/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{p.~441}. The link leads to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The report gives news of the discovery from Heinrich Wilhelm Olbers’s letter of 7 March to Carl Friedrich Gauss, followed by the account of the search at the Göttingen Observatory. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
