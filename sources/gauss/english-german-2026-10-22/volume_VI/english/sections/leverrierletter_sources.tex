\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Schumacher on Le Verrier’s Planet (22 October 1846)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the letter on p.~471 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on 22 October 1846 about his observation of the previous evening. The letter appears at the foot of \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049450_00056/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{the spread with cols.~95--96} of \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXV, no.~582. The collected citation gives p.~95 and 5 November 1846.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal heading identifies Gauss as the writer and the editor as the recipient. The collected text begins with the place and date. Its notation and the concluding statement about a circular orbit are retained. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
