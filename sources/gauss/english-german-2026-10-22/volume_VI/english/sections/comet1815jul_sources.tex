\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (3 July 1815)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report headed Comet in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~385--387. The first printing appeared under Göttingen in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~105 (3 July 1815). The collected heading gives pp.~1041..1043. The complete first printing runs from p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538687_00367/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1041} through p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538687_00371/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1045} in the Bavarian State Library copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing gives the ascending node of the parabolic orbit as $82\,43\,6$, while the collected edition marks the units, $82^\circ\,43'\,6''$. For the brightness on 20~June, the first printing gives $\frac{9}{10}$ on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538687_00370/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1044} and the collected edition $\frac{3}{10}$. The fraction in the orbital period is $\frac{1}{3}$ in the first printing, with the preceding digits faint, and $\frac{1}{8}$ in the collected edition’s $77\frac{1}{8}$ years. The body retains the collected readings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
