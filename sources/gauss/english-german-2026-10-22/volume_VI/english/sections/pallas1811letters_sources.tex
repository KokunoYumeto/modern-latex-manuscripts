\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (10 February and 29 August 1811)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the two letters in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~331--333. The letters by Carl Friedrich Gauss appeared under the heading \textit{Astronomische Beobachtungen und Bemerkungen, vom Hrn. Prof. Gauss in Göttingen. Aus zweyen Briefen Desselben} in the \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1814}, edited by Johann Elert Bode (Berlin, 1811), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538343_00254/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{246}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538343_00255/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{247}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538343_00256/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{248} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538343_00257/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{249}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing gives 25 May for the Vesta opposition and 3 hours for the Paris time of the 1807 Ceres opposition, where the collected edition gives 15 May and 8 hours. The second term for the perturbation of the latitude of Ceres begins with $+14{,}17$ in the first printing and $-14.17$ in the collected edition. Two first-print footnotes concern sending the meridian observations to Gauss and printing the ephemerides later in the volume. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
