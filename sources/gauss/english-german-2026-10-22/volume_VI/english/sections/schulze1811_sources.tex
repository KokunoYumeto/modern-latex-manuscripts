\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Schulze on the system of the world}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), p.~533. It first appeared unsigned under Leipzig in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, 23 February 1811, pp.~305--306. The collected edition gives issue~21, and the first printing identifies itself as issue~31.
\par\smallskip\noindent The review concerns Gottlob Leberecht Schulze’s \textit{Darstellung des Weltsystems. Ein Leitfaden für den Unterricht in der Astronomie auf Schulen} (Leipzig, Baumgärtner, 1811). Catalogue entry~14/138 records the octavo edition with 390 numbered pages, XXIV preliminary pages and four folded plates.
