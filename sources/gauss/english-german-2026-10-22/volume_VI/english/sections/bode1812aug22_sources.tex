\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Bode (22 August 1812)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter of 22 August 1812 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~357--360. Its first printing appeared in \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00005/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{\textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1815}}, edited by Johann Elert Bode (Berlin, 1812), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00253/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{245}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00254/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{246}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00255/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{247}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00256/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{248}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538344_00257/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{249}, in the digitized Bavarian State Library copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected letter gives January in the Juno observations and June in the comparison. The first printing also has those month labels. It supplies 1811 beside the epoch and a decimal comma in the last declination difference on p.~248. The collected letter has no year on the epoch line and has $-45\;0$ in that difference. The yearbook also contains three notes signed B. on p.~249, including a reference to the Juno ephemeris that follows in the yearbook. These belong to the first printing. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
