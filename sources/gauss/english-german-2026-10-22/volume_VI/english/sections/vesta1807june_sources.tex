\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the June 1807 Report on Vesta}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s June 1807 report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~285--288. Von Zach quotes Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers and Carl Friedrich Gauss. The first printing is article XLIII, \textit{Fortgesetzte Nachrichten über den neuen von Dr. Olbers in Bremen entdeckten Planeten Vesta}, in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmelskunde}, vol.~XV, \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0597.tif}{pp.~590}--600. The collected excerpt begins in the last paragraph on \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0600.tif}{p.~593} and ends near the top of \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0606.tif}{p.~599}. The journal scans are from the Thüringer Universitäts- und Landesbibliothek Jena.
\par\medskip
The collected edition gives the historical note as Note~27. Its wording \textit{des Herr Verlegers} appears as \textit{des Herrn Verlegers} in the first printing on \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0601.tif}{p.~594}. The first elements and their comparison with observations appear on \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0603.tif}{p.~596}, and the second elements on \href{https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/rsc/viewer/jportal_derivate_00237836/Monatliche_Correspondenz_130168688_15_1807_0605.tif}{p.~598}. The first printing’s footnote on p.~598 refers to the entry for 91 Virginis in article XXIV of the same journal, vol.~IX (March 1804), \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538601?page=253}{p.~239}, digitized by the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek. That reference belongs to the first printing. The six editable altar signs follow the contours in the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
