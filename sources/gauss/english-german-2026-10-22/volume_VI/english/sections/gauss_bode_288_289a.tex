\par\indent I thank Your --- very much for kindly sending me the observations of Vesta. I would have sent you the results I had obtained 18 days ago, had I not had reason to distrust them at first and later to expect that I could soon improve them. As you will see below, that improvement is still impracticable.

\par\indent I observed Vesta on only two evenings. The planet’s high position soon made the observations difficult for me, and I could all the more readily give them up afterward because Messrs. \textsc{Olbers} and \textsc{Harding} kindly sent me their observations with each post. Here are my two observations.

\par\indent On 20 April, when I had received Dr. \textsc{Olbers}’s observation of the 17th and thus had an interval of 19 days, I undertook the first determination of the orbit of this new planet. As early as the 21st I sent Dr. \textsc{Olbers} the elements I had found. But the interval was so short, the observations were of limited accuracy, since most had been made only with a ring micrometer, and in this case the small inclination of the orbit made an accurate determination harder. I still did not dare to put these results forward even as reliable approximations. I therefore reserved the option of improving them as soon as later observations made that possible. Yet the observations I received afterward continued to agree so closely with the elements that the difference could not yet be determined at all. Since this is in effect just as good as if the elements had also been based directly on the later observations, I now think they can indeed be accepted with confidence as a first approximation. Only at the end of April and the beginning of this month did the difference start to become noticeable. One can now see at least that the calculated right ascensions are a little too large and the declinations a little too small. But the differences are still too small, and their size is still indicated too inconsistently by the observations, for a second calculation of the orbit to be useful at this point. Once the error has become somewhat larger, this will be done. Here, then, are my \emph{Elements} I. \emph{of Vesta.} (They already appear above on \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538339?page=208}{p.~198} of this Yearbook, \textsc{Bode}.) [They are printed on \hyperlink{vesta1807-first-elements}{p. 286} of this volume.]
