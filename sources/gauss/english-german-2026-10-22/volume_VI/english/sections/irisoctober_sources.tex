\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Iris observations in October 1847}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the excerpts on p.~475 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on 11 and 21 October 1847. Between the two letters, Schumacher reports observations from a later letter by Gauss.
\par\smallskip\noindent The excerpts come from \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXVI, no.~616, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00129/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~241--242} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049451_00136/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~255--256}. The colophon on col.~256 dates the issue to 8 November 1847. The two reports give different values for some of the same observations on 13 and 14 October. The tables follow the collected edition in both places. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
