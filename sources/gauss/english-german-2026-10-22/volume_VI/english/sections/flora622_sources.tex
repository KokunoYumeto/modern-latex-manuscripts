\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Flora observations of December 1847}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the Göttingen report on p.~476 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Heinrich Christian Schumacher reports Carl Friedrich Gauss’s meridian-circle observations of Flora on 10--12 December 1847.
