\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Bode's yearbooks}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~548--552. It first appeared under Berlin in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~22, 6 February 1813, pp.~209--220.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected text gives 2811 in the entry for Huth. The first printing and the 1815 yearbook’s contents give 1811. In the first printing, Bessel’s entry after Huth is numbered 3 again and the entry between 19 and 21 is numbered 29. The collected text gives 4 and 20, respectively.
\par\smallskip\noindent Johann Elert Bode calculated and edited both yearbooks. Their title pages give Berlin, 1811 for \textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für das Jahr 1814} and Berlin, 1812 for the volume for 1815. Both were available from the author and on commission from J.~E. Hitzig and were printed by C.~F.~E. Späthen.
