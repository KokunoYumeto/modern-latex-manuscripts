\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the 3 March 1803 Letter to Bode}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter to Johann Elert Bode of 3 March 1803 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~234--235. Gauss compares Olbers’s observations after the rediscovery of Pallas with the elements used for the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00597/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{December 1802 ephemeris}, then gives preliminary revised elements and a new comparison.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538335_00189/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{\textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1806}, p.~179} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538335_00190/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~180} (Berlin, 1803), identifies Pallas in its title. Its first comparison table omits the Bremen mean times. Its footnote refers to the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538334_00238/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{\textit{Astronomisches Jahrbuch für 1805}, p.~228}. The collected letter gives a bracketed reference to p.~233 of the \textit{Werke}.
\par\smallskip\noindent Translation and typesetting produced by AI. Yearbook and journal scans from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
