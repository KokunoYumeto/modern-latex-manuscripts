\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Bode’s Astronomical Yearbook for 1822\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~619--622. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~23, 7 February 1820, pp.~225--232. Johann Elert Bode’s yearbook for 1822 appeared in Berlin in 1819, available from the author and on commission through Ferdinand Dümmler.

The German wording in the review’s errata refers to \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538351_00245/full/1650,/0/default.jpg}{p.~239 of the yearbook}. \emph{Merklich} means “noticeable” and \emph{unmerklich} “imperceptible.” \emph{Doch kann ich schon glauben} means “But I can well believe” and \emph{doch kann ich schwer glauben} “But I can hardly believe.”
