\textbf{VESTA.}

\hrulefill

Monatliche Correspondenz. Vol. XV. Pp. 502–508. May 1807.

\hrulefill
\end{center}

--- --- ---

\par\indent “As soon as I could be fairly sure,” (Dr. \textsc{Olbers} writes to us on 22 April) “that I really was the first to discover this new planet, I asked our incomparable \textsc{Gauss}, who has done such outstanding work on all these small planets, to choose a name and a sign for it. Dr. \textsc{Gauss} granted my request. ‘You urge me,’ he replied, ‘so earnestly to accept the honor of standing as godfather to your planet that I cannot decline it, however little claim I have to it. So be it. I can think of no lovelier name for the planet than that of the goddess whom the peoples of antiquity made the guardian goddess of pure morals, spotless virtue and domestic happiness. If my choice does not strike you as unsuitable, let your little daughter be called \emph{Vesta!}’ I think the name is very well chosen. For its sign, Dr. \textsc{Gauss} chose a symbolic representation of the sacred fire burning on the goddess’s altar \VestaAltarBody{}. This too seems to me to serve its purpose in every respect.”

\begin{flushright}
[\textsc{von Zach.}]
\end{flushright}
