\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the notice of Picot and Sigorgne}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated notice follows Gauss’s text on p.~507 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). It appeared unsigned under the heading \emph{Genf} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~164, 13 October 1808, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00623/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{1639}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538674_00624/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{1640}. The linked copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The French titles cited in the notice mean \emph{On the Twenty-One Most Recent Comets and the New Planets}, by Professor P. Picot, and \emph{Demonstration of the Immediate Creation of the Earth in a Solid State and of the Impossibility of Physical Causes for the Formation of Its Shape}, by Abbé Sigorgne. Gauss reports on an excerpt from a letter by Olbers in the first item. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
