\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to Schumacher (20 July 1830)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter of 20 July 1830 to Heinrich Christian Schumacher on p.~462 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The extract first appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~VIII, no.~185, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049433_00169/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~321--322}, in August 1830.
\par\smallskip\noindent For Pallas, the collected edition gives 58.5 in the seconds of mean time on 27 April and 81~44.3 for the time on 30 April. The journal gives 58,6 and 31~44,3, respectively. The journal also has a longer Ceres caption and ends with Gauss’s signature. The wording, figures and layout of the tables here follow the collected edition.
