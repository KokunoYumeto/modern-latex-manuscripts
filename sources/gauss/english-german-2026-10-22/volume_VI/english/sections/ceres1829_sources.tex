\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Observations of Ceres in Göttingen (1829)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the notice on p.~462 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Carl Friedrich Gauss’s observations first appeared as \textit{Beobachtungen der Ceres in Göttingen} in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~VII, no.~161, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049432_00211/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~329--330}, in June 1829.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition attributes the observations to Gauss in the bracketed heading and puts all eight in one table. The declination cell for 16 March is empty there. The journal sets the observations in two blocks, puts a dash in that cell and ends with Gauss’s signature. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
