\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Opposition of Pallas (1834)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the notice on p.~463 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Heinrich Christian Schumacher presents three observations that Carl Friedrich Gauss made at Göttingen. The notice appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XI, no.~262, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049436_00218/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~403--404}, issued on 9 July 1834. The Bavarian State Library catalogue dates the bound volume to 1833.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the units with the first observation and signs the notice \textit{Schumacher.} The journal uses separate unit headings and the signature \textit{S.} The table and signature here follow the collected edition.
