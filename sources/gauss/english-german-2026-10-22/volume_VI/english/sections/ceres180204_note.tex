\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the April 1802 Ceres Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the German excerpt in Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~209--210. Franz Xaver von Zach reports on Ceres and presents calculations by Carl Friedrich Gauss and diameter observations by Johann Hieronymus Schröter.
\par\smallskip\noindent Von Zach’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00391/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{original article}, no.~XL, appeared in the April 1802 issue of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde} (Gotha, Becker), vol.~V, pp.~379--404. The collected text draws on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00401/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~389--390} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00414/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~402--404}. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00416/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{last page} also contains the end of the original article. Journal scans from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
