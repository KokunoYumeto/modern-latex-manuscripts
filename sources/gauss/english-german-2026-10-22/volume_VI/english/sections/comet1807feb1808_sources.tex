\begin{center}\textbf{Source for the Notice on the Comet of 1807}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the notice dated 25 February 1808 as printed in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~298--299. The notice appeared without a byline under the heading \textit{Göttingen} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue~32, \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00000317/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~313}, \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00000318/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{314} and \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00000319/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{315}. Its reporter speaks of Gauss in the third person.
\par\medskip
The first printing sets out the differences separately on p.~314. The collected edition places them alongside the observations, and its bracketed sentence describes this arrangement. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
