\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Juno (9 September 1812)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the letter of 9 September 1812 in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~360--361. The letter first appeared as item XXIV of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmelskunde}, vol.~XXVI (September 1812), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538615_00307/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{297}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538615_00308/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{298}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538615_00309/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{299}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing repeats month and declination labels by ditto marks, has $+22{,}4$ for the July 12 right-ascension difference, and specifies north after the geocentric latitude of the opposition. The collected text omits those ditto marks, gives $+22\;4$ and leaves the direction unstated in the opposition result. Both editions say fourth and sixth columns in the discussion of the earlier intersection. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
