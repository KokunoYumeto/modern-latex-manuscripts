\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Moon observations (February 1822)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the first notice headed \textit{Mondsbeobachtungen} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~441. H. C. Schumacher first published the notice as \textit{Sterne, deren grade Aufsteigung mit der des Mondes zu vergleichen ist} in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~I, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049426_00061/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{no.~7}, February 1822, cols.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049426_00065/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{105--106}. He signed it S. The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049426_00005/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{title page} of the Bavarian State Library copy of the bound volume gives Altona 1823 and names the Hammerich and Heineking printing house.
\par\smallskip\noindent Schumacher names Professor Encke as the source of the stars for the coming lunations and Gauss as the source of the Göttingen observations for the preceding ones. The first print uses primes and double primes where the collected table uses superscript m and s. Both leave the differences for the two Moon rows empty. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
