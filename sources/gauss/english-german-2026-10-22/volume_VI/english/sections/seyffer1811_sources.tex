\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Seyffer on the longitude of Munich}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~533--534. It first appeared under Munich in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~78, 18 May 1811, pp.~773--775.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected review gives the Latin title \textit{Super longitudine geographica speculae astronomicae regiae, quae Monachii est, ex triginta septem defectionibus Solis observatis et ad calculos revocatis nunc primum definita}. Seyffer’s 1810 title page also identifies the work as \textit{Commentatio altera}. The digitized copy is held by the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing uses primes and double primes for the two longitude figures. The collected text gives superscript m and s, and its punctuation reads \textit{Brünn. Berlin}. The translation follows the collected text.
