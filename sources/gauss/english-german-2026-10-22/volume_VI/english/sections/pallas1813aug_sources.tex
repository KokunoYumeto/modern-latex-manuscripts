\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (31 August 1813)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the letter of 31 August 1813 in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~368--369. It first appeared as item XXI, \textit{Aus einem Schreiben des Hrn. Prof. Gauss}, in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmelskunde}, vol.~XXVIII (August 1813), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00205/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{197}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00206/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{198}, here linked to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing opens with four points. The collected text opens with three dashes. For 16 August the first printing gives five points in both declination cells. The collected text gives five in the observations and four in the differences. It also omits the marks that repeat the month in the first table and uses raised u, m and s for the time units. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
