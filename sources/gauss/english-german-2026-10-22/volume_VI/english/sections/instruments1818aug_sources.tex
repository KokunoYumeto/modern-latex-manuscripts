\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Instruments (8 August 1818)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Instrumente} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~410--414. The report first appeared under the heading Göttingen in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~127, 8 August 1818, pp.~\href{https://books.google.com/books?id=Uy1ZAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA1257}{1257}--\href{https://books.google.com/books?id=Uy1ZAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA1267}{1267}. The first-print links lead to the digitized copy on Google Books. The report speaks in the first person plural and refers to Gauss in the third person.
\par\smallskip\noindent In the table of Polaris observations and the sentence that follows it, p.~\href{https://books.google.com/books?id=Uy1ZAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA1263}{1263} of the first printing has a decimal comma in the correction for 26 June and gives the mean correction with the final digits 83. The collected edition has no decimal point in that cell and gives the mean with the final digits 88. The first printing also has decimal commas in three Uranus entries on pp.~\href{https://books.google.com/books?id=Uy1ZAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA1264}{1264}--\href{https://books.google.com/books?id=Uy1ZAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA1265}{1265} where the collected text has no visible decimal points. The translation retains the collected readings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
