\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the letter of 3 August 1811}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Gauss’s letter to Bernhard August von Lindenau in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~326--327. The first printing appeared as an extract in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXIV, August 1811, pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00190/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{180}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00191/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{181} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538613_00192/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{182}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal gives Gauss’s title as \textit{Ritter der westphälischen Krone}. An editorial footnote on p.~182 says that the ephemeris will appear in the next issue. The collected edition omits the title and footnote and includes the bracketed handwritten remark, which is absent from the first printing. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
