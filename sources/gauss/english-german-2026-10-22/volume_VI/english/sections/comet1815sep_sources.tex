\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (18 September 1815)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~389--391, printed in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~149 (18 September 1815), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538688_00113/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1473}--\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538688_00116/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1476}. The first-print links lead to the Bavarian State Library copy. For the citation of Möbius’s elements, see \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538617_00593/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{\textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXVIII, p.~577}, where a note signed N. gives Möbius’s comparison and revised elements.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing has \textit{Fraunhofer} on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538688_00114/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1474} and three asterisks before the Juno passage on p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538688_00115/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{1475}. Both printings give 59~13~3 for the comet’s right ascension on 30~March and 30.1 seconds (30,1 in the first printing) for Juno’s declination on 29~March, whereas the collected August letter to Bode gives 59~13~2 and 38.1 respectively. Both report printings give the double-prime sign in the time on 1~March.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing gives Juno’s perihelion longitude as 53~14~53,8 without unit marks and marks the inclination as $13^\circ\,14'\,0''1$. The collected report marks the longitude and leaves the inclination components unmarked. The last sentence has \textit{Hrn.\ N.} in the first printing and names Nicolai in the collected edition. The translation follows the collected readings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
