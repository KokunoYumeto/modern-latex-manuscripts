\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Schumacher’s Report on d’Arrest’s Comet (January 1845)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the extract on pp.~470--471 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). Heinrich Christian Schumacher reports observations by Petersen and Carl Friedrich Gauss. The report on the comet discovered by d’Arrest begins on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049447_00184/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{col.~351} of \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXII, no.~526. The collected extract is from \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049447_00185/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~353--354}, and the collected citation dates the issue to 25 January 1845.
\par\smallskip\noindent The journal also prints observations and computations by other contributors, including orbital elements by Rümker and Sievers. The collected extract retains Schumacher’s remarks about the elements and ends with the position of the last comparison star on Harding’s chart and Schumacher’s signature. The table follows the collected reading for repeated fields. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
