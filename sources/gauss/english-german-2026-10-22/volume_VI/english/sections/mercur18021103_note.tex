\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the 1802 Mercury Transit Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Franz Xaver von Zach’s account of Gauss’s observations in Braunschweig as excerpted in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~231--232. The excerpt includes the times of both contacts at egress, the mean times in a bracketed manuscript passage and five observations of the difference in right ascension between the Sun and Mercury.
\par\smallskip\noindent The \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00581/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} is article LXIII of the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~VI, pp.~567--575. The collected excerpt begins with \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00584/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{Gauss’s observations on p.~570} and ends with \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538598_00585/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{his table on p.~571}. The collected heading gives 3 November 1802. The journal heading gives 8 November, and the manuscript passage in the collected text gives 9 November. The heading and manuscript date are reproduced as they appear in the \textit{Werke}.
