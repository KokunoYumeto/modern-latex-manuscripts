\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the Letter on the Second Comet of 1805}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter of 20 May 1806 to Franz Xaver von Zach as printed in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~270--275. The letter appeared in the \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XIV, July 1806, article~VIII, part~2, \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=85}{pp.~75}--\href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538606?page=96}{86}. The journal scans are from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek. The time column and the bracket identifying local times follow the collected edition, which prints \textit{dasss} on p.~273.
\par\medskip
Gauss cites Jérôme Lalande’s \textit{Histoire céleste française} (Paris, 1801), \href{https://archive.org/details/histoireclestef00lalagoog/page/n47/mode/1up}{p.~20}, \href{https://archive.org/details/histoireclestef00lalagoog/page/n64/mode/1up}{p.~37}, \href{https://archive.org/details/histoireclestef00lalagoog/page/n227/mode/1up}{p.~200} and \href{https://archive.org/details/histoireclestef00lalagoog/page/n516/mode/1up}{p.~477}, and the \textit{Connaissance des tems} for \href{https://cdt.imcce.fr/items/show/152}{year~XI} and \href{https://cdt.imcce.fr/items/show/154}{year~XIII}.
