\begin{flushright}Göttingen, 31 October 1850.\end{flushright}

I am sending you, my dearest friend, my three meridian observations of \emph{Victoria}. Apart from the difficulty of observing in even a dimly lit field, their precision is somewhat impaired because in each case the reduction elements could be obtained only from more distant observations.
