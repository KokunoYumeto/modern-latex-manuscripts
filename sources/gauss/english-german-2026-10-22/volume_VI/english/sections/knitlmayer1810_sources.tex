\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of Knitlmayer on planetary distances}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~528--530. It first appeared unsigned under Brünn in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~179, 10 November 1810, pp.~1777--1782.
\par\smallskip\noindent Gauss reviews Ferdinand Knitlmayer’s \textit{Versuch einer genauen Darstellung des Progressionsverhältnisses der Planeten- und Trabantenabstände von ihren Centralkörpern.} Constant von Wurzbach’s \textit{Biographisches Lexikon des Kaiserthums Oesterreich}, vol.~12 (1864), p.~153, records the book’s imprint as Gastl, Brünn, 1808.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected review reads \emph{geocentrische Progression} where the first printing reads \emph{geometrische Progression}. The translation follows the collected text. Both printings show a minus sign before the fraction in the density critique and print the cube root in the satellite formula without a bar over the radicand.
