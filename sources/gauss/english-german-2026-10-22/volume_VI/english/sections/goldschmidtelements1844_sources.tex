\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Notice of Goldschmidt’s Elliptical Elements (1844)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the text on p.~464 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874). The notice refers to Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter of 2 January 1844 to Heinrich Christian Schumacher, published on 18 January 1844. It credits Dr. Goldschmidt with calculating the elements. \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~XXI, no.~494, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10049446_00119/full/1800,/0/default.jpg}{cols.~221--222}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition has a bracketed notice about the elements. The journal letter gives the elements, a comparison table and Gauss’s accompanying discussion. The translation follows the scope of the collected notice. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
