\begin{center}\textbf{Source for Ceres, Elements XII}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the notice of 10 March 1808 in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~299--300. The notice appeared under the heading \textit{Göttingen} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue~40, \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00000397/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{pp.~393}, \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00000398/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{394} and \href{https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1808:00000399/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{395}. The text refers to Gauss in the third person.
\par\medskip
The first printing compares five oppositions. The collected edition also prints the comparison for 1808 and the differences for Elements XIII. Schering identifies both additions as being in Gauss’s hand. The collected edition also supplies the bracketed attribution to Harding. The unfilled latitude entry for 1808 and the irregular separator among the leaders for true longitude follow the collected edition. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
