\begin{center}\Large\bfseries Sources for the review of Bode’s astronomical yearbook for 1821\end{center}

The translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s review in \emph{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~611--614. The review first appeared in \emph{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~80, 20 May 1819, pp.~793--800. Johann Elert Bode’s yearbook for 1821 was published in Berlin in 1818 by the author, on commission with Ferdinand Dümmler.
