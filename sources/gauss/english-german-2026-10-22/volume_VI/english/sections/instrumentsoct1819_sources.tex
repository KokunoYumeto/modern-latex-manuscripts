\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Instruments (18 October 1819)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Instrumente} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~422--424. The report first appeared under \textit{Göttingen} in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~167, 18 October 1819. The collected edition cites pp.~1665--1669. In the \href{https://books.google.com/books?id=SggWAAAAYAAJ&pg=PA1665}{New York Public Library copy digitized by Google}, the report continues onto \href{https://books.google.com/books?id=SggWAAAAYAAJ&pg=PA1670}{p.~1670}, with the observations of 85 Pegasi and the final paragraph on its proper motion.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the opening cross-reference to the \hyperlink{native-415}{earlier report on Repsold’s meridian circle}. The translation follows the collected edition’s probable-error notation and retains the numeral 1 in the Draco designation and the missing preposition in the phrase about the pulley and the ceiling. The reporter speaks in the first person plural and refers to Gauss in the third person. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
