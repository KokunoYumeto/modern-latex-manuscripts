\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the review of d’Aguila}\end{center}
\noindent This AI-translated review follows Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), p.~541. It first appeared under Paris in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~102, 27 June 1812, pp.~1009--1010.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first digit of the denominator is crowded in the collected printing. The first printing on p.~1009 supports the reading \(1/59\). The reviewed \href{https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb30005371x}{book by C. J. E. H. d’Aguila} is listed in the Bibliothèque nationale de France catalogue as printed in Paris by Delance in 1806. The collected review spells the printer’s name \textit{Délance}.
