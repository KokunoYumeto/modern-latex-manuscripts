\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the June 1802 Pallas Report}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the German excerpt in Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~212--216. Franz Xaver von Zach reports on the discovery of Pallas by Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers. The excerpt contains calculations by Carl Friedrich Gauss and quotes from one of his letters.
\par\smallskip\noindent Von Zach’s \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00607/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{original article}, no.~LX, appeared in the June 1802 issue of \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde} (Gotha, Becker), vol.~V, pp.~591--606. The excerpt follows pp.~591--600 and resumes with the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00624/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{ephemeris on p.~606}. The reference to the unsuccessful circular fit leads to the \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00498/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{preceding issue, p.~486}. The first and second sets of elements are compared with observations on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00610/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~594} and \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00614/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~596}.
\par\smallskip\noindent In the first comparison, the collected edition gives the right-ascension difference for Olbers’s 1 May observation as \(+20.0\). The journal gives \(+5.0\). The later orbital arc is \(7^\circ 26'\) in the collected edition and \(7^\circ 23'\) \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00611/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{in the journal}. The translation follows the collected figures. Its English rendering of the clause about the parallax calculation on p.~214 takes \textit{einfachen} in the apparent sense of \textit{einfacher}. The break before the ephemeris follows the collected excerpt after the paragraph on \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538597_00618/full/1450,/0/default.jpg}{p.~600}.
