\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Gauss to von Lindenau, 30 August 1808}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows Carl Friedrich Gauss’s German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~146--147, including the sextant note. The passage is on pp.~271--273 of article XXXIV, \textit{Auszug aus einem Schreiben des Herrn Professors Gauss}, in the \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538609?page=293}{September 1808 issue} of Franz Xaver von Zach’s \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz zur Beförderung der Erd- und Himmels-Kunde}, vol.~18, pp.~269--273. The journal’s note on p.~271 announces the paper on spherical astronomy in the \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10538609?page=299}{following issue}. The collected text prints \textit{Reispiel} where the journal has \textit{Beyspiel}, meaning “example”. \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Collected German text}.
