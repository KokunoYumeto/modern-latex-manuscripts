[VON LINDENAU TO GAUSS.]

[Among \textsc{Gauss}’s papers.]
\end{center}

\begin{flushright}Altenburg, 17 Feb. 1806.\end{flushright}

--- --- --- I passed the letter in which Your Honour kindly gave me your opinion of \textsc{Rohde}’s reply to the criticism on to Mr Obh. \textsc{von Zach}. As you will have seen from the M. C., he saw fit to print the whole letter. I hope this will accord with your wishes. --- ---

I must also mention a small doubt that occurred to me on this occasion. Your Honour declares \textsc{Rohde}’s method wrong even in \emph{theory}, and I certainly find your reasons quite convincing. On the other hand, when I consider that \textsc{Rohde}’s expression, as I showed in my reply, is analogous to the known relation between masses, orbital periods and mean distances, this in turn made me hesitate to declare the method wrong without qualification. --- ---
