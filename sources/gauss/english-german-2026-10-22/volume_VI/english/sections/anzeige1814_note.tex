\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for the 1814 Comet Review}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the German text in Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~VI (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~65--67. The review appeared in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~3, 3 January 1814, pp.~25--28. \href{https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb10538685?page=31}{First printing at the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek}. \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{Collected German text}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected text dates the observatory’s observations from 9 to 25 April. The first printing gives 7 to 25 April. In both editions the example at the end uses observations from 7, 14 and 21 April.
