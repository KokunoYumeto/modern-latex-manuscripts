\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Comet (8 April 1815)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows the report headed Comet in Gauss’s \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~382--383. It appeared under Göttingen in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~55 (8 April 1815), pp.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538686_00545/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{537}, \href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538686_00546/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{538}. The links lead to the Bavarian State Library’s digitized copy.
\par\smallskip\noindent The collected edition gives the longitude of perihelion as $246^\circ\,7'\,2''$. The first printing gives $146^\circ\,7'\,2''$. Both print Enke and give 59 13 3 as the right ascension on 30 March. The translation uses Auriga for the constellation called Fahrmann in the collected edition and Fuhrmann in the first printing.
