\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Meridian Circle (5 June 1820)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \textit{Meridiankreis} in \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), pp.~429--433. The \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1820:00000901/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} appeared under \textit{Göttingen} in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~91, 5 June 1820, pp.~905--912. The digitized copy is held by the Göttingen State and University Library. The report speaks in the first person plural and names Gauss as the observer in the third person. Its reference to Reichenbach’s transit instrument leads to the \hyperlink{native-427-part-lower}{October 1819 report}.
\par\smallskip\noindent The first printing has 14 seconds on \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1820:00000902/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{p.~906}. The collected edition prints a point before 14. On \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1820:00000907/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{p.~911}, the first printing includes the collimation error in the Pole Star heading. The collected edition names the culmination error there. The collected 11 April Mars entry has a space where the \href{https://images.p.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/image/gdz:PPN319721507_1820:00000908/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{first printing} places a comma between 20 and 3 in the time. This translation preserves the collected readings. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
