\begin{center}\textbf{Sources for Pallas (letter of 15 April 1812)}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation follows \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236007467}{\textit{Werke}, vol.~VI} (Göttingen, 1874), p.~349. Gauss’s letter of 15 April appeared as item XXXVI, \textit{Aus einem Schreiben des Hrn. Prof. Gauss}, in \textit{Monatliche Correspondenz}, vol.~XXV, April 1812, p.~\href{https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb10538614_00403/full/1600,/0/default.jpg}{389}. The digitized copy is held by the Bavarian State Library.
\par\smallskip\noindent The notation follows the collected edition. The observation uses the Pallas symbol, and the expression in the paragraph on perturbations uses the symbols for Jupiter and Pallas. Translation and typesetting produced by AI.
