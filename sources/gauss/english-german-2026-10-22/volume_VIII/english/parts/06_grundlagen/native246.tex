\mbox{.\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .} I am likewise most grateful for the other things you sent,
and for the Russian papers by
\textsc{Lobachevsky} I am probably indebted in the first instance to your son, to whom
I expressed my wish some years ago when he was here.
Please commend me warmly to his kind remembrance. My
Russian must have grown somewhat rusty,
as for more than a year I have had no occasion
even to look at a single Russian character. I hope, however, at my first free
moment to make up quickly for what I have missed, and I shall then devote particular attention
to reading those interesting papers. The short
German work by \textsc{Lobachevsky} I already
owned myself. \mbox{.\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .}
