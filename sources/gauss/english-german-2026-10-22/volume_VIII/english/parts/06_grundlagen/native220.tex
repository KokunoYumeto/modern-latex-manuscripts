If two intersecting straight lines (1) and (2) make the angles $A'$, $A''$, respectively, with a third line (3) that intersects them, and if a fourth straight line (4) in the same plane is likewise intersected by (1) at the angle $A'$, then (4) will be intersected by (2) at the angle $A''$.

Yet this proposition itself requires proof. Indeed, one can say that it is essentially the very proposition to be proved.
