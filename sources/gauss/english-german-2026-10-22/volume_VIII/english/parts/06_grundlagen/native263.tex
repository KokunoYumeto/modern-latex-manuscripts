\noindent It follows easily from 1) that for infinitesimal $P$, when $Q=90^{0}$:

\[
R = 90^{0} - gPQ\,.\,P.
\]

\begin{center}\input{figure263c.tex}\end{center}

\noindent Similarly, if $P,Q,R$ are right angles and $PQ$ is infinitesimal, we obtain:

\[
PSQ = \frac{PQ}{fr},
\]
\[
RQS = gr\,.\,PSQ = \frac{gr}{fr}\cdot PQ,
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}RSQ &= 90^{0} - gr\,.\,RQS = 90^{0} - gr^{2}\,.\,PSQ\\ &= 90^{0} - \frac{gr^{2}}{fr}\cdot PQ,\end{aligned}
\]

\noindent hence

\[
PSR = 90^{0} + \frac{1-gr^{2}}{fr}\cdot PQ.
\]

\begin{center}\input{figure263d.tex}\end{center}

\noindent From this we obtain further

\[
R'S' = RS - fRR'\cdot\frac{1-gr^{2}}{fr}\cdot PQ,
\]
\[

\]
