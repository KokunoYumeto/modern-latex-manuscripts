\begin{center}
{\Large\textsc{[Volume determinations\\ in non-Euclidean geometry.]}}



[1.]

\textsc{Gauss} to \textsc{Encke}. Göttingen, 1 February 1841.
\end{center}


\mbox{.\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .} I am beginning to read Russian with some facility, and
I take great pleasure in it. Mr \textsc{Knorre} has sent me a short paper
written in Russian by \textsc{Lobachevsky} (in Kazan). This and
a short work in German on parallel lines
(of which there is a most foolish review in \textsc{Gersdorf}'s Repertorium) have made me
quite eager to read more by this acute mathematician. As
\textsc{Knorre} told me, the proceedings of the University of Kazan (written in Russian)
contain a great many of his papers. \mbox{.\ .\ .}
