\noindent On the other hand, for $dA = 0$, III. gives
\[
\sin B \,.\, da = \cos B\, g(a) \,.\, db,
\]
hence
\[
g(c) \sin A = g(a) \cos B,
\]
or, using V. 2),
\[
\tag*{V. 3)} g(c) = g(a)\, g(b).
\]
Furthermore, multiplying V. 1) and V. 2) gives
\[
\mathrm{tg}\, A \,.\, \mathrm{tg}\, B \,.\, g(a)\, g(b) = 1,
\]
\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
which, by V. 3), becomes
\[
\tag*{V. 4)} \mathrm{tg}\, A \,.\, \mathrm{tg}\, B \,.\, g(c) = 1
\]


It is easy to see that relations V. 1), 2) and 3) suffice to derive all
10 equations relating three elements at a time of a right triangle,
namely the equations for the following groups in order
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
a,\ b,\ c & A,\ a,\ c;\ B,\ b,\ c\\
A,\ b,\ c;\ B,\ a,\ c & A,\ B,\ a;\ A,\ B,\ b\\
A,\ a,\ b;\ B,\ a,\ b & A,\ B,\ c.
\end{array}
\]
Everything therefore depends on determining the still unknown
function $g(x)$ occurring in these relations. The following considerations
lead to its determination.

12. In the triangle $PQR$, let the angle at $Q$ be right and the angle at $P$
infinitesimal, so that its sine can be replaced by the arc. Then
the angle at $R$ differs infinitesimally from a right angle, and
relation V. 1), in which $A$, $B$ must be replaced by $R$, $P$, respectively, becomes
\[
90^{0} - R = g(PQ) \,.\, P
\]
or
\[
\tag*{(C)} R = 90^{0} - g(PQ) \,.\, P.
\]

13. Let the quadrilateral $PQRS$ have right angles at $P$, $Q$ and $R$, while the side
$PQ$ is infinitesimal. Let the diagonal $QS$ be equal to $r$. Then by
(A') we have
\[
PQ = f(r) \,.\, PSQ
\]
or
\[
PSQ = \frac{PQ}{f(r)},
\]
and likewise
\[
RQS = \frac{RS}{f(r)}.
\]
Now extend $PQ$ by $QT = PQ$ and $SR$ by $RU = SR$. Then the quadrilateral
$PTUS$ has right angles at $P$ and $T$, and the sides $PS$ and $TU$ are
equal. Hence by (B) we have
\[
SU = g(PS) \,.\, PT
\]
or, since $RS$ is infinitesimal, up to infinitesimals of the second
order,
\[
RS = g(r) \,.\, PQ
\]
and to the same degree of accuracy,
\[
\tag*{7)} RQS = \frac{g(r)}{f(r)} \,.\, PQ.
\]
