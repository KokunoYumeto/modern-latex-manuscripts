III. Let two straight lines $ab$, $ac$ intersect at $a$ at the angle $\varphi$, and let a third straight line $de$ be such that $ab\TripleParallel ed$, $ac\TripleParallel de$. Then $de$ also lies in the same plane as $ab$ and $ac$, and the area of the region between these lines is finite and depends only on the angle $\varphi$. Evidently in $S$ the lines $de$ and $bac$ form just one straight line when $\varphi=180^{0}$, so the value of that area vanishes with $180^{0}-\varphi$. Thus let the area in general be $=f(180^{0}-\varphi)$, where $f$ denotes a function symbol.
