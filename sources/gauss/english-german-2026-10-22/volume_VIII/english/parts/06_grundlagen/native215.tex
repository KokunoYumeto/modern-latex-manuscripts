PARALLELISM.
\end{center}

1. $ab\ast $ is parallel to $cd\ast $ if

1) both lie in one plane,

2) they do not intersect,

3) every line $af\ast $ within the region $\ast bacd\ast $ intersects $cd\ast $.

2. Parallelism is independent of the initial point of the line $cd\ast $.

3. Parallelism is independent of the initial point of the line $ab\ast $.

I. $a'b\ast $ is also parallel to $cd\ast $ if $a'$ is on $ab\ast $.

\textls{Proof.} $af\ast $ intersects $cd\ast $, so $a'f\ast $ will also intersect it.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
