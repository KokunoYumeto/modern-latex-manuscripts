\medskip
The author attempts to prove this theorem by reductio ad absurdum, enumerating the other
cases that would be possible if the theorem were false, and attempting to rule out each
one. The author claims that under that assumption one of the
following five cases would have to occur. The successive segments, starting from $OM$,
would be


\par\hangindent1.5em 1) all equal to one another, or
\par\hangindent1.5em 2) each subsequent one greater than the one before it, or
\par\hangindent1.5em 3) some equal to one another and the next greater or smaller, or
\par\hangindent1.5em 4) some successive ones would decrease progressively and those following them
would increase progressively, or
\par\hangindent1.5em 5) they would alternately increase and decrease.
