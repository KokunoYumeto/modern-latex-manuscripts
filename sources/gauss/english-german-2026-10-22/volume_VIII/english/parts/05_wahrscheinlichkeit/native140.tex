\begin{center}
[A PROBLEM IN THE THEORY OF PROBABILITY.]
\end{center}

\bigskip
\hrule
\bigskip

Let $\mu$ be the probability of a particular outcome $E$ in a
single trial. Then $1-\mu$ is the probability that this
outcome fails to occur.

The probability that, among $n$ independent trials, $k$
produce the outcome $E$ is

\[
\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)....(n-k+1)}{1.2.3....k}(1-\mu)^{n-k}\mu^k = \varphi k.
\]
