The mean value of $n$ [for $m=5$ and $6$] is

\[
(5,1)+2(5,2)+3(5,3)+4(5,4)
\]
\[
= 10x-10xx+5x^3-x^4,
\]
\[
(6,1)+2(6,2)+3(6,3)+4(6,4)+5(6,5)
\]
\[
= 15x-20xx+15x^3-6x^4+x^5
\]

and in general

\[
(m,1)+2(m,2)+3(m,3)+\cdots = \frac{(1-x)^m-(1-mx)}{x}.
\]

Denote the numerical coefficients as follows. For example, for $(8,5)$ use
$C^{8.5}$. Then, for\,example, $3C^{8.5}+C^{8.6}=C^{9.6}$,
[and in general

\[
(m-n)C^{m.n}+C^{m.n+1}=C^{m+1.n+1}.
\]

Further,] in general

\[
(m-n)x(m,n)+(1-(m-n-1)x)(m,n+1)=(m+1,n+1).
\]

It follows readily, on setting

\[
(m,1)+2(m,2)+3(m,3)+4(m,4)+\cdots = S_m
\]

and recalling that

\[
(m,0)+(m,1)+(m,2)+(m,3)+\cdots = 1
\]

that

\[
S_{m+1} = (1-x)S_m+mx,
\]

and from this we obtain the value given above

\[
\frac{(1-x)^m-(1-mx)}{x}.
\]
