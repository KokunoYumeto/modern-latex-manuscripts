\begin{center}\Large\bfseries REMARKS ON THE EIGHTH VOLUME.\end{center}

This eighth volume of \textsc{Gauss}'s Works supplements the first three volumes and the fourth volume, with the exception of its geodetic part. Its contents are as follows.

The divisions \textit{Arithmetic, Analysis} and \textit{Numerical Calculation} contain a series of individual notes and papers subsequently found among \textsc{Gauss}'s papers.

The \textit{Theory of Probability} division chiefly contains a number of documents on the discovery of the method of least squares.

The \textit{Geometry} divisions contain notes and letters on the foundations of geometry, Geometria situs, theorems and problems of elementary geometry, the application of complex quantities to geometry, and the theory of curved surfaces.

The divisions \textit{Arithmetic and Algebra} and \textit{Analysis and Theory of Functions} were edited by Mr. \textsc{Fricke} in Braunschweig, the divisions \textit{Numerical Calculation} and \textit{Theory of Probability} by Messrs. \textsc{Börsch} and \textsc{Krüger} in Potsdam, and the \textit{geometrical} divisions by Mr. \textsc{Stäckel} in Kiel. The note \textit{Allgemeinste Auflösung des Problems der Abwickelung der Flächen} (pages 447 to 449) was edited by Mr. \textsc{Weingarten} in Charlottenburg.

Mr. \textsc{Brendel} in Göttingen was responsible for the general editorship.

\textsc{Schering}, who had intended to add a supplementary volume to the fourth volume, left a number of preparatory materials, chiefly copies from the papers and correspondence, which could be used in preparing this edition.\hfill\mbox{\textsc{F. Klein.}}
