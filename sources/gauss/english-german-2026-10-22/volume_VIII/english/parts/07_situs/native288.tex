An interesting problem appears to be giving an analytic criterion for whether a given point lies inside or outside the figure.

The solution is easy. Taking the point as the origin of the coordinates, count all the points
\[
\begin{array}{cllll}
\alpha, & \text{where} & y, & -y', & xy'-yx' \\
\beta, & \text{where} & y, & -y', & yx'-xy' \\
\gamma, & \text{where} & -y, & y', & xy'-yx' \\
\delta, & \text{where} & -y, & y', & yx'-xy'
\end{array}
\]
with the indicated quantities positive. We then have
\[
\alpha = \gamma, \qquad \beta = \delta.
\]
If now $\alpha - \beta = 0$, the point lies outside. If $\alpha - \beta = \pm 1$, it lies inside.
