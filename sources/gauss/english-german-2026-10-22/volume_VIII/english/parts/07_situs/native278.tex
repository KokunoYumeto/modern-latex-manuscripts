\medskip \textbf{A THEOREM IN GEOMETRIA SITUS.}
\end{center}

Let the amplitude of an entire curve that returns to itself be
$=\pm n\,.\,360^0$. It has at least the following numbers of knots:
\[
\begin{array}{r@{\quad}rrrrrrrr}
&1&0&1&2&3&4&5&6\\
\text{for }n={}&0&1&2&3&4&5&6&7.
\end{array}
\]
The proof does not seem easy. It will probably help to divide the curve along its course into pieces bounded by the points where its direction is $=90^0(2n+1)$, then move an infinite straight line whose direction is $=90^0$ in the direction $0^0$ across the surface and pay due attention to the order of the pieces.
