\noindent where $\varepsilon$ is either $+1$ or $-1$ and the sign of $x$
remains undetermined. Yet since the counterpart of $n$ is $m$:

\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
y&&x \\

\end{array}

\noindent Accordingly, by following the pattern, the quantities $(a), (b),
(c)$ etc.\ are readily determined.

Now denote the character of any part of the surface that
e.\,g. adjoins the $+$ or $-$ side of $a$, by $\mathrm{Ch}\pm a$,
and the values of $(a), (b)$ etc.\ for $a=b=c=d=e=\text{etc.}=1$, by
$A:a$. Then

\[
2\,\mathrm{Ch}\,\varepsilon a - \varepsilon - A:a = \text{Const.} = k,
\]

\noindent and if the area of the figure is $= \Omega$, then the entire lateral deviation
toward the $+$ side is

\[
= k\,.\,360^0 - \frac{\Omega\,.\,720^0}{\text{area of the sphere}}.
\]
