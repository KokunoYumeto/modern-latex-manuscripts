\medskip
\textbf{[INTERPOLATION OF THE COTANGENTS AND COSECANTS OF SMALL ARCS.]}
\end{center}

The following formulas are the most useful for interpolating the cotangents
and cosecants of small arcs:

If the values correspond as follows
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cc}
arcs & cotangents (or cosecants) \\
$a$ & $\theta$ \\
$x$ & $y$ \\
$a'$ & $\theta'$, \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
then
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{I.}\quad x &= a+\frac{(\theta-y)a(a'-a)}{(\theta-y)a+(y-\theta')a'}
=a+\frac{(\theta-y)(a'-a)}{\theta-\theta'+\dfrac{a'-a}{a}(y-\theta')}\\[5pt]
&=a'-\frac{(y-\theta')a'(a'-a)}{(\theta-y)a+(y-\theta')a'}
=a'-\frac{(y-\theta')(a'-a)}{\theta-\theta'+\dfrac{a'-a}{a'}(y-\theta)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{II.}\quad y&=\frac{a'\theta'-a\theta}{a'-a}+\frac{(\theta-\theta')aa'}{(a'-a)x}
=\theta-\frac{(\theta-\theta')(x-a)a'}{(a'-a)x}\\[5pt]
&=\theta'+\frac{(\theta-\theta')(a'-x)a}{(a'-a)x}\,.
\end{aligned}
\]
