\begin{center}REMARKS.\end{center}

\textsc{Gauss} wrote the preceding note on the inserted leaf of a bound collection containing some of his papers from the years 1799 to 1813.

The integration with respect to $\lambda$ is based on the familiar formula
\[
\int_0^\pi\frac{d\psi}{(u+v\cos\psi)^2}=\frac{\pi u}{(u+v)^{\frac32}(u-v)^{\frac32}};
\]
where one is to set
\[
\begin{aligned}
\psi&=2\lambda\\
u&=\tfrac12(a^2+b^2)\cos\varphi^2+c^2\sin\varphi^2\\
v&=\tfrac12(a^2+b^2)\cos\varphi^2
\end{aligned}
\]
in accordance with the substitution
\[
\xi=\cos\varphi\cos\lambda,\qquad\eta=\cos\varphi\sin\lambda,\qquad\zeta=\sin\varphi
\]
for the coordinates.\par\nobreak\hfill\textsc{Stäckel.}\par
