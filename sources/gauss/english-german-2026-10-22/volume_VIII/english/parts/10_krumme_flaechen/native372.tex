\begin{center}
{\large POSTHUMOUS PAPERS AND CORRESPONDENCE.\par}
\bigskip
MOST GENERAL RULES FOR FINDING\\
CENTERS OF OSCULATING CIRCLES\\
AT ANY GIVEN POINT OF A GIVEN CURVE.
\end{center}

Let the position of any point in a given plane be determined by two variables $x,y$ (whether distances from given points or lines, or angles, etc.). Let
\[
P=0,\qquad P'=0
\]
be the equations of two curves, with $P,P'$ denoting arbitrary functions of $x,y$. Further, let
\[
\begin{aligned}
dP&=p\,dx+q\,dy,&dP'&=p'\,dx+q'\,dy,\\
d^2P&=r\,dx^2+2s\,dx\,dy+t\,dy^2,&d^2P'&=r'\,dx^2+2s'\,dx\,dy+t'\,dy^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Then the curves have the point determined by
\[
x=\xi,\qquad y=\eta
\]
in common if, for these values,
\[
P=P';
\]
and at that point they have the same tangent if, in addition,
\[
pq'=p'q;
\]
