\noindent\hspace*{1.2em}[If we set
\begin{equation}\tag*{[10]}
\left\{\begin{aligned}
\frac{dt}{du}&=t',&\frac{d\theta}{du}&=\theta',\\
\frac{ddt}{du^2}&=t'',&\frac{dd\theta}{du^2}&=\theta'',
\end{aligned}\right.\qquad \frac{ds}{du}=s',
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\tag*{[11]}
\left\{\begin{aligned}
da&=x^{(2)}dt+x^{(3)}d\theta,&d\alpha&=x^{(3)}dt+x^{(4)}d\theta,\\
db&=y^{(2)}dt+y^{(3)}d\theta,&d\beta&=y^{(3)}dt+y^{(4)}d\theta,\\
dc&=z^{(2)}dt+z^{(3)}d\theta,&d\gamma&=z^{(3)}dt+z^{(4)}d\theta,
\end{aligned}\right.
\end{equation}
we obtain:]
\[
\begin{aligned}
s\xi&=at'+\alpha\theta'\\
s\eta&=bt'+\beta\theta'\\
s\zeta&=ct'+\gamma\theta',
\end{aligned}
\]
[and from this, by differentiation with respect to $u$, we obtain:]
\begin{equation}\tag*{[12]}
\left\{\begin{aligned}
s'\xi+\xi's\tau&=a't'+\alpha'\theta'+at''+\alpha\theta''\\
s'\eta+\eta's\tau&=b't'+\beta'\theta'+bt''+\beta\theta''\\
s'\zeta+\zeta's\tau&=c't'+\gamma'\theta'+ct''+\gamma\theta''.
\end{aligned}\right.
\end{equation}
[If these equations are multiplied in order by $X,Y,Z$ and added, the identities
\[
\begin{aligned}
aX+bY+cZ&=0\\
\alpha X+\beta Y+\gamma Z&=0,
\end{aligned}
\]
which follow from equation 3, give:]
\begin{equation}\tag*{[13]}
\left\{\begin{aligned}
-\tau s\cos\psi&=t't'(Xx^{(2)}+Yy^{(2)}+Zz^{(2)})\\
&\quad+2t'\theta'(Xx^{(3)}+Yy^{(3)}+Zz^{(3)})\\
&\quad+\theta'\theta'(Xx^{(4)}+Yy^{(4)}+Zz^{(4)}),
\end{aligned}\right.
\end{equation}
[and this equation determines the normal curvature.]
