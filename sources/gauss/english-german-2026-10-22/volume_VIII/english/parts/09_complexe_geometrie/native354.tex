If
\[
A^2 + B^2 = -C^2
\]
is to hold, we will have
\[\begin{aligned}
A &= (m+m'i)^2 - (n+n'i)^2 \\
B &= 2(m+m'i)(n+n'i) \\
C &= (m+m'i)^2 i + (n+n'i)^2 i.
\end{aligned}\]

Expanding this gives
\[\begin{aligned}
(m+m'i)^2 &= m^2 - m'^2 + 2mm'i \\
(n+n'i)^2 &= n^2 - n'^2 + 2nn'i \\
(m+m'i)(n+n'i) &= mn - m'n' + i(m'n+n'm),
\end{aligned}\]
hence
\[\begin{aligned}
A &= m^2 - m'^2 - n^2 + n'^2 + 2i(mm' - nn') \\
B &= 2(mn - m'n') + 2i(m'n+n'm) \\
C &= -2(mm'+nn') + i(m^2+n^2-m'^2-n'^2).
\end{aligned}\]

Conversely, the real numbers can also be recovered from these.

For $m = 2$, $n = 1$, $m' = 1$, $n' = 1$, we obtain
\[
\begin{array}{lcl}
A = & 3+2i & \\
B = & 2+6i & \qquad A^2+B^2+C^2 = \left\{\begin{array}{r} 5+12i \\ -32+24i \\ +27-36i \end{array}\right\} = 0. \\
C = & -6+3i &
\end{array}
\]
