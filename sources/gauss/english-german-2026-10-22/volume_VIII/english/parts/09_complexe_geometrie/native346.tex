\begin{center}
\textbf{[THE CONIC SECTIONS.]}
\end{center}

\begin{center}
[I.]

\textbf{THEOREM.}
\end{center}

If $t$, $t'$ are the complex values of two conjugate semiaxes of an
ellipse and $e$ is the complex value of the eccentricity (distance of a
focus), then
\[
tt + t't' = ee.
\]

The points of an ellipse are given by the formula:
\[
g e^{ix} + h e^{-ix},
\]
where $x$ must run through all values from $x = 0$ to $x = 2\pi$.

Theorem. Two conjugate semiaxes are then
\[
g e^{ix} + h e^{-ix} \quad \text{and} \quad g i e^{ix} - h i e^{-ix}.
\]
Thus
\[
4gh = ee.
\]
