If the data of the problem are given, as it were, at random, one can seek a solution either geometrically, or half geometrically and half analytically (combining calculation with a rough drawing), or purely analytically. In the first two cases any impossibility becomes apparent of itself \textls[120]{in the course} of the work. But in the last procedure one \textls[120]{always} finds a result.
