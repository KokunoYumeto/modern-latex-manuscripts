\begin{center}
\bfseries STEREOGRAPHIC PROJECTION OF THE SPHERICAL SURFACE.
\end{center}

Conjugate complex numbers are always to be denoted here by the lowercase and uppercase forms of the same letter.

The radius of the sphere is set $=1$.

$n$: linear magnification factor.

1. At the point denoted by the complex number $a$,
\[
n = 1+aA.
\]

2. The opposite point is denoted by the complex number $-\dfrac{1}{A}$. At that point, therefore,
\[
n = 1+\frac{1}{aA} = \frac{1+aA}{aA}.
\]

3. To the bicomplex number $a$ there corresponds the tricomplex number
\[
\frac{2a+(1-aA)\eta}{1+aA}.
\]

4. General transformation formula:
\[
t' = \frac{at-b}{ct+d},
\]

where $t$, $t'$ refer to one and the same point in the first and second forms of representation, respectively.

Here we have
\[
n' = \text{Mod.}\ \frac{ad+bc}{(ct+d)^2}(1+tT).
\]

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}The minimum value occurs for
\[
t = \frac{C}{D}
\]

\noindent and is
\[
\text{Mod.}\ \frac{ad+bc}{cC+dD}\cdot\frac{D}{d} = \text{Mod.}\ \frac{ad+bc}{cC+dD}.
\]
