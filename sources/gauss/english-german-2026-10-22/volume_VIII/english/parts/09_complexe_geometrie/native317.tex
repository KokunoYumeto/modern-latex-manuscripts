\indent \mbox{. . . . . .} For an example in which \textsc{Pothenot}'s problem is physically (i.e. optically) impossible, suppose the three directions are required to make angles of $120^0$ with one another. This is always impossible as soon as the three points form a triangle in which an obtuse angle is greater than $120^0$.
