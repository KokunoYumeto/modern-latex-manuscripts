\indent $P$ and $Q$ represent one and the same point of the sphere in the two projections. $ACB$ is perpendicular to $CPQ$, and the angles satisfy $CAP = PAQ$.

If $AD$ is perpendicular to $PA$ and $D$ lies on $QPC$ extended, then $D$ is the stereographic representation of the point opposite that point on the sphere.

A circle centered at $Q$ with radius $QA$ is the stereographic representation of the great circle whose pole is that point of the sphere.

If $a+bi$ is the complex number for central projection and $r$ is the radius, then the complex number for the stereographic projection is

\[
\frac{(a+ib)r}{aa+bb}\left\{\BareRoot{(aa+bb+rr)}-r\right\}.
\]
