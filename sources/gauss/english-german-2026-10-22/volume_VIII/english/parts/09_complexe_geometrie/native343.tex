\noindent 3. Four numbers $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ are harmonically related if the
product of the sums of the two pairs is twice the sum of the
products of the two pairs, or if

\[
(a+b)(c+d) = 2ab+2cd.
\]

If the numbers are real, they represent four points on a straight line,
which is thus divided harmonically.

Associated with that equation is

\[
(c-a)(d-b)+(d-a)(c-b) = 0.
\]

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}Each number is calculated from the other three by the formulas

\[
\begin{aligned}
a &= \frac{bc+bd-2cd}{-d-c+2b} = \frac{b(c+d)-2cd}{2b-(c+d)} = \frac{2bb-2cd}{2b-(c+d)}-b \\
  &= +b-\frac{2(c-b)(d-b)}{2b-c-d} \\
  &= +c-\frac{(d-c)(c-b)}{2b-c-d}.
\end{aligned}
\]

If $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ are complex numbers, the points they represent lie
on a circle and the products of the sides $ac.bd$ and $ad.bc$ are equal.
