If we first apply this series to the equation
\[
\operatorname{tang}B\mathord{.}\sin T\mathord{.}\operatorname{tang}\tfrac12r=\sin\tfrac12t
\]
or
\[
\tfrac12m\tau=\sin\tfrac12t
\]
and set \(x=\frac12m\tau\) and \(y=\frac12t\), we obtain (I)
\[
\begin{aligned}
\log t={}&\log\tau+\log m+\tfrac1{24}mm\tau\tau+\tfrac{11}{2880}m^4\tau^4\\
&+\tfrac{191}{362880}m^6\tau^6+\tfrac{2497}{29030400}m^8\tau^8+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]

\indent Similarly, applying it to the equation
\[
\frac{\sin T\sin\frac12r}{\cos B}=\sin\tfrac12l
\]
or
\[
\tfrac12n\lambda=\sin\tfrac12l
\]
gives (II)
\[
\begin{aligned}
\log l={}&\log\lambda+\log n+\tfrac1{24}nn\lambda\lambda+\tfrac{11}{2880}n^4\lambda^4\\
&+\tfrac{191}{362880}n^6\lambda^6+\tfrac{2497}{29030400}n^8\lambda^8+\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
