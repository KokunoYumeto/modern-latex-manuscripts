Finally, the seven terms of \(H\) have the following values
\[
\begin{aligned}
1)\quad&-\frac{kk\cos T^2}{aacc}\mathord{.}ss\\
2)\quad&-\frac{kk\cos T^2}{12aa(1-ee)cc}\mathord{.}(1-2eess+ees^4)\\
3)\quad&+\frac{kk\sin T^2}{12aacc}\mathord{.}ss\\
4)\quad&+\frac{eekk\cos T^2}{4aa(1-ee)}\mathord{.}ss\\
5)\quad&-\frac{kk\sin T^2}{24aacc}\mathord{.}ss\\
6)\quad&+\frac{kk\cos T^2}{12aacc}\mathord{.}ss\\
7)\quad&+\frac{kk\cos T^2}{24aa(1-ee)cc}\mathord{.}(1+ss-2ees^4)
\end{aligned}
\]
Terms 1 and 6 cancel each other. Combining the remaining terms gives
\[
H=\frac{kk\sin T^2}{24aacc}\mathord{.}ss-\frac{kk\cos T^2}{24aa(1-ee)}\mathord{.}(1-10eess)
\]
from which it follows, in accordance with \(l=\lambda(1+Hrr)\), that
\[
l=\lambda(1+\tfrac1{24}\tau\tau-\frac{1-ee}{24k^4}\mathord{.}(1-10eess)\beta\beta)\quad .\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .\quad(11)
\]

Formulas 9, 10, 11, which give the solution to our problem, differ from formulas III, II, I (Arts. 25, 24, 23) only in that the former have \(\tau\) and \(\beta\) inside the parentheses where the latter have \(t\) and \(b\). As is easy to see, this produces only fifth-order differences in the final results. The former formulas, as their derivation shows, are accurate up to but excluding fifth order. This proves that formulas I, II, III found by the first method (Arts. 23---25) have the same accuracy.
