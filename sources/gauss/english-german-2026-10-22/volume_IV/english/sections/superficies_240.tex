\par\indent Let us eliminate the quantities \(\beta,\gamma\) from equations 1, 4, 7. We do this by multiplying the equations by \(bc'-cb',\ b'C-c'B,\ cB-bC\), respectively, and adding them. This gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
&(A(bc'-cb')+a(b'C-c'B)+a'(cB-bC))\alpha\\
&\quad=D(bc'-cb')+m(b'C-c'B)+n(cB-bC)
\end{aligned}
\]
an equation we can readily put in the following form:
\[
AD=\alpha\Delta+a(nF-mG)+a'(mF-nE)
\]
Likewise, eliminating the quantities \(\alpha,\gamma\) or \(\alpha,\beta\) from the same equations gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
BD&=\beta\Delta+b(nF-mG)+b'(mF-nE)\\
CD&=\gamma\Delta+c(nF-mG)+c'(mF-nE)
\end{aligned}
\]
Multiplying these three equations by \(\alpha'',\beta'',\gamma''\) and adding, we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
DD''&=(\alpha\alpha''+\beta\beta''+\gamma\gamma'')\Delta\\
&\quad+m''(nF-mG)+n''(mF-nE)\quad .\ .\ .\ .\quad(10)
\end{aligned}
\]
\par\indent Treating equations 2, 5, 8 in the same way gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
AD'&=\alpha'\Delta+a(n'F-m'G)+a'(m'F-n'E)\\
BD'&=\beta'\Delta+b(n'F-m'G)+b'(m'F-n'E)\\
CD'&=\gamma'\Delta+c(n'F-m'G)+c'(m'F-n'E)
\end{aligned}
\]
Multiplying these equations by \(\alpha',\beta',\gamma'\) and adding gives:
\[
\begin{aligned}
D'D'&=(\alpha'\alpha'+\beta'\beta'+\gamma'\gamma')\Delta\\
&\quad+m'(n'F-m'G)+n'(m'F-n'E)
\end{aligned}
\]
