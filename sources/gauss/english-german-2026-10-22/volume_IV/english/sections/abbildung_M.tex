\begingroup\small
\par\indent[Article 10, beside the last equation for determining \(\lambda\)] or \(\lambda=\cos u^*\), if the minimum value [of the ratio of magnification] is to occur at \(u=u^*\).

[Article 12, beside the equation that introduces the quantity \(w\) in this printing] The symbol \(\omega\) was used in print against my intention. It should be \(w\).

[Article 13, beside the equations for the mapping determined by the function \(f\upsilon=\upsilon-i\log k\), the corresponding equations for the function \(f\upsilon=a\upsilon-i\log k\) are written down. They were later included in the first paper of the \textit{Untersuchungen über Gegenstände der höhern Geodäsie}.]
