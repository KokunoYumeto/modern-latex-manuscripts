\par\medskip\begin{small}\noindent\textbf{Source note.} The notice of 28 October 1822 is translated from \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236005081}{\textit{Werke}, Band~IV (1873), pp.~368--370}. It reviews Carl Reinhard Müller's \textit{Theorie der Parallelen} (Marburg, 1822). The phrase \textit{abweichende Progression} is rendered as \textit{decreasing progression} in light of the other cases in Gauss's list.\par\end{small}
