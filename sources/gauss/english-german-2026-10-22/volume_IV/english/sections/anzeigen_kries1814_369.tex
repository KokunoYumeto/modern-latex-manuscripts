More generally, we would have liked to see some use of spherical trigonometry. In discussing the spheroidal shape of the Earth, the author might also have explained \textit{more explicitly} how the concept of geographical latitude must be modified from the spherical case and how the relative position with respect to the Earth's equator, axis and centre depends on that latitude. These are minor points that take nothing away from the book's overall value. The author could easily attend to them if a new edition should be called for, as it well might be for a book that can be so warmly recommended for teaching.
