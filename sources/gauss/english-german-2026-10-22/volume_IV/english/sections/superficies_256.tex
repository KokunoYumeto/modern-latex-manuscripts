\Article{24}
In our case the equations of Article 22 give
\[
\begin{gathered}
n\sin\psi=\frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}p},\quad\cos\psi=\frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}q},\quad-n\cos\psi=m\mathord{.}\frac{\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\mathrm{d}p},\quad\sin\psi=m\mathord{.}\frac{\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\mathrm{d}q}\\
nn=nn(\frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}q})^2+(\frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}p})^2,\quad nn\mathord{.}\frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}q}\mathord{.}\frac{\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\mathrm{d}q}+\frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}p}\mathord{.}\frac{\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\mathrm{d}p}=0
\end{gathered}
\]
With the help of these equations, the fifth and sixth of which are already contained in the others, we can expand \(r,\varphi,\psi,m\) or any functions of these quantities in series. We present here those especially worthy of attention.

Since for infinitesimal values of \(p,q\) we must have \(rr=pp+qq\), the series for \(rr\) begins with the terms \(pp+qq\). We obtain the terms of higher order by the method of undetermined coefficients\footnote{We considered it unnecessary to set down the calculation here. It can be shortened somewhat by a few devices.} with the help of the equation
\[
(\tfrac{1}{n}\mathord{.}\frac{\mathrm{d}rr}{\mathrm{d}p})^2+(\frac{\mathrm{d}rr}{\mathrm{d}q})^2=4rr
\]
which gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\relax[1]\quad rr={}&pp+\tfrac{2}{3}f^0ppqq+\tfrac{1}{2}f'p^3qq+(\tfrac{2}{5}f''-\tfrac{4}{45}f^0f^0)p^4qq\quad\text{etc.}\\
&+qq+\tfrac{1}{2}g^0ppq^3+\tfrac{2}{5}g'p^3q^3\\
&+(\tfrac{2}{5}h^0-\tfrac{7}{45}f^0f^0)ppq^4
\end{aligned}
\]

Next, from the formula \(r\sin\psi=\frac{1}{2n}\mathord{.}\frac{\mathrm{d}rr}{\mathrm{d}p}\), we have
\[
\begin{aligned}
\relax[2]\quad r\sin\psi={}&p-\tfrac{1}{3}f^0pqq-\tfrac{1}{4}f'ppqq-(\tfrac{1}{5}f''+\tfrac{8}{45}f^0f^0)p^3qq\quad\text{etc.}\\
&-\tfrac{1}{2}g^0pq^3-\tfrac{2}{5}g'ppq^3\\
&-(\tfrac{3}{5}h^0-\tfrac{8}{45}f^0f^0)pq^4
\end{aligned}
\]
