\Article{30}
The values of the seven terms of \(F\) are, in order
\[
\begin{aligned}
1)\quad&-\frac{kk\cos T^2}{12aacc}\mathord{.}ss\\
2)\quad&-\frac{kk\cos T^2}{12aa(1-ee)cc}\mathord{.}(1-2eess+ees^4)\\
3)\quad&+\frac{kk\sin T^2}{12aa(1-ee)cc}\mathord{.}(1-2eess+ees^4)\\
4)\quad&+\frac{eekk\cos T^2}{4aa(1-ee)^2}\mathord{.}(1-2eess+ees^4)\\
5)\quad&-\frac{kk\sin T^2}{24aacc}\mathord{.}ss\\
6)\quad&+\frac{kk\cos T^2}{12aa(1-ee)cc}\mathord{.}(1-2eess+ees^4)\\
7)\quad&+\frac{kk\cos T^2}{24aa(1-ee)^2cc}\mathord{.}(2-3ee+(ee+2e^4)ss-(2ee+e^4)s^4)
\end{aligned}
\]
Here terms 2 and 6 cancel each other. Terms 1, 4 and 7 combine to give
\[
+\frac{kk\cos T^2}{24aa(1-ee)^2}\mathord{.}(2+3ee+(2ee-12e^4)ss+5e^4s^4)
\]
while terms 3 and 5 combine to give
\[
+\frac{kk\sin T^2}{24aa(1-ee)cc}\mathord{.}(2-(1+3ee)ss+2ees^4)
\]
or, since \(2-(1+3ee)ss+2ees^4\) is identical to \(2cckk+(1-ee)ss\), they give
\[
+\frac{k^4\sin T^2}{12aa(1-ee)}+\frac{kk\sin T^2}{24aacc}\mathord{.}ss
\]
If one now also replaces \(\frac{k^4\sin T^2}{12aa(1-ee)}\) with
\[
\frac{k^4}{12aa(1-ee)}-\frac{k^4\sin T^2}{12aa(1-ee)}
\]
and combines everything, one obtains
\begingroup\fontsize{10}{12}\selectfont
\[
F=\frac{k^4}{12aa(1-ee)}+\frac{kk\sin T^2\operatorname{tang}B^2}{24aa}+\frac{kk\cos T^2}{24aa(1-ee)^2}\mathord{.}(5ee+(4ee-14e^4)ss+5e^4s^4)
\]
