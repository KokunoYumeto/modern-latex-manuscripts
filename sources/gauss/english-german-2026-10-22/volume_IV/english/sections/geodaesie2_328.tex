\par\noindent\hspace*{\parindent} Consider that the expansion above assumes only that \(Z\) is a variable quantity depending on \(u\) and that its derivative \(\frac{\mathrm{d}Z}{\mathrm{d}u}=z\) is expressed by some function of \(X\) and \(Y\). The results obtained can therefore be carried over directly to any other variable in the same situation, in particular to \(X\) or \(Y\) itself. It suffices to replace \(L,\mathrm{L},l\) and the variously marked forms of \(\mathrm{h}\) with \(T,\mathrm{T},t\) and the corresponding forms of \(\mathrm{f}\), or with \(B,\mathrm{B},b\) and the corresponding forms of \(\mathrm{g}\), respectively. Thus equation (4), which is otherwise of no direct use here, first gives us the following two equations, likewise accurate up to but excluding fourth order.
\[
\begin{aligned}
T&=\mathrm{T}+\tfrac18(\mathrm{ff}'+\mathrm{f}''\mathrm{g})rr\\
B&=\mathrm{B}+\tfrac18(\mathrm{fg}'+\mathrm{gg}'')rr
\end{aligned}
\]
From this we first infer that \(h\) and \(\mathrm{h}\), as the values of \(z\) obtained by substituting \(T\) and \(B\), or \(\mathrm{T}\) and \(\mathrm{B}\), for \(X\) and \(Y\), differ by a quantity of second order. This difference is determined, with accuracy up to but excluding fourth order, by the formula
\[
h=\mathrm{h}+\tfrac18(\mathrm{ff}'+\mathrm{f}''\mathrm{g})rr\mathord{.}(\tfrac{\mathrm{d}z}{\mathrm{d}X})+\tfrac18(\mathrm{fg}'+\mathrm{gg}'')rr(\tfrac{\mathrm{d}z}{\mathrm{d}Y})
\]
where the partial derivatives \((\frac{\mathrm{d}z}{\mathrm{d}X})\) and \((\frac{\mathrm{d}z}{\mathrm{d}Y})\), or \(z',z''\), are to take their particular values at \(X=\mathrm{T},\ Y=\mathrm{B}\), namely \(\mathrm{h}'\) and \(\mathrm{h}''\). Thus, with accuracy up to the fourth order,
\[
\mathrm{h}=h-\tfrac18(\mathrm{ff'h}'+\mathrm{fg'h}''+\mathrm{f''gh}'+\mathrm{gg''h}'')rr
\]
and substituting this value in equation (5) gives, with accuracy up to but excluding fifth order,
\[
\begin{aligned}
l={}&-hr+\tfrac1{24}(2\mathrm{ff'h}'+2\mathrm{fg'h}''+2\mathrm{f''gh}'+2\mathrm{gg''h}''\\
&\qquad-\mathrm{ffh}'''-2\mathrm{fgh}''''-\mathrm{ggh}^{\mathrm{v}})r^3
\end{aligned}
\]
