\par\medskip\begin{small}\noindent\textbf{Source note.} The notice of 20 April 1816 is translated from \textit{Werke}, Band~IV (1873), pp.~364--368. The title page and p.~24 of \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN603351808}{Johann Christoph Schwab's \textit{Commentatio} (Stuttgart, 1814)} were consulted in the SUB Göttingen copy, 8 MATH III, 65. The Latin quotations follow Gauss's review. Matthias Metternich's work is cited in its Mainz 1815 edition. Francesco Valentini's \href{https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-91963}{German--Italian dictionary} records the mathematical sense of \textit{angebliche Größe}, rendered here as \textit{assignable magnitude}.\par\end{small}
