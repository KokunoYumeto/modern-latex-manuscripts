\textit{Investigation of the present condition of the professors' widows' fund at Göttingen.}\par\medskip
\textit{Preface.}\par
\end{center}
\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}In the opinion I submitted on the matter of the widows' fund on 8 January of this year, I outlined the essential elements of the method which I regard as the only suitable one for arriving at as thorough a judgment as the nature of the subject permits. I have now completed the admittedly very lengthy calculations described there, and their results are contained in the second division of this memorandum.

Since, however, I cannot assume that most members of the body to which this paper is submitted are closely acquainted with the principles of such calculations, I thought they would welcome seeing the subject considered from other sides as well, and from more accessible points of view. Although this approach cannot yield truly \textit{precise}
\SourceLeaf{131}
results, but only general estimates and indications, it is nevertheless important to find these in agreement with the results of a more rigorous method, and in any case everything is thereby placed in a clearer light. Moreover, these explanations are closely connected with the re-examination of a key point in the existing regulations, an examination which I consider absolutely necessary and in which no obscurity must remain. I have therefore presented these developments in the first part of this essay in such detail and, I hope, so clearly that they can easily be followed.
