\Article{25}
Let \(\lambda,\lambda',\lambda''\) etc. further denote the values of the functions \(v,v',v''\) etc. obtained when for \(x,y,z\) etc. we substitute their most plausible values \(A,B,C\) etc., namely
\SourceLeaf{037}
\[
\begin{aligned}
aA+bB+cC+\text{etc.}+l&=\lambda\\
a'A+b'B+c'C+\text{etc.}+l'&=\lambda'\\
a''A+b''B+c''C+\text{etc.}+l''&=\lambda''
\end{aligned}
\]
etc.; let us put, in addition,
\[
\lambda\lambda+\lambda'\lambda'+\lambda''\lambda''+\text{etc.}=M
\]
so that \(M\) is the value of the function \(\Omega\) corresponding to the most plausible values of the indeterminates, and hence, by what we proved in art. 20, the minimum value of this function. Then \(a\lambda+a'\lambda'+a''\lambda''+\text{etc.}\) will be the value of \(\xi\) corresponding to the values \(x=A,\allowbreak y=B,\allowbreak z=C\) etc., and therefore \(=0\), that is, we shall have
\[
\Sigma a\lambda=0
\]
and similarly
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Sigma b\lambda=0,\quad\Sigma c\lambda=0\quad\text{etc.;}\\
&\text{and also}\quad\Sigma\alpha\lambda=0,\quad\Sigma\beta\lambda=0,\quad\Sigma\gamma\lambda=0\quad\text{etc.}
\end{aligned}
\]
Finally, multiplying the expressions for \(\lambda,\lambda',\lambda''\) etc. respectively by \(\lambda,\lambda',\lambda''\) etc. and adding, we obtain \(l\lambda+l'\lambda'+l''\lambda''+\text{etc.}=\lambda\lambda+\lambda'\lambda'+\lambda''\lambda''+\text{etc.}\), or
\[
\Sigma l\lambda=M
\]
\Article{26}
In the equation \(v=ax+by+cz+\text{etc.}+l\), substituting expressions VII of art. 21 for \(x,y,z\) etc. and applying the reductions evident from what precedes gives
\[
v=\alpha\xi+\beta\eta+\gamma\zeta+\text{etc.}+\lambda
\]
and in the same way we shall have identically
\[
\begin{aligned}
v'&=\alpha'\xi+\beta'\eta+\gamma'\zeta+\text{etc.}+\lambda'\\
v''&=\alpha''\xi+\beta''\eta+\gamma''\zeta+\text{etc.}+\lambda''
\end{aligned}
\]
etc. Multiplying either these equations or equations I of art. 20 respectively by \(\lambda,\lambda',\lambda''\) etc. and adding shows that identically
\[
\lambda v+\lambda'v'+\lambda''v''+\text{etc.}=M
\]
