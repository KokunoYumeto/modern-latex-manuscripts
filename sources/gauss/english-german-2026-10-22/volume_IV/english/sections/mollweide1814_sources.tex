\par\medskip\begin{small}\noindent\textbf{Source note.} The notice of 2 May 1814 in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen} is translated from \textit{Werke}, Band~IV (1873), pp.~361--362. It reviews \href{https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/15849974}{Karl Brandan Mollweide's \textit{Commentationes mathematico-philologicae tres}} (Leipzig, 1813). The copy at ETH-Bibliothek Zürich, Rar~21664, contains the right-triangle theorem on p.~46 and the discussion of Columella's approximation on p.~71.\par\end{small}
