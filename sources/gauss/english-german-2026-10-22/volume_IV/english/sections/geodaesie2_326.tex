\begin{aligned}

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{d}x'&=x'''\mathrm{d}X+x''''\mathrm{d}Y,&\qquad \mathrm{d}x''&=x''''\mathrm{d}X+x^{\mathrm{v}}\mathrm{d}Y\\
\mathrm{d}y'&=y'''\mathrm{d}X+y''''\mathrm{d}Y,&\mathrm{d}y''&=y''''\mathrm{d}X+y^{\mathrm{v}}\mathrm{d}Y\\
\mathrm{d}z'&=z'''\mathrm{d}X+z''''\mathrm{d}Y,&\mathrm{d}z''&=z''''\mathrm{d}X+z^{\mathrm{v}}\mathrm{d}Y
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus, insofar as \(Z\) is implicitly a function of \(u\) alone, we have
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\mathrm{d}Z}{\mathrm{d}u}&=z\\
\frac{\mathrm{dd}Z}{\mathrm{d}u^2}&=xz'+yz''\\
\frac{\mathrm{d}^3Z}{\mathrm{d}u^3}&=xx'z'+xy'z''+x''yz'+yy''z''+xxz'''+2xyz''''+yyz^{\mathrm{v}}
\end{aligned}
\]

\indent The successive derivatives of \(X\) and \(Y\) can be developed in the same way, or derived directly from those of \(Z\) by replacing \(z\) with \(x\) and \(y\), respectively, with the same derivative marks, if any.

Let the \textit{particular} values of the four quantities \(u,X,Y,Z\) at the two points to which our problem relates be, in that order,
\[
\begin{array}{lllll}
\text{for the first point}&R-\tfrac12r,&T+\tfrac12t,&B+\tfrac12b,&L+\tfrac12l\\
\text{for the second point}&R+\tfrac12r,&T-\tfrac12t,&B-\tfrac12b,&L-\tfrac12l
\end{array}
\]
and likewise let their values at the point midway between the two on the geodesic line be \(R,\mathrm{T},\mathrm{B},\mathrm{L}\), respectively. The italic letters \(T,B,L\) must therefore be carefully distinguished from the upright letters \(\mathrm{T},\mathrm{B},\mathrm{L}\).
