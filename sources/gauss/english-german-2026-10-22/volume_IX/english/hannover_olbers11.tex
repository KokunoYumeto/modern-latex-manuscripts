\begin{center}
\textsc{Gauss} to \textsc{Olbers}.\quad Göttingen, 14~June 1830.
\end{center}

\par\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}\mbox{.\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .}\quad I intend to devote the time I have left this summer for my own work to continuing my paper on biquadratic residues, to bring this work, whose earliest beginnings date back to 1805, closer to completion.
It will require at least two more substantial papers. For now, however, I shall stop at one and devote the first leisure I have after that to a different subject again, probably the theoretical methods of higher geodesy.
For quite some time it has been my fate always to take up work in which one cannot proceed \textls[120]{quickly} with the exposition.\quad\mbox{.\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .}
