\section*{About this translation}
\noindent Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, Volume IX, \textit{Geodesy. Continuation of Volume IV}. The Royal Society of Sciences at Göttingen issued the historical edition in 1903, with B. G. Teubner in Leipzig as distributor. L. Krüger prepared the volume with A. Börsch’s assistance. M. Brendel directed the general editorial work.

This English edition covers the historical preliminaries and pp.~1--528, with the Hanover foldout map and publisher's advertisement. OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol (Ultra) produced the AI translation and typesetting from the editable transcription of the 1903 edition. The volume preserves the attributions of the historical texts, editorial remarks and illustrations.
