\begin{center}
\textsc{Gauss} to \textsc{Bessel}.\quad Göttingen, 20~November 1824.
\end{center}

\par\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}.\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .\quad I have visited 12 stations this year and carried the triangles as far as the Weser (the farthest point is on the Garlster Heide between Osterholz and Vegesack).
At first I always left the third vertices for later, and when I left Bremen after the middle of August, I still had 6 places to visit.
Things went tolerably well at all of them, but at the last, the Wilseder Berg, the bad weather plagued me so much that even after a three-week stay I did not finish quite to my satisfaction. In the end I had to set a deadline and returned to Göttingen at the end of October.

\par\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}I reserve for a future letter a more detailed account of the outcome of the measurements, the great difficulties I had to contend with (one of them, the incessant burning of the moors, will still be fresh in your own memory), and some interesting phenomena that appeared in the course of the work.\quad\mbox{.\ .\ .\ .\ .\ .}
