\begin{center}\Large\textbf{On Determining the Constants of the Bifilar Magnetometer}\end{center}

Carl Friedrich Gauss’s paper appeared in the \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins} for 1840, article I. All fourteen articles and the historical note are AI-translated from the German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~404--426, with AI-produced typesetting. The formulas, numerical values and historical units follow the German edition.

\medskip\noindent
For related instrument terminology, see the Wiechert’sche Erdbebenwarte Göttingen’s account of the Göttingen Magnetic Club and the Deutsches Museum catalogue entry on the mirror mount for bar magnets, edited by Julia Bloemer and Benjamin Mirwald, no.~1117, version of 24 August 2016. The museum entry describes a unifilar instrument.
