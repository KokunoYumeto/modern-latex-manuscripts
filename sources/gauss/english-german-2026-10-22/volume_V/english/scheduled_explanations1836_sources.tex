\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Plots and Numerical Readings for the Scheduled Observations of 1836}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents the entire excerpt from Carl Friedrich Gauss’s explanations of the plots and numerical readings for the scheduled observations, as collected in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~568--576. It includes both notes and the two tables of readings at Marburg. The omission marks belong to the collected excerpt.

The full original article appeared as section V of \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins im Jahre 1836}, edited by Carl Friedrich Gauss and Wilhelm Weber (Göttingen, Dieterichsche Buchhandlung, 1837), pp.~90--103. The translation follows the collected excerpt.
