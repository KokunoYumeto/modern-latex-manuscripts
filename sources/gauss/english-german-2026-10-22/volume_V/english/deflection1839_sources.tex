\begin{center}\Large\textbf{On a Way to Make Deflections Easier to Observe}\end{center}

Carl Friedrich Gauss’s work appeared in the \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins} for 1839, article II. The complete eight articles and historical note are AI-translated from the German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~395--403, with AI-produced typesetting. A swing runs from one extreme position to the next. The notation, numerical table and historical units follow the German edition.

\medskip\noindent
For the instrument terminology, see the Deutsches Museum catalogue entry \textit{Kommutator}, edited by Julia Bloemer and Benjamin Mirwald, no.~1062, version of 24 August 2016.
