\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Magnetic Needle in Copenhagen and Milan\\5 and 6 November 1834}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents Carl Friedrich Gauss’s complete text as printed in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~537--540, including his footnote on Christopher Hansteen’s maps of magnetic intensity.

The first printing appeared in Heinrich Christian Schumacher’s \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~12, no.~276, 21 March 1835, cols.~185--188. The copperplate referred to in the text accompanies that printing. Its upper curve shows Copenhagen and its lower curve Milan, with separate scale keys.
