\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Adjusting the Knife Edges of a Balance}\end{center}

The complete report on Carl Friedrich Gauss’s method for adjusting the knife edges of a beam balance is AI-translated from the German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~511--513, with AI-produced typesetting.

The report appeared in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, issue~41, 13 March 1837, pp.~401--405. The reprint in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~14, no.~327 (1837), cols.~241--244, attributes it to Gauss. The text speaks of Gauss in the third person and reports on his talk at the Royal Society of Sciences.
