\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Plots and Numerical Readings for the Scheduled Observations of 1837}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents the entire excerpt from Carl Friedrich Gauss’s explanations of the plots and numerical readings for the scheduled observations, as collected in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~576--579. It includes Gauss’s note on the November observations. The omission marks belong to the collected excerpt.

The full original article appeared as section VIII of \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins im Jahre 1837}, edited by Carl Friedrich Gauss and Wilhelm Weber (Göttingen, Dieterichsche Buchhandlung, 1838), pp.~130--137. The translation follows the collected excerpt.
