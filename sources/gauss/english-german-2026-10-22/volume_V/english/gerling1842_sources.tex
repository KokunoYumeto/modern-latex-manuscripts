\begin{center}\Large\textbf{The Mathematical and Physical Institute in Marburg}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents Carl Friedrich Gauss’s complete notice about the new arrangements at the Marburg institute as collected in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~596--598. The notice draws on a report that Christian Ludwig Gerling sent to Gauss.

The notice first appeared in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~54, 4 April 1842, pp.~537--540. The translation follows the collected text and its coordinate notation.
