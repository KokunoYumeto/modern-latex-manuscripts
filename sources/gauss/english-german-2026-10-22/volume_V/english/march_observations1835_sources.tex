\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Magnetic Observations\\March 1835}\end{center}

This is an AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, of the complete March 1835 report on Carl Friedrich Gauss’s magnetic observations, as collected in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~528--536. It includes the six-row table of monthly means and the footnote on the suspension of the bar. The report gives an extract from Gauss’s account to the Royal Society at Göttingen and refers to him in the third person.

The first printing appeared in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~36, 7 March 1835, pp.~345--357. Johann Christian Poggendorff’s journal reprinted it as article XVI in the \textit{Annalen der Physik und Chemie}, vol.~34 (110), 1835, pp.~546--556, with its own editorial notes and references.
