\begin{center}\Large\textbf{General Theory of Terrestrial Magnetism}\end{center}

AI-translated from the German text in Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, volume V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~119--193, with AI-produced typesetting. The edition contains both titles, the introduction, all 41 articles and eight historical notes, the addendum with its historical editorial passages, and the comparison and auxiliary tables. The two diagrams on the historical plate are reproduced as editable traces, with approximate hatching and stroke weights.

The separate historical map of \(V/R\) named in the text is outside the available editable source. The three divisions of the chart described in Article 30 can be consulted in the reproductions from the 1839 annex in Glassmeier and Tsurutani’s edition.

Elizabeth Juliana Sabine’s English translation, revised by Sir John Herschel, appeared in Richard Taylor’s \textit{Scientific Memoirs}, volume 2 (1841), pp.~184--251. It and the revised translation by K.-H.~Glassmeier and B.~T.~Tsurutani in \textit{History of Geo- and Space Sciences} 5 (2014), pp.~11--62, were consulted for comparison.
