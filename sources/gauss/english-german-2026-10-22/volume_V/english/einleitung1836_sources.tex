\begin{center}
\Large\textbf{Introduction to the Magnetic Association's 1836 Observations}
\end{center}

Carl Friedrich Gauss wrote this introduction for the first volume of the \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins}, edited by Gauss and Wilhelm Weber (Göttingen, 1837), pp.~3--12. The English text is AI-translated, with AI-produced typesetting, from the collected German edition, \textit{Werke}, volume V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~345--351. The historical place names and the distance unit \textit{Meilen} follow that text.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Historical English translation}\par\smallskip
\noindent W.~Francis translated the introduction for Richard Taylor's \textit{Scientific Memoirs}, vol.~II (London, 1841), Article~II, pp.~20--25. Professor Lloyd and Major Sabine revised the translation. The historical English text also appears with editorial notes in André Koch Torres Assis (ed.), \textit{Wilhelm Weber's Main Works on Electrodynamics Translated into English}, vol.~I (Montreal, 2021), ch.~8, pp.~81--84.
