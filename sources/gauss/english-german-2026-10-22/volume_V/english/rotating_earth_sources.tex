\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Fundamental Equations for the Motion of Heavy Bodies on the Rotating Earth}\end{center}

The two letters from Carl Friedrich Gauss to Johann Friedrich Benzenberg, dated 2 February and 8 March 1803, and the complete derivation are AI-translated from the German text of \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~495--503. The typesetting is AI-produced, including the editable diagram. The equations and reported numerical values follow the collected edition.

The letters and derivation also appeared in Johann Friedrich Benzenberg’s \textit{Dr. Benzenbergs Versuche über die Umdrehung der Erde} (Dortmund, bey Mallinkrodt, 1804), pp.~349--353 and 363--371, respectively. The copy linked here is held by ETH-Bibliothek Zürich (Rar 1913). S. Draszow and U. Backhaus give a separate modern account of fall experiments in \textit{Dreht sich die Erde wirklich?} (Augsburg, 2003).
