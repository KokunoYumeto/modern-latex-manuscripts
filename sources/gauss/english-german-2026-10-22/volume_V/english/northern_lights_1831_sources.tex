\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Northern Lights on 7 January 1831}\end{center}

The complete report on the northern lights, including the extract from Professor Gerling’s account in Marburg and the notice of Professor Harding’s observations in Göttingen, is AI-translated from the German in Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~517--519, with AI-produced typesetting. It appeared in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen} on 28 February 1831.
