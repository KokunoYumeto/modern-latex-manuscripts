\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Procedure for Scheduled Observations}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents Carl Friedrich Gauss’s article as collected in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~541--556. It includes both authorial notes, the worked observations and the observation schedules.

The article first appeared as section II of \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins im Jahre 1836}, edited by Carl Friedrich Gauss and Wilhelm Weber (Göttingen, Dieterichsche Buchhandlung, 1837), pp.~34--50. The translation follows the wording and numerical notation of the collected edition.
