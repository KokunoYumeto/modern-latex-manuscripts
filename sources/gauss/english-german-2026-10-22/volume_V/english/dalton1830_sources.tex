\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Review of Benzenberg’s \textit{On Dalton’s Theory}}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents Carl Friedrich Gauss’s complete review of Johann Friedrich Benzenberg’s book in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~583--591, and the preceding section title, Reviews of Works by Other Authors. Gauss examines how Benzenberg applied Dalton’s hypothesis to barometric height measurements.

The review first appeared in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~196, 11 December 1830, pp.~1945--1956. The translation follows the collected edition’s text, numerical notation and ten tables.
