\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Instructions for Determining the Magnetic Effect Exerted by a Bar Magnet at a Distance}\end{center}

Carl Friedrich Gauss’s paper appeared in the \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins} for 1840, article II. All seven articles and the introduction are AI-translated from the German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~427--435, with AI-produced typesetting. The coordinates, signs, historical units and both numerical tables follow that edition.

\medskip\noindent
For related terminology, see the U.S. Geological Survey’s introduction to geomagnetism and the GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences’ overview of the Earth's magnetic field. The coordinates in this paper follow Gauss’s conventions.
