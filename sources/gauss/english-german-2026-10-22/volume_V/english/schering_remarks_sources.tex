\begin{center}\textbf{Schering’s Remarks}\end{center}
\noindent This AI translation of Ernst Schering’s \textit{Bemerkungen} is based on the German text in Carl Friedrich Gauss, \textit{Werke}, vol.~V, pp.~637--640, edited by the Royal Society of Sciences in Göttingen. See the \href{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN236006339}{Göttingen digitization} and the \href{https://archive.org/download/Werkecarlf05gausrich/page/n648}{second printing (1877)}.
\par\smallskip\noindent D. H. Delphenich’s separate English translation, \href{https://neo-classical-physics.info/uploads/3/4/3/6/34363841/schering_-_remarks_on_gauss.pdf}{\textit{Remarks}}, is based on the Göttingen edition of 1867, pp.~637--640.
\par\smallskip\noindent The work on diffraction mentioned by Schering is F. M. Schwerd, \href{https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-16523}{\textit{Die Beugungserscheinungen aus den Fundamentalgesetzen der Undulationstheorie}}, Mannheim, Schwan \& Goetz, 1835. The digitized copy is held by the ETH Library, Zürich, Rar 2268.
