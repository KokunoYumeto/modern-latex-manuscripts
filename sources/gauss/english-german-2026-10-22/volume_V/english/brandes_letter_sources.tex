\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Letter from Gauss to Brandes}\end{center}

Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter to Heinrich Wilhelm Brandes is AI-translated from the German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~509--510, with AI-produced typesetting. The complete letter and the collected edition’s bracketed note are included.

Brandes quotes the letter in his article \textit{Linsenglas} in Johann Samuel Traugott Gehler’s \textit{Physikalisches Wörterbuch}, vol.~6, part~1 (Leipzig, E.~B. Schwickert, 1831), pp.~437--439. The title page credits Brandes, Gmelin, Horner, Muncke and Pfaff with preparing this revised edition. The linked copy is held by ETH-Bibliothek Zürich (Rar 8737: 6/1).
