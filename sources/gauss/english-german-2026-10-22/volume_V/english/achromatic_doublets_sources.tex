\begin{center}\Large\textbf{On Achromatic Doublet Objectives}\end{center}

Carl Friedrich Gauss’s paper on achromatic doublet objectives and the elimination of colour dispersion is AI-translated from the German text of \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~504--508, with AI-produced typesetting. The complete paper includes the bracketed historical note and both editable tables.

The paper appeared in the November--December 1817 issue of \textit{Zeitschrift für Astronomie und verwandte Wissenschaften}, edited by B.~von Lindenau and J.~G.~F. Bohnenberger, vol.~4, pp.~345--351. The historical journal and the collected edition are separately linked. Gauss cites Bohnenberger’s related 1816 paper in vol.~1, pp.~279 and 392.
