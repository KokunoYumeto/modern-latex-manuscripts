\begin{center}\Large\textbf{A New Aid for Magnetic Observations}\end{center}

This notice of Carl Friedrich Gauss's lecture of 19 September 1837 appeared in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen} on 30 October 1837. The text is AI-translated from the collected German edition, \textit{Werke}, volume V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~352--356, with AI-produced typesetting. The lengths and the weight of the magnet retain the historical units \textit{Meile}, \textit{Fuß} and \textit{Pfund}.

\medskip\noindent
Gauss's separate treatise on the same instrument appeared in the volume of the \textit{Resultate} for 1837, published in 1838. William Francis translated that treatise for Richard Taylor's \textit{Scientific Memoirs}, vol.~II (1841), pp.~252--267, with revisions by Major Sabine and Professor Lloyd. The opening of that translation was consulted in André Koch Torres Assis (ed.), \textit{Wilhelm Weber's Main Works on Electrodynamics Translated into English}, vol.~I (2021), ch.~11.
