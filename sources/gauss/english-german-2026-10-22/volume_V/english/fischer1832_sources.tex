\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Review of Fischer’s Instructions for Making Artificial Magnets}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents Carl Friedrich Gauss’s complete review of Friedrich Fischer’s book in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~591--594. Gauss describes methods of magnetising straight bars and horseshoe magnets and reports his own experiments.

The review first appeared in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~145, 10 September 1832, pp.~1441--1445. The reviewed book bears the imprint Heilbronn, 1833.
