\expandafter\def\csname HistoricalNoteText544\endcsname{In volume 9 of the Astronomische Nachrichten, this distinguished naturalist has also given us a general map of the \textit{total} intensity. Much as we must acknowledge this fine work with gratitude, I cannot refrain from remarking that a general map of the \textit{horizontal} intensity would be far more useful in \textit{many} respects, especially together with a reliable general map of declination, in laying a thorough foundation for a general theory. For \textit{this} purpose, specifying the magnetic force by giving the total intensity, inclination and declination (which are generally regarded as the simplest choice of elements) is precisely the least useful way to determine it. I must reserve a fuller development of this claim, which may seem paradoxical to some, for another place. I can only hope that this naturalist will soon give us what is needed from the abundance of treasures he has collected.}
