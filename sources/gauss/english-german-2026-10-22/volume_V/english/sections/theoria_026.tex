In part, like the method the distinguished \textsc{Laplace} had used for exterior points, it relies on considering infinite series that do not always converge, a consideration that could certainly have been avoided. Moreover, on closer examination, this solution by the eminent \textsc{Ivory}, which at a glance might seem to bear some resemblance to ours, will be found to rest on \textit{entirely different} principles.
