The following table compares our formulas with observations at 91 points across all parts of the Earth. Since the three maps from which the data for our calculation were taken are intended to represent conditions in the most recent period, we included only observations from that period in the comparison, giving preference to places where all three magnetic elements had been observed. We cannot yet require the observations to be exactly contemporaneous without reducing the observations at our disposal to a very small number.

I give the following details about the observations used here [at the end of this article] for comparison.
