\begin{center}\Large\textbf{On Using the Magnetometer to Determine Absolute Declination}\end{center}

Carl Friedrich Gauss’s paper appeared in the \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins} for 1841, article I. The full paper and both historical notes are AI-translated from the German text in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~436--443, with AI-produced typesetting. The geometrical argument, observation records and numerical notation follow that edition.

\medskip\noindent
For related theodolite terminology, see Helmut Fuchs and Tatjana Koukal, \textit{Vermessungskunde für Forstwirte}, pp.~91--92, Universität für Bodenkultur. Gauss’s instrument geometry and conventions govern the translation.
