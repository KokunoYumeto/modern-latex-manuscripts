\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Magnetic Observatory in Göttingen}\end{center}

The complete August 1834 report on the Göttingen magnetic observatory, including both tables, is AI-translated from the German in Carl Friedrich Gauss’s \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~519--525, with AI-produced typesetting. It describes the observatory’s building and instruments, coordinated observations of magnetic declination, measurements of the Earth’s magnetic intensity and Wilhelm Weber’s wire connection for galvanic experiments and telegraphic signalling.

The report appeared in the \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, no.~128, 9 August 1834, pp.~1265--1274. An abridgement appeared in \textit{Polytechnisches Journal}, vol.~55 (1835), miscellany, p.~393 onwards. An abridged English excerpt of the telegraph passage appeared in the unsigned \textit{Sketch of William Edward Weber} in \textit{Popular Science Monthly}, vol.~40, February 1892.
