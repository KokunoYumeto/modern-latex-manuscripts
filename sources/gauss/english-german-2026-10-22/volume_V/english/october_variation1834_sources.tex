\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Observations of Magnetic Variation\\in Göttingen and Leipzig}\end{center}

The complete collected version of Carl Friedrich Gauss’s report on the observations of 1 and 2 October 1834 is AI-translated from the German in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~525--528, with AI-produced typesetting. It includes the worked example with the six readings, the five intermediate means and the final mean.

The journal version appeared as article XLII in Johann Christian Poggendorff’s \textit{Annalen der Physik und Chemie}, vol.~33 (109), 1834, pp.~426--433. The journal’s two larger observation tables, plate reference, editorial notes and extended schedule belong to that version. The collected text follows its own readings and scope.
