\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Review of the Magnetic Association’s 1836 Results}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents Carl Friedrich Gauss’s complete notice of the first volume of results edited by Gauss and Wilhelm Weber, as collected in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~595--596. The notice describes the volume’s five articles and ten lithographed plates.

The notice first appeared in \textit{Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen}, nos.~102--103, 29 June 1837, pp.~1021--1023. The journal gives the book imprint as 1837. The collected notice gives 1838, which is retained in the translation.
