\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Three Years of Daily Declination Observations}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents Carl Friedrich Gauss’s article as collected in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~556--568. It includes the monthly means, the comparisons of morning and afternoon observations, and the tables of fluctuations.

The article first appeared as section III of \textit{Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins im Jahre 1836}, edited by Carl Friedrich Gauss and Wilhelm Weber (Göttingen, Dieterichsche Buchhandlung, 1837), pp.~50--62. The translation follows the wording and numerical notation of the collected edition.
