\begin{center}\Large\textbf{On Wilkes’s Observations near the Magnetic South Pole}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents the entire excerpt from Carl Friedrich Gauss’s letter to Heinrich Christian Schumacher in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), p.~580. It records Gauss’s provisional estimate of the pole’s position based on Wilkes’s reported measurements.

Gauss dated the letter 9 February 1841 in Göttingen. The full letter appeared in \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, edited by Heinrich Christian Schumacher, vol.~18, no.~417, cols.~143--144 (Altona, 4 March 1841). The translation follows the wording and numerical notation of the collected excerpt.
