\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Letter from Gauss to Schumacher\\23 April 1836}\end{center}

This AI translation into English, with AI-produced typesetting, presents the letter extract printed in \textit{Werke}, vol.~V (Göttingen, 1867), pp.~540--541. It includes both comparisons of the Campa and Shelton clocks.

Heinrich Christian Schumacher published the extract in the miscellaneous news column of \textit{Astronomische Nachrichten}, vol.~13, no.~310, 11 June 1836, col.~359. The translation follows the wording and notation of the collected edition.
