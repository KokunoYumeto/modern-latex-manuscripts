\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Terrestrial magnetism}\end{center}
The complete Latin treatise is AI-translated from \textit{Werke}, volume V (1867), pp.~79--118. It includes the 1832 presentation and 1841 publication title, the repeated title, the introduction, all 28 articles and Gauss’s six historical notes.

The German version by Dr.~Kiel, edited by E.~Dorn (Wilhelm Engelmann, Leipzig, 1894, \textit{Ostwald’s Klassiker}, no.~53), and Susan P.~Johnson’s July 1995 English draft translated from Kiel’s German are separate translations consulted for comparison.
