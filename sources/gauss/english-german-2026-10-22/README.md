# Gauss: English and German LaTeX sources

[English reader, 459 pages](../../../reader-pdfs/gauss/english-german-2026-10-22/Gauss_Collected_English.pdf) · [German reader, 482 pages](../../../reader-pdfs/gauss/english-german-2026-10-22/Gauss_Werke_Deutsch.pdf) · [Edition and scope](../../../docs/gauss-english-german.md) · [Download all sources](Gauss_English_German_Collected_LaTeX.zip)

The source tree contains complete Volume I in both languages, the historical
title matter and first two complete papers of Volume II, and the title and
complete introduction of its third paper. The remainder of Volume II and the
other volumes are outside this edition.

The `volume_I` and `volume_II` directories each contain `english` and `german`
sources. Compile `main.tex` in each directory with XeLaTeX twice. The
`originals` directories contain historical source-language passages retained
separately from the translations. See `README.txt` for bibliographical details.

English and German translations prepared using OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol.
