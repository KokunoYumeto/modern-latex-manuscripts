# Weber Volume I: complete German working reader

Start with [`reader/Weber_VolumeI_German_Complete_Working_SourceRepair_20260731.pdf`](reader/Weber_VolumeI_German_Complete_Working_SourceRepair_20260731.pdf). It is the current 420-page German working reader for Heinrich Weber's *Lehrbuch der Algebra*, Volume I, through Section 188 and the printed errata.

The complete editable source is [`source/Weber_VolumeI_German_Complete_Working_SourceRepair_20260731.tex`](source/Weber_VolumeI_German_Complete_Working_SourceRepair_20260731.tex). The Zenodo release also groups the reader, source, concise QA, and current source-check crops in one ZIP.

This release replaces the p.88 reader as the current Volume I German reading surface. It does not alter the older English Volume I reader or the Volume II and III working readers.

The whole Volume I body has undergone a source-fidelity repair pass, held-section retranscription, four global consistency sweeps, and broad visual spot checks. A later stricter page-by-page cold re-verification is complete through printed p.124; printed p.125 is the next cursor. This is therefore a complete **working reader**, not a claim of full page-by-page recertification, a critical edition, or mathematical certification.
