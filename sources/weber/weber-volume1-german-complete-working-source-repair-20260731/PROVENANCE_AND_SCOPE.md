# Provenance and scope

The controlling source is the public scan of Heinrich Weber, *Lehrbuch der Algebra*, Volume I:

- 686 physical PDF pages
- 51,955,203 bytes
- SHA-256 `50BA482A39C9918AC81B31D631B65B11C37C5E67BEC42C559F6D504A28196DEB`
- printed-page to source-PDF locator used in this audit: physical page = printed page + 26

The full scan is not duplicated in this package. Fourteen recent source-derived bands and tight crops are included as compact verification witnesses. Their hashes, dimensions, page locators, and review dispositions are recorded in `visual_witnesses/VISUAL_WITNESS_MANIFEST.csv`.

The reader is a repaired scholarly working reconstruction. Its body is source-oriented, but the release does not claim a new critical edition, complete symbol-by-symbol recertification, legal rights clearance, or transfer of underlying rights. The selected crops are provided to make the actual recent checking work reproducible without requiring readers to regenerate them.
