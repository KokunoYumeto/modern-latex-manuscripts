# SGA3 diagram final-fidelity correction

Date: 2026-07-28

This is the controlling additive correction to the earlier SGA3 diagram
review guidance. It preserves the exact Claude source-method artifact and all
earlier release receipts as history, while raising the evidence required for
any future claim of final diagram fidelity.

## Controlling review scale

For every native or reconstructed SGA3 diagram:

- inspect the complete delivered page at 300 dpi for context, pagination,
  nearby prose, captions, footnotes, clipping, and collisions;
- compare the complete diagram or relevant detail to the authority at about
  5000 dpi by default;
- use a targeted 9000-dpi crop where a label, punctuation mark, arrowhead,
  decoration, crossing, or other feature remains ambiguous;
- inspect every node and edge manually, including exact labels, indices,
  primes, fonts, arrow direction, arrow style, hooks, surjections, curvature,
  crossings, label side and placement, and terminal punctuation; and
- bind the review to a top-level session lead who did not merely accept a
  contact sheet or build report.

Contact sheets, OCR, extracted text, build success, and 300-dpi-only approval
do not close final diagram fidelity. Existing 600-dpi and 1200-dpi evidence
remains legitimate history and review context; this correction does not
invalidate or supersede it.

Every delivered diagram must be native editable TeX. PNG or other raster
diagram crops may be retained privately as authority witnesses, with hashes
and coordinates recorded where appropriate, but they must not appear as
diagram content in a new public reader or payload.

## Ownership and release effect

Top-level sessions must own disjoint diagram ranges. A final receipt must name
the owned range, state that it does not overlap another active session, and
bind the lead-signed high-zoom evidence to the exact source image, diagram
source, delivered PDF, and any repair.

Earlier diagram-bearing SGA3 checkpoints remain immutable working history.
They are not deleted or silently rewritten. Reopen an earlier approval only
when it relied on 300-dpi evidence alone or when an independent review finds a
material defect. Existing 600-dpi and 1200-dpi evidence remains valid. A held
package is superseded additively by a no-overwrite corrected successor;
unrelated payloads are not duplicated.

Loop-1 text and equation checkpoints remain useful custody objects, but a
diagram-bearing scope is held from new public transport until its complete
native-TeX diagram set and lead-signed high-zoom evidence are present. This
does not withdraw or delete earlier public working-history objects.
