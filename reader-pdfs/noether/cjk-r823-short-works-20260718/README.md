# Noether R823 CJK short-work readers

This folder mirrors the eight PDFs directly exposed on Zenodo record
[21423112](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21423112): complete Noether Papers 26
and 36 in Simplified Chinese, generic Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

The exact editable TeX, German R823 controls, decision records, manifests, build
evidence, and render checks are preserved under
`sources/noether/r823-synchronized-components-20260718/02_cjk_r823_components/`
and in file `09` of the Zenodo record. The PDFs were independently rebuilt,
visually checked, verified against their manifests, and downloaded back from
the public record without a hash mismatch.

Status: complete for these two bounded works and technically validated. They
are working translations, not externally native-reviewed editions. The
Traditional Chinese texts are generic adaptations, not Taiwan- or Hong-Kong-
localized editions. Nothing here is a critical edition or certification of the
broader CJK cumulative readers.
