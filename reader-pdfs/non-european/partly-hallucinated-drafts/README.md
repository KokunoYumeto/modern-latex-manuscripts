# Partly hallucinated drafts

The readers in this folder are AI-produced drafts of mathematical classics from China and India, and the translations
and combined volumes made from them. They are partly hallucinated: they contain problems, commentary or verses that
the historical authors did not write, presented as if they were the original text. Do not quote or cite them as the
historical texts.

## Up for adoption

These editions are good projects that we want to see finished, but our compute is limited and we cannot say when we
will return to them. Anyone is welcome to take one up. Work from a printed edition (for the Chinese texts, the
*Sibu congkan* or *Siku quanshu* texts on Chinese Wikisource are a good start; for the *Āryabhaṭīya*, the text on
Sanskrit Wikisource); the drafts show the planned scope and layout, not the text. Announce a scope with the
[adoption issue](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/modern-latex-manuscripts/issues/new?template=adopt.yml); the
[adoption board](../../../docs/adopt.md) lists the other open projects.

## What is wrong in each file

| Files | What is not the historical text |
|---|---|
| 40-01 Chinese Original – *Nine Chapters*, vols. 1–9 | Many problem statements are genuine, but the commentary attributed to Liu Hui is largely invented; it uses modern terms such as 最大公約數, 長方體 and 圓柱體. About 16% of the text occurs in the *Sibu congkan* edition. |
| 10-01 English, 20-01 modern Chinese | Made from 40-01. |
| 40-02 Chinese Original – Li Ye, *Ceyuan haijing*, vols. 1–12 | Volume 1 and volumes 5–8 follow the *Siku quanshu* text; volumes 2–4 and 9–12 are mostly summaries written in classical style; the appendix is modern. |
| 10-02 English, 20-02 modern Chinese, 30-01 Arabic | Made from 40-02. |
| 40-05 Chinese Original – Qin Jiushao, *Shuxue jiuzhang*, fasc. 1–9 | A paraphrase in modern Chinese with invented details. In 推計土功, for example, it adds hauling distances that are not in Qin's problem and gives 1,260 丈 where Qin has 一千二十六丈 (1,026). |
| 10-03 English, 20-04 modern Chinese, 30-02 Arabic | Made from 40-05. |
| 50-01 Indian Original – Āryabhaṭa, *Āryabhaṭīya* | The *Daśagītikā* verses are not Āryabhaṭa's: they give the numbers of planetary revolutions in Sanskrit number words, where his text uses his syllabic numerals (verse 1.3: युगरविभगणास् ख्युघृ … गुरु ख्रिच्युभ …). |
| 10-07 English | Made from 50-01. |
| 50-03 Indian Original – Bhāskara II, *Līlāvatī* | A 16-page introduction with selected problems and a vocabulary, not the text. |
| 10-09 English | Made from 50-03. |
| 01–05 Combined volumes | Contain the drafts above. |

The comparisons were made with the *Sibu congkan* and *Siku quanshu* texts on Chinese Wikisource and with the
*Āryabhaṭīya* on Sanskrit Wikisource.

The other AI-produced readers in `reader-pdfs/non-european/` (Yang Hui, Zhu Shijie, the *Fenlei shishu* and geometry
readers, *Sunzi suanjing*, *Bījagaṇita*, Brahmagupta in English, the al-Kāshī, al-Khwārizmī, al-Ṭūsī and Khayyām
readers, and the reference selections) have not been compared with printed sources. *Sunzi suanjing* follows the
received text for the most part, with modern explanations added. The transcriptions of Rosen (1831), Ruska (1917) and
Smith and Karpinski (1911) follow their printings.
