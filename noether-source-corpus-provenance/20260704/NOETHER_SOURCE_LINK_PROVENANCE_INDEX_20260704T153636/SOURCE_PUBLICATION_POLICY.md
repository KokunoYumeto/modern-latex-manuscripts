# Source Publication Policy

- Source corpus means existing source-level mathematical material and its provenance trail, not generated translations.
- The first publishable unit is the link/provenance index.
- LaTeX payload zips may be published when they are source-level candidates, pass blocker scans, and are accompanied by file-level provenance manifests.
- PDF/DOCX/source-archive payloads remain manifest-only until license/source gating is complete.
- Candidate records without license evidence are manifest-only until cleared.
- Native-review, approval, and canonical-translation claims are out of scope for this source-corpus index.

- Credential-like query strings and scanner trigger values are redacted before publication; matching payload files remain blocked until manually cleared.
