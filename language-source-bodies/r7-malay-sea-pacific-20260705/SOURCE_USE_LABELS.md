# Source-Use Labels

`source_gated_draft_support_body`

Target-language mathematical bodies with enough source-canon baseline for
scoped draft/non-canonical support. Current rows are Indonesian proof/specialist
PDF/text witnesses and limited Malaysian Malay course/register or Malay
abstract witnesses. These are still not native reviewed or accepted
terminology.

`comparator_only_body_not_translation_authority`

Glossaries, PRPM/MABBIM/search snapshots, or other comparator bodies. These may
support search and register comparison but do not authorize translation,
accepted terminology, or local adoption.

`route_context_body_not_translation_authority`

Official or academic route/context bodies that show language, curriculum,
policy, or math-access context but do not provide exact target-language
higher-algebra source content.

`source_return_body_requires_exact_topic_gate`

Bodies captured during SEA/Pacific source-return work, often lower-math,
glossary, platform, language-resource, or exact fallback evidence. These require
an exact topic/register gate before any draft translation use.

`title_listing_body_not_translation_authority`

Listings, book indices, or title-only bodies. They are provenance or discovery
evidence only.

`source_package_context_body_not_math_authority`

Source archives or TeX-family packages that are source-level bodies but not
target-language mathematical proof/algebra authority for this lane. Current
examples are language-resource/source-package context rows.

`gap_no_body_not_copied`

Rows with no literal body copied because the source was blocked, missing,
title-only without body, or an explicit status/search gap.
