## Read this first

This collection is a work in progress. Its transcriptions and translations are AI-produced, except where an edition credits human work. When the project started, AI tools could not yet do this work reliably, and much of what is here comes from experiments in finding a method that works; those results are rough. The tools have become more reliable since.

Some editions are in a readable state, but even the readable ones very likely contain errors, or at least inelegancies, and only some of them are of decent quality. Many are still worth reading, especially translations that exist nowhere else. Apart from a few tentative exceptions, none of it is at the level of a human mathematician's work. Read with discretion, and check important statements and formulas against the original.

# Modern LaTeX Editions of Mathematics Manuscripts

Historical mathematics transcribed into modern LaTeX and translated into other languages, with reader PDFs and editable sources. The larger editions have their own repositories, listed below; this repository holds the overview, the smaller editions and earlier work.

Zenodo has blocked access to our account, so please use our GitHub repositories from now on.

## Editions

### Emmy Noether, mathematical works

Papers 1–43, the 1929/30 lectures written up by M. Deuring, and the Kapferer–Noether work. The German text is checked page by page against the printed originals; every edition comes in two readings, the text as printed and the text with the printed misprints corrected. Overview of all languages: [kokunoyumeto.github.io/emmy-noether](https://kokunoyumeto.github.io/emmy-noether/).

### Carl Friedrich Gauss, collected works

The original-language *Werke*, Volumes I–VIII (Volume V in selected works, Volume VII to Article 130), and English and German translations of Volume I and works from Volumes II and III: [gauss-collected-works](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/gauss-collected-works).

### Heinrich Weber, *Lehrbuch der Algebra* I–III (1895–1908)

The German text and an English translation of all three volumes, with the printed tables of contents: [weber-lehrbuch-der-algebra](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/weber-lehrbuch-der-algebra).

### al-Khwārizmī

The algebra (Cairo 1939), the geography (Mžik 1926) and the Latin arithmetic, each in its own language with notes on the errors of the printed editions: [al-khwarizmi-public](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/al-khwarizmi-public).

### Chinese mathematical classics

The *Nine Chapters* with Liu Hui's commentary, the *Ceyuan haijing*, the *Shushu jiuzhang* and the *Sunzi suanjing*: punctuated Chinese text and a complete English translation, unit by unit: [chinese-mathematical-classics](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/chinese-mathematical-classics).

### Yoshio Mikami, *The Development of Mathematics in China and Japan* (1913)

The complete text with the figures: [mikami-development-of-mathematics-in-china-and-japan](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/mikami-development-of-mathematics-in-china-and-japan).

### al-Battānī, the *Zīj* (Nallino, 1899–1907)

The Arabic text and tables, Nallino's Latin translation and apparatus, and an English translation of the canons and tables: [al-battani](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/al-battani).

### Ernst Steinitz

German text and English translation: configurations (1894, 1897), modules in algebraic number fields (1899, 1911, 1912), continuity and irrational numbers (1899), abelian groups (1901), the one-sided polyhedron (1905), and the algebraic theory of fields, §§1–24 (1910): [reader-pdfs/steinitz](reader-pdfs/steinitz/).

### J. J. Sylvester, *Collected Mathematical Papers*, Volume I

Through page 493 of the book: [reader-pdfs/sylvester](reader-pdfs/sylvester/).

### I. M. Gelfand's seminar

A Russian transcription of the handwritten seminar notes with comparison PDFs; the Russian and English reading editions are in progress: [gelfand-seminar-russian-diplomatic](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/gelfand-seminar-russian-diplomatic).

### Luigi Bianchi, *Lezioni*

Volume I of the *Lezioni di geometria differenziale* (1902) in Italian, English and Chinese, and pages 1–116 of the *Lezioni sulla teoria dei gruppi continui finiti di trasformazioni* (1903) in Italian and English: [bianchi-lezioni](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/bianchi-lezioni).

### G. Frobenius, papers on group characters (1896–1903)

Ten papers, German text and English translation: [frobenius-group-characters](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/frobenius-group-characters).

### J. Willard Gibbs, *Scientific Papers*, Volume I

Printed pages 1–134: [gibbs-scientific-papers](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/gibbs-scientific-papers).

### Paul Gordan

*De linea geodetica*, the *Thetafunctionen* paper, the *Theorie der Abelschen Functionen* (with A. Clebsch) and the *Formensystem* in the original language and English, and the German text of the *Vorlesungen über Invariantentheorie*: [paul-gordan](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/paul-gordan).

### Adolf Kneser, *Lehrbuch der Variationsrechnung* (1900)

The German text, an English and a Chinese translation: [kneser-lehrbuch-der-variationsrechnung](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/kneser-lehrbuch-der-variationsrechnung).

### James Clerk Maxwell, *A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism*, Volume I (1873)

Printed pages 1–79 and 111–267: [maxwell-treatise-electricity-magnetism](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/maxwell-treatise-electricity-magnetism).

### Henri Poincaré, *Œuvres*, Tome I (1928)

French text and English translation, from the beginning through the memoir on curves defined by a differential equation (without its Chapters XV–XVI): [poincare-oeuvres-tome-1](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/poincare-oeuvres-tome-1).
